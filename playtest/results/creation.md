## Interpretation

These are broad exploratory builds, not prescribed class builds. Later utility
purchases intentionally explore cross-class access. Banked DP is retained rather
than inventing filler purchases. Summon/control cards are not automatically
purchased because the first combat experiment excludes those layers.
