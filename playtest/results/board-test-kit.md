# Next physical or VTT test session

This is a reproducible tabletop setup, not a claim that a map simulation was run.
Use the four level-1 builds in results/builds.json: Protector, Blade, Arcanist,
Shepherd. All learned cards remain available; first pass voluntarily limits play to
ordinary attacks, Defend, shield, healing and movement to isolate the foundation.

Coordinates are (column,row), zero-based. Diagonals cost one. Record initial order,
turn choices, positions, damage, healing, new wounds, Grit and elapsed real minutes.
Play paired encounters with the same preparation; healing enabled/disabled is a
policy comparison. Explain to players why a known card is disabled in that test.

## Board A: open approach

Board 10 × 7, no obstacles. PCs: Protector (3,2), Blade (3,4), Arcanist (1,2),
Shepherd (1,4). Four enemies: (6,1), (6,2), (6,4), (6,5). Use the candidate anchor:
HP 14, physical resistance 1, Speed 6, physical attack 5/6/8, adjacent, Agility defense.
No potions, no cover. Enemy priorities: attack a reachable upright target; if several,
choose lower current HP. Do not move through occupied standing creatures.

This tests whether the Protector and Blade can meaningfully protect the back line,
and whether the healer can reach a patient without ignoring positioning. Creature
spare Maneuvers unused for the first comparison, matching the numerical baseline.

## Board B: single doorway

Same board and starts. Wall occupies column 4 except doorway (4,3). No diagonal
movement through a wall corner (provisional test convention). Heavy cover may apply
through the doorway if DA rules an actual partial attack path; hidden/no path cannot
be targeted. Record any ruling rather than quietly treating a wall as three Banes.

This tests occupied squares, patient access, and whether the front character creates
real protection. AoE is still disabled on the first run.

## Add exactly one feature per paired replay

1. Allow casting to provoke damage-only Basic Melee; let casters Disengage and move.
2. Compare Strong interruption using the same map. Do not add Counterspell yet.
3. Add an enemy Ember Beast; players may spend Main Actions extinguishing.
4. Add one grappler and let an affected character actually move after escaping.
5. Add a second grappler; allow ally Force assistance.
6. Add one summon card; track every occupied square including uncommanded bodies.
7. Add Expose Enemies, with eligible adjacent allies and no free-attack chains.
8. Use a level-3 continuation with Flame Fan; its rectangle and compulsory inclusion
   are written on the card. Do not grant an Adept card to a level-1 character.

Reset to baseline between features. These comparisons should measure changes, not
accumulate eight unmeasured complications into one final encounter. For each case,
record what action or position could have prevented the adverse outcome and whether
players could recognize that option before it happened.

Do not use elapsed round count alone as difficulty: record wounds, resource use,
turns with no meaningful choice, and rules lookups. Speed at the table is one of the
system's explicit goals and is not measurable through Monte Carlo timing.
