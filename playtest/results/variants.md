# Alternative advancement choices

These share the validated level-1 starts, then spend differently. The durability
plans buy Toughened once per level from level 2, not retroactively at level 1.
All grants are counted as carried in this deliberately conservative inventory
audit; redundant medium armor can be left behind when heavy armor is acquired.

| Blade | durability | 7 | 25 | 3 | 30 | 6/16 |
| Arcanist | specialist | 3 | 16 | 2 | 13 | 1/4 |
| Arcanist | specialist | 7 | 16 | 2 | 47 | 1/4 |
| Shepherd | support | 3 | 20 | 2 | 5 | 3/10 |
| Shepherd | support | 7 | 24 | 2 | 43 | 3/10 |

These eight additional snapshots validate spending trade-offs; they are not
included in the primary combat sweep. HP cards can be affordable at higher levels,
so the once-per-level restriction should be evaluated alongside the opportunity
cost of 2 DP. Specialized purchases can leave more banked DP in this small catalog.
