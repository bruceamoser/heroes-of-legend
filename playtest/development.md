# Creation and advancement

## Agreed sequence

1. Assign Brawn, Fortitude, Agility, Guile, Knowledge, Reason, each −2 through +2,
   totaling +3. They do not increase with level or DP.
2. Choose ancestry and two of its five traits. Culture is independent of ancestry.
3. At level 0, choose one culture pair and one background pair. Each grants its
   listed rank-1 discipline and card. Grants must supply their card prerequisites.
4. Level-0 DP = 10 + Knowledge + Reason + Guile, including negative adjustments.
   Buy eligible Novice cards; **no discipline purchases at level 0**. Spend or lose
   this pool before level 1.
5. At level 1, choose a class meeting both permanent-attribute minimums (Odd none).
   Choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from its list and one signature option.
6. A fresh level-1 DP pool uses the same formula. Buy disciplines at class prices
   and eligible cards; spend or lose. An existing rank-1 grant does not become rank 2.
7. Each duplicate granted discipline or learned card supplies 1 DP to that stage.
   No free replacement selection and no duplicate equipment. Paid duplicate cards
   are not allowed unless explicitly repeatable.
8. Apply explicit equipment grants, counting an already-owned compatible item once;
   describe ordinary equipment appearance. Calculate HP, capacity, bulk, Speed, Grit.

Mental sum ranges −3 to +6 because all attributes total +3: each stage has 7–16 DP
before duplicates. Example: Knowledge 2, Reason 1, Guile −1 gives 12 DP each stage.
Stage expiry does not remove learned cards or granted disciplines.

## Advancement — Agreed

DA grants milestone levels. From level 2 onward gain 8 + new level DP: 10, 11, 12,
13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18 through level 10. Bank unused DP; spend only in a safe place
as determined by DA. No class-based automatic HP or attribute growth.

Cards cost 2/4/8 DP for Novice/Adept/Master; minimum level 1/3/7 (Novice purchases
also allowed at the level-0 creation stage). Card prerequisites are purchase gates,
not only activation gates. Discipline ranks 1–3 must be bought in order, with no
additional level gate. Cards are independent, not chains.

Class grants a chosen exclusive signature at levels 1, 3, 7. Only class and level
are signature prerequisites; extra signatures of your class may be bought at the
normal tier cost. No multiclassing or acquiring another class's signatures.

HP = 12 + ancestry contribution + class contribution + Fortitude + chosen trait/card
benefits. Starting values approved as test fixtures; see ancestry/class tables.
Small HP-increase cards may be bought once per level, without banking missed
purchases. Grit starts 2 at level 1, gains 1 at level 3 and 1 at level 7; expenditure
is permanent. A once-only Master card can grant one additional Grit.

Magic items may modify attributes, usually at most ±2 each. Highest positive and
strongest negative apply, not sums of same-sign modifiers; final attributes may
exceed ±2. Curse text may override. Items do not change permanent creation DP,
class eligibility, or HP established from Fortitude.

## Test ledger requirements

Record every grant and purchase in order, prerequisite ranks at purchase, DP cost,
remaining pool, expiry, inventory, and recipe choices. Advance using actual catalog
IDs. Banked DP is valid; spending everything is not proof of a better build.
Coins are a single currency; **provisional fixture** starting coins are 10 base,
zero ancestry/background/class adjustment pending economy design. Baseline combat
buys nothing with these coins. Potions are a separate explicit scenario supply.
