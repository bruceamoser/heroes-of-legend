# Equipment

## Agreed

Ordinary melee weapon: 1 Bulk. Ordinary ranged weapon: 1 Bulk. Attack cards define
mechanical usage and outcome; weapon appearance does not change damage. Multiple
compatible cards reference one owned item. Acquisition grants a missing ordinary
item once, not another if already owned. Selling/losing an item does not reactivate
the old acquisition grant. Specialty items must explicitly state exceptions.

Capacity = 10 + 3 × Brawn. Encumbrance = carried Bulk above capacity, minimum 0.
Speed loses that amount. Most meaningful items/bundles are Bulk 1, incidental items
0, very few items 2. No exempt backpack or hidden free inventory. Containers do not
remove content Bulk. Coins on person need no Bulk; DA judges treasure loads.

Inventory ladder: d20 → d12 → d10 → d8 → d6 → d4 → out. After each use roll its die;
on 1 reduce a step, otherwise retain. Last use still works. No attributes or Boons.
Each bottle/bundle tracked separately; no pooling. Ammunition tests once per attack,
not per AoE target. Depleted expendables no longer take Bulk; retain reusable tools.

Draw weapon as part of its attack. Drop free. Stow/pick up accessible item Maneuver.
Armor changes outside combat unless card says otherwise. Multiple worn armor pieces
are allowed, each Bulk counts, strongest resistance per type applies.

Magic equipment is additive: e.g. +1 fire damage, Burning 1 on Strong. It may be a
weapon, armor, cloak, belt, eyeglass, etc. Requirements and whether identification
is necessary are explicit. No attunement layer. Removing an item ends its continuing
benefits but does not undo damage already dealt. Identification does not teach an
ability or waive prerequisites.

There is no automatic general equipment kit. Only explicit ability grants populate
starting inventory. Test scenarios can separately provide named supplies, including
potions, and must count them in Bulk. No default pricing/resale schedule is adopted.

Greater and Superior potions restore 10 and 16 HP respectively. Supply die is
independent of strength; all restore Life energy, minimum 1 after resistance.
