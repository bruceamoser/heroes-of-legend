# Ancestries

HP contributions and base Speed are approved starting fixtures. All traits are
provisional. Choose two distinct traits; identical numeric bonuses do not stack.
Culture is independent of ancestry. Tolkien-inspired flavor is a direction, not
a source of numerical statistics.

| Ancestry | HP | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Human | 2 | 6 |
| Elf | 2 | 6 |
| Dwarf | 4 | 5 |
| Halfling | 1 | 5 |

## Human

| Trait | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Hardy | HP +1 |
| Long Stride | Base Speed +1 |
| Adaptable | Choose one ordinary spoken language |
| Steady Hands | Boon to ordinary delicate repairs outside combat |
| Familiar Face | Boon when seeking mundane lodging through conversation |

## Elf

| Trait | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Long Stride | Base Speed +1 |
| Keen Sight | Boon to visually searching a distant scene |
| Woodland Step | Ignore difficult terrain from natural undergrowth during voluntary movement |
| Long Memory | Boon to recalling events personally witnessed |
| Graceful | Boon to balancing on a narrow stationary surface |

## Dwarf

| Trait | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Hardy | HP +1 |
| Stone Sense | Boon to noticing unsafe stonework |
| Sure Footed | Boon to balancing on loose stone |
| Craft Heritage | Boon to evaluating ordinary worked metal, not crafting permission |
| Deep Voice | Distinctive resonant voice; flavor only |

## Halfling

| Trait | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Hardy | HP +1 |
| Quiet Step | Boon to moving quietly outside combat |
| Small Comforts | Boon to finding mundane shelter in inhabited countryside |
| Keen Hearing | Boon to locating a sound source |
| Unassuming | Boon to appearing harmless before hostile action |
