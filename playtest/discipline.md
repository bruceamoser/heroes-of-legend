# Disciplines

Ranks bought sequentially; no level gates on ranks. Card purchase gates are separate.
Costs per rank are agreed: Home 1/2/4, Adjacent 2/4/8, Foreign 3/6/12, Opposed 4/8/16.
The reduced 12-discipline catalog and each class affinity below are provisional.
Melee unifies weapon appearance rather than reinstating separate sword/axe skills.
All unlisted affinities are Foreign. Odd pays Adjacent for every discipline.
