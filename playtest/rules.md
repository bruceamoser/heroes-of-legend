# Rules

## Resolution — Agreed

Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and a consequence matters. State the stakes
first. Correct fictional action can reveal a clue without rolling. Ordinary plausible
actions need no purchased skill; specialist procedures require their card.

Roll **3d6 + one printed or appropriate attribute**: Brawn, Fortitude, Agility,
Guile, Knowledge, Reason. There are no skill ranks or other numeric roll modifiers.
Weak: total 8 or below; Standard: 9–14; Strong: 15 or above. Weak gives progress,
not automatic success. Three retained natural 6s give Strong plus a benefit;
three retained natural 1s give Weak plus a complication. DA adjudicates unspecified
benefits/complications; automated tests omit these extra riders and report that limit.

Boons and Banes cancel, then cap the net at three. Roll one extra d6 per remaining
Boon/Bane and keep the highest/lowest three. Difficulty uses Banes, not variable
success thresholds. Example: 2 Boons and 1 Bane give 4d6, keep the highest three.

Declare a card before rolling. All learned cards are available; there is no prepared
hand limit. Card text determines its attribute, range, requirements, and outcomes.
A card may improve an ordinary action without making that action exclusive.

## Turns — Agreed

Once at combat start, one unmodified 3d6: 12+ players start, otherwise creatures.
That side starts every round. Alternate one character from each side, each side
choosing who acts; finish the remaining side's turns when the other runs out.
Every character acts once per round.

One Main Action, one Maneuver, movement up to Speed, one Reaction. Reaction refreshes
at the start of your turn and is available initially. Main converts to Maneuver;
Maneuver converts to another movement allowance. No reverse conversion. Movement
can be split. Converting both actions allows a full sprint of three Speed allowances.
Effects that tick do so at the end of the affected character's turn.

## Attacks — Agreed structure; provisional damage in catalog

Attacks always connect; outcome determines damage and other effects. Player attacker
rolls once, including against another player; the receiving player does not also roll.
When a creature attacks a player, that player makes the printed defense roll without
spending an action. Strong defense receives Weak attack effects, Standard receives
Standard, Weak receives Strong. Defense may use any appropriate printed attribute.
Helplessness does not remove the defense roll; it applies three Banes.

A player's AoE uses the worst applicable net Boon/Bane situation among eligible
affected targets for one shared roll. Individual resistance then applies. Creature
AoE gives each affected player a separate defense roll. Range, shape, exclusions,
and inclusion are printed on the card. All distances are squares, with no mandatory
real-world conversion. Partial cover adds 1 Bane to an attack, heavy cover 3;
for player defense these become Boons. No visible target or no attack path prevents
direct targeting unless the card explicitly permits otherwise. Hidden is not cover.

Highest matching resistance applies to each damage type; do not add armor values.
Highest matching weakness offsets matching resistance. Resolve each component with
floor 0, then apply a minimum of 1 to the entire damaging event, not each component.
An event with no damage instruction does not gain damage from this minimum. Physical
is one type. Example: physical 3 + fire 1 against physical resistance 4 and fire
resistance 0 deals 1 total. Additional fire was fully responsible for that damage.
Working resistance ceilings: 3 at levels 1–2, 4 at 3–6, 6 at 7–10; provisional balance
ceilings, not permission to acquire those amounts without an actual card.

Bonuses of the same kind do not stack: use the highest. Boons/Banes stack by their
own rules; numbered conditions stack when their entries permit. Different resistance
types coexist. Armor resistance occurs after the roll and changes no outcome tier.

## Movement — Agreed

Normal square costs 1 movement, difficult square 2. Diagonal costs 1. You cannot pass
an occupied square unless its occupant is prone (cost 2); never end in an occupied
square. Prone crawl costs 2 per normal square, 3 per difficult square. Standing costs
2 movement. Speed is ancestry base + Agility − encumbrance, minimum 0.

Forced movement spends the terrain value of each entered square. Any positive
remaining push enters the next square even if its cost exceeds the remaining budget,
then stops. Forced movement does not provoke. Movable creatures/objects struck join
one moving group; all members take collision damage, and the group shares one
remaining movement budget. A hard obstruction stops the group. Collision damage is
remaining push budget for a movable collision, remaining +1 for a hard obstruction,
minimum 1, with resistance. The original mover bounces one square opposite at the
end if that square is available; no extra collision damage from the bounce.

**Open geometry detail:** group footprint on bends and congested boards is not
settled. Test straight-line collisions explicitly; do not pretend a scalar model
validates arbitrary board geometry.

## Optional test layers

**Agreed:** opportunity attack uses a Reaction and Basic Melee only. Voluntarily
leaving reach provokes before departure; Disengage prevents it that turn. Casting
adjacent, standing, stowing, and retrieving were identified as further triggers.
**Provisional test:** these resolve before the action, cause damage only, and cancel
completion only if the actor cannot continue (e.g. reaches 0 HP). A separate Strong-
interrupt experiment compares a proposed alternative. Neither interruption variant
is a settled rule. Potion retrieval is included in use; do not charge a second action.

Free attacks cost no actions, may occur multiple times per turn, and never trigger
additional free attacks. Only the granting card determines their attack permission.

Summons act on their controller's turn. Main command grants summon Main + Maneuver;
Maneuver command grants summon Maneuver + movement. Same action conversions apply.
One command per summoned creature per turn; two different summons can receive the
two commands. A summoning Main grants an immediate free Main command, counting as
that creature's command. Unlimited summons, but no uncommanded actions/movement,
no inherent Reactions, no summoning chains. Controller rolls. Body blocking remains
a balance issue even for inactive summons.

## Exploration and group work — Agreed

No routine travel roll or exhaustion track. DA calls rolls for consequential hazards.
Social rolls resolve plausible attempts, not involuntary player beliefs. No retry of
an unchanged failed approach without a meaningful change.

Outside combat, a group task sets a target and eligible contributors. Each rolls:
Weak contributes 1, Standard 2, Strong 3. Critical/Fumble retain 3/1 plus rider.
Specify binary (one effort, no retained progress) or cumulative (retain progress).
Do not scale the target upward merely because more players contribute. A participant
contributes a roll or Aid, not both. No group checks in combat; no group crafting or
derivation. Example: a stuck door target 7 opened by contributions 2+2+3; 2+2+2 does
not open it. A cumulative excavation target 7 retains that 6 progress.
