# Crafting, foraging, identification

**Agreed:** Specialist cards supply permission. Every crafted product requires a
recipe, normally found or derived. Acquiring a crafting card grants a selection
of compatible recipes. Recipe count is unresolved; this kit provisionally grants
**two ordinary recipes**. Recipes are reusable and may be shared; no group crafting
or derivation. Materials normally come from foraging rather than assumed carried
stock. Carrying material bundles counts Bulk and checks their inventory die per use.

Consumable crafting/foraging: Weak/Standard/Strong increases one chosen supply's
inventory die by 1/2/3 ladder steps up to its maximum. May start from out (first step
d4). This is not multiple independent bundles per outcome. Permanent construction
and derivation use cumulative 1/2/3 progress against a printed target.

Identification is separate from derivation. Card outcome controls revealed
information; item text controls whether understanding is needed to use it. An ordinary
magical sword may work immediately while an arcane tome may require identification.

## Provisional recipes

| ID | Product | Permission | Materials / attempt | Time / attempt | Target or result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| arrows | Ammunition, max d8 | Field Craft | Suitable wood/feathers or 1 supply use | 1 hour | Raise 1/2/3 steps |
| torches | Lighting, max d6 | Field Craft | Suitable wood/fat or 1 supply use | 1 hour | Raise 1/2/3 steps |
| rations | Food, max d8 | Field Craft | Safe edible forage or 1 supply use | 1 hour | Raise 1/2/3 steps |
| rope | Rope | Field Craft | Suitable fibers or 1 supply use | 1 hour | 4 cumulative progress |

A Field Craft purchaser chooses two of these. No free ingredients are implied.
Forest Forage supplies suitable mundane materials or food only in a plausible forest;
DA states depletion/time constraints before rolls. No endless repeated roll in one
exhausted patch. A successful recipe derivation needs physical item access throughout.

Derive Recipe: ordinary item target 12, 4 hours per attempt; special items have
individually printed targets and intervals (possibly years). No arbitrary high target
is presumed to balance combat until its actual time and campaign consequences are
specified. Recipes for magic items and healing potions are outside this first kit.

## Complete entry example

A Human Intellect with Frontier culture (Lore 1 + Forest Forage) and Apprentice
background (Craft 1 + Field Craft) begins with the permissions, tools, and two
ordinary recipes needed to work. In a viable forest, spend one hour foraging a
material supply, then one hour attempting the arrows or torches recipe. Check the
material die after the crafting attempt. This does not require carried starting
materials or an unpriced purchase. Full level-1/3/7 ledgers are in
[craft-route.json](results/craft-route.json). Outside a suitable environment, materials
must actually be acquired; owning the recipe does not create them.
