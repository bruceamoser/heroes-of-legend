# Review a book chapter

Compare teaching prose against current design/rules and catalog entries. Check player-roll direction, action costs, timing, prerequisites and shared terms. Ensure examples obey the rules and help play. Remove redundant boilerplate, not necessary limits. Build and inspect affected pages. Flag new rulings instead of silently inventing them.
