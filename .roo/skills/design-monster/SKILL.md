# Develop a creature

Use design/bestiary/card-layout.md, procedure.md and encounter guidance. Print all attributes, defenses, CV, explicit Stability grants and unique actions. DA attack tables are Weak/Standard/Strong defense outcomes. Keep monster abilities separate from player cards. Summon eligibility is explicit; author a separate player profile where appropriate. Provide a stable artwork reference. CV is an estimate, not a balance guarantee.

Check the shared monster-ability catalog before writing a new capability. Reuse a
template across appropriate creatures; local descriptive text can express their
different bodies and styles. Create a separate template for a meaningful change
in trigger, target, effect or limitation, not merely a new creature name. Choose
Maneuvers and Reactions for the creature's trope and role; do not fill a quota.
Keep complete rules on the creature, and keep command-only summon profiles
separate from DA Reactions. Run the bestiary action-coverage audit when changing
these assignments and update the review judgments and provisional CV as needed.
