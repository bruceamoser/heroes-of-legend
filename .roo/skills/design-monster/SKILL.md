# Develop a creature

Use design/bestiary/card-layout.md, procedure.md and encounter guidance. Print all attributes, defenses, CV, explicit Stability grants and unique actions. DA attack tables are Weak/Standard/Strong defense outcomes. Keep monster abilities separate from player cards. Summon eligibility is explicit; author a separate player profile where appropriate. Provide a stable artwork reference. CV is an estimate, not a balance guarantee.

Follow [project instructions](../../../.github/copilot-instructions.md).
