# Recommendation — hol-rulebook

**Verdict:** Purge the named-equipment catalog in two passes rather than one, because the catalog is load-bearing in ways its own prose denies. PASS ONE relocates before anything is deleted: the seven weapon properties become a menu a player selects when taking a kit Armament option; the Armament line gains an option that holds two weapons, since the combat chapter's procedure requires the Light property on an off-hand weapon and no current option can produce one; the nine class starting kits resolve to categories instead of 43 named entries; and the armour slot values and gear-table note rules land on the kit lines that price them. PASS TWO deletes the five named tables, de-registers them from the glossary and reference-sheet lookup surfaces, and repairs the six items the tables were silently propping up. Do not begin pass two before pass one lands: deleting first removes play rather than clutter.

**Confidence:** 0.78

## Resolved (per the librarian)
- t-01: The kit model is not a complete replacement as written. Armament is a single select of five options and only one of them carries a shield, so a two-weapon build cannot be created even though the combat chapter defines the procedure and gates it on the Light property. Four of the nine class starting kits issue a second weapon. The named tables must not be deleted before a two-weapon option exists in the kit.
- t-02: The residue is not wholesale and not negligible. Every weapon property except Versatile has a consumer, but the consumer set is far smaller than raw greps suggest because of homonyms. Versatile is decorative and may be retired. Finesse, Light, Loading, Thrown, Reach and Two-Handed must be relocated to the kit and card layer before their definitions are removed. Armour slots are item-level values that no per-class rule reproduces, so they survive only as a kit value or an explicit per-class figure.
- t-03: The catalogue's consumers are enumerable and the blast radius is measured: 400 named-item lines across the nine files, 644 name occurrences, 77 table rows. Six defects must be repaired in the same pass, including the Plate 3 Armor entry requirement that no armour table supports, the Stealth-column promise that resolves to nothing, and the reference-sheet reprint that has silently diverged from the equipment chapter.

## Dissenting views

- contrarian on t-01: Round 2 abandoned the blanket refute and argued deletion-as-drafted holds for the weapons table and its reprint: the bestiary's weapon-labelled attack lines each print their own damage row and five name weapons with no catalog row at all, so that consumer is not a consumer; and the property burden shrinks because the two-slot rule already exists as a category rule and the off-hand tag has no procedure. Recorded, and partially accepted: the shrunk property burden is why Versatile is retired, but the two-weapon procedure the contrarian looked for sits outside the nine-file source set and does exist.

## Rulings applied

- r-001 (t-01): The council must treat the category kit as an incomplete substitute, not a complete one. Five mutually exclusive Armament options cannot record a loadout carrying two weapons, cannot price an object that qualifies under two different lines, and carry no item-level property, attack-mode or range value. Withdrawing the catalog therefore cannot be a pure deletion of tables: the Armament line must be extended or an explicit rule must declare extra carried weapons to be free fiction outside the budget, and the class kit lists and character-creation exemplars must be reconciled to whatever the kit can actually say.
- r-002 (t-02): The council must treat the residue as real but mixed, and must treat deletion as more than a single-file editorial change. Part of what the tables carry is already stated item-independently elsewhere and survives removal; the remainder does not. No surviving surface reproduces the mapping from a specific object to its properties, its range band, its Discipline entry requirement, its armour slot value, or the rules that the gear catalog attaches to items by name. Those items must have a stated destination before, or in the same change as, the rows that carry them.
- r-003 (t-03): The council must treat the consequences as cross-chapter and partly unadjudicated. Withdrawal strands item-named consumers in the class, character-creation, magic-item, bestiary, glossary and reference-sheet chapters, and it collides with a live contradiction already in the book: the Disciplines chapter asserts an armour entry requirement that no armour table records. That contradiction must be decided explicitly as part of the removal, not inherited silently, and the ranks that only a named item currently justifies call for a balance decision of their own instead of being swept away with the rows.
