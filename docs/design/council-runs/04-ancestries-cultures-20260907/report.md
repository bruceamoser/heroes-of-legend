- Final verdict: Chapter 4 (Ancestries & Cultures) is REJECTED as a publishable unit, 7-0 unanimous, on a defect set the orchestrator re-verified against canon and the built PDF. TIER 1 (mechanical, same-day micro-PR, chapter-local + one cross-chapter companion): (1) 04-ancestries-cultures:181 - the Forge-Trained Dwarf Adept gift prices a flat +6 damage add-on to a maintained weapon hit; the only surviving flat damage rider in the whole book after the 2026-08-19 weapons rework, and off-budget (Adept Standard 9 + 6 = 15, not on the 6/9/12 row), so it also breaks the chapter's own ceiling rule (no gift out-performs a class ability at the same tier). Reprice as a one-row damage-tier bump, the sanctioned convention (cf. ch15:193 Charge Attack). (2) 04-ancestries-cultures:92 - the Halfling Lucky trait glosses a boon as 'an extra d6', contradicting ch06:45 (a boon = roll 4d6 keep highest three); delete the parenthetical. Companion cross-chapter: 21-glossary.qmd:27 carries the same stale 'An extra d6 added to a 3d6 roll' gloss and is self-contradicting on the same line (it also says 'Roll 4d6 and keep the highest three') - fix there too (mechanical, one line). (3) 04-ancestries-cultures:157 - the Human gift 'Second Wind' collides with the ch06:145 bonus-roll result 'Second Wind' (both 6-HP recovery); rename the gift to 'Second Breath' (verified 0 occurrences book-wide, so the rename is safe and single-file). (4) 04-ancestries-cultures:96/102 - the mid-chapter figure is pinned between two forced pagebreaks, rendering PDF p.67 as a near-blank page (figure + caption only); drop one flanking pagebreak so the image flows with adjacent text. TIER 2 (substantive): none. TIER 3 (design decision, to decisions-pending, NOT implemented): 04-ancestries-cultures:144 promises the gifts 'follow the same card format as class abilities', but all four gift tables (lines 148-193) are 3-column (Gift/Tier/Effect) with no Discipline-prerequisite column, while ch08:109/168 states Adept and Master cards always require Disciplines (tier = prereq count: 1/2/3). This is a genuine fork, not a mechanical defect: (A) gifts are exempt as innate bloodline traits (the chapter's own 'inherited, not learned' framing) and line 144's 'same card format' is an overstatement to be reworded; or (B) gifts carry discipline prerequisites and a 4th column plus ~20 new prereq assignments is owed - a content wave, not a micro-PR. No locked ruling covers gift prereqs, and the two directions are substantively different, so it goes to Bruce unimplemented. VERIFIED NOT findings (orchestrator re-check, per verify-before-trust): gift counts are 5 per ancestry (the game-architect's '3/4/3/4' is a counting artifact); the gift tables carry no prereq column (the game-architect's 'prereq shapes verified' is a false positive on that point - the librarian's defect 3 is the real one); every skill name in the culture tables resolves in the ch07 roster; every Discipline exists in the ch08 taxonomy; @sec-chapter-character-creation resolves (02:1); the Quick Study cross-ref resolves (09:185); Second Breakfast's 6-HP healing sits on the Adept budget row; 0 em-dashes, 0 damage dice, 'DA' used throughout (no GM); the ch02 ancestry table (02:66) states the Lucky trait without the bad gloss, corroborating the fix. The 7-0 is unanimous; the single dissent is the game-architect's two counting artifacts, which the orchestrator corrected against the file.
