## Rulings applied

- r-001 (t-03): Rule for the permission layer: a weapon's identity is carried only by what it permits, never by magnitude. The identity surfaces are the Discipline recipe in the weapon table's requirement column, the weapon's property set, and the tags a card header can read; a flat damage number, tier bump or better-weapon ladder printed on a weapon row is out of bounds, because the largest gear-sourced value that leaves the damage ladder intact is zero. The requirement column is the single live gate: the class-kit prerequisite waiver is deleted or narrowed to a pointer rather than left standing beside it, and recipes are to be repaired by price and card coverage rather than by a number. A multiplier surface on hero-side weapons, damage type included, may not ship unless the same change prints the gate that stops it lifting a row above the next band. Round 2 is to be built inside this boundary.
