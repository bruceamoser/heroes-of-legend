## Outcome

**Verdict: adopt r-001 as the operating boundary for round 2 and build weapon identity entirely in
the permission layer.**

- **t-01 - REJECTED** (refute 5, support 1). The equipment layer does not carry development
  richness: the kit spends 0 of the 32 DP by level 10 and supplies no rung of the L1-L10 ladder.
  Development richness has to come from the card pool and the Discipline recipe.
- **t-02 - REJECTED** (refute 5, support 0). No filing defended weapons-as-flavour as shipped
  delivering the combat-strategy goal: the weapon table carries eight strict dominations, a weapon
  acts through geometry and permission rather than magnitude, and the kit tag vocabulary has no
  satisfaction rule.
- **t-03 - RULED (r-001, support 6, refute 1), binding and immutable.** Weapon identity is carried
  only by what a weapon permits: the Discipline recipe in the requirement column, the property set,
  and the card-visible Kit tags. No flat damage number, tier bump or better-weapon ladder on a
  weapon row, because the largest gear-sourced value that keeps the damage ladder intact is **zero**.
  A multiplier surface, damage type included, ships only together with the printed gate that
  prevents it lifting a row above the next band.
- **t-04 - RESOLVED** (support 7, refute 0). Round 2 converged inside the r-001 boundary: the recipe
  is the permission route, the property layer carries the round-to-round decision, development
  choice comes from the card pool, **the named-equipment purge does not proceed as written**, and
  the cost is a **measured blast radius (6 of 25 chapters, 148 changed lines, 386 to 388 folios)**
  rather than an estimate.
- **problem-scoping - CONTESTED and NOT DECIDED** (refute 3, support 2, quorum 4). The ruling schema
  seals only `t-NN` topic ids, so no ruling could be emitted and the engine cannot close this topic.
