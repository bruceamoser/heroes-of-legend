## What the council established

- **Item DR is the headline defect, and it is ONE defect class with two instances** (Spellguard Shield
  `:356`, Adamantine Plate `:380`). The chapter printed a passive reduction on a wearer in a book whose
  hero DR comes from the Iron Skin talent (ch16, ch15, ch09). The defect is the **source**, not the size:
  r-002 explicitly carries the shield's magnitude forward unchanged.
- **The Adamantine Plate's flat 7 and the shield's 2 both go.** The ruling covers the plate even though
  only the architect's ballot raised it.
- **The three-way roll contradiction is real but the potion is not the culprit.** `:28` said attribute
  plus skill, `:42` said attribute only, `:442` printed a bare 3d6. The ruling fixes the two prose
  statements and preserves the consumable idiom, because a self-contained consumable roll is the
  book's own shape (ch22's morale check is another no-modifier 3d6).
- **The two Uncommon cloaks were a genuine domination pair**, plus an echoed flavour line. The judge
  ruled both halves, including the flavour echo no round ever answered.

## Destroyed leads (do not re-file)

- **The per-attacker reading of the DR invariant is wrong.** The contrarian recomputed both readings:
  an invariant keyed to the attacker's damage row would condemn the SHIPPED Iron Skin Master rung
  (DR 8) as well, so the premise fails. r-002 records the refutation as a condition. The invariant
  binds the defender's play band (DR 2 / 6 / 9), and the surviving defect is the SOURCE of the number.
- **The caption comma is a BOOK-WIDE pattern, not a ch17 defect.** Four files carry a stray comma
  before the caption's closing underscore (`13-combat:438`, `17-magic-items:35`, `19-gm-guidance:382`,
  `20-bestiary:242`). The editor-in-chief correctly declined to file it as chapter-local; it ships as a
  four-file mechanical sweep instead.
- **Shadow Thorn's "ignore 1 point of DR from non-magical armor" is legal.** The librarian showed the
  bestiary does print armour-sourced DR for monsters, and monster DR is a stat-block value the armour
  rework deliberately kept.
- **Also cleared:** the Chalice's multi-target healing composition, Dazzled as a defined condition,
  the Frost Nova magnitudes, the Locket's attitude tiers (they exist in ch14's ladder), the Bag of
  Holding's pounds, and Discipline items not bypassing the L3/L7 purchase gates.
