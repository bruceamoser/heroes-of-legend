# Recommendation — hol-rulebook

**Verdict:** Publish chapter 11 once r-001's remaining chapter-local conditions land; do not rebuild it. The council's closing position is that the chapter holds where the run tested it hardest - its 68-card inventory, its tier-in-heading convention, the Protection ward ladder's figures and ranks, its eight agreeing cross-references and the plant heading's plural all survive - and that what fails is small, card-local and mechanical. Two items are already implemented and merged: the round-one mechanical suite the rulings confirm (PR #638) and the arcane Protection trio rename (PR #640). Four ruling conditions remain to implement post-close, all chapter-local: key Turn the Air's cold figure to a value on the row its own Adept card carries, or declare it a leveled condition with its level named; replace the bare four on Ward of Iron's Strong line with a Master-row value or a sanctioned tier step; put the paired header tags in one consistent order at 11:375/11:541 and 11:456/11:521 and stop spending the Ward tag on cards that grant no damage reduction; and strike the header modifier no rung of the same card uses at 11:438, 11:541 and 11:604, rename Paperweight's coin-keyed object and close its open-ended strongest rung at 11:133 and 11:137. Neither figure may be paid for out of the locked rows 4/6/8, 5/8/11, 7/10/14 or the cantrip line 1/3/5. The internal-consistency topic is terminal - quorum rejected it, so its refutes are recorded but charter no repair of their own - and four items belong to other chapters rather than to this one: the DR carve-out paragraph and its five named granters, the divine plant heading, the magic-system chapter's cantrip comparative clause, and the two missing book-level definitions (ending an effect by magic, and the header mode word).

## Resolved (per the librarian)
- t-01: Ruled by r-001. The topic has shrunk to two live off-row figures on the merged text and the rest is spent. First, Turn the Air (11:473-481) must print an Adept-row value for its cold figure, whose row floor is five, or declare the chip on the card as a leveled condition with its level named; the printed one must not remain. Second, Ward of Iron (11:491-500) must print a Master-row value, or a sanctioned tier step, in place of the bare four, which sits three under the Master floor of seven; the value four must not remain on the card. Both corrections are made on the card: the locked rows 4/6/8, 5/8/11 and 7/10/14 and the cantrip line 1/3/5 must not be amended to accommodate either card, and no cantrip's exempt figure may be cited as licence for a sub-row figure printed on a tiered card. The Ember Lance derived pair and the misplaced illustration are closed findings that must not be reopened or re-filed under t-01. The unrebutted chapter-local wording items stay inside t-01 as editorial repairs and create no new topic: strike the header modifier no rung of the same card uses at 11:438, 11:541 and 11:604, and rename Paperweight's coin-keyed object and close its open-ended strongest rung at 11:133 and 11:137. The framing and disposition filings f-027 and f-028 went unrebutted and are taken as the topic's working disposition.
- t-02: Rejected: seven refutes against the reject quorum of four. The topic is terminal and charters no repair of its own - no sealed ruling covers it and none of its refutes is commissioned as work, so the chapter's internal-consistency charges stand on the record without being re-litigated here. Nothing under t-02 moves a row, a figure or a section.
- t-03: Ruled by r-002 for the supporting filings on every axis they engaged: the ward ladder's figures and ranks stay as printed (ranks 1/1, 2/2 and 3/3 against grants of one, two and three, a top value of three against the tier caps three, four and six, and each ward card's no-stacking line), the card inventory stands, the eight agreeing cross-references stand, the plant heading at 11:334 keeps its plural, and the cantrip line with this chapter's three cantrip top rungs does not move.
