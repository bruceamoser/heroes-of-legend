M2 [15-equipment:132,148] Oil (flask) and Volatile Reagents: replace bespoke '1 ongoing fire damage until they spend an action' with 'applies Burning 1' per the ch13 conditions table (13-combat:174), unifying the two near-duplicate cells. M4 [15-equipment:120,125] Improvised weapon parentheticals '(1/1/2 damage)' -> '(1/1/2 + Brawn)', anchoring improvised weapons to the Basic Unarmed card (09-talents-abilities:287-295) and stating the improvised-weapon rule once in the chapter. M5 [15-equipment:52] Melee Damage note 'Missile and thrown attacks add Agility' -> flat, no attribute added, per 08-disciplines:195 and the flat 1/2/3 Basic Archery/Thrown cards (09-talents-abilities:257-277). M6 [15-equipment:199] Controlling a Mount: stop redefining Command as mount direction; movement/steering is free and the Command maneuver (13-combat:60) grants the mount an extra move; the Survival/Nature control check stays as a separate DA-called rule, reconciled in one pass with ch13. M7 [15-equipment:116] Bedroll 'Required for a full rest in the wild... recover half HP (rounded down) from rest' -> 'long rest' vocabulary (ch19:95-103) with recovery phrased as half the long-rest recovery, rounded down. M8 [15-equipment:118] Caltrops 'halve its speed until the end of its next turn' -> Slowed X condition with a stated feet reduction per 13-combat:187. M9 [15-equipment:25-43] Weapon table: add Range values for Shortbow/Longbow (60/120 ft, per 09-talents-abilities:259) and keep the Thrown 20/60 ft convention for thrown weapons. M10 [15-equipment:97,99] Encumbrance: remove one of the surrounding forced pagebreaks so the section stops rendering alone on a page, and add one short worked example of the 10 + (Brawn x 5) slots formula. M11 [15-equipment:27-43,80-87] Bold the first column of the weapons and armor tables to match ch16's bolded armor names (16-armor-shields:31-38) and rename the armor header 'Discipline Req' to 'Discipline'. M12 [15-equipment:210-212] Trim the Cavalry Arrives callout so it no longer restates the Charge Attack tier table in prose (let the table carry the numbers); note the callout titles at ## inside callout divs are book convention per the layout-expert's verified build (TOC lists only the five top-level sections), so no heading-level change is required. (b) SUBSTANTIVE (4 dispatched implementation passes): S1 [15-equipment:239] Vehicle chase: rewrite as a bounded, resolvable one-roll opposed contest - define what counts as a success, compare numeric Speed ratings, add a tie-break and a termination bound so chases end in a fixed number of rounds instead of stalling forever (ties currently produce narration, not progress; a draft-animal wagon wins a round vs a riding horse only ~28% of the time). S2 [15-equipment:174,220-227,231,239] Vehicle Speed: put numeric speeds in the Speed column (or a defined pointer to the draft animal's rating) and document the 'Draft animal (-2)' carriage/chariot penalty, so the chase rule's 'higher Speed rating' comparison has values to compare; wagon-vs-horse (40 vs 60 ft) is a 10-point gap the single +1 rule cannot represent. S3 [15-equipment:132,148] Oil economy: cap the 1-sp flask exploit - add a per-encounter limit or capped stacking so trivial coin cannot out-damage every Basic attack card each round; land together with M2. S4 [15-equipment:74-91,95-99,238-240] Voice pass: add an opening flavor beat to Armor, a one-line register beat to Encumbrance, and a short chapter outro after the Chases tip; vary the stock sentence closing the rations and waterskin deprivation ladders [15:136,149]; keep Charge Attack as the canonical name and add a cross-reference from Brace's 'charge' trigger (09-talents-abilities:461-469). (c) DESIGN-DECISION (4 need Bruce; implement each recommended default, logged veto-revertible in decisions-pending.md): D1 [15-equipment:25-43,52 vs 08-disciplines:197] Weapon damage curve: the budget chapter promises the per-weapon curve (dagger 1/2/3 to greatsword 3/5/8) lives in Equipment, but the weapon table has no Damage column and the Melee Damage note says the weapon never changes damage; default: keep the cards-are-damage design (09-talents-abilities:239: weapon disciplines gate stronger cards, not higher damage) and correct ch08's deferral instead of adding a column. D2 [15-equipment:120,125,147] Improvised weapon curve: the 1/1/2 parentheticals match no row on the 1/2/3-to-3/5/8 ladder; default: anchor improvised weapons to Basic Unarmed at 1/1/2 + Brawn (mechanical M4) and define the rule once here. D3 [15-equipment:25-43] Weapon Cost column: 16 of 17 rows are blank while the chapter promises gp pricing; default: populate all weapon costs for parity with the fully priced gear and mount tables. D4 [15-equipment:201] Moving-mount ranged penalty: the -2 flat (reduced to odd -1 at Adept) sits outside the book's even-step ladder and Boon/Bane convention (the mounted-vs-foot +1/-1 at [15-equipment:207] are consistent with the modifier law and survive); default: Bane on ranged attacks from a moving mount at Novice, Bane dropped at Adept+ Survival/Nature, ignored at Master. Also design-flagged, for Bruce to fold into D1/D4's session: [15-equipment:178] heavy barding (+3 DR, 200 gp) makes a 600 gp warhorse effectively unkillable by light weapons - default: cap barding DR or state mounts remain valid targets of focused fire.
