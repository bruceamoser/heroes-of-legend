SHIPPED IN THE COUNCIL-PASS COMMIT (a8b496d, PR #482), verified on current main:
1. [09-talents-abilities:13,15 + 21-glossary:85] Broken: talents defined as unconditionally passive while seven of twenty-one Talent cards carry an activation (Fortune's Favor, Refuse to Fall, Miracle Worker, Chaotic Insight are Reactions; Battlefield Assessment an Action; Quick Study Free; Rally the Troops a Maneuver), and the glossary named Fortune's Favor as its activation-free example. Expected: the definition admits activated talents and names the Action field as the tell. Fix shipped: 'Most talents are passive ... a few are activated talents, and their card's Action field says so', glossary example reworded (f-005, f-006; f-001 corroborates).
2. [09-talents-abilities:41] Broken: Combat Reflexes Adept granted '+1 Initiative', a +N modifier to a roll, which the Boon/Bane law forbids outside its three named exceptions (Initiative is a 3d6 + Agility roll). Expected: a Boon. Fix shipped: 'a Boon on your Initiative roll' (f-001, f-003).
3. [09-talents-abilities:43, 09-talents-abilities:113] Broken: two passive cards failed the template's strict-superset rule at Master (Combat Reflexes dropped the Adept initiative bonus; Death Blow dropped the adept adjacent-target splash). Expected: the higher rank strictly contains the lower. Fix shipped: both Master lines now carry the lower effect forward (f-001, f-002).
4. [09-talents-abilities:301] Broken: 'Each is gated by weapon Disciplines' is false for Shield Slam (2 Armor, canon at 08-disciplines:122) and Renewal (1 Protection). Expected: the blanket claim scoped. Fix shipped: the sentence now names both exceptions (f-006).
5. [09-talents-abilities:529,531 and 09-talents-abilities:129] Broken: usage-limit drift (Renewal hid 'once per scene' inside the Action field while every other limited card states it in the body; Miracle Worker's Novice row trailed its frequency where its own Adept row led with it). Expected: one placement, body-side. Fix shipped: Renewal's limit moved into the Weak line; Miracle Worker Novice now leads with the frequency (f-005, f-002).

OPEN, MECHANICAL (chapter-local; the load-bearing remaining item):
6. [09-talents-abilities:307-311 Riposte, :335-339 Cyclone, :377-381 Shield Slam, :391-395 Parry, :447-451 Brace] Broken: five of the seven numeric-row maneuvers print a base row one or two tiers below the tier their own Discipline count sets. Tier equals rank count (08-disciplines:133, 08-disciplines:208), the budget keys damage to tier (08-disciplines:228-230), canon states 'An Adept Brimstone Burst deals 6/9/12' (08-disciplines:240) and Flat takes 'the budget row exactly as listed' (08-disciplines:246). Expected bases: Riposte 2/4/6 to 6/9/12; Parry's DR row +2/+4/+6 to +6/9/12; Brace's 'the attacker takes 2/4/6' to 6/9/12; Shield Slam 1/2/3 to 6/9/12 (Shield Slam is canonised as the two-rank Adept example at 08-disciplines:122); Cyclone 2/4/6 to 9/15/21. Dual Strike and Renewal, both one rank, are already on-row. Fix: raise the five bases; each card's existing Adept and Master lines are already correct. Two members read these lines the other way (f-001 and f-004 expected the Novice row 2/4/6 and flagged only Shield Slam); the tier-keyed reading is adopted on the canon above (see rationale).
7. [09-talents-abilities:61,63] Broken: Weapon Focus Adept and Master print '+2 damage tiers' and '+3 damage tiers', but the only sanctioned damage booster is the tier bump 'always phrased +1 damage tier'. Expected: single-step phrasing (or an explicit multi-tier grant rule). Fix: restate both lines as +1 damage tier (f-004, f-002).
8. [09-talents-abilities:17 with :59-63, :219-223, :321-329, :363-371, :433-441, :475-483, :489-497] Broken: the chapter sanctions 'Bonuses from different talents stack' and ships multiple uncapped +1-tier boosters, yet only Death Blow states a ceiling ('maximum 21'); two or more boosters on one hit leave the printed table entirely (a Master Strong 21 has no row above it). Expected: every result lands on a row. Fix: one global rule (no result rises above its card's Strong row; absolute maximum 21) applied to every tier-boost effect, the way 15-equipment:220 already caps its own charge locally (f-002).

OPEN, DESIGN JUDGMENT (veto-able defaults, none blocking a build):
9. [09-talents-abilities:307-315 and :528-539] Riposte is a usage-limit-free Reaction counterattack and only Renewal prints a limit; extra Reactions from Combat Reflexes therefore convert to repeated full-damage counters within a round. Default: leave the family limit-free and let the Reaction economy (22-reference-sheets:61, 1/round) plus the extra-Reaction talent's own DP cost govern it; if a cap is wanted, add it to Reaction maneuvers uniformly, not to one card (f-002).

OPEN, LAYOUT (verify on a fresh build):

Expected: each card holds together so a GM can read one card in one place. Note: the theme changed twice after the sampled PDF (orphaned-heading fix #84/#476, continuous folios #478), so re-verify on a clean build and, only if the tearing persists, bind each card in a keep-together block. Confine the fix to the card band; card text is not touched.

ROUTED OUT (neighbouring file, not ch09's to fix):
11. [07-skills:132] The Parry disambiguation sentence points at @sec-chapter-combat for the weapon maneuver, but the maneuver cards live in this chapter and combat itself points readers here (13-combat:81); repoint the ch07 sentence.

VERIFIED NOT a defect (do not re-open):

This is a book-wide Card Template v2 question, not a ch09 blocker.

- Rally the Troops Master's 'Frightened or Dazed' uses the sanctioned binary condition name; Dazed's leveled form appears only where a level is carried.
- Linguist (1 Lore) and Battlefield Assessment (1 Tactics) gate on real Disciplines (08-disciplines:57-58), and Shield Slam's Armor / Renewal's Protection gates are canon.
- All 42 card names are collision-free against every other chapter as card names; the only name reuse is the ch07 Parry skill, already disambiguated and adjudicated there.
