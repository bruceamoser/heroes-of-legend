# Heroes of Legend — Card Inventory v1 (workable list)

Card Template v2 (approved by Bruce, 2026-08-12): Name (type) · Disciplines · Action · Range/Area/Duration · Keywords. Body: Weak paragraph, Standard paragraph, Strong paragraph (Novice budget row), then Adept and Master additions (their budget rows). No cost field (standard cost applies, stated once in the rules). No flavor. No tables. Mechanics-only vocabulary.

Budget rows: Novice 2/4/6 · Adept 6/9/12 · Master 9/15/21 (W/S/St). Attribute-scaled: budget − 3 + keyed attribute (weapons). Keywords must be defined in the glossary: Boon, Bane, Piercing X, DR, Prone, Slowed, Restrained, Frozen, Dazed, Dazzled, Unconscious, Bane on sight, Morale check, Recurring X/round.
