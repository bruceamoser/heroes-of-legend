# W-008 — the flavour pass (2026-09-27, cycle 35)

**Walkthrough 7 of `references/architect-role.md`:** does each mechanic's printed flavour sell its
mechanic, and does any flavour contradict its own rule. It is the last of the seven walkthroughs, and
the only one whose criterion had no instrument.

**Instrument:** `scripts/flavour-pass.py` (new, in the skill). It parses `origin/main` (or `--rev`) and
screens four candidate classes, printing each with its address. `--selftest` plants one defect per
check plus a negative control and fails if any plant is missed or the control is flagged (**it does**).
Coverage guard: ch11's own printed total sentence is the oracle (75 cards), and a short parse exits 2
rather than printing a clean line.

| Check | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | does a rung carry a flavour clause at all | coverage count |
| **A2** | does a CARD carry flavour anywhere (the stat-block-soup shape) | candidate list |
| **B** | does the text name a class of thing the book never defines | candidate list |
| **C** | does a rung's duration contradict the card's own Range field | candidate list |
| **D** | retired vocabulary in card text, negation contexts exempt | candidate list |

## Class 1 — a rung keyed to a subsystem the book does not have

`11:34`, Arcane Mark (cantrip), Weak rung: *"The mark is faint. Detection spells reveal it, but the
image is blurry."* The qualifier `detection` appears **once in the whole book**, on that line. The rung
tells the reader that a mechanic is gated by a category of magic, and the category does not exist.

**Fix (landed):** name the printed spell. `11:365` *Eldritch Sight* (Novice Arcane, "Reveal 1 clue
about magic") is the book's own answer to "what reveals a hidden magical mark", so the rung now reads
*"Eldritch Sight reveals it, but the image is blurry."* No rule and no number is added; the look-up now
resolves to a card the reader can turn to.

## Class 2 — flavour that promises a mechanic the rung does not fund

`11:110`, Acid Splash (cantrip), Strong rung: *"5 acid damage. The acid clings and keeps eating."*
Nothing on the card recurs and nothing in the rung has a duration, so the clause invites a table
argument about ongoing damage at a rung whose row is fixed. The rung below already carries the
corrosion mechanic (*"Ignores 1 point of DR against objects"*), so the Strong clause now describes
corrosion on the same axis instead of a rider: *"It eats through cloth, leather, and thin metal."*
This is a flavour repair, not a rebalance: the row 1/3/5 is untouched.

**The class has a shape worth grepping for**: a persistence verb (keeps, clings, lingers, spreads,
gnaws) in a rung that prints no duration and no recurring damage. Book-wide it returns seven lines;
six of them are legal (the persistence is the printed effect, or the line prints its own duration),
and this one was the only defect.

## Class 3 — the stat-block-soup shape, card level

Three cards printed bare numbers on every rung: `Tough` (09, `+2 maximum HP.`), `Renewal` (09,
`Regain 4/6/8 HP.`), `Thread of Ruin` (11, `5/8/11 damage.`). 85 of ch09's 87 cards and 72 of ch11's 75
carry flavour, so these three are deviations from their own chapter's convention rather than a house
style. Each gained one clause in the register of its neighbours (PR #727). No numbers move.

## Read and judged NOT defects

- **The 52 (now 45) bare rungs inside otherwise-flavoured cards.** The book's convention is to put
  flavour where it carries the card and let the shorter rungs stay plain; a card with flavour on one
  rung is not soup. The card-level check (A2) is the actionable one.
- **The `Basic Attack` family** (18 rungs) prints numbers only, by law: those cards are the printed
  floor. Excluded explicitly rather than counted as defects.
- **The whole-book orphan screen's two survivors**: `08:45` ("Warding, abjuration, protective magic")
  and `11:15` ("The cold arithmetic of necromancy"). Both are atmospheric nouns in prose with no
  mechanic keyed to them; unlike the Arcane Mark line, neither asks the reader to go and find one.
  Registered as register, not defect.

## What this pass does not measure

The instrument measures whether flavour is **present** and whether it **contradicts** anything
checkable. It cannot say whether a clause is *good*, and no count here should be read as a quality
score: 45 rungs without a clause are the book's own rhythm, not 45 defects. The readable half of the
criterion stays a read, which is what this transcript is for.
