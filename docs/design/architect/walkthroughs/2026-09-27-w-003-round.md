# W-003 — the round walkthrough (combat and the action economy)

**Date:** 2026-09-27 (architect cycle 26). **Method:** `references/architect-role.md` walkthrough 3
("four heroes plus opposition, one full round. Count the rolls before anyone acts, and how often a
player has no meaningful option"). **Corpus:** `origin/main` at `71f9871`, read for every rule quoted.
**Instrument:** `scratch/w003-round.py` (real dice, seed 270927) — every roll below is a real roll, not
an illustration. Party and opposition are the book's own worked-example party and stat blocks.

## The set-up, from the printed book

- Party: the worked example's four (`13:454`): Lyra (Odd), Kael (Blade), Roric (Protector), Zara
  (Unbalanced). Where the example does not print a value (Roric's / Zara's attributes) the same-class
  pregen supplies it (`02:602` Gorma, `02:643` Corwin), marked `[B]` below.
- Opposition: Knight of the Iron Circle (C3) + two Cultists (C½) — the book's own round (`13:456`),
  budget 4, i.e. Easy-to-Standard at Novice.
- Rules used as printed: initiative `3d6 + Agility` (`13:19`); one Action, Movement, Maneuver, 1/round
  Reaction (`13:29-50`); attacks always hit and the roll reads the damage tier (`13:85`); NPC attacks
  resolve on the player's Defense roll with the Challenge penalty (`13:109`, `06:111`); DR subtracts and
  a hit lands for at least 1 (`13:95`); morale on the printed triggers (`13:306-330`).

## The round

**Before anyone acts: 7 initiative rolls** (one per combatant, `13:19`), 4 by players and 3 for creatures
whose Agility the book does not print (GAP 1). Rolled at +0 for the NPCs as a stand-in.

```
Kael 15 > Cultist A 14 > Knight 11 > Zara 10 > Lyra 10 > Cultist B 9 > Roric 8
```

| # | Turn | Action | Roll | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kael | attack the Knight | 3d6 12 +1 = 13 | Standard, Basic Melee 3 − DR 3 = **1**; Knight 9 HP; Reaction held for Riposte |
| 2 | Cultist A | Dark Bolt at Lyra | Lyra 3d6 7 +2 −1 = 8 | **Weak** defence, so the cultist's Strong 8 lands; Lyra 10 → **2 HP** |
| 3 | Knight | longsword at Zara | Zara 3d6 7 +0 −3 = 4 | **Weak** defence, Knight's Strong 11; Zara 11 → **0 HP** |
| 4 | Zara | Ember Lance at Cultist B | 3d6 15 +1 = 16 | Strong, 8 damage; Cultist B 17 → 9 HP |
| 5 | Lyra | Gust + Maneuver: Defend | 3d6 7 +1 = 8 | Weak, 4 damage; Cultist A 17 → 13 HP; Boon held on her next Defence |
| 6 | Cultist B | dagger at Lyra | Lyra 3d6 12 +1 = 13 | Standard, 6 damage − DR 0; Lyra 2 → **0 HP** |
| 7 | Roric | warhammer on the Knight | 3d6 9 +2 = 11 | Standard, 4 − DR 3 = **1**; Knight 8 HP |

**Rolls in the round: 10** (7 turns: 4 hero action rolls, 3 player Defence rolls) — plus 7 initiative =
**17 player dice rolls** for one round of a three-creature fight. At the live Standard size
(six creatures, `19:53`) the same round is 10 turns and about **13 action/Defence rolls**. Two heroes
went to 0 HP and neither had spent a Grit; the Knight had taken 2 damage.

**Null turns: 0.** Every combatant had an Action and a Maneuver available on every turn, and no turn
resolved to nothing. The only option-less state the book prints is *unconscious and stable* (`13:363`:
no further death rolls), which is the consequence of losing rather than a defect in the turn economy —
the route back is an ally's heal.

## Findings — three, in the order a table hits them

**GAP 1 — 38 of 48 stat blocks print no Attributes, and the round needs one before it starts.**
Initiative is `3d6 + Agility` (`13:19`, `03:80`, `21:181`) and the book never says who rolls for the
creatures in a game where "the DA never touches the dice. This is not a suggestion. It is the rules"
(`13:105`). Only **10 of 48** blocks carry the Attributes field the stat-block table documents
(`20:24`), so for the Knight, the Cultists and 35 others there is no Agility to add. The same missing
value is the Challenge of any opposed contest against them (`06:71`: "the target's relevant attribute
modifier, negated"), which is how Shove and Grapple resolve (`13:64-65`). **Not decided here: the
repair shape is a convention (print the field in 38 blocks, each needing authored values, or print a
default), so it is ledger row 193 with its evidence.**

**GAP 2 — an NPC's attack advantage has no roll to sit on.** The Knight's *Leadership* ("allies within
10 ft have Boon on attacks", `20:134`) and *Pack Tactics* on 3 blocks (`20:42`, `20:50`, `20:108`) plus
the Lich's *Marshal Undead* (`20:633`) and the *Marked* condition (`13:206`, `21:171`, `22:107`) all
modify an attack roll that, in this system, **nobody makes**: the attack against a hero resolves on the
hero's own Defence roll (`13:109`). Nothing in the book converts an attacker-frame modifier into the
defence frame, so a DA holding the Knight's Leadership has a Boon with no roll to apply it to.

**GAP 3 — the one site that does convert gets the sign backwards, and that is measurable.** The
Defence Roll quick reference (`13:121`) lists `[Bane], [*Cover* (half or full cover)]`, while the cover
table it sits beside says cover means "Attacks against you have Bane" (`13:433`) and "Anything between
you and incoming fire helps" (`13:434`). Because a Defence roll reads in reverse (high roll = take the
NPC's Weak damage), a Bane on *that* roll is worse for the defender. Measured over all 216 rolls against
a Cultist, hero Agility +2 (`scratch` probe, all-216 enumeration):

| reading | take Weak (best) | take Standard | take Strong (worst) |
|---|---|---|---|
| no cover | 16.2% | 67.6% | 16.2% |
| half cover as the cover table means it | 35.5% | 58.8% | 5.7% |
| **half cover as the quick reference prints it** | **5.7%** | 58.8% | **35.5%** |
| full cover as the cover table means it | 66.1% | 33.3% | 0.7% |
| full cover as the quick reference prints it | 0.7% | 33.3% | 66.1% |

Cover as printed in the quick reference **more than doubles** the chance the defender takes the
attacker's Strong damage. Against the Knight the same inversion reads 37.5% → 61.7%.

**GAP 4 — the Challenge penalty is unbounded, and the defence roll stops discriminating at the top of
the range.** The penalty is the stat block's rating imposed directly (`06:111`), while the difficulty
dial the book prints everywhere ends at "Nearly Impossible −6" (`06:226`, `07:81`, `21:235`, `22:44`)
and `06:111`'s own dragon example reads "A dragon might be −6". The bestiary runs to **Challenge 12**
(`20:443`). Enumerated over all 216 rolls for a Master hero (Agility +2, Dodge 3):

| creature | penalty | Strong defence | Standard | Weak defence (you take STRONG damage) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C3 | −3 | 25.9% | 64.8% | 9.3% |
| C6 | −6 | 4.6% | 57.9% | 37.5% |
| C7 | −7 | 1.9% | 48.1% | 50.0% |
| C10 | −10 | 0.0% | 16.2% | 83.8% |
| C12 | −12 | 0.0% | 4.6% | 95.4% |

At −12 the mechanic is switched off in the same way a collapsed DR band switches it off, which the
book already treats as a broken mechanic rather than a weak tier. **Decided as row 192** (cap at the
dial's own −6), work order to follow.
