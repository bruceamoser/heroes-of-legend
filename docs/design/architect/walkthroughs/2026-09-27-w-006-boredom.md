# W-006 - the boredom pass (dominated choices, and what the book repeats)

**Date:** 2026-09-27 (architect cycle 30). **Method:** `references/architect-role.md` walkthrough 6: "find
dominated choices: cards strictly worse than a peer at the same tier, and the most forgettable page in the book."
**Corpus:** `origin/main` at `813f7ef`, read for every card and every number quoted (208 card headings: ch09 87, ch11 75, ch12 46).
**Instrument:** `scripts/dominated-choice-census.py` (skill; 3 planted defects + 4 negative controls in `--selftest`).

## Why the screen had to be built rather than borrowed

`choice-space-sweep.py` was named in the plan as this pass's screen. It is the wrong instrument now and
it is also **broken**: it enumerates kit tag-combinations, and the kit system was deleted from the book
in `#577`/`#586`/`#626`, so it has no live target; its own `--selftest` raises `IndexError` at
`report()` because the `BRUCE_RULE` build it asserts on no longer exists in the synthetic space. A gate
whose control fails must not be quoted. The pass therefore needed a card-corpus instrument, and the
screen it produced is the deliverable that outlives this cycle.

## The sweep - 208 of 208 cards parsed

```
STRICT DOMINANCE: 3 ordered pair(s) to read
  Basic Archery  BEATS Basic Thrown   [range 120>60]
  Storm Javelin  BEATS Venom Lance    [range 60>30]
  Tremor         BEATS Static Field   [adds prone]
INTERCHANGEABLE: 1 pair(s)   Precision == Bulk
COVERAGE: 199 of 208 cards expose a machine-readable channel
```

Six parse defects were found and fixed **by watching the number move**, each one a class the skill
already records elsewhere and this instrument re-committed:

| Defect | Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `CARD_RE`'s `\s*$` ate the newline, so the field line was read as empty | every Disciplines/Action/Range/Keywords parsed as empty; the whole census ran on card KIND alone (ranks 0 for all 208) | take the first non-blank line of the block as the field line |
| Field value captured with `[^·\n]*` | a `·`-joined requirement ("2 Melee · 1 Stealth") truncated to its first Discipline, understating ranks and tier (the exact trap `tier-census.py` was repaired for) | split the line on its own `*Field:*` markers |
| `+1 damage tier` matched `N damage` | every tier-bump card read as a 1/1/1 dud | skip a match followed by " tier" |
| multi-attack cards print per-hit AND total | Dual Strike read 2/3/4 instead of 4/6/8 | prefer the parenthesised total |
| removal and precondition clauses read as imposition | "remove Frightened or Dazed" made a support talent look dominant; "Against a Blinded target" made a rider out of a precondition | three-way split (imposes / removes / keys on), plus a fourth for conditions the card puts on its own CASTER |
| keyword-only channels compared | 24 illumination-cantrip pairs, because both print `Bane` | a pair is only comparable on a substantive channel (damage, a condition, a removal) |

Two more were model limits, not bugs, and they are the reason the read matters more than the count:
**condition TIMING is not modelled** (Force Wave > Tremor was a phantom: Force Wave lands Prone only on
Strong, Tremor on Standard), and **delivery SHAPE is not the range number** (Wall of Cinders' 30-ft wall
read as "30 ft" and flagged a zone-control spell as beaten by a single-target one). Shape is now parsed;
timing is not, and every remaining pair was read.

## What the read found

**1. Genuine and decided: Tremor strictly contains Static Field.** Both are Adept Arcane spells at 2
ranks, both `Requires: focus:arcane`, both Action, both a **10-ft radius**, both on the same 5/8/11 row,
and both key off the same failure clause (`Agility or Dodge rank resists` on the Weak rung). Tremor adds
**Prone at Standard**, and its Strong reads `11 bludgeoning damage; targets are Prone and lose their
Maneuver` against Static Field's `11 lightning damage to all adjacent; targets lose their Maneuver`: a
superset on every axis, at identical cost. The only axis left is damage type, and it does not carry the
choice - measured across all 49 bestiary blocks, protections are Frightened x3, poison/charm (Undead
Nature), Magic Resistance x2, Legendary Resistance, and four vulnerabilities (Area Effects, Fire, Water,
**Bludgeoning**). Lightning is protected against nowhere; bludgeoning is, if anything, favoured. Every
one of the nine class cost tables prices Earth and Wind identically, and `10:92` puts both in the arcane
caster's list, so the two cards are bought from the same pool at the same price. A dominated card is dead
content (invariant 8), and this is the card-corpus instance of the class already fixed for the weapon
table (row 185, Crossbow, #680).

**2. Genuine and filed as its own issue: the one-roll principle is unenforced in the card corpus.**
`13:161` prints "Effects resolve by the same roll as the damage... **never a second roll**. One roll, one
tier, both outcomes. **No check.**" Ledger row 38 applied it to ch17's six item riders in August. Three
card sites in ch11 still ask the target to roll to avoid the card's own effect: **Venom Lance** (two
rungs), **Touch of the Grave** (two rungs), **Phantasmal Image** (one). Exempt after reading: Morale
clauses (a printed subsystem, `13:307`, where a *player* rolls for the creature), Ghost Sound's Reason
check (fiction-facing: is the sound magical, not whether the effect lands), and the six "Boon on the next
Fortitude check" support clauses.

**3. Not converted this cycle: Thaumaturgy's Strong rung.** Removing its Reason check leaves an at-will
cantrip that makes every Novice-tier hostile flee for a round, every round - a minion lockdown loop. The
repair needs an at-will bound (the printed cantrip scale, e.g. Dancing Lights' "one creature of your
choice"), which is a change to the card's target count. Filed separately with the fork named.

## What is NOT a defect, stated so the next pass does not re-file it

- **Basic Archery > Basic Thrown** (120 vs 60 ft): both `Requires: weapon:ranged`; the range is the
  WEAPON class's property, and weapons are flavour/possession (armament collapse). The parser cannot see
  the weapon property behind the shared string.
- **Storm Javelin > Venom Lance**: the machine sees only the 60-vs-30 range. Read, the Strong rungs
  diverge (Javelin: Bane on next 2 attacks + arcs for half; Lance: Bane + lose Maneuver) and Lance's
  Standard is gated by the second roll that finding 2 removes. After that fix neither card contains the
  other.
- **Precision == Bulk** (the one survivor of the duplicate test): a token-set comparison cannot see
  direction. Precision is "use Agility for Brawn", Bulk is "use Brawn for Agility" - mirror halves of the
  documented Substitution Family (`09`, the callout after Bulk, which names six talents). By design.
- **The 47-pair duplicate list that the first run printed** was the substitution and reroll families plus
  every pair of passive self talents: an identity test on parsed axes alone pairs any two cards that
  print no damage and no condition. The text-similarity gate (Jaccard >= 0.85 on the effect text) leaves
  exactly one pair in 208 cards: the book holds **no duplicated card text**.

## The most forgettable page

The pass's second half asked which page earns its space least. Measured on the card corpus instead of
guessed: after the duplicate gate, **zero of 208 cards restate another card's effect text**, and the
Succinct line's other component - dead content - is already at 0 printed places per unusable element. The
one card that fails the "would a player ever pick this" test is Static Field, which is this cycle's
repair: 1 of 208. The reference chapter's restatement is deliberate lookup surface, not duplication.

## Model limits to carry forward

1. **Condition timing is not modelled** (which rung carries the condition). Any future dominance screen
   must state this, because it manufactures phantoms in both directions.
2. **Damage type is a measured axis, not a parse axis.** Report it separately, as done here (49 blocks,
   0 protections against lightning, 1 vulnerability to bludgeoning), rather than assuming either way.
3. **The vocabulary bounds the finding.** This instrument was blind to "lose their Maneuver" until the
   rider list was extended; adding the rider vocabulary took the screen from 16 pairs to 3. State the
   vocabulary a count was produced under. The count is never the finding.
