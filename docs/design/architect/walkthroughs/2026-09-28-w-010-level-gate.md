# W-010 - the level walkthrough (gates 1 / 3 / 7)

**The walkthrough question** (`architect-role.md`, walkthrough 2): *for each class at each gate
(1 / 3 / 7): is there something worth buying, and can the DP actually be spent?* It was last run as
W-002 on 2026-09-26, before the level-1 4 DP grant (#674), the class pool's move to 12 DP, the HP
formula (#813) and the template attack cards (#821). Everything below is re-derived from
`origin/main`, nothing recalled.

Career DP by gate, at Knowledge 0 / Fortitude 0: **20 / 26 / 39** at levels 1 / 3 / 7, and **48** by

K+F = −4 / 0 / +4). The level-1 4 DP grant is *inside* the 12 DP class pool (`02:208`), not on top
of it - the first draft of the instrument double-booked it and printed 43 where the book says 39.

## 2. The census: can a class buy its own shelf at its own gate?

Nine classes, **90 shelf rows (36 Novice / 29 Adept / 25 Master)**, ten per class. The canon index
counts 90 class abilities independently, so the parse is calibrated from two directions.

| Class | Novice (cheapest own row) | Adept (cheapest) | Master (cheapest) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protector | 3 rows - Shield Bash 2 DP | 4 - Unshakeable 4 DP | 3 - Interpose 8 DP |
| Blade | 4 - Step Through 2 | 4 - Eviscerate 4 | 2 - Death Mark 8 |
| Arcanist | 4 - Arcane Feedback 2 | 3 - Spell Echo 4 | 3 - Overchannel 8 |
| Shepherd | 4 - Bolstering Prayer 2 | 3 - Sanctified Ground 4 | 3 - Divine Intervention 8 |
| Intellect | 4 - Field Study 2 | 3 - Leverage 4 | 3 - Encyclopedic Mind 8 |
| Odd | 4 - Improvised Solution 2 | 3 - Wrong Context 4 | 3 - Everything At Once **10** |
| Leader | 4 - Read the Room 2 | 3 - Coordinated Strike 4 | 3 - Stalwart Resolve 8 |
| Unbalanced | 4 - Exploit Chaos 2 | 3 - Volatile Surge 5 | 3 - Unmake **9** |
| Shadow | 5 - Slip Away 2 | 3 - Shadow Strike 4 | 2 - Ghost 8 |

**Verdict: no gate is dead and no DP strands.** Every class can buy an ability of its own at the
tier the gate opens, and the dearest own-shelf entry in the book (12 DP) fits the 39 DP a level-7
hero has accrued. Two shapes are worth recording and are not defects:

- **The unlock never arrives with enough DP to buy the thing it unlocks in the same breath.**
  Level 3 pays 3 DP and the cheapest Adept purchase is 4; level 7 pays 3 and the cheapest Master is
  8. The book prints carry-over as deliberate ("saving toward an 8 DP Master card is a real
  strategy", `05:773`), so a hero either banks from level 2 or waits one level. Ruling 165's spirit
  holds: the gate is a *permission*, and the DP is a plan.
- **The Odd and the Unbalanced are the only classes whose Master entry is dearer than the flat
  price** (10 and 9 DP): both must buy a fresh rank for a requirement their grants cannot cover
  ("3 any, across 3 different types" for the Odd, an Opposed elemental pair for the Unbalanced).
  They are paying their class's own cost structure, which is what the table is for.

## 3. The finding: the rank gained at levels 3, 6 and 9 has no printed price

`08:103` grants it: *"At levels 3, 6, and 9, you gain one additional Discipline rank of your choice:
a new Discipline, or a rank in one you already have."* `18:49/52/55` lists it in the **Other Gains**
column beside the free `+1 Attribute`; `18:63` counts them (*"Total Discipline ranks gained: 3"*);
`02:159` and `08:99` say rank 3 *"comes from level progression, not creation"* and *"Depth beyond
rank 2 comes from progression picks at levels 3, 6, and 9."*

**Not one sentence in the book prices the pick.** The cost table (`05:800`, four structures,
rank 1/2/3) is stated to price two things: ranks bought at creation (`02:159`) and the ranks a card's
requirement adds to its own price (`08:175`). By the book's own convention a free rank gets said out
loud - the Odd's wildcard *"costs no Development Points"* (`05:307`, mirrored at `21:71`) - so the
level pick's silence reads as a gap, not a ruling.

**Two readings, and the blast radius measured:**

| Case | FREE (the book's own reading) | PAID (the cost table governs) |
|---|---|---|
| Deepen a held Discipline to rank 3, Home rate | 0 DP | 2 + 4 = **6 DP** |
| Deepen at Opposed rate | 0 DP | 8 + 16 = **24 DP** |
| The cross-class build `08:103` invites ("Want your Blade to pick up Fire?") - three ranks at the Blade's Opposed rate | 0 DP | 4 + 8 + 16 = **28 DP, 54–64% of a minimum career** |

On the shelves themselves the two readings differ by only +0 to +3 DP per class (the paid arm's
premium on the cheapest entry), because a class's grants already cover most requirements - so
**reachability survives either reading and the cost lands on the hero's other cards.** That is why
this is a learnability defect with a price rather than a broken gate: a reader cannot tell how much
of a 44–52 DP career the level picks consume, and the answer changes what the hero can build.

**Repair, smallest lever, no number moved (issue #834):** the sentence that grants the rank states
its price. Landed in this cycle; veto-revertible, and the reversal is to price the picks instead.

## 4. A second defect, in the instrument rather than the book

`class-loadout-economy.py` - the gate that checks each class's printed *"which still leaves room for
a card"* promise - was measuring against a **hard-coded 8 DP class pool**, the pre-#674 figure. The
book now prints 12 DP at level 1 (`02:208`, mirrored at `22:250`), so the instrument had been
reporting the Shepherd as OVER for a pool that no longer exists: her 7 DP of ranks + a 2 DP card is
**9 of 12**, not 9 of 8. Repaired by parsing the pool from ch02's own sentence and checking its own
arithmetic (`5 + 4 != 12` exits 2), with the selftest now asserting the Shepherd's shape *fits* the
printed pool and that the OVER branch is still reachable. Live run: **all nine classes close; no
class misnames a 4/8/16 charge "at your Foreign rate" any more.**

A constant the book moved is how a green gate starts lying. The same rot is why the census above
parses its career figures instead of setting them.

## 5. The player's log, in the order a hero hits it

1. *How much do I have at level 1?* - Class 12 DP plus background 8 + Knowledge + Fortitude; both
   pools must be spent to zero. Answered (`02:22`, `02:117`, `02:208`).
2. *Can I always spend the last point?* - Yes: a rank-1 purchase at a Home rate costs 1 DP, so no
   pool strands on an odd remainder. Answered (`05:800`).
3. *Do my class's printed abilities cost DP, or do I just get them?* - Bought, at the flat card
   price (`05:550`). Answered.
4. *How many can I hold before running out of things I want?* - The Novice shelf alone is 36 rows,
   plus 161 damage cards. Answered by supply.
5. *What exactly does "Adept abilities unlock at Level 3" let me buy?* - Any Adept card, 4 DP
   (`02:210`). Answered.
6. *And the Discipline rank that comes with level 3 - do I pay for it?* - **Not answered. §3.**
7. *My attribute is already +2; can the level-4 increase push it to +3?* - No, "+1 (up to the +2
   maximum)" (`03:72`); the gain must go to a stat below +2, and with six attributes summing to +3
   there is always one. Answered.
8. *Do my HP grow as I level?* - No; Grit and cards carry it (`03:78`). Answered.
9. *Can I undo a bad level-1 purchase?* - One retrain per level (`18:103`). Answered.

Eight of nine answered from the book alone. The one that is not is the one this cycle repaired.

## 6. Verdict

**Playable: the level economy holds.** Nine classes × three gates, 90 own-shelf rows, all affordable
at the gate that opens them, on both readings of the level picks; the career total reconciles with
the printed range; no gate is dead. **Cohesive: one gap, now closed** - the rank the book grants is
the rank the book never priced. **Succinct: no dead content found at the gates** (the two dearest
Master entries are priced by their own class tables, not mispriced).
