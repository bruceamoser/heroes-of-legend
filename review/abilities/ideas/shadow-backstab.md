# Shadow idea: Blade in the Back

Reserved for the Shadow class. Do not use on Blade.

**Concept:** Reaction when you make a melee weapon attack against a creature that is adjacent to one of your allies or unaware of you. After the attack resolves, apply Bleeding 2 to the target.

This fits the assassin side of Shadow. It should be reviewed with Shadow's other level 1 choices so the class can also support burglar and infiltrator roles.
