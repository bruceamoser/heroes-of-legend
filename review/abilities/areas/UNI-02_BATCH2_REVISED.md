## Positive wording rule

State what the ability does. Use neutral limits when a boundary matters. Avoid
phrases such as "grants no", "does not", "cannot" and "no magic" unless the
sentence cannot be made positive without losing the rule.

## U1 Catch That!

# Catch That!

*Your hand snaps out beneath the falling bottle, catching it before it shatters on the stones.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One falling, unattended item in your square or an adjacent square, within reach, before it lands or falls out of reach.

Catch the item in a free hand. The item must be small enough to hold in that hand. The ability catches falling objects; attacks and items held by another creature follow their own rules.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Reaction / Skill

**Limits:** Purchase once; each use spends your Reaction.

**ID:** catch-that

## U2 Another Tongue

# Another Tongue

*You answer the stranger in the language of their childhood, and hear their surprise give way to recognition.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self

When you learn this ability, choose one ordinary language you do not already know. You can speak and understand it, and read and write its usual script if it has one. Record the language on your sheet. A signed language uses signing instead of speech. Choose from the campaign's ordinary languages with the DA. Secret and supernatural languages are learned through their own paths.

**Duration:** Permanent knowledge of the chosen language.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once; choose one language when learned.

**ID:** another-tongue

## U3 Message by Heart

# Message by Heart

*You repeat the message under your breath, matching every word until you can hear it without the speaker.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Time:** One minute of rehearsal.

**Target / range / shape:** One message of up to 50 words that you can hear, or read in a script you know.

After rehearsing the message for one minute, you can reproduce its words exactly from memory. You may retain unfamiliar words this way without understanding them. You can retain one message at a time; memorizing another replaces it. Recalling the stored message takes only the normal time to speak or write it. Translation and understanding come from your own knowledge or other abilities.

**Duration:** Until you memorize a replacement message.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill

**Limits:** Purchase once; one retained message at a time.

**ID:** message-by-heart

## U4 A Party Piece

# A Party Piece

*You lift your instrument, find the room's rhythm, and let the first phrase carry your argument.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, while playing your chosen instrument.

When you learn this ability, choose one ordinary instrument you own, such as a lute, flute, drum, fiddle or horn. While you play that instrument, gain 1 Boon to any Guile roll. The instrument must be in your hands and in playing condition. The Boon can support performance, persuasion, deception and other Guile approaches that the music carries. Ordinary performance remains available to everyone; a performance with no uncertain consequence needs no roll.

**Duration:** While you play the chosen instrument.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires an ordinary Musical Instrument (Bulk 1), chosen when you learn this ability; grants none.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Social / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once; choose one instrument when learned.

**ID:** a-party-piece

## U5 Quick Exit

# Quick Exit

*You turn a careful retreat into one last quick step beyond the enemy's reach.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when you use Disengage.

Gain 1 additional square of available movement this turn. Spend it using normal terrain costs and movement restrictions. The bonus applies to the Disengage movement; your Speed and converted movement allowances stay unchanged. Disengage's protection from opportunity attacks applies normally.

**Duration:** Until the end of this turn; unused movement expires.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Passive

## U7 Carry the Wounded

# Carry the Wounded

*You get a shoulder under your friend's arm and take their weight before the next arrow falls.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing creature, or one adjacent creature at 0 HP.

You can move with the creature at half your Speed. The creature moves with you and ends adjacent. You can carry them through doors, up stairs and across ordinary terrain. If you fall prone, you set them down safely in an adjacent square. One creature at a time; a second carrier or a litter can share the load.

**Duration:** While you carry them.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a free hand; grants none.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Support

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** catch-a-falling-friend

**Stable ID note:** The old title is replaced. The stable ID stays `catch-a-falling-friend` so existing links keep resolving.

## Hold a Conversation

No U number was assigned in your list. The cleaned version is included here for confirmation.

# Hold a Conversation

*You find the question that makes a stranger set aside what they were doing and listen.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Time:** One minute per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** One nonhostile creature you share a language with; one minute

Ordinary conversation remains available to everyone; this ability adds a reliable way to steer it. Choose one plausible conversation objective. Weak: 1 progress and one topic the listener welcomes. Standard: 2 progress, that topic, and one concern about your request. Strong: 3 progress, both details, and one subject the listener is reluctant to discuss.

**Duration:** Information and earned progress persist for the stated task; completed changes remain until circumstances alter them.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Social

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** hold-a-conversation
