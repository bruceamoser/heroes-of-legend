# MA1: The Mountain Walks

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

A once-per-scene charge that uses your Main Action and movement, contacts creatures and breakable barriers, and continues only if the path opens.

## Proposed final text

# The Mountain Walks

*You set your shoulder to the world and take one step that the battlefield cannot refuse.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Cost:** Your Main Action and your movement for the turn.

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one straight line up to your Speed.

You must have movement remaining. Move in a straight line. When your line reaches a square occupied by a creature, push that creature 2 squares out of your path, applying Stability. If the creature leaves the square, you can continue through it. If the creature cannot leave, it is knocked Prone and your charge stops in the last legal square before it. Apply the normal collision rules.

When your line reaches a nonmagical barrier no thicker than 1 square, the barrier breaks and leaves a passage. You can continue through the passage. The DA confirms the barrier is breakable and the line is clear enough for the charge.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Master · **Minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 6 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Charge

**Limits:** Once per scene; one line; uses your Main Action and movement for the turn.

**ID:** the-mountain-walks

## Why this is not the baseline

Force is an adjacent Brawn check for a push of 1/2/3. This is a charge that uses your Main Action and movement, contacts a creature or barrier, pushes 2 with Stability, and continues only if the path opens. It is a new movement permission.

## Power note

At 6 DP it is a Master battlefield-shaping move, once per scene, and it consumes both your Main Action and your movement. Stable creatures and crowded spaces can stop the charge. The push uses the existing Stability and collision rules.
