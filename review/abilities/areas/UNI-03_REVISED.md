# UNI-03 corrected direction: Universal Adept

**Status:** UA1 through UA6 approved 2026-10-29. The six-card Universal Adept set is complete. Replaces the baseline pass in `UNI-03.md`.

## Why the first pass failed

The first six cards mostly restated the baseline:

- The Door Was Never Locked was a Force task.
- The Unbroken was the standard Dying Grit option.
- The Impossible Escape was a standard Agility escape task.
- A Name Worth Knowing and The Web of Whispers were standard social and investigation tasks.
- The Prepared Mind called for a Reason check that does not exist.

A Universal Adept card must add a permission, exception, new effect or action-economy change. It cannot be a renamed basic action.

## UA1 The Strong Arm

**Attribute:** Brawn · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** Throw a heavy object that is normally too large to throw.

**Mechanic:** Main Action, Brawn, one heavy object you can lift with both hands and one visible target within 4 squares. Make a Brawn attack. On a hit, deal 3/4/6 physical damage and push the target 1 square or knock it Prone. The object lands in the target's square or the nearest free square and becomes difficult terrain or cover. Once per scene.

**Why this is not the baseline:** Basic Throw covers a suitable carried object. Force is a check to move, break or grapple. This is a new heavy-object attack with a terrain result.

**Power check:** Stronger than a 2-DP Novice attack; narrower than an Adept discipline because it needs a heavy object and is once per scene.

## UA2 The Last Word

**Attribute:** Fortitude · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** Take one attack or one Maneuver when you enter Dying.

**Mechanic:** Reaction, no roll, when you enter Dying. Take one attack or one Maneuver before the Dying procedure resolves. Then you are Dying, mark the wound, and resolve Dying normally. Once per scene.

**Why this is not the baseline:** The Dying procedure offers Grit or a death roll at the start of your turn. A Dying character does not normally act. This is a new last action before Dying takes hold.

**Power check:** Strong but self-only and once per scene. It does not heal you or prevent Dying.

## UA3 The Impossible Escape

**Attribute:** Agility · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** Squeeze through gaps and escape a grapple or restraint as a Reaction.

**Mechanic:** Passive: you can squeeze through a gap wide enough for your head and shoulders at half Speed. Once per scene, when an effect would Grapple or restrain you, you can escape as a Reaction before the condition takes hold and move to an adjacent free square.

**Why this is not the baseline:** There is no standard squeeze permission. Grapple escape is normally a Force Main Action. This is a new movement and Reaction permission.

**Power check:** Strong escape tool; limited to gaps the DA confirms and one Reaction per scene.

## UA4 A Name Worth Knowing

**Attribute:** Guile · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** Declare a contact who owes you a small favor.

**Mechanic:** Maneuver, no roll, once per scene, in a settlement, court, guild or crew you have visited or heard of. Name one contact. The DA introduces them and states one small favor they owe you. The contact hears you out and grants 1 Boon to your first social task with them. A large request still needs a social task, payment or proof.

**Why this is not the baseline:** A normal social task is a check. This creates a contact and a favor without a check.

**Power check:** Narrative permission plus 1 Boon; once per scene; the favor is small.

## UA5 The Web of Whispers

**Attribute:** Knowledge · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** Ask one question and get a truthful answer from your network or research.

**Mechanic:** Main Action, no roll, once per scene. Ask one question about a person, place, faction or event your network or research could plausibly know. The DA answers truthfully from what that network would know. The answer may be incomplete or point to a source. You also learn who can tell you more.

**Why this is not the baseline:** A normal investigation task is a check plus evidence. This is a direct information permission.

**Power check:** Strong information tool; limited by the network's reach and once per scene.

## UA6 The Lucky Trinket

**Attribute:** None (passive) · **Price:** 3 DP · **Minimum level:** 3

**New permission:** A personal trinket the character believes is magical grants 2 Boons once a day on one non-combat roll.

**Why this is not the baseline:** A normal non-combat roll uses standard Boons and Banes; this is a once-a-day luck resource tied to a trinket.
