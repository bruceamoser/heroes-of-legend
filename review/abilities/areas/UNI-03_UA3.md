# UA3: The Impossible Escape

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

A Maneuver and Agility roll with improved Grapple progress: 2/3/free, plus a passive squeeze through narrow gaps.

## Proposed final text

# The Impossible Escape

*You find the one loose thread in the guard's grip, the cell's frame or the rope around your wrists.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when Grappled.

Make an Agility check.

- **Weak:** Reduce your Grappled rating by 2.
- **Standard:** Reduce your Grappled rating by 3.
- **Strong:** You are free of the Grapple; your Grappled rating becomes 0.

You can squeeze through a gap wide enough for your head and shoulders at half Speed.

**Duration:** Instant; the reduction or escape lasts until the Grapple is reapplied.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Escape

**Limits:** The squeeze is passive. The Grapple reduction is a Maneuver.

**ID:** the-impossible-escape

## Why this is not the baseline

Force reduces Grappled by 1/2/3 as a Main Action using Brawn. This is a Maneuver using Agility with 2/3/free progress. The Weak and Standard results beat the standard ladder, and the Strong result ends the Grapple outright. The passive squeeze is a separate movement permission.

## Power note

At 3 DP it is a strong escape tool: cheaper action, better progress, and a Strong result that frees you completely. The cost is your Maneuver and the Agility check, and it only works while you are Grappled.
