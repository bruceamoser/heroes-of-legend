# UA1: The Strong Arm

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

A Main Action ranged attack at range 4 using weapons or improvised items that normally require two hands to throw.

## Proposed final text

# The Strong Arm

*You get both hands under the cart, the boulder or the broken beam, and send it where it needs to go.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 4 squares.

You can throw weapons or improvised items that a normal person would require two hands to throw. Make a Brawn attack.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage; the target is pushed 1 square.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage; the target is knocked Prone.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a weapon or improvised item that a normal person would require two hands to throw.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Attack

**Tags:** Universal / Attack / Skill

**Limits:** One object per attack.

**ID:** the-strong-arm

## Why this is not the baseline

Basic Throw handles a suitable carried object at range 3 with 2/3/5 damage. The Strong Arm throws weapons or improvised items that normally require two hands, reaches 4 squares, and deals 3/4/6 with a push on Standard or Prone on Strong. Force remains an adjacent Brawn check. This is a new ranged attack option.

## Power note

At 3 DP it sits above a 2-DP Novice discipline attack and below a 4-DP Adept discipline package. The two-handed object requirement is the limiter; the damage numbers are the tuning lever.
