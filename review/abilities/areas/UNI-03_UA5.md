# UA5: The Web of Whispers

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

Ask one question and get a truthful answer from your network or research, once per scene.

## Proposed final text

# The Web of Whispers

*You send word through the people who owe you a favor, the people who fear you, and the people who simply like to talk.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one question about a person, place, faction or event your network or research could plausibly know.

Ask one question. The DA answers truthfully from what that network would know. The answer may be incomplete or point to a source. You also learn who can tell you more.

**Duration:** The answer and contact remain known.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Investigation / Passive

**Limits:** Once per scene; one question.

**ID:** the-web-of-whispers

## Why this is not the baseline

A normal investigation task is a check plus evidence. This is a passive information permission: one question per scene, answered from the network's knowledge. It gives the player a reliable way to ask the world a question.

## Power note

At 3 DP it is a strong information tool. It is Passive because it has no roll and no combat action, and the once-per-scene limit is the brake. The answer may point to a source or to another contact who knows more.
