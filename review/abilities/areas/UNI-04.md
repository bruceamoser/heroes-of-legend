# UNI-04: Universal Master abilities

**Status:** Complete 2026-10-29. Three approved: One More Dawn (8 DP), The Mountain Walks (6 DP), Touched by the Divine (6 DP). The Road Unrolls was cut.

## The funnel

The Universal ladder now narrows to three Master abilities:

- Novice: 13 abilities at 1 or 2 DP.
- Adept: 6 abilities at 3 DP.
- Master: One More Dawn at 8 DP, plus The Mountain Walks and Touched by the Divine at 6 DP.

## Price note

One More Dawn is the premium 8-DP Master Universal. The two new cards are 6 DP,
one step below the 8-DP Master discipline. That gives the Master lane one premium
passive and two broad active gifts.

## Power target

A Master Universal at 6 DP must sit above the 3-DP Adept Universal cards and
below an 8-DP Master discipline. Each of the two new cards grants a new
permission or exception, once per day or once per scene. Each is a new permission.
The point is the exception, not a renamed basic action.

## The Mountain Walks

# The Mountain Walks

*You set your shoulder to the world and take one step that the battlefield cannot refuse.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one straight line up to your Speed.

Move up to your Speed in a straight line. You can move through spaces occupied by creatures and through nonmagical barriers no thicker than 1 square. Each creature whose space you enter is pushed 1 square out of your path. A barrier you pass through breaks and leaves a passage. The DA confirms the barrier is breakable and the line is clear enough for the charge.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Master · **Minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 6 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Charge

**Limits:** Once per scene; one line.

**ID:** the-mountain-walks

## Touched by the Divine

# Touched by the Divine

*You dreamed that every person in the world spoke at once. A being of light touched your forehead. When you woke, you understood every spoken language.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

You understand every spoken language you hear. Your own speech and literacy remain the languages you have learned.

**Duration:** Permanent.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Master · **Minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 6 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Language / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once.

**ID:** touched-by-the-divine
