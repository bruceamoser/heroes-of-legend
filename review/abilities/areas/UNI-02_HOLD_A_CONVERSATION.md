**Target:** One nonhostile creature you share a language with.

## Proposed final text

# Hold a Conversation

*You find the question that makes a stranger set aside what they were doing and listen.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Time:** One minute per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** One nonhostile creature you share a language with.

Ordinary conversation remains available to everyone; this ability adds a reliable way to steer it. Choose one plausible conversation objective. Make a Guile check to make progress toward the social task.

- **Weak:** 2 progress.
- **Standard:** 3 progress.
- **Strong:** 4 progress.

**Duration:** The progress persists for the social task.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Social

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** hold-a-conversation

## Why this shape

Progress only at 2/3/4. It is a social task accelerator: one roll, one minute, and a clear improvement over the standard 1/2/3 ladder. Ordinary conversation remains available to everyone. The Boon alternative would add a setup roll before the social task; this version keeps the card to one roll.

## Power note

At 2 DP it is a strong but narrow social accelerator. It takes a minute, applies to one nonhostile creature sharing a language, and only advances the chosen social task.
