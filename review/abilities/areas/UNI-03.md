# UNI-03 first pass: Universal Adept abilities

**Status:** First-pass proposal. 0 of 6 approved. No book or ledger changes.

## The funnel

The Universal ladder is deliberately narrowing:

- Novice: 13 abilities, 1 or 2 DP.
- Adept: 6 abilities, 3 DP, minimum level 3.
- Master: fewer still, 8 DP, minimum level 7.

Six Adept abilities, one per attribute, give every character concept a natural
choice without turning the tier into a second discipline list. The price is 3 DP,
one step above a Novice discipline and one below an Adept discipline.

## Power target

An Adept Universal must sit between two real benchmarks:

- It must be clearly stronger than a 2-DP Novice discipline ability such as
  Braced Harness (Stability 1/2/3 for one forced movement) or A Verse for
  Courage (1 Boon to one ally's next roll).
- It must stay below a 4-DP Adept discipline ability such as A Line for Everyone
  (a roped route plus 1 Boon for up to six companions), A Lock Has a Language
  (1/2/3 lock progress plus information and relocking), or A Ribcage for a
  Shield (1 Boon to an ally's defense plus a push on a Strong defense).

The six cards below use once-per-scene or one-task limits to stay under the
Adept discipline ceiling. They do not require a discipline rank.

## The six cards

| Attribute | Card | Fantasy | Core mechanic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brawn | The Door Was Never Locked | The strong hero makes a path or a wall. | 1/2/3 progress to break, lift, shift or brace one object. |
| Fortitude | The Unbroken | The hero refuses the final blow. | Reaction when entering Dying; spend 1 Grit for the existing full-HP recovery. |
| Agility | The Impossible Escape | The escape artist slips the cell, rope or grip. | 1/2/3 escape progress; finish with 2 squares without provoking. |
| Guile | A Name Worth Knowing | Reputation opens doors. | 1/2/3 social access progress with one circle and one request. |
| Knowledge | The Web of Whispers | The spymaster sends a question through the network. | 1/2/3 investigation progress; finish with a fact and a contact. |
| Reason | The Prepared Mind | The planner already has the piece. | Once per scene, one Reason check for a Boon, a common item and a revealed fact. |

## The Door Was Never Locked

# The Door Was Never Locked

*You set your shoulder, find the weak point in the frame, and make the door remember it was only wood.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Time:** One minute per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent door, barrier, heavy object or structural support.

Make a Brawn check to make progress toward a physical task: break, lift, shift or brace the object. The DA sets the task's progress target and states what failure costs.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

When the task is complete, choose one: the object breaks or opens; it becomes a barrier that grants cover; or it moves into an adjacent square. The DA applies the normal cover, movement and collapse rules.

**Duration:** The change lasts until the object is repaired, moved or destroyed.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Environment

**Limits:** Once per scene; one object per use. Each object is a separate task.

**ID:** the-door-was-never-locked

## The Unbroken

# The Unbroken

*The blow lands, the world goes white, and you decide that this is not where your story ends.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when you enter Dying.

Spend 1 Grit to regain full HP immediately, using the same Grit option the Dying procedure offers at the start of your turn. You still mark the wound. Once per scene.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Recovery

**Tags:** Universal / Grit / Recovery

**Limits:** Once per scene; spend 1 Grit.

**ID:** the-unbroken

## The Impossible Escape

# The Impossible Escape

*You find the one loose thread in the guard's grip, the cell's frame or the rope around your wrists.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Time:** One minute per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when Grappled, restrained or confined.

Make an Agility check to make progress toward an escape task. The DA sets the task's progress target and the consequence of failure.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

When the task is complete, you slip free and can move up to 2 squares without provoking opportunity attacks. A restraint you escape remains unlocked, broken or loose.

**Duration:** Instant; the escape is permanent.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Escape

**Limits:** Once per scene; each restraint or confinement is a separate task.

**ID:** the-impossible-escape

## A Name Worth Knowing

# A Name Worth Knowing

*You speak your name, or the name of the deed they have already heard, and watch the door open a little wider.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Time:** One hour per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** One settlement, court, guild or similar social circle you can reach.

Ordinary social action remains available; this ability is for doors that need a name. Make a Guile check to make progress toward a social access task: gain an audience, shelter, a loan, an introduction or safe passage. The DA sets the target from the circle's suspicion and your reputation.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

When the task is complete, you gain the access you sought, and one person in the circle is willing to speak with you again. A hostile court may demand a favor, proof or a price.

**Duration:** The access lasts for the current visit or as the fiction allows.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Social

**Limits:** Once per scene; one circle and one request per use.

**ID:** a-name-worth-knowing

## The Web of Whispers

# The Web of Whispers

*You send word through the people who owe you a favor, the people who fear you, and the people who simply like to talk.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** One hour per attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** A settlement, road, guild or network of contacts you can reach.

Make a Knowledge check to make progress toward an investigation task: send a question through your contacts and gather information. The DA sets the target from the network's reach and the secret's rarity.

- **Weak:** 1 progress.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress.

When the task is complete, you learn one useful fact and the name or location of one contact who can take the matter further. The answer is only as good as the network; a remote village may know nothing of a royal secret.

**Duration:** The information and contact remain known.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Investigation

**Limits:** Once per scene; one question and one network per use.

**ID:** the-web-of-whispers

## The Prepared Mind

# The Prepared Mind

*You have been ready for this since the road turned, and now the piece you needed is already in your hand.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Once per scene, declare one plausible preparation you made earlier: a map, a tool, a disguise, a route, a contingency or a warning. Make a Reason check.

- **Weak:** Gain 1 Boon to one roll related to the preparation.
- **Standard:** Also produce one common mundane item that fits the preparation.
- **Strong:** Also learn one fact your preparation would have revealed.

The DA approves the preparation. It must fit your character, the scene and what you could have carried. The item remains after the scene; the Boon is used on one roll.

**Duration:** The item and information persist; the Boon ends when used.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None; the item comes from your preparation.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Preparation

**Limits:** Once per scene.

**ID:** the-prepared-mind
