# ANC-03 — Halfling ancestry review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1287](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1287)  
**Branch:** `codex/1287-ancestry-halfling`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/12_Ancestry_Halfling.xlsx`  
**Scope:** five Halfling traits. Pairs are not separately reviewed.

## Trait decisions

| # | Trait | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bigger on the Inside | Approved | Keep the Frightened Boon and add +1 maximum HP. It is now the Halfling durability trait. |
| 2 | Under Their Noses | Approved | +1 Boon to hiding; may hide behind an adjacent standing creature at least one size larger, even in combat and without other cover. Positive wording only. |
| 3 | Quick Tongue | Approved | 1 Boon to Guile to negotiate or argue; 2 Boons to Guile when defending your own or an ally’s actions. Replaced Quiet as a Burrow. |
| 4 | A Little Luck | Approved | Once per day, before an attribute roll, gain 3 Boons. Intentionally impactful at the system cap. |
| 5 | Endless Pockets | Approved | Your Bulk capacity increases by 1. Replaced Second Breakfast. |

## Change record

- `CHG-HAL-004` — Rename Quiet as a Burrow to Quick Tongue.

- `CHG-HAL-006` — Replace the quiet-movement rules with the Quick Tongue Guile benefits.
- `CHG-HAL-007` — Increase A Little Luck from 1 Boon to 3 Boons once per day; remove the negative no-reroll/inventory sentence.
- `CHG-HAL-008` — Rename Second Breakfast to Endless Pockets.
- `CHG-HAL-009` — Rewrite the narrative for Endless Pockets.
- `CHG-HAL-010` — Replace the inventory-check rules with +1 Bulk capacity.

## Notes

The trait keeps `trait-halfling-stout-heart`. Halfling now has an HP trait; it still has no Speed trait. Halfling is Size Small; the Foundry token footprint is 0.5 × 0.5. Endless Pockets is a +1 Bulk capacity trait.
