# MA3: The Road Unrolls

**Status:** Cut 2026-10-29. Not part of the approved set.

## Current concept

Once a day, name a destination; learn the safest route and one danger, and travel it in half the normal time.

## Proposed final text

# The Road Unrolls

*You set the map down, trace the line the land has been keeping for you, and find the way through.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One destination you have heard of; the party.

Once a day, name a destination you have heard of. The DA reveals the safest route and one danger along it. The party travels that route in half the normal time and gains 1 Boon to navigation tasks for the journey. The route must be plausible; a sealed realm or an unknown sea requires its own way through.

**Duration:** The journey.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Master · **Minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 6 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Exploration / Passive

**Limits:** Once a day; one destination.

**ID:** the-road-unrolls

## Why this is not the baseline

Navigation tasks normally require checks and time. This is a passive once-a-day travel permission: a safe route, one danger, half the travel time, and 1 Boon to navigation tasks. The player names the destination; the DA reveals the route.

## Power note

At 6 DP it is a campaign-scale travel ability. The route must be plausible; a sealed realm or an unknown sea requires its own way through. Once a day, one destination.
