# ANC-04 — Human ancestry review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1288](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1288)  
**Branch:** `codex/1288-ancestry-human`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/13_Ancestry_Human.xlsx`  
**Scope:** five Human traits. Pairs are not separately reviewed.

## Trait decisions

| # | Trait | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Never Quite Finished | Approved | Maximum HP +1 and 1 Boon to Fortitude rolls to resist death marks. Differentiates Human from Dwarf Hard as Bedrock. |
| 2 | Adaptable | Approved | Gain 1 additional DP at each level, including level 0. Replaced Road in the Blood. |
| 3 | A Foot in Two Worlds | Approved | Choose another ancestry; learn one native language and gain one trait from its list as this trait’s benefit. Keep Human base HP/Speed. |
| 4 | Ingenuity | Approved | Once per scene, assemble a temporary tool, implement or weapon from nearby materials; 1 Boon to the next roll using it; lasts the scene. Replaced Make Room at the Fire. |
| 5 | The Impossible Streak | Approved | Once per day, describe the difficult way you are taking on an ordinary noncombat roll; gain 3 Boons. |
