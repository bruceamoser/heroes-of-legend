# CLS-03: Intellect class signature review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1291](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1291)  
**Branch:** `codex/1291-class-intellect`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/22_Class_Intellect.xlsx`  
**Scope:** nine signatures, three at levels 1, 3 and 7.

## Signature decisions

| Level | Signature | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spot the Leverage | Approved | Tactical read on a visible creature or obstacle within 5; Intellect and nearby allies gain 1 Boon to their next action against it. One application per target per scene. |
| 1 | Make the Connection | Approved | One minute reviewing everything learned about one case, person, place or problem. The DA reveals one missed detail grounded in established facts. |
| 1 | Angle of Attack | Approved | Main Action Knowledge-based melee weapon attack for 3/5/7 physical damage. Requires a melee weapon. |
| 3 | A Better Angle | Approved | Maneuver: willing ally within 4 moves 3 without provoking, then makes one Basic Melee or Basic Ranged attack as part of the Maneuver. The ally action remains available. |
| 3 | Diagnose the Failure | Approved | Reaction when an ally within 5 has a Weak attack or skill result: the ally rerolls with 1 Boon. Once per round; uses the Intellect Reaction. |
| 3 | Predict the Motion | Approved | Passive: use Knowledge instead of Brawn or Agility for defense against all physical attacks. |
| 7 | A Route for Everyone | Approved | Self and up to three allies move 3 squares without provoking in an order you choose; each gains 1 Boon to its next defense. |
| 7 | Shatter the Guard | Approved | Main Action Knowledge attack for 5/7/9 physical damage. Target loses 1 resistance for the scene; one ally gains 1 Boon against it. |
| 7 | Test the Hypothesis | Approved | One minute: self and up to three allies form a plan. The DA confirms truth, uncertainty and one risk; each participant gains 2 Boons to a roll following the plan. Once per scene. |
