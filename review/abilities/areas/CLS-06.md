## Signature decisions

| Level | Signature | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the Gap | Approved | Maneuver. Block a three-square frontage until the start of your next turn; enemies cannot move past it. |
| 1 | Step Between | Approved | Reaction. Swap squares with an adjacent ally and become the target of the single-target attack. |
| 1 | Hand on the Collar | Approved | Maneuver. Pull an ally up to 2 squares toward you; if it was adjacent, move into its square. |
| 3 | Break the Charge | Approved | Reaction, Brawn. Trigger: enemy moves within 2 squares toward an ally. 2 / 2 + Prone / 4 + Prone + Dazed 1. |
| 3 | Over My Body | Approved | Reaction. Take a single-target attack that would drop an ally within 3 squares; remain at 1 HP if it would drop you. |
| 3 | Make a Refuge | Approved | Maneuver. Allies within 5 squares gain 1 Boon to defense and Stability 1; each may move 1 square toward you. |
| 7 | The Long Road | Approved | Main Action. Allies within 5 squares move their Speed without provoking; carry one Dying ally. |
| 7 | Take the Wound | Approved | Reaction. Take an ally’s wound and roll the wound table with 1 Boon. |
| 7 | Radiant Threshold | Approved | Main Action, Reason. 3/5/7-square radiant line; 1/2/3 radiant damage to creatures in or crossing it. |
