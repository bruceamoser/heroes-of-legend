# UNI-02: Universal Novice abilities

**Status:** In review, 1 of 13 decided.
**Scope:** The 13 no-discipline Novice abilities.
**Purpose:** Establish the Universal baseline before designing Adept and Master options.
**Order:** Six 1-DP exceptions first, then the seven 2-DP Novice abilities.

## Baseline questions

- A 1-DP Universal should be small, specific and cinematic. It should not be a
  generic bonus that every character wants.
- A 2-DP Universal is the workhorse tier. It may be passive or rolled, but it
  should open a clear kind of competence rather than add a bigger number.
- Universal abilities must remain below discipline and signature spotlight power.
- The format should be functional Type plus Tags, with `Tier: Novice` and a
  separate `Minimum level: 1`.

[Card conventions](../card-conventions.md) · [Catalog](../catalog.md)

### Why

The mechanics already meet the small-Universal baseline: a specific trigger, a
cinematic moment, no roll, one purchase, and a real cost in the Reaction. The
change is nomenclature and taxonomy only. It also tests whether a 1-DP Universal
should stay this narrow, or whether it needs a second mode such as deflecting an
item too large to catch.

### Conditioned Body decision

Current: Maximum HP +1. At most one purchase at each level; unused purchases
cannot be banked. This is a level-by-level HP tax and the main reason every
character would end up with the same Universal package.

- **Option A, recommended:** Maximum HP +2. Purchase once. No attribute increase.
- **Option B:** Maximum HP +1. Purchase at most once at Novice, Adept and Master;
  maximum +3. No attribute increase.
- **Option C:** Keep the current repeatable +1 per level.
