# UA6: The Lucky Trinket

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29. 2 Boons to the player confirmed.

## Current concept

A personal trinket the character believes is magical. Once a day, it gives 2 Boons on one non-combat roll.

## Proposed final text

# The Lucky Trinket

*You turn the coin over in your pocket, or touch the smooth stone at your throat, and believe, as you always have, that it brings you luck.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

You carry a personal trinket you believe is magical. It may or may not be. Once a day, when you make a non-combat roll, you can use the trinket to gain 2 Boons on that roll.

**Duration:** The trinket remains; the 2 Boons apply to one non-combat roll.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a personal trinket.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Luck / Passive

**Limits:** Once a day; one non-combat roll.

**ID:** the-lucky-trinket

## Why this is not the baseline

A normal non-combat roll uses the standard Boon and Bane rules. This card gives a once-a-day pool of 2 Boons from a personal trinket. It is a new resource with a story hook, not a renamed skill check.

## Power note

At 3 DP it is a strong non-combat luck ability. The once-a-day limit and the non-combat restriction are the brakes. The trinket is the character hook, and the belief is the flavor.
