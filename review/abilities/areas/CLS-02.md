# CLS-02: Blade class signature review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1290](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1290)  
**Branch:** `codex/1290-class-blade`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/21_Class_Blade.xlsx`  
**Scope:** nine signatures, three at levels 1, 3 and 7.

This review was reset to baseline after the backstab concept was identified as a Shadow ability. The Blade is being reviewed from signature 1 again.

## Signature decisions

| Level | Signature | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reckless Charge | Approved | Maneuver: move 2 squares toward an enemy, make a Basic Melee attack as part of the Maneuver with +1/+2/+3 damage by result, take 1 Bane on defense until next turn. Main Action remains. |
| 1 | Measured Guard | Approved | Reaction: 1 Boon to defense; on a Strong defense, make a Basic Melee attack against the attacker if within reach. The counterattack is part of the Reaction and does not use the Main Action. |
| 1 | Cleave | Approved | Main Action Brawn attack against all adjacent creatures, including allies. Weak 1 damage; Standard 1 damage and push 1; Strong 1 damage and push 2. |
| 3 | Not So Fast | Approved | Reaction when an enemy voluntarily enters reach: make a Basic Melee attack, then its movement ends and it cannot move until the start of its next turn. |
| 3 | Return Stroke | Approved | Reaction after a Standard or Strong defense: one free Basic Melee attack against the attacker. Requires a melee weapon and the ability to attack. |
| 3 | Twitch Reflexes | Approved | Passive: take one additional Reaction each round. |
| 7 | Dance Through Steel | Approved | Main Action, Agility: one roll against up to three adjacent enemies; Weak 4 damage, Standard 4 damage and move 1, Strong 6 damage and move 2. |
| 7 | Get Out of Here | Approved | Main Action Brawn throw against an adjacent creature your size or smaller. Weak 3 squares; Standard 5 squares and Dazed 1; Strong 7 squares and Dazed 2. Any direction, lands prone. |
| 7 | Relentless Pursuit | Approved | Reaction when a visible enemy within 2 begins voluntary movement. Chase up to Speed without provoking; if adjacent, free Basic Melee with 1 Boon and Slowed 1/2/3 by result. |
