# ANC-02 — Elf ancestry review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1286](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1286)  
**Branch:** `codex/1286-ancestry-elf`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/11_Ancestry_Elf.xlsx`  
**Scope:** five Elf traits. Pairs are not separately reviewed.

## Trait decisions

| # | Trait | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Footfall on the Leaves | Approved | In woodlands/forests, explicitly ignore difficult terrain and gain +1 Speed. Solid obstacles, occupied squares and magical difficult terrain still apply. |
| 2 | A Lifetime Ago | Approved | Once per day, call on ancestral knowledge for 1 Boon to a Knowledge or Reason roll. Name unchanged. |
| 3 | Nature’s Whispers | Approved | Gain 1 Boon to a roll interpreting living signs. No terrain or disturbance qualifier. Renamed from The Wood Whispers. |
| 4 | Grace of the Elder Folk | Approved | Keep as written: Base Speed +1. No rider. |
| 5 | Dream beneath the Stars | Approved | Rest in waking reverie; aware of ordinary sights/sounds but not Hidden/subtle threats; counts as sleep for rest/recovery, no extra downtime/treatment/Grit. |

## Change record

- `CHG-ELF-001` — Broaden Footfall on the Leaves to natural woodland difficult terrain and +1 Speed while in wooded terrain.
- `CHG-ELF-002` — Replace place/community memory with a once-per-day ancestral knowledge Boon.
- `CHG-ELF-003` — Rewrite the narrative around ancestral memory.
- `CHG-ELF-004` — Rename The Wood Whispers to Nature’s Whispers.
- `CHG-ELF-005` — Simplify the rules to 1 Boon on a roll interpreting living signs.
- `CHG-ELF-006` — Clarify Dream beneath the Stars and remove the Magical Asleep sentence.
- `CHG-ELF-007` / `CHG-ELF-008` — Positive-wording pass on Footfall on the Leaves and Dream beneath the Stars.

## Notes

The trait keeps `trait-elf-light-tread`. It is intentionally strong inside woodland and does nothing outside it.
