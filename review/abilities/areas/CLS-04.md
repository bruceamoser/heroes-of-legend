# CLS-04: Leader class signature review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1292](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1292)  
**Branch:** `codex/1292-class-leader`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/23_Class_Leader.xlsx`  
**Scope:** nine signatures, three at levels 1, 3 and 7.

## Signature decisions

| Level | Signature | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Follow My Signal | Approved | Keep the two-recipient Aid. One two-recipient Aid on your turn; each ally within 3 and able to see or hear the signal. |
| 1 | Lead the Way | Approved | Maneuver: when you move using Speed, willing allies within 3 follow along your path up to the distance moved, maximum 2 squares. Allies who come within 3 can join for the remainder. No movement cost; normal opportunity attacks. |
| 1 | Stand Together | Approved | Self and one adjacent willing ally each gain 2 Boons to defense while adjacent until the start of your next turn. |
| 3 | Blow the Horn | Approved | Maneuver: one Guile roll against every enemy within 5 that can hear. Weak Frightened 1; Standard Frightened 1 and Dazed 1; Strong Frightened 2 and Dazed 1. |
| 3 | Lead by Example | Approved | Main Action Guile melee attack for 2/4/6 physical damage. Standard chooses one effect, Strong chooses two: Dazed 1, ally move 1, or ally attack Boon. |
| 3 | Press the Opening | Approved | Maneuver: one willing adjacent ally makes one free Basic Melee attack; on Strong, push the target 1. Once per turn. |
| 7 | Coordinated Rush | Approved | Main Action: two allies move 3 without provoking, then each makes one free Basic Melee; push 1 on Strong. |
| 7 | Refuse the Rout | Approved | Self and up to three allies reduce one Frightened or Dazed condition by 2, gain a defense Boon, and move 1 without provoking. |
| 7 | Watch the Rear | Approved | Reaction: 2 Boons to an ally defense; after the attack, the ally may move up to 3 squares without provoking. |
