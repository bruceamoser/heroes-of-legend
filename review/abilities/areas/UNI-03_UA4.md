# UA4: A Name Worth Knowing

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

Invent a contact in the current location or situation, define the mutual favor, and gain 2 Boons in social interactions with them. Once per location or situation.

## Proposed final text

# A Name Worth Knowing

*You speak your name, or the name of the deed they have already heard, and watch the door open a little wider.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; the current location or situation.

Invent one contact you know that may be here. You and the DA define the contact and the small favor they owe you or you owe them, such as a meal, a bed, an introduction, a rumor, a place to lie low, or a small loan. You have 2 Boons in your social interactions with them.

**Duration:** The contact and favor persist until used or the fiction changes.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Adept · **Minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Social

**Limits:** Once per location or situation; one contact.

**ID:** a-name-worth-knowing

## Why this is not the baseline

A normal social task is a check. This card lets the player invent a contact, define the mutual favor, and gain 2 Boons in social interactions with them. The contact is a new narrative permission, and the player has authorial control over who knows their name.

## Power note

At 3 DP it is a strong social utility. The 2 Boons apply to social interactions with that contact, and the ability is limited to once per location or situation. The mutual favor keeps the contact grounded in the fiction.
