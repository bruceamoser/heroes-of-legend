# CLS-05: Odd class signature review

**Status:** Approved (9 of 9)  
**Book issue:** [#1293](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1293)  
**Branch:** `codex/1293-class-odd`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/24_Class_Odd.xlsx`  
**Scope:** nine signatures, three at levels 1, 3 and 7.

## Signature decisions

| Level | Signature | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pocket Sand | Approved | Reaction: enemy within 2 that can see you; 1 Bane to its attack or skill roll, then Blinded 1 until the end of its next turn. Once per scene. |
| 1 | Use Their Voice | Approved | Maneuver: spirit calls in the target’s own voice; ally moves 2 without provoking and gains a Boon against the target. |
| 1 | Borrowed Tongue | Approved | Maneuver in combat or one minute out of combat: a spirit grants understanding and speech of one heard language for the scene. |
| 3 | Foul Wind | Approved | Main Action Fortitude. Allies may fall prone; non-prone creatures within 5 take 1/2/4 poison. |
| 3 | Out of Body | Approved | Main Action projection. Body prone with 3 defense Banes; spirit Speed 5, passes through nonmagical solids, magic damages body; Snap Back or Manifest. No once-per-scene limit. |
| 3 | A Door Where There Wasn’t | Approved | Maneuver. Temporary door-sized passage in a nonmagical barrier, max one square thick; only the Odd and carried gear pass; once per scene. |
| 7 | Borrowed Life | Approved | Main Action, no roll. Once per scene, invite a spirit and choose one skill, tool or weapon; gain 3 Boons with it for the scene. The spirit speaks through you and colors your mannerisms; you remain in control. |
| 7 | The Unseen Procession | Approved | Main Action, no roll. Spectral figures fill the area: you and each ally within 5 gain 1 Boon to the next defense and may move 1 square without provoking; each enemy within 5 gains 1 Bane to its next attack. Until end of round; once per scene. |
| 7 | Not Quite Yet | Approved | Reaction, once per day per character. When an ally within 5 would enter Last Words, delay onset to the beginning of its next turn. It makes no death rolls during the delay; if it still has three death marks then, Last Words begin. |

## Correction

Row 9 was initially closed out before a decision. Bruce approved it on
2026-10-27 with one amendment: the delay ends at the beginning of the ally's
next turn, not the end.
