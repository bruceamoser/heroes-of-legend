## Conditioned Body

### Current mechanics and Option C

- **Action:** Passive. **Attribute:** None (no roll). **Target:** Self.
- **Effect:** Maximum HP +1 per purchase.
- **Frequency:** At most one purchase at each character level. Missed purchases
  cannot be banked.
- **Cost:** 2 DP per purchase.
- **Duration:** Permanent maximum HP increase for each permitted purchase.
- **No attribute increase.**
- **Current limits text:** At most one purchase per level; unused purchases cannot
  be saved.

**Exact math:** if the character buys it at every level, at level L the maximum
is +L HP for 2L DP. At level 3 that is +3 HP for 6 DP. At level 7 it is +7 HP for
14 DP. At level 10 it is +10 HP for 20 DP. If the campaign has no fixed level cap,
the cap is the campaign's cap. The card itself does not name a level cap.

**Table judge:** this is repeatable, but only once per level. It is cheap enough
that every character can buy it every level, which is why it tends to flatten the
Universal pool.

### Option A

- **Effect:** Maximum HP +2.
- **Frequency:** Purchase once. No further purchases.
- **Cost:** 2 DP.
- **Duration:** Permanent.
- **No attribute increase.**

**Exact math:** +2 maximum HP for 2 DP at any level. Maximum +2 forever.

### Option B

- **Effect:** Maximum HP +1 at Novice, +1 at Adept, +1 at Master; maximum +3.
- **Frequency:** At most one purchase at each tier. No purchases at other levels.
  Missed purchases cannot be banked.
- **Cost:** 2 DP per purchase, 6 DP total for the maximum.
- **Duration:** Permanent for each permitted purchase.
- **No attribute increase.**

**Exact math:** +1 at level 1, +2 at level 3, +3 at level 7; maximum +3 HP for
6 DP. It is a bounded tier progression rather than a level-by-level tax.
