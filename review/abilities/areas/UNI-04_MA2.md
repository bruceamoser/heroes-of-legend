# MA2: Touched by the Divine

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29.

## Current concept

A passive, permanent gift: you understand every spoken language you hear.

## Proposed final text

# Touched by the Divine

*You dreamed that every person in the world spoke at once. A being of light touched your forehead. When you woke, you understood every spoken language.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

You understand every spoken language you hear. Your own speech and literacy remain the languages you have learned.

**Duration:** Permanent.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Master · **Minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 6 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Language / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once.

**ID:** touched-by-the-divine

## Why this is not the baseline

Languages are normally learned one at a time. This is a passive, permanent understanding of every spoken language you hear. Your own speech and literacy remain the languages you have learned, so the gift opens understanding without replacing the language system.

## Power note

At 6 DP it is a broad utility gift. It is understanding; speaking a language still comes from the languages you know. The gift is permanent and requires no roll.
