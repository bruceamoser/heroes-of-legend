# ANC-01 — Dwarf ancestry review

**Status:** In review  
**Book issue:** [#1285](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1285)  
**Branch:** `codex/1285-ancestry-dwarf`  
**Workbook:** `review/abilities/workbooks/10_Ancestry_Dwarf.xlsx`  
**Scope:** five Dwarf traits and all ten trait pairs.

## Trait decisions

| # | Trait | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hard as Bedrock | Approved | Keep +1 maximum HP; rename from Stubborn as Bedrock. Mechanic otherwise unchanged. |
| 2 | Stone Mason | Approved | Broadened to general stone inspection, stone crafting and structural weakness; renamed from The Mountain Remembers. Stable ID unchanged. |
| 3 | Rooted in Stone | Approved | Stability +1 and a blanket 1 Boon to Guile rolls. The combination is deliberately strong. |
| 4 | An Honest Maker’s Hand | Approved | Once per day, improve a craft/repair result one step (Weak → Standard → Strong). Narrative rewritten around craftsmanship. |
| 5 | A Grudge Well Kept | Approved | First creature to damage you each combat becomes a permanent grudge; 1 Boon to attack rolls against every marked creature. No cap; repeat damage does not stack. |

## Trait pairs

Not separately reviewed. If the traits are well designed, the pairs work; the Pairs sheet remains reference guidance.

## Change record

- `CHG-ANC-001` — Rename `Stubborn as Bedrock` to `Hard as Bedrock`; keep `Maximum HP +1`.
- `CHG-ANC-002` — Rename `The Mountain Remembers` to `Stone Mason`; stable ID unchanged.
- `CHG-ANC-003` — Broaden the rules to stone inspection, stone crafting, traps/passages, stone type/patina and structural weakness.
- `CHG-ANC-004` — Replace the forced-movement Boon with Stability +1 and add a blanket 1 Boon to Guile rolls.
- `CHG-ANC-005` — Rewrite the narrative around craftsmanship.
- `CHG-ANC-006` — Replace inspection with a once-per-day craft/repair result bump.
- `CHG-ANC-007` — Replace the once-per-combat retaliation with permanent, stackable-across-creatures grudges.
- `CHG-ANC-008` / `CHG-ANC-009` / `CHG-ANC-010` — Positive-wording pass on Hard as Bedrock, Stone Mason and A Grudge Well Kept.

## Notes

Trait 1 keeps the stable ID `trait-dwarf-deep-constitution`; no mechanics changed. Trait 2 keeps `trait-dwarf-stone-reader`; the rules now cover general stone expertise and structural weakness, but still do not reveal an untouched wall’s far side. Trait 3 keeps `trait-dwarf-low-center`; Stability +1 is passive, and the blanket Guile Boon is the deliberate offset. Trait 4 keeps `trait-dwarf-maker-s-eye`; it now owns general crafting while Stone Mason owns stone. Trait 5 keeps `trait-dwarf-carved-history`; grudges persist across combats and there is no cap, but a creature is only marked once.
