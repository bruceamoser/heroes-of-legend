# Conditioned Body

**Status:** Approved 2026-10-29. Option C.

## Current card

Passive. Maximum HP +1. At most one purchase at each level; missed purchases cannot be banked. No attribute increase. Tier 1, DP 2, no requirement.

## Chosen mechanic

Option C. Maximum HP +1 per purchase. One purchase at each character level. 2 DP per purchase. Each level's purchase stands on its own. Playtesting may show whether the level-by-level cost is too flat.

## Proposed final text

# Conditioned Body

*You remember the work that built this strength: another load, another climb, another day you kept going.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Maximum HP +1 per purchase. Purchase once at each character level. Each level's purchase stands on its own. This ability increases maximum HP only.

**Duration:** Permanent maximum HP increase for each permitted purchase.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier:** Novice · **Minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Training

**Tags:** Universal / Training / Passive

**Limits:** At most one purchase per level; each level's purchase stands on its own.

**ID:** conditioned-body

## Why this is the right starting point

C keeps the current once-per-level mechanic. At level L it gives +L maximum HP for 2L DP. The card prints no level cap; the campaign's cap is the limit. Playtesting is the check on whether the level-by-level tax is too flat.
