# Recovery, scars and treatment

**Agreed foundation retained from the design conversation, not old ability text.**

HP returns to full after a safe breath. Grit and wounds do not reset. Each entry into
Dying causes a wound, including damage that returns a stabilized character to Dying.
At the start of a Dying character’s turn, choose Grit or the death roll; that choice
stands for the turn. Stabilization is a universal Maneuver. Ordinary healing can
return a living character to activity but cannot remove their scar.

Wound roll: 3d6 plus one die per prior wound, capped at the approved working limit
of five dice; retain the highest three, sort ascending, read as a three-digit result.
Only 666 reaches the 600 band. It gives Last Words: two rounds of speech only,
then final death. Exceptional reversal abilities are not supplied casually here.

Physical, Elemental, Mental, Radiant and Life/Necrotic wound tables have already
been authored and reviewed. They are preserved as [the approved wound reference](../../playtest/wounds.md),
not replaced by new wounds for this catalog. The next editorial pass can move that
reviewed content intact after its treatment labels are reconciled.

Treatments require the correct capability. Each wound has a recovery value and
states exactly which penalties completing treatment relieves. Weak/Standard/Strong
adds 1/2/3 recovery, at most one treatment per wound per day across all healers.
Scars and accumulated wound count remain. Lost parts require an explicit replacement
or exceptional remedy; generic treatment does not regrow them.

Fresh treatment cards cover Soft Tissue, Bone, Mental, Elemental, Radiant and Life
injury. Map the approved wound’s treatment label to these capabilities during the
editorial audit; a similar-sounding ability is not automatically interchangeable.

## Death and Grit procedure

At the start of a Dying turn, spend one Grit to regain full HP or make a Fortitude
roll. Weak adds one death mark; Standard changes nothing; Strong stabilizes at 0
and clears marks. Fumble adds two marks; Critical restores 1 HP and clears marks.
Three marks cause Last Words. Damage while already Dying adds one mark per event,
not another wound. Damage while stabilized adds the new wound and one mark.

Healing above 0 clears death marks. Stabilization clears marks but leaves you at 0
and unable to act. Resolve Dying before ending combat; a breath does not erase a
pending death. Last Words ends in death at the end of the second following turn.

This procedure is transcribed from the agreed survival reference, not designed
from an old card. Ordinary potion/spell healing cannot reverse Last Words.
