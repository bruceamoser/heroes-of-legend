# Stability

**Approved by Bruce, October 13, 2026.** Every character and creature starts with
Stability 0. An ancestry trait, ability, equipment card or printed creature trait
must explicitly grant a higher value. Stability is not calculated from attributes,
ancestry size, armor, HP or level. Use the highest applicable grant, never their
sum, to a maximum of **3**.

Stability consumes the movement of a push, pull or throw, minimum 0. Apply the
initial target’s Stability once before it moves. When the moving group strikes a
new creature, that creature’s Stability consumes remaining movement once as it
joins the chain. If none remains, the group stops. Never charge the same creature
twice during one forced movement, even if several occupied squares collide.

Stability does not resist Prone, grappling, conditions or damage. It does not
reduce voluntary movement or a thrown projectile’s range. A printed push of 1 can
therefore move a Stability-0 target but not a Stability-1 target; its other effects
still resolve. A stopped push does not erase the attack’s damage or conditions.

**Example:** a push of 5 strikes a creature with Stability 1, leaving 4. It moves
one normal square, leaving 3, then strikes a creature with Stability 2. That new
creature consumes 2, leaving 1 for the moving group: enough to move one more normal
square before stopping.

Use the existing terrain and collision rules. Spending Stability does not itself
create damage. **Timing proposal for review:** resolve contact collision damage
using the movement remaining on arrival, then deduct the new member’s Stability
before further travel. This preserves an impact even when the new member stops
the chain; Bruce has approved consumption through the chain, not this damage-order
detail. No occupied squares may overlap when the group stops.
