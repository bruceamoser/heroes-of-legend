# Scars and Survival

Every survived incident that brought a hero to 0 HP leaves a descriptive scar.
Choose with the player after danger passes. There is no wound roll, accumulated
wound modifier, injury table or Recovery Value. A scar never adds a mechanical
penalty or benefit. The following prompts are invitations, not random results.

| What happened | A possible lasting reminder |
|---|---|
| Steel or claws | A pale line across the shoulder; a nick through an eyebrow |
| Fire or lightning | A silver streak of hair; branching marks on the arm |
| Cold | White fingertips; a patch of frost-pale skin |
| Crushing force | A seam along the jaw; a story about the shield that broke |
| Mental magic | A recurring dream; a phrase you remember when frightened |
| Radiant magic | A bright fleck in the iris; a sun-shaped mark |
| Life-draining magic | A white lock of hair; a shadow beneath the old injury |
| A desperate rescue | A companion's name tattooed beside the mark |

The player decides how a remembered event affects their hero's personality. Do not
impose trauma, disability or involuntary behavior as the price of surviving.
A chosen scar cannot justify an automatic Bane, lost action or movement penalty.

Record the scar, the incident and who helped you survive. No severity or treatment
track is needed. A later adventure may give the mark new meaning. The
[recovery rules](recovery.md) govern HP, stabilization, death rolls and Grit.
