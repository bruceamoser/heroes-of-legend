# Magic and spellbooks

A spellbook gathers a related family of spells. Its colored rows show the sections you learn as your studies deepen; each page adds a spell to your repertoire.

## Learning your books

Begin studying a magical discipline for 1 DP, then choose a book. Learn its sections in order: I costs 1 DP and teaches pages 1–2; II costs 2 DP and teaches pages 3–4; III costs 2 DP and teaches pages 5–6. At character level 3, learn section IV for 3 DP: it teaches pages 7–8 and all three advanced pages, A1–A3. Record the named final section for any combined abilities that call for it.

The first page of every book costs 0 SP to cast. Keep every learned page available for use. Spellcraft offers three training paths and the Channeling book; its capacity paths add to your shared SP reserve.

## Casting a spell

Choose a learned spell and legal targets, and commit its stated action. Resolve any opportunity attack first. If you can continue, recheck your targets, pay the printed SP and resolve the spell. You need a free hand and a clear path to a target unless the spell describes a remote connection. Measure range in squares from your position. A point or destination must be visible when chosen unless the spell explicitly uses a known place, memory, object or person instead. Carry ordinary gear within your usual capacity when a spell transports you.

Use the spell's printed attribute for any roll. A block marked Automatic resolves its stated effect directly. Three values always mean Weak / Standard / Strong. Attacks use the familiar 3d6 resolution and deal at least 1 damage after resistance and reductions. Add a condition at its listed rating, then use its normal timing and decay. Apply healing after the recipient's Life resistance, restore at least 1 HP when HP is missing, and stop at maximum HP. Healing spends SP independently of the recipient's first-aid treatment.

For several targets, work out the relevant Boons and Banes for each, roll once using the combination least favorable to you, and apply the result to every target. A spell that says every creature includes allies. A spell that says choose or up to a number lets you select its recipients. Affect each creature once per casting, even if it occupies several squares in the area. A cone widens from your square to the stated width at its far edge; a line is one square wide. Count radius distance using normal grid distance.

Players make the rolls. When an NPC casts against heroes, each affected player rolls the appropriate defense: Fortitude against bodily alteration, poison or life drain; Agility against aimed rays, bursts and moving hazards; Reason against thought, emotion, divination or magical binding. A spell or creature profile may name a more specific defense. Read the caster's outcomes in reverse: a Weak defense receives its Strong effect, Standard receives Standard, and Strong receives Weak. For an NPC-only exchange use the ordinary DA resolution procedure. Resolve automatic environmental and willing effects as printed.

## Actions and Rituals

**Main Action** spells include attacks, substantial healing, battlefield control and summoning. Attack cantrips also use a Main Action. **Maneuver** spells handle brief, small effects, such as sending a message, lighting an object or moving a small item. The action printed in each spell is the procedure to use.

A **Reaction** spell names its trigger and when to respond. Spend your available Reaction when that event occurs. A defense bonus applies before the roll; a reduction or redirection applies at the timing stated in its block. The ordinary action conversions still apply to casting, including using a Main Action for a Maneuver spell.

A **Ritual** gives its required casting time alongside the label. Pay SP and supply any listed materials at the beginning, following a printed daily schedule or payment timing when given. Work continuously for the stated time or scheduled work period, keeping the required subjects and materials present. Finish the Ritual before receiving its effect. An interrupted Ritual must be started again with fresh costs. Ritual work is active activity rather than restorative rest. Materials described as consumed are used up by the attempt at the printed time, or at the beginning when no time is given.

## Duration and sustained magic

An instant spell resolves once. A fixed-duration spell lasts for its printed time. In combat, count 10 rounds for each minute. You may end your own ongoing spell with a Maneuver. When it changes ordinary material permanently, the resulting object or terrain follows ordinary rules afterward. A magical effect ending leaves its described consequences in place: healed HP stays restored and damage stays dealt.

For a spell marked **Sustained**, casting maintains it until the end of your next turn. Starting on the turn after you cast it, spend a Maneuver and 1 SP by the end of each turn to maintain it through the end of your following turn, within the printed maximum duration. Perform that upkeep once per turn. A Main Action may convert to the required Maneuver. The upkeep's listed movement or renewal is part of that same Maneuver. A separate attack or other activity that names a Main Action still uses it.

You may sustain one spell at a time. Beginning another sustained spell ends the previous one. Unconsciousness, death, or missing an upkeep ends sustain. If a block says a condition is renewed, raise that spell's remaining contribution to the named rating rather than adding another instance each round. Conditions decay normally after renewal ends.

Identical named ongoing benefits on one recipient use the strongest value and the latest expiry; repeated castings refresh rather than multiply the benefit. Each independent damaging zone follows its stated per-round limit. Resolve damage when entering or ending a turn in such a zone, as printed; forced movement can cause entry. A round begins with the first creature's turn and ends after the last creature's turn.

## Summons, forms and invitations

A spell that creates a summoned form uses its listed profile, one immediate Main command on arrival, continuing commands and the six-square tether from Summoned Companions. Commands maintain these forms for the printed duration; they use the command procedure rather than sustained-spell upkeep. Their HP tracks the form, and they disperse at 0 HP or when their controller becomes unconscious or dies. Healing they perform spends the controller's SP. Summoned forms receive activity through commands and return to energy when their duration ends.

The roster's ratings 1, 3 and 7 describe which profiles a calling can produce. The spell names its permitted category and maximum rating. Use the summoned profile rather than the DA creature's combat entry. Animal shapes worn by a hero instead keep that hero's HP, attributes and resources, using the spell's stated movement and natural attacks.

A Familiar bond or invitation to a named planar being concerns an independent creature. It keeps its own needs, choices and relationships. Negotiate its service through the ordinary social procedures. A resurrection restores the named person only with that person's agreement. Lifegiving, Mass Lifegiving and True Resurrection share one lifetime return per person; Grit is separate.

## Illusions and informed choices

An illusion creates the sights, sounds or sensations its block describes. Physical interaction reveals an insubstantial image. To study a plausible illusion by observation, a hero spends a Main Action and rolls Reason for 1/2/3 progress toward target 3. Once complete, the hero recognizes it. When an NPC examines a hero's illusion, the player rolls Guile and the observer gains 3/2/1 progress on the player's Weak/Standard/Strong result. Keep that observer's progress for that illusion. Sound alone reveals no physical barrier or support.

Mental and social spells state the information, progress, conditions or choices they provide. Use the social objective procedure for concessions, with the DA setting a feasible goal around the listener's motives. A hero's player supplies their character's private thoughts or chooses their response where the spell calls for one. A spell that offers a command or resistance effect gives that choice to the target's controller.

## Unraveling a spell

Cancellation is progress against a particular casting. Its target is **2 + the spell's printed SP cost**, unless the effect states its own remedy. Use the target of the affected spell rather than any extra SP spent transferring or sustaining it. Keep accumulated progress while that same casting remains active. Completing the objective ends that casting; incomplete progress expires when it ends. A Reaction that interrupts casting must reach its target during that trigger to prevent resolution. Its block states any partial effect.

Spellbook spells, including Rituals, may be unraveled when the cancellation entry can reach and identify the effect. Removing a lasting enchantment follows its explicit remedy or Unessence. A specific remedy such as Remove Curse may end an effect immediately when the effect says so. Ordinary material shaped by a completed spell remains an ordinary object afterward.

## Sharing Spell Points

Casting and upkeep costs are paid separately from any SP moved. Transfer existing points from a donor's current reserve into a recipient's missing SP; the amount moved is the smallest of the spell's limit, the donor's available points, and the recipient's remaining capacity. Subtract the exact amount received from the donor. A reserve of 0 transfers 0.

Choose two different personal reserves for a transfer. A summoned form, animated group or magical duplicate uses its controller's resource rules and provides no independent SP reserve. A willing donor chooses its contribution. For unwilling participants, use the printed roll and the ordinary player-facing defense procedure; transferred SP itself causes no HP damage. A redirected transfer resolves once to its final recipient.

A transmitted spell uses its own action category and costs as stated by the channeling block. Its original caster makes any casting roll and handles sustain; the releasing ally uses the named Reaction. Rituals retain their full ritual time and preparation, and are outside effects that explicitly transmit a Main Action spell.

Choose a spell from outside the Channeling book when you use Channel Spell or Lord Channels. Channeling one of the book's own spells would send the magic back as arcane feedback.
