# Summoning: choice and commands

## Choose a creature, not another spell

The summoning card specifies category, maximum eligible tier, number, placement and
duration. Choose an explicitly eligible bestiary entry each casting. The six
proposed families are Animal, Fey, Spirit, Elemental, Undead and Construct; each has independent
Novice, Adept and Master cards. Higher cards may select eligible lower-tier entries.
All eighteen currently summon one creature. More powerful tiers expand eligibility,
not the number of independent actors. No prerequisite-card chains.

A category is an explicit bestiary tag. A ghost is not automatically selectable
under both Spirit and Undead; its entry must say so. These are summoned forms, not
permission to seize an existing creature or extract permanent equipment.

## Pay for activity

**Agreed:** an uncommanded summon does nothing. It does not follow, keep watch,
alert its controller, continue attacking, search, help or perform a standing order.
It takes no independent Reaction, including an opportunity attack. A prior order
never pays for another turn. Outside combat the same rule prevents unattended
work, scouting or guard duty; the controller must actively spend the required time
and actions directing it.

- Controller spends **Main Action**: one summon receives **Main Action + Maneuver**.
- Controller spends **Maneuver**: one summon receives **Maneuver + movement**.
- A summon receives at most one command on the controller’s turn. The controller
  may spend these two actions on different summons.
- Normal conversions apply: Main → Maneuver → movement. There is no separate free
  movement grant in the Main-command package. For example, convert its Maneuver
  to movement to approach, then spend Main on an attack.
- A newly summoned creature receives the summoning card’s immediate free Main
  command. That is its command for this turn; it cannot also receive a paid one.
- Resolve the granted activity on the controller’s turn. It cannot be banked for
  an enemy turn. No summon chains and no independent summon turns.

Commanding has no roll. Any permitted attack or task uses the summon profile’s
printed procedure. A command is not automatic success or a way to invent a skill.

## Orders in ordinary language

These examples describe intent, not a new menu of abilities granted to every creature.

| Order | Execution within the paid package |
|---|---|
| Attack that creature | Use a permitted attack. A Main command can convert its Maneuver to movement to approach first. |
| Defend that spot | A Maneuver command supplies movement and Defend, if the profile permits Defend. It does not grant free attacks at intruders. |
| Move there / follow me | Use only the granted movement, with legal routes and endpoints. Following stops when that movement ends. |
| Move and hide | Move to real concealment, then use the profile’s printed hiding action if the remaining budget permits it. No automatic Hidden. |
| Move and keep watch | Move, then use its printed observation action if affordable. It can report what it notices during that commanded activity; no continuing lookout afterward. |
| Help them | Use Aid or a printed support ability, if permitted and affordable. |
| Fetch or handle this | Use permitted movement and interaction; anatomy, reach, carried Bulk and action costs still apply. |
| Track this | Use the profile’s tracking procedure and available senses. The command does not grant tracking expertise. |

If a creature lacks the required capability or the package lacks the necessary
actions, it cannot complete that order. A longer task needs continued commands
and the printed activity time; one spoken instruction never buys hours of labor.

## What still happens without a command

The creature remains present and can be damaged or forcibly moved. Existing
conditions, durations, tether checks and already-resolved effects continue normally.
These are consequences, not permission for new activity. A previous Defend benefit
lasts only to its printed expiry; it does not renew itself.

The summon profile must anchor any “your turn” duration or condition tick to the
controller’s turn. Passive defenses such as resistance remain applicable. Do not
import automatic attacks, active auras or triggered abilities from a monster entry
without an explicit summon-profile procedure consistent with command-only activity.

## Bestiary work still required

For every eligible entry, define:

1. Allowed summoning categories and minimum summoning tier (1, 3 or 7).
2. HP, resistances, footprint, Speed, movement modes, senses and carrying limits.
3. Permitted actions and their costs, including attack roll/attribute/outcomes and
   any hiding, observation, tracking, support or interaction capabilities.
4. What the controller rolls when the summon is attacked. the controlling player rolls the creature’s printed defense attribute against an NPC attack.
5. Condition and duration anchors, healing compatibility and special restrictions.

No entry has been certified by this pass. The old embedded hound, cat, stag,
sentinel and other sample statistics are retired as selection permissions; they
are not a finished bestiary. CV can inform review, but cannot by itself authorize
an ability-rich monster as a player summon. Multi-square tether measurement and
other incapacities short of unconsciousness still need explicit rules;
until settled, neither can grant the summon independent activity.

**Latest agreement:** summons disappear when their controller becomes unconscious
or dies. Players roll their creatures’ printed attack and defense attributes;
see [session resolution](session-resolution.md).
