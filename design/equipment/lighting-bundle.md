# Lighting Bundle

*You shelter the first small flame until it takes hold and pushes back the dark.*

## Rules

- **ID:** item-lighting-bundle
- **Bulk:** 1
- **Requirements / supply:** Inventory d6
- **Acquisition:** Included among your paid starting provisions. Also available with coins, through a printed ability grant, or as treasure.

Light radius: the bearer’s square and all squares within 3 squares. Light or extinguish a torch with a Maneuver. One check per hour of use. Ordinary light reveals what could be seen, not Hidden targets automatically.

No DP price. Purchase does not grant training or an ability.

**Market price:** 5 coins. Proposal; see [market rules](../market.md).
