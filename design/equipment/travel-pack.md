# Travel Pack

*You tighten the straps and tuck the loose ends away before shouldering the load.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-travel-pack

**Market price:** 3 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

A reusable backpack or shoulder pack. Its own Bulk is 1, even when empty. Contents retain their full Bulk; it grants no capacity increase or concealed inventory slots.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
