# Book of Quiet Doors

**ID:** item-book-of-quiet-doors. **Status:** Proposal — special equipment. **Form:** Tome. **Bulk:** 1.

**Requirements:** Scholarship 1; identification required. **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

After studying for 10 minutes, gain 1 Boon to the next mundane lock attempt this scene. Grants no lock-working permission.

Additive to the appropriate underlying card only. No DP purchase; found or bought for coins at a DA-set price. No special recipe is supplied by owning it.
