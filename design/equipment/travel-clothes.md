# Travel Clothes

*You pull on well-mended clothes whose seams have learned your stride.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-travel-clothes · **Status:** Market proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Market price:** 4 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

A durable outfit with boots and cloak. One ordinary worn outfit is descriptive and contributes 0 Bulk; a carried spare is Bulk 1. Proposed creation baseline: everyone already owns one plain outfit, with no coin credit for declining it. This is the only new free starting equipment proposed here.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
