# Restraints

*You check the key against the lock before closing the irons.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-restraints

**Market price:** 8 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

Reusable manacles and key. With a free hand and manacles that fit, spend a Maneuver to cuff one adjacent creature that has surrendered, is helpless, or is Grappled 3 or higher. This takes no roll. Once the manacles are locked, a grappler can release their hold while the captive remains cuffed.

The manacles bind the limbs they enclose. The DA determines which movements and actions the captive can perform with those limbs bound. Open the manacles with the key, or use the ordinary objective rules to pick the lock, slip the cuffs, or break them when feasible.
