# Winter Lining

*You draw the lining close and let its quiet warmth meet the bitter air.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-winter-lining. **Status:** Proposal — special equipment. **Form:** Armor lining. **Bulk:** 1.

**Requirements:** No discipline requirement; understanding not required. **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

Grants cold resistance 1 while worn; highest matching resistance applies, cap 3. Does not grant armor training.

Additive to the appropriate underlying card only. No DP purchase; found or bought for coins at a DA-set price. No special recipe is supplied by owning it.
