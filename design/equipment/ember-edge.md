# Ember Edge

*You lift the weapon and watch a red gleam travel along its working edge.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-ember-edge. **Status:** Proposal — special equipment. **Form:** A melee weapon. **Bulk:** 1.

**Requirements:** Flame 1; understanding not required. **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

Adds 1 fire damage to attacks made with it. On Strong also applies Burning 1. Cannot apply to ranged attacks.

Additive to the appropriate underlying card only. No DP purchase; found or bought for coins at a DA-set price. No special recipe is supplied by owning it.
