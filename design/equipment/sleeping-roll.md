# Sleeping Roll

*You unroll the blanket and tuck its worn edge beneath your head.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-sleeping-roll

**Market price:** 4 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

A bedroll, blanket and ground cloth for one sleeper. Ordinary comfort and protection from damp ground, not automatic protection from dangerous cold or a safe-place ruling.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
