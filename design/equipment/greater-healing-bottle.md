# Greater Healing Bottle

*You swallow the strong draught and feel warmth spread deep into the hurt.*

## Rules

- **Status:** Proposal; ordinary equipment principles are agreed.
- **ID:** item-greater-healing-bottle
- **Bulk:** 1
- **Requirements / supply:** Inventory d4 proposal
- **Acquisition:** Explicit ability grant, coins, or DA treasure; no automatic extra starting kit.

Use [Use a Healing Potion](../cards/use-a-healing-potion.md): restore 10 HP before Life resistance, capped by missing HP. The giver needs a free hand and spends a Maneuver; another recipient spends a Reaction unless at 0 HP. Check the bottle’s inventory die after use; the final use still works.

No DP price. Coin prices remain unassigned pending economy review.
