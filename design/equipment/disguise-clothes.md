# Disguise Clothes

*You exchange familiar colors for another trade’s working clothes and check the fit.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-disguise-clothes

**Market price:** 10 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

One plausible outfit for a declared mundane role. No automatic identity, credential or Boon; a special disguise ability still follows its card.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
