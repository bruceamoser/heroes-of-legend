# Climbing Fittings

*You lay out the hook, harness and iron fittings, testing each before trusting your weight to it.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-climbing-fittings

**Market price:** 8 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

Reusable harness, hook, hammer and anchors. Rope is separate. Enables plausible anchoring; no automatic climb, Boon or specialist permission. Abandoned fittings need replacement at a DA-quoted fraction of the kit price.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
