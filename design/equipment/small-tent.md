# Small Tent

*You pull the canvas taut and listen as the rain begins to patter above it.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-small-tent

**Market price:** 12 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

Shelters two resting people from ordinary weather. Needs a suitable site and ten minutes to pitch or pack. No guarantee of safe rest or weather immunity.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
