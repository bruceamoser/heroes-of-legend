# Small Necessities

*You unfold a cloth of useful odds and ends: thread, chalk, soap and a flint worn smooth by use.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-small-necessities

**Market price:** 2 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

Bundle of ordinary firestarting, cleaning, sewing and marking supplies. Inventory d6; check after a scene of substantial consumption, not after every strike of the flint. Supplies no lock tools, medicine, ammo, food or crafting recipe ingredients.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
