# Padlock

*The shackle closes with a solid click as you turn the small key.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-padlock

**Market price:** 5 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

A mundane lock and key for a suitable closure. The DA sets any forced-entry or lock task from the situation; the price grants no universal difficulty.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
