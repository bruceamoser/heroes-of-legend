# Ordinary Heavy Armor

*You settle into its reassuring weight, each fastening bearing the marks of your care.*

## Rules

- **ID:** item-ordinary-heavy-armor
- **Bulk:** 1
- **Requirements / supply:** Learn [Heavy Armor I](../disciplines/armor.md#harness) to benefit while wearing this suit.
- **Physical resistance when worn:** 3
- **Acquisition:** Explicit ability grant, coins, or DA treasure; no automatic extra starting kit.

Choose the suit’s appearance. It grants physical resistance 3 while worn. Without the matching learned training, subtract 3 from effective Agility. Use the highest armor resistance and the largest applicable untrained armor penalty.

Receive an ordinary suit when you learn a technique that requires this armor category. You can also buy a suit with coins.
