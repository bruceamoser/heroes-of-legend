# Clearing Tonic Supply

*You lift the bottle to the light and watch its sharp green tint settle.*

## Rules

**ID:** item-clearing-tonic

**Market price:** 30 coins. **Bulk:** 1.

A d4 Clearing Tonic supply. With a free hand, spend a Maneuver to administer a dose to yourself or one adjacent willing or unconscious creature. Roll Knowledge: reduce either Poisoned by 2/3/4 or Dazed by 1/2/3 on Weak/Standard/Strong. Check the supply after use. It restores no HP. Owning it grants no recipe.

Buying equipment grants no DP advancement, training or ability. Use normal item-handling actions unless an applicable card says otherwise.
