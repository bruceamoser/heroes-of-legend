**133 records individually reviewed, October 13, 2026.** See the [full findings](1149-full-card-review.md).

Each row records a reading of narrative, rules procedure, practical usefulness and VTT handling. Retain means no immediate edit identified, not proven balanced. Revised includes copyediting; Decision identifies remaining design or shared-rule dependencies even where the text was improved. Family-level dependencies in the main report apply to all relevant cards, not only rows marked Decision.
