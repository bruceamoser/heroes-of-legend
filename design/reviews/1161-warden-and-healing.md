# Warden and Novice healing traditions

**Issues #1161 and #1162.** Bruce approved building the Warden concept and adding
simple healing expressions. Individual class statistics, signatures and spell
riders below remain proposals for review. No existing character or rule is replaced.

## Warden foundations

[Warden](../classes/warden.md) centers on pursuit, reading the land and getting
companions through danger. Proposed minimums are Fortitude 0 and Knowledge 0;
HP contribution +4. Home disciplines are Wilderness and Marksmanship; Adjacent
are Arms and Athletics, with all others Foreign. Choose two rank-1 grants from
those four. The usual duplicate compensation and sequential rank costs apply.

The minimums express stamina and field knowledge without requiring archery.
Life, Calling or other magic can be purchased normally; the class neither grants
an automatic animal companion nor requires supernatural powers. There is no
quarry counter, new movement action, tracking subsystem or special resource.

near hazards need playtesting. Signature advantages should feel useful without
turning every shared option into a poor purchase. None requires an earlier signature.

## Lira can now be a Warden

Keep [Lira’s](../characters/lira-ashbow.md) attributes, ancestry, origins, purchases
and equipment unchanged. Fortitude +1 and Knowledge +1 meet Warden minima. Choose
Arms 1 and Athletics 1 as her class grants and Keep the Quarry Close as her free
signature instead of Measured Guard. She buys no discipline ranks, so changing
class affinities changes no costs. Both classes contribute +4 HP: HP 20, Speed 8,
Bulk 4/10 and both ten-DP ledgers stay valid. Her bow now has a signature that
helps her pursue and reposition after a successful shot. This is an optional
conversion example; the original six sheets remain unchanged.

## Four healing expressions at level 1

| Card | Requirement / attribute | Weak / Standard / Strong | Identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Shared Breath](../cards/shared-breath.md) | Life 1 / Reason | 3 / 6 / 9 HP | Greatest straightforward restoration; open to any tradition. |
| [Stitch the Living Pattern](../cards/stitch-the-living-pattern.md) | Scholarship 1 / Knowledge | 3 / 5 / 7 HP | Arcane repair; Strong also steadies the recipient’s next spell roll if HP was restored. |
| [Mercy in the Light](../cards/mercy-in-the-light.md) | Radiance 1 / Reason | 3 / 5 / 7 HP | Divine blessing; Standard/Strong ease temporary Frightened by 1/2. |
| [Sap and Breath](../cards/sap-and-breath.md) | Life 1 / Reason | 3 / 5 / 7 HP | Nature or earth-mother healing; Standard/Strong ease temporary Bleeding by 1/2. |

Only two cards are added: nature healing already has its own complete card. Its
fiction can be a grove’s green life or a goddess’s blessing without changing rules.
No new deity list or tradition prerequisite is introduced.

Every option costs 2 DP to learn, a Main Action to use, and 1 HP paid first while
remaining above zero. A free hand is required. None bypasses Moser’s law, Life
resistance, the missing-HP cap or the minimum-one restoration rule. Healing-harmed
recipients and unwilling targeting are explicit on the two new cards.

Scholarship and Radiance provide alternative entry disciplines for these particular
spells. They do not grant Life ranks or access to other Life cards. Life retains
the stronger direct restoration and its existing higher-tier options. This broader
entry is an intentional proposal to make traditions accessible, not a silent
change to discipline purchase rules.

**Examples:** At 1 HP a caster cannot cast either new spell, because payment must
leave them above zero. On Strong, Stitch restores 7 before Life resistance and
missing HP; if it restores any HP, one spell-roll Boon waits until the end of the
recipient’s next turn. At full HP it grants no Boon. Mercy’s Standard restores 5
and reduces temporary Frightened by 1; it does not remove a permanent starred fear.

No encounter simulation or new balance claim accompanies these additions.

## Validation

Catalog audit and whitespace checks pass: 343 cards, including 90 signatures
and 235 shared abilities. Warden has exactly three class-and-level-only choices
at each milestone, with standard tier prices. Both new heals include explicit
payment, free hand, resistance, outcomes and expiry. These are authoring checks,
not measured power or VTT automation tests. Historical surveys remain snapshots.
