# Concrete actions and readable rules

October 13, 2026, issue #1147. Bruce identified Borrow Tomorrow’s Weather as an
example where the previous writing pass had supplied a slogan and unexplained dice
language rather than a usable description and procedure.

## Findings and corrections

Read all 322 rule-card narrative introductions and reviewed recurring compressed
rule language across the catalog. Rewrote 75 descriptions that relied on metaphor,
omitted the character’s action, or left the resulting effect unclear. Existing clear
descriptions were retained. This is a targeted editorial correction, not a claim that
every sentence in the catalog has reached final book quality.

Examples:

- [Borrow Tomorrow’s Weather](../cards/borrow-tomorrow-s-weather.md): reach through
  a tear in the air, pull out weather, toss it onto the ground. The rules now explain
  target selection, the roll, which die determines the effect, duration and a worked example.
- [Breath of the Wyrmling](../cards/breath-of-the-wyrmling.md): the character inhales
  and breathes a stream of fire, rather than merely sensing something ancient smile.
- [Spring in the Sleeve](../cards/spring-in-the-sleeve.md): level the wrist, release
  the catch, and fire a hook that strikes and pulls; the old rattling joke never explained it.
- [The Otter’s Bargain](../cards/the-otter-s-bargain.md): webbed fingers and stronger
  swimming strokes describe the actual transformation instead of an abstract relationship to tides.

The same unexplained die-selection rule appeared on Let the Spark Decide and A
Splendid Mistake. All three now identify the three dice used after Boons/Banes,
explain which face numbers select the effect, and separate that selection from the
attack or duration result. No extra die is rolled. The weather example uses 2, 4,
5 and Guile +1: smallest die 2 gives ice; total 12 gives Standard duration.

This preserves the proposed random selection. Whether these cards need it is a
**design question**, not something resolved by clearer prose. Choosing the effect
before the roll would be a simpler alternative, but would change this path’s current
unpredictable-magic premise. No such redesign is adopted by this editorial change.

Expanded compressed instructions on swimming, animal calming, distraction, poison
and Dazed relief, Life damage, five wound treatments and three regional foraging
cards. Removed copied wound-treatment limits from unrelated cards; retained the
once-per-wound-per-day limit on treatment. Replaced generic “worst applicable situation”
with an instruction to compare targets’ net Boons/Banes and use the least favorable
combination for the one player roll. NPC area defenses remain separate player rolls.
Ordinary task text now explains what the DA establishes and whether progress carries
over, instead of merely referring to an objective and cumulative target.

## Checks and remaining limits

No card IDs, tiers, prices, discipline requirements, attributes or action costs changed.
The three spells retain their original die-selection mapping and result numbers.
The shared-card survey metadata checks and the authoring/link audit still apply.
No new combat simulation or book-layout work was needed.

The revised writing guidance requires a human-style read of action, visible effect,
resolution order and applicable limits. A heading-presence check cannot certify
those qualities. Existing open core decisions remain open; terminology clarification
does not settle them by implication.
