# Language reference review — #1265

Bruce requested a language list in both books and a full DA section on using,
adapting and building languages. The new shared source is `rules/languages.md`;
the DA supplement is `da-book/manuscript/04a-languages.md`.

## New defaults for review

- Common plus one ordinary home-community language at creation, with no DP charge.
- Eleven ordinary rows (including Common, one named regional choice and Way Sign),
  plus six uncommon/ancient rows. A regional or Elder Tongue selection names one
  language, not an entire category.
- Unrestricted knowledge includes its normal written form where one exists.
  Explicit spoken-only, understands-only and cannot-speak grants remain limited.
- Campaign substitutions are declared before choices; no ancestry locks.

These defaults fill previously unspecified space. They are proposals, not prior
agreements. Existing ancestry/ability grants, action costs and translation outcomes
are unchanged. A Foot in Two Worlds still grants two ordinary spoken languages;
Translate the Fragment interprets one text, not permanent fluency.

## References reconciled

- Creature Common, Draconic, Fey, Giant, Goblin, Infernal, Kobold and Orc now have
  concrete choices in the list.
- Generic court, scholarly, regional, sacred, woodland, oath, maker and lifetime
  entries have DA assignment guidance; they do not grant all languages in a category.
- Mimicry/disguise abilities still do not grant languages or memories.
- Hold a Conversation's shared-language requirement is not bypassed by an interpreter.
- Summon communication does not bypass commands or grant actions.
- No Language Study DP purchase or training calendar was invented. This remains
  a future catalog decision if Bruce wants permanent learning beyond explicit grants.

## Implementation

Both books render the same Markdown language reference. DA source assembly and
hash coverage include the reference and the additional worldbuilding section.
Character creation links to the reference. The six example heroes record concrete
language choices, including Oren’s spoken-only extras, and the training sheet has
a language field. Current PDFs are review builds, not a
new release or replacement of the published v16.0.0 assets.

## Validation

The final DA proof has 307 pages; the Player’s Book proof has 238 pages. Both
book audits pass. Shared-reference coverage, internal links and required language
headings are checked by their respective audits. The language pages were inspected
at reading size; final example-hero and sheet changes were rendered separately.
No encounter simulations were run for this content/layout change.
