# Class signatures: identity, appeal and a deliberate power edge

**October 13, 2026 — issues #1153, #1154 and #1155.** All 81 signatures were
reviewed and revised. This report supersedes the signature findings in the
[full catalog review](1149-full-card-review.md); the older ledger and fingerprints
remain a historical record of that earlier text.

Bruce's agreed standard is that signatures define the class and should be **slightly
more powerful than shared cards of the same tier**. Three weak options do not make
a meaningful milestone. The individual effects below are concrete proposals for
review, not a claim of measured balance or Bruce's approval of every number.

Every signature still requires only its class and minimum level, with no discipline
or previous-signature gate. Levels 1/3/7 and prices 2/4/8 are unchanged. All shared
abilities and their fourteen Passive options are unchanged. Signatures modifying
spells or attacks work with the applicable category, not one required purchased card.
Universal Aid, Force, Defend, Disengage and Basic Melee remain available reference
actions, not new prerequisites.

## The nine identities

| Class | Level 1: establish | Level 3: expand | Level 7: deliver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arcanist | Bend spell angles, add a spark's shove, or read active magic | Move after casting, protect one creature from an area, or turn spell pressure aside | Move a companion through space, shape a spell's final displacement, or ward a circle |
| Blade | Flow through melee, hinder a target, or guard and reposition | Enter decisively, counterattack, or take an opponent's footing | Strike a surrounding group, pursue and strike, or dictate where the duel moves |
| Intellect | Find leverage, connect evidence, or give useful directions | Direct an ally, diagnose mechanisms, or predict an attack | Route the party, reconstruct events, or expose a target for allies |
| Leader | Signal two allies, withdraw together, or hold a paired line | Order a retreat, call a basic strike, or interrupt panic and shock | Coordinate a rush, rally a group, or cover an ally's escape |
| Odd | Reframe a task, distract usefully, or escape after exertion | Assist unusually well, uncover an alternate approach, or pull a companion out of trouble | Apply ingenious force, escape with a partner, or turn pain into a response |
| Protector | Intercept, anchor, or arrive ready to defend | Push an attacker back, extract the fallen, or make a refuge | Charge into a strike, clear the circle, or escort companions through danger |
| Shadow | Slip away, land quietly, or cover an ally's passage | Cross difficult ground, strike and leave, or open a lock quickly | Shroud an escape, exploit a companion's disabling effect, or hide a party's trail |
| Shepherd | Support returning movement, stabilize at range, or ease a condition | Calm shock, call the wounded nearer, or transfer life carefully | Reach the dying, restore health and composure, or settle a whole group's distress |
| Unbalanced | Drive through, charge, or rise fighting | Break an obstruction, throw a creature, or shove back through pain | Scatter enemies, wrench free and retaliate, or finish a push with a knockdown |

These columns describe independent options, not three fixed builds or progression
chains. A Blade can choose a defensive beginning and an aggressive Master signature.
An Odd never needs a second class's signature to complete a concept.

## What changed beyond increasing numbers

- Strong-only riders that seldom established an identity now often work on Standard
  too, or give a smaller benefit on Weak. They do not turn Weak into automatic task
  completion.
- Return Stroke, Exploit Confusion and Relentless Pursuit explicitly grant Basic
  Melee only. The attack uses the recipient's Brawn and normal basic damage. Free
  attacks never generate another free attack through a trigger on damage, movement
  or conditions.
- Defiant Rescue now declares before the attack roll, approaches and intercepts
  once. It no longer sees lethal damage and asks the table to rewind it. Watch the
  Rear grants defense and moves the ally *after* resolution, with no targeting rewind.
- Unseen Passage supplies a bounded visual shroud on the card. It does not rely on
  an undefined Invisible condition; offensive actions end it. It is not immunity
  to areas, sound-based targeting or physical obstacles.
- Open the Way, Fling the Captive and Crush the Barrier are complete actions. A
  Shadow gets a fast quiet locksmith, and an Unbalanced can throw someone without
  first buying a grappling chain.
- Make the Connection, Diagnose the Failure, Test the Hypothesis and Leave No
  Pursuit deliver concrete exploration results. Obvious clues remain free; evidence
  and plausible methods still matter. No ability invents a mystery's answer.
- Careful Transfer and Restore Composure are complete healing spells with their
  full 3/7 HP payment, free hand and normal Life resistance. Condition-only care
  restores no HP and therefore does not disguise free healing.
- One Last Remark works after ordinary damage, with an enhanced Last Words use.
  The dying speaker gains no actions and Last Words remains final.

## Comparison method

The [81-card comparison ledger](1153-signature-comparisons.md) names a same-tier
shared comparator for every signature and explains the intended edge. Where no
close same-tier shared analogue exists, it says so and also names the closer
lower-tier comparison. A conditional passive cannot be valued by pretending it
contains the attack it modifies: the player still pays for that action and ability.
Likewise, a no-roll support action is compared with its recipients, range and timing,
not only its largest printed number.

“Slightly stronger” is a qualitative design target here. It means a deliberate,
useful advantage with understandable constraints, not strict dominance in every
circumstance. A signature can give better action efficiency while having fewer
recipients, or better reliability while losing range. Shared cards still supply
important damage, spells and specialist permissions.

## Table and VTT handling

Every signature has concrete narrative, an action, attribute/no-roll instruction,
actual target field, complete procedure, duration, equipment and limits. A separate
Tags field describes attack, defense, movement, recovery, utility or magic while
retaining Signature as the record type. No signature inspects an individual die,
requires a new resource pool or grants a second Reaction.

Track only the printed counters: once on your turn, once per examined question or
obstacle, next-roll benefits, and anchored expiration. If an effect applies before
a defense, show a prompt before that existing roll; do not roll a second defense.
Granted movement has a legal route and free endpoint. Same-kind reduction uses the
highest value; conditions and Boons/Banes follow their usual rules.

Repeated applications of an ongoing effect do not create extra copies. That does
not add a general cap to different Boon sources. A card reducing a temporary
condition does not remove a starred wound source, erase scars or refund spent actions.

## What still needs playtesting or a shared ruling

**Buying multiple signatures:** learned signatures are all available; there is no
new equipped-signature limit. A Blade can combine movement, Slowed and Prone riders
on one Main attack. That is intentional synergy, but it is the first combination
to test against an equally developed character purchasing shared abilities. Individual
small advantages can create a larger combined gap.

**Burst turns:** Press the Opening can accompany the Leader's Main attack or another
command. It is once on the Leader's turn, uses Basic Melee, and consumes a Maneuver.
Test the resulting team output before changing ordinary free attacks or adding a
new global cap. Master pursuit and condition-trigger attacks are also priorities.

**Control and recovery timing:** the shared decision about midturn changes to Dazed,
Grappled and action categories remains. The revised cards do not restore spent
actions. Grappled 3 still cannot self-release through these signatures; both sides'
actual contributions matter when a hold is reduced.

**Movement and perception:** crowded collision groups, ordinary jumping/falling,
and the wider Hidden system still need their common reference. Soft Landing gives
its own short jump; Unseen Passage states its own visual-targeting protection. Neither
settles those unrelated global rules. NPC-versus-NPC/summon resolution and attacks'
NPC defense attributes remain separate work.

**Healing-harmed creatures:** the new signature heals target willing recipients.
As with shared healing, a willing creature harmed by healing takes the listed HP
amount as Life damage instead; beneficial condition/movement riders do not apply.
The rules for forcing healing on unwilling creatures remain unresolved; these cards
do not silently supply an offensive targeting procedure.

The next useful test is a small group of complete same-level characters, with actual
signature purchases and terrain, comparing how often each option matters and how
quickly it resolves. No new numerical simulation or live VTT automation is claimed
by this editorial rewrite.

## Worked checks

- **Independent throw:** Fling the Captive needs only Unbalanced level 3. With no
  Grapple card, a Standard Brawn result deals 4 physical, pushes 2 and knocks prone.
  Against physical resistance 3, damage is 1 before any collision. At Grappled 3,
  the user has no Main Action and cannot use it.
- **Leader setup:** Press the Opening spends the Leader's Maneuver. The ally rolls
  its own Basic Melee; the Leader does not roll a second command check. The Leader
  retains Main. A converted extra Maneuver cannot use this signature a second time
  on that turn.
- **Paid rescue:** Careful Transfer at 3 HP cannot be cast; paying 3 must leave the
  caster above zero. At 4 HP it leaves 1. Standard restores 10 before the recipient's
  Life resistance and reduces temporary Bleeding by 1. No scar is removed.
- **No rewind:** Defiant Rescue is declared before the original attack/defense roll.
  The Protector moves, qualifies as target, then that attack resolves once against
  the Protector. It cannot be declared after learning the hit would be lethal.
- **Bounded shroud:** Unseen Passage spends Main and moves up to three squares.
  The Shadow cannot be chosen as a visible target, but an area including its square
  still affects it. Its next attack ends the shroud immediately after resolution;
  the card grants no automatic Boon or extra attack.
- **Evidence first:** Test the Hypothesis can reliably reconstruct what the examined
  evidence supports. If the killer left no identifying evidence, it cannot name the
  killer; it identifies the account's limit and an available lead instead.

## Verification

The structural catalog audit passes, and the shared-card survey still finds 223
shared cards and fourteen shared passives. A comparison against parent commit
`bddac75` confirms all 81 signature IDs, tier gates, DP prices and class requirements
are preserved: three choices at each of three milestones for all nine classes.
All 81 benchmark links name an existing shared card of the same tier.

The [signature manifest](1153-signature-manifest.json) records before/after hashes
for this editorial revision. Previous manifests remain historical snapshots. These
checks validate coverage and record integrity, not encounter balance or player appeal.
