# Heroes between battles

**October 13, 2026 revision for Bruce’s review.** The direction is agreed: a modest
shared passive selection, stronger utility development, meaningful mixed disciplines,
and evocative narrative followed by precise rules. Individual cards and numbers are
proposals. These routes are suggestions, never prerequisite chains or class restrictions.

These paths overlap by design. Riding enters through [Saddle and Rein](cards/saddle-and-rein.md),
without pretending we have settled mounted combat. Treatment develops through
[A Steady Hand at Dawn](cards/a-steady-hand-at-dawn.md), which improves a weak treatment
result without adding daily attempts or removing scars. Ordinary actions remain available.

## Fourteen shared passives, not a second skill sheet

The seven existing passives remain: Conditioned Body, Ordinary Recipe Study, Pack
with Purpose, Take Them Alive, Wear Medium Armor, Wear Heavy Armor and One More Dawn.
The seven additions are A Knot You Can Trust, A Good Place to Stop, The Right Small
Courtesy, Live in the Mask, Read the Maker’s Hand, A Steady Hand at Dawn and The Land
Keeps No Secrets. Their benefits concern particular situations; none adds a skill
modifier or replaces the six attributes.

## Requirements should tell a story

Ember Along the Edge now uses Flame 1 **and** Arms 1. It is available at Novice,
but the character must actually have both fields. A Sword Made of Dawn continues
that idea at Flame 3 and Arms 1. Neither card requires owning the other.

A Line for Everyone uses Athletics 2 and Wilderness 1: placing the line and reading
the terrain both matter. Where Only Eagles Pass stays Athletics 3, so a committed
climber need not buy magic or an unrelated discipline to reach an ultimate.

A Company of Reflections uses Mind 2 and Guard 2; Four Winds at Once uses Storm 2
and Athletics 2. These are Master combinations, still level 7, not cheap early
access simply because neither field reaches rank 3. A Voice across the Valley uses
Mind 2 and Storm 2 for a genuinely new communication capability.

Ordinary herbal brewing calls on Craft and Medicine, not Life by default. Magical
water breathing draws on Wilderness and Life, but restores no HP and therefore does
not pay a healing cost. All origin pairs still supply the requirements of their
granted card. All signatures still require only class and level.

## Three purchase checks

**Spellblade at level 0:** Learning Houses can grant Flame 1 and Cinder Cast;
Household Guard can grant Arms 1 and Hook and Drive. Ember Along the Edge then costs
2 DP and requires no level-0 discipline purchase. One Flame grant alone no longer
qualifies the character. The origin pair remains a choice, not a mandated build.

**Protector learning to rig a route at level 3:** starting with Athletics 1,
raising Athletics to 2 costs 6 DP at the proposed Foreign price. Wilderness 1 costs
3, and A Line for Everyone costs 4: **13 DP**. The level-3 grant alone is 11, so the
character needs 2 saved DP or an earlier Wilderness grant. A Pure Athletics 3 route
remains available later. This is a real investment, not just two icons on a card.

**Arcanist reaching a Master communication card:** if Storm 1 and Mind 1 are already
owned, Storm 2 costs 4 at Adjacent and Mind 2 costs 6 at Foreign. The level-7 card
costs 8, for **18 DP** total. Level 7 grants 15 DP, so at least 3 must be saved or
invested earlier. The example assumes no other purchases from that award.

Bruce accepts up to 35 grant-eligible unowned shared choices as useful starting breadth;
this is not a target to cut or a guarantee that all choices are equally attractive.

Creation DP and prices are unchanged. These examples establish affordability under
stated assumptions, not balance or an optimum path.

## Writing and adjudication review

All 322 learned/universal/signature entries now begin with narrative and then Rules.
The same separation applies to 20 ancestry traits, four ancestry records, 50 origins,
nine classes, 18 disciplines, 22 equipment records and ten recipes. Narrative is not
an extra source of mechanical permission.

Specific repairs include explicit distance/endpoints for Borrow the Shadow and Carry
to Safety; movement and an ally cost for Veiled Crossing; explicit recipients for Hold
the Passage; a no-roll Icebound Path because its three results were identical; potion
items linking the full use procedure; and Through the Spearpoints using a defensive
Boon instead of extra damage reduction beyond the resistance framework.

The old Hidden acquisition/perception decision, opportunity timing, summon-versus-NPC
resolution and collision geometry remain in [open decisions](open-decisions.md). We
have not settled those core questions by slipping a new rule into narrative prose.
The earlier survey’s mention of a “Universal Hide” referred to ordinary hiding, but
there is no separate Hide card in the current universal set; that action’s detailed
procedure belongs in the perception decision.

Read [the revised survey](reviews/1143-revision-review.md) for counts and remaining
limits. The original [October 13 survey](reviews/1141-passive-utility-and-access.md)
is retained as a historical baseline, not silently updated to describe different cards.
