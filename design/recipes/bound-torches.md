# Bound Torches

*You bind the dry fibers tightly, leaving a head that will take the flame evenly.*

## Rules

- **ID:** recipe-bound-torches
- **Status:** Proposal — ordinary recipe
- **Permission:** Trail Workshop, Make Something Useful or Enchanting Workshop; the recipe alone grants no crafting permission
- **Tools:** Craft tools
- **Materials per attempt:** Suitable wood, fiber and fat, obtained in the scene or one compatible carried bundle use
- **Time per attempt:** One hour
- **Result:** Inventory supply, maximum d6

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak raises one matching supply by 1 inventory step; Standard by 2; Strong by 3, up to the printed cap. The first step from out creates d4; later steps are d6, d8, d10, d12, d20. Check carried material supply after the attempt. No group crafting.

[Product](../equipment/lighting-bundle.md). This recipe can be one of the two ordinary choices granted by Trail Workshop or Ordinary Recipe Study. It is reusable and does not grant free ingredients.
