# Lesser Healing Bottle

*You keep the mixture at a patient simmer, watching for the color your teacher showed you.*

## Rules

The useful bottle is the one you prepared before you needed it.

**Recipe permission:** [Bottled Morning](../cards/bottled-morning.md).
**Tools:** Mixing kit. **Time:** One hour per attempt.
**Materials:** Fresh moonmint, clean water and one measure of prepared life salts; one compatible material-bundle use per attempt if carried.

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak/Standard/Strong raises one bundle by 1/2/3 inventory steps, from out to d4 first, maximum d4. Check material supply after the attempt. No group crafting. Ingredients must actually be acquired.

A Lesser Healing Bottle restoring 6 HP before Life resistance. Use the universal potion action.

Each finished bundle/bottle is Bulk 1. Recipe knowledge creates no free stock. This is a ordinary recipe; the plants and salts are fictional ingredients, not real preparation instructions.
