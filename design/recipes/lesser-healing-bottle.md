# Lesser Healing Bottle

*You keep the mixture at a patient simmer, watching for the color your teacher showed you.*

## Rules

The useful bottle is the one you prepared before you needed it.

**Permission:** Learn this formula from a teacher or discover it in play.
**Tools:** Mixing kit. **Time:** One hour per attempt.
**Materials:** Fresh moonmint, clean water and one measure of prepared life salts; one compatible material-bundle use per attempt if carried.

Roll Knowledge. Weak/Standard/Strong raises one bundle by 1/2/3 inventory steps, from out to d4 first, maximum d4. Check material supply after the attempt. No group crafting. Ingredients must actually be acquired.

A Lesser Healing Bottle restores 6 HP before Life resistance. Use the universal potion action.

Each finished bundle/bottle is Bulk 1.

**ID:** recipe-lesser-healing-bottle
