# Smoke Flask

The useful bottle is the one you prepared before you needed it.

**Recipe permission:** [Smoke in a Bottle](../cards/smoke-in-a-bottle.md).
**Tools:** Mixing kit. **Time:** One hour per attempt.
**Materials:** Dry puffleaf, prepared mineral dust and a stoppered bottle; one compatible material-bundle use per attempt if carried.

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak/Standard/Strong raises one bundle by 1/2/3 inventory steps, from out to d4 first, maximum d6. Check material supply after the attempt. No group crafting. Ingredients must actually be acquired.

Smoke supply for Smoke in a Bottle; no damage or poison.

Each finished bundle/bottle is Bulk 1. Recipe knowledge creates no free stock. This is a proposed ordinary recipe; the plants and salts are fictional ingredients, not real preparation instructions.
