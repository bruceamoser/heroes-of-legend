# Smoke Flask

*You seal the cloudy mixture quickly, before the first acrid curl reaches your eyes.*

## Rules

The useful bottle is the one you prepared before you needed it.

**Permission:** [Smoke in a Bottle I](../disciplines/craft.md).
**Tools:** Mixing kit. **Time:** One hour per attempt.
**Materials:** Dry puffleaf, prepared mineral dust and a stoppered bottle; one compatible material-bundle use per attempt if carried.

Roll Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress toward target 3. Each completed project creates one single-use flask, Bulk 1, not an inventory-die bundle. Consume one batch of ingredients per finished flask; no free stock is created. Use the range, duration and smoke effects of your learned Smoke in a Bottle improvements.
