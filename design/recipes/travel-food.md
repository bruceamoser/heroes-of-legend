# Travel Food

*You salt, wrap and dry the ingredients, packing a little hospitality for the road.*

## Rules

- **ID:** recipe-travel-food
- **Status:** Proposal — ordinary recipe
- **Permission:** Trail Workshop, Make Something Useful, or a mixing card that explicitly grants this recipe; the recipe alone grants no crafting permission
- **Tools:** Craft tools or the granting card’s mixing kit
- **Materials per attempt:** Safe edible forage, obtained in the scene or one compatible carried bundle use
- **Time per attempt:** One hour
- **Result:** Inventory supply, maximum d8

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak/Standard/Strong provides 1/2/3 progress or supply steps. For a supply, raise one bundle only; first step from out is d4. Check consumed material supply after the attempt. No group crafting.

[Product](../equipment/food-bundle.md). This recipe can be one of the two ordinary choices granted by Trail Workshop or Ordinary Recipe Study. It is reusable and does not grant free ingredients.
