# Clearing Tonic

*You crush the sharp-smelling leaves and strain the mixture until it runs clear.*

## Rules

**Permission:** Learn this formula from a teacher or discovery; it is not automatically granted by Craft access.
**Tools:** Mixing kit. **Time:** One hour per attempt.
**Materials:** Bitter starleaf, clean water and a filtering cloth; one compatible material-bundle use per attempt if carried.

Roll Knowledge. Weak/Standard/Strong raises one bundle by 1/2/3 inventory steps, from out to d4 first, maximum d4. Check material supply after the attempt. No group crafting. Ingredients must actually be acquired.

Produces [Clearing Tonic](../equipment/clearing-tonic.md); use its printed administration rules. It restores no HP.

Each finished bundle/bottle is Bulk 1. Recipe knowledge creates no free stock. This is a ordinary recipe; the plants and salts are fictional ingredients, not real preparation instructions.
