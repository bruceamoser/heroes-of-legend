# Simple Replacement Leg

*You shape and fit the pieces around the person who will have to trust their weight to them.*

## Rules

- **ID:** recipe-simple-replacement-leg
- **Status:** Proposal — ordinary recipe
- **Permission:** Trail Workshop; the recipe alone grants no crafting permission
- **Tools:** Craft tools
- **Materials per attempt:** Suitable wood, leather straps and padding, obtained in the scene or one compatible carried bundle use
- **Time per attempt:** One hour
- **Result:** Permanent prosthesis, target 8

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak/Standard/Strong provides 1/2/3 progress or supply steps. For a supply, raise one bundle only; first step from out is d4. Check consumed material supply after the attempt. No group crafting.

Requires Fit a Replacement before relieving any wound effect; the wound must permit replacement. This recipe can be one of the two ordinary choices granted by Trail Workshop or Ordinary Recipe Study. It is reusable and does not grant free ingredients.
