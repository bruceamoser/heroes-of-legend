# Wooden Brace

*You trim the supports until they meet snugly, then bind the join against its load.*

## Rules

- **ID:** recipe-wooden-brace
- **Status:** Proposal — ordinary recipe
- **Permission:** Trail Workshop, Make Something Useful or Enchanting Workshop; the recipe alone grants no crafting permission
- **Tools:** Craft tools
- **Materials per attempt:** Suitable wood and bindings, obtained in the scene or one compatible carried bundle use
- **Time per attempt:** One hour
- **Result:** Permanent ordinary repair aid, target 4

Roll the crafting card’s attribute. Weak adds 1 progress; Standard 2; Strong 3. Accumulate progress until the printed target is met, then receive one finished product. Check carried material supply after each attempt. No group crafting.

The product is one ordinary support brace, Bulk 1, not a second tools grant. This recipe can be one of the two ordinary choices granted by Trail Workshop or Ordinary Recipe Study. It is reusable and does not grant free ingredients.
