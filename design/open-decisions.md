# Further playtest work

The November revision integrates health, sequential disciplines, spellbook lists, combined abilities and social objectives into the books. The integration is tracked in HOL #1313 and Foundry #272; new mechanics introduced during integration require review alongside the resulting PDFs.

- Write complete HOL casting profiles for the 319 spellbook entries: action or casting time, attribute, target, range, outcomes, duration, costs and limits. The book currently prints the complete learned-page atlas; source descriptions are effect briefs, not complete spell procedures. Resolve utility, summoning, ritual and channeling edge cases explicitly.
- Playtest HP growth, the death-roll cadence, descriptive scars, once-per-rest first aid, full overnight recovery and the three lifetime Grit grants.
- Test automatic +2 maximum SP per level after level 1 alongside both Spellcraft capacity paths, recovery and channeling. Compare meaningful spell uses per day and martial contribution, not only damage.
- Test 1/2/2/3 DP sequential paths, level-3 endpoints, origin grants, creation pools and 3/4/5 DP level-7 combinations. Pay particular attention to cumulative conditions and hybrid abilities.
- Review the eleven magical combination procedures completed during integration, especially resurrection limits, persistent structures and shared action economy.
- Test social progress targets, credible contributions and mixed-group objectives. Check whether Influence improves play without making ordinary conversation depend on learned abilities.
- Recalibrate creature CV and encounter budgets against the revised character options. Test healer encounters, disabling combinations, summons and solo action economy.
- Gather feedback on whether learned paths and the two-column spellbook lists reduce choice paralysis at the table.

Numbers appear as ordinary rules in the player-facing book. This work list records the validation still needed; it does not add competing rulings or promise a particular encounter duration.
