# Further playtest work

The current catalog and both books are the approved baseline. Previously settled
rulings are recorded directly in their rules, not kept as competing proposals.

Remaining work concerns evidence and new content, rather than approval of the
existing system:

- Calibrate creature CV and encounter budgets against the actual catalog and table play.
- Test healer encounters, disabling combinations, summons and solo action economy.
- Gather feedback on character choices and the clarity of cards during play.
- Decide any future permanent language-learning purchase or campaign progression;
  current language grants and translation rules are already defined.
- Expand mounted play, rare recipes or other options only through a new design decision.

The existing rules do not promise a particular encounter duration or wound rate.
Those remain design targets. Use [encounter guidance](bestiary/encounters.md) and
record actual play before changing numbers.
