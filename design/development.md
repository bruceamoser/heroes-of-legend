# Develop a character

## Create a level-1 hero

Assign Brawn, Fortitude, Agility, Guile, Knowledge and Reason from -2 through +2, totaling +3. Attributes stay fixed as you gain levels. Choose an ancestry and two of its five traits. Record the characteristic and its benefits and side effects together.

Choose a culture and background independently of ancestry. Each offers two disciplines; begin studying one from each. Choose a class whose attribute minimums you meet, then begin studying one discipline from its starting list. Choose three distinct disciplines across these choices. Select one level-1 class signature.

Your starting Development Points (DP) are **10 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason**, including negative values, plus explicit trait benefits. Use these points to learn abilities and spellbook sections. Starting disciplines are already yours to study. An additional discipline costs the 1, 2 or 3 DP printed for your class; then learn its entries at their listed prices. Save unspent DP for later development.

## Learning your paths

Choose a path in a discipline you study and learn its entries in order. Pay each entry’s DP cost and meet its level requirement. Advanced entries become available at level 3 and mastery at level 7. Apply improvements together unless an entry replaces a previous value. You can study several paths.

In the discipline atlas, circles mark level-1 entries, triangles mark level-3 entries, and diamonds mark level-7 entries. Solid gold shows an entry you know. A gold border around green shows one you can learn now. Follow the lines to see what comes next.

Choose a spellbook in a magical discipline you study. Learn its sections in order and add the spells marked with that section number. Keep the spells you learn, and cast each for its printed SP cost. The first spell in each book costs 0 SP. In Spellcraft, Soul Capacity and Mind Capacity increase your maximum SP, Recovery restores spent SP, and Channeling lets you transfer SP.

Every hero can use the basic actions, including melee and ranged attacks, Aid, Stabilize and Sustain Spell.

## Signatures

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may change a signature selection. Choose a replacement from the same milestone and update any benefits that depend on knowing both signatures. Each earned milestone still supplies one selection; changing it neither spends nor grants DP, repeats an acquisition benefit, nor restores used resources.

## Equipment and starting numbers

Take compatible ordinary equipment required by your learned techniques for free. One item can serve several compatible techniques. A plain worn outfit is free. From **40 + ancestry + culture + background + class coin contributions**, pay for starting food, water, lighting and a lesser healing potion. Return unwanted starting provisions at their full price before finishing creation. Spend the balance on other gear. Light, medium and heavy armor grant physical resistance 1, 2 and 3 while worn. If you lack the matching training, subtract that suit’s rating from effective Agility.

Maximum HP is **10 + level x (ancestry HP contribution + class HP contribution) + Fortitude + fixed benefits**. Begin with full HP. Each later level adds ancestry + class contributions to maximum HP; Fortitude and fixed benefits apply once. When you have positive HP, add that level's HP gain to current HP too. At 0 HP, only maximum HP grows.

Maximum SP is **2 × level + ancestry SP + 2 × positive class casting attribute + Spellcraft bonuses + other SP bonuses**. Spellcraft bonuses come from its Soul Capacity and Mind Capacity paths. Add other SP bonuses only when a rule explicitly grants them. A class attribute of 0 or less adds 0 SP. Use your base value for the class’s casting attribute. Begin with your full reserve. Later maximum-SP gains enlarge capacity without restoring spent points. Safe overnight rest restores the reserve.

Capacity is 10 + 3 x Brawn. Every Bulk above capacity reduces Speed by 1. Speed is ancestry base + Agility - encumbrance, minimum 0. Stability starts at 0; use the highest explicit source, maximum 3. Matching resistance uses the highest value, maximum 3. Damage immunity removes its named damage before resistance.

Gain 1 Grit at level 1, 1 at level 3 and 1 at level 7. Each point can be used once and is gone permanently. Using Grit restores you to half maximum HP, rounded up. Record current HP/SP, first-aid availability, death tallies and scars separately. See [recovery](rules/recovery.md).

## Advancement

The DA awards milestone levels. From level 2 onward, gain **4 + the new level DP**, plus explicit trait benefits. Save points or spend them when the DA judges you are somewhere safe. Add the HP gain described above and **2 maximum SP**. Neither a level gain nor a signature change refreshes spent Grit.

| New level | DP gained | Other development |
|---|---:|---|
| 2 | 6 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 3 | 7 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP; advanced branch entries; signature choice; 1 Grit |
| 4 | 8 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 5 | 9 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 6 | 10 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 7 | 11 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP; masteries and master spells; signature choice; 1 Grit |
| 8 | 12 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 9 | 13 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 10 | 14 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |

Record what you learned and the DP you spent. At level 3 you can learn advanced entries, and at level 7 you can learn masteries. Follow each path’s order and pay the listed costs.
