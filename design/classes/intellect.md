# Intellect

*You see a pattern where others see only trouble, and trust your understanding enough to act.*

The ferry mechanism has seized. The boatman wants an axe. You ask him to pull the lever again while you watch the pin nobody has greased in twenty years.

An Intellect gives the company something it can act on. The same habit serves you in a ruin, at a workbench, or across from an opponent who always guards the same side. Spot the Leverage suits the investigator who looks for the detail that changes the plan. Make It Last can turn a plain lamp, tool, or shield into something the company will miss when it’s gone. An Intellect who chooses Angle of Attack brings the same patient eye to a fight.

You might like a workbench with room for somebody else’s tools, or dislike an answer that comes too easily. What did you once insist was impossible? Who had the pleasure of showing you how it worked?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Knowledge, Reason. **HP contribution:** +1 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Knowledge at creation: 2 × max(0, Knowledge).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Scholarship, Craft, Mentalism, Divination. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Craft, Scholarship. **2 DP:** Medicine, Mentalism, Divination, Force, Spellcraft. **3 DP:** Arms, Armor, Guard, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Influence, Elements, Life, Invocation, Wards.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one choice without spending DP. At level 3 or 7, deepen a signature you know or begin another line at that milestone. Each higher choice includes its line’s foundation. A level-7 choice includes its level-3 improvement only if you chose that improvement at level 3. Record one current version of each line, with its learned improvements together.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Review dependent choices together so each resulting line reflects the milestones you selected. Your class determines these choices; this does not grant a second class.
