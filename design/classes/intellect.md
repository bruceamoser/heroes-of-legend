# Intellect

*You see a pattern where others see only trouble, and trust your understanding enough to act.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Knowledge, Reason; HP contribution +1 at level 1 and per level thereafter. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct disciplines from Scholarship, Craft, Medicine, Mentalism, Divination.

**Learning costs:** Discipline access costs 1 DP for every class. Learned steps cost 1/2/2/3 DP; the last requires level 3. Combined abilities require level 7 and cost 3/4/5 DP for two/three/four disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one signature; additional signatures are not purchased separately. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Find leverage, connect evidence, or attack with Knowledge.
- **Level 3:** Direct an ally’s movement and attack, help an ally reroll a Weak result, or defend with Knowledge.
- **Level 7:** Route the party, test a plan with the DA, or expose a target for allies.


**Starting coin contribution:** +8 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
