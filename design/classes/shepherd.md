# Shepherd

*You listen for the wavering voice and help it find its way back to the others.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Reason, Guile; HP contribution +3. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Life, Radiance, Medicine, Wilderness.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Life, Radiance. Adjacent: Medicine, Wilderness. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Keep them standing with a heal, a steady voice, or a hand at the threshold.
- **Level 3:** Triage two allies, mend a wound, or raise the Fold.
- **Level 7:** Share life, answer with divine wrath, or mark the flock against the first fall.


**Starting coin contribution:** +3 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
