# Shepherd

*You listen for the wavering voice and help it find its way back to the others.*

At the end of a bad crossing, one traveler insists they can keep walking. You notice their hands won’t close around the cup. You find somewhere dry to sit and give them time to tell you what happened.

A Shepherd keeps people on their feet and helps them through trouble they cannot face alone. A punishing journey or a fight that leaves someone frightened and hurt gives you plenty to do. Mend makes care an immediate part of danger. Steady Voice suits a Shepherd who can reach a frightened companion through the noise. With Borrowed Shape, you might understand how different a familiar path feels on four feet.

You might love the quiet after a fever breaks, or resent the idea that the slowest traveler should be left behind. Your care may come from faith, a trade, a promise, or long practice. Who cared for you when you could give them nothing in return?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Reason, Guile. **HP contribution:** +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Reason at creation: 2 × max(0, Reason).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Life, Wards, Medicine, Wilderness. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Life, Wards. **2 DP:** Wilderness, Medicine, Invocation, Spellcraft. **3 DP:** Arms, Armor, Guard, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Elements, Mentalism, Divination, Force, Fencing, Tactics.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Choose a replacement from your class’s options for the same milestone, then apply any interactions between the signatures you know.
