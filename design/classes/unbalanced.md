# Unbalanced

*You meet the obstacle with everything you are, and let it discover whether that is enough.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Brawn, Fortitude; HP contribution +6. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Arms, Athletics, Armor, Storm.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Arms, Athletics. Adjacent: Armor, Storm. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Charge, rage, or hurl.
- **Level 3:** Rampage, turn pain into Boons, or throw an ally over the line.
- **Level 7:** Break the ground, refuse to fall, or launch yourself as a missile.


**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
