# Unbalanced

*You meet the obstacle with everything you are, and let it discover whether that is enough.*

The barricade has started to give. You hear the beam crack and know there is time for exactly one decision. The others can follow through if you make a gap wide enough.

An Unbalanced brings force to the point where it will matter most. In a crowded fight you can break up the knot around a companion. At a contested passage you can give the company a way through before the chance passes. Go Berserk calls up the fury to stay in a fight. Charge suits the runner who sees a lane through the confusion. Grab and Hurl lets you turn a struggle over footing into an abrupt change of position.

The force you bring can come from anger, joy, or a lifetime of knowing what your body can do. You might enjoy a fair contest and hate seeing strength used to humiliate someone. Who can talk you down when the fight is over?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Brawn, Fortitude. **HP contribution:** +3 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Knowledge at creation: 2 × max(0, Knowledge).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Arms, Athletics, Armor, Elements. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Arms, Athletics. **2 DP:** Armor, Elements. **3 DP:** Guard, Marksmanship, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Medicine, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Life, Mentalism, Divination, Invocation, Force, Wards, Spellcraft.

These prices begin study in an additional discipline. Each learned path or spellbook prints its own costs and requirements. Nonmagical paths cost 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP in sequence, with their fourth entry available at level 3 and seventh at level 7.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one choice without spending DP. At level 3 or 7, deepen a signature you know or begin another line at that milestone. Each higher choice includes its line’s foundation. A level-7 choice includes its level-3 improvement only if you chose that improvement at level 3. Record one current version of each line, with its learned improvements together.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Review dependent choices together so each resulting line reflects the milestones you selected. Your class determines these choices; this does not grant a second class.
