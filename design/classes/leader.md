# Leader

*You turn to your companions and give them a reason to move together.*

Two people reach for the same rope while nobody watches the stairs. You catch one person’s eye and point to the landing. A friend takes up the slack. By the time the threat appears, everyone has room to do their part.

A Leader makes the company’s separate efforts count together. A good signal can get everyone through a dangerous crossing or bring two companions into a fight at just the right moment. Follow My Signal can help two companions with a word, a gesture, or a scrap of song. Lead the Way suits someone willing to go through first. Stand Together is for the person others look to when they are close to breaking.

You might enjoy watching someone you trained take charge. Perhaps you dislike plans made without asking who will carry them out. What earns your trust? Whose judgment will you follow when your own plan falls apart?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Guile, Fortitude. **HP contribution:** +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Guile at creation: 2 × max(0, Guile).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Influence, Guard, Arms, Scholarship. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Guard, Influence. **2 DP:** Arms, Scholarship, Mentalism, Wards. **3 DP:** Armor, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Medicine, Craft, Elements, Life, Divination, Invocation, Force, Spellcraft.

These prices begin study in an additional discipline. Each learned path or spellbook prints its own costs and requirements. Nonmagical paths cost 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP in sequence, with their fourth entry available at level 3 and seventh at level 7.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one choice without spending DP. At level 3 or 7, deepen a signature you know or begin another line at that milestone. Each higher choice includes its line’s foundation. A level-7 choice includes its level-3 improvement only if you chose that improvement at level 3. Record one current version of each line, with its learned improvements together.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Review dependent choices together so each resulting line reflects the milestones you selected. Your class determines these choices; this does not grant a second class.
