# Blade

*You make the hard choices in the space between one stroke and the next.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Agility, Brawn; HP contribution +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter. No multiclassing.

**Starting SP contribution:** +3 maximum SP once, at level 1.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct disciplines from Arms, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge.

**Learning costs:** Discipline access costs 1 DP for every class. Learned steps cost 1/2/2/3 DP; the last requires level 3. Combined abilities require level 7 and cost 3/4/5 DP for two/three/four disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one signature; additional signatures are not purchased separately. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Charge into a melee attack, cleave adjacent creatures, or counterattack after a Strong defense.
- **Level 3:** Gain additional Reactions, counterattack after a Standard or Strong defense, or stop an entering enemy’s movement.
- **Level 7:** Strike a surrounding group, pursue and strike, or throw an adjacent creature.


**Starting coin contribution:** +4 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
