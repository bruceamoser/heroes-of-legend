# Blade

*You make the hard choices in the space between one stroke and the next.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Agility, Brawn; HP contribution +4. No multiclassing.

**Proposal:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Arms, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge.

**Proposed pricing:** Home: Arms, Marksmanship. Adjacent: Athletics, Subterfuge. All others Foreign; no Opposed assignment proposed yet.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Flow through melee, hinder a target, or guard and reposition.
- **Level 3:** Enter decisively, counterattack, or take an opponent's footing.
- **Level 7:** Strike a surrounding group, pursue and strike, or dictate where the duel moves.

See the [full signature review](../reviews/1153-signature-review.md) for comparisons
and remaining playtest questions. Individual effects remain proposals.

**Starting coin contribution:** +4 coins (proposed allocation). See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
