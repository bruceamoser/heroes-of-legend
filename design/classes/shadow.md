# Shadow

*You learn the unguarded routes through a world that rarely looks in the right direction.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Agility, Guile; HP contribution +3. No multiclassing.

**Proposal:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Subterfuge, Marksmanship, Mind, Athletics.

**Proposed pricing:** Home: Subterfuge, Marksmanship. Adjacent: Mind, Athletics. All others Foreign; no Opposed assignment proposed yet.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Slip away, land quietly, or cover an ally's passage.
- **Level 3:** Cross difficult ground, strike and leave, or open a lock quickly.
- **Level 7:** Shroud an escape, exploit a companion's disabling effect, or hide a party's trail.

See the [full signature review](../reviews/1153-signature-review.md) for comparisons
and remaining playtest questions. Individual effects remain proposals.
