# Shadow

*You learn the unguarded routes through a world that rarely looks in the right direction.*

The steward keeps the keys on a silver chain. Everyone else is watching the dancers. You are watching the steward’s face each time someone mentions the west gallery.

A Shadow finds opportunities that close as soon as everyone notices them. You shine when the company needs a way in, an opening in a fight, or a conversation that buys somebody else time to act. Fade Behind puts you on the side of the room no one is watching. Blade in the Back rewards the moment an enemy’s attention is elsewhere. Fast Talk gives you something to do when you are the person everyone has turned to look at.

You might love a crowded festival or the privacy of a roof above an empty street. Perhaps you cannot stand a person who confuses discretion with disloyalty. Who knows your real name, and what did they do to earn it?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Agility, Guile. **HP contribution:** +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Guile at creation: 2 × max(0, Guile).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Subterfuge, Marksmanship, Fencing, Athletics. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Marksmanship, Subterfuge. **2 DP:** Athletics, Mentalism, Divination, Fencing. **3 DP:** Arms, Armor, Guard, Wilderness, Medicine, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Elements, Life, Invocation, Force, Wards, Spellcraft, Tactics.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Choose a replacement from your class’s options for the same milestone, then apply any interactions between the signatures you know.
