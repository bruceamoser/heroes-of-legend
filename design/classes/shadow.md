# Shadow

*You learn the unguarded routes through a world that rarely looks in the right direction.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Agility, Guile; HP contribution +3. No multiclassing.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct rank-1 disciplines from Subterfuge, Marksmanship, Mind, Athletics.

**Discipline pricing:** Home: Subterfuge, Marksmanship. Adjacent: Mind, Athletics. All others Foreign; no Opposed disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Extra signatures cost 2/4/8 DP. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Start with a bleed, a vanish, or a lie.
- **Level 3:** Cripple a target, open the way, or read the room.
- **Level 7:** Interrupt an action, become a ghost, or cut with a word.


**Starting coin contribution:** +3 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
