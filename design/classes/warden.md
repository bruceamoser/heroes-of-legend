# Warden

*You read the broken fern, the distant cry and the turn of a fleeing creature. When the road ends, your companions look to you.*

The deer have crossed the stream and circled back along the bank. The people you are following went straight across. You take the company upstream to a shallower crossing and ask them to keep their eyes on the opposite shore.

A Warden reads the land and makes it useful to the people traveling with them. In a pursuit, the ground tells you where to hurry and where to wait. On an expedition, the same eye can find the trail that gets everyone to shelter before dark. Steady Aim suits the hunter who waits for a clear shot. Beast Speech makes the creatures you meet part of the expedition’s story. Twin Strike gives a Warden with two blades a reason to close the distance.

You might love a familiar stretch of river in flood or resent a traveler who leaves trouble for whoever comes next. What place do you know well enough to miss when it changes?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Fortitude, Knowledge. **HP contribution:** +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Knowledge at creation: 2 × max(0, Knowledge).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Wilderness, Marksmanship, Arms, Athletics. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Marksmanship, Wilderness. **2 DP:** Arms, Athletics, Life, Divination. **3 DP:** Armor, Guard, Subterfuge, Medicine, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Elements, Mentalism, Invocation, Force, Wards, Spellcraft.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Choose a replacement from your class’s options for the same milestone, then apply any interactions between the signatures you know.
