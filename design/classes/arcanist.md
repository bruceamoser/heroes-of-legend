# Arcanist

*You study the impossible until your hands can give it a shape the world must briefly accept.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** minimum 0 in Agility, Knowledge; HP contribution +1 at level 1 and per level thereafter. No multiclassing.

**Starting SP contribution:** +3 maximum SP once, at level 1.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct disciplines from Elements, Force, Invocation, Scholarship, Spellcraft.

**Learning costs:** Discipline access costs 1 DP for every class. Learned steps cost 1/2/2/3 DP; the last requires level 3. Combined abilities require level 7 and cost 3/4/5 DP for two/three/four disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one signature; additional signatures are not purchased separately. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Bend a spell’s origin, push creatures with a corner spark, or read ongoing magic.
- **Level 3:** Vanish after casting, open linked windows for an ally, or convert spell damage into a push.
- **Level 7:** Wear a magical aegis, cast a spell twice, or ward a circle.


**Starting coin contribution:** +6 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
