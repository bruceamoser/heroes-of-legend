# Arcanist

*You study the impossible until your hands can give it a shape the world must briefly accept.*

A thread of ash climbs the stair against the draft. You hold up a hand and the company stops. Someone has worked magic here. The ash is going to show you where they stood.

An Arcanist asks what a spell can do in this particular room, with these people in it. An elemental working might change the ground under a fight. A careful ritual might be the answer to a door the company would rather leave intact. Bent Trajectory lets an attack come from an unexpected angle. Study the Pattern suits a reader of workings, and Deliberate Spark is for the caster who sees an advantage in moving friends and foes a little farther apart.

You might savor a clean diagram or love the apprentice who asks the awkward question. What magical shortcut do you refuse to take? What impossible thing would make you put your notes down and try it anyway?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Agility, Knowledge. **HP contribution:** +1 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Casting attribute:** Knowledge.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Knowledge at creation: 2 × max(0, Knowledge).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Elements, Force, Invocation, Spellcraft. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Elements, Force, Spellcraft. **2 DP:** Scholarship, Divination, Invocation, Wards. **3 DP:** Arms, Armor, Guard, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Medicine, Craft, Influence, Life, Mentalism, Fencing, Tactics.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Choose a replacement from your class’s options for the same milestone, then apply any interactions between the signatures you know.
