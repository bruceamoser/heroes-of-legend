# Odd

*You have never fit the expected answer, and have learned to make that useful.*

## Rules

**Agreed:** no attribute minimums; HP contribution +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter. No multiclassing.

**Starting SP contribution:** +3 maximum SP once, at level 1.

**Starting disciplines:** choose two distinct disciplines from Arms, Marksmanship, Armor, Guard, Athletics, Subterfuge, Influence, Scholarship, Wilderness, Craft, Medicine, Elements, Life, Wards, Mentalism, Invocation, Force, Divination, Spellcraft.

**Learning costs:** Discipline access costs 1 DP for every class. Learned steps cost 1/2/2/3 DP; the last requires level 3. Combined abilities require level 7 and cost 3/4/5 DP for two/three/four disciplines.

Choose one class-exclusive signature at levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one signature; additional signatures are not purchased separately. No discipline requirements.

## Signature identity in play

The revised options aim for these moments, with a deliberate advantage over shared
cards of their tier. Each is an independent choice, not a progression chain.

- **Level 1:** Speak a language through a spirit, distract with a borrowed voice, or blind an enemy with pocket sand.
- **Level 3:** Release a poisonous wind, open a passage through a barrier, or project your spirit from your body.
- **Level 7:** Borrow another’s expertise, fill the field with spectral witnesses, or delay a companion’s death long enough for a rescue.

**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
