# Odd

*You have never fit the expected answer, and have learned to make that useful.*

A stranger at the ferry calls out in a language nobody in the company knows. You recognize the voice of the spirit at your shoulder. There is just enough time to decide whether to invite it in.

An Odd brings possibilities the company might never think to ask for. When the company reaches for a crowbar, you may be the one who asks whether the door has a name. Borrowed Tongue can turn a stranger’s warning into a conversation. Use Their Voice draws an enemy’s eye away from a companion. The Bargain suits an Odd whose power comes with a relationship worth playing out at the table.

You might collect the names of forgotten things, prefer a door with a story to one with a lock, or be the most practical person in a room full of omens. What makes the uncanny feel ordinary to you? What bargain would you walk away from, even if nobody understood why?

## Rules

**Attributes:** No attribute minimums. **HP contribution:** +2 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Guile at creation: 2 × max(0, Guile).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Athletics, Subterfuge, Influence, Spellcraft. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **2 DP:** Arms, Armor, Guard, Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Medicine, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Elements, Life, Mentalism, Divination, Invocation, Force, Wards, Spellcraft.

Pay the listed price to begin studying another discipline. Then follow the costs and requirements of the path or spellbook you choose.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7 without spending DP. Choose from your class’s options for that level. Each signature is an ability you can use in its own right. Some offer an additional benefit when you also know another named signature; that benefit appears in the ability’s rules.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Choose a replacement from your class’s options for the same milestone, then apply any interactions between the signatures you know.
