# Protector

*You see who needs shelter and step into the space between them and harm.*

The door shakes in its frame. Behind you, the apothecary is trying to get two frightened children out a window. You brace the latch with your shoulder and ask, very calmly, how much longer they need.

A Protector makes a company brave enough to take risks. You read a room for the person in danger and a battlefield for the ground you can actually hold. Step Between suits the guardian who stays within reach of their companions. Healing Hands carries that care past the moment of injury. An oathkeeper might choose Oathstrike and make an enemy answer for hurting someone they swore to defend.

You might take pleasure in a good watch roster, a repaired strap, or the moment someone trusts you enough to rest. What would make you lower your shield? Who taught you that asking for help can be part of keeping people safe?

## Rules

**Attributes:** Minimum 0 in Fortitude, Reason. **HP contribution:** +3 at level 1 and per level thereafter.

**Starting SP contribution:** Twice your positive Reason at creation: 2 × max(0, Reason).

**Starting disciplines:** Choose one of Armor, Guard, Arms, Medicine. Your culture and background each contribute one other starting discipline. Choose three distinct disciplines across these selections.

**Learning costs:** **1 DP:** Armor, Guard. **2 DP:** Arms, Medicine, Wards. **3 DP:** Marksmanship, Athletics, Subterfuge, Wilderness, Craft, Scholarship, Influence, Elements, Life, Mentalism, Divination, Invocation, Force, Spellcraft.

These prices begin study in an additional discipline. Each learned path or spellbook prints its own costs and requirements. Nonmagical paths cost 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP in sequence, with their fourth entry available at level 3 and seventh at level 7.

## Signature development

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7. Each milestone grants one choice without spending DP. At level 3 or 7, deepen a signature you know or begin another line at that milestone. Each higher choice includes its line’s foundation. A level-7 choice includes its level-3 improvement only if you chose that improvement at level 3. Record one current version of each line, with its learned improvements together.

You may switch signatures under the ordinary retraining rules. Review dependent choices together so each resulting line reflects the milestones you selected. Your class determines these choices; this does not grant a second class.
