## Meaning of this milestone

This is the first authoring pass and reproducible budget analysis. The decision contract records settled direction; the detailed candidate mechanics identify how it might be realized. The assembled books and Workshop catalog still use the existing published rules. Do not import these planning tables as playable cards or infer approval of every numerical proposal from approval of the architecture.

The next content milestone must turn the selected candidates into complete authored traits, signature profiles, discipline branches and spell blocks, then reconcile both manuscripts and layouts. The 319-spell duration/control review and the exact seven-step page layouts remain work, not completed deliverables. Preserve the original Build-Offs as the before-revision comparison and use explicitly revised fixtures for later tests.

## Review emphasis

- A drawback should arise naturally and matter during play without making a modest trait an unattractive trap. Several first-pass time costs need comparison against ordinary careful work; avoid turning most ancestry use into waiting one minute.
- Signature strength means a defining capability or valuable action choice. Proposed reductions to older signatures are candidates to test, not automatic corrections. Healing Hands uses a Maneuver in the candidate so it offers something distinct from ranged Main Action healing.
- Mastery should reward depth while class-priced entry leaves genuine room for breadth. Numerical affordability does not prove that any offered capability is attractive.
- Noncombat reactions need observable triggers and activity-based usage limits. Their text should not require counting six-second rounds through a negotiation or long project.
- Moonbeam and similar hazards retain intentional advantages from corridors, blocking and forced movement. Control spells give explicit player-facing escape attributes and no NPC saving rolls.
