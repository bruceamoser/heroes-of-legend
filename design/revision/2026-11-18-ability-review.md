# November 18 ability procedure review

Bruce approved 10 base DP at level 1. Add signed Guile, Knowledge and Reason and any explicit trait bonus. Later awards remain 4 + the new level. The six worked heroes keep their purchases and show the extra two points available.

Reviewed the 385 current nonmagical path and Spellcraft training entries, 319 spells, 90 class signatures and 19 universal actions for prerequisites, operative effects and outcome bands. Stable identities, printed costs, learning prerequisites and level gates were preserved. The review concerns player procedures. Creature encounter balance remains in its playtest.

## Ordinary opportunities to use a technique

- Counterexample I works when you help a listener assess a claim. No need to have contradicting evidence in hand. The range, relevant exchange, comprehension and one-use trigger still govern it.
- Open under Pressure and Field Repair can start when the hero has access and suitable tools. A preliminary roll from a different step adds nothing to these procedures.
- Gentle Hand works with receptive and wary animals, with time accounting for the latter. Watchful Shot responds to visible voluntary movement in range. Its later training adds forced movement.
- Locate Creature uses either familiarity or a personal possession as its connection. Long Door requires a destination you know from visiting it. The spell can use any such visit.

## When an effect defines the technique

- Driving Opening I pushes 1 square on Standard and 2 on Strong. Its second entry pushes 2 on either qualifying result. Battering Stroke improves reliability with Push 2 on Weak too and still adds Dazed on Strong.
- Hobbling Aim's slow, Shield Bash's push, Exploit the Opening's ally movement, and Fencing's Bind the Blade and Strip the Grip apply on Standard and Strong. Existing later entries now improve rating, reach, placement or reliability.
- Sleep and Hold Person inflict their named disabling conditions on Standard and Strong. Flesh to Stone retains a prior casting that still Slows the target before a Standard or Strong casting petrifies it. Banishment’s willing extraplanar return applies on Standard and Strong.
- Measured Cut’s Bleeding, Sweeping Blade’s additional push, Ray of Sickness’s Poisoned, and Healing Hands’ extra treatment remain Strong bonuses. The normal attack or healing procedure already works across the ordinary bands.

Communication, accessible targets, legal movement, proper equipment, consent for beneficial or consensual effects, reaction windows and meaningful limits still apply. For example, Catch the Click requires a trap you can notice, while moving its actual mechanism also calls for reach and tools. Hunt at the Crossroads follows its existing ambush ruling.
