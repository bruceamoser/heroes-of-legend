# Seven-section spellbooks — November 16, 2026

Authority: Bruce’s correction that magical paths should follow the martial construction, followed by his clarification that **each 4 / 4 / 5 DP purchase teaches one spell**. Tracked in [Workshop #310](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend-foundry/issues/310) and [book #1332](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1332).

## Current contract

Every one of the 29 spellbooks, including Channeling, follows seven sequential steps. Costs are 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP; gates are 1/1/1/3/3/3/7. The first four sections each teach two spells, and the final three each teach one spell. Eleven spells remain in each book. A complete path costs 21 DP after the unchanged class-priced discipline entry.

The three initial circles, three level-3 triangles and final level-7 diamond are the same construction as martial paths. The content remains a themed spell list; magical branches do not inherit Main/Maneuver/Reaction directional assignments. No independent mastery fan or intersection purchase remains. Sections V and VI develop advanced magic; VII supplies the book’s strongest culmination.

## Content and source treatment

The prior fourth section taught five spells at once. Its advanced spells now have individual learning steps. The uneven separate mastery folios are reconciled with the final three slots of their own books. The attached audits account for retained, strengthened, replaced and retired spells; similar themes are not claimed to be identical mechanics. Stable spell IDs survive when identity survives, including a spell moved to another numbered position. A replacement retains its own identity.

- [Elements, Life, Mentalism and Divination audit](spell-progression-310-north.md).
- [Invocation, Force, Wards and Spellcraft audit](spell-progression-310-south.md).

The old independent purchases return to available DP in the Workshop. The old section-IV purchase also returns for relearning because its grant changed. Existing first-three-section learning, gear and live resources remain; no migration grants a replacement spell. This transition applies once, and later saves retain newly learned sections. Revised Build-Offs use the new sequence; their historical baseline remains unchanged.

## Presentation and acceptance

Each book retains one facing two-page spread, with its full colored spell list and eleven complete blocks, discipline tokens and unique existing artwork. Seven section colors and explicit level labels identify the new learning order. General prices are taught once in the magic chapter. The DA advancement reference and character-sheet reference use the same construction.

Source audits, both compiled PDFs with visual inspection, exact source imports, construction fixtures and browser learning/save tests establish consistency. These checks do not establish encounter balance. Native Foundry source pins and packs remain unchanged until its separate integration phase.
