# #299 discipline content allocation — first pass

This is a proposed content allocation for review, not an approved catalog or a change to either book. It applies the approved seven-step nonmagical progression and removal of all inter-discipline intersections. Exact new effects, ranges, costs, and limits still need card authoring and playtesting. Existing names below identify source material to preserve or deliberately rewrite; a destination does not authorize copying its old timing unchanged.

Sources: [current disciplines](../disciplines/), [combined abilities](../combined-abilities.md), [revision contract](rewrite-299-contract.md), [signature allocation](rewrite-299-signature-plan.md). Use stable source IDs during implementation even when names or homes change.

## Reading the allocation

North offers a Main Action; west a Maneuver; east a Reaction with an observable trigger; south is the discipline's wildcard. Each row has seven purchases: the initial capability, two improvements, a major development at level 3, two improvements to that development, and mastery at level 7. The tables name steps 1, 4 and 7; they deliberately do not invent four intermediate buttons. Improvements should strengthen those particular capabilities rather than add unrelated actions.

For noncombat work, action type does not replace elapsed work. Inspecting the clue in your hand can be a Main Action; following a trail still takes time. A field brace can support an object immediately; rebuilding it still needs tools, materials and work. A brief appeal can fit an action; a negotiated treaty remains an extended encounter. Every final card must state these distinctions.

A Reaction outside combat must identify an event, what the character already knows or can observe, the intervention before resolution, and a limit tied to that event, recipient, task or journey. Passing six seconds is never a fresh opportunity to repeat it. None of these proposals creates automatic clues, universal language, willing negotiators, ingredients or safe routes.

### Arms

The current blanket rule that Arms attacks require a melee weapon must change for the unarmed route. A free hand for an opportunity attack remains a universal rule; this route makes deliberate unarmed fighting a developed role. It is available to any class through Arms, not a Monk-only signature. Nonlethal intent remains declared before rolling, not chosen after seeing damage.

## Magical books and Spellcraft

**Proposed authoring decision:** attach a clearly labeled level-7 advanced spell entry to the end of each relevant book, reached through that book alone. Its learned spells can include the relocated abilities above. This is a book extension, not a fifth universal section assigned by symmetry and not an intersection. Final names, costs and bundle sizes require review. Four-section book learning stays distinct from seven-step martial development.

Preserve the existing four-section book progression, its 1/2/2/3 DP prices, and Section IV at level 3 teaching all its current spells, including its three advanced spells. The confirmed level-7 gate applies to the former combined spells relocated above; their proposed single-book attachments do not move existing Section IV spells. Review any concern about the power of a current Section IV spell as a separate, visible content decision rather than silently adding a gate. Keep lists, spell blocks and learned-spell exports consistent when approved attachments are implemented.

Restoration and Passage acquire several advanced candidates. Do not give each every relocated spell for the price of a single ordinary section by accident. The authoring pass must decide a coherent learned bundle and its DP cost, or multiple individually priced advanced spell entries attached to that same book. This draft proposes homes, not automatic free grants. The latter remains an alternative for approval, not an implementation instruction.

Spellcraft is explicitly exceptional:

- Soul Capacity: training branch, seven-step nonmagical-style progression, develops SP capacity. Its benefit need not pretend to be a Main Action.
- Mind Capacity: separate seven-step capacity training branch, providing a second deliberate investment route; avoid merely duplicating the same fictional training or stacking ambiguously.
- Recovery: seven-step training branch, develops recovery through real rest and relaxation. It preserves recovery frequency and time requirements rather than creating a repeatable combat refill.
- Channeling: spellbook, with spell sections and a level-7 advanced attachment if needed. Sending or taking SP uses explicit willing/unwilling targets, rolls, limits and action timing. Preserve the no-channeling-a-channeling-spell rule and arcane-feedback explanation.

Spellcraft therefore does **not** satisfy the north-Main/west-Maneuver/east-Reaction/south-wildcard pattern literally. Its three training paths plus one book are an intentional exception; show their actual benefit and timing in both atlas and list view. Neither a capacity purchase nor a newly learned channeling spell is itself an automatic SP refill.

## Authoring checks before replacing live content

1. Write every step's actual mechanic, rather than shipping these concept labels as rules. Record its source ID, action/casting type, attributes, equipment, conditions, uses and rest limits.
2. When moving a Main Action into a Maneuver lane, budget the complete turn. A setup can improve the next attack; it must not quietly duplicate a full attack while leaving the Main Action untouched.
3. East branches need genuinely observable triggers and limits. A noncombat Reaction does not turn repeated statements, warnings or failed work into unlimited progress.
4. Keep all old-path dispositions explicit, especially Arms Disruption, Armor Harness, Wilderness Shelter, Medicine Triage, Craft Construction and Scholarship Lore. Their mergers make room for new functionality; they are not permission to lose their useful mechanics.
5. Preserve universal actions, attacks dealing at least 1 after resistance except immunity, non-attack damage reaching 0, ordinary collision, real inventory, first-aid recipient limits, descriptive scars and lifetime Grit.
6. Review each relocated spell's fixed/directed/Sustained duration and initial/escape attribute independently. Magical flavor alone does not decide whether an effect requires Sustain.
7. Recheck the full 13 Build-Offs after implementation, particularly shared unarmed access, frontline armor progression, magical weapon builds and noncombat reactions. A legal seven-step graph does not establish that the resulting character is useful or fun.
