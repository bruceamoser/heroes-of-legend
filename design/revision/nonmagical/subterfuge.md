# Subterfuge
Work between what people notice and what they assume.

Ordinary stealth, deception and lock manipulation remain possible. Concealment requires a plausible hiding place; none of these abilities makes you invisible. Tools and props must be carried.

## Stealth

### Quiet Passage I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Agility rolls to move quietly while concealed. You cannot hide in plain sight without a distraction or cover.

### Quiet Passage II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Quiet Passage removes extra movement cost imposed solely by sneaking.

### Quiet Passage III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Quiet Passage extends its Boon to one willing adjacent companion moving with you and using the same concealment.

### Vanish into Cover
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Agility. Move up to 2 squares into cover, then add 1/2/3 progress toward evading pursuers. The DA sets the objective; become Hidden when it is completed and concealment remains plausible.

## Locks

### Patient Hand I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Guile, with lock tools. Work on one reachable nonmagical lock or mechanism; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s opening objective. Basic tools cannot defeat an impossible mechanism.

### Patient Hand II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Hand adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Patient Hand III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Hand takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Open under Pressure
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Guile, with lock tools. Work on a nonmagical lock already examined with Patient Hand; add 1/2/3 progress. Until this lock opens, you may repeat this faster action.

## Sleight

### Light Fingers I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Guile. With a free hand, palm or plant one unattended palm-sized object within reach; add 1/2/3 progress toward doing so unnoticed. The DA sets the objective and decides whether scrutiny allows the attempt.

### Light Fingers II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Light Fingers adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Light Fingers III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Light Fingers can also target an accessible object worn or carried by a distracted creature; not a held weapon, secured container or worn armor.

### Switch the Prize
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Guile. Using Light Fingers, exchange a reachable palm-sized object with a similar object you carry as one task. Use its improved progress; the exchange occurs when the objective is completed.

## Traps

### Read the Snare I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 minute · Reason. Inspect a reachable suspected trap without touching its trigger; add 1/2/3 progress to identify the trigger and danger. Inspection cannot reveal inaccessible components.

### Read the Snare II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Snare adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Read the Snare III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
After Read the Snare identifies a trap, gain 1 Boon on your Guile rolls to disarm that trap with suitable tools.

### Turn the Trap
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes · Guile, with tools. Reconfigure an identified nonmagical trap; add 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s objective. On completion, choose a reachable trigger position or safe bypass. Its damage and scale do not increase.
