# Guard
Be where someone needs you before they have to ask.

These abilities require a held shield. They grant no extra reactions. Damage prevention always leaves at least 1 damage from each attack.

## Interception

### Interpose I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Reaction. After an adjacent ally rolls defense, reduce the physical damage of that attack by 1, minimum 1. You must be able to reach the ally.

### Interpose II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Interpose reduces physical damage by 2 instead of 1.

### Interpose III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Before Interpose, you may move 1 square to reach the ally. This movement does not trigger reactions.

### Take the Blow
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. Before an adjacent ally rolls defense against a single-target attack, become its target. You roll your own defense and receive all effects; the ally receives none.

## Shieldwork

### Shield Bash I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 1/2/3 physical damage. On Strong, push it 1 square directly away.

### Shield Bash II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Shield Bash deals 2/3/4 physical damage instead of 1/2/3.

### Shield Bash III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Shield Bash also knocks the target Prone on Strong after the push.

### Break the Line
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Attack up to two adjacent creatures. Roll once; each takes 1/2/3 physical damage and is pushed 1 square directly away.

## Shelter

### Sheltering Guard I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Maneuver. Choose one adjacent ally. Until your next turn starts, it gains 1 Boon on ranged defense rolls while adjacent to you. Reusing this ability replaces its previous effect.

### Sheltering Guard II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Sheltering Guard can protect two adjacent allies instead of one.

### Sheltering Guard III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Sheltering Guard also gives its protected allies Stability 1 while adjacent to you.

### Shield Canopy
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action. Until your next turn starts, you and up to three adjacent allies reduce damage from ranged attacks by 2, minimum 1. An ally loses this benefit when no longer adjacent.

## Watchfulness

### Guarded Passage I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Reaction. When an adjacent ally triggers a movement reaction, impose 1 Boon on its defense against that reaction. Declare before the defense roll.

### Guarded Passage II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Guarded Passage gives 2 Boons instead of 1 to that defense.

### Guarded Passage III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Guarded Passage also reduces damage from that reaction by 1, minimum 1.

### Rescue Step
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Move up to 2 squares to an ally, then move with that willing ally up to 2 more squares. Both movements ignore movement reactions. Finish adjacent; both must have a legal route.
