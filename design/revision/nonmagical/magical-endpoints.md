# Magical endpoint references

These references preserve the spell-list endpoints in the Magic Forge catalog. Each requires its preceding three learned sections and character level 3. Learning the endpoint teaches pages 7–8 and all three advanced pages immediately. The combined abilities require the endpoint, not merely the discipline or an earlier page.

## Heart of the Furnace
ID: flame:3
Discipline: Elements
List: Flame
Pages 7–8: Fireball; Fire Shield
Advanced pages: Fire Storm; Delayed Blast Fireball; Incendiary Cloud

## Voice of the Tempest
