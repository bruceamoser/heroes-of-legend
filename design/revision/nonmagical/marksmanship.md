# Marksmanship
Find the line between your hand and a distant mark.

Your basic ranged attack is already available. Each attack requires a ranged weapon and ammunition, or a thrown weapon. Targets must be visible and within range. A thrown weapon stays where it lands.

## Precision

### Measured Shot I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Agility. Attack one creature within 8 squares for 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Measured Shot II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Measured Shot deals +1 damage on every result: 3/4/6.

### Measured Shot III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Measured Shot has range 12 squares instead of 8.

### Split the Flight
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Attack up to three creatures within 8 squares. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. Spend one projectile per target. Measured Shot improvements do not apply.

## Pinning

### Hobbling Shot I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Agility. Attack one creature within 8 squares for 1/2/4 physical damage. On Strong, apply Slowed 1.

### Hobbling Shot II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Hobbling Shot applies Slowed 2 instead of 1 on Strong.

### Hobbling Shot III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Hobbling Shot also applies Slowed 1 on Standard; Strong still applies Slowed 2.

### Hold the Pass
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Attack up to two creatures within 8 squares. Roll once; each takes 1/2/3 physical damage and Slowed 1. Spend one projectile per target.

## Readiness

### Watchful Shot I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Reaction. When a visible enemy within 6 squares finishes voluntary movement, attack it with Agility for 1/2/3 physical damage. Resolve after it moves; you need a ready weapon.

### Watchful Shot II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Watchful Shot has range 8 squares instead of 6.

### Watchful Shot III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Watchful Shot deals 1/2/4 physical damage instead of 1/2/3.

### Covering Fire
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Attack one enemy within 8 squares for 1/2/4 physical damage. One ally within 4 squares of you may immediately move 2 squares without triggering reactions from that enemy.

## Thrown Weapons

### Hurl I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Agility. Throw one held weapon at a creature within 4 squares for 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Hurl II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Hurl has range 6 squares instead of 4.

### Hurl III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
After Hurl, draw one accessible replacement weapon without spending an action. This does not retrieve the thrown weapon.

### Ricochet
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Throw one weapon at two creatures within 4 squares of you and 2 squares of each other. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. The weapon lands beside the second target.
