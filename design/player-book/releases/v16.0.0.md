# Heroes of Legend v16.0.0 — Player’s Book

The rebuilt Heroes of Legend system now has a complete, illustrated Player’s Book
for levels 1–10. This major release replaces the previous book as the player-facing
reference while retaining the **Playtest Edition** designation.

## Inside the book

- Character creation and advancement, core resolution, combat, exploration,
  equipment, crafting, healing, conditions and wound recovery.
- 4 ancestries, 18 cultures, 32 backgrounds, 10 classes and 18 disciplines.
- 350 abilities, including class signatures, with complete rules and outcomes.
- 43 equipment entries, 10 recipes, 73 summoned companion profiles and 280 wound results.
- Four fantasy character paths, six fully worked heroes and four printable sheets.
- Class discipline-cost tables, a complete class comparison matrix, ability browsing
  tables and separate Universal, Novice, Adept and Master indexes.
- The approved cover, forest-and-gold card styling, transparent class portraits and
  130 unique illustration placements.

## Edition scope

This is the player book. It includes summon profiles needed for player abilities;
the complete 192-creature bestiary and encounter construction belong to the separate
Dungeon Architect book. The previous Quarto edition and v15.1 PDF are historical.

This remains a digital playtest release, not a final print-production edition or a
claim of completed balance testing. The book visibly identifies provisional rulings;
`design/player-book/decision-log.md` records their scope. The version number follows
the archived v15.1 playtest; previous GitHub drafts used date-based tags.
