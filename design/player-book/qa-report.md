18 universal actions and 242 shared abilities), 4 ancestries, 20 ancestry traits,

ability index's name/page links. Typst compilation resolves the document labels.

## Review boundaries

This is author QA, not independent approval or a new balance simulation. The PDF is
a digital playtest proof, not a final print production file. Interior art is deliberately
represented by dimensioned briefs. Publication notices, final art/bleed/color and
professional copy editing remain part of release production.

`decision-log.md` records new conflicts and gaps. Two provisional rules are explicitly
boxed in the book: wound-table selection after minimum damage from fully resisted
components, and legal placement of crowded forced-movement groups. Numerical weakness
arithmetic and large-summon tether measurement still require explicit DA agreement.
The contradictory offensive-healing paragraph is excluded from the potion entry;
actual spell-healing rules are retained. No withdrawn proposal is revived.
