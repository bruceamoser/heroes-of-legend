# Current rules cross-reference

The approved decisions are incorporated in source and teaching text. This list is
navigation, not a competing set of provisional rulings.

- [Damage and weakness](../rules/foundations.md): highest matching resistance and weakness, normal caps, event minimum.
- [Scars](../rules/wounds.md): descriptive reminders of surviving at 0 HP; no rolls or mechanical penalties.
- [Stability](../rules/stability.md): collision timing and legal moving-group placement.
- [Summoning](../rules/summoning.md): energy forms, no received healing, controller-paid healing and nearest-square tether.
- [Session resolution](../rules/session-resolution.md): NPC-only rolls, interruption, perception and movement.
- [Languages](../rules/languages.md): starting choices, explicit grants and communication.
- [Learning](../rules/learning.md): sequential paths and ordinary actions.
- [Objectives](../rules/objectives.md): shared progress, work intervals and social encounters.
- [Development](../development.md): level-1 creation, advancement and automatic SP growth.
- [Masteries](../combined-abilities.md): level-7 disciplines and spellbook reference.
- [Recovery](../rules/recovery.md): Grit, Dying, scars, overnight rest and first aid.

New design questions belong in a new issue and require Bruce’s review. Encounter
calibration remains playtest work; see [further work](../open-decisions.md).

## November 16 rewrite

Creation now uses one level-1 process, three distinct starting disciplines and 8 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason DP. Later awards are 4 + the new level. Nonmagical seven-step paths cost 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP with gates 1/1/1/3/3/3/7; spellbooks now follow the same seven-step prices and gates under the November 16 correction in [#310](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend-foundry/issues/310). Sections I–IV teach two spells each; V–VII teach one each. Class-priced additional disciplines, signature development, revised ancestry traits and explicit spell-control attributes are implemented in the canonical sources. See [rewrite record](../revision/rewrite-299.md).

## November 18 starting DP and ability review

Start at 10 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason DP plus explicit trait benefits. Later awards are 4 + the new level. Core effects such as Driving Opening’s push and Sleep’s sleep condition work on Standard and Strong. Extra riders can stay Strong-only. See the [ability review](../revision/2026-11-18-ability-review.md).
