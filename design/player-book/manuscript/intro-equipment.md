# Equipment entries

These entries describe the things you can carry, buy, find and use. Each gives its
Bulk, price and any special requirements or effects. Record the actual item once in
your inventory, even when several learned abilities use it. Buying equipment with
coins does not buy training or a discipline rank.

Ordinary weapon appearance is yours to describe; your attack card supplies its combat
mechanics. Special equipment adds only the effects printed here. For consumables,
record the current inventory die and check it after the stated use. Keep frequently
used items and occupied hands visible on your character sheet.
