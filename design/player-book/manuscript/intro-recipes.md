# Recipes

A recipe is the knowledge of how to make a particular thing. It tells you which
knowledge or technique permits the work, what tools and materials you need, how long an
attempt takes, and what the result produces. Gather its ingredients and use the technique described in the recipe.

Read the entry before beginning. A project may accumulate progress toward a completion
target; a supply recipe may instead raise an existing inventory die. Follow the printed
outcomes and limits. Starting crafting abilities provide their stated recipe choices;
you can learn further compatible recipes through the appropriate ability or discover
them during play.

**Permission** tells you how to learn or carry out the work. Trail Workshop teaches its named recipe choices. Other formulas can be learned from teachers or discovery; gather their materials and equipment before beginning.

For every recipe, roll the crafting ability’s printed attribute at the end of an
attempt. Work alone; there is no group crafting. Accumulate project progress until
you meet the completion target. For supplies, the first inventory step from empty
creates d4; later steps are d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20, up to the recipe’s cap.
Check any carried material supply after each attempt. You must actually obtain the
ingredients; prepare the finished stock through crafting. Recipes remain known for reuse.
The plants and mixtures here are fictional game ingredients.
