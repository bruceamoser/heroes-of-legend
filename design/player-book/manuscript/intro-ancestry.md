# Ancestry, culture and background

Choose the people, places and experiences that shaped you. Choose two ancestry traits, then one complete culture pair and one complete background pair. Any ancestry may come from any culture. An ancestry’s familiar temperament describes a tendency, never an obligation or an automatic attribute modifier.

Your ancestry supplies inherited traits. Culture describes a community and its traditions; background recalls something you did or experienced before adventuring. These choices offer connections, memories and capabilities. Only a trait’s printed rules grant mechanical benefits. Base-statistic changes are permanent; other benefits last while their stated circumstances apply.

## Stature and space

For ancestry comparisons, Gnomes, Halflings and Goblins are Small; Humans, both Elves, both Dwarves, Tallfolk and Oath-Bound are Medium. Gnomes are physically smaller than halflings within the same category. Tallfolk remain Medium despite their height. A Large creature is one explicitly described as Large by its own entry. These labels provide the comparison used by Under Their Noses; they grant no automatic changes to damage, reach, Speed, Bulk capacity or equipment rules. Use physical description to decide whether a body can fit through an opening; an explicit trait may change the cost or difficulty of doing so.

## Your other beginnings

Choose one option each from your culture and background. Begin the listed discipline and learn its first entry. Add 1 level-0 DP for each discipline or entry you already know.
