# Basic Actions

Every character can use these universal actions from the start. On your turn, use your Main Action, Maneuver, and movement as the action directs. Spend your Reaction when its trigger occurs, including on another character's turn. Some actions require gear or a specific situation, such as a shield to block or a summon to command.

Your disciplines develop these capabilities through practiced techniques. Your class signatures appear with your class, personal talents with character creation, and spells with their spellbooks.
