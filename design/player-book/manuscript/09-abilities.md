# Abilities

An ability is a practiced technique, a spell, a talent or a reliable piece of expertise.
Its first lines describe what your hero does. Its rules tell you how that moment
changes the game. Declare the ability before rolling and use its printed attribute.

The numbered diamond is the minimum level: **1 Novice**, **3 Adept**, or **7 Master**.
Novice purchases are allowed in level-0 creation. Shared cards normally cost 2, 4 or 8 DP; the six exceptions below cost 1 DP.
Every listed discipline rank must be owned before learning. No earlier card is
required merely because its name or theme resembles a later one. The symbols beside
a card name identify its disciplines; each discipline entry shows its symbol. Full
requirements are also written out, so you never have to identify a symbol alone.

Universal actions are available to everyone. Their diamond reads **U** and they cost
no DP, but they do not give you free equipment. The shared catalog follows by tier,
alphabetically within each tier. Your class's signature choices are printed with
that class. Each tier opens with a quick selection table. The tiered index at the back includes
shared and class signature abilities, with requirements and page references.

## Small abilities, one DP

These shared Novice abilities cost **1 DP each**, require no discipline and may
be purchased once each, during level 0 or later. They are not free Universal grants.

- [Another Tongue](../../cards/another-tongue.md): learn one ordinary language.
- [Message by Heart](../../cards/message-by-heart.md): retain one message exactly.
- [Catch That!](../../cards/catch-that.md): catch a falling item with your Reaction.
- [A Party Piece](../../cards/a-party-piece.md): entertain with a practiced specialty.
- [Quick Exit](../../cards/quick-exit.md): gain one movement square when you Disengage.
- [Look Over Here!](../../cards/look-over-here.md): distract an adjacent attacker to aid an ally’s defense.

## Read the entry in play

Check the action, attribute, targets, range and equipment. Make any choices the card
requires, then pay its costs. If it calls for a roll, apply just the outcome you rolled.
Track duration and limits afterward. “Once per round” resets at the start of your
next turn. “Scene” means one continuous situation; a brief pause does not reset it.

Type tags help you find abilities; they do not add rules. A card can be Attack,
Magic and Control at once. Main Action, Maneuver, Reaction, Movement and Passive
are separate action labels. Passive abilities cost no action unless their text says
otherwise. A long activity still takes its printed full time.

Your equipment's appearance supplies the fiction. The attack supplies its mechanics.
If you describe a cleaving strike with a stout walking stick, use the chosen card's
range, damage and limitations. Special equipment adds its explicit effects only.

The ordinary way to attempt an activity remains available. An expert climbing card
can provide better results, but you can choose an ordinary climb instead. Buying
expertise gives you new options rather than forbidding everyone else from trying.

## Shared card conventions

Unless a card states a limit, you may use it again by paying its action and stated
costs. A free attack cannot trigger another free attack. An absent equipment grant
means the ability supplies no item. Read the Gear line for specific grants and
requirements; compatible abilities can share one item. These conventions apply to
class signatures as well as shared abilities.

Instant effects resolve once; applied conditions use their normal duration and decay.
For an area attack, make one roll using the least favorable net Boons/Banes among
the targets. Apply it to each affected target. When a creature attacks an area,
each affected player instead makes a defense roll. Target lines determine inclusions.

A summoning ability lets you choose an eligible profile anew each casting. Use that
profile’s statistics and capabilities. The summon immediately receives one free Main
command, counting as its command for that turn. Later commands cost your actions;
without one it does nothing. It acts on your turn, has no independent Reactions,
cannot summon, and disappears beyond 6 squares, including after forced movement.
See [Summoned Companions](11-summons.md) for profiles and the complete procedure.
