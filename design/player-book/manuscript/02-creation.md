# Create Your Hero

## Assign attributes

Assign a value from −2 to +2 to each of the six attributes. Their total must be **+3**.
A straightforward starting array is +2, +1, +1, +0, +0, −1, arranged as you choose.
Check your intended class's minimums before settling the array.

| Attribute | Common uses |
|---|---|
| Brawn | Exert strength, use Force, climb and make many melee attacks. |
| Fortitude | Endure harm, resist bodily threats and make death rolls; contributes to HP. |
| Agility | Move deftly, remain Hidden, jump and make many ranged attacks; contributes to Speed. |
| Guile | Deceive, misdirect, improvise and exploit an opening. |
| Knowledge | Apply learning, recognize patterns, craft and use learned techniques. |
| Reason | Notice details, perceive danger, judge a situation and keep your thinking clear. |

These are not exclusive lists. Roll the attribute printed on the ability you use.
Some techniques deliberately use an unusual attribute, such as Knowledge for a
carefully studied attack. There is no added skill rating.

Your chosen attributes stay fixed as you gain levels. Use those values for creation
DP and class eligibility. An item can change your effective attribute while its
rules apply, even outside the creation range. See [Equipment and Recipes](10-equipment.md)
for bonuses, penalties, and items that set an attribute to a specific value.

## Choose ancestry

Choose one ancestry and two of its five traits. Record the described characteristic, benefit and any side effect together. Ancestry contributes HP each level and SP and coins once at creation. Any ancestry can grow up in any culture and follow any class whose requirements the character meets.

## Create a level-1 hero

Assign Brawn, Fortitude, Agility, Guile, Knowledge and Reason from -2 through +2, totaling +3. Attributes stay fixed as you gain levels. Choose an ancestry and two of its five traits. Record the characteristic and its benefits and side effects together.

Choose a culture and background independently of ancestry. Each offers two disciplines; begin studying one from each. Choose a class whose attribute minimums you meet, then begin studying one discipline from its starting list. Choose three distinct disciplines across these choices. Select one level-1 class signature.

Your starting Development Points (DP) are **8 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason**, including negative values, plus explicit trait benefits. Use these points to learn abilities and spellbook sections. Starting disciplines are already yours to study. An additional discipline costs the 1, 2 or 3 DP printed for your class; then learn its entries at their listed prices. Save unspent DP for later development.

## Learning your paths

A nonmagical discipline has four paths: north develops a Main Action, west a Maneuver, east a Reaction, and south a distinctive capability. Reactions can answer events in exploration, conversation and practical work as well as combat. Timed work retains its printed minutes or hours.

Each path has seven sequential purchases. Learn the base ability and two improvements first. At level 3, learn its major development and two further improvements; at level 7, its mastery becomes available. Prices are **1 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 5 DP**, totaling 21 for a complete path. Improvements add together unless a new value replaces an earlier total. The fourth entry has its own ability; the fifth and sixth improve that fourth entry. Mastery gives the path its strongest expression.

Circles mark beginning studies, triangles level-3 studies, and diamonds level-7 masteries. In the atlas, solid gold marks learned entries; a gold border around green marks something you can learn now. Follow each path's own line. You can develop several paths and combine their effects when their rules permit.

Magical disciplines gather four themed spellbooks. Each book follows the same seven steps, prices and level gates. Sections I–IV each teach two spells; sections V, VI and VII each teach one spell. The final spell is the book’s level-7 mastery. Each book’s first spell costs 0 SP to cast. Spellcraft offers Soul Capacity, Mind Capacity and Recovery training, plus the Channeling spellbook.

The six independent 1-DP talents remain available once each: Another Tongue, Message by Heart, Catch That!, A Party Piece, Quick Exit and Look Over Here! Every hero retains the basic actions, including melee and ranged attacks, Aid, Stabilize and Sustain Spell.

## Signatures

Choose one signature at each of levels 1, 3 and 7. At a later milestone, develop a signature you already know or learn another signature appropriate to that milestone. This is one choice, not an additional signature plus an improvement. A later selection includes the foundation needed to use it; its text identifies any extra benefit that depends on a development you actually selected earlier. Keep each signature line together on the sheet.

You may change a signature selection. Check its dependent developments together and choose replacements when necessary. Each earned milestone still supplies one selection; changing it neither spends nor grants DP, repeats an acquisition benefit, nor restores used resources.

## Equipment and starting numbers

Gather the equipment your techniques use. The entry tells you what you need and what it supplies. A plain worn outfit is free. Compatible abilities can share one weapon.

> **Starting coins**\
> 40 + ancestry + culture + background + class coin contributions

Spend these coins on weapons, armor, tools and supplies. With the appropriate training, light, medium and heavy armor grant physical resistance 1, 2 and 3 respectively.

> **Maximum HP**\
> 10 + level × (ancestry HP + class HP) + Fortitude + fixed benefits

Begin with full HP. Count Fortitude and fixed benefits once.

> **Maximum SP**\
> 2 base SP + ancestry SP + twice your positive class mental attribute + 2 × (level − 1) + learned capacity and explicit benefits

Every hero starts with 2 base SP. Add twice your chosen class mental attribute if it is positive. Learned capacity comes from the Soul Capacity and Mind Capacity paths in the Spellcraft discipline. The first entry in each path adds 2 maximum SP to the same reserve. Begin with full SP. See [Starting SP](02a-starting-sp.md) for the class attributes and examples.

> **Capacity**\
> 10 + 3 × Brawn

> **Speed**\
> Ancestry base Speed + Agility + applicable trait bonuses − 1 per Bulk above Capacity. Minimum 0.

> **Stability and resistance**\
> Stability starts at 0. Use the highest explicit source of Stability and the highest matching resistance. Each has a maximum of 3. Apply damage immunity before resistance.

> **Grit**\
> Gain 1 Grit at levels 1, 3 and 7. Spend each point once. When you spend Grit, return to half maximum HP, rounded up.

Record current HP and SP, first-aid availability, death tallies and scars separately. See [recovery](../../rules/recovery.md).

## Advancement

The DA awards milestone levels. Apply these gains each time you reach a new level after level 1.

> **DP gained**\
> 4 + your new level, plus explicit trait benefits.

Save the points or spend them when the DA judges you are somewhere safe.

> **HP gained**\
> Add your ancestry HP + class HP to maximum HP. If you have positive HP, add the same amount to current HP. At 0 HP, increase only your maximum.

> **SP at a new level**\
> Add 2 to **maximum SP**. Keep your **current SP** at its previous value. If you have **3 / 8 SP** before reaching level 2, you have **3 / 10 SP** afterward. A safe overnight rest fills your reserve to **10 / 10 SP**.

Neither a level gain nor a signature change refreshes spent Grit.

| New level | DP gained | Other development |
|---|---:|---|
| 2 | 6 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 3 | 7 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP; advanced branch entries; signature choice; 1 Grit |
| 4 | 8 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 5 | 9 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 6 | 10 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 7 | 11 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP; masteries and master spells; signature choice; 1 Grit |
| 8 | 12 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 9 | 13 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |
| 10 | 14 | HP growth; +2 maximum SP |

Record what you learned and the DP spent. The only later learning gates are levels 3 and 7; reaching a gate makes its studies available while their sequential requirements and prices still apply.
