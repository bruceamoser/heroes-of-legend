# Disciplines and Character Paths

A discipline is a field of study. An ability is something you have learned to do
with it. Your hero might study Arms to practice martial techniques, Life to restore
others, or Scholarship to understand and exploit what others overlook.

Ranks run from 1 to 3 and must be bought in order. There is no level gate on a rank,
but the ability you want may require level 3 or 7. Read the requirements before
spending: Arms 2 and Subterfuge 1 means you must own both. A rank by itself grants
no action, equipment, attribute bonus or unrestricted use of that discipline's cards.

Class affinity determines what a rank costs. All abilities at the same tier use the
same DP price. This lets a class have a natural direction while leaving room for
unexpected study. Odd uses the same Adjacent prices across the entire list.

## Choose a direction

Imagine what you want to do for the company. A healer might protect a retreat,
restore HP, treat lasting injuries, or combine all three. A nature-focused hero
might become a beast, command an animal, control the ground, or guide companions
through dangerous country. These are different choices even when they share a theme.

The character paths that follow are suggestions, not subclasses. Mix them freely
where you meet the requirements. The listed cards do not waive DP, tier or discipline
requirements, and a suggested future card is not part of your starting equipment.

Keep a few abilities ready for the scenes you expect most often. You may know several
attacks, but you still have only the actions you can spend. A useful Maneuver, Reaction,
travel skill or social technique can matter more than another similar Main Action.
