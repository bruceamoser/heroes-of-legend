# Table References

Use these pages when the action pauses for a rule. Keep your most-used abilities
beside your sheet, along with the conditions currently affecting you. Basic Actions
lists the actions any hero can use.

## Your turn in order

1. Resolve any start-of-turn instructions. If Dying, choose Grit or a death roll.
2. Refresh your turn budget. If Dazed or Grappled, choose the action categories you retain.
3. Move and take permitted actions in your chosen order. Declare an ability before rolling.
4. Resolve end-of-turn conditions, then reduce temporary numbered ratings by 1.
5. Mark continuing effects and keep your available Reaction ready for its trigger.

## Quick rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Ordinary roll | 3d6 + one attribute; Weak ≤8, Standard 9–14, Strong ≥15 |
| Boons / Banes | Cancel; add up to 3 net dice; keep highest / lowest 3 |
| Natural 111 / 666 | Weak + complication / Strong + benefit. Death rolls use only the band. |
| Initiative | The ambushing side starts. Otherwise roll unmodified 3d6 once. Players start on 12+, opposition on 11 or less. Same side opens every round. |
| Action conversion | Main → Maneuver → movement; never upward |
| Ordinary movement | Normal 1, difficult 2. Same cost diagonally. |
| Prone movement | Crawl at normal 2 or difficult 3, including diagonally. Stand for 2. |
| Occupied space | Cross if the occupant is Prone. Pay 2 or the usual entry cost, whichever is higher. End in a free square. |
| Cover | Partial 1, heavy 3. Apply Banes to the attack roll or Boons to the defense roll. An attack needs a path. |
| Damage defenses | Remove immune types. Use highest matching resistance, cap 3. Attacks have a minimum of 1 if any damage survives immunity. |
| Stability | Default 0; highest explicit grant, cap 3; deduct once per new creature |
| Free attacks | Spend no actions; never trigger more free attacks |
| Maximum SP, level 1+ | 2 × level + ancestry SP + 2 × positive class mental attribute + Spellcraft bonuses + other SP bonuses |
| Healing spell cost | Printed spell cost; pay before resolving |
| Potion | Spend a Maneuver with a free hand. An unconscious or restrained recipient counts as willing. Another recipient spends a Reaction if conscious and above 0 HP. |

## Glossary

**Ability.** A learned technique, spell or other capability with its own
procedure. “Card” refers to that rules entry, whether printed on a card or in this book.

**Action.** Something a character chooses to do. An action can call for a roll;
a roll itself is not an extra action. A Main Action is a specific part of the turn budget.

**Attribute.** One of Brawn, Fortitude, Agility, Guile, Knowledge or Reason. Add only
the attribute called for by the action or DA.

**Benefit.** A useful additional advantage, including the extra consequence of a
natural 666. Apply the stated Strong outcome as well.

**Boon / Bane.** An extra die that changes which three dice you retain. Boons and
Banes cancel before applying their net cap.

**Bulk.** An item's inventory load. Capacity and carried Bulk determine encumbrance.

**Class.** Your hero’s training at level 1. Your class offers starting disciplines and signature choices at levels 1, 3 and 7.

**Complication.** An additional problem that does not erase progress already earned.

**Condition.** A named state with defined effects and removal. A condition's rating
may measure severity, duration or both.

**Consequence.** What is at stake in an uncertain attempt. Establish it before rolling.

**DA.** Dungeon Architect, the participant who presents and adjudicates the world.

**Discipline.** A field of study with four paths or spellbooks. Your culture, background and class each supply one starting discipline. An additional discipline costs the 1, 2 or 3 DP printed for your class.

**DP.** Development Points, spent to study disciplines and learn entries. Start with 10 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason, plus explicit benefits. Gain 4 + the new level at each later level. Save unspent points.

**DR.** Damage reduction. Armor DR is physical resistance, applied after the attack outcome.

**Dying.** A player character's state at 0 HP before stabilization or recovery.
Further damage at 0 causes an immediate death roll; surviving the incident leaves a descriptive scar.

**Effect.** A change produced by a rule. It might be immediate damage, movement,
a condition or a continuing benefit. Its duration does not repeat instant damage.

**Evasion / Threat.** A creature's printed dice adjustments to player attacks /
player defenses. They are Boons or Banes, not added numerical totals.

**Free attack.** An attack granted by a specific rule without spending an action.
It cannot trigger another free attack.

**Grit.** A lifetime reserve gained once at levels 1/3/7. Spend one to return to half maximum HP, rounded up; it never refreshes.

**Hidden.** A universal concealed state, maintained by actual circumstances. Attacking
or openly revealing yourself ends it.

**HP.** Current capacity to keep going. Record current and maximum separately.

**Inventory die.** A supply's remaining uncertainty. Roll after use; step down on 1.

**Death.** Three failures on death rolls kill unless Grit or an ability intervenes immediately. The player can describe their hero’s Last Words.

**Maneuver.** A smaller action used for movement techniques, setup, support and practical tasks.

**Passive.** A capability that requires no action unless its text says otherwise.

**Reaction.** Your limited response to a stated trigger. An ordinary defense roll
is not itself a Reaction.

**Treatment allowance.** One attempt per recipient between safe overnight rests, shared across First Aid and learned treatments.

**Resistance.** Reduction to a matching damage type; highest source, maximum 3.
Life resistance also reduces magical healing and potions.

**Safe place.** A location the DA judges suitable for spending saved DP. Brief and Night’s Rests have their own time and safety requirements.

**Signature.** An ability you choose from your class at level 1, 3 or 7.

**Stability.** Resistance to the distance of forced movement. Default 0, cap 3.
It does not reduce damage, Prone or other conditions.

**Learning.** Learn the entries of an ability path or the sections of a spellbook in order. Check the listed DP and level requirement. Mark each spell taught by a section you learn.

**Sustain.** A Maneuver costing 0 SP. Sustain one spell at a time, as its text directs. A fixed duration continues for its stated time; moving a directed effect uses its printed action.

**Scar.** A descriptive reminder of surviving an incident at 0 HP; no mechanical penalties or treatment track.

## Keep notes between sessions

Write down unfinished business, promises and discoveries the company might return to. When the group makes a ruling you expect to use again, note it where everyone can find it.

## Survival at a glance

When you fall to 0 HP, you enter Dying, fall prone and lose consciousness. Roll Fortitude at the start of each
Dying turn and immediately after each damaging event while already at 0 HP.
Weak adds one failure, Strong adds one success and Standard changes neither.
Use only the result band, including on triples. Three failures mean death.
Three successes stabilize you at 0 HP. An adjacent ally can stabilize you with
a Maneuver. Clear both tallies on stabilization or recovery above 0 HP.
Spend Grit for half maximum HP, rounded up, even after the third failure.
