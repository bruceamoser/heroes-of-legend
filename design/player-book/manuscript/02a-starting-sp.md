# Starting SP

## Introduction

Your level contributes twice its value in Spell Points: 2 SP at level 1, 6 at level 3 and 14 at level 7. Add SP from your ancestry, class and any capacity or benefits you learned. Calculate the reserve after making your creation choices, then begin with that many SP. Record learned spells alongside the reserve.

## Maximum SP

**2 × level + ancestry SP + 2 × positive class casting attribute + Spellcraft bonuses + other SP bonuses**

A class attribute of 0 or less contributes 0. Use your base value for the class’s casting attribute. Ancestry, class and learned bonuses are added once. The level contribution grows by 2 SP each level.

## Learned capacity and recovery

Soul Capacity and Mind Capacity are paths in the Spellcraft discipline. Both increase the same reserve. Soul Capacity I and Mind Capacity I each add 2 maximum SP. Add each learned increase once, using the amount printed for that entry. Recovery restores spent SP through its named rest activity. Channeling transfers existing SP between reserves. Learning a new entry or raising maximum SP after creation does not itself refill the reserve.

## Growth and examples

At each level after 1, increase your maximum SP by 2. Keep your current SP at its previous value. For example, a hero with 3 / 8 SP reaches level 2 with 3 / 10 SP. A safe overnight rest fills the new reserve. These gains add up to 4 more maximum SP at level 3 and 12 more at level 7, before additional learned benefits.

**Hill Dwarf Protector, Reason +1:** 2 × 1 level + 0 ancestry + 2 class = **4 SP** at level 1. At level 3, the reserve is **8 SP** before learned capacity.

**High Elf Arcanist, Knowledge +2:** 2 × 1 level + 2 ancestry + 4 class = **8 SP** at level 1. Learning Soul Capacity I adds 2, making **10 SP**. At level 7 with that same learning, the reserve is **22 SP**.

## Current and maximum SP

Record current SP separately. Pay a spell's printed SP when casting; each book's first spell costs 0 SP. Sustain uses a Maneuver and 0 SP. A Brief Rest restores 3 SP once per day. A Night’s Rest fills your reserve. Other recovery and transfer follow their listed time and limits.
