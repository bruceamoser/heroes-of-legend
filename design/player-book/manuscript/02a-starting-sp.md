# Starting SP

## Introduction

Your maximum Spell Points (SP) combine your origins, class and learned capacity. Calculate the reserve after completing each creation stage, then start with that many SP. Record your learned spellbook pages alongside your reserve.

## Maximum SP

At level 1 and later: **6 + 2 × (level − 1) + learned capacity + other explicit benefits**

## Starting contributions

| Source | Maximum SP | When added |
|---|---:|---|
| Any ancestry | +1 | Level 0 |
| Any culture | +1 | Level 0 |
| Any background | +1 | Level 0 |
| Any class | +3 | Level 1 |

Each contribution is added once. Every character has **3 maximum SP at level 0** and **6 at level 1**, before learned capacity and other explicit benefits. These contributions do not vary by ancestry or class.

## Learned capacity

Spellcraft opens **Soul Capacity** and **Mind Capacity**. Each has the same increases; you may learn either or both, following each path in order.

| Learned step | SP added | Total from that path |
|---|---:|---:|
| I | +2 | +2 |
| II | +2 | +4 |
| III | +3 | +7 |
| IV · level 3 | +3 | +10 |

Both paths add to **one SP reserve** as you learn their steps. Spellcraft also offers Recovery to replenish spent SP and Channeling to move power between creatures.

## Growth by level

From level 2 onward, gain **2 maximum SP at every level**, regardless of ancestry or class. Add learned capacity and any other explicit benefits to the values below.

| Character level | Base maximum SP |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 3 |
| 1 | 6 |
| 2 | 8 |
| 3 | 10 |
| 4 | 12 |
| 5 | 14 |
| 6 | 16 |
| 7 | 18 |
| 8 | 20 |
| 9 | 22 |
| 10 | 24 |

## Current and maximum SP

Record current SP separately. Pay a spell's printed cost before resolving it; page 1 of every spellbook costs 0 SP. Increasing maximum SP after creation does not restore spent points. A safe overnight rest restores the reserve; other recovery and channeling use their printed limits.

## A reserve without Spellcraft

**Level-1 Hill Dwarf Protector:** 1 ancestry + 1 culture + 1 background + 3 class = **6 SP**. At level 3, the base reserve is **10 SP**, even without magical training.

## A reserve with learned capacity

**Level-1 Wood Elf Arcanist:** 6 base + 2 from Soul Capacity I + 2 from Mind Capacity I = **10 SP**. At level 7, with Soul Capacity III and Mind Capacity II, the maximum is 18 base + 7 + 4 = **29 SP**.
