# Summoned Companions

You call across a boundary, and something answers: paws on stone, a flicker of
ancestral light, a shape unfolding from cold air. The creature acts through your
attention. Its presence gives you choices, not a second independent turn.

Choose from the profiles in this chapter when a summoning ability permits the
category and tier. Higher-tier cards can choose eligible lower-tier creatures.
Category names alone do not grant access to an unlisted creature.

## Command the creature

Spend your **Main Action** to give one summon a Main Action and a Maneuver. Spend
your **Maneuver** to give one summon a Maneuver and movement. Normal conversions
apply. The Main-command package has no separate movement allowance: convert its
Maneuver to movement if it needs to approach before attacking.

A summon receives at most one command on your turn. You can spend your Main Action
on one creature and your Maneuver on another. A summoning card's immediate free
Main command counts as that creature's command for the turn.

An uncommanded summon does **nothing**. It does not follow, keep watch, alert you,
continue a previous task, or take Reactions and opportunity attacks. Existing
conditions, durations and passive defenses still operate. Resolve its turn-based
timing on your turn. You cannot summon creatures that summon more creatures.

## Keep the connection

A summon disappears beyond **6 squares** from you, including after forced movement
of either participant. It also disappears at 0 HP, or when you become unconscious
or die. Its duration may end earlier. It leaves no permanent equipment or loot.

For a large footprint, agree the tether measurement with the DA before placement;
the general rules do not yet define every multi-square case. Do not quietly gain
extra reach by moving only one corner of a creature inside the boundary.

## Give a useful order

“Attack that creature,” “defend this spot,” “move into cover,” and “help them cross”
are clear intentions. The creature still needs the printed capability and enough
commanded actions to carry them out. A previous order never buys activity next turn.
Outside combat, you must continue directing a longer task for its full time.

Roll the creature's attack attribute, not your own, and read its player-facing
attacker outcomes. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the
incoming attack using the creature's value. Apply the profile's attack or defense
Boons/Banes and resistance normally. There is never a second opposed roll.

The profiles below include only the summoned form's permitted capabilities. A
monster's dramatic description does not grant its summoned form extra actions,
active auras, independent attacks or skills.
