# Ember Cities

*Kilns, glassworks and night markets make the streets glow after sundown.*

*What beautiful thing did you first learn to make?*

## Rules

Choose one:

- **Flame 1** and [Speak with the Hearth](../cards/speak-with-the-hearth.md).
- **Craft 1** and [Make Something Useful](../cards/make-something-useful.md).

**Type:** Culture · **Status:** Proposal. Your ancestry does not restrict this choice.

Each option is a pair. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card’s equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +10 coins (proposed allocation). See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
