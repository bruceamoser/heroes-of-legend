# Cloister Valleys

*Little lights drift above the cloister paths after dusk. In the quiet rooms below, patient listeners teach troubled minds to find their footing again.*

*Which lesson did you question before you left?*

## Rules

Choose one:

- **Wards access** and [Protection I](../spellbooks/protection.md).
- **Medicine access** and [Field Dressing I](../disciplines/medicine.md).

**Type:** Culture

Each option grants discipline access and its named first learned entry. Each duplicate grant gives 1 DP for this creation stage; it never advances a path automatically. Equipment is not granted unless explicitly stated.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
