# Walled Communities

*Your home knows the comfort of a gate and the danger of believing it solves everything.*

*Who always greeted you from the wall?*

## Rules

**Type:** Culture. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; take the access and first entry together.

- **Armor access** and [Armored Training I](../disciplines/armor.md).
- **Guard access** and [Shield Bash I](../disciplines/guard.md).

Gain the chosen access and first entry at level 0. Each duplicate grant gives 1 DP for that stage.

**Starting coin contribution:** +10 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
