# Walled Communities

*Your home knows the comfort of a gate and the danger of believing it solves everything.*

*Who always greeted you from the wall?*

## Rules

**Type:** Culture. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; do not mix the rank from one with the card from another.

- Armor 1 and [Wear Medium Armor](../cards/wear-medium-armor.md).
- Guard 1 and [Shielding Step](../cards/shielding-step.md).

Grant at level 0. Duplicate grants provide 1 stage DP each; never promote a rank automatically. No discipline purchases at level 0. Ancestry does not restrict this choice. Follow each card’s explicit equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +10 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
