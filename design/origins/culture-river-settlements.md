# River Settlements

*The river gives your people passage and livelihood. You learned to cross its currents and to lend breath to those it almost took.*

*What do you miss about the sound of the docks?*

## Rules

**Type:** Culture. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; take the access and first entry together.

- **Athletics access** and [Strong Swimmer I](../disciplines/athletics.md).
- **Life access** and [Restoration I](../spellbooks/restoration.md).

Gain the chosen access and first entry at level 0. Each duplicate grant gives 1 DP for that stage.

**Starting coin contribution:** +7 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
