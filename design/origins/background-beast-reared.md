# Beast-Reared

*Whether raised, rescued or apprenticed among beasts, you know a world of scent and posture.*

*Which animal behavior do you still find reassuring?*

## Rules

Choose one:

- **Wilderness 1** and [Borrow the Beast](../cards/borrow-the-beast.md).
- **Calling 1** and [Summon Animal — Novice](../cards/call-the-faithful-hound.md).

**Type:** Background · **Status:** Proposal. This is a past, not a required personality or moral alignment.

Each option is a pair. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card’s equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins (proposed allocation). See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
