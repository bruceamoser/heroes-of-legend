# Shore Gatherer

*The shore fed you if you learned when to arrive and what to leave alone.*

*What did the tide bring you once that it should not have?*

## Rules

**Type:** Background. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; do not mix the rank from one with the card from another.

- Wilderness 1 and [Forage Coast](../cards/forage-coast.md).
- Athletics 1 and [Expert River Crossing](../cards/expert-river-crossing.md).

Grant at level 0. Duplicate grants provide 1 stage DP each; never promote a rank automatically. No discipline purchases at level 0. Ancestry does not restrict this choice. Follow each card’s explicit equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins (proposed allocation). See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
