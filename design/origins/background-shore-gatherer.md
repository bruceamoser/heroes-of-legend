# Shore Gatherer

*The shore fed you if you learned when to arrive and what to leave alone.*

*What did the tide bring you once that it should not have?*

## Rules

**Type:** Background. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; take the access and first entry together.

- **Wilderness access** and [Wild Harvest I](../disciplines/wilderness.md).
- **Athletics access** and [Strong Swimmer I](../disciplines/athletics.md).

Gain the chosen access and first entry at level 0. Each duplicate grant gives 1 DP for that stage.

**Starting coin contribution:** +5 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
