# Highland Holds

*Your neighbors keep winter in their storehouses and thunder in their songs. Frost preserves the harvest; a sharp report carries a warning across the crags.*

*What did your neighbors trust you to carry?*

## Rules

**Type:** Culture. **Status:** Proposal. Choose one paired option; take the access and first entry together.

- **Elements access** and [Water I](../spellbooks/water.md).
- **Elements access** and [Air I](../spellbooks/air.md).

Gain the chosen access and first entry at level 0. Each duplicate grant gives 1 DP for that stage.

**Starting coin contribution:** +7 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
