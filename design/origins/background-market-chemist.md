# Market Chemist

*You know which bottle smells like a cure and which only smells expensive.*

*Which customer made you stop exaggerating?*

## Rules

Choose one:

- **Craft 1** and [Smoke in a Bottle](../cards/smoke-in-a-bottle.md).
- **Scholarship 1** and [Identify a Mixture](../cards/identify-a-mixture.md).

**Type:** Background

Each option is a pair. Duplicate grants give 1 level-0 DP each; no automatic higher rank. Follow the card’s equipment grant.

**Starting coin contribution:** +10 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
