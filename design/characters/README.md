# Six level-1 characters — complete creation walkthroughs

Created from the approved catalog on `main` after PRs #1132–#1158. These are worked
character choices, not new rules, random samples or combat-balance results. Every
character stops at level 1. Follow the linked sheet for both complete DP ledgers,
origin pairs, ancestry traits, signature, equipment and learned-card checklist.

All start at full HP, Grit 2, no wounds and no death marks. Unspent creation DP
expires separately at each stage. Ancestry and culture are independent: Mara is a
human of Forest Communities, and Oren is a human raised in Deep Halls.

## What was checked

- All attributes are −2 through +2, total +3, and satisfy the chosen class minima.
- Each origin pair exists in its source. Only those rank-1 grants qualify level-0
  purchases; no class rank or purchased rank is borrowed from level 1.
- Every purchased card is Novice. Requirements are held before each purchase;
  class-granted disciplines are valid and distinct. Duplicate ranks yield 1 DP
  each at the correct stage, never rank 2.
- Class Home/Adjacent/Foreign prices and all running balances were checked.
- All six signatures belong to the chosen class and level. No extra signature
  purchases or advanced-tier cards are assumed.
- HP, Speed, capacity and carried Bulk were recomputed. Each item is traced to an
  explicit grant; compatible duplicate grants are counted once. Described weapons
  use the cards’ mechanics, not statistics imported from their appearance.
- Oren’s six recipe choices are distinct and compatible. Recipe knowledge does not
  supply ingredients or finished products. Other characters have no recipe grants.

These checks were run against the source Markdown with a temporary validation
script and followed by a reading of the sheets. The normal catalog audit also
passes. This checks these six builds, not every possible creation route. All
purchased disciplines here are rank 1: buying deeper ranks without access to
higher-tier cards did not serve these particular concepts.

## Findings from actual creation

**The concepts work with independent origins and printed attributes.** Pip fights
with Guile, Oren with Knowledge, and the elemental scholar does not need Brawn to
make a useful attack. Mara can heal or become a beast, but cannot cast while in
that form. Those are meaningful differences at level 1.

**The pure archer lacks a signature that celebrates archery.** Blade supplies
Marksmanship choices and favorable prices, but its three Novice signatures favor
melee proximity. Measured Guard gives Lira a useful emergency escape; it does not
express mastery of the bow. This is a catalog gap exposed by the example, not a
reason to invent a new signature inside the build.

**Odd DP remainders are real.** Pip loses 1 at level 0; Vaelis loses 1 at each stage;
Oren loses 1 at level 0. Two-DP cards and the ban on level-0 discipline purchases
make these remainders unavoidable for the chosen shopping lists. They were not
silently carried over.

**Starting equipment is sparse by design, but expedition supplies remain a gap.**
Vaelis and Mara have no granted mechanical equipment. They can use their magic,
but have no assumed food, water container, rope, bedroll or potions. Oren can craft
once materials are available; his six recipes are not six finished items. No free
pack or invented coin budget was used to conceal this issue.

**Starting coins are still unspecified.** Each sheet explicitly leaves them
unassigned. Completing that field requires the base/ancestry/background/class
coin values promised by the development rules. Everything with a defined numeric
creation rule is resolved.

**The hand and card loads are visible.** Borin begins with weapon and shield;
Lira uses both hands for her bow. Neither has an automatic potion hand. Each
character has 11–15 learned cards plus the 18 universal references; the universal
set does not imply possession of its required shield, potion or weapon. A play
sheet should foreground a few likely choices while keeping the rest accessible.

**This is not a complete class survey.** Leader, Unbalanced and Odd are not sampled,
and these builds do not establish comparative combat power or encounter CV.
