### [Under Their Noses](../ancestries/trait-halfling-small-passage.md)

You fold yourself into the spaces others overlook. Gain 1 Boon to hiding; an adjacent standing creature at least one Size larger can provide a place to hide from an observer when it stands between you and that observer, even during combat.

### [Quick Tongue](../ancestries/trait-halfling-quiet-company.md)

When you openly identify whose interests you are arguing for, gain 1 Boon to Guile to negotiate or argue, or 2 Boons when defending your own or an ally’s actions. Take 1 Bane to Guile rolls to pass yourself off as a neutral party for the remainder of that exchange.

## Class signature

[Fade Behind](../cards/signature-shadow-fade-behind.md)

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Disengage. Then attempt to Hide with 1 Boon, even if you are currently observed, provided you have cover or obscurement. You still make the ordinary Hide attempt. If you succeed, you are Hidden until you attack, are revealed, or the concealment ends.

**Duration:** Immediate; Hidden uses its normal duration.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Frequency:** Once on your turn.

This is the one free signature choice for level 1.

## Your learned techniques

**Measured Cut I.** Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, you may move 1 square after the attack.

**Measured Cut II.** Measured Cut deals 3/4/6 physical damage.

**Driving Opening I.** Maneuver. Choose one visible creature. Before your turn ends, your next Main Action melee weapon attack against it also pushes it 1 square directly away from your attack position on Strong. This modifies one attack, not each target of a sweep. Reusing this technique replaces its unused effect.

**Quiet Passage I.** Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Agility rolls to move quietly while concealed. Concealment needs cover or a plausible distraction.

**Patient Hand I.** Main Action · Guile, with lock tools. Examine and manipulate one reachable simple nonmagical fastening; add 1/2/3 progress toward opening it. The DA fixes the objective and work interval first: an intricate lock takes 10 minutes per attempt instead of a Main Action. Impossible mechanisms require another approach.

**Patient Hand II.** Patient Hand adds 1/3/4 progress.

**Catch Your Balance I.** Reaction. When you would fall Prone, remain standing instead. You must be conscious and able to move. No roll.

## At the table

Use Measured Cut II as your Main Action and Driving Opening as your Maneuver. Fade Behind is an alternative Maneuver for escape and concealment. Catch Your Balance is a situational Reaction; a held dagger also permits the universal Opportunity Attack. Patient Hand II works with the lock tools supplied by Patient Hand. Keep the other hand free for locks and climbing.

All universal actions remain available. An improvement changes its named technique; keep its benefits together on the sheet. First aid starts available, with no death marks or scars.
