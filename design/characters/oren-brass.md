## Level 1 — class and purchases

Choose [Intellect](../classes/intellect.md). Class grants **Craft 1 and Scholarship 1**; 2 duplicate(s) give **+2 DP**, not higher ranks.
Free signature: [Make the Connection](../cards/signature-intellect-make-the-connection.md). Its use procedure follows; the linked card remains the complete reference:

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Time:** One minute.

**Target / range / shape:** An accessible person, object or scene with evidence you can examine.

Spend one minute examining the evidence and state a question. Learn one supported fact relevant to it and a specific way to investigate further: a person to question, place to examine or test to perform, if one exists. If the evidence cannot answer your question, learn what kind of evidence is missing. No roll; obvious clues remain available to everyone. This does not reveal thoughts or invent events.

**Duration:** Information remains known.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.


**Signature limit:** One reading per question until new evidence becomes available.

Fresh pool: **13 + 2 = 15 DP**. All discipline purchases below occur before cards that need them.

## At the table

Anatomy of an Opening uses Knowledge in a fight; Oren chooses its optional melee-weapon grant. Make the Connection turns actual evidence into a fact and follow-up lead. Crafting and identification make discoveries useful, while Medicine supports wound care. Oren’s card grants supply tools and recipe knowledge; his shopping ledger adds ordinary ingredients, but no finished crafted supplies.
