### [A Lifetime Ago](../ancestries/trait-elf-age-of-memory.md)

Spend a Maneuver in still reverie letting an ancestral image take shape. Once between safe overnight rests, gain 2 Boons to your next Knowledge or Reason roll about the natural world or the past, made before the end of your next turn.

While recalling the memory and until the start of your next turn, take 1 Bane to Reason rolls to notice your present surroundings. You may describe an ancestral impression, while the DA determines what the roll reveals.

### [Dream beneath the Stars](../ancestries/trait-elf-star-familiar.md)

You slip into a quiet reverie while remaining aware of ordinary nearby sights and sounds. Reverie counts as sleep during a safe overnight rest, with the same required time.

Use the ordinary overnight-rest duration and recovery frequency. Reverie replaces the sleep portion; active work interrupts it.

## Class signature

[Deliberate Spark](../cards/signature-arcanist-deliberate-spark.md)

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One corner of your square; each creature in the four squares that share that corner and are in line of effect.

Choose a corner of your square and spark a brief flare of gas there. Each creature in the four squares that share that corner, including you, is pushed 2 squares directly away from that corner. A creature in more than one of those squares is pushed once. The spark does not provoke opportunity attacks. Resolve the push as forced movement; normal collision rules apply.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 0 SP, usable once on your turn. Its casting does not provoke opportunity attacks.

This is the one free signature choice for level 1.

## Your learned techniques

**Flame I.** Learn Fire Bolt, Boil Liquid.

**Flame II.** Learn Burning Hands, Heat Metal.

**Water I.** Learn Ray of Frost, Create or Destroy Water.

**Air I.** Learn Elementalism — Air, Feather Fall.

**Portents I.** Learn True Strike, Guidance.

**Soul Capacity I.** Passive. Increase maximum SP by 2.

**Soul Capacity II.** Increase maximum SP by another 2; this path now grants +4 maximum SP.

**Mind Capacity I.** Passive. Increase maximum SP by 2.

**Recovery I.** 1 uninterrupted hour of safe rest, meditation or quiet leisure. Once between safe overnight rests, recover 1 SP. This can occur during permitted quiet rest time; ordinary overnight recovery remains unchanged.

## At the table

Fire Bolt provides a free Main Action attack; Flame II adds Burning Hands and Heat Metal. Deliberate Spark is a Maneuver that can also push allies and yourself, so choose its corner carefully. Air I supplies Feather Fall as a Reaction. Both hands begin free. The two capacity paths add 6 SP, producing 13 maximum SP; learning them is not a separate refill.

All universal actions remain available. An improvement changes its named technique; keep its benefits together on the sheet. First aid starts available, with no death marks or scars.
