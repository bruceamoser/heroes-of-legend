# Stoic Bearing

*Your grounded posture gives 1 Stability while standing on a supporting surface.*

## Rules

Your grounded posture gives 1 Stability while standing on a supporting surface. After listening to the other party’s position before answering, gain 1 Boon to Guile for patient, honest bargaining in that exchange. While taking this deliberate approach, take 1 Bane to Guile rolls to conceal your own intentions in the same exchange.

**Ancestry:** Hill Dwarf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.

**ID:** trait-dwarf-low-center
