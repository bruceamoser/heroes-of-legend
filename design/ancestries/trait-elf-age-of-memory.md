# A Lifetime Ago

*Once per day, before you make a Knowledge or Reason roll, you may call on the knowledge of your ancestors to gain 1 Boon to that roll.*

## Rules

Once per day, before you make a Knowledge or Reason roll, you may call on the knowledge of your ancestors to gain 1 Boon to that roll.

**Ancestry:** Wood Elf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-elf-age-of-memory
