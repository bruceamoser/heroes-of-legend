# The Weight of Centuries

*You recall an encounter as vividly as the day you lived it.*

## Rules

You recall an encounter as vividly as the day you lived it. Once between safe overnight rests, describe a personally experienced encounter before a relevant Knowledge or Reason roll and gain 2 Boons.

**Ancestry:** High Elf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.

**ID:** trait-high-elf-weight-of-centuries
