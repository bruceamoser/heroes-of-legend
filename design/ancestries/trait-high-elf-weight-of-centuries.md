# The Weight of Centuries

*You close your eyes, and the room returns as it stood a lifetime ago.*

## Rules

Once per day, before a Knowledge or Reason roll concerning something you personally encountered, gain 2 Boons by recalling that experience. Describe what you remember. This grants no knowledge of events before your birth or experiences you never had.

**Ancestry:** High Elf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
