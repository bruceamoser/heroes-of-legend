# Human

*Restless, adaptable and fiercely invested in a life that never feels long enough.*

Humans have no single stature, complexion or way of making a home. A short life beside the elder peoples can give an ordinary season an urgent importance: learn the trade, mend the friendship, see what lies beyond the ridge. Some make roots; others make roads between the places they love.

Adaptability takes many forms. One human gathers languages and acquaintances; another learns by failing until a stubborn problem yields. Neither ambition nor restlessness is compulsory. A human hero may want a quiet life enough to undertake a dangerous journey to protect it.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar fantasy,
not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +2; base Speed 6.

- [Never Quite Finished](trait-human-weathered.md) — There is always one more person to help, one more hill to cross.
- [Adaptable](trait-human-far-walker.md) — You learn quickly, turning every new experience into another useful skill.
- [A Foot in Two Worlds](trait-human-many-homes.md) — You learned early that a stranger is sometimes only a person whose words you do not know.
- [Ingenuity](trait-human-shared-table.md) — You look at what is available and build the tool you need.
- [The Impossible Streak](trait-human-practiced-patience.md) — Someone says it cannot be done, and you hear a challenge.

**Starting coin contribution:** +1 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
