# Gnome

*Tiny fey bodies, long lives and minds forever asking what else a thing could become.*

Gnomes are fey creatures, smaller even than halflings, with deft hands, keen senses and an intelligence that can make a complicated device seem almost embarrassingly obvious. Their faces carry the expressions of long experience, however easily a stranger mistakes their stature for youth.

Some say the gnomes left the world of fairie to explore possibilities its inhabitants had overlooked. Others insist they were thrown out, and disagree enthusiastically about what invention caused the trouble. Gnomes tell both stories. Whether anyone remembers the whole truth is another question.

Inventing and tinkering delight many gnomes: an unfamiliar mechanism invites examination, an accepted limitation invites an experiment. Others apply that same searching intelligence to music, medicine, argument or the behavior of friends. A gnome does not inherit a workshop or a profession. Their fey nature and extraordinary mind travel with them wherever they grow up.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP +1; base Speed 6; Size Small.

- [What’s It Made Of?](trait-gnome-whats-it-made-of.md) — You turn the device over, already asking why its maker put the hinge there.
- [Eyes from Elsewhere](trait-gnome-one-more-question.md) — The painted door is convincing. Its shadow has forgotten where the sun is.
- [Fingers in the Works](trait-gnome-fingers-in-the-works.md) — You ease one small part out of place, and the whole machine loses its argument.
- [Small Target](trait-gnome-small-target.md) — You tuck yourself behind the low stone as the arrow strikes its edge.
- [Bright Idea](trait-gnome-bright-idea.md) — You draw three lines in the dust, add a spoon, and suddenly everyone understands.

**Starting coin contribution:** +1 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
