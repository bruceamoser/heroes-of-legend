# An Answering Thread

*You show the stranger your mark. For a moment, neither of you stands alone.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Guile rolls to establish trust with someone who freely reveals that they, too, carry a promise, debt or duty they chose to honor. You need a shared means of communication. This neither reveals concealed obligations nor makes anyone trust you automatically.

**Ancestry:** Oath-Bound · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
