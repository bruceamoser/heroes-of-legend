# Endless Pockets

*Your carefully arranged pockets hold an impossible amount.*

## Rules

Your carefully arranged pockets hold an impossible amount. Gain 2 Bulk capacity for stowed objects that fit through your pockets or bag openings. Finding anything in those crowded folds takes two Maneuvers rather than one when retrieving a stowed object during an encounter.

While using the extra capacity, retrieval takes two Maneuvers. When your load fits your ordinary capacity, retrieve items normally. Worn armor and readied or held equipment use their normal Bulk.

**Ancestry:** Halfling · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.

**ID:** trait-halfling-resilient
