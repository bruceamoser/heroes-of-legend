# Make Room at the Fire

*You can turn a cold greeting into a place at the table.*

## Rules

Once per scene, when you offer genuine food, shelter or shared work to a nonhostile person, gain 1 Boon to the next Guile roll seeking ordinary hospitality or trust from them. No automatic friendship or compelled response.

**Ancestry:** Human · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-human-shared-table · **Status:** Proposal for Bruce’s review. No DP cost.
