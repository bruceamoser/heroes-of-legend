# What’s It Made Of?

*You turn the device over, already asking why its maker put the hinge there.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Knowledge rolls to understand the construction, function or failure of a mechanism or manufactured mixture. Obvious working parts need no roll to identify. This does not automatically identify a maker, a secret ingredient or a magical property.

**Ancestry:** Gnome · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
