# Quick Tongue

*When you openly identify whose interests you are arguing for, gain 1 Boon to Guile to negotiate or argue, or 2 Boons when defending your own or an ally’s actions.*

## Rules

When you openly identify whose interests you are arguing for, gain 1 Boon to Guile to negotiate or argue, or 2 Boons when defending your own or an ally’s actions. Take 1 Bane to Guile rolls to pass yourself off as a neutral party for the remainder of that exchange.

**Ancestry:** Halfling · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.

**ID:** trait-halfling-quiet-company
