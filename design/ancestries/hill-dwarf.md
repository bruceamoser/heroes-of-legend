# Hill Dwarf

*Broad shoulders, patient hands and a home made where mountain meets open sky.*

Long ago, dwarven peoples took different roads. Some built their halls within mountains opening onto the surface world. Others traveled deeper into the earth. Centuries of separation, perhaps much longer, have left their descendants visibly different.

Hill Dwarves are the broad, stocky surface people: solid limbs, strong backs, dense hair and faces weathered by forge heat, mountain wind or ordinary years of work. Their halls connect stone interiors to roads, daylight and neighboring communities. Deep Dwarves are their distant kin, but a stranger would seldom mistake one for the other.

Endurance, craft and a lasting memory are familiar expressions of Hill Dwarf identity. A remembered debt may be a kindness worth returning as readily as an injury worth answering. A dwarf need not live in a mountain, work a forge or keep a grudge; the same steady body can belong to a sailor, gardener or traveling musician.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP contribution +3 at creation and per level; base Speed 5.

- [Hard as Bedrock](trait-dwarf-deep-constitution.md) — Your ancestors did not dig a home from the mountain by yielding politely.
- [Stone Mason](trait-dwarf-stone-reader.md) — You know a mason’s lie by the stone around it.
- [Rooted in Stone](trait-dwarf-low-center.md) — Let the storm waste its strength on you.
- [An Honest Maker’s Hand](trait-dwarf-maker-s-eye.md) — Your thumb finds the seam; your ear knows the ring of sound metal.
- [A Grudge Well Kept](trait-dwarf-carved-history.md) — You remember who struck first. You intend them to remember what followed.

**Starting coin contribution:** +2 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
