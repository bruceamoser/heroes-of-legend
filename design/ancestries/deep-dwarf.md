# Deep Dwarf

*Grey skin, great watchful eyes and a quiet step beneath the weight of the world.*

The ancestors of Deep Dwarves went farther down. Over long generations, the people of the depths became shorter and slighter than their surface cousins. Their skin ranges through shades of grey, and their large eyes gather the faint light of the underground. Narrow shoulders and economical movements suit passages where a broad surface dwarf would struggle.

Many are naturally shy. They watch before approaching, speak after listening and prefer a familiar companion to the attention of a crowded room. Reserve does not diminish curiosity, courage or conviction. A quiet Deep Dwarf may be the first to notice danger and the one who refuses to abandon a friend.

Stone, darkness and small changes in air or vibration shape their experience. Those raised above ground bring the same sensitive bodies to unfamiliar surroundings. No ancestral instinct supplies a map of tunnels they have never walked.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP +2; base Speed 5.

- [Eyes for the Dark](trait-deep-dwarf-eyes-for-the-dark.md) — You study the passage in a faint wash of reflected light.
- [The Stone Tells You](trait-deep-dwarf-stone-tells-you.md) — You rest your palm against the floor and wait for the answering tread.
- [A Sense of Depth](trait-deep-dwarf-never-off-the-map.md) — You wake in the dark and know which wall lies beneath the mountain.
- [A Patient Breath](trait-deep-dwarf-holds-its-own.md) — You slow your breathing while the others hurry through the stale passage.
- [Through the Narrow Places](trait-deep-dwarf-where-tall-folk-cannot-stand.md) — You lower your head and slip beneath the fallen beam without breaking stride.

**Starting coin contribution:** +2 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
