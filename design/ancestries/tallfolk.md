# Tallfolk

*Long limbs, great hands and a world whose doorways rarely give you room.*

Tallfolk stand well above most neighboring peoples. Long arms, powerful frames and large hands make their scale evident even when they sit quietly. A borrowed chair, a crowded wagon or a low lintel can demand more thought than a heavy load.

Some enjoy the attention their height brings; others become practiced at folding themselves out of the way. Their bodies offer reach and leverage, but neither strength nor stature decides their character. A Tallfolk healer may work with extraordinary gentleness, while a shy scholar learns to endure being the first person everyone notices.

Their origins and communities belong to the campaign. They need no named giant ancestor or distant kingdom to take their place beside other adventurers. Choose how your hero lives at this scale, and which of its advantages they have learned to use.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP +5; base Speed 6.

- [Long Reach](trait-tallfolk-long-reach.md) — You catch the latch above your companions’ heads without rising onto your toes.
- [Broad Back](trait-tallfolk-broad-back.md) — You settle the awkward load across your shoulders and leave a hand free for the door.
- [A Big Shadow](trait-tallfolk-a-big-shadow.md) — You unfold to your full height, and wait for the room to notice.
- [Built for the Weather](trait-tallfolk-built-for-the-weather.md) — You pull your collar closed and keep pace through the driving sleet.
- [Long Stride](trait-tallfolk-you-take-the-blow.md) — You cross the courtyard in a few easy strides while your companions hurry to keep pace.

**Starting coin contribution:** +1 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
