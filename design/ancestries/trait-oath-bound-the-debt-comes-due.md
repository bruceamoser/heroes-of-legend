# The Debt Comes Due

*You lend your companion your certainty and feel your own next step grow heavier.*

## Rules

Once per day, before an ally within 3 squares rolls, spend your Reaction and name a concrete action you will attempt before the end of your next turn. Give the ally 2 Boons to that roll and immediately take 1 damage that bypasses resistance. The next roll you make for the named action before that deadline also takes 1 Bane. You must have at least 2 HP to use this trait. The damage is paid even if that action never occurs.

**Ancestry:** Oath-Bound · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Reaction.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
