# Wood Elf

*Short, dark-haired and quick; the next possibility is already tugging at your sleeve.*

Wood Elves are shorter and more lightly built than High Elves, with dark hair, nimble limbs and expressive, angular faces. Similar ears and facial structures have given both peoples the name elf in other tongues. A Wood Elf watching a High Elf deliberate may wonder how anyone can bear to leave an interesting idea untried for so long.

Quick movement often accompanies quick feeling. Curiosity, anger and affection can arrive with equal force; many Wood Elves trust the first useful opening and discover its consequences afterward. Their rashness can become daring, and their impulsiveness can become generosity. Neither prevents a Wood Elf from learning patience or taking a promise seriously.

Their bodies lend themselves to balance and movement. Woodlands give those gifts abundant expression, but a Wood Elf raised among rooftops, ships or crowded streets remains a Wood Elf. Their community determines what those quick hands learn to do.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP contribution +2 at creation and per level; base Speed 6.

- [Footfall on the Leaves](trait-elf-light-tread.md) — Even the fallen leaves seem reluctant to betray your passing.
- [A Lifetime Ago](trait-elf-age-of-memory.md) — You remember when the old road was new.
- [Nature’s Whispers](trait-elf-leaf-listener.md) — A startled bird tells you what a shouted warning never could.
- [Grace of the Elder Folk](trait-elf-unhurried-grace.md) — Where others hurry, you seem simply to arrive.
- [Dream beneath the Stars](trait-elf-star-familiar.md) — Your body rests while part of you walks old paths.

**Starting coin contribution:** +2 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
