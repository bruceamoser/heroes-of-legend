# Never Where You Aim

*The blow catches empty air. You are already a step nearer your friends.*

## Rules

Once per round, after an attack against you resolves and deals no damage, you may spend your Reaction to move 1 square into a legal unoccupied space. This movement costs no movement from your turn but follows ordinary terrain and movement restrictions; it does not cancel other effects of the attack.

**Ancestry:** Goblin · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Reaction.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
