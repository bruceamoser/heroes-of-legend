# Small Target

*You tuck yourself behind the low stone as the arrow strikes its edge.*

## Rules

While you have physical cover from a ranged attack, gain 1 Boon to your defense roll against that attack. Against a player-controlled attacker, apply 1 Bane to their attack roll instead. Apply only one of these modifiers to an attack. Cover must obstruct the attacker’s line to you; another creature’s presence alone is insufficient.

**Ancestry:** Gnome · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
