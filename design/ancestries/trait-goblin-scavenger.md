# Waste Nothing

*You turn the broken hinge over. There is good work left in it yet.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Knowledge rolls to repair an ordinary item using salvaged parts, or to recover usable materials from damaged equipment. You need access to the item and suitable tools. This creates no free finished item, coins or special crafting ingredients; recover only material actually present.

**Ancestry:** Goblin · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**Status:** Proposed ancestry review draft; not approved.
