# Bigger on the Inside

*Your heart races, yet your small frame keeps going.*

## Rules

Your heart races, yet your small frame keeps going. Gain 1 maximum HP and 1 Boon to rolls affected by Frightened.

**Ancestry:** Halfling · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.

**ID:** trait-halfling-stout-heart
