# A Sense of Depth

*You wake in the dark and know which wall lies beneath the mountain.*

## Rules

Underground, you always know which way is up and whether your route is rising or descending. Gain 1 Boon to Reason rolls to navigate underground or retrace a subterranean route. This reveals neither an unknown route nor what lies beyond a wall.

**Ancestry:** Deep Dwarf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
