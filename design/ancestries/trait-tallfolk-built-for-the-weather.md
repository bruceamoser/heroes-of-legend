# Built for the Weather

*Your body adjusts steadily to long exposure and travel.*

## Rules

Your body adjusts steadily to long exposure and travel. Gain 1 Boon to Fortitude rolls against natural cold, heat or the fatigue of a long march.

**Ancestry:** Tallfolk · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the description gives a time or action.

**Duration:** As described; changes to base statistics are permanent.
