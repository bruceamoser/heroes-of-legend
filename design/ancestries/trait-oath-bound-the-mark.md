# The Mark

*You uncover your palm, and the answering lines catch the light.*

## Rules

Choose a visible inherited mark on your skin. You may make it glow softly or cease glowing at will; it illuminates no neighboring square and cannot reveal Hidden creatures. Gain 1 Boon to Guile rolls when you openly stake your own reputation on a promise you intend to keep. It does not compel belief. Clothing may cover the mark.

**Ancestry:** Oath-Bound · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
