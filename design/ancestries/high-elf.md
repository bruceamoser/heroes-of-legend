# High Elf

*Tall, strong and deliberate; a long life leaves room to consider every word.*

High Elves are tall and strongly built, with pointed ears and the fine, angular facial structure that leads outsiders to call them kin to Wood Elves. They are a different ancestry. Beside a compact, restless Wood Elf, a High Elf has the presence of a standing stone: upright, composed and difficult to hurry.

Their long lives encourage a broad view of consequences. Many listen through an argument before speaking, practice a movement until it wastes no effort, or remember a slight long after its author has forgotten it. Confidence can harden into arrogance, especially when patience is mistaken for superior judgment. Others learn that living a long time is no guarantee of being right.

A High Elf might be a laborer, a wanderer or a scholar. Strength and deliberation belong to their physical and temperamental inheritance; wealth, titles and courtly manners depend on the life they have led.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP contribution +2 at creation and per level; base Speed 6.

- [The Practised Hand](trait-high-elf-practised-hand.md) — You turn the tiny fitting once, then set it exactly where it belongs.
- [Still Water](trait-high-elf-old-courtesies.md) — You let the clamor spend itself before deciding what deserves your attention.
- [Starlight Eyes](trait-high-elf-starlight-eyes.md) — Under the last light of evening, you can still count the stitches.
- [Measured Strength](trait-high-elf-name-that-opens-doors.md) — You set your feet, test the weight and lift without a wasted motion.
- [The Weight of Centuries](trait-high-elf-weight-of-centuries.md) — You close your eyes, and the room returns as it stood a lifetime ago.

**Starting coin contribution:** +2 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
