# Fingers in the Works

*You ease one small part out of place, and the whole machine loses its argument.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Knowledge rolls to build, repair, disable or alter a mechanical device, using suitable tools and materials. This grants no recipe, special item, damage increase or shortcut around an ability’s printed requirements.

**Ancestry:** Gnome · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**Status:** Proposed ancestry review draft; not approved.
