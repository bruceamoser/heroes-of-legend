# Halfling

**Status:** HP +1 and base Speed 5 are approved starting values. All five traits below are fresh proposals. Choose two. Culture and background are independent.

- [Stout Heart](trait-halfling-stout-heart.md): Gain 1 Boon to rolls affected by Frightened; cancel Banes normally, not immunity.

- [Small Passage](trait-halfling-small-passage.md): DA may allow passage through openings suited to your actual size; occupied-square rules still apply.

- [Quiet Company](trait-halfling-quiet-company.md): Gain 1 Boon to moving quietly outside combat; grants neither Hidden nor invisibility automatically.

- [Good Cheer](trait-halfling-good-cheer.md): Gain 1 Boon when a consequential social activity offers sincere comfort; no wound treatment permission.

- [Resilient](trait-halfling-resilient.md): Maximum HP +1.
