# Halfling

Quiet feet, a generous table and courage that surprises people who judge by height.

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar fantasy,
not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +1; base Speed 5. **Trait status:** revised proposals.

- [Bigger on the Inside](trait-halfling-stout-heart.md) — Your knees may shake. Your friends will never see you leave them.
- [Under Their Noses](trait-halfling-small-passage.md) — The grand folk rarely remember to look beneath the table.
- [Quiet as a Burrow](trait-halfling-quiet-company.md) — You learned to come home late without waking the household.
- [A Little Luck](trait-halfling-good-cheer.md) — The latch catches your coat a heartbeat before you step into trouble.
- [Second Breakfast](trait-halfling-resilient.md) — You know how to make a small comfort last.
