# Halfling

*Quiet feet, a generous table and courage that surprises people who judge by height.*

Halflings are small, compact people, larger than gnomes, with a gift for making limited space comfortable. Others often notice their height before noticing the person, a mistake a halfling may patiently correct or quietly put to use.

Hospitality and good company are familiar pleasures, but a generous table does not require an easy life. Courage can live beside trembling knees, a sharp tongue beside warm manners, and a taste for home beside an irresistible curiosity about the road. A halfling raised far from halfling communities brings their own customs to those possibilities.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. These are invitations to a familiar fantasy,
not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture.

**Starting values:** HP +1; base Speed 5; Size Small.

- [Bigger on the Inside](trait-halfling-stout-heart.md) — Your knees may shake. Your friends will never see you leave them.
- [Under Their Noses](trait-halfling-small-passage.md) — The grand folk rarely remember to look beneath the table.
- [Quick Tongue](trait-halfling-quiet-company.md) — You can talk your way through a negotiation, an argument or an accusation.
- [A Little Luck](trait-halfling-good-cheer.md) — The latch catches your coat a heartbeat before you step into trouble.
- [Endless Pockets](trait-halfling-resilient.md) — Your clothes have pockets in places that should not have pockets, and they hold more than they should.

**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
