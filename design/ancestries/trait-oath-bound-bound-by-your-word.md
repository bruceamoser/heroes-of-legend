# Bound by Your Word

*You remember what you promised, and find one more measure of resolve.*

## Rules

Once per day, before a roll to protect a person, place or object named in a promise you freely made, gain 2 Boons. State the promise and how this action protects its subject.

**Ancestry:** Oath-Bound · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**Status:** Batch review proposal; not approved.
