# An Honest Maker’s Hand

*Your thumb finds the seam; your ear knows the ring of sound metal.*

## Rules

After handling an ordinary metal or stone item, recognize shoddy workmanship and common material without a roll. Gain 1 Boon when a consequential inspection seeks subtler defects. This is not crafting or magical identification permission.

**Ancestry:** Dwarf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive unless the effect says otherwise.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**ID:** trait-dwarf-maker-s-eye
