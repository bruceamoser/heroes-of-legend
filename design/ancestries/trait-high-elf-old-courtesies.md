# Still Water

*You let the clamor spend itself before deciding what deserves your attention.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Reason rolls to recognize deception or maintain concentration amid noise, argument or distraction. This does not remove conditions or grant a Boon to attack or defense rolls.

**Ancestry:** High Elf · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.
