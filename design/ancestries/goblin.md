# Goblin

*Quick hands, shared effort and a future finally open to your own making.*

Goblins are small, quick-bodied people with prominent ears and alert, expressive faces. For generations, orcs enslaved them, and outsiders learned to associate goblins with the cruelty of those who held them. Freedom did not immediately erase that reputation. It did give goblins the chance to build lives whose purpose was their own.

Many now seek a place through useful work, trade, exploration and the steady proof of being a reliable neighbor. Resourcefulness honed under deprivation can become skilled repair, careful husbandry or a gift for seeing value where others see rubbish. Cooperation can become friendship and enterprise rather than a necessity of survival.

A goblin adventurer may want to support a community, win recognition or simply discover what freedom feels like beyond familiar roads. Others were born free and have never seen an orc. Their ancestry carries a history; each goblin chooses what to make of the life ahead.

## Rules

Choose two of the five traits below. Temperamental tendencies are invitations, not a required personality. Any ancestry may grow up in any culture. Only the printed rules grant mechanical benefits.

**Starting values:** HP +1; base Speed 6; Size Small.

- [Waste Nothing](trait-goblin-scavenger.md) — You turn the broken hinge over. There is good work left in it yet.
- [Better Together](trait-goblin-more-of-us-than-you.md) — Your companion draws their eye, and you know exactly when to step in.
- [Quick on Your Feet](trait-goblin-run-away-better.md) — You are halfway across the yard before the others have found their stride.
- [The Other Side of the Fire](trait-goblin-other-side-of-the-fire.md) — You recognize the guarded silence and make your offer without stepping closer.
- [Quick Hands](trait-goblin-never-where-you-aim.md) — The awkward bundle settles into your grip before anyone notices it slipping.

**Starting coin contribution:** +0 coins. See [starting coins](../starting-coins.md).
