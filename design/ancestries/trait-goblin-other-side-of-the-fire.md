# The Other Side of the Fire

*You recognize the guarded silence and make your offer without stepping closer.*

## Rules

Gain 1 Boon to Guile rolls to negotiate safe passage, an exchange or mutual aid with someone who is captive, displaced or treated as an outsider by the surrounding community. You need a shared means of communication.

**Ancestry:** Goblin · **Choice:** one of your two traits · **Action:** Passive.

**Duration:** While its stated circumstances apply; permanent for base statistics.

**Status:** Batch review proposal; not approved.
