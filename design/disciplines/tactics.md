# Tactics
Read the fight, make a plan and put someone in position to use it.

Attacks from Tactics use Knowledge and require a wielded melee weapon. A willing ally can follow a cue if they perceive and understand your signal. Boons from Tactics and Aid do not add together on a single roll. Apply only the highest grant from those sources. A creature can hold the Boons from only one Tactics ability at a time. A new grant replaces the old ability’s unused Boons.

## Openings

### Exploit the Opening I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Knowledge. Attack one visible adjacent enemy for 1/3/4 physical damage. On Standard or Strong, cue one willing ally within 3 squares of you. That ally may move 1 square after the attack, following the usual movement reactions.

### Exploit the Opening II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Exploit the Opening deals 2/4/5 physical damage.

### Exploit the Opening III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Movement granted by Exploit the Opening avoids reactions from the target of your attack.

### Divide Their Attention I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Knowledge. Attack one visible adjacent enemy for 2/4/6 physical damage. On Standard or Strong, choose one willing ally within 3 squares. It gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that enemy before your next turn starts.

### Divide Their Attention II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Divide Their Attention deals 3/5/7 physical damage.

### Divide Their Attention III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
On Strong, Divide Their Attention may grant its Boon to two willing allies within range.

### Spring the Trap
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Knowledge. Once per scene, attack a visible adjacent enemy for 4/6/8 physical damage. On Standard or Strong, up to three willing allies within 4 squares may each move 2 squares, avoiding movement reactions from the target. Each gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that target before your next turn starts. On Strong, the target also gains Dazed 1.

## Coordination

### Clear the Way I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Give one willing ally within 3 squares a cue and choose attack or defense. The ally gains 1 Boon on its next roll of that type before your next turn starts. No roll.

### Clear the Way II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Clear the Way reaches up to 5 squares.

### Clear the Way III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Clear the Way can instead cue the ally for a chosen noncombat task. Its next roll for that task gains the Boon during the exchange or work interval. Outside combat, contribute this aid or your own roll to the objective during that interval.

### Set the Formation I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Knowledge. Choose a visible enemy and up to two willing allies within 4 squares. On any result, one chosen ally may move 1 square without triggering movement reactions from that enemy. On Standard or Strong, each chosen ally gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that enemy before your next turn starts.

### Set the Formation II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Set the Formation allows every chosen ally to move the square.

### Set the Formation III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Set the Formation can choose up to three willing allies within range.

### The Perfect Moment
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver · Knowledge. Once per scene, choose a visible enemy and up to three willing allies within 6 squares. Each ally may move 1/2/3 squares without triggering movement reactions from that enemy. Each gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that enemy before your next turn starts, or 2 Boons on Strong.

## Contingencies

### Call the Warning I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. Before a willing ally within 3 squares rolls defense against an attack from an enemy you can see, give it 1 Boon on that defense if it can follow your cue. No separate roll.

### Call the Warning II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Call the Warning reaches up to 5 squares.

### Call the Warning III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
After a Strong defense aided by Call the Warning, the ally may move 1 square without triggering movement reactions from that attacker.

### On My Signal I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When a visible enemy within 4 squares ends voluntary movement, cue one willing ally within 4 squares. It may move up to 2 squares without triggering movement reactions from that enemy. No roll.

### On My Signal II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
On My Signal can cue two willing allies within range.

### On My Signal III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Each ally moving with On My Signal may move up to 3 squares.

### Roads Out
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction. Once per scene, when a visible enemy within 6 squares ends voluntary movement, choose up to three willing allies within 6 squares. You and each chosen ally may move up to 3 squares without triggering movement reactions. Each chosen ally gains 1 Boon on its next defense against that enemy before your next turn starts. No roll.

## Foresight

### Read the Pattern I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Observe one visible enemy. Until your next turn starts, gain 1 Boon on the next attack you make against it or the next defense you roll against it, whichever comes first. No roll.

### Read the Pattern II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
When you use Read the Pattern, you may first move 1 square. Follow the usual movement reactions.

### Read the Pattern III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
You may choose whether to keep the Boon for an attack roll or a defense roll against the chosen enemy when you use Read the Pattern.

### Rehearse the Exchange I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Knowledge. Study one visible enemy. Move up to 1 square, then gain 1 Boon on your next attack or next defense roll against it before your next turn starts. Choose which when you use this technique. On Strong, you may move up to 2 squares instead. Follow the usual movement reactions.

### Rehearse the Exchange II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Rehearse the Exchange gives 2 Boons instead of 1 on Strong.

### Rehearse the Exchange III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Movement from Rehearse the Exchange avoids movement reactions from the chosen enemy.

### Three Moves Ahead
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver · Knowledge. Once per scene, choose up to two visible enemies. Move up to 2/3/4 squares without triggering movement reactions from them. Until your next turn starts, gain 2 Boons on your next attack against a chosen enemy and 1 Boon on your next defense against a chosen enemy. These are two distinct rolls. Taking this stance replaces any unused Boons from your other Tactics techniques.
