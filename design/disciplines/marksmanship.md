# Marksmanship
Find the line between your hand and a distant mark.

Each attack requires a ready ranged weapon and ammunition or a held thrown weapon. Spend one projectile per target unless the technique says otherwise; a thrown weapon stays where it lands. Choose visible targets within the technique’s stated reach. Basic Ranged and Basic Throw can reach farther using the ordinary weapon range rule. Improvements affect only their named technique.

## Precision

### Measured Shot I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Agility. Attack one creature within 8 squares for 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Measured Shot II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Measured Shot deals 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Measured Shot III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Measured Shot has range 12 squares.

### Split the Flight I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Attack up to three creatures within 8 squares. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. Each is struck once.

### Split the Flight II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Split the Flight has range 12 squares.

### Split the Flight III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Split the Flight deals 2/3/5 physical damage. You may also spend a Main Action observing one visible creature within 12 squares to mark a vulnerable part of its anatomy or construction for this scene; no roll. The DA identifies a plausible exposed part, or states that none is visible.

### Anatomist’s Volley
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Agility. Attack up to three enemies within 12 squares. Roll once. Each takes 3/5/8 physical damage. For a target whose vulnerable part you know from Split the Flight’s study or evidence, apply that hit ignoring up to 2 physical resistance. Apply immunity and other reductions normally.

## Pinning

### Hobbling Aim I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Choose one visible creature within 8 squares. Your next Main Action ranged or thrown attack against it before this turn ends also applies Slowed 1 on Standard or Strong. This modifies one target of one attack. Reusing this technique replaces its unused effect.

### Hobbling Aim II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
On Strong, Hobbling Aim applies Slowed 2 instead of 1.

### Hobbling Aim III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
On Weak, Hobbling Aim also applies Slowed 1.

### Hold the Pass I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Choose up to two visible creatures within 8 squares. Before your turn ends, your next Main Action ranged or thrown attack applies Slowed 1 to each chosen creature it attacks, on any result. This grants no extra targets or attacks and replaces Hobbling Aim for that attack.

### Hold the Pass II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold the Pass can choose creatures within 12 squares.

### Hold the Pass III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold the Pass applies Slowed 2 instead of 1 to those targets.

### Close the Escape
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, choose up to three visible creatures within 12 squares. Your next Main Action ranged or thrown attack this turn applies Slowed 2 to each chosen creature it attacks; on Strong their voluntary movement becomes 0 until the end of their next turns. Forced movement still applies. This replaces Hold the Pass or Hobbling Aim on that attack and grants no extra targets.

## Readiness

### Watchful Shot I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction · Agility. When a visible enemy within 6 squares finishes voluntary movement, attack it for 1/2/3 physical damage after that movement.

### Watchful Shot II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Watchful Shot has range 8 squares.

### Watchful Shot III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Watchful Shot deals 1/2/4 physical damage. It can also respond when a visible enemy finishes forced movement within range.

### Covering Fire I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Agility. When an ally within 4 squares begins voluntary movement, attack one visible enemy within 8 squares for 1/2/4 physical damage before that movement. That ally’s first 2 squares of this movement trigger no reactions from that enemy.

### Covering Fire II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Covering Fire has attack range 12 squares and can protect an ally within 6 squares.

### Covering Fire III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Covering Fire deals 2/3/5 physical damage and protects that ally’s first 3 squares from the target’s movement reactions.

### Hunt at the Crossroads
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · 10 minutes. Prepare an ambush along a trail you have followed, at a site you have surveyed by actually examining its routes and sight lines. Choose up to four willing allies and a visible trigger line up to 6 squares long. Once before your next safe overnight rest, when a tracked enemy crosses the line, you and each chosen ally may spend an available Reaction to make one known basic attack against it if in range. These attacks gain 1 Boon and +2 damage. Preparation ends if you leave the site. Resolve awareness and initiative normally; preparation grants no additional surprise turn.

## Thrown Weapons

### Hurl I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Agility. Throw one held weapon at a creature within 4 squares for 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Hurl II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Hurl has range 6 squares.

### Hurl III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
After Hurl, draw one accessible replacement weapon without spending an action. This does not retrieve the thrown weapon.

### Ricochet I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Throw one weapon at two creatures within 4 squares of you and 2 squares of each other. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. The weapon lands beside the second target.

### Ricochet II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Ricochet has range 6 squares from you; its targets must still be within 2 squares of each other.

### Ricochet III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Ricochet deals 2/3/5 physical damage to each target.

### Steel across the Gap
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Agility. Jump up to 4 squares to a visible legal landing without movement reactions. At any point during the jump, throw one held weapon at up to two visible enemies within 6 squares of you and 2 squares of each other. Roll once; each takes 3/5/7 physical damage. The weapon lands beside the second target, or beside the only target if there is one.
