# Scholarship
Ask the question that turns an old fact into a useful one.

Bring a question and the evidence you can examine. The DA sets the objective and gives truthful findings supported by that evidence, including what remains uncertain. After completing an investigation, pursue a new question or new evidence. Learn spells and recipes through their own study requirements.

## Investigation

### Read the Evidence I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Reason. Examine one accessible clue within reach; add 1/2/3 progress toward a concrete question about it. Searching a scene instead takes 10 minutes per attempt. You may also recall a relevant fact about a subject or reliable description: 1 minute, Knowledge, 1/2/3 progress toward a question set before rolling.

### Read the Evidence II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Evidence adds 1/3/4 progress to examination, scene searches and recall.

### Read the Evidence III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Evidence takes 5 minutes per scene-search attempt; recalling a fact takes a Main Action.

### Reconstruct the Scene I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason to compare up to three accessible clues, or 10 minutes to reconstruct a larger scene you can examine. Add 1/2/3 progress toward the order of up to three related events. On completion the DA states supported conclusions and gaps; this reveals no unseen thoughts.

### Reconstruct the Scene II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Reconstruct the Scene adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Reconstruct the Scene III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Reconstruct the Scene may connect up to five related events, with 1 Boon when at least two independent sources corroborate the sequence.

### The Truth before the Council
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · 10 minutes · Reason. Present your findings and their supporting evidence to up to four decision-makers who understand you. Use testimony, records, exhibits or an explanation of what you found. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to each relevant acceptance objective. On completion a listener acknowledges the supported facts and grants the attainable concession established with the DA. Unsupported claims and inaccessible facts remain unproven.

## Planning

### Survey the Ground I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver · Reason. Study one visible approach within 6 squares; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying its usable footing and visible hazards. Surveying a whole site takes 10 minutes per attempt instead. Concealed threats require evidence to discover.

### Survey the Ground II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Survey the Ground adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Survey the Ground III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Survey the Ground also reveals one feasible fallback route when its objective completes, if one exists.

### Prepared Retreat I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Once per scene, direct up to three willing allies within 6 squares along a visible or familiar fallback route. Each immediately moves 2 squares without movement reactions. They must perceive your instructions; changed or blocked routes provide no passage. No roll.

### Prepared Retreat II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Prepared Retreat may direct four allies and lets each move 3 squares.

### Prepared Retreat III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
You can also spend a Maneuver to point out an opening against a visible enemy to up to three allies within 6 squares. Each ally who perceives your cue gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that enemy before your next turn ends. Once per creature per scene. This grants no extra attacks or targets.

### Name the Decisive Weakness
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per creature per scene, describe an opening in a visible enemy’s stance, construction or tactics. Up to four allies within 6 squares who perceive your explanation gain 2 Boons on their next attack against it before your next turn ends; each such attack ignores 1 point of one matching resistance. Immunity remains. Use this benefit in place of Prepared Retreat’s attack Boon for that creature.

## Disputation

### Counterexample I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. Before a listener within 6 squares rolls to assess a claim, ask a question or talk through the claim with them. If the listener understands you, give that roll 1 Boon. Once per distinct claim. No roll of your own.

### Counterexample II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Counterexample grants 2 Boons.

### Counterexample III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Counterexample may aid two listeners assessing the same claim, before their rolls. Reaction: before an ally within 6 squares rolls defense against an attack you can perceive, point out the danger. If the ally understands you, give that defense 1 Boon. Once per attacker per scene.

### Correct the Record I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Knowledge. When a listener within 6 squares is about to act on a factual error, explain what it has missed before it acts. Add 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s objective for accepting your correction. On completion the listener understands the error and decides how to act. Once per claim per conversation. The listener must be able to understand you.

### Correct the Record II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Correct the Record adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Correct the Record III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Correct the Record may address up to three listeners with separate acceptance objectives, using one roll.

### Proof against Panic
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Knowledge. When a group within 6 squares is about to act on an alarming claim or misunderstanding, offer a credible correction it can understand before it acts. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to up to four listeners’ separate correction objectives. A listener whose objective completes also reduces Frightened arising from that mistaken belief by 2. Once per distinct claim per scene; true danger, magical compulsion and player decisions are unaffected.

## Languages

### Patient Translation I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · 10 minutes · Knowledge, with a glossary or parallel text. Add 1/2/3 progress toward translating one short passage in an unfamiliar nonmagical language. A reference is required; ciphers need a separate objective.

### Patient Translation II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Translation adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Patient Translation III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Translation takes 5 minutes per attempt.

### Bridge of Words I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · 10 minutes with a willing speaker and usable shared signs or reference text. Establish simple communication about directions, quantities and immediate needs for this conversation, without a roll. Abstract bargains and hidden meanings still require translation.

### Bridge of Words II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Bridge of Words takes 5 minutes and can include up to three willing speakers who share the unfamiliar language.

### Bridge of Words III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Once Bridge of Words is established, gain 1 Boon on translation rolls using notes or examples gathered from that conversation.

### The Lost Lexicon
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · 1 hour · Knowledge, with at least three substantial texts or a text and a willing fluent speaker. Add 3/5/7 progress toward reconstructing an unfamiliar nonmagical writing system. On completion create a working glossary that serves as a reference for Patient Translation for anyone who can read your notes. Missing vocabulary, ciphers and magical meanings remain separate questions; the glossary does not grant fluent speech.
