# Scholarship
Ask the question that turns an old fact into a useful one.

Information must be supported by accessible evidence. Knowledge of lore never grants an unlearned spell or recipe. The DA supplies truthful findings within the available evidence; unsupported answers remain unknown.

## Lore

### Recall the Record I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 minute · Knowledge. Examine a subject or reliable description; add 1/2/3 progress toward recalling a relevant fact. Before rolling, name the question and the DA sets its objective. You cannot retry unchanged evidence after completion.

### Recall the Record II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Recall the Record adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Recall the Record III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Recall the Record takes a Main Action per attempt instead of 1 minute.

### Name the Weakness
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. After you identify a creature’s documented weakness through Recall the Record, tell up to three allies within 6 squares. Each gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that creature before your next turn ends. Once per creature per encounter.

## Investigation

### Read the Evidence I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Reason. Examine a scene without disturbing it; add 1/2/3 progress toward answering one concrete question about its physical evidence. The DA sets the objective and identifies what access is required.

### Read the Evidence II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Evidence adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Read the Evidence III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Evidence takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Reconstruct the Scene
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes · Reason. At a scene already investigated, add 1/2/3 progress toward reconstructing the order of up to three related events. On completion, the DA distinguishes supported conclusions from gaps; this reveals no unseen thoughts.

## Languages

### Patient Translation I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Knowledge, with a glossary or parallel text. Add 1/2/3 progress toward translating one short passage in an unfamiliar nonmagical language. No reference means no attempt; ciphers need their own objective.

### Patient Translation II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Translation adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Patient Translation III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Translation takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Bridge of Words
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes with a willing speaker and usable shared signs or reference text. Establish simple communication about directions, quantities and immediate needs for this conversation. No roll; abstract bargains and hidden meanings still require translation.

## Planning

### Survey the Ground I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Reason. Inspect a visible site; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying one usable approach and its visible hazards. The DA sets the objective; concealed threats require evidence to discover.

### Survey the Ground II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Survey the Ground adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Survey the Ground III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Survey the Ground also reveals one feasible fallback route when its objective is completed, if any exists.

### Prepared Retreat
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. At a site you surveyed, direct up to three willing allies within 6 squares along the identified fallback route. Each immediately moves up to 2 squares without movement reactions. Once per encounter; blocked or changed routes provide no passage.
