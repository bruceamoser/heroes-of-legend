# Scholarship
Ask the question that turns an old fact into a useful one.

Bring a question and the evidence you can examine. The DA sets the objective and gives truthful findings supported by that evidence, including what remains uncertain. After completing an investigation, pursue a new question or new evidence. Learn spells and recipes through their own study requirements.

## Investigation

### Read the Evidence I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Reason. Examine one accessible clue within reach; add 1/2/3 progress toward a concrete question about it. Searching a scene instead takes 10 minutes per attempt. You may also recall a relevant fact about a subject or reliable description: 1 minute, Knowledge, 1/2/3 progress toward a question set before rolling.

### Read the Evidence II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Evidence adds 1/3/4 progress to examination, scene searches and recall.

### Read the Evidence III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Evidence takes 5 minutes per scene-search attempt; recalling a fact takes a Main Action.

### Reconstruct the Scene I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason to compare up to three clues already in hand, or 10 minutes to reconstruct a larger investigated scene. Add 1/2/3 progress toward the order of up to three related events. On completion the DA states supported conclusions and gaps; this reveals no unseen thoughts.

### Reconstruct the Scene II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Reconstruct the Scene adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Reconstruct the Scene III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Reconstruct the Scene may connect up to five related events, with 1 Boon when at least two independent sources corroborate the sequence.

### The Truth before the Council
DP: 5 | Level: 7
10 minutes · Reason. Present a case supported by investigated evidence, including a physical exhibit you can demonstrate, to up to four decision-makers who understand you. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to each relevant acceptance objective. On completion a listener acknowledges the supported facts and grants the attainable concession established with the DA. Unsupported claims and inaccessible facts remain unproven.

## Planning

### Survey the Ground I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver · Reason. Study one visible approach within 6 squares; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying its usable footing and visible hazards. Surveying a whole site takes 10 minutes per attempt instead. Concealed threats require evidence to discover.

### Survey the Ground II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Survey the Ground adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Survey the Ground III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Survey the Ground also reveals one feasible fallback route when its objective completes, if one exists.

### Prepared Retreat I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Once per scene, at a site you surveyed, direct up to three willing allies within 6 squares along its identified fallback route. Each immediately moves 2 squares without movement reactions. They must perceive your instructions; changed or blocked routes provide no passage. No roll.

### Prepared Retreat II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Prepared Retreat may direct four allies and lets each move 3 squares.

### Prepared Retreat III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
You can also name a weakness established by observation or reliable evidence as a Maneuver: tell up to three allies within 6 squares; each gains 1 Boon on its next attack against that creature before your next turn ends. Once per creature per scene. This grants no extra attacks or targets.

### Name the Decisive Weakness
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per creature per scene, expose one vulnerability in a visible enemy’s anatomy, construction or tactics that you established through evidence. Up to four allies within 6 squares who perceive your explanation gain 2 Boons on their next attack against it before your next turn ends; each such attack ignores 1 point of one matching resistance. Immunity remains. This replaces the preceding step’s weaker weakness benefit for that creature; it creates no vulnerability unsupported by the evidence.

## Disputation

### Counterexample I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. When someone presents a claim contradicting evidence you already possess, before a listener’s roll to assess that claim, identify the contradiction to that listener within 6 squares. If it understands you, give that roll 1 Boon. Once per distinct claim; invented objections grant no repeated opportunities. No roll of your own.

### Counterexample II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Counterexample grants 2 Boons.

### Counterexample III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Counterexample may aid two listeners assessing the same claim, before their rolls. Reaction: after you have seen a creature make the same attack earlier in this scene, warn one ally within 6 squares before its defense against that repeated attack; if it understands you, grant 1 defense Boon. Once per attacker per scene.

### Correct the Record I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Knowledge. When a listener within 6 squares is about to act on a factual error contradicted by evidence you can present, state the correction before the action. Add 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s objective for accepting that correction. On completion the listener understands the error; its decisions remain its own. Once per claim per conversation; the listener must perceive and understand the evidence.

### Correct the Record II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Correct the Record adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Correct the Record III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Correct the Record may address up to three listeners with separate acceptance objectives, using one roll.

### Proof against Panic
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Knowledge. When a false claim or misidentified visible danger is about to cause a group within 6 squares to flee, attack or abandon a task, present evidence contradicting it before that choice resolves. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to up to four listeners’ separate correction objectives. A listener whose objective completes also reduces Frightened arising from that mistaken belief by 2. Once per distinct claim per scene; true danger, magical compulsion and player decisions are unaffected.

## Languages

### Patient Translation I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
10 minutes · Knowledge, with a glossary or parallel text. Add 1/2/3 progress toward translating one short passage in an unfamiliar nonmagical language. A reference is required; ciphers need a separate objective.

### Patient Translation II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Translation adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Patient Translation III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Translation takes 5 minutes per attempt.

### Bridge of Words I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes with a willing speaker and usable shared signs or reference text. Establish simple communication about directions, quantities and immediate needs for this conversation, without a roll. Abstract bargains and hidden meanings still require translation.

### Bridge of Words II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Bridge of Words takes 5 minutes and can include up to three willing speakers who share the unfamiliar language.

### Bridge of Words III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Once Bridge of Words is established, gain 1 Boon on translation rolls using notes or examples gathered from that conversation.

### The Lost Lexicon
DP: 5 | Level: 7
1 hour · Knowledge, with at least three substantial texts or a text and a willing fluent speaker. Add 3/5/7 progress toward reconstructing an unfamiliar nonmagical writing system. On completion create a working glossary that serves as a reference for Patient Translation for anyone who can read your notes. Missing vocabulary, ciphers and magical meanings remain separate questions; the glossary does not grant fluent speech.
