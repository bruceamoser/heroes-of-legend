# Athletics
Find a way where the easy road ends.

Ordinary climbing, swimming and jumping remain available without this discipline. Extra movement requires a legal route and does not bypass barriers. Progress rolls use the task procedure in the introduction.

## Climbing

### Sure Ascent I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Brawn or Agility rolls to climb a surface with usable holds. You still need a feasible route.

### Sure Ascent II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Sure Ascent lets you climb 1 square per movement spent instead of paying extra for climbing; rolls for danger still apply.

### Sure Ascent III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Sure Ascent also lets you keep one hand free while stationary on a climb with usable holds.

### A Line for Everyone
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action. With rope and a secure anchor within reach, rig a line up to 10 squares long. Up to six creatures may climb it at normal movement cost while it remains secured. It cannot bridge unsupported open space.

## Leaping

### Running Vault I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Maneuver. After moving at least 2 squares this turn, jump up to 2 squares horizontally to a visible landing. Spend no additional movement. You cannot finish in occupied space.

### Running Vault II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Running Vault reaches up to 3 squares instead of 2.

### Running Vault III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Running Vault also ignores movement reactions during the jump.

### Follow My Leap
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Use Running Vault and guide one willing adjacent ally through the same jump immediately after you. The ally lands adjacent to your destination; both landing squares must be legal.

## Swimming

### Strong Swimmer I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Brawn rolls to swim through rough water. You still need breathable air and cannot swim through an impossible current.

### Strong Swimmer II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Strong Swimmer removes extra movement cost for swimming in calm water.

### Strong Swimmer III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Strong Swimmer lets you tow one willing or helpless creature no larger than you without additional swimming movement cost.

### Carry Them to Shore
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Swim up to 2/3/4 squares, towing one willing or helpless adjacent creature no larger than you. The current must permit passage. The passenger moves with you.

## Balance

### Catch Your Balance I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Reaction. When you would fall Prone, remain standing instead. You must be conscious and able to move; no roll.

### Catch Your Balance II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Catch Your Balance can also catch a ledge within your reach when you begin to fall, leaving you hanging instead of falling.

### Catch Your Balance III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
After Catch Your Balance, you may move 1 square to a safe unoccupied surface within reach without triggering reactions.

### Catch Them Falling
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. Catch one adjacent falling creature no larger than you. If you have secure footing and a free hand, bring it to a free adjacent square. Otherwise you both hang from a reachable secure hold. No hold means you cannot use this ability.
