# Athletics
Find a way where the easy road ends.

Climbing, swimming and jumping are ordinary actions. These techniques improve them, using real holds, breathable air, supports and available equipment. Movement requires a physically possible route and legal landing. A movement allowance is not permission to cross an impassable barrier.

## Climbing

### Sure Ascent I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action. Climb up to 2 squares along a surface with usable holds, paying no movement. If danger requires a roll, use Brawn or Agility as appropriate with 1 Boon; a Weak result advances at least 1 square where passage is physically possible, with the DA’s stated complication. You also gain that Boon on ordinary climbing rolls.

### Sure Ascent II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Sure Ascent lets you climb at 1 square per movement spent during ordinary movement. Its Main Action climbs up to 3 squares.

### Sure Ascent III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
You may keep one hand free while stationary on a climb with usable holds. Sure Ascent’s Main Action climbs up to 4 squares.

### A Line for Everyone I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action, with rope and a secure anchor within reach. Rig a line up to 10 squares long. Up to six creatures may climb it at normal movement cost while secured. The rope must reach the route and does not bridge unsupported open space. No roll.

### A Line for Everyone II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Line for Everyone can rig up to 15 squares of carried rope; creatures using it gain 1 Boon on climbing rolls for remaining hazards.

### A Line for Everyone III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Line for Everyone can serve up to ten creatures. With secure anchors at both ends that you can physically reach, it can form a supported crossing over open space; it needs enough rope and supports for the load.

### The Living Bridge
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action. With a sound rope secured to an anchor you can reach, brace a crossing up to 20 squares long that you have physically established. Until you move, release it or become unable to act, up to eight willing creatures can each traverse the line at normal movement cost with 2 Boons on any required climbing or balance rolls. You may support a load totaling up to twice your normal carrying capacity; greater loads require additional supports. The route and anchor must bear that load.

## Leaping

### Running Vault I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. After moving at least 2 squares this turn, jump up to 2 squares horizontally to a visible unoccupied landing. Spend no additional movement. Movement reactions apply.

### Running Vault II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Running Vault reaches up to 3 squares.

### Running Vault III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Running Vault ignores movement reactions during the jump.

### Follow My Leap I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Use Running Vault and guide one willing adjacent ally through the same jump immediately after you. It lands adjacent to your destination; both landings must be legal.

### Follow My Leap II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Follow My Leap reaches up to 4 squares horizontally.

### Follow My Leap III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Follow My Leap can guide two willing adjacent allies through the jump, each to a different legal square adjacent to your destination.

### Bounding Company
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, after moving at least 2 squares, leap up to 5 squares horizontally and guide up to four willing allies initially within 2 squares through the same crossing. Each lands within 2 squares of your landing in a separate legal square. These jumps trigger no movement reactions. All travelers must be able to move; this grants no flight beyond the landing.

## Balance

### Catch Your Balance I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. When you would fall Prone, remain standing instead. You must be conscious and able to move. No roll.

### Catch Your Balance II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Catch Your Balance can also catch a secure ledge within reach when you begin to fall, leaving you hanging instead. You need a free hand.

### Catch Your Balance III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
After Catch Your Balance, you may move 1 square to a safe unoccupied surface within reach without movement reactions.

### Catch Them Falling I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. Catch one adjacent falling creature no larger than you. With secure footing and a free hand, bring it into a free adjacent square. Otherwise, if a reachable hold supports both of you, you both hang there. Without a suitable hold this intervention is unavailable. No roll.

### Catch Them Falling II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Before Catch Them Falling, move 1 square along a safe surface without movement reactions to reach the falling creature.

### Catch Them Falling III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Catch Them Falling can catch a creature one Size larger than you when your footing and anchor support both creatures.

### Defy the Fall
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction. When a rope-linked group begins to fall, secure its rope to an anchor within reach or hold it yourself. Arrest the falls of up to four connected creatures whose combined weight the rope and anchor can bear; if holding it yourself, the load cannot exceed twice your normal carrying capacity. They hang from the rope and take no damage from this arrested fall. Spend a Main Action on each following turn to hold it yourself; releasing or losing the support resumes the fall. No roll.

## Swimming

### Strong Swimmer I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Brawn rolls to swim through rough water. You still need breathable air and a current that permits passage.

### Strong Swimmer II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Strong Swimmer removes extra movement cost for swimming in calm water.

### Strong Swimmer III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
You may tow one willing or helpless creature no larger than you without additional swimming movement cost.

### Carry Them to Shore I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Swim up to 2/3/4 squares, towing one willing or helpless adjacent creature no larger than you. The current must permit passage; the passenger moves with you.

### Carry Them to Shore II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Carry Them to Shore swims 3/4/5 squares.

### Carry Them to Shore III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Carry Them to Shore can tow two willing or helpless adjacent creatures, each no larger than you, using a hand or sound tether for each.

### Against the Flood
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Brawn. Swim up to 4/6/8 squares through passable water, towing up to four willing or helpless creatures on sound tethers. Their combined load cannot exceed twice your carrying capacity. During this movement you and those passengers gain 2 Boons on rolls against the current or floating debris. This does not provide air or protection from unrelated attacks.
