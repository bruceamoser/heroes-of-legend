# Arms
Distance, timing and the practiced turn of a weapon.

Your basic melee attack is already available. Every attack here requires a melee weapon. Improvements affect only their named ability.

## Bladework

### Measured Cut I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, you may move 1 square after the attack.

### Measured Cut II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Measured Cut deals +1 damage on every result: 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Measured Cut III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Measured Cut also applies Bleeding 1 on Strong.

### Sweeping Blade
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Attack up to three adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. Measured Cut improvements do not apply.

## Impact

### Driving Blow I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, push it 1 square directly away from you.

### Driving Blow II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Driving Blow pushes 2 squares instead of 1 on Strong.

### Driving Blow III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Driving Blow also applies Dazed 1 on Strong, whether or not the target can be pushed.

### Battering Stroke
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Deal 2/3/5 physical damage to one adjacent creature; push it up to 2 squares. If another creature blocks that push, both take 1 physical damage and the push ends.

## Disruption

### Hamstring I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, apply Slowed 1.

### Hamstring II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Hamstring applies Slowed 2 instead of Slowed 1 on Strong.

### Hamstring III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Hamstring deals +1 damage on every result: 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Pinning Strike
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, its movement is 0 until the end of its next turn. Forced movement still affects it.

## Dueling

### Blade Ward I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Reaction · After resolving your melee defense roll, before taking damage: reduce that attack’s damage by 2, minimum 1. No additional roll.

### Blade Ward II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Blade Ward reduces damage by 3 instead of 2.

### Blade Ward III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
After Blade Ward resolves, you may move 1 square. This movement does not trigger reactions.

### Riposte
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · After resolving a melee attack against you, attack its source if adjacent. Roll Brawn for 2/3/5 physical damage. You must still have your reaction available.
