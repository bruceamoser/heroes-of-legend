# Arms
Distance, timing and the practiced turn of a weapon.

Bladework, Impact and Dueling require a wielded melee weapon. Unarmed techniques use a free hand, foot or similar natural contact; a held weapon or shield is not an unarmed strike. Each attack uses its printed damage, rather than adding basic-attack damage. Improvements affect only their named technique. Movement requires a legal route; reactions to movement apply unless stated otherwise.

## Bladework

### Measured Cut I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Brawn or Agility, chosen before rolling. Attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. On Strong, you may move 1 square after the attack.

### Measured Cut II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Measured Cut deals 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Measured Cut III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Measured Cut also applies Bleeding 1 on Strong.

### Sweeping Blade I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Attack up to three adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. Each creature is struck once.

### Sweeping Blade II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Sweeping Blade deals 2/3/5 physical damage to each target.

### Sweeping Blade III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Sweeping Blade also pushes each target 1 square directly away on Strong. Resolve collisions normally.

### Iron Tempest
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Brawn. Move up to 3 squares, ignoring extra movement cost from difficult terrain, and attack up to three enemies adjacent at any point along that route. Roll once; each takes 3/5/8 physical damage. Each creature is struck once. Once per scene, you may instead strike four enemies for 4/7/10; after this sweep gain Stability 3 until your next turn starts.

## Impact

### Driving Opening I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Choose one visible creature. Before your turn ends, your next Main Action melee weapon attack against it also pushes it 1 square directly away from your attack position on Strong. This modifies one attack, not each target of a sweep. Reusing this technique replaces its unused effect.

### Driving Opening II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Driving Opening pushes 2 squares on Strong.

### Driving Opening III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Driving Opening also applies Slowed 2 on Strong to that target. Before rolling the modified attack against a living creature with positive HP, you may declare it nonlethal: if its damage would reduce the target to 0 HP, leave it at 1 HP and make it Unconscious for 1 minute or until it next takes damage. It falls Prone.

### Battering Stroke I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Prepare your next Main Action melee weapon attack against one chosen visible creature before this turn ends. That attack pushes the target 2 squares directly away on any result; resolve collisions normally. On Strong it also applies Dazed 1. This replaces Driving Opening on that attack.

### Battering Stroke II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Battering Stroke also applies Slowed 2 on Strong. Its modified attack may use Driving Opening’s learned nonlethal declaration.

### Battering Stroke III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Battering Stroke also sets the target’s movement to 0 until the end of its next turn on Strong. Forced movement still affects it.

### Break the Encirclement
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, move up to 4 squares, including a horizontal jump up to 3 squares to a visible landing, without movement reactions. Prepare Battering Stroke for your next Main Action melee weapon attack this turn. After that attack, up to two willing allies within 3 squares of you may each move 2 squares without movement reactions. This grants no attack itself.

## Dueling

### Blade Ward I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. After resolving your defense against a melee attack, before taking its damage, reduce that attack’s damage by 2 after resistance, respecting the attack minimum. No roll.

### Blade Ward II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Blade Ward reduces damage by 3 instead of 2.

### Blade Ward III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
After Blade Ward resolves, you may move 1 square without movement reactions.

### Riposte I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Brawn or Agility, chosen before rolling. After a melee attack against you resolves, attack its source if adjacent for 2/3/5 physical damage. You must survive and be able to act.

### Riposte II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Riposte deals 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Riposte III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
After Riposte, move 1 square without movement reactions; on Strong also apply Dazed 1 to its target.

### Duelist’s Boundary
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Agility. When a visible adjacent enemy begins an attack against you or an ally, before its roll, strike it for 3/5/8 physical damage. On Standard or Strong it also gains Dazed 1. If it remains able to attack, resolve its attack normally; reduce its damage by 3 after resistance, respecting the attack minimum. This spends your ordinary Reaction and grants no further retaliation.

## Unarmed

### Empty-Hand Strike I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Brawn or Agility, chosen before rolling. Strike one adjacent creature unarmed for 2/3/5 physical damage. Choose an available hand, foot or other natural contact; restraint that prevents that contact prevents the strike.

### Empty-Hand Strike II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Empty-Hand Strike deals 3/4/6 physical damage.

### Empty-Hand Strike III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Empty-Hand Strike also applies Slowed 1 on Strong. Before rolling against a living creature with positive HP, you may declare the strike nonlethal: if its damage would reduce the target to 0 HP, leave it at 1 HP, Prone and Unconscious for 1 minute or until it next takes damage.

### Flowing Hands I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn or Agility. Move up to 2 squares without movement reactions and strike up to two different creatures adjacent at any point of the route. Roll once; each takes 1/2/4 physical damage. You may make each strike nonlethal using Empty-Hand Strike’s declaration before rolling.

### Flowing Hands II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Flowing Hands deals 2/3/5 physical damage to each target.

### Flowing Hands III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Flowing Hands also applies Dazed 1 and pushes each target 2 squares directly away on Strong; resolve collisions normally.

### Empty-Hand Tempest
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Brawn or Agility. Move up to 4 squares without movement reactions and strike up to three different adjacent creatures along that route. Roll once; each takes 3/5/7 physical damage, and on Strong becomes Prone. You may declare these strikes nonlethal before rolling. Once per scene, after the strikes, move up to 2 further squares without movement reactions.
