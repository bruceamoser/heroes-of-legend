# Wards

Protect people and places; secure and unravel magic.

Begin studying this discipline for 1 DP, then choose one of its four spellbooks. Learn the book’s sections in order, adding their pages to your repertoire.

- [Edris’s Book of Protection](../spellbooks/protection.md).
- [Edris’s Book of Unraveling](../spellbooks/unraveling.md).
- [Edris’s Book of Sanctuary](../spellbooks/sanctuary.md).
- [Edris’s Book of Seals](../spellbooks/seals.md).
