# Subterfuge
Work between what people notice and what they assume.

Find cover, get your tools within reach and examine the mechanism or person you mean to outwit. The DA sets the objective and consequences before you roll. Work on components you can reach, and keep concealment between you and anyone watching. Use a Reaction when you perceive its stated trigger.

## Locks

### Patient Hand I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Guile, with lock tools. Examine and manipulate one reachable simple nonmagical fastening; add 1/2/3 progress toward opening it. The DA fixes the objective and work interval first: an intricate lock takes 10 minutes per attempt instead of a Main Action. Impossible mechanisms require another approach.

### Patient Hand II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Hand adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Patient Hand III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Hand takes 5 minutes rather than 10 on intricate locks.

### Open under Pressure I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Guile, with lock tools. Work on a nonmagical lock already examined through at least one Patient Hand attempt; add 1/2/3 progress. You may repeat this faster action until that lock opens, provided its workings remain accessible.

### Open under Pressure II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Open under Pressure adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Open under Pressure III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Open under Pressure gains 1 Boon when you have both lock and repair tools at hand.

### Silent Entry
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Guile for a simple fastening, or 1 minute for an intricate mechanism, with lock and repair tools. Add 3/5/7 progress toward opening one reachable nonmagical door, lock or fastening without visible damage. At completion you can close it to appear undisturbed to casual inspection. Separate alarms and traps remain separate tasks.

## Sleight

### Light Fingers I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver · Guile, with a free hand. Palm or plant one unattended palm-sized object within reach; add 1/2/3 progress toward doing so unnoticed. The DA sets the objective and whether scrutiny permits the attempt. The object moves when that objective completes.

### Light Fingers II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Light Fingers adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Light Fingers III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Light Fingers can also target an accessible object worn or carried by a distracted creature, other than a held weapon, secured container or worn armor.

### Switch the Prize I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Guile. Exchange a reachable palm-sized object with a similar object you carry. Use Light Fingers’ requirements and add 1/3/4 progress toward this single unnoticed-exchange objective. The exchange occurs on completion.

### Switch the Prize II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Switch the Prize gains 1 Boon when the replacement has been prepared to closely resemble the original.

### Switch the Prize III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Switch the Prize can exchange an object up to the size of a closed book if you have two free hands and a suitable distraction; other restrictions remain.

### The Perfect Substitution
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver · Guile during an ongoing conversation. Engage a listener who understands you while exchanging an accessible palm-sized object within reach for a similar object you carry. Roll once; add 2/4/6 progress to separately established deception and unnoticed-exchange objectives. The exchange occurs only when its own objective completes. Once per exchange, with a new plausible statement or distraction; repeating it unchanged gives no progress. Held weapons, armor and secured containers remain excluded.

## Traps

### Catch the Click I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. When you see a reachable nonmagical mechanism begin to trigger, warn one creature within 3 squares that can perceive you before its defense or avoidance roll; that roll gains 1 Boon. Once per triggering event. You can also inspect a suspected reachable trap for 1 minute, rolling Reason for 1/2/3 progress to identify its trigger and danger.

### Catch the Click II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Catch the Click’s inspection adds 1/3/4 progress; its warning reaches 6 squares.

### Catch the Click III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
After identifying a trap through that inspection, gain 1 Boon on Guile rolls to disarm it with suitable tools.

### Turn the Trap I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When a nonmagical trap you identified begins to trigger, use tools in hand to shift a reachable trigger or guide one willing adjacent creature 1 square to a legal destination before the trap resolves. This movement triggers no movement reactions; the trap then resolves normally at its actual area. Once per triggering event. You can also reconfigure an identified trap with 10 minutes and tools: roll Guile for 1/2/3 progress; completion establishes one reachable trigger or safe bypass without increasing damage or scale.

### Turn the Trap II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Turn the Trap’s reconfiguration adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Turn the Trap III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Turn the Trap can guide two willing adjacent creatures 1 square each before resolution, with separate legal destinations. Reaction: before your defense against an adjacent sighted attacker, scatter carried sand or grit with a free hand to gain 1 Boon on that defense. Consume the handful; once per attacker per scene. This grants no blindness condition and does not work on a creature that does not rely on sight.

### Read the Killing Ground
DP: 5 | Level: 7
10 minutes · Reason. Survey a visible area up to 6 squares across; add 3/5/7 progress to identify accessible nonmagical traps, triggers and a safe route. Completion reveals supported findings and inaccessible gaps. Companions following your exact unchanged route avoid triggering its identified traps. Reaction: when a companion within 6 squares starts to leave that route toward an identified trigger, warn it before the step; if it perceives you, it may cancel that step and choose another legal step using the same movement. Once per misstep; no extra movement.

## Stealth

### Quiet Passage I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Passive. Gain 1 Boon on Agility rolls to move quietly while concealed. Concealment needs cover or a plausible distraction.

### Quiet Passage II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Quiet Passage removes extra movement cost imposed solely by sneaking.

### Quiet Passage III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Quiet Passage extends its Boon to one willing adjacent companion moving with you through the same concealment.

### Vanish into Cover I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Agility. Move up to 2 squares into cover, then add 1/2/3 progress toward evading pursuers. Become Hidden when the DA’s objective completes and concealment is plausible.

### Vanish into Cover II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Vanish into Cover adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Vanish into Cover III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Vanish into Cover moves up to 3 squares; this movement triggers no movement reactions from creatures that cannot see you when you begin moving.

### The Hidden Road
DP: 5 | Level: 7
1 hour · Agility, with rope and concealment materials. Establish a concealed route up to 20 squares long across terrain you can physically traverse; add 3/5/7 progress toward the DA’s route objective. On completion, up to six companions following your instructions can traverse it at normal movement cost with 2 Boons to remain unnoticed. It creates no unsupported bridge. Discovery or damage ends these benefits until the route is repaired and concealed again.
