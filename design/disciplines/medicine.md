# Medicine
Recognize hurt, and work patiently to ease it.

Carry suitable medical supplies. First aid allows one attempt per recipient between safe overnight rests, shared across all healers; a healing technique uses that attempt when used, not only on a favorable result. HP never exceeds maximum. Condition care is separate. Willing or helpless patients can receive care; healing and stabilization use the ordinary death-tally rules. Reactions occur before the stated roll or consequence.

## First Aid

### Field Dressing I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action, with a free hand and bandaging: reduce an adjacent patient’s Bleeding by 2, minimum 0, without HP restoration or a first-aid attempt. Alternatively spend 10 minutes and roll Knowledge to restore 3/5/7 HP to a patient within reach, consuming its first-aid attempt.

### Field Dressing II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Field Dressing’s first aid restores 4/6/8 HP; its immediate bandaging reduces Bleeding by 3.

### Field Dressing III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Field Dressing’s first aid takes 5 minutes. Its bandaging can be performed as a Maneuver.

### Emergency Treatment I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Knowledge, with medical supplies. Apply Field Dressing’s current healing immediately to an adjacent patient and mark its first-aid attempt used.

### Emergency Treatment II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Emergency Treatment restores 5/7/9 HP instead of Field Dressing’s amount.

### Emergency Treatment III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Emergency Treatment also reduces Bleeding by 2, and you may move 1 square toward the patient before treating it without movement reactions.

### A Way through Death
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Knowledge, with medical supplies. Move up to 3 squares to a patient no larger than you, restore 5/8/11 HP using its available first-aid attempt, then carry it up to 3 squares and place it in a legal adjacent square. Both movements ignore movement reactions. You may rescue a patient whose allowance is spent without healing or consuming supplies. A free hand is needed to carry it; no armor training is required.

## Nursing

### Patient Care I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. With suitable water, blankets or other supplies and a free hand, reduce temporary Burning or Freezing on one adjacent patient by 2, minimum 0. No roll, HP restoration or first-aid attempt.

### Patient Care II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Care reduces the chosen condition by 3.

### Patient Care III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Patient Care treats two adjacent patients with supplies for both.

### Ready the Remedy I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Administer one prepared medical remedy you carry to an adjacent patient, using its printed effect. It must be a remedy ordinarily administered as a Main Action, not a spell, ritual or healing potion. Alternatively apply Patient Care and give that patient 1 Boon on its next Fortitude roll against an existing nonmagical disease before your next turn ends. No additional roll.

### Ready the Remedy II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
After Ready the Remedy administers a prepared dose, also reduce the patient’s temporary Bleeding by 1 with suitable bandaging.

### Ready the Remedy III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Ready the Remedy can administer two prepared doses to two different adjacent patients in the same Maneuver, consuming each dose. Each may also receive its bleeding care.

### Watch through the Night
DP: 5 | Level: 7
During a safe overnight rest, spend your two quiet hours tending up to four willing patients with suitable supplies. Each gains 2 Boons on its next Fortitude roll against an existing nonmagical disease before its next safe overnight rest, and reduces temporary Poisoned by 2 at the end of this care. Each patient benefits once per rest. Normal sleep, healing and recovery requirements remain; this is not an additional rest.

## Resuscitation

### Keep Them Breathing I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. Before an adjacent living creature rolls a death roll, support its breathing with a free hand and grant that roll 1 Boon. Apply its result normally. No extra roll or healing.

### Keep Them Breathing II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Keep Them Breathing grants 2 Boons.

### Keep Them Breathing III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Before Keep Them Breathing, move 1 square to reach the patient without movement reactions.

### Hold the Moment I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When a living patient within 2 squares is about to make a death roll, move up to 2 squares to reach it without movement reactions and use a free hand to grant that roll 2 Boons. After the roll, reduce its Bleeding by 2 with suitable bandaging. This spends one Reaction and does not grant another death roll.

### Hold the Moment II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold the Moment can reach a patient within 3 squares by moving up to 3 squares.

### Hold the Moment III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold the Moment also reduces temporary Burning or Freezing by 2 after the death roll if you have appropriate water or insulating supplies.

### One More Chance
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Knowledge. Once per safe overnight rest, when an adjacent living creature would receive its third death-failure mark, use a free hand and medical supplies before death resolves. Roll: on Weak it gains the mark normally; on Standard it remains Dying at two failure marks without adding the triggering mark; on Strong stabilize it at 0 HP, clearing both death tallies as normal. This provides no HP or Grit and cannot affect an already dead creature. A patient can benefit from this named intervention only once between its safe overnight rests, regardless of healer.

## Diagnosis

### Read the Symptoms I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · 10 minutes · Knowledge. Examine a patient with observable symptoms or a sample; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying a nonmagical disease or toxin and a plausible remedy.

### Read the Symptoms II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Symptoms adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Read the Symptoms III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Symptoms takes 5 minutes per attempt.

### Prepare the Remedy I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · 1 hour · Knowledge, with ingredients appropriate to a diagnosed toxin. Prepare one dose by adding 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s preparation objective. Administer the completed dose as a Main Action to reduce that toxin’s Poisoned by 2. Consume it on use. Other disease effects require their stated remedy.

### Prepare the Remedy II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Prepare the Remedy adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Prepare the Remedy III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Prepare the Remedy’s dose reduces its toxin’s Poisoned by 3 instead of 2.

### The Field Hospital
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · 30 minutes, with medical supplies and a safe prepared shelter. Treat up to six patients, using each available first-aid attempt. Roll Knowledge once; each restores 5/8/11 HP and reduces temporary Bleeding or Poisoned by 2, choosing separately per patient. A patient with a spent allowance receives condition care only. Supplies must cover all patients. This creates no extra first-aid allowance, SP recovery or overnight rest.
