# Medicine
Recognize hurt, and work patiently to ease it.

Carry medical supplies and use suitable treatment. First aid allows one attempt per recipient between safe overnight rests, shared across all healers. Healing never exceeds maximum HP.

## First Aid

### Field Dressing I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Knowledge, using medical supplies. Treat one creature within reach; restore 3/5/7 HP. This replaces ordinary first aid and consumes the recipient’s first-aid attempt.

### Field Dressing II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Field Dressing restores 4/6/8 HP instead of 3/5/7.

### Field Dressing III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Field Dressing takes 5 minutes instead of 10.

### Emergency Treatment
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Knowledge, using medical supplies. Apply Field Dressing’s current healing immediately to one adjacent creature. Mark the recipient’s first-aid attempt as used.

## Triage

### Staunch I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action. With a free hand and suitable bandaging, reduce an adjacent creature’s Bleeding by 2, minimum 0. This restores no HP and consumes no first-aid allowance.

### Staunch II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Staunch reduces Bleeding by 3 instead of 2.

### Staunch III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Staunch takes a Maneuver instead of a Main Action.

### Keep Them Breathing
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When an adjacent creature takes damage at 0 HP, grant 1 Boon to its immediate death roll. Declare before the roll. Apply that roll’s result to its death roll tallies.

## Diagnosis

### Read the Symptoms I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Knowledge. Examine a willing or helpless creature; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying a nonmagical disease or toxin and a plausible remedy. You need observable symptoms or a sample.

### Read the Symptoms II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Symptoms adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Read the Symptoms III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Symptoms takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Prepare the Remedy
DP: 3 | Level: 3
1 hour · Knowledge, with ingredients appropriate to a diagnosed toxin. Prepare one dose; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s preparation objective. Administer as a Main Action: reduce that toxin’s Poisoned by 2. Other disease effects need their stated remedy.

## Nursing

### Patient Care I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Main Action. Warm or cool one adjacent willing creature with suitable water, blankets or other supplies; reduce temporary Freezing or Burning by 2. No roll, HP restoration or first-aid expenditure.

### Patient Care II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Care reduces the chosen condition by 3 instead of 2.

### Patient Care III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Patient Care can treat two adjacent willing creatures with the same Main Action, with enough supplies for both.

### Watch through the Night
DP: 3 | Level: 3
During a safe overnight rest, tend up to four willing patients during your two quiet hours. Each gains 1 Boon on its next Fortitude roll against an existing nonmagical disease before its next overnight rest. Normal sleep and recovery requirements remain.
