# Wilderness
Read a living landscape and find your place within it.

These abilities use real terrain, animals and available resources. They do not summon food, compel animals or replace magical effects. Each search needs an accessible area with plausible supplies.

## Tracking

### Read the Trail I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Reason. Examine physical tracks; add 1/2/3 progress toward following a trail to the DA’s next landmark. Each completed objective reveals direction, approximate numbers and freshness.

### Read the Trail II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Trail adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Read the Trail III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Read the Trail takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Cut Them Off
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes · Reason. From a trail you have followed, identify a feasible interception route; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s objective. Completion reveals the route and travel time; it does not guarantee you arrive first.

## Foraging

### Wild Harvest I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 hour · Knowledge. Search a fresh area with edible resources. Gather 1/2/3 person-days of food, or that many person-days of drinkable water if a water source exists. The DA defines the area before rolling; it yields once per day to the whole party.

### Wild Harvest II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Wild Harvest yields 2/3/4 person-days instead of 1/2/3.

### Wild Harvest III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Wild Harvest gathers both food and water in the same hour when both resources exist; each uses the rolled yield.

### Find the Hidden Spring
DP: 3 | Level: 3
1 hour · Reason. Search terrain that could hold water; add 1/2/3 progress toward locating a concealed source. Completion reveals its location and visible hazards. It creates no water and does not purify a polluted source.

## Shelter

### Trail Shelter I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 hour, with available shelter materials. Prepare a dry campsite for up to two people against ordinary rain and wind. No roll. It does not make a dangerous location safe or accelerate overnight recovery.

### Trail Shelter II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Trail Shelter accommodates four people instead of two.

### Trail Shelter III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Trail Shelter takes 30 minutes instead of 1 hour.

### A Camp without a Trace
DP: 3 | Level: 3
30 minutes · Guile. Conceal a completed camp and its approach; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s concealment objective. Completion hides casual signs; direct search can still discover the camp.

## Animal Handling

### Gentle Hand I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 minute · Reason. Calm one nonmagical animal within 2 squares that can perceive you; add 1/2/3 progress toward ending its immediate fear or agitation. Provide a credible calming approach; immediate danger can prevent progress.

### Gentle Hand II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Gentle Hand adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Gentle Hand III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Gentle Hand can address up to three animals sharing the same cause of agitation as one objective.

### Lead the Herd
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes · Reason. Guide up to six calm nonmagical animals along a safe route; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s handling objective. Completion gets them moving with you; danger or mistreatment ends cooperation.
