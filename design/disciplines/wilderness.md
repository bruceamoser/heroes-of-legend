# Wilderness
Read a living landscape and find your place within it.

Use real terrain, available resources and credible handling of animals. The DA sets objectives, accessible areas and hazards before rolls. Food and water gathering yields once per area per day for the whole party; repeated searches do not refill it. Animals retain their own motives.

## Tracking

### Read the Trail I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Reason. Read tracks or signs immediately before you; add 1/2/3 progress toward identifying direction, approximate numbers and freshness. Following a trail to the next landmark takes 10 minutes per attempt instead. The DA defines each objective from accessible evidence.

### Read the Trail II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Trail adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Read the Trail III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Read the Trail takes 5 minutes per attempt when following a trail.

### Cut Them Off I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason to interpret a visible junction on a trail you followed, or 10 minutes to scout a wider interception route. Add 1/2/3 progress toward finding a physically feasible interception. Completion reveals the route and travel time, not a guarantee of arriving first.

### Cut Them Off II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Cut Them Off adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Cut Them Off III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
After completing Cut Them Off, you and up to four companions gain 1 Boon on travel rolls to traverse that identified route until its next landmark. Changed or blocked terrain requires a new route.

### Unerring Pursuit
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Reason at a visible junction, or 10 minutes to scout the surrounding area. From a trail you followed, add 3/5/7 progress toward predicting the quarry’s next reachable landmark from its tracks and known terrain. Completion identifies the best-supported destination and gaps in the evidence. You and up to six companions gain 2 Boons on pursuit and traversal rolls along that route until reaching it. This does not locate a quarry that left no readable evidence.

## Animal Handling

### Gentle Hand I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver · Reason. Reassure one nonmagical animal within 2 squares that can perceive you and is already receptive to your presence; add 1/2/3 progress toward calming its immediate fear. An unfamiliar agitated animal takes 1 minute per attempt instead. Immediate danger or an implausible approach prevents progress.

### Gentle Hand II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Gentle Hand adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Gentle Hand III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Gentle Hand may address three animals sharing the same cause of agitation as one objective.

### Lead the Herd I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver · Reason. Direct up to six calm nonmagical animals already cooperating with you toward a visible safe route; add 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s handling objective. Establishing initial cooperation takes 10 minutes per attempt instead. Completion gets them moving with you; danger or mistreatment ends cooperation.

### Lead the Herd II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Lead the Herd adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Lead the Herd III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Lead the Herd can guide twelve calm animals, and you gain 1 Boon on handling rolls to keep that herd together in ordinary travel.

### Wild Accord
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver · Reason. Give a familiar, cooperative nonmagical animal group of up to twelve one understandable direction: follow, wait, scatter to safety or approach a named visible place. Add 3/5/7 progress to the DA’s objective for overcoming present fear or confusion; ordinary cooperation with a safe instruction needs no roll. Completion gets the group carrying out that direction. This grants no attacks or additional combat turns; serious danger or mistreatment ends cooperation.

## Trailcraft

### Trail Warning I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. When a visible natural hazard begins to threaten a companion within 3 squares, before its avoidance roll, warn it. If it can perceive you, that roll gains 1 Boon. The hazard must be something you can observe, such as shifting footing or a falling branch. Once per hazard event.

### Trail Warning II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Trail Warning reaches 6 squares.

### Trail Warning III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Trail Warning may aid two companions affected by the same hazard, each before its roll.

### Turn Back the Danger I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When a visible natural hazard is about to affect one willing companion within 3 squares, guide it up to 1 square along a legal safe route before resolution if it perceives you. This triggers no movement reactions. Resolve the hazard at its actual location afterward; it gains 1 Boon on any remaining avoidance roll. Once per hazard event.

### Turn Back the Danger II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Turn Back the Danger reaches 6 squares.

### Turn Back the Danger III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Turn Back the Danger can guide two willing companions, each to a separate legal square, and grants 2 Boons to their remaining avoidance rolls.

### Safe Passage
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction. When one visible natural hazard threatens a group within 6 squares, warn up to six willing companions who can perceive you. Each may move up to 2 squares along a legal route you can see before the hazard resolves, without movement reactions. Each gains 2 Boons on any remaining avoidance roll against that event. Once per hazard event; it does not cancel the hazard, cross impassable terrain or reveal hidden threats.

## Provisioning

### Wild Harvest I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
1 hour · Knowledge. Search an accessible area with plausible resources; gather 1/2/3 person-days of food, or that much drinkable water if a source exists. The DA defines the area before rolling. You can also spend 1 hour with available materials to shelter two people from ordinary rain and wind, without a roll.

### Wild Harvest II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Wild Harvest yields 2/3/4 person-days. Its ordinary shelter accommodates four people.

### Wild Harvest III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Wild Harvest gathers both food and water in the same hour when both exist, each at the rolled yield. Its ordinary shelter takes 30 minutes.

### A Camp without a Trace I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
30 minutes · Guile. Conceal a completed camp and its approach; add 1/2/3 progress to the DA’s concealment objective. Completion hides casual signs; direct search remains possible. You can also search water-bearing terrain for 1 hour, rolling Reason for 1/2/3 progress toward finding a concealed spring; completion reveals its location and visible hazards, not purification.

### A Camp without a Trace II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Camp without a Trace adds 1/3/4 progress to concealment and spring searches.

### A Camp without a Trace III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Camp without a Trace’s ordinary shelter accommodates six people, and concealment attempts take 15 minutes instead of 30.

### Shelter against the Siege
DP: 5 | Level: 7
1 hour, with tools and enough sound structural and concealment materials. Build a concealed fortified shelter for up to eight people on stable ground. No roll. Occupants behind its walls gain 2 Boons on defense against attacks from outside; each door or wall section has 15 HP and physical resistance 2. Open doors provide no protection. The shelter does not automatically make overnight rest safe; ordinary object damage rules apply.
