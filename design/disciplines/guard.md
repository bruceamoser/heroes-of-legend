# Guard
Be where someone needs you before they have to ask.

These techniques require a held shield. Reductions apply after resistance and respect the minimum for attacks, except immunity. Use your ordinary Reaction for reactive techniques. Protection never supplies an extra defense roll or stacks copies of the same technique. All movement requires legal routes and destinations.

## Shieldwork

### Shield Bash I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Brawn. Attack one adjacent creature with your shield for 1/2/3 physical damage. On Strong push it 1 square directly away.

### Shield Bash II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Shield Bash deals 2/3/4 physical damage.

### Shield Bash III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Shield Bash also knocks its target Prone on Strong after the push.

### Break the Line I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Brawn. Attack up to two adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 1/2/3 physical damage and is pushed 1 square directly away. Resolve collisions normally.

### Break the Line II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Break the Line deals 2/3/4 physical damage.

### Break the Line III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Break the Line pushes 2 squares instead of 1; on Strong each target also becomes Prone.

### Shatter the Formation
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Brawn, with a melee weapon and shield. Attack up to three adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 2/4/6 physical damage and is pushed up to 2 squares directly away. On Strong each also becomes Prone. Blocked movement stops the push, not damage or Prone; resolve collisions normally. Each target is struck once.

## Shelter

### Sheltering Guard I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Choose one willing adjacent ally. Until your next turn starts it gains 1 Boon on ranged defenses while adjacent. Reusing this technique replaces its previous effect. No roll.

### Sheltering Guard II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Sheltering Guard can protect two adjacent allies.

### Sheltering Guard III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Sheltering Guard also gives each protected ally Stability 1 while adjacent.

### Shield Canopy I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Until your next turn starts, you and up to three willing adjacent allies reduce ranged attack damage by 2 after resistance while they remain adjacent. Reusing this technique replaces its previous effect. No roll.

### Shield Canopy II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
When you use Shield Canopy, you may first move 1 square toward an ally without movement reactions; choose protected allies after moving.

### Shield Canopy III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Shield Canopy also gives its protected creatures 1 Boon on melee defense while they remain adjacent.

### The Moving Bulwark
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, choose up to four willing allies within 3 squares who can perceive your signal. You and each chosen ally may move 2 squares without movement reactions. Until your next turn starts, you and chosen allies within 3 squares gain 1 Boon on defense and reduce attack damage by 3 after resistance. Leaving range suspends the protection until returning. No roll.

## Interception

### Interpose I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. After an adjacent ally rolls defense, before applying physical damage from that attack, reduce that damage by 1 after resistance. You must be able to reach the ally. No roll.

### Interpose II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Interpose reduces physical damage by 2.

### Interpose III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Before Interpose you may move 1 square to reach the ally without movement reactions.

### Take the Blow I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. Before an adjacent ally rolls defense against a single-target attack, become its target if the attacker can legally target you. Roll your defense and receive all effects; the ally receives none.

### Take the Blow II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Before Take the Blow you may move up to 2 squares without movement reactions to reach an ally within 2 squares. Become a legal target before substituting.

### Take the Blow III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
When Take the Blow intercepts a melee attack immediately following the attacker’s voluntary movement, give your defense 1 Boon. After it resolves, the attacker’s remaining voluntary movement this turn becomes 0; forced movement still applies.

### The Last Safe Step
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction. When an ally within 4 squares is about to take damage from an attack or fall, move up to 4 squares to reach it. If you reach it, move together up to 2 more squares and reduce that damage by 4 after resistance. Neither creature triggers movement reactions during this rescue. An attack still resolves against its original target; a fall ends only if the destination supports both creatures. Fall damage can reach 0. No roll beyond the original defense.

## Watchfulness

### Guarded Passage I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. Before an adjacent ally rolls defense against a reaction triggered by its movement, grant that defense 1 Boon. No roll.

### Guarded Passage II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Guarded Passage grants 2 Boons instead of 1.

### Guarded Passage III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Guarded Passage also reduces damage from that reaction by 1 after resistance.

### Rescue Step I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Move up to 2 squares to a willing ally, then move with it up to 2 more squares. Both creatures ignore movement reactions during this movement and finish adjacent. You can carry a helpless ally no larger than you instead, with a free hand and normal carrying limits. No roll.

### Rescue Step II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Rescue Step’s initial approach may cover 3 squares instead of 2.

### Rescue Step III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Rescue Step may escort two willing allies together, each starting and finishing adjacent to you; alternatively carry one helpless ally as before.

### The Long Road
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action. Once per scene, guide up to four willing allies initially within 3 squares along legal routes up to 6 squares each, moving up to 6 squares yourself. All ignore movement reactions and must finish within 3 squares of you. Instead of one willing traveler, carry a helpless creature no larger than you, without additional encumbrance for this move; place it adjacent afterward. This does not heal or stabilize. No roll.
