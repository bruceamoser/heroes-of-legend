# Craft
See the useful thing waiting inside the material.

Use suitable tools, materials and a place to work. No ability creates free materials or waives a recipe’s ingredients. Record progress for one project at a time; its target and work interval are fixed before rolling. Crafting is individual work.

## Repairs

### Mend and Fit I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
10 minutes · Knowledge, with tools and matching materials. Repair one nonmagical item; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s repair objective. The item must retain enough structure to repair.

### Mend and Fit II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Mend and Fit adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Mend and Fit III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Mend and Fit takes 5 minutes per attempt instead of 10.

### Field Repair
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Knowledge, with tools and materials. Work on an item already examined with Mend and Fit; add 1/2/3 repair progress. Until it is repaired, you may repeat this faster action. It grants no improvement beyond restoring function.

## Supplies

### Trail Workshop I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Passive. Learn two ordinary recipes from Bound Torches, Fletched Ammunition, Travel Food, Twisted Cord and Wooden Brace. With tools, work at the recipe’s interval and roll Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress toward its target, or 1/2/3 inventory steps if it produces a supply. Use its ingredients and output cap.

### Trail Workshop II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Trail Workshop teaches two additional recipes from that list.

### Trail Workshop III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Trail Workshop adds 1/3/4 progress or inventory steps instead of 1/2/3 when making those recipes. Output caps still apply.

### Make Do
DP: 3 | Level: 3
10 minutes · Knowledge, with tools and suitable salvage. Improvise one ordinary hook, wedge, lever or similar simple tool; add 1/2/3 progress toward target 3. It lasts for one task. This cannot create weapons, armor, consumable ingredients or valuables.

## Construction

### Raise a Frame I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 hour · Knowledge, with tools and structural materials. Build a nonmagical brace, barricade or shelter; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s construction objective. Fix size, strength and material requirements before work.

### Raise a Frame II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Raise a Frame adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Raise a Frame III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Raise a Frame takes 30 minutes per attempt instead of 1 hour.

### A Roof before Nightfall
DP: 3 | Level: 3
1 hour, with tools and enough sound materials. Build a weatherproof shelter for six people on stable ground. No roll. It resists ordinary weather, not attacks or extreme hazards; the location must still be safe for overnight recovery.

## Mixtures

### Smoke in a Bottle I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
1 hour · Knowledge, with alchemical tools and one purchased batch of smoke ingredients. Add 1/2/3 progress toward target 3 to prepare one smoke flask. Main Action to throw within 4 squares: smoke fills a 1-square-radius area until your next turn ends; sight through it is blocked.

### Smoke in a Bottle II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Smoke in a Bottle flasks have throwing range 6 squares instead of 4.

### Smoke in a Bottle III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Smoke in a Bottle flasks last until the end of your second turn after throwing instead of your next turn.

### Screen the Retreat
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action. Throw two prepared Smoke in a Bottle flasks at separate points within their range. Resolve each normally. This consumes both flasks and grants no free ingredients.
