# Craft
See the useful thing waiting inside the material.

Use suitable tools, materials and a place to work. No technique supplies missing ingredients or makes valuable material from nothing. The DA fixes a project’s objective, work interval, size and required materials before rolling. Crafting is individual work; no task gains repeated progress just because several characters describe the same completed step.

## Repairs

### Mend and Fit I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action, with tools and a suitable brace or fastening: temporarily secure one damaged nonmagical item within reach so it can support its ordinary stationary load until your next turn ends. It gains no HP and cannot attack or protect from attacks while broken. Alternatively work for 10 minutes and roll Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress toward repairing an item that retains enough structure.

### Mend and Fit II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Mend and Fit’s repair work adds 1/3/4 progress. Its temporary brace lasts 1 minute unless the item moves or receives further damage.

### Mend and Fit III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Mend and Fit’s repair work takes 5 minutes per attempt.

### Field Repair I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Knowledge, with tools and materials. Repair an item already examined through at least one Mend and Fit work attempt; add 1/2/3 progress. Repeat this faster action until the repair completes. Completion restores its ordinary function; HP restoration for an object with HP is set as part of the repair objective before work.

### Field Repair II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Field Repair adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Field Repair III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Field Repair gains 1 Boon when you can reach and work on all damaged components.

### Make It Whole
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Knowledge, with tools and matching replacement parts. Add 3/5/7 progress toward repairing one examined nonmagical mechanism or object within reach, including a shattered item whose essential pieces or replacements are available. The DA fixes the complete repair objective and restored HP before work. Completing it restores normal function, not magical properties or expended ammunition. Large structures still require access to every affected section.

## Mixtures

### Smoke in a Bottle I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
1 hour · Knowledge, with alchemical tools and one purchased batch of smoke ingredients. Add 1/2/3 progress toward target 3 to prepare one smoke flask. Maneuver: throw a prepared flask to a visible point within 4 squares, consuming it. Smoke fills a 1-square-radius area until your next turn ends and blocks sight through it. No throwing roll.

### Smoke in a Bottle II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Smoke in a Bottle flasks have throwing range 6 squares.

### Smoke in a Bottle III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Smoke in a Bottle flasks last until the end of your second turn after throwing.

### Screen the Retreat I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Throw two prepared Smoke in a Bottle flasks at separate visible points within their range. Consume both and resolve each normally.

### Screen the Retreat II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Screen the Retreat may place its two flasks within 8 squares.

### Screen the Retreat III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
After Screen the Retreat, you and one willing adjacent ally may each move 1 square without movement reactions from creatures whose sight of you is blocked by the smoke.

### Screen the Company
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Throw up to three prepared smoke flasks at visible points within 8 squares, consuming each. Each cloud fills a 2-square radius until the end of your second turn after throwing. You and up to three willing allies within 3 squares may each move 2 squares afterward; creatures whose sight of a traveler is blocked by these clouds cannot make movement reactions against that movement. Smoke blocks allies’ sight as well. No roll.

## Safeguards

### Catch the Failure I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. When a visible component begins to fail during a nonmagical task you are attending within reach, use tools in hand to arrest it before the consequence resolves. Give the worker 1 Boon on the associated roll to prevent injury or material loss, if such a roll is possible. Once per distinct failure event; this does not improve the task’s progress roll or reverse damage already applied.

### Catch the Failure II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Catch the Failure grants 2 Boons.

### Catch the Failure III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Before Catch the Failure you may move 1 square along a safe route without movement reactions to reach the component.

### Save the Work I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction. When a visible component fails during a nonmagical project within reach, use a free hand and suitable tools to preserve one recoverable component or batch of ingredients from that failure. It grants no progress and does not prevent other consequences. Once per project between safe overnight rests; the DA identifies what can physically be caught or secured.

### Save the Work II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Save the Work may preserve two recoverable components or batches from the same failure.

### Save the Work III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Save the Work also grants the worker 2 Boons on its permitted roll against injury from that event, before that roll.

### Prevent Catastrophe
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction. Once per scene, when an observed nonmagical structure or mechanism within reach begins to collapse, jam or burst, use tools and a suitable support to hold it for one round before its failure resolves. It must be no larger than a doorway, wagon or 2-square section of a larger structure. Creatures have time to escape or repair it; unresolved failure occurs at the end of your next turn. Attacks, magic and continued damage resolve normally and can destroy the support sooner. No roll; the support must physically bear the load.

## Workshop

### Trail Workshop I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Passive. Learn two recipes from Bound Torches, Fletched Ammunition, Travel Food, Twisted Cord and Wooden Brace. With tools and ingredients, work at the recipe’s interval and roll Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress or inventory steps; its output cap applies. You may also build a brace, barricade or shelter with structural materials: 1 hour and Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s construction objective.

### Trail Workshop II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Trail Workshop teaches two more recipes from its list. Its construction work adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Trail Workshop III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Trail Workshop adds 1/3/4 progress or inventory steps to its recipes, respecting output caps; construction attempts take 30 minutes.

### A Roof before Nightfall I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
1 hour, with tools and sound materials on stable ground. Build a weatherproof shelter for six people without a roll. It resists ordinary weather, not attacks or extreme hazards. You can also improvise a simple hook, wedge, lever or similar tool from suitable salvage: 10 minutes and Knowledge for 1/2/3 progress toward target 3. It lasts one task and cannot be a weapon, armor, consumable ingredient or valuable.

### A Roof before Nightfall II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Roof before Nightfall accommodates eight people. Its improvised-tool work adds 1/3/4 progress.

### A Roof before Nightfall III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
A Roof before Nightfall takes 30 minutes with materials already gathered, and you gain 1 Boon on subsequent repair rolls for that shelter.

### A City Opens Its Gates
DP: 5 | Level: 7
1 day · Reason. Through an on-site demonstration of a feasible civic project, broker one agreement among up to four willing factions you can communicate with. Build a visible model or working example with real materials. Roll once; add 4/7/10 progress to each faction’s separate agreement objective. Completion secures the commitments established by the DA, such as access, labor or a ceasefire. New terms need a new objective; this creates neither resources nor control over a settlement.
