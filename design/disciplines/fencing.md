# Fencing
Find the distance where a deliberate step matters more than a heavy swing.

Fencing requires a one-handed melee weapon suited to precise cuts or thrusts, such as a saber, shortsword or rapier, wielded in one hand. Each attack uses its printed damage. Follow the usual movement reactions unless the technique says otherwise.

## Pointwork

### Direct Thrust I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Agility. Attack one adjacent creature for 1/2/4 physical damage. On Standard or Strong, you may move 1 square after the attack without triggering movement reactions.

### Direct Thrust II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Direct Thrust deals 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Direct Thrust III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
On Strong, Direct Thrust lets you move up to 2 squares.

### Broken Tempo I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Move up to 2 squares, then attack one adjacent creature for 2/3/5 physical damage. Neither this approach nor a 1-square step you may take immediately after the attack triggers movement reactions.

### Broken Tempo II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Broken Tempo deals 2/4/6 physical damage.

### Broken Tempo III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
On Strong, Broken Tempo also applies Dazed 1 to the target.

### The Unseen Line
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Agility. Move up to 4 squares and attack one enemy adjacent at any point along your route for 2/4/7 physical damage. On Strong, apply Dazed 1 and Bleeding 1. You may continue the route after the attack. This movement avoids movement reactions.

## Footwork

### Passing Step I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. Move 1 square without triggering movement reactions. No roll.

### Passing Step II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Passing Step lets you move up to 2 squares instead of 1.

### Passing Step III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
One square of Passing Step may cross a square occupied by a willing ally. You must end in a legal unoccupied square.

### Outside Measure I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Move up to 3 squares without triggering movement reactions. If you end adjacent to any enemy you were not adjacent to when you began this Maneuver, choose one of them. Gain 1 Boon on your next melee weapon attack against it this turn. No roll.

### Outside Measure II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
One square of Outside Measure may cross a square occupied by a willing ally. You must end in a legal unoccupied square.

### Outside Measure III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Outside Measure lets you move up to 4 squares.

### Needle Through the Press
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, move up to 5 squares without triggering movement reactions, ignoring extra movement cost from difficult terrain. You may pass through occupied squares and must end in a legal unoccupied square. Choose one enemy you passed adjacent to. Gain 2 Boons on your next melee weapon attack against that enemy this turn. This replaces any unused Boons from Outside Measure. No roll.

## Blade Contact

### Deflecting Parry I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction. Before you roll defense against an adjacent enemy’s melee weapon attack, add 1 Boon to that defense. No separate roll.

### Deflecting Parry II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
On a Strong defense, Deflecting Parry lets you move 1 square after the attack without triggering movement reactions.

### Deflecting Parry III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Deflecting Parry works against melee attacks from adjacent enemies using weapons, natural attacks or unarmed strikes.

### Answering Steel I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Agility. When an adjacent enemy finishes a melee attack against you and your defense was Strong, strike it for 1/2/4 physical damage. You must be able to act and the enemy must still be adjacent.

### Answering Steel II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Answering Steel deals 2/3/5 physical damage.

### Answering Steel III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
You may use Answering Steel after a Standard or Strong defense.

### Counter in Tempo
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Agility. When an adjacent enemy finishes a melee attack against you or an adjacent ally and the defense was Standard or Strong, attack it for 2/4/7 physical damage. On Strong it gains Dazed 1. After your counter, you may move up to 2 squares without triggering movement reactions. You must be able to act and the enemy must still be adjacent.

## Blade Tricks

### Bind the Blade I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Agility. Attack an adjacent enemy wielding a melee weapon for 1/2/3 physical damage. On Strong, that weapon’s next attack takes 1 Bane if made before your next turn starts.

### Bind the Blade II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Bind the Blade imposes its Bane on Standard or Strong.

### Bind the Blade III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Bind the Blade deals 1/3/4 physical damage.

### Strip the Grip I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Agility. Attack an adjacent enemy holding a weapon for 1/3/4 physical damage. On Strong it drops one held weapon of your choice in its square if it can release the weapon. If it cannot release the weapon, its next attack with it takes 1 Bane before your next turn starts.

### Strip the Grip II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
On Standard, choose one of the target’s held weapons. Its next attack with that weapon takes 1 Bane if made before your next turn starts.

### Strip the Grip III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
On Strong, you may move the dropped weapon into an unoccupied square adjacent to the target instead.

### Surrender the Steel
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Agility. Once per scene, attack an adjacent enemy holding a weapon for 2/4/6 physical damage. On Standard or Strong it drops one held weapon of your choice if it can release it. Move the dropped weapon up to 2 squares along a clear route to an unoccupied square. On Strong, you may instead guide it up to 2 squares along a clear route into your free hand. On Standard or Strong, if the target cannot release the weapon, it takes 2 Banes on its next attack with that weapon before your next turn starts.
