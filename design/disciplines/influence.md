# Influence
Open a door, steady a friend or turn a room.

Use social objectives: the DA decides credible approaches, attainable concessions and what completes an exchange. A roll changes progress, never another player’s decisions. Another roll requires a new fact, offer or changed circumstance. Brief appeals can fit a Main Action; negotiations take the time their conversation needs. Use a Reaction when its stated trigger occurs during the exchange.

## Negotiation

### Make the Case I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Main Action · Reason for a brief appeal, or one exchange for a longer discussion. Present a credible argument or offer to someone who understands you; add 1/2/3 progress toward the social objective established by the DA.

### Make the Case II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Make the Case adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Make the Case III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Once per objective, add 1 extra progress to Make the Case when you answer a listener’s concern with a new fact, concrete offer or demonstration.

### Common Ground I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason for a brief proposal, or one exchange for detailed terms. Propose common terms to two willing negotiating sides who understand you. Roll once; add 1/2/3 progress to each side’s separate acceptance objective. Each side retains its established boundaries.

### Common Ground II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Common Ground adds 1/3/4 progress to each objective.

### Common Ground III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Common Ground can address three willing negotiating sides with separate objectives.

### A Treaty in Common Words
DP: 5 | Level: 7
30 minutes · Reason. With willing representatives of up to three sides and a working language, interpreter or shared reference text, formulate one concrete agreement. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to each side’s acceptance objective. On completion the DA states that side’s commitments and known reservations plainly. A shared reference permits simple terms; complex terms need adequate translation. No side accepts impossible terms, and the agreement is not magical enforcement.

## Leadership

### Steady Voice I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Maneuver. One willing ally within 4 squares who hears and understands your reassurance reduces Frightened by 1. No roll.

### Steady Voice II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Steady Voice reduces Frightened by 2.

### Steady Voice III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Steady Voice affects two willing allies within range.

### Rally the Line I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Maneuver. Up to three willing allies within 6 squares who perceive and understand your signal reduce Frightened by 2. One of them may also move 1 square toward you without movement reactions. No roll.

### Rally the Line II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Rally the Line can affect four allies.

### Rally the Line III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Each ally affected by Rally the Line may move 1 square toward you without movement reactions.

### Hold the Company Together
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Maneuver. Once per scene, up to four willing allies within 6 squares who perceive and understand you reduce Frightened and Dazed by 2 each, gain 1 Boon on their next defense before your next turn starts, and may move 2 squares toward you without movement reactions. This grants no additional actions or Reactions. No roll.

## Rejoinder

### Plausible Rejoinder I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Reaction · Guile. When a listener who understands you challenges your account or questions your distraction, answer before its response resolves; add 1/2/3 progress toward the DA’s deception objective. Once per distinct challenge per exchange. You may also present an ordinary plausible false account during an exchange using the same progress; repetition and impossible claims earn no progress.

### Plausible Rejoinder II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Plausible Rejoinder adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Plausible Rejoinder III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Once per objective, add 1 extra progress to Plausible Rejoinder when you give the listener a new reason to believe the account, such as a telling detail, prop or supporting witness.

### Hold Their Attention I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Reaction · Guile. When up to three listeners within 6 squares who understand you begin to turn attention from your ongoing plausible distraction, renew it with a new detail before they act. Add 1/2/3 progress toward holding their attention for one minute. On completion allies gain 1 Boon on unnoticed tasks outside those listeners’ direct sight for that minute. Once per distinct interruption; you may also begin this distraction through an ordinary exchange.

### Hold Their Attention II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold Their Attention adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Hold Their Attention III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Hold Their Attention can engage five listeners; an immediate obvious danger ends its distraction.

### Turn the Conversation
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Reaction · Guile. Once per conversation, when listeners are about to dismiss your distraction or false account, offer a credible alternative explanation before they act. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to up to four listeners’ separate objectives for accepting it. Each completed objective keeps that listener engaged for one minute, giving allies 2 Boons on unnoticed tasks outside its sight during that time. Contradictory proof and impossible explanations cannot be talked away; direct danger ends the distraction.

## Pressure

### Draw the Line I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
One exchange · Brawn. State a credible immediate consequence you can carry out to a listener who perceives and understands the threat; add 1/2/3 progress toward a limited concession established by the DA.

### Draw the Line II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Draw the Line adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Draw the Line III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Once per objective, add 1 extra progress to Draw the Line when you show or explain how you could carry out the consequence without attacking the listener.

### Terms of Surrender I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason. Offer a feasible surrender to up to three enemies who understand you. Roll once; add 1/2/3 progress to their shared objective if they share a motive, otherwise to one chosen enemy’s objective. They continue acting until an objective completes.

### Terms of Surrender II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Terms of Surrender adds 1/3/4 progress.

### Terms of Surrender III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Terms of Surrender can address six enemies with a shared motive; otherwise choose one as before.

### A Way to Yield
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Main Action · Reason. Once per scene, offer a credible, immediately available path out of a conflict to up to six enemies who understand you: surrender under stated protection, withdraw through an open route, or release captives for safe passage. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to their shared objective if their motives align, otherwise one chosen enemy’s objective. On completion they accept the established concession while you honor its terms. This grants no automatic ceasefire before completion and no control over independent allies.
