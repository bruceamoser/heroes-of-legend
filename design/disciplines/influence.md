# Influence
Open a door, steady a friend or turn a room.

Use the social objective procedure. A roll changes progress, never another player’s decisions. The DA determines credible approaches and attainable concessions. Arguments need a new fact, offer or changed circumstance to earn another roll; repetition earns no progress.

## Negotiation

### Make the Case I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
One exchange · Reason. Present a credible argument or offer to someone who can hear and understand you. Add 1/2/3 progress toward an agreed social objective; the DA judges whether the approach applies.

### Make the Case II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Make the Case adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Make the Case III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Once per objective, presenting new evidence that directly addresses the listener’s stated concern adds 1 extra progress to Make the Case.

### Common Ground
DP: 3 | Level: 3
One exchange · Reason. Propose terms to two willing negotiating sides that can hear you. Roll once and add 1/2/3 progress to each side’s separate acceptance objective. Each side still decides within its established boundaries.

## Leadership

### Steady Voice I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
Maneuver. One willing ally within 4 squares who can hear you reduces Frightened by 1. No roll; it must understand your reassurance.

### Steady Voice II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Steady Voice reduces Frightened by 2 instead of 1.

### Steady Voice III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Steady Voice can affect two willing allies within range instead of one.

### Rally the Line
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action. Up to four willing allies within 6 squares who can hear and understand you reduce Frightened by 2 and may each move 1 square toward you. This movement does not trigger reactions.

## Deception

### Plausible Story I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
One exchange · Guile. Offer a plausible false account to listeners who understand you; add 1/2/3 progress toward a defined deception objective. Contradictory proof or impossible claims can make the objective unavailable.

### Plausible Story II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Plausible Story adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Plausible Story III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Once per objective, a relevant prepared prop or corroborating witness adds 1 extra progress to Plausible Story.

### Hold Their Attention
DP: 3 | Level: 3
One exchange · Guile. Engage up to three listeners in a plausible distraction; add 1/2/3 progress toward holding their attention for one minute. Completion gives allies 1 Boon on unnoticed tasks outside those listeners’ direct sight for that minute.

## Pressure

### Draw the Line I
DP: 1 | Level: 0
One exchange · Brawn. State a credible immediate consequence that you can carry out; add 1/2/3 progress toward a limited concession. The listener must perceive and understand the threat. The DA determines its credibility.

### Draw the Line II
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Draw the Line adds 1/3/4 progress instead of 1/2/3.

### Draw the Line III
DP: 2 | Level: 0
Once per objective, demonstrating the threatened capability without attacking the listener adds 1 extra progress to Draw the Line.

### Terms of Surrender
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Main Action · Reason. Offer a feasible surrender to up to three enemies who understand you. Roll once; add 1/2/3 progress to their shared objective if they share a motive, otherwise one chosen enemy’s objective. They do not stop fighting before completion.
