# Spellcraft
Study your reserves, restore your focus and channel magical power.

Soul Capacity, Mind Capacity and Recovery are training paths. Learn their seven steps in order, with steps four through six available at level 3 and mastery at level 7. Channeling is a separate four-section spellbook. Capacity adds to one shared maximum-SP pool and the automatic +2 maximum SP per level after level 1; learning capacity does not refill current SP. Recovery never exceeds maximum. These training paths need no invented combat action.

## Soul Capacity

### Soul Capacity I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
Passive. Increase maximum SP by 2.

### Soul Capacity II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Increase maximum SP by another 2; this path now grants +4 maximum SP.

### Soul Capacity III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
Increase maximum SP by another 3; this path now grants +7 maximum SP.

### Deep Reserves I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
Passive. Increase maximum SP by another 3; this path now grants +10 maximum SP.

### Deep Reserves II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Increase maximum SP by another 3; this path now grants +13 maximum SP.

### Deep Reserves III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
Increase maximum SP by another 3; this path now grants +16 maximum SP.

### Soul Wellspring
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Passive. Increase maximum SP by another 4; this path now grants +20 maximum SP. Once between safe overnight rests, when you spend SP to cast a spell, you may pay up to 2 of its SP from this reserve without reducing current SP. Declare the amount before casting; any unused part of that one payment is lost, not saved for another spell. This grants no additional casting or current-SP recovery.

DP: 4 | Level: 3

### Mind Palace
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Passive. Increase maximum SP by another 4; this path now grants +20 maximum SP. Once between safe overnight rests, when you begin a ritual you have learned, reduce its total printed SP cost by 2, minimum 0. For a ritual with daily payments, reduce only its first payment. Declare before beginning; interruption still spends that benefit. Work time, materials and other requirements remain.

## Recovery

### Recovery I
DP: 1 | Level: 1
1 uninterrupted hour of safe rest, meditation or quiet leisure. Once between safe overnight rests, recover 1 SP. This can occur during permitted quiet rest time; ordinary overnight recovery remains unchanged.

### Recovery II
DP: 2 | Level: 1
The same single recovery rest restores 2 SP in total.

### Recovery III
DP: 2 | Level: 1
The same single recovery rest restores 3 SP in total.

### Quiet Renewal I
DP: 3 | Level: 3
The same single recovery rest restores 4 SP in total. Learning this step does not grant another use if that rest has already been used.

### Quiet Renewal II
DP: 4 | Level: 3
The same single recovery rest restores 6 SP in total.

### Quiet Renewal III
DP: 4 | Level: 3
The same single recovery rest restores 8 SP in total.

### Perfect Repose
DP: 5 | Level: 7
The same single recovery rest restores 12 SP in total and takes 30 uninterrupted minutes of safe rest or quiet leisure instead of an hour. It remains once between safe overnight rests and does not count as a safe overnight rest. No Grit, first-aid allowance or other limited use is refreshed.

## Channeling

Learn the Channeling spellbook’s four sections in order. Its spells transfer or recover SP using their own costs, targets and actions. A channeling spell cannot channel another channeling spell; attempting that transfer causes arcane feedback and provides no SP. Capacity training grants no channeling spell by itself.
