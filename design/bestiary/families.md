## Names from earlier drafts

Skeleton Sentry was Bone Sentry; Skeleton Archer was Bone Archer; Skeleton
Colossus was Bone Colossus; Zombie Shambler was Restless Corpse; Vampire Courtier
was Blood Courtier; Lich — Tomb Sage was Tomb Sage; Ghost — Sorrow-bound was
Sorrow Ghost. Their stable file paths and IDs are unchanged. Crypt Ghoul remains
the ordinary ghoul entry; Ghast is its new, more oppressive cousin.
