# Moon Hart (CV 25)

*A luminous white hart with silver antlers and dark, thoughtful eyes. Its presence is associated with safe paths, lost travelers and the life energy spent to bring someone home.*

**HP:** 26 · **Speed:** Ground 9 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 1 (Heavy Frame, passive grant; not derived from size).

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** Understands Common and Fey; communicates through simple images

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +2; Agility +2; Guile +1; Knowledge +1; Reason +2

**Eligibility:** Fey 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Moonlit antlers | Brawn | None | 4 radiant damage. | 5 radiant damage. Push 1. | 7 radiant damage. Push 2. |


**Moonlit antlers:** One adjacent creature.

### Battle Mending — Main Action

One adjacent living ally; free hand (the hart’s antlers provide this). The controller pays 3 HP first, bypassing resistance, and must remain above 0. The hart does not also pay this cost. A player rolls this creature’s Reason: Weak heals 5, Standard 10, Strong 15 HP. Life resistance reduces healing, minimum 1. This spell provokes. Against a healing-harmed target, roll the same Reason attack and apply the listed healing result as Life damage, without healing riders. Same controller-paid cost.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** None to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
