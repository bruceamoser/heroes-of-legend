# Unicorn (CV 25)

*A pale horn rises from the brow of an alert forest horse, reflecting stars even under a closed canopy.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 26 · **Speed:** 9 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 1.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** Common

**Eligibility:** Fey 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use the controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or if the controller is unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot. No summon chains.

**Carrying:** At most 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No crafting training.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver horn | Reason | 1 Boon | 3 radiant damage. | 5 radiant damage. Push 1. | 8 radiant damage. Push 3. |

**Silver horn:** One adjacent creature. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

## Maneuvers

**Warding Light:** One willing ally within 3 squares reduces Frightened by 1. Until this creature’s next turn, that ally also has 1 Boon on its next defense against a mental attack; for an NPC ally, impose 1 Bane on that player attack. Consumed even if canceled; does not stack with itself.

**Defend:** Gain 1 Boon to defenses until the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** Brawn 1 Boon; one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders.
