# Wolf (CV 8)

*A rangy predator with dense gray fur, a narrow muzzle and long legs built for patient pursuit. Wolves live and hunt as a pack, protecting the den before risking a disabling fight.*

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** Ground 8 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body.


**Senses:** Keen scent within 6 squares.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**Eligibility:** Animal 1

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Snapping jaws | Brawn | None | 2 physical damage. | 3 physical damage. | 4 physical damage. |
| Pinning bite | Brawn | None | 1 physical damage. | 2 physical damage; Grappled 1. | 3 physical damage; Grappled 1. |


**Snapping jaws:** One adjacent creature.

**Pinning bite:** One adjacent creature. The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Circle the Prey — Maneuver

If another conscious, non-Paralyzed ally (not an uncommanded summon) is adjacent to an adjacent enemy, move up to 2 squares without provoking from that enemy. End adjacent to it. Other opportunity attacks and terrain costs apply.

## Passives

**Pack Hunter:** When attacking a target adjacent to another active ally, impose 1 additional Bane on that target’s defense (or gain 1 attack Boon when controlled). Unconscious, Paralyzed and uncommanded summoned allies do not qualify.


**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** None to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
