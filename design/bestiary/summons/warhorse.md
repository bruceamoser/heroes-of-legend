# Warhorse (CV 14)

*A broad-chested horse bears the marks of old harness straps and turns toward familiar commands even in chaos.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 9 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** None. · **Stability:** 1.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Eligibility:** Animal 1

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use the controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or if the controller is unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot. No summon chains.

**Carrying:** At most 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No crafting training.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trained kick | Brawn | None | 2 physical damage. | 4 physical damage. Push 1. | 6 physical damage. Push 3. |

**Trained kick:** One adjacent creature. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

## Maneuvers

**Driving Stride — Maneuver:** Move up to 3 squares in a straight line without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Grants no attack or automatic movement of a rider or held creature.

**Defend:** Gain 1 Boon to defenses until the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** Brawn None; one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders.
