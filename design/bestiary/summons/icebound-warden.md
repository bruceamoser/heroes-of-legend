# Icebound Warden (CV 23)

*A broad elemental body of layered lake ice, with trapped bubbles for eyes and thick, blunt hands. It exists to preserve something beneath the cold rather than to spread winter indiscriminately.*

**HP:** 22 · **Speed:** Ground 5 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** None. · **Stability:** 1 (Heavy Frame, passive grant; not derived from size).

**DR / resistance:** Physical 1; cold 3 · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body. Cannot swallow potions.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile +0; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**Eligibility:** Elemental 3

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Maneuvers

### Lay the Rime — Maneuver

*It sweeps a frozen hand across the ground, leaving raised crusts of ice that catch at hurried feet.*

Make up to 3 connected empty ground squares within 3 squares difficult terrain for everyone until the start of the controller’s next turn. Requires a supporting surface. No entry damage, automatic fall or bridge over empty space. Reusing this ability replaces its previous patch.

Use only as part of a command; no independent activity.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** 1 Boon to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
