# Clay Golem (CV 35)

*A hulking figure of fired earth bears thumbprints the size of bowls and writing pressed into its chest before baking.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason +0

**HP:** 32 · **Speed:** 5 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** None. · **Stability:** 2.

**DR / resistance:** Physical 2 · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary living-target healing does not repair it; a repair ability must explicitly permit constructs.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Eligibility:** Construct 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use the controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or if the controller is unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot. No summon chains.

**Carrying:** At most 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No crafting training.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Potter's grip | Brawn | 1 Boon | 3 physical damage. | 6 physical damage. Grappled 1. | 9 physical damage. Grappled 2. |

**Potter's grip:** One adjacent creature. The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

## Maneuvers

**Shed the Grasp:** Choose one holder and reduce that holder’s Grappled contribution by 1, also reducing this creature’s total Grappled rating by 1. End that hold if its contribution reaches 0. It must have a Maneuver available; cannot use this while Grappled 3. No damage or free movement.

**Defend:** Gain 1 Boon to defenses until the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** Brawn 1 Boon; one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders.
