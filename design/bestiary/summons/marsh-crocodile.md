# Marsh Crocodile (CV 17)

*An immense water predator with armored ridges, a muscular tail and jaws broad enough to seize a grown traveler. Its low silhouette is almost indistinguishable from a muddy log.*

**HP:** 20 · **Speed:** Ground 4; swim 7 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** None. · **Stability:** 1 (Heavy Frame, passive grant; not derived from size).

**DR / resistance:** Physical 1 · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +0

**Eligibility:** Animal 3

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bank drag | Brawn | 1 Boon | 3 physical damage. | 4 physical damage. Pull 1. | 6 physical damage. Pull 2. |
| Locking jaws | Brawn | 1 Boon | 2 physical damage. | 3 physical damage; Grappled 1. | 5 physical damage; Grappled 2. |


**Bank drag:** One adjacent creature.

**Locking jaws:** One adjacent creature. The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Brace the Tail — Maneuver

While touching the bed or bank, gain Stability 2 until its next turn; highest source only.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** 1 Boon to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
