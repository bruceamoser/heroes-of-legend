# Restless Corpse (CV 8)

*A swollen walking corpse with stiff joints, torn clothing and grasping hands. It has little judgment, but enough dead weight and persistence to obstruct a narrow passage.*

**HP:** 16 · **Speed:** Ground 3 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Bane. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility -2; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason -1

**Eligibility:** Undead 1

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** does not breathe or eat; only listed immunities apply.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clutching hands | Brawn | None | 1 physical damage. | 2 physical damage. Grappled 1. | 3 physical damage. Grappled 2. |


**Clutching hands:** One adjacent creature. The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** None to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
