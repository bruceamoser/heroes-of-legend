# Ghost — Sorrow-bound (CV 23)

*A translucent human figure in rain-soaked clothing, its expression fixed between grief and accusation. Details of its appearance belong to the last unresolved moments of its life.*

**HP:** 16 · **Speed:** Ground 6 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** Physical 2 · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Spirit: ordinary magical HP healing applies; potions cannot be swallowed.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** Languages known in life

**Attributes:** Brawn -1; Fortitude +0; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +1; Reason +2

**Eligibility:** Spirit 3

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Summoned form has no letter anchor or Anchor Bound.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** does not breathe or eat; only listed immunities apply.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accusation | Reason | 1 Boon | 2 mental damage. | 3 mental damage. Dazed 1. | 5 mental damage. Dazed 2. |


**Accusation:** One visible creature within 5 squares. Spell; provokes beside an enemy.

### Spectral Passage — Maneuver

Move up to 2 squares through nonliving walls, finishing in a visible legal empty space. Cannot cross living bodies or remain in a wall. Provokes on leaving reach; does not bypass a ward expressly barring spirits.

## Passives



**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** 1 Boon to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
