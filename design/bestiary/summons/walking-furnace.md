# Walking Furnace (CV 34)

*A mobile foundry furnace on thick iron legs, with a bellows chest and a hinged firebox mouth. Old production orders still govern the heat and weight of its machinery.*

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** Ground 4 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Bane. · **Stability:** 1 (Heavy Frame, passive grant; not derived from size).

**DR / resistance:** Physical 2; fire 3 · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body.


**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge +0; Reason +0

**Eligibility:** Construct 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** does not breathe or eat; only listed immunities apply.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Iron ram | Brawn | 1 Boon | 3 physical damage. | 5 physical damage. | 7 physical damage. |
| Furnace breath | Brawn | 1 Boon | 4 fire damage. | 6 fire damage. Burning 1. | 8 fire damage. Burning 2. |


**Iron ram:** One adjacent creature.

**Furnace breath:** All creatures in a cone of 3 rows, 1/3/5 squares wide. Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

## Maneuvers

### Stoke the Core — Maneuver

*Coals brighten inside its body and a hot breath spills between teeth or iron seams.*

Add 1 Fire damage to its next damaging Main Action attack before the end of the controller’s turn. Apply it once per target damaged by that attack, using the normal mixed-damage resistance and minimum rules. This grants no extra attack or Burning. Repeated uses do not add more Fire damage.

Use only as part of a command; no independent activity.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** 1 Boon to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
