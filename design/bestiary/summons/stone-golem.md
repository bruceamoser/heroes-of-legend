# Stone Golem (CV 45)

*A carved guardian moves with the grinding patience of a gate being opened after centuries of rust.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason +0

**HP:** 40 · **Speed:** 4 · **Footprint:** 2x2

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** None. · **Stability:** 3.

**DR / resistance:** Physical 3 · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Communication:** None

**Eligibility:** Construct 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use the controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or if the controller is unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot. No summon chains.

**Carrying:** At most 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No crafting training.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 3; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monument fist | Brawn | 1 Boon | 4 physical damage. | 7 physical damage. Push 1. | 10 physical damage. Push 3. |

**Monument fist:** One visible creature within 2 squares. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

## Maneuvers

**Become Still:** Gain 1 additional Bane against the next player attack before its next turn, or 1 defense Boon when player-controlled. Ends if it voluntarily moves. Does not make it Hidden, alter its resistance or stack with itself.

**Defend:** Gain 1 Boon to defenses until the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** Brawn 1 Boon; one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders.
