# Cave Bat

*A small cave-dwelling bat with oversized ears, thin dark wings and tiny hooked feet. Sound guides it through darkness, while bright light and loud disturbances unsettle its roost.*

**HP:** 4 · **Speed:** Ground 1; fly 8 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.


**Senses:** Sound-based sight 6 squares; fails while Deafened and does not penetrate walls.

**Communication:** None

**Attributes:** Brawn -2; Fortitude -1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**Eligibility:** Animal 1

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or controller unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot.

**Carrying:** up to 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No granted crafting training.

Roll its printed attack attribute and read the attacker outcomes below. When it is attacked, roll the defense attribute named by the incoming attack. Passive defenses remain; no independent active effects.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small bite | Agility | 1 Bane | 1 physical damage. | 1 physical damage. | 2 physical damage. |


**Small bite:** One adjacent creature.

**Defend — Maneuver:** gain 1 Boon to defenses until the start of the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** 1 Bane to attack; Brawn, one adjacent target; Weak attack 1, Standard 1, Strong 2 physical damage. No specialty riders. No opportunity Reaction.
