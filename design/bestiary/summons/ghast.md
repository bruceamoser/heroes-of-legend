# Ghast (CV 25)

*A ghast wears stolen finery over a taut, hungry body. Its breath announces it before its careful footsteps do.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +0; Agility +2; Guile +1; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 24 · **Speed:** 7 · **Footprint:** 1x1

**Summoned form — player reference.**

**Defense modifier:** 1 Boon. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Summoned energy form: cannot be healed; disappears at 0 HP without leaving a body. Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Eligibility:** Undead 7

Main command: Main + Maneuver; Maneuver command: Maneuver + movement. One command per summon per controller turn; immediate summoning command counts. No activity or alerts without a command; no Reactions or opportunity attacks. Turn ticks use the controller’s turn. Beyond 6 squares, or if the controller is unconscious/dead, it disappears; at 0 HP it disappears. No permanent loot. No summon chains.

**Carrying:** At most 1 Bulk; anatomy limits handling. No crafting training.

**Against offensive healing:** Fortitude defense; ordinary player attacks still use the defense attribute named on that attack.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

## Attacks — Main Action

| Attack | Attribute | Attack modifier | Weak attack | Standard attack | Strong attack |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graveyard claws | Brawn | 1 Boon | 3 physical damage. | 5 physical damage. | 7 physical damage. Paralyzed 1. |

**Graveyard claws:** One adjacent creature. Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

## Maneuvers

**Grave Stench:** One adjacent breathing creature gains Poisoned 1. Once per turn; no damage.

**Defend:** Gain 1 Boon to defenses until the controller’s next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main:** Brawn 1 Boon; one adjacent target; Weak attack 2, Standard 3, Strong 5 physical damage. No specialty riders.
