## Details

### Measured arrow

**Target:** One visible creature within 9 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Slowed 2. **Standard defense:** Slowed 1.

**Limit:** Ammunition inventory d6. After each use, roll it; on 1 reduce d6 to d4, or d4 to empty. Cannot use this attack while empty.

### Measured Step

*It chooses its footing, shifting just far enough to clear the worst angle.*

Move up to 2 squares, paying normal terrain costs. This movement provokes normally and does not grant Hidden.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Slow the leading pursuer and step toward a prepared firing lane. Abandon the perch before a defender can seal the exit.

**Encounter and conversation:** The archer will hold fire for a recognized truce signal, even when allies demand a shot.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
