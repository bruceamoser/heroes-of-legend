# Goblin Firestarter (CV 11)

*Soot-dark goggles and carefully stoppered clay bottles distinguish this goblin artisan from the rest of the work crew.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 11 · **Speed:** 7 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Resistances:** None\

## Details

### Bottled spark

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

**Limit:** Ammunition inventory d6. After each use, roll it; on 1 reduce d6 to d4, or d4 to empty. Cannot use this attack while empty.

### Scatter Embers

*A spray of glowing cinders hangs between the creature and its pursuer.*

One adjacent creature gains Burning 1. Once per turn; no immediate damage. The condition uses normal resistance and decay.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Throw sparks from a sheltered angle, saving the adjacent ember trick for someone who closes. It needs a retreat when its bottles run out.

**Encounter and conversation:** A failed demolition threatens the settlement; the firestarter knows which supports are still sound.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
