## Details

### Gnawing edge

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### Scatter Before the Blast

*The swarm opens around the incoming sweep, then folds back into a ragged cloud of wings or fur.*

Before an area attack that includes this creature is rolled, impose 1 Bane on that attack. Against an NPC area attack, grant 1 Boon to this creature's defense instead. This protection belongs to the swarm; a player area attack still uses one roll with the worst relevant target modifiers. No movement, new swarm or immunity is granted.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Scatter Body:** Area attacks ignore its physical DR, not other resistance. One HP pool and one turn; cannot overlap another creature at rest.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Food sacks have been opened from beneath, with no large footprints nearby.

**Purpose:** Reach food and escape larger predators.

**Tactics and retreat:** Bite at the edge of a crowd and flow back toward shelter.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Use an area attack or remove the food source; the swarm cannot occupy a hero’s square.

**Habitat:** Cellar; sewer · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Spoiled stores and evidence of a breached cellar.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
