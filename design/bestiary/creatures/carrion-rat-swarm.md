Force, Defend, search, hide and feasible interaction follow the

## At the table

**Signs:** Food sacks have been opened from beneath, with no large footprints nearby.

**Purpose:** Reach food and escape larger predators.

**Tactics and retreat:** Bite at the edge of a crowd and flow back toward shelter.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Use an area attack or remove the food source; the swarm cannot occupy a hero’s square.

**Rewards:** Spoiled stores and evidence of a breached cellar. Summoned bodies and equipment leave no permanent loot.
