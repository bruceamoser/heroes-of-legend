# Barrow Guard (CV 25)

*An armed burial guardian in tarnished armor, with turf caught in its shield and remnants of an oath worked into its clothing. It remembers its duty more clearly than its life.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +1; Agility +0; Guile +0; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Oath blade

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Shield Hunker

Before a shield-blockable attack resolves, impose 1 Bane on player attacks this shield can block until the start of its next turn. The DA judges blockability. Uses its Reaction; stacks with Defend, not another activation of itself.

### Cover a Companion

*The guardian steps into the line of danger, making the attacker work around its raised guard.*

Before a visible enemy attacks a willing ally adjacent to this creature, impose 1 Bane on that attack roll if this creature can see the attacker. Against an NPC attack, grant that ally 1 Boon to its defense instead. This creature must also be adjacent to the attacker, or hold a shield the DA judges can obstruct the attack. No movement, target substitution or damage transfer. For a single player area attack, use the normal worst-target modifiers; do not roll again.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Every disturbed grave has been filled in again from within.

**Purpose:** Keep the sworn burial place intact.

**Tactics and retreat:** Shield Hunker at the tomb entrance and push desecrators back.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Return stolen grave goods or show permission from the rightful heir.

**Habitat:** Barrow; ancient road · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Melee weapon, shield and burial jewelry worth 30 coins if legitimately recovered.

**Summoning:** Undead 3 — [player reference](../summons/barrow-guard.md).
