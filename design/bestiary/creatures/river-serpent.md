## Details

### River coils

*A hooked limb braces against the bank and heaves its catch toward the dark water.*

**Target:** One creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 2. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

### Coiling grip

*A coil or grasping limb closes around the target and braces against its struggling weight.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Coil Against the Pull

*Coils knot and the heavy body locks against the ground before it can be dragged away.*

Before this creature is forcibly moved while touching solid ground or a fixed support, gain Stability 2 for that forced movement only. Use the highest Stability, maximum 3. In a collision chain, declare before its Stability is deducted; this does not prevent prior collision damage. No effect on Prone or grapples.

### Sound the Depths

*It slips beneath the surface with scarcely a ripple.*

Move up to 2 squares using its printed swim speed without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Water grants only the cover or concealment the scene actually provides; no automatic Hidden.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Fish vanish from a deep pool; drag marks score the landing stage.

**Purpose:** Keep boats away from a spawning pool.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull someone from a low bank, then seize only if prey remains adjacent.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Stay on high banks and offer a route for boats away from the spawning water.

**Habitat:** River; lake · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A wreck beneath the pool may contain a bounded cargo selected by the DA.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
