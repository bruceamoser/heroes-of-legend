# Fire Giant (CV 56)

*Heat glows through the seams of a master smith's armor, and every tool is large enough to serve as a siege engine.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

## Details

### Forge hammer

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

### Thrown stone

**Target:** One visible creature within 7 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Limit:** Requires an available loose stone; picking it up is part of this attack. No stone means this attack is unavailable.

### Scatter Embers

*A spray of glowing cinders hangs between the creature and its pursuer.*

One adjacent creature gains Burning 1. Once per turn; no immediate damage. The condition uses normal resistance and decay.

### Bat Aside the Missile

*A broad hand or haft meets the incoming missile with the ease of brushing a branch aside.*

Before one physical ranged attack from a visible attacker against this creature is rolled, impose 1 Bane on that attack. Against an NPC attack, grant 1 Boon to its defense instead. No protection against area attacks; no missile is returned and the attack still resolves.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use the hammer where it threatens a group’s route through the forge, but preserve valuable tools and work in progress.

**Encounter and conversation:** A broken treaty has cut off ore shipments; craftsmanship may earn an audience where threats fail.

**Habitat:** Highland; quarry; remote stronghold · **Communication:** Common and Giant

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
