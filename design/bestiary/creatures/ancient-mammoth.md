# Ancient Mammoth (CV 44)

*A towering, shaggy elephant with a domed skull, sweeping tusks and a thick trunk. Old herd leaders recognize familiar routes and threats across generations.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +2

**HP:** 34 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 3 (3x3 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** 1 Boon · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 2

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Tusk sweep

*A great limb scythes across the ground, forcing everyone nearby to move.*

**Target:** Up to two creatures within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push each target 2. **Standard defense:** Push each target 1.

### Driving tusk

*A heavy tusk lifts the intruder and drives it away from the herd.*

**Target:** One creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** push 5. **Standard defense:** push 3. **Strong defense:** push 1.

### Shelter the Young

*It turns at the cry beside it, bringing its whole body between the danger and the one it protects.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to a willing ally within 3 squares, move up to 2 squares, ending adjacent to that ally. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. This movement grants no attack or automatic protection; its body obstructs only the squares it actually occupies. Movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger it.

### Plant the Weight

*It spreads its stance and settles its weight into the ground.*

While standing on solid ground, gain Stability 3 until its next turn. Use the highest source only, never add sources.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Trees are stripped well above a person’s reach.

**Purpose:** Keep the herd together through a dangerous crossing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sweep clustered attackers, then turn its body between them and the calves.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Split the encounter from the herd’s route rather than trying to overpower it.

**Habitat:** Tundra; highland · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Old travelers’ offerings at a traditional crossing; no automatic ivory market.

**Summoning:** Animal 7 — [player reference](../summons/ancient-mammoth.md).
