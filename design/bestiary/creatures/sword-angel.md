# Sword Angel (CV 43)

*A winged champion bears an unadorned blade and armor marked with the places where it chose to stand between others.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 34 · **Speed:** 6; fly 8 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2\*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

## Details

### Merciful edge

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** radiant.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Shield Hunker

Before a shield-blockable attack resolves, spend its Reaction to impose 1 Bane on player attacks it can block until the start of its next turn. The DA judges blockability. Stacks with Defend through normal Boon/Bane rules, not with another activation of itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Push an attacker away from a protected person and keep the shield ready. A credible account of unseen victims can change whom it protects.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its orders are honorable but incomplete; showing the unseen victims can change its allegiance in the dispute.

**Habitat:** Sacred boundary; refuge; contested oath · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
