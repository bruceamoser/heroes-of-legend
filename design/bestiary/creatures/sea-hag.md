# Sea Hag (CV 21)

*Salt crystals cling to a sea hag's tangled hair, and her eyes reflect the shore as it looked before the last great storm.*

## Details

### Drowned reflection

**Target:** Only a creature that can see this creature. One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Frightened 2 of this creature. **Standard defense:** Frightened 1 of this creature.

### Ride the Current

*It catches a moving seam of water and lets the current carry its weight.*

While in water, move up to 3 squares using its printed swim speed. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. On dry ground this Maneuver has no effect.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Show a threatening reflection from water, then ride the current toward an escape route. A blinded target cannot see the reflection.

**Encounter and conversation:** She keeps the names of drowned sailors in a shell chest and will trade one for a promise made on dry land.

**Habitat:** Old woodland; crossroads; hidden court · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
