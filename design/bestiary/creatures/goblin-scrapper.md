# Goblin Scrapper

*A small, wiry goblin salvager with keen eyes, patched work clothes and pockets full of useful scraps. Quick hands and knowledge of the ruins compensate for modest reach.*

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 7 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Scrap knife

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Roll Away:** Normal terrain costs and occupied-square restrictions apply.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Bright scraps are tied to trip-free routes through the rubble.

**Purpose:** Protect a salvage claim and avoid being cheated.

**Tactics and retreat:** Roll Away between cover and a distracted opponent; bargain when cornered.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Recognize the salvage claim or trade a useful tool.

**Habitat:** Ruin; roadside · **Communication:** Common and Goblin

**Loot:** Melee weapon, tool scraps and 3 coins.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
