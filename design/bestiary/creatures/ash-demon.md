*A restless demon sheds soot from a body shaped like a fire struggling to remember an animal.*

## Details

### Ash-laden claws

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

### Scatter Embers

*A spray of glowing cinders hangs between the creature and its pursuer.*

One adjacent creature gains Burning 1. Once per turn; no immediate damage. The condition uses normal resistance and decay.

### Heated Hide

*The struck scales flare, and a lash of heat follows the retreating blade.*

After an adjacent attacker deals HP damage to this creature, spend Reaction to apply Burning 1 to that attacker. It is a condition, not a free attack, and cannot trigger this ability in return. No trigger when the incoming action is itself a free attack. Summoned forms never receive this Reaction.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Burn the nearest participant in the feud and make close pursuit unpleasant. It loses a useful battlefield when the rivals cooperate.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is feeding on a feud and loses its audience when the rivals stop answering each insult.

**Habitat:** Broken shrine; summoned court; place of conflict · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
