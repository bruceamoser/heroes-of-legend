## Details

### Root-and-reed embrace

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Weave the Roots

*Roots braid themselves into firm footholds across the treacherous ground.*

Choose up to 3 connected difficult-terrain ground squares within 3 squares; they count as normal terrain until its next turn. Requires soil or living vegetation; cannot bridge empty space or bypass occupied squares.

### Spore Curtain

*A slow shake of its crown releases a drifting veil of pale spores that hangs between the trunks.*

Fill up to 3 connected empty squares within 3 squares with spores that give partial obstruction until the start of this creature's next turn. Apply 1 Bane when that obstruction lies in an attack's path, following the normal targeting rules. The spores do not grant Hidden or cause a condition or damage. Reusing this ability replaces its previous cloud.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Hold whoever blocks the old watercourse while weaving safe footing across the wet ground. Respecting the channel can end the fight.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is rebuilding a blocked wetland channel through fields that people now depend on.

**Habitat:** Wetland; overgrown ruin; old forest · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
