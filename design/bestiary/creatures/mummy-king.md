## Details

### Scepter of the last court

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** Life.

**Weak defense:** Frightened 2 of this creature. **Standard defense:** Frightened 1 of this creature.

### Make Room

*A clipped order sends one companion into the opening before it closes.*

One willing active ally within 3 squares that can hear it may spend that ally’s Reaction to move up to 2 squares. Movement provokes normally and pays terrain costs. An uncommanded summon cannot act; summons have no Reaction to spend.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Keep a guard between itself and a challenger; spend Make Room only when that guard still has a Reaction.

**Encounter and conversation:** The royal seal commands the wrong heir; deciphering the succession can divide its servants.

**Habitat:** Crypt; burial ground; ruined settlement · **Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
