# Harbor Siren

*An aquatic fey with a humanlike face, webbed hands and a voice that carries clearly over water. Its relationship with a harbor may be older than the people now living there.*

**HP:** 17 · **Speed:** 5; swim 8 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

| Main Actions | Damage |
|---|---|
| Basic Melee | 5/3/2 |
| Undertow song | 5 + Dazed 2 / 3 + Dazed 1 / 2 |

| Maneuvers | Effect |
|---|---|
| Defend | Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. |

| Reactions | Effect |
|---|---|
| Opportunity Attack | Basic Melee against the provoking creature. |

## Details

### Undertow song

*For a moment, the victim remembers a pain that has not happened yet.*

**Target:** One hearing creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Sailors remember the same dream and forget the same hour.

**Purpose:** Keep company in a harbor that has forgotten an old pact.

**Tactics and retreat:** Confuse a watcher with song, then slip into the water.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Ask about the pact and stop answering every invitation alone.

**Habitat:** Harbor; sea cave · **Communication:** Common and Fey

**Loot:** A negotiated return of lost cargo; never automatic mind control.

**Summoning:** Fey 3 — [player reference](../summons/harbor-siren.md).
