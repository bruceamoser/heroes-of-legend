## Details

### Undertow song

*For a moment, the victim remembers a pain that has not happened yet.*

**Target:** One hearing creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### Sound the Depths

*It slips beneath the surface with scarcely a ripple.*

Move up to 2 squares using its printed swim speed without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Water grants only the cover or concealment the scene actually provides; no automatic Hidden.

### Keep the Rhythm

*A familiar rhythm cuts through panic and brings one companion back to the moment.*

One willing ally within 3 squares that can hear it reduces either Frightened or Dazed by 1. Once per turn. This does not recalculate action categories already selected at turn start.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Sailors remember the same dream and forget the same hour.

**Purpose:** Keep company in a harbor that has forgotten an old pact.

**Tactics and retreat:** Confuse a watcher with song, then slip into the water.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Ask about the pact and stop answering every invitation alone.

**Habitat:** Harbor; sea cave · **Communication:** Common and Fey

**Loot:** A negotiated return of lost cargo; never automatic mind control.

**Summoning:** Fey 3 — [player reference](../summons/harbor-siren.md).
