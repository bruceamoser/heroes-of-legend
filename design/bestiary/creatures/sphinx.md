# Sphinx (CV 38)

*A lion-bodied keeper with a thoughtful human face rests among inscriptions repaired more often than the surrounding walls.*

## Details

### Verdict of stone

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Study the Opening

*It waits for a tell in the stance, then turns its attention to the exposed line.*

Its next attack before the end of this turn imposes 1 additional Bane on player defense, or gains 1 Boon when player-controlled. One attack only; no stacking with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Study a challenger before delivering a verdict. A party that listens to the warning may achieve more than one that wins an exchange of blows.

**Encounter and conversation:** It tests whether visitors understand a warning, not whether they can guess a clever password.

**Habitat:** Wild frontier; ancient ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
