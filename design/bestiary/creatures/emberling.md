# Emberling (CV 8)

*A small elemental made of glowing coals and flickering flame, with bright cracks for eyes. Warmth draws it; uncontrolled curiosity makes it dangerous around dry fuel.*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Resistances:** Fire (3)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Coal touch

*Heat crawls along the strike, or frost flowers across the skin where it passes.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

### Stoke the Core

*Coals brighten inside its body and a hot breath spills between teeth or iron seams.*

Add 1 Fire damage to its next damaging Main Action attack before the end of this turn. Apply it once per target damaged by that attack, using the normal mixed-damage resistance and minimum rules. This grants no extra attack or Burning. Repeated uses do not add more Fire damage.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Weakness:** Cold damage gives it 1 Bane on its next attack before its next turn (1 Boon to that player defense). Does not stack with itself.

**Healing:** Elemental: magical HP healing applies; cannot swallow potions. Printed immunities only.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Small footprints of soot lead out of a cold hearth.

**Purpose:** Find something warm enough to call home.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sting an intruder and retreat behind hot ground.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Offer a safe brazier instead of chasing it through dry grass.

**Habitat:** Hearth; volcanic field · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A useful clue in its hearth; no endless fuel.

**Summoning:** Elemental 1 — [player reference](../summons/emberling.md).
