## Details

### Skyward hooves

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Wing Step

*A single hard wingbeat carries it toward a better angle.*

Move up to 2 squares using its printed fly speed without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. This grants no attack, automatic lift of another creature or hovering exception.

### Bank Away

*A struck feather spins loose as the great wings tilt and carry the creature out of the firing line.*

After a ranged attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, fly up to 2 squares using its printed fly speed. Pay normal terrain costs, provoke normally and finish in a legal position. This creature must survive and remain capable of flight. No trigger from a free attack; no attack or automatic lift of a held creature.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Push a threat away from a rescuer and use flight to reach safe ground. Carrying a rider does not add attacks or bypass occupancy.

**Encounter and conversation:** It will carry someone toward a rescue but refuses a harness offered as ownership.

**Habitat:** Wild frontier; ancient ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
