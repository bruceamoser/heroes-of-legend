## Details

### Hanging tendril

**Target:** One visible creature within 3 squares. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 3. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Become Still

*Its body settles into the posture of a piece of the surrounding stonework.*

Gain 1 additional Bane against the next player attack before its next turn, or 1 defense Boon when player-controlled. Ends if it voluntarily moves. Does not make it Hidden, alter its resistance or stack with itself.

### Hook the Retreat

*The hooked limb follows the withdrawing foe, catching just as the space opens between them.*

After a visible enemy voluntarily moves from adjacent to this creature and ends that movement exactly 2 squares from it, pull that enemy 1 square toward this creature along a clear path. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules. No separate attack damage. Forced movement and movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger this; it is an alternative to spending the Reaction on an opportunity attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull one creature toward its hanging position while staying near stone it can brace against. It cannot pull through a solid obstruction.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its den contains a safe ledge and a dangerous shortcut; careful light reveals which is which.

**Habitat:** Cavern; forgotten laboratory; buried ruin · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
