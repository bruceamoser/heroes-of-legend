# Stone Knuckle (CV 9)

*A squat elemental assembled from pebbles and rough stones around a dense central core. Its blunt hands and planted feet suit it to guarding a small patch of ground.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +1; Agility -1; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 12 · **Speed:** 4; burrow 2 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Pebble fist

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### Lock the Frame

*The joints grind shut and the body becomes a single weight planted against the shove.*

Before this creature is forcibly moved while on solid ground, gain Stability 3 for that forced movement only. Use the highest source. In a collision chain, declare before its Stability is deducted; prior collision damage still resolves. This does not resist Prone or grapples.

### Plant the Weight

*It spreads its stance and settles its weight into the ground.*

While standing on solid ground, gain Stability 3 until its next turn. Use the highest source only, never add sources.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Elemental: magical HP healing applies; cannot swallow potions. Printed immunities only.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Fresh pebbles pile around an undisturbed buried marker.

**Purpose:** Keep the ground around its resting place unbroken.

**Tactics and retreat:** Defend a narrow route and jab whoever tries to move it.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Restore the marker or agree to dig elsewhere.

**Habitat:** Quarry; cave · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** No gemstones are guaranteed inside it.

**Summoning:** Elemental 1 — [player reference](../summons/stone-knuckle.md).
