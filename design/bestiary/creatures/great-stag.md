# Great Stag (CV 15)

*A tall woodland deer crowned with spreading antlers and supported by long, powerful legs. The herd follows its caution; in a rut or a crisis, that caution becomes a sudden charge.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 9 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Antler sweep

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Shelter the Young

*It turns at the cry beside it, bringing its whole body between the danger and the one it protects.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to a willing ally within 3 squares, move up to 2 squares, ending adjacent to that ally. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. This movement grants no attack or automatic protection; its body obstructs only the squares it actually occupies. Movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger it.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Deep hoof prints cross a fence without breaking it.

**Purpose:** Lead the herd away from danger.

**Tactics and retreat:** Drive one pursuer back, then run toward open ground.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Do not corner the herd; give its leader an escape route.

**Habitat:** Wilderness · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A naturally shed antler may serve as a story clue or craft ingredient at the DA’s discretion.

**Summoning:** Animal 3 — [player reference](../summons/great-stag.md).
