# Giant Spider

*A dog-sized hunting spider with a swollen abdomen, hooked feet and pale silk glands beneath its jaws. Its web is both a trap and an extension of its senses.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**HP:** 16 · **Speed:** 6; climb 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Venomous fangs

*A needle catches the skin, and a bitter numbness spreads from the puncture.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Poisoned 2. **Standard defense:** Poisoned 1.

### Web cast

*White strands snap between the pillars and tighten around boots and wrists.*

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** None.

**Weak defense:** Slowed 3. **Standard defense:** Slowed 2. **Strong defense:** Slowed 1.

**Limit:** An adjacent helper or the target may spend Main to remove 1 of this Slowed. Fire damage to the target removes all of this web’s Slowed after damage resolves. Otherwise decay normally.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Layers of wrapped prey hang above a clean patch of floor.

**Purpose:** Keep prey from reaching the edge of its web.

**Tactics and retreat:** Cast web from above, then bite a slowed target; abandon a burning web.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Cut a clear route and threaten the supporting strands.

**Habitat:** Cavern; forest ruins · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Objects from wrapped packs, not limitless silk supplies.

**Summoning:** Animal 3 — [player reference](../summons/giant-spider.md).
