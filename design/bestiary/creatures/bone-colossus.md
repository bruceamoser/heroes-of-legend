# Bone Colossus

*A towering assembly of skulls, ribs and long bones bound into a single siege body. Many dead supplied its frame, but only one controlling purpose directs it.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason +0

**HP:** 38 · **Speed:** 5 · **Size:** 3 (3x3 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** 1 Boon · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 2

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Ossuary sweep

*A great limb scythes across the ground, forcing everyone nearby to move.*

**Target:** Up to two creatures within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push each 4. **Standard defense:** Push each 2.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Graves stand empty in a widening circle.

**Purpose:** Break whatever its maker marked with a black handprint.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sweep two defenders aside and advance toward the marked structure.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Destroy or obscure the mark; exploit doors too small for the assembled body.

**Habitat:** Battlefield; necropolis · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** One command plaque; the bones carry no usable armor.

**Summoning:** Undead 7 — [player reference](../summons/bone-colossus.md).
