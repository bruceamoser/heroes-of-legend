# Storm Column (CV 38)

*A turning column of rain, grit and lightning with a dimly human outline at its center. Its shape continually reforms around the pressure of the wind holding it together.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +2; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** 0; fly 7 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 1\*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\

**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Forked flash

*Heat crawls along the strike, or frost flowers across the skin where it passes.*

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** lightning.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

### Driving gust

*Dust lifts in a sheet and cloaks snap straight behind their wearers.*

**Target:** All creatures in a line 6 from this creature. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** None.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 2. **Strong defense:** Push 1.

### Wind Step

*The body uncoils into a ribbon of air and gathers at the edge of the gust.*

Move up to 3 squares using a printed movement mode without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Cannot pass through solid objects or occupied squares.

### Thunderous Answer

*The blow jars loose a thundercrack that rolls back across the space between them.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, its visible attacker within 3 squares gains Deafened 2 if it can hear this creature. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. No damage or interruption of the resolved attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Elemental: magical HP healing applies; cannot swallow potions. Printed immunities only.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Debris forms rings around a place untouched by rain.

**Purpose:** Break a confinement that has lasted too long.

**Tactics and retreat:** Push intruders from an exposed ridge; strike with lightning when they spread out.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Leave the ridge and break the binding stones from sheltered ground.

**Habitat:** Mountain; storm coast · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A shattered binding ring worth 40 coins as salvage, once.

**Summoning:** Elemental 7 — [player reference](../summons/storm-column.md).
