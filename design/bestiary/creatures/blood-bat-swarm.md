Force, Defend, search, hide and feasible interaction follow the

## At the table

**Signs:** Livestock wake with shallow cuts and panic at dusk.

**Purpose:** Feed quickly and return to the roost.

**Tactics and retreat:** Rend the nearest isolated target, then retreat if the swarm is badly damaged.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Fight under a low roof and use area effects; protect the livestock behind screens.

**Rewards:** No carried treasure. Summoned bodies and equipment leave no permanent loot.
