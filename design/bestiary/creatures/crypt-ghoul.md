# Crypt Ghoul (CV 18)

*A gaunt, corpse-eating undead with stretched lips, long nails and flesh drawn tight over its joints. It retains enough cunning to seek an isolated victim and avoid a determined group.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -1; Reason +1

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 7 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Raking claws

*The claws snag and drag; blood follows the withdrawing talons.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Grave touch

*The touch is colder than winter iron, and the limb refuses to answer.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** cold.

**Weak defense:** Paralyzed 1; cannot use this attack next turn.

**Limit:** Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

### Shambling Advance

*The corpse lurches after the nearest warm body, dragging its ruined feet without ever looking away.*

Move up to 2 squares toward a visible living creature within 6 squares; its next attack against that creature before turn end imposes 1 additional Bane on the player defense. For a player-controlled creature, gain 1 attack Boon instead. It must end closer to that creature than it began. For this movement only, treat difficult ground as normal; pay other costs and provoke normally. One attack only; repeated uses do not stack. This grants no attack and cannot be used while movement is prevented.

### Rattle of the Grave

*A dry sound rises from the corpse, like someone knocking from inside a coffin.*

One creature within 2 squares that can hear it gains Frightened 1 of this creature. Once per turn; no damage.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Bones are cracked for marrow and stacked with unnatural care.

**Purpose:** Feed without returning to the grave.

**Tactics and retreat:** Strike with Grave Chill, then rake on the intervening turn; avoid concentrated light and numbers.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Keep a rescuer beside the front line and deny isolated feeding targets.

**Habitat:** Crypt; battlefield · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** The latest victim’s recoverable pack.

**Summoning:** Undead 3 — [player reference](../summons/crypt-ghoul.md).
