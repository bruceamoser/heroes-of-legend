## Details

### Pouncing claws

*It gathers itself, then arrives in a rush of claws and weight.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** After an approach of 2 squares, Prone. **Standard defense:** After an approach of 2 squares, Prone.

### Raking bite

*The cat grips close and tears before its prey can turn.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Shelter the Young

*It turns at the cry beside it, bringing its whole body between the danger and the one it protects.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to a willing ally within 3 squares, move up to 2 squares, ending adjacent to that ally. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. This movement grants no attack or automatic protection; its body obstructs only the squares it actually occupies. Movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger it.

### Stalking Step

*It folds into the broken light, leaving only a leaf moving where it stood.*

Move up to 2 squares without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving, ending in a square with cover or concealment from that enemy. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Does not grant Hidden; a separate Hide action still needs concealment and a roll when uncertain.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** A kill has been hauled into the branches above the path.

**Purpose:** Feed without risking a disabling injury.

**Tactics and retreat:** Approach from concealment, pounce once, and withdraw if surrounded.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Watch overhead and deny a straight approach; it will abandon a kill to a larger threat.

**Habitat:** Wilderness · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Bones and objects beneath its favorite tree.

**Summoning:** Animal 3 — [player reference](../summons/great-cat.md).
