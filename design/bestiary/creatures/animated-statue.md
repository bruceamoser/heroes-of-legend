# Animated Statue (CV 22)

*A civic statue has climbed down from its pedestal, leaving a clean pair of footprints in old soot.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason +0

**HP:** 22 · **Speed:** 5 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates a complete outcome. All attacks use printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Ceremonial spear

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Become Still

*Its body settles into the posture of a piece of the surrounding stonework.*

Gain 1 additional Bane against the next player attack before its next turn, or 1 defense Boon when player-controlled. Ends if it voluntarily moves. Does not make it Hidden, alter its resistance or stack with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary living-target healing does not repair it; a repair ability must explicitly permit constructs.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Repeat the civic duty literally, using reach and stillness to guard its chosen boundary. A repaired inscription may offer a better answer than force.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is trying to perform a public duty described by an inscription that has lost several crucial words.

**Habitat:** Workshop; old civic building; sealed vault · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Construct 3 — [player reference](../summons/animated-statue.md).
