## Details

### Amber lash

**Target:** One sensed creature within 2 squares with an unobstructed path. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Shed the Grasp

*The surface peels away, leaving a useless handful while the body gathers again.*

Choose one holder and reduce that holder’s Grappled contribution by 1, also reducing this creature’s total Grappled rating by 1. End that hold if its contribution reaches 0. It must have a Maneuver available; cannot use this while Grappled 3. No damage or free movement.

### Clinging Recoil

*The cut sprays sticky droplets that stretch into threads across the attacker's hands and boots.*

After an adjacent attacker’s attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, apply Slowed 2 to that attacker. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. This grants no grapple, extra damage or equipment destruction.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Touch and ground vibrations within 3 squares on the same surface; no sight-required attack.

**Tactics and retreat:** Reach along a wall to hold one nearby creature, then shed a competing grasp if a Maneuver remains. It receives no automatic duplicate body when struck.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its feeding pattern follows discarded alchemical waste back to a workshop that claims to be innocent.

**Habitat:** Drain; cavern; abandoned workshop · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
