# Chain Jailer (CV 34)

*A broad fiend wrapped in hooked chains and iron identification tags. It presents captivity as collection of a debt, whether that debt is honest or manufactured.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +1; Reason +1

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2\*

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2), Fire (2)\
**Immunities:** None

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Hooked chain

*A hooked limb braces against the bank and heaves its catch toward the dark water.*

**Target:** One creature within 3 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 2. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

### Binding chain

*A coil or grasping limb closes around the target and braces against its struggling weight.*

**Target:** One creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Hook the Retreat

*The hooked limb follows the withdrawing foe, catching just as the space opens between them.*

After a visible enemy voluntarily moves from adjacent to this creature and ends that movement exactly 2 squares from it, pull that enemy 1 square toward this creature along a clear path. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules. No separate attack damage. Forced movement and movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger this; it is an alternative to spending the Reaction on an opportunity attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Unbroken locks hang open beside empty cells.

**Purpose:** Collect people who believe they owe a debt.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull a target out of formation and seize on the following turn.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Challenge the claimed debt, break line of sight, and keep an ally ready to use Force.

**Habitat:** Prison; infernal breach · **Communication:** Common and Infernal

**Loot:** A prison key, debt records and 90 coins held in trust for its master.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
