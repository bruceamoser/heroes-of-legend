# Skeleton Champion (CV 25)

*A crowned helm sits crookedly on this skeleton, but its sword follows the patient lines of a practiced duelist.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +0; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 22 · **Speed:** 5 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates a complete outcome. All attacks use printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Notched sword

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Driving strike

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Shield Hunker

Before a shield-blockable attack resolves, spend its Reaction to impose 1 Bane on player attacks it can block until the start of its next turn. The DA judges blockability. Stacks with Defend through normal Boon/Bane rules, not with another activation of itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Hold a doorway with the shield, then push an intruder back into the approach. Follow the burial oath rather than a fleeing distraction.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its grave oath names a person, not a dynasty; proving the current lord is an impostor can turn its blade.

**Habitat:** Crypt; burial ground; ruined settlement · **Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Undead 3 — [player reference](../summons/skeleton-champion.md).
