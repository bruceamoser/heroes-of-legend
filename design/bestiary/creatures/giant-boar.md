# Giant Boar (CV 23)

*A boar with a ridge of iron-gray bristles has tusks polished by years of uprooting saplings and turning stones.*

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

## Details

### Ploughing tusks

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Shoulder Through

*It lowers its weight and makes the space in front of it suddenly too small.*

Push one adjacent creature 1 square. Apply Stability and normal collision rules. Once per turn; no separate damage.

### Break the Approach

*It lashes out with shoulder or horn, spoiling the straight line of the charging foe.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to this creature, push that enemy 1 square directly away. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules; no separate attack damage. Forced movement and movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger this.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Drive straight through a defended approach, using the shoulder to clear a lightly braced foe. Turn away once the grain or escape route is secured.

**Encounter and conversation:** It has learned that grain carts are easier to raid than fenced fields.

**Habitat:** Wilderness; nesting ground · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Animal 3 — [player reference](../summons/giant-boar.md).
