## Details

### Grave thread

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** Life.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### Rattle of the Grave

*A dry sound rises from the corpse, like someone knocking from inside a coffin.*

One creature within 2 squares that can hear it gains Frightened 1 of this creature. Once per turn; no damage.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Offerings are arranged around a newly opened grave.

**Purpose:** Buy a promised reunion, even if the promise is false.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use a life-drinking spell from behind followers; flee if the ritual is disproved.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Discover whom they are trying to recover; destroying a lie can work better than killing its victim.

**Habitat:** Crypt; abandoned shrine · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Ritual notes, plain focus and 9 coins.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
