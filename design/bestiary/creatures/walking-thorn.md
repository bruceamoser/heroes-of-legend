## Details

### Barbed branch

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Scatter Thorns

*Brittle hooks fall from its branches and catch across the path.*

Place difficult terrain in up to 3 connected empty ground squares within 2 squares until its next turn. No entry damage; ordinary clearing after the fight removes the thorns.

### Barbed Recoil

*The struck bough bends back, dragging a rake of hooked thorns across the hand that struck it.*

After an adjacent attacker's attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, apply Bleeding 1 to that attacker. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. This is a condition, not another attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Bleed a close intruder and litter the approach with difficult terrain. The scattered thorns cause no additional entry damage.

**Encounter and conversation:** It follows the scent of a stolen cutting from the hedge it once guarded.

**Habitat:** Wetland; overgrown ruin; old forest · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
