# Crystal Ooze (CV 22)

*A broad, transparent gel containing shards of stone and glass, easily mistaken for a clean polished surface. Its slow body is adhesive and dense rather than nimble.*

## Details

### Closing gel

*A coil or grasping limb closes around the target and braces against its struggling weight.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Clinging Recoil

*The cut sprays sticky droplets that stretch into threads across the attacker's hands and boots.*

After an adjacent attacker’s attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, apply Slowed 2 to that attacker. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. This grants no grapple, extra damage or equipment destruction.

### Shed the Grasp

*The surface peels away, leaving a useless handful while the body gathers again.*

Choose one holder and reduce that holder’s Grappled contribution by 1, also reducing this creature’s total Grappled rating by 1. End that hold if its contribution reaches 0. It must have a Maneuver available; cannot use this while Grappled 3. No damage or free movement.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Mimic Shell:** While still, resembles an ordinary object. Reason examination can expose it. Touching or opening it reveals the creature but grants no free attack; concealment is required to hide again.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Touch; ground vibration within 3 squares on the same surface.

**Signs:** Bootprints end at a patch of unusually clean stone.

**Purpose:** Absorb warmth and organic matter.

**Tactics and retreat:** Wait near a narrow passage, then seize one traveler.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Probe suspicious floors and stay beyond its reach.

**Habitat:** Ruin; cavern · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** One lost object encased in the body; no automatic gem value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
