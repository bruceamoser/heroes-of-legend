# Hedge Sprite

*A tiny winged fey with thornlike features, leaf-colored clothes and a fierce sense of local etiquette. Its home may be no larger than a hedge, but its claim to it is absolute.*

**HP:** 6 · **Speed:** 4; fly 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Actions

Damage is **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

| Main Actions | Damage |
|---|---|
| Basic Melee | 4/2/1 |
| Thorn dart | 3/2/1 |

| Maneuvers | Effect |
|---|---|
| Defend | Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. |

| Reactions | Effect |
|---|---|
| Opportunity Attack | Basic Melee against the provoking creature. |

## Details

### Thorn dart

**Target:** One visible creature within 4 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Bootlaces are tied to roots; milk disappears from a closed cup.

**Purpose:** Have small courtesies observed.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use a thorn dart and flee into cover; negotiate for a sincere apology.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Offer a small service and ask what rule was broken.

**Habitat:** Hedgerow; garden · **Communication:** Common and Fey

**Loot:** A button or tiny carved key with a local story.

**Summoning:** Fey 1 — [player reference](../summons/hedge-sprite.md).
