# Hedge Sprite

*A tiny winged fey with thornlike features, leaf-colored clothes and a fierce sense of local etiquette. Its home may be no larger than a hedge, but its claim to it is absolute.*

**HP:** 6 · **Speed:** Ground 4; fly 6 · **Footprint:** 1x1

## When attacked

**Evasion:** 1 Bane to player attacks. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** None

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

## On its turn

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

### Thorn dart — Main Action

**Target:** One visible creature within 4 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Threat:** 0 (no modifier) to that defense.

- **Weak defense:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard defense:** 2 physical damage.
- **Strong defense:** 1 physical damage.

**Defend — Maneuver:** 1 additional Bane on player attacks against it until its next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main Action / opportunity Reaction:** one adjacent target, Agility defense, Threat 0; Weak defense 4, Standard 2, Strong 1 physical damage. No specialty riders. An opportunity spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves; that action continues if capable.

## Encounter cues

**Signs:** Bootlaces are tied to roots; milk disappears from a closed cup.

**Purpose:** Have small courtesies observed.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use a thorn dart and flee into cover; negotiate for a sincere apology.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Offer a small service and ask what rule was broken.

**Habitat:** Hedgerow; garden · **Communication:** Common and Fey

**Rewards:** A button or tiny carved key with a local story.

**Summoning:** Fey 1 — [player reference](../summons/hedge-sprite.md).
