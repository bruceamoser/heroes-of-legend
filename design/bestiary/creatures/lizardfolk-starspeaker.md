## Details

### Falling star

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** radiant.

**Weak defense:** Blinded 2. **Standard defense:** Blinded 1.

**Limit:** Spell: provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### Ride the Current

*It catches a moving seam of water and lets the current carry its weight.*

While in water, move up to 3 squares using its printed swim speed without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. On dry ground this Maneuver has no effect.

### Spell Screen

*A sharp gesture splinters the incoming light into a cloud of harmless sparks.*

Before one spell attack against this creature resolves, impose 1 Bane on that attack roll (or grant 1 defense Boon to its player controller). The protection belongs to this creature. For a player’s single AoE attack roll, include it when finding the worst target modifiers, as normal; do not make a separate roll or rescore outcomes for other targets. If players defend separately against an NPC area, it benefits only its own recipient. This is defensive interference, not cancellation; the resulting spell outcome still resolves.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Work from water, use radiant flashes to spoil pursuit, and keep a current-assisted route back to the warning station.

**Encounter and conversation:** An approaching flood has been mistaken for an omen of war; the speaker needs witnesses to the warning.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
