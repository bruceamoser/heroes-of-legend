# Manticore (CV 27)

*A lion-bodied hunter wears a disturbingly human expression beneath a mane of coarse quills.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +0; Agility +2; Guile +1; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** 6; fly 8 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1)\
**Immunities:** None

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates a complete outcome. All attacks use printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Tail spike

**Target:** One visible creature within 8 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Driving strike

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Wing Step

*A single hard wingbeat carries it toward a better angle.*

Move up to 2 squares using its printed fly speed. Pay normal movement costs and provoke normally. This grants no attack, automatic lift of another creature or hovering exception.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Fire a tail spike from an elevated approach, then reposition. Use the driving strike when a climber reaches its perch.

**Encounter and conversation:** It demands stories and fresh provisions from travelers, and remembers which bargains were kept.

**Habitat:** Wild frontier; ancient ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
