# Clockwork Scout

*A small brass surveying machine with jointed feet, a glass lens and a speaking grille. Its precise movements serve observation and reporting rather than a sustained fight.*

**HP:** 6 · **Speed:** Ground 6; climb 5 · **Footprint:** 1x1

## When attacked

**Evasion:** 1 Bane to player attacks. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary HP healing does not repair it; destroyed at 0. Combat repair requires an explicit repair ability. Workshop repair remains a design question.

## On its turn

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

### Needle — Main Action

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Threat:** 1 Boon to that defense.

- **Weak defense:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard defense:** 2 physical damage.
- **Strong defense:** 1 physical damage.

**Defend — Maneuver:** 1 additional Bane on player attacks against it until its next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main Action / opportunity Reaction:** one adjacent target, Agility defense, Threat 1 Boon; Weak defense 4, Standard 2, Strong 1 physical damage. No specialty riders. An opportunity spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves; that action continues if capable.

## Passives

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

## Encounter cues

**Signs:** Pinprick tracks stop at every junction.

**Purpose:** Return an accurate account of a short route.

**Tactics and retreat:** Evade pursuit and use its needle only if cornered.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Cover the lens or recover the controller’s command token.

**Habitat:** Workshop; ruin · **Communication:** Reports simple spoken directions while active

**Rewards:** Delicate parts worth 8 coins from a real destroyed scout; no summoned salvage.

**Summoning:** Construct 1 — [player reference](../summons/clockwork-scout.md).
