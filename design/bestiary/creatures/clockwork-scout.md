# Clockwork Scout (CV 5)

*A small brass surveying machine with jointed feet, a glass lens and a speaking grille. Its precise movements serve observation and reporting rather than a sustained fight.*

**Attributes:** Brawn -1; Fortitude +0; Agility +2; Guile -1; Knowledge +1; Reason +1

**HP:** 6 · **Speed:** 6; climb 5 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Boon · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Needle

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary HP healing does not repair it; destroyed at 0. Combat repair requires an explicit repair ability. Workshop repair remains a design question.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Pinprick tracks stop at every junction.

**Purpose:** Return an accurate account of a short route.

**Tactics and retreat:** Evade pursuit and use its needle only if cornered.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Cover the lens or recover the controller’s command token.

**Habitat:** Workshop; ruin · **Communication:** Reports simple spoken directions while active

**Loot:** Delicate parts worth 8 coins from a real destroyed scout; no summoned salvage.

**Summoning:** Construct 1 — [player reference](../summons/clockwork-scout.md).
