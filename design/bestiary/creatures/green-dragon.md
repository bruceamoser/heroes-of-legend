# Green Dragon (CV 80)

*A forest dragon wears living moss between its green scales and speaks with the patient courtesy of a practiced liar.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +1

**HP:** 48 · **Speed:** 7; fly 9 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 2

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates a complete outcome. All attacks use printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Bitter mist

**Target:** All creatures in a cone of 3 rows, 1/3/5 squares wide; no exclusions. Each player rolls defense separately. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Poisoned 2. **Standard defense:** Poisoned 1.

**Limit:** Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

### Raking claws

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Draw a Veil

*Mist gathers in a thin curtain that blurs the line of approach.*

Place partial obstruction in up to 3 connected visible squares within 3 squares until its next turn. It adds 1 Bane to attacks whose path crosses the veil. Normal cover rules apply; it does not grant Hidden, block movement or cause damage.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Poison a crowded approach and draw mist to complicate the answer. Negotiate when a credible bargain protects the dragon's long-term influence.

**Encounter and conversation:** It offers to end a logging dispute only if both sides accept it as the permanent judge.

**Habitat:** Remote domain; defended lair · **Communication:** Common and Draconic

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
