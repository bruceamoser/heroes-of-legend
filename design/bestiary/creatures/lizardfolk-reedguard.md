# Lizardfolk Reedguard (CV 21)

*A broad-tailed guard wears woven reeds over tough scales and carries a spear designed to hold rather than pierce deeply.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 20 · **Speed:** 6; swim 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

## Details

### Reed-barbed spear

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Sound the Depths

*It slips beneath the surface with scarcely a ripple.*

Move up to 2 squares using its printed swim speed, paying terrain costs. Provokes normally. Water grants only the cover or concealment the scene actually provides; no automatic Hidden.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Hold a trespasser in shallow water while allies clear the nursery. The guard shares its own grapple restrictions and cannot freely swim away with a captive.

**Encounter and conversation:** The guarded water is a nursery, not a treasure pool; respecting that distinction changes the encounter.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
