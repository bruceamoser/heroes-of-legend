# Wraith (CV 39)

*A torn silhouette hangs in the air around two pale sparks, trailing the sound of someone trying to breathe underwater.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +0; Agility +2; Guile +1; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 28 · **Speed:** 6; fly 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2\*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** Direct sunlight (see details)\
**Resistances:** Physical (2), Life (1)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Winterless touch

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** Life.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Slip Through Shadow

*Its outline folds into a seam of darkness and gathers again a few steps away.*

Move up to 2 squares through connected dimly lit or dark squares without provoking. Normal terrain and occupancy rules apply. Does not grant Hidden or allow passage through solid objects.

### Recoil into Shadow

*Its wounded outline stretches into the nearest shadow and runs along the dark edge of the room.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, move up to 2 squares through connected dimly lit or dark squares without provoking. This creature must survive and remain capable. Pay normal terrain costs and obey occupancy; no passage through solid objects or automatic Hidden. No trigger from a free attack; no attack is granted.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Weakness:** In direct sunlight its attacks grant the defender 1 additional Boon (or impose 1 Bane on its attacks when player-controlled). This does not add damage or change ordinary illumination into sunlight.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Approach through darkness and pressure a separated traveler. Its dark movement still cannot pass through a sealed wall.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its hunting route circles the place its name was erased from a memorial.

**Habitat:** Crypt; burial ground; ruined settlement · **Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Undead 7 — [player reference](../summons/wraith.md).
