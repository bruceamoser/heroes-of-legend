## Details

### Tin-pan refrain

**Target:** Only creatures that can hear this attack. One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Keep the Rhythm

*A familiar rhythm cuts through panic and brings one companion back to the moment.*

One willing ally within 3 squares that can hear it reduces either Frightened or Dazed by 1. Once per turn. This does not recalculate action categories already selected at turn start.

### Rally at the Blow

*A sharp call catches the wounded companion's attention before pain can turn into panic.*

After a visible willing ally within 3 squares takes HP damage from an attack, reduce that ally's Frightened by 1 if it can hear this creature. Then give it 1 Boon on its next defense before the start of this creature's next turn; for an NPC ally impose 1 Bane on the corresponding player attack. The defense benefit is consumed on that roll even if canceled and does not stack with itself. No healing or action is granted.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Stay close enough to steady a frightened companion while directing the distracting refrain at whoever is breaking the crew's formation.

**Encounter and conversation:** Learn the work song and the crew may mistake the party for expected reinforcements.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
