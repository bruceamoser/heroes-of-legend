Weak/Standard/Strong **2/3/5 physical damage**, no rider.
Uses its held melee weapon or natural strike; this explicit profile is also its
only opportunity attack, spending Reaction. Summoned forms have no Reaction.

Force, Defend, search, hide and feasible interaction follow the
[creature procedure](../procedure.md). Listed special actions do not grant extra
actions. Standard turn budgets and conversion apply.

## At the table

**Signs:** Small fires start wherever arguments have already begun.

**Purpose:** Turn a small bad decision into a lasting obligation.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sting and retreat; bargain outrageously when cornered.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Refuse hurried bargains and put out the ordinary fires before chasing it.

**Rewards:** One incriminating letter and 7 coins. Summoned bodies and equipment leave no permanent loot.
