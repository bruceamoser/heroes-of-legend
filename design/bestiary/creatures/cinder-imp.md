## Details

### Tail sting

*A needle catches the skin, and a bitter numbness spreads from the puncture.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Poisoned 2. **Standard defense:** Poisoned 1.

### Slip the Rush

*As the pursuer lunges into reach, it ducks sideways through the space the rush left open.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to this creature, move up to 1 square without provoking from that enemy. Other opportunity attacks and normal terrain costs apply. This movement grants no attack; it cannot trigger from forced movement or movement granted by a free attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Small fires start wherever arguments have already begun.

**Purpose:** Turn a small bad decision into a lasting obligation.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sting and retreat; bargain outrageously when cornered.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Refuse hurried bargains and put out the ordinary fires before chasing it.

**Habitat:** City; ruined shrine · **Communication:** Common and Infernal

**Loot:** One incriminating letter and 7 coins.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
