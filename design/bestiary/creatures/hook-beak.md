## Details

### Hooked rush

*It gathers itself, then arrives in a rush of claws and weight.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** After moving 2 toward target, Prone. **Standard defense:** After moving 2 toward target, Prone.

### Stalking Step

*It folds into the broken light, leaving only a leaf moving where it stood.*

Move up to 2 squares without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving, ending in a square with cover or concealment from that enemy. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Does not grant Hidden; a separate Hide action still needs concealment and a roll when uncertain.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Trees carry both claw marks and ragged tufts of feathers.

**Purpose:** Keep intruders from a nest hidden under fallen timber.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pounce from the side of the trail and stand over a downed threat.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Avoid the nest and make a wide, visible retreat.

**Habitat:** Deep forest · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** An old traveler’s charm caught in the nest.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
