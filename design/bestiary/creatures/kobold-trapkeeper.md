# Kobold Trapkeeper

*The trapkeeper taps the floor twice before crossing it and looks annoyed when anyone fails to notice the warning.*

### Main — Dart

**Attack attribute:** Agility · **Target defense:** Agility

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Spell:** No.

- **Weak:** 2 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 physical damage.


**Scatter caltrops — Maneuver, once per scene:** choose 2 connected empty ground squares within 2 squares. They are difficult terrain until cleared with a Main Action from an adjacent square. No damage or entry attack.

### Basic melee and ordinary options

**Basic melee:** Main Action; Brawn against Agility; one adjacent creature;
Weak/Standard/Strong **1/2/4 physical damage**, no rider.
Uses its held melee weapon or natural strike; this explicit profile is also its
only opportunity attack, spending Reaction. Summoned forms have no Reaction.

Force, Defend, search, hide and feasible interaction follow the
[creature procedure](../procedure.md). Listed special actions do not grant extra
actions. Standard turn budgets and conversion apply.

## At the table

**Signs:** Colored knots identify safe footholds to those who know the code.

**Purpose:** Keep larger intruders out of a shared home.

**Tactics and retreat:** Fight behind prepared rubble and retreat through known gaps.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Ask for a guide or follow the marked safe path; the traps defend a place, not a species-wide grudge.

**Rewards:** Light tools, ranged weapon and 4 coins. Summoned bodies and equipment leave no permanent loot.
