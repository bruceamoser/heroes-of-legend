## Details

### Staff

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### Battle Mending

One adjacent living ally; free hand. Pay 1 HP first, bypass resistance, and remain above 0. A player rolls this creature’s Reason: Weak heals 3, Standard 6, Strong 9 HP. Life resistance reduces healing, minimum 1. This spell provokes. Against a healing-harmed target instead, use Fortitude defense: Weak takes 9, Standard 6, Strong 3 Life damage. Same cost; no healing riders.

### Warding Light

*A warm light settles over one companion like a hand on the shoulder.*

One willing ally within 3 squares reduces Frightened by 1. Until this creature’s next turn, that ally also has 1 Boon on its next defense against a mental attack; for an NPC ally, impose 1 Bane on that player attack. Consumed even if canceled; does not stack with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Used bandages hang beside a small field shrine.

**Purpose:** Keep the people under their care alive.

**Tactics and retreat:** Heal an ally before trading staff blows; withdraw with wounded companions when life costs become dangerous.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Pressure the healer’s position, separate them from patients, or negotiate safe evacuation.

**Habitat:** Camp; temple · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Staff, shrine token and 12 coins.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
