# Clay Helper

*A rough humanoid of fired and unfired clay, with broad hands and the maker’s thumbprints still visible. It understands simple work instructions but has no independent craft training.*

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** 4 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Boon · **Threat:** 1 Boon · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

| Main Actions | Damage |
|---|---|
| Basic Melee | 5/3/2 |
| Clay palm | 3/2/1 |

| Maneuvers | Effect |
|---|---|
| Defend | Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. |

| Reactions | Effect |
|---|---|
| Opportunity Attack | Basic Melee against the provoking creature. |

## Details

### Clay palm

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary HP healing does not repair it; destroyed at 0. Combat repair requires an explicit repair ability. Workshop repair remains a design question.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Wet clay patches mend the same pair of footprints.

**Purpose:** Complete one simple assigned task.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use Force to clear a route; strike only if commanded or threatened.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Give a valid instruction or show the maker’s token.

**Habitat:** Workshop; farm · **Communication:** Understands Common instructions; cannot speak

**Loot:** A maker’s mark and ordinary tools, if present; the body is not a free material supply.

**Summoning:** Construct 1 — [player reference](../summons/clay-helper.md).
