# Clay Helper

*A rough humanoid of fired and unfired clay, with broad hands and the maker’s thumbprints still visible. It understands simple work instructions but has no independent craft training.*

**HP:** 10 · **Speed:** Ground 4 · **Footprint:** 1x1

## When attacked

**Evasion:** 1 Boon to player attacks. · **Stability:** 0.

**DR / resistance:** None · **Condition immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

**Weakness:** None

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary HP healing does not repair it; destroyed at 0. Combat repair requires an explicit repair ability. Workshop repair remains a design question.

## On its turn

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

### Clay palm — Main Action

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Threat:** 1 Boon to that defense.

- **Weak defense:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard defense:** 2 physical damage.
- **Strong defense:** 1 physical damage.

**Defend — Maneuver:** 1 additional Bane on player attacks against it until its next turn; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Basic Melee — Main Action / opportunity Reaction:** one adjacent target, Agility defense, Threat 1 Boon; Weak defense 5, Standard 3, Strong 2 physical damage. No specialty riders. An opportunity spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves; that action continues if capable.

## Passives

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

## Encounter cues

**Signs:** Wet clay patches mend the same pair of footprints.

**Purpose:** Complete one simple assigned task.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use Force to clear a route; strike only if commanded or threatened.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Give a valid instruction or show the maker’s token.

**Habitat:** Workshop; farm · **Communication:** Understands Common instructions; cannot speak

**Rewards:** A maker’s mark and ordinary tools, if present; the body is not a free material supply.

**Summoning:** Construct 1 — [player reference](../summons/clay-helper.md).
