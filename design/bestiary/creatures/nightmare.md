# Nightmare (CV 31)

*A dark horse runs on smoking hooves, with a mane that streams backward even when it stands still.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 28 · **Speed:** 9; fly 8 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1\*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1), Fire (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Funeral hooves

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Frightened 2 of this creature. **Standard defense:** Frightened 1 of this creature.

### Slip Through Shadow

*Its outline folds into a seam of darkness and gathers again a few steps away.*

Move up to 2 squares through connected dimly lit or dark squares without provoking. Normal terrain and occupancy rules apply. Does not grant Hidden or allow passage through solid objects.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Drive a threat away from the offered saddle, then use darkness to change position. Its movement cannot pass through a sealed stable wall.

**Encounter and conversation:** It returns to the place its last rider broke an oath, offering a saddle to anyone willing to finish the journey.

**Habitat:** Broken shrine; summoned court; place of conflict · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
