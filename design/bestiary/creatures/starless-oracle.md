# Starless Oracle (CV 29)

*An alien, many-eyed figure with pale skin and an uneven arrangement of joints. Its attention slips between the present and possible futures, making its advice useful and dangerous.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +2; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 28 · **Speed:** 5 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0\*

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Mental (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Future shock

*For a moment, the victim remembers a pain that has not happened yet.*

**Target:** One visible creature within 7 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### Seen It Already

*It starts moving a heartbeat before the strike, following a future the attacker has not yet reached.*

Before one attack from a visible attacker against this creature is rolled, impose 1 Bane on that attack. Against an NPC attack, grant 1 Boon to its single defense roll instead. This changes no target or outcome after dice have been rolled; it does not cancel the action.

### Study the Opening

*It waits for a tell in the stance, then turns its attention to the exposed line.*

Its next attack before the end of this turn imposes 1 additional Bane on player defense, or gains 1 Boon when player-controlled. One attack only; no stacking with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** People independently write down the same unfamiliar constellation.

**Purpose:** Arrange a future in which its prison was never built.

**Tactics and retreat:** Disrupt one hero’s decisions at a time and retreat while offering useful truths.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Ask verifiable questions and act on shared plans rather than private visions.

**Habitat:** Observatory; deep ruin · **Communication:** Common; does not read unspoken thoughts mechanically

**Loot:** A true but incomplete prediction; no automatic ability theft.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
