## Details

### Tide fork

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 3. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Ride the Current

*It catches a moving seam of water and lets the current carry its weight.*

While in water, move up to 3 squares using its printed swim speed. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. On dry ground this Maneuver has no effect.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull a foe toward a channel the guard can traverse quickly. Forced movement still pays terrain and Stability costs.

**Encounter and conversation:** The guard is closing a channel because something larger is hunting beyond it.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
