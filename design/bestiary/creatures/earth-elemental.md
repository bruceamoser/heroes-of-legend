# Earth Elemental (CV 36)

*A walking knot of stone carries veins of bright mineral through a body that sheds gravel with every step.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** 4 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 3

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 2

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (3)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Actions

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates a complete outcome. All attacks use printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Bedrock fist

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Plant the Weight

*It spreads its stance and settles its weight into the ground.*

While standing on solid ground, gain Stability 3 until its next turn. Use the highest source only, never add sources.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Plant its weight before pushing intruders away from the excavation. A visibly repaired breach can change its purpose.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is trying to close a wound cut through the hill by careless excavation.

**Habitat:** Disturbed elemental site · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Elemental 7 — [player reference](../summons/earth-elemental.md).
