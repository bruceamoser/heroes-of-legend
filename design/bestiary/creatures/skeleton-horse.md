# Skeleton Horse (CV 16)

*Moonlight shines through the ribs of a skeletal charger whose tack still bears a funeral procession's faded ribbons.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 9 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Iron-shod hooves

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Driving Stride

*Hooves hammer a straight course through dust and broken grass.*

Move up to 3 squares in a straight line without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Grants no attack or automatic movement of a rider or held creature.

### Fall to Pieces

*The skeleton lets its joints collapse, spilling loose bones beneath the passing blow.*

Before a physical attack against this creature is rolled, gain Physical resistance 3 against that attack only. Use the highest matching resistance. After the attack resolves, it becomes Prone if still present, even if it was already Prone. This does not move its occupied footprint or reassemble it; standing costs the normal 2 movement.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Strike whoever bars the rider's route, then spend its Maneuver on straight-line movement. It cannot carry someone through an occupied square.

**Encounter and conversation:** It waits at a ruined milestone for a rider carrying the correct burial token.

**Habitat:** Crypt; burial ground; ruined settlement · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Undead 3 — [player reference](../summons/skeleton-horse.md).
