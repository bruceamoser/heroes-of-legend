## Details

### Water tug

*A hooked limb braces against the bank and heaves its catch toward the dark water.*

**Target:** One visible creature within 3 squares. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 2. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

### Flicker Aside

*Its outline gutters like a flame in a draft and catches again just beyond the reaching hand.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, teleport to a visible empty square within 1 square. This creature must survive and remain capable. The destination must support its footprint and be reachable by an unobstructed straight path; this does not pass through walls or occupied squares. No opportunity attacks from this teleport. No trigger from a free attack; no attack is granted.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Elemental: magical HP healing applies; cannot swallow potions. Printed immunities only.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** A trickle runs uphill to avoid a fouled pool.

**Purpose:** Restore a blocked or poisoned flow.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull a trespasser from the bank and escape downstream.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Clear the obstruction or stop the contamination.

**Habitat:** Stream; well · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Clean water where it belongs, not a conjured stock of potions.

**Summoning:** Elemental 1 — [player reference](../summons/rill-sprite.md).
