# Cave Bat (CV 4)

*A small cave-dwelling bat with oversized ears, thin dark wings and tiny hooked feet. Sound guides it through darkness, while bright light and loud disturbances unsettle its roost.*

**Attributes:** Brawn -2; Fortitude -1; Agility +2; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**HP:** 4 · **Speed:** 1; fly 8 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** 1 Boon · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Small bite

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Sound-based sight 6 squares; fails while Deafened and does not penetrate walls.

**Signs:** Fine droppings beneath a dry ceiling shelf.

**Purpose:** Find insects and a quiet roost.

**Tactics and retreat:** Flee light and noise; bite only when trapped.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Open a route to the ceiling and stop pursuing it.

**Habitat:** Wilderness · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** No saleable treasure.

**Summoning:** Animal 1 — [player reference](../summons/cave-bat.md).
