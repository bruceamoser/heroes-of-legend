# Poltergeist (CV 18)

*Chairs scrape and small objects tremble around a wavering figure, while dust outlines spectral hands that cannot hold still.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +0; Agility +2; Guile +1; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 18 · **Speed:** 6; fly 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Hurled keepsake

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Requires an unattended object of at most 1 Bulk within 3 squares of the poltergeist. The attack moves that object; it cannot take a held item. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Rattle the Room

*Loose objects jump and settle in all the wrong places.*

Move one unattended object of at most 1 Bulk within 3 squares into an empty square adjacent to its starting square. Cannot harm a creature, take a held item, trigger an attack or move anything fixed in place.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Hurl one unattended keepsake as its attack, then use harmless object movement to point out the forgotten sequence of events. The Maneuver grants no second attack.

**Encounter and conversation:** The disturbance repeats the order of a forgotten accident and stops when someone finally understands it.

**Habitat:** Haunted home; abandoned room · **Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Spirit 3 — [player reference](../summons/poltergeist.md).
