# Ancestor Guardian (CV 18)

*A recognizable ancestral figure bearing the clothing and weapon of its lifetime. Its outline is spectral, but the protective purpose that holds it here is direct and personal.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility +0; Guile +0; Knowledge +1; Reason +2

**HP:** 20 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Remembered blade

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** radiant.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Cover a Companion

*The guardian steps into the line of danger, making the attacker work around its raised guard.*

Before a visible enemy attacks a willing ally adjacent to this creature, impose 1 Bane on that attack roll if this creature can see the attacker. Against an NPC attack, grant that ally 1 Boon to its defense instead. This creature must also be adjacent to the attacker, or hold a shield the DA judges can obstruct the attack. No movement, target substitution or damage transfer. For a single player area attack, use the normal worst-target modifiers; do not roll again.

### Warding Light

*A warm light settles over one companion like a hand on the shoulder.*

One willing ally within 3 squares reduces Frightened by 1. Until this creature’s next turn, that ally also has 1 Boon on its next defense against a mental attack; for an NPC ally, impose 1 Bane on that player attack. Consumed even if canceled; does not stack with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Spirit: magical healing works; cannot swallow potions.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** A family token grows warm near danger.

**Purpose:** Protect descendants without choosing their lives for them.

**Tactics and retreat:** Stand between danger and the vulnerable; push a threat away.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Appeal to the duty that keeps it here or settle a family wrong.

**Habitat:** Family shrine; home · **Communication:** Languages known in life

**Loot:** An inherited story, burial key or directions to a family cache.

**Summoning:** Spirit 3 — [player reference](../summons/ancestor-guardian.md).
