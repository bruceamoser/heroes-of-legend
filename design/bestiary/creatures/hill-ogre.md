# Hill Ogre (CV 20)

*A heavily built giant with thick hands, blunt features and clothing pieced together from sacks and hides. Its strength is formidable, but its needs and fears are often straightforward.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile +0; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

## Details

### Timber club

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Hurl aside

*One broad hand sweeps the obstacle from the ogre’s path.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** push 4. **Standard defense:** push 3. **Strong defense:** push 1.

### Bat Aside the Missile

*A broad hand or haft meets the incoming missile with the ease of brushing a branch aside.*

Before one physical ranged attack from a visible attacker against this creature is rolled, impose 1 Bane on that attack. Against an NPC attack, grant 1 Boon to its defense instead. No protection against area attacks; no missile is returned and the attack still resolves.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Crushed fences mark a route between isolated farms.

**Purpose:** Get food, useful company or a place where people stop chasing it.

**Tactics and retreat:** Push the loudest threat away and pick a fight with one opponent at a time.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Offer a task it can understand and a meal it does not have to steal.

**Habitat:** Hill; ruined farm · **Communication:** Common and Giant

**Loot:** Mismatched belongings and 18 coins; return stolen goods when practical.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
