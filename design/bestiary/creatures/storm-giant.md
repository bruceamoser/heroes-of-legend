# Storm Giant (CV 58)

*A towering sentinel carries a spear whose metal hums before the weather changes.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 46 · **Speed:** 7; swim 7 · **Size:** 3 (3x3 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2\*

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 2 Banes · **Stability:** 3

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2), Lightning (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Storm-crowned spear

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** lightning.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Thrown stone

**Target:** One visible creature within 7 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Limit:** Requires an available loose stone; picking it up is part of this attack. No stone means this attack is unavailable.

### Thunderclap

*A sharp report rolls through the space like a door slammed by the sky.*

One hearing creature within 2 squares gains Deafened 1. Once per turn; no damage.

### Thunderous Answer

*The blow jars loose a thundercrack that rolls back across the space between them.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, its visible attacker within 3 squares gains Deafened 2 if it can hear this creature. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. No damage or interruption of the resolved attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 3; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Daze the nearest dangerous challenger while using deafening thunder to spoil hearing-dependent coordination. It still has only one Main Action.

**Encounter and conversation:** The sentinel is trying to break a sea curse and treats anyone carrying its mark as a possible witness.

**Habitat:** Highland; quarry; remote stronghold · **Communication:** Common and Giant

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
