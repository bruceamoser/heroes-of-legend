## Details

### Binding arms

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Ink Cloud

*Dark ink unfolds through the water and swallows the outline of its arms.*

While submerged, place heavy cover in up to 3 connected water squares within 2 squares until its next turn. Fully concealed targets cannot be individually targeted. No damage or movement obstruction. Once per scene.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Hold one intruder long enough to recover the disputed object; use ink to create a retreat, not as an automatic Hidden state.

**Encounter and conversation:** The octopus has mistaken a bell carried aboard ship for something stolen from its den.

**Habitat:** Wilderness; nesting ground · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.
