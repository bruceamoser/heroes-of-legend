## Details

### Corner-shot dart

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Slowed 2. **Standard defense:** Slowed 1.

**Limit:** Ammunition inventory d6. After each use, roll it; on 1 reduce d6 to d4, or d4 to empty. Cannot use this attack while empty.

### Tunnel Scamper

*It drops low and races along the wall where the loose stones are thinnest.*

Move up to 2 squares without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Treat rubble as normal terrain for this movement only; all other terrain costs and occupied-square restrictions apply. Provoke normally from other enemies.

### Slip the Rush

*As the pursuer lunges into reach, it ducks sideways through the space the rush left open.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to this creature, move up to 1 square without provoking from that enemy. Other opportunity attacks and normal terrain costs apply. This movement grants no attack; it cannot trigger from forced movement or movement granted by a free attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use rubble to change firing angles. The scout values an unblocked tunnel behind it and abandons an exposed straight corridor.

**Encounter and conversation:** Its tunnel marks identify both escape routes and places where the ceiling is about to fail.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
