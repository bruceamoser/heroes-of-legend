## Details

### Cold spark

**Target:** One visible creature within 3 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** cold.

### Flicker Aside

*Its outline gutters like a flame in a draft and catches again just beyond the reaching hand.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, teleport to a visible empty square within 1 square. This creature must survive and remain capable. The destination must support its footprint and be reachable by an unobstructed straight path; this does not pass through walls or occupied squares. No opportunity attacks from this teleport. No trigger from a free attack; no attack is granted.

### Guide the Light

*A ribbon of lanternlight runs along the path, picking out each stone on the way to shelter.*

Brightly light up to 3 connected squares within 3 squares until the start of this creature's next turn. Requires an unobstructed path from this creature to each square; it may choose unlit squares. Ordinary darkness imposes no Boons or Banes for seeing those squares; cover, concealment and hiding still use the normal rules. This neither reveals Hidden creatures automatically nor penetrates opaque obstacles. Reusing this ability replaces its previous illuminated area.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares.

**Signs:** Small lights move against the wind above sunken ground.

**Purpose:** Draw a witness to the place where someone was forgotten.

**Tactics and retreat:** Stay beyond melee reach and use a cold sting only if pursued.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Listen to the pattern of its movement; it may be leading toward a grave, not an ambush.

**Habitat:** Marsh; graveyard · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A buried name or object, not treasure carried by the light.

**Summoning:** Undead 1 — [player reference](../summons/grave-wisp.md).
