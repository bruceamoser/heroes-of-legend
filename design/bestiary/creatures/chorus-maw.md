## Details

### Many-mouthed chorus

**Target:** Only creatures that can hear this attack. All creatures in a cone of 3 rows, 1/3/5 squares wide; no exclusions. Each player rolls defense separately. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

**Limit:** Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

### Follow the Echo

*The sound arrives from the wrong corner as the figure drifts out of reach.*

Move up to 2 squares using a printed movement mode without provoking. Normal terrain and occupancy rules apply; no passage through solid objects or automatic Hidden.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Daze a clustered group with the chorus, then move or use Basic Melee during the required pause. Separate speakers can make its information easier to untangle.

**Encounter and conversation:** One voice knows where a missing person went, but asking it draws the others' attention.

**Habitat:** Cavern; forgotten laboratory; buried ruin · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
