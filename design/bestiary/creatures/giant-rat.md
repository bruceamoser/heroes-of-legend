## Details

### Sewer bite

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Tunnel Scamper

*It drops low and races along the wall where the loose stones are thinnest.*

Move up to 2 squares. Treat rubble squares as normal terrain for this movement only; all other terrain costs and occupied-square restrictions apply. Provokes normally.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Bite only when cornered or defending the nest, then scamper through rubble toward a route larger pursuers find awkward.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is stealing bedding for a nest, and driving it away exposes what frightened it out of the drains.

**Habitat:** Wilderness; nesting ground · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.
