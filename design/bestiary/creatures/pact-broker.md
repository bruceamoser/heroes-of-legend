## Details

### Fine-print rebuke

**Target:** One visible creature within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Borrow a Voice

*A familiar voice comes from an unfamiliar mouth with exactly the remembered hesitation.*

Copy the sound of a voice it has heard until its next turn. This grants no knowledge, obedience, language or combat modifier. A suspicious listener may use Reason with normal Boons/Banes when being deceived has consequences.

### Reproach the Caster

*A whispered accusation rides the fading spell back toward the one who shaped it.*

After a spell attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, its visible caster within 6 squares gains Frightened 2 of this creature if the caster can hear it. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. Frightened applies only while this creature is perceptible; the spell is not canceled.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Daze an impatient challenger while using a familiar voice to provoke an admission. Speech alone creates no automatic contract or control.

**Encounter and conversation:** The broker may accept a clever lawful substitute but will not confuse a threat with an argument.

**Habitat:** Broken shrine; summoned court; place of conflict · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
