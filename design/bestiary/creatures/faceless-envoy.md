## Details

### Unmaking touch

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Blinded 2. **Standard defense:** Blinded 1.

### Wear a Borrowed Face

*Its features soften and settle into a face it has studied.*

Copy the appearance and voice of one observed person of the same Size until dismissed or reduced to 0 HP. Keep its own statistics and equipment; gain no memories, languages or powers. Suspicious examination uses a player Reason roll when consequences warrant one.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Keep a convincing appearance until challenged, then blind a close threat and retreat. Its disguise never changes its combat statistics.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is carrying a genuine peace offer, but its borrowed identity could ruin the negotiations if exposed carelessly.

**Habitat:** Cavern; forgotten laboratory; buried ruin · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
