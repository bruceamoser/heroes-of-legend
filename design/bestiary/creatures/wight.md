# Wight (CV 27)

*An armored corpse watches with the offended dignity of a lord whose court has forgotten to die.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +0; Knowledge +0; Reason +1

**HP:** 24 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Oath-draining blade

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** Life.

**Weak defense:** Slowed 2. **Standard defense:** Slowed 1.

### Rattle of the Grave

*A dry sound rises from the corpse, like someone knocking from inside a coffin.*

One creature within 2 squares that can hear it gains Frightened 1 of this creature. Once per turn; no damage.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Lifeless Body — Passive:** Does not breathe or eat; only its listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** Its controller uses Fortitude defense when player-controlled; a player attacking it rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Dark sight 6 squares; ordinary hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Separate the oath-breaker from companions, then use fear to make the approach costly. It values its old post more than pursuit.

**Encounter and conversation:** It demands a toll once lawful but now ruinous; the original charter can expose its limits.

**Habitat:** Crypt; burial ground; ruined settlement · **Communication:** Common; understands old oaths

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Undead 7 — [player reference](../summons/wight.md).
