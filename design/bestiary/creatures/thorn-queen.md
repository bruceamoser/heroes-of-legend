*A regal woodland fey with branchlike antlers and a gown of living leaves and briars. Her authority binds a particular place and its promises, not every creature of the forest.*

## Details

### Thorn lash

**Target:** One visible creature within 4 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### Root snare

*Roots punch through the ground and lace together around the trespassers’ legs.*

**Target:** 5 connected visible ground squares within 6 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** None.

**Weak defense:** Difficult terrain until next turn; Slowed 2. **Standard defense:** Difficult terrain until next turn; Slowed 1. **Strong defense:** Difficult terrain until next turn.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

**Limit:** Grounded creatures only. Terrain lasts until the start of this creature’s next turn; conditions decay normally. No entry damage or repeated attack.

### Barbed Recoil

*The struck bough bends back, dragging a rake of hooked thorns across the hand that struck it.*

After an adjacent attacker's attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, apply Bleeding 1 to that attacker. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. This is a condition, not another attack.

### Weave the Roots

*Roots braid themselves into firm footholds across the treacherous ground.*

Choose up to 3 connected difficult-terrain ground squares within 3 squares; they count as normal terrain until its next turn. Requires soil or living vegetation; cannot bridge empty space or bypass occupied squares.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Fresh briars grow across the borders of a disputed wood.

**Purpose:** Recover a stolen name, promise or stretch of land.

**Tactics and retreat:** Raise Root Snare beneath pursuers and punish the one who breaks through.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Bring evidence of the old agreement or offer a genuinely equivalent settlement.

**Habitat:** Ancient forest · **Communication:** Common and Fey

**Loot:** A negotiated favor or 120 coins in old tribute, not an automatic royal relic.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
