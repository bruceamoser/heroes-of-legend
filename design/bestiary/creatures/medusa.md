## Details

### Stoneward gaze

**Target:** Only a creature that can see this creature. One visible creature within 4 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Paralyzed 1.

**Limit:** Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

### Study the Opening

*It waits for a tell in the stance, then turns its attention to the exposed line.*

Its next attack before the end of this turn imposes 1 additional Bane on player defense, or gains 1 Boon when player-controlled. One attack only; no stacking with itself.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Study an opening before the gaze, then reposition or use Basic Melee during its required pause. A target that cannot see the medusa is safe from this gaze.

**Encounter and conversation:** The medusa has been blamed for statues far older than its arrival and wants the true sculptor found.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
