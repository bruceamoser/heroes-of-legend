## Details

### Bewildering reed

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

### Keep the Rhythm

*A familiar rhythm cuts through panic and brings one companion back to the moment.*

One willing ally within 3 squares that can hear it reduces either Frightened or Dazed by 1. Once per turn. This does not recalculate action categories already selected at turn start.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Distract the most aggressive listener while helping an ally recover composure. A completed song or honest admission may settle the encounter.

**Encounter and conversation:** The satyr knows the road out of a dangerous revel but wants someone to admit why they entered it.

**Habitat:** Old woodland; crossroads; hidden court · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Fey 3 — [player reference](../summons/satyr.md).
