# Chimera (CV 61)

*Three unlike heads share a scarred body and a restless pair of wings, pulling its attention toward incompatible appetites.*

## Details

### Threefold mauling

**Target:** Up to two adjacent creatures; each player rolls defense separately. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Driving strike

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Divided Watch

*One head follows the threat while the other watches the escape route.*

The next player attack against it before its next turn has 1 additional Bane; when player-controlled, its next defense instead gains 1 Boon. Consumed by that attack even if canceled. Does not stack with itself.

### Second Head Watches

*While one head snaps forward, another turns toward the hand reaching for a spell or weapon.*

When a visible enemy within 2 squares starts an action that normally provokes an opportunity attack, make one free Basic Melee attack against it before the action resolves. Spend the Reaction. The 2-square reach applies only to this Reaction; the provoking action continues if its actor remains capable. No specialty attack or free-attack chain; cannot trigger from a free attack or its granted movement. This grants no extra Reaction and is an alternative to the normal adjacent opportunity attack.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Threaten two close foes with the mauling attack and watch the next approach with its spare heads. It receives no extra turns for extra heads.

**Encounter and conversation:** Luring one head can open a route, but all three must be satisfied to keep the creature away for long.

**Habitat:** Wild frontier; ancient ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
