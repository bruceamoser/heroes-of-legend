# Ogre Taskmaster (CV 36)

*An ogre overseer carries a tally board tied to a heavy club and remembers every debt owed to the work gang.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +0; Guile -1; Knowledge -1; Reason +0

**HP:** 36 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Chain-wrapped club

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Prone.

### Thrown stone

**Target:** One visible creature within 7 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Limit:** Requires an available loose stone; picking it up is part of this attack. No stone means this attack is unavailable.

### Make Room

*A clipped order sends one companion into the opening before it closes.*

One willing active ally within 3 squares that can hear it may spend that ally’s Reaction to move up to 2 squares. Movement provokes normally and pays terrain costs. An uncommanded summon cannot act; summons have no Reaction to spend.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use an ally's willing Reaction to close a gap, then knock an intruder down. The gang's loyalty matters more than the volume of the order.

**Encounter and conversation:** The gang will abandon the overseer if shown that their promised pay never existed.

**Habitat:** Highland; quarry; remote stronghold · **Communication:** Common and Giant

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
