## Details

### Hammering gust

**Target:** One visible creature within 5 squares. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Wind Step

*The body uncoils into a ribbon of air and gathers at the edge of the gust.*

Move up to 3 squares using a printed movement mode without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. Cannot pass through solid objects or occupied squares.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Push a foe out of the broken wind channel and use Wind Step to find an open route. Walls and occupied squares remain obstacles.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is trapped in a mill's broken wind channel and lashes out whenever the machinery tries to start.

**Habitat:** Disturbed elemental site · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Elemental 3 — [player reference](../summons/air-elemental.md).
