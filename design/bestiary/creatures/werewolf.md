## Details

### Hunting claws

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### Change Guise

*The creature shifts between a familiar face and its hybrid shape.*

Change between humanoid and hybrid appearance. Keep current HP, attributes, resistances, conditions and printed movement; no healing or refreshed resources. Natural attacks require hybrid form; held weapon attacks and Basic Melee remain available in humanoid form. Equipment remains carried or worn where it fits. No infection or extra inventory capacity.

### Follow the Pack

*One hunter moves and another flows into its wake, tightening the circle without a sound.*

After a visible active ally ends voluntary movement adjacent to a visible enemy within 3 squares of this creature, move up to 2 squares, ending adjacent to that ally. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally. The ally must be conscious and not Paralyzed; an uncommanded summon cannot trigger this. No attack or command is granted, and movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger it.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Adopt hybrid form before using the claws and follow the quarry through open ground. A familiar voice may reveal the grievance driving the hunt.

**Encounter and conversation:** The hunt follows an old grievance, and someone who recognizes the voice beneath the growl may learn why.

**Habitat:** Settlement; frontier; contested road · **Communication:** Common and its community’s language

**Loot:** Ordinary carried gear and 8 coins; special rewards belong to the adventure.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
