# Reef Hunter

*A large reef shark with a pale belly, dark back and rows of serrated teeth. It is built for swift turns in open water and cannot pursue prey onto land.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility +1; Guile +0; Knowledge -2; Reason +1

**HP:** 22 · **Speed:** 0; swim 9 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Serrated bite

*The claws snag and drag; blood follows the withdrawing talons.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Bleeding 2. **Standard defense:** Bleeding 1.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Fishing lines return cleanly severed.

**Purpose:** Feed and return to deeper water.

**Tactics and retreat:** Strike a separated swimmer and keep moving through open water.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Leave the water or stay in a protected group; it cannot follow onto land.

**Habitat:** Coast; reef · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** No carried treasure.

**Summoning:** Animal 3 — [player reference](../summons/reef-hunter.md).
