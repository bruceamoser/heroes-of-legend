## Details

### Funeral toll

*For a moment, the victim remembers a pain that has not happened yet.*

**Target:** All other creatures in a 3x3-square area centered on a visible square within 5. **Player defense:** Reason. **Damage type:** mental.

**Weak defense:** Dazed 2. **Standard defense:** Dazed 1.

**Spell:** Provokes when cast beside an enemy.

### Answering Lament

*Its outline shudders with the injury and a grieving note rises from somewhere behind the attacker.*

After an attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, one visible attacker within 3 squares that can hear it gains Frightened 2 of this creature. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. Frightened applies only while this creature is perceptible.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Spirit: magical healing works; potions cannot be swallowed.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Ash-gray ribbons hang from trees along its route.

**Purpose:** Gather witnesses for an uncompleted funeral.

**Tactics and retreat:** Sound its bell at a cluster, then drift away while they recover.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Speak the names of the unburied or carry the bell toward consecrated ground.

**Habitat:** Battlefield; marsh · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A recovered list of the lost; a grateful community may offer 60 coins.

**Summoning:** Spirit 7 — [player reference](../summons/mourning-bell.md).
