# Wild Boar (CV 9)

*A low, heavy wild pig covered in coarse bristles, with curved tusks and a thick shield of hide over its shoulders. Usually wary, it becomes fiercely direct when cornered or defending young.*

## Details

### Tusks

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Break the Approach

*It lashes out with shoulder or horn, spoiling the straight line of the charging foe.*

After a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to this creature, push that enemy 1 square directly away. Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules; no separate attack damage. Forced movement and movement granted by a free attack cannot trigger this.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Turned soil and bark stripped from the roots of trees.

**Purpose:** Keep a feeding patch or its young undisturbed.

**Tactics and retreat:** Charge the nearest intruder and use cover to stop pursuit.

**Counterplay and conversation:** A sturdy obstacle interrupts its approach; backing away from the young may end the danger.

**Habitat:** Wilderness · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A hunter with the appropriate card can make use of the carcass.

**Summoning:** Animal 1 — [player reference](../summons/wild-boar.md).
