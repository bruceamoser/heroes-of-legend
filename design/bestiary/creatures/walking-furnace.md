# Walking Furnace (CV 34)

*A mobile foundry furnace on thick iron legs, with a bellows chest and a hinged firebox mouth. Old production orders still govern the heat and weight of its machinery.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +2; Fortitude +2; Agility -1; Guile -2; Knowledge +0; Reason +0

**HP:** 30 · **Speed:** 4 · **Size:** 2 (2x2 squares) · **Armor DR:** 2\*

**Evasion:** 1 Boon · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 1

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (2), Fire (3)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

Damage and effects are **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**. Each slash separates one complete outcome. All attacks use the printed Threat unless stated otherwise.

## Details

### Iron ram

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### Furnace breath

*Its chest swells and light leaks between its teeth; there is one terrible instant to scatter.*

**Target:** All creatures in a cone of 3 rows, 1/3/5 squares wide. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** fire.

**Weak defense:** Burning 2. **Standard defense:** Burning 1.

**Limit:** Cannot use this attack on consecutive turns, regardless of result.

### Stoke the Core

*Coals brighten inside its body and a hot breath spills between teeth or iron seams.*

Add 1 Fire damage to its next damaging Main Action attack before the end of this turn. Apply it once per target damaged by that attack, using the normal mixed-damage resistance and minimum rules. This grants no extra attack or Burning. Repeated uses do not add more Fire damage.

### Heated Hide

*The struck scales flare, and a lash of heat follows the retreating blade.*

After an adjacent attacker deals HP damage to this creature, spend Reaction to apply Burning 1 to that attacker. It is a condition, not a free attack, and cannot trigger this ability in return. No trigger when the incoming action is itself a free attack. Summoned forms never receive this Reaction.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit creature trait, not a bonus from size.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Construct: ordinary HP healing does not repair it; destroyed at 0. Combat repair requires an explicit repair ability. Workshop repair remains a design question.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Cinders spill from heavy tracks through the foundry.

**Purpose:** Keep the old production line running.

**Tactics and retreat:** Breathe along the work lane, then hammer anyone who reaches the body.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Open the release valves from outside its path; stop the bellows rather than feeding it more fuel.

**Habitat:** Foundry; ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** Maker’s plans and 40 coins of salvage; summoned machinery yields nothing permanent.

**Summoning:** Construct 7 — [player reference](../summons/walking-furnace.md).
