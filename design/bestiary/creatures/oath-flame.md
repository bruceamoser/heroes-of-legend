# Oath Flame (CV 28)

*A humanlike figure of shrine fire and bright ash, carrying a blade shaped by the oath it embodies. It has judgment within that promise, not limitless divine authority.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +2; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +1; Reason +2

**HP:** 28 · **Speed:** 6 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 1\*

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (1), Radiant (3)\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Promise made fire

*The blow lands with enough force to send its victim stumbling backward.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** radiant.

**Weak defense:** Push 2. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

### Spell Screen

*A sharp gesture splinters the incoming light into a cloud of harmless sparks.*

Before one spell attack against this creature resolves, impose 1 Bane on that attack roll (or grant 1 defense Boon to its player controller). The protection belongs to this creature. For a player’s single AoE attack roll, include it when finding the worst target modifiers, as normal; do not make a separate roll or rescore outcomes for other targets. If players defend separately against an NPC area, it benefits only its own recipient. This is defensive interference, not cancellation; the resulting spell outcome still resolves.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Spirit: magical healing works; cannot swallow potions.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** A neglected shrine burns without fuel on the anniversary of an oath.

**Purpose:** Make someone answer for a freely given promise.

**Tactics and retreat:** Drive the oathbreaker into the open and Defend against interference.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Show that the oath was fulfilled, coerced or made impossible by its beneficiary.

**Habitat:** Shrine; battlefield · **Communication:** Common and the language of its oath

**Loot:** A restored oath may earn a temple’s aid; no portable holy weapon.

**Summoning:** Spirit 7 — [player reference](../summons/oath-flame.md).
