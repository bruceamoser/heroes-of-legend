# Veteran Captain (CV 27)

*A seasoned officer with maintained armor, visible campaign scars and a weapon worn smooth at the grip. Authority rests on experience and the ability to bring people home alive.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +2; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +1; Reason +1

## Details

### Commanding cut

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** A camp has dry powder, covered food and posted escape routes.

**Purpose:** Complete the assignment with as few losses as possible.

**Tactics and retreat:** Rally a threatened ally, attack whoever breaks the line, and offer terms before a rout.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Cut communication or reveal that the employer has betrayed the troops.

**Habitat:** Fort; battlefield · **Communication:** Common and one regional language

**Loot:** Melee weapon, medium armor, order papers and 25 coins.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
