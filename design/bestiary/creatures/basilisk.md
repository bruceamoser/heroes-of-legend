# Basilisk

*Its eyelids slide sideways. Around it, mice lie stiff as little carvings until they begin to twitch again.*

### Main — Heavy bite

**Attack attribute:** Brawn · **Target defense:** Agility

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Spell:** No.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage.


### Main — Stone stare

**Attack attribute:** Reason · **Target defense:** Fortitude

**Target:** One visible creature within 4 squares that can see it. **Spell:** Yes; provokes next to an enemy.

- **Weak:** No damage. Slowed 1.
- **Standard:** No damage. Slowed 2.
- **Strong:** No damage. Paralyzed 1.

**Procedure:** [Stone Stare](../../monster-abilities/stone-stare.md).

### Basic melee and ordinary options

**Basic melee:** Main Action; Brawn against Agility; one adjacent creature;
Weak/Standard/Strong **2/3/5 physical damage**, no rider.
Uses its held melee weapon or natural strike; this explicit profile is also its
only opportunity attack, spending Reaction. Summoned forms have no Reaction.

Force, Defend, search, hide and feasible interaction follow the
[creature procedure](../procedure.md). Listed special actions do not grant extra
actions. Standard turn budgets and conversion apply.

## At the table

**Signs:** Animals move with stiff legs near a trail of broad clawed feet.

**Purpose:** Bask undisturbed and catch slow prey.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use Stone Stare on an approaching target, then bite if it remains close.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Avert the eyes and accept the printed Banes; use obstacles to break sight.

**Rewards:** A naturalist may seek a shed scale; no automatic permanent petrification cure is required. Summoned bodies and equipment leave no permanent loot.
