# Gate Archon (CV 43)

*A broad celestial sentinel wears a gate's geometry in its armor, with keys that ring only when a boundary is crossed.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +0; Fortitude +1; Agility +1; Guile +1; Knowledge +2; Reason +2

**HP:** 38 · **Speed:** 5 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 3\*

**Evasion:** None · **Threat:** 1 Bane · **Stability:** 2

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** Physical (3), Radiant (2)\
**Immunities:** None

## Details

### Threshold hammer

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Fortitude. **Damage type:** radiant.

**Weak defense:** Push 3. **Standard defense:** Push 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Mark the Boundary

*A sharp line of light marks a boundary across the floor.*

Place up to 3 connected empty ground squares within 3 squares as difficult terrain until its next turn. No damage, wall or prohibition against crossing; all creatures pay the same terrain costs.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 2; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Mark a difficult approach, then push intruders back across it.

**Encounter and conversation:** It cannot abandon its post, but it can authorize someone else to deal with the threat beyond it.

**Habitat:** Sacred boundary; refuge; contested oath · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Ineligible.
