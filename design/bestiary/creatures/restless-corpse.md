# Zombie Shambler (CV 9)

*A swollen walking corpse with stiff joints, torn clothing and grasping hands. It has little judgment, but enough dead weight and persistence to obstruct a narrow passage.*

**Attributes:** Brawn +1; Fortitude +1; Agility -2; Guile -2; Knowledge -2; Reason -1

**HP:** 16 · **Speed:** 3 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Boon · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None\
**Resistances:** None\
**Immunities:** Bleeding; Poisoned; Asleep

## Details

### Clutching hands

*A coil or grasping limb closes around the target and braces against its struggling weight.*

**Target:** One adjacent creature. **Player defense:** Agility. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Grappled 2. **Standard defense:** Grappled 1.

**Limit:** The holder suffers its own Grappled contribution. A new result replaces its lower contribution, not adds to it. Release freely; separation beyond reach, unconsciousness or death ends the hold.

### Clinging Recoil

*A ruined hand closes on cloth and flesh even as the blow tears through the corpse.*

After an adjacent attacker’s attack resolves and deals HP damage to this creature, apply Slowed 2 to that attacker. This creature must survive and remain capable. No trigger from a free attack or another retaliatory effect. This grants no grapple, extra damage or equipment destruction.

### Shambling Advance

*The corpse lurches after the nearest warm body, dragging its ruined feet without ever looking away.*

Move up to 2 squares toward a visible living creature within 6 squares; its next attack against that creature before turn end imposes 1 additional Bane on the player defense. For a player-controlled creature, gain 1 attack Boon instead. It must end closer to that creature than it began. For this movement only, treat difficult ground as normal; pay other costs and provoke normally. One attack only; repeated uses do not stack. This grants no attack and cannot be used while movement is prevented.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses the printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature. Spends its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Lifeless Body:** does not breathe or eat; only the listed immunities apply.

**Healing:** Healing-harmed: ordinary healing resolves as Life damage instead; no beneficial riders.

**Against offensive healing:** controller defends with Fortitude if this is player-controlled; a player attacking it still rolls only their attack.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Signs:** Scrapes run low along doors and stairs.

**Purpose:** Reach the place it was ordered to occupy.

**Tactics and retreat:** Use its body to block a passage; seize anyone who stays adjacent.

**Counterplay and conversation:** Lead it away from the route rather than being pinned in the doorway.

**Habitat:** Graveyard; ruin · **Communication:** None

**Loot:** A personal token can identify the dead.

**Summoning:** Undead 1 — [player reference](../summons/restless-corpse.md).
