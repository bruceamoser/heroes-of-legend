## Details

### Undertow arm

**Target:** One visible creature within 2 squares. **Player defense:** Brawn. **Damage type:** physical.

**Weak defense:** Pull 3. **Standard defense:** Pull 1.

**Limit:** Apply Stability, terrain costs and normal collision rules to the printed distance.

### Ride the Current

*It catches a moving seam of water and lets the current carry its weight.*

While in water, move up to 3 squares using its printed swim speed without provoking from one visible enemy chosen before moving. Pay normal terrain costs and provoke normally from other enemies. On dry ground this Maneuver has no effect.

### General details

**Basic Melee:** One adjacent creature; physical damage; Agility defense. Uses printed Threat. No specialty riders.

**Defend:** Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until its next turn. Combines with Evasion through normal Boon/Bane rules; repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Basic Melee against the provoking creature; spend its Reaction before the provoking action resolves. That action continues if capable.

**Heavy Frame — Passive:** Stability 1; an explicit trait, not a bonus calculated from Size.

**Healing:** Living: ordinary HP healing applies.

**Senses:** Ordinary sight and hearing.

**Tactics and retreat:** Pull an obstacle toward a clear channel and ride the current after it. It gains no automatic drowning attack from its watery body.

**Encounter and conversation:** It is following stolen spring water and can be persuaded to return once the source is restored.

**Habitat:** Disturbed elemental site · **Communication:** Common

**Loot:** Recoverable possessions or lair clues chosen for the adventure; body parts grant no automatic special equipment or coin value.

**Summoning:** Elemental 3 — [player reference](../summons/water-elemental.md).
