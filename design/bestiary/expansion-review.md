# Family expansion review — October 15, 2026

Issues #1190–#1195 cover the source survey, four content groups and integration.
The roster grows from 84 to **192 creatures**, with **244 specialty attack choices**,
**93 monster-only abilities** and **73 explicit summon selections**. The 350-card
character catalog is unchanged.

## What the expansion adds

The [family finder](families.md) now exposes skeletons, zombies, ghouls, ghasts,
ghosts, shadows, wraiths, mummies, vampires and liches by familiar names. Existing
HOL names retain their stable paths and IDs. New families include hobgoblin squads,
ogre roles, elemental giants, classic fantastic beasts and broader supernatural
encounters. Every addition has original appearance, tactics and an encounter hook.

Mortal creatures describe roles and circumstances, not an ancestry's moral nature.
Celestials can be misguided protectors; fiends can be bargainers; a beast may simply
be defending its nest. These are playable situations rather than mandatory fights.

## Boundaries kept visible

- All new numerical values, summon tiers and named capabilities are proposals.
  Existing approved rules and creature numbers are unchanged by the expansion.
- 43 new monster abilities are separate from player purchases. Effects use existing
  conditions, movement, cover, inventory dice and action costs. There is no imported
  AC, spell-slot, disease, extra-head action or equipment-destruction subsystem.
- Named Main Actions are choices. Giants have available-stone ranged alternatives;
  dragons can use claws while their breath is unavailable. Extra heads do not grant
  extra turns. Grapplers share their own contribution's restrictions.
- Sound-dependent attacks explicitly require hearing. Sight or a printed special
  sense still determines whether a target can be selected.
- The new unicorn protects through light and displacement. The existing Moon Hart
  retains the distinct HP-healer role; no new repeatable summoned healing pool was
  introduced. Vampire Spawn transfers limited HP actually lost by a living target
  and is not an eligible summon.
- Strong classic motifs are expressed within current rules: cockatrice numbing is
  temporary Paralyzed, corrosion hampers movement, and a hydra has a two-target bite.
  Permanent petrification, corrosion that destroys possessions, severed-head regrowth,
  possession and swallowing are not silently added as universal procedures.

## Checks and next review

The read-only bestiary audit checks every card, CV title, defense column, outcome
effects, inverse player reference, attribute parity, resistance/Stability cap,
links and CV arithmetic. The full design audit checks the wider catalog. A separate
comparison confirms that old card mechanics retain their values; the familiar-name
changes do not change their damage, HP, defenses or permissions.

New additions need Bruce's content review before randomized balance testing. Priority
scenes are a mixed goblin crew, ghast with zombies, ogre command group, giant with rough
terrain, a dragon breath cycle and new summon choices. The existing CV seed does not
price all combinations reliably; matching a budget is not evidence of encounter length.
No new combat simulation or final book layout was performed.
