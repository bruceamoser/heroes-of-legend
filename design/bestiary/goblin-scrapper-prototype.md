# Goblin Scrapper

*A small, wiry goblin salvager with keen eyes, patched work clothes and pockets full of useful scraps. Quick hands and knowledge of the ruins compensate for modest reach.*

**HP:** 8 · **Speed:** 7 · **Size:** 1 (medium) · **Armor DR:** 0

**Evasion:** 1 Bane · **Threat:** None · **Stability:** 0

**Weaknesses:** None  
**Resistances:** None  
**Immunities:** None

## Actions

Damage is **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense**.

| Main Actions | Damage |
|---|---|
| Basic Melee | 4/2/1 |
| Scrap Knife | 5/3/2 |

| Maneuvers | Effect |
|---|---|
| Roll Away | Move up to 2 squares in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks. |
| Defend | Player attacks suffer 1 additional Bane until the start of its next turn. |

| Reactions | Effect |
|---|---|
| Opportunity Attack | Basic Melee against the provoking creature. |

## Details

**Attacks:** Both attacks target one adjacent creature, deal physical damage and use Agility defense. Apply the printed Threat to either attack. Evasion applies to player attacks against the goblin.

**Roll Away:** Pay normal terrain costs from the 2-square allowance; normal occupied-square restrictions apply.

**Defend:** Adds to Evasion through the normal Boon/Bane rules. Repeated Defend does not stack.

**Opportunity Attack:** Resolves before the provoking action. That action continues if the creature remains capable.

**Senses and speech:** Ordinary sight and hearing; speaks Common and Goblin.

**Tactics:** Strike, then roll behind cover. Bargain when cornered.

**Encounter:** Guards a salvage claim in ruins or beside a road. Bright scraps mark safe routes through the rubble. Recognizing its claim or offering a useful tool can avoid a fight.

**Loot:** Melee weapon, tool scraps and 3 coins.

## Design notes — outside the creature card

Approved layout sample, issue #1180. Applied to the [catalog entry](creatures/goblin-scrapper.md) and the full creature roster in issue #1182. The catalog is the maintained play reference.

- **Armor DR** always prints physical resistance, including 0. Add an asterisk when additional resistances appear below; list those by type and value, such as Fire (1). Do not require the DA to add the header and list together.
- **Evasion/Threat** spell out Boons or Banes rather than signed numbers. “None” means no modifier. The damage-order legend is printed once.
- **Size 1 (medium)** follows Bruce’s example. The label does not grant Stability; standard size terminology across the catalog remains a later consistency pass.
- **Roll Away** replaces Stalking Step and adds Bruce’s explicit protection against opportunity attacks. It now has a separate monster ability entry. CV remains provisional.
- Keep Defend and Opportunity Attack visible so the action list does not omit options the creature already has. The two Main Actions are choices, not two attacks per turn.
- No weakness values are introduced by this layout sample. Their numerical meaning needs to be settled before populated examples are published.
- Existing attributes and provisional CV bookkeeping remain in the linked catalog entry, outside this play reference.
