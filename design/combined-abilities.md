# Combined disciplines

Magical and nonmagical disciplines can combine freely. A magical ability spends its printed SP each time it is used, before resolution; you must have enough SP. Learning costs DP once, separately from casting. Each ability still uses only its stated action and attribute.

Each ability below requires character level 7 and every listed endpoint. Learn the ability separately after meeting all requirements: 3 DP for two disciplines, 4 DP for three, or 5 DP for four. These are new abilities, not automatic improvements to their prerequisite techniques.

A combination does not require a lower-count combined ability unless explicitly listed. The discipline count describes breadth of training, not a separate character level. Reactions still use your available Reaction, damage cannot fall below 1, and first-aid abilities still use the recipient’s first-aid allowance. An encounter ends when the immediate conflict is resolved; briefly pausing or restarting an action does not reset an encounter limit.

## Iron Tempest
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: arms:bladework:4, armor:harness:4
Main Action · Brawn, wearing armor and wielding a melee weapon. Move up to 3 squares, ignoring extra movement cost from difficult terrain, and attack up to three enemies adjacent at any point along that route. Roll once; each takes 3/5/8 physical damage. Each target is struck once. Movement reactions resolve normally. This is a new attack; Measured Cut improvements do not apply.

## Shatter the Formation
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: arms:impact:4, guard:shieldwork:4
Main Action · Brawn, with a melee weapon and shield. Attack up to three adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 2/4/6 physical damage and is pushed up to 2 squares directly away. On Strong, each also becomes Prone. Blocked movement stops the push; it does not cancel damage or Prone. Do not also resolve Battering Stroke or Shield Bash.

## The Unbroken Gate
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: armor:bracing:4, guard:interception:4
Main Action, wearing armor and holding a shield. Until your next turn starts, you cannot move voluntarily or be forcibly moved. Whenever an adjacent ally is targeted by a single-target attack, you may become its target before its defense roll; roll your own defense and receive all effects. These interceptions require no Reaction. Area attacks are unaffected. All attacks still deal at least 1 damage.

## The Last Safe Step
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: guard:watchfulness:4, athletics:balance:4
Reaction. When an ally within 4 squares is about to take damage from an attack or fall, move up to 4 squares along a legal route to reach it. Neither of you triggers movement reactions during this rescue. If you reach it, move together up to 2 more squares and reduce that damage by 4, minimum 1. An attack still resolves against its original target. A fall ends only if the destination supports both creatures.

## Anatomist’s Volley
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: marksmanship:precision:4, scholarship:lore:4
Main Action · Agility, with a ranged weapon. Attack up to three visible enemies within 12 squares whose weaknesses you identified with Recall the Record. Spend one projectile per target and roll once; each takes 3/5/8 physical damage, ignoring up to 2 physical resistance. Other reductions apply normally. This ability does not reveal weaknesses or share its damage benefit with other attacks.

## Steel across the Gap
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: marksmanship:thrown-weapons:4, athletics:leaping:4
Main Action · Agility, with a thrown weapon. Jump up to 4 squares to a visible legal landing without triggering movement reactions. At any point during the jump, throw the weapon at up to two visible enemies within 6 squares of you and 2 squares of each other. Roll once; each takes 3/5/7 physical damage. The weapon lands beside the second target, or the only target if there is one.

## Silent Entry
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: subterfuge:locks:4, craft:repairs:4
1 minute · Guile, with lock tools and repair tools. Work on one reachable nonmagical door, lock or mechanical fastening. Add 3/5/7 progress toward opening it without visible damage; the DA sets the objective. On completion, you may close it afterward so it appears undisturbed to casual inspection. This neither suppresses a separate alarm nor bypasses a trap; those remain separate tasks.

## The Perfect Substitution
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: subterfuge:sleight:4, influence:deception:4
One exchange · Guile. Engage a listener who understands you while exchanging one accessible palm-sized object within reach for a similar object you carry. Roll once and add 2/4/6 progress to both the deception and unnoticed-exchange objectives, each set separately by the DA. The exchange occurs only when its own objective completes. Held weapons, worn armor and secured containers cannot be exchanged.

## Read the Killing Ground
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: subterfuge:traps:4, scholarship:investigation:4
10 minutes · Reason. Examine a visible area up to 6 squares across; add 3/5/7 progress toward identifying its accessible nonmagical traps, their triggers and a safe route through them. At completion, the DA identifies all supported findings and any inaccessible gaps. You and companions following your exact instructions can traverse the identified route without triggering those traps while their arrangement remains unchanged.

## Shelter against the Siege
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: wilderness:shelter:4, craft:construction:4
1 hour, with tools and enough timber, stone or other sound materials. Build a concealed fortified shelter for up to eight people on stable ground. No roll. Occupants behind its walls gain 2 Boons on defense against attacks from outside; each door or wall section has 15 HP and physical resistance 2. Every damaging attack against a section deals at least 1. The shelter offers no protection through an open door and does not automatically make overnight rest safe.

## A Way through Death
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: medicine:first-aid:4, armor:endurance:4
Main Action · Knowledge, wearing armor and carrying medical supplies. Move up to 3 squares to a willing or helpless creature no larger than you. Restore 5/8/11 HP using its available first-aid attempt, then carry it up to 3 squares to a legal destination. Both movements ignore movement reactions. You may perform the rescue without healing if its allowance is spent; this restores no HP and consumes no supplies.

## A Treaty in Common Words
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: influence:negotiation:4, scholarship:languages:4
30 minutes · Reason. With willing representatives of up to three sides and a working language, interpreter or shared reference text, formulate one concrete agreement. Roll once and add 3/5/7 progress to each side’s separate acceptance objective. On each completion, the DA states that side’s commitments and known reservations plainly. No side is compelled to accept impossible terms, and the agreement creates no magical enforcement.

## Break the Encirclement
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: arms:bladework:4, guard:shieldwork:4, athletics:leaping:4
Main Action · Brawn, with a melee weapon and shield. Move up to 4 squares, including a jump up to 3 squares, without movement reactions. Attack up to four enemies adjacent at any point on the route. Roll once; each takes 3/6/9 physical damage and is pushed 1 square directly away from your attack position. Afterward, up to two willing allies within 3 squares of your destination may each move 2 squares without movement reactions.

## The Moving Bulwark
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: armor:deflection:4, guard:shelter:4, influence:leadership:4
Main Action, wearing armor and holding a shield. Choose up to four willing allies within 3 squares who can hear you. You and each chosen ally may immediately move 2 squares without movement reactions. Until your next turn starts, chosen allies within 3 squares of you gain 1 Boon on defense and reduce attack damage by 3, minimum 1. You gain the same protection. Reusing this ability replaces its previous effect.

## Hunt at the Crossroads
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: marksmanship:readiness:4, wilderness:tracking:4, scholarship:planning:4
10 minutes. Prepare an ambush along a trail you have followed, at a site you have surveyed. Choose up to four willing allies and a visible trigger line up to 6 squares long. Once before the next safe overnight rest, when a tracked enemy crosses the line, you and each chosen ally may spend an available Reaction to make one known basic attack against it if in range. These attacks gain 1 Boon and +2 damage. The preparation ends if you leave the site.

## The Hidden Road
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: subterfuge:stealth:4, athletics:climbing:4, wilderness:shelter:4
1 hour · Agility, with rope and concealment materials. Establish a concealed route up to 20 squares long across terrain you can physically traverse; add 3/5/7 progress toward the DA’s route objective. On completion, up to six companions following your instructions can traverse it at normal movement cost with 2 Boons on rolls to remain unnoticed. It creates no bridge across unsupported space. Discovery or physical damage to the route ends these benefits until repaired.

## The Field Hospital
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: medicine:first-aid:4, craft:mixtures:4, wilderness:shelter:4
30 minutes, with medical supplies and a safe, prepared shelter. Treat up to six willing creatures, each using its available first-aid attempt. Roll Knowledge once; each restores 5/8/11 HP and reduces temporary Bleeding or Poisoned by 2, choosing separately for each patient. A patient with a spent allowance receives condition care only. Supplies must cover every patient. This creates no extra healing allowance, SP recovery or overnight rest.

## The Truth before the Council
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: scholarship:investigation:4, influence:negotiation:4, subterfuge:sleight:4
10 minutes · Reason. Present a case supported by evidence you investigated, including a physical exhibit you can handle and demonstrate. Address up to four decision-makers who can understand you. Roll once; add 3/5/7 progress to each relevant acceptance objective. Each listener whose objective completes acknowledges the supported facts and grants the attainable concession agreed with the DA. This does not prove unsupported claims or reveal facts absent from the evidence.

## The Day We Held
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: arms:bladework:4, armor:bracing:4, guard:interception:4, influence:leadership:4
Main Action · Brawn, with armor, a melee weapon and shield. Once per encounter, attack up to four adjacent enemies. Roll once; each takes 4/7/10 physical damage. Until your next turn starts, you cannot be forcibly moved, and you and up to four chosen allies within 3 squares reduce attack damage by 4, minimum 1. Each chosen ally may immediately move 2 squares toward you without movement reactions. This grants no extra attacks or Reactions.

## Bring Everyone Home
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: medicine:first-aid:4, armor:endurance:4, athletics:balance:4, guard:watchfulness:4
Main Action · Knowledge, with armor, a shield and medical supplies. Once per encounter, move up to 8 squares without movement reactions. Along the route, treat up to three different willing or helpless creatures within reach: each with an available first-aid attempt restores 5/8/11 HP from one shared roll and spends that attempt. Carry one creature no larger than you along any part of the route without added encumbrance. Healing cannot exceed maximum HP or restore a spent allowance.

## A City Opens Its Gates
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: influence:negotiation:4, scholarship:languages:4, subterfuge:sleight:4, craft:construction:4
1 day · Reason. Through an on-site demonstration of a feasible civic project, broker an agreement among up to four willing factions with whom you can communicate. Use a visible model or working example built with real materials; do not invent resources. Roll once; add 4/7/10 progress to each faction’s separate agreement objective. Completion secures the specific commitments the DA established, such as access, labor or a ceasefire. New terms or changed circumstances need a new objective; this is not control over a settlement.

## Stormsteel Arc
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: air:3, arms:bladework:4
Main Action · Brawn · 3 SP, with a melee weapon. Sweep lightning through up to three visible enemies within 3 squares. Roll once; each takes 3/6/9 lightning damage. On Strong, each also gains Dazed 1. This is a new magical attack, not a use of Measured Cut; its improvements do not apply.

## The Invisible Shieldbearer
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: telekinesis:3, guard:interception:4
Reaction · 2 SP, with a held shield. When an ally within 8 squares is about to take damage from an attack, project a force barrier to it. Reduce that attack’s damage by 4, minimum 1, then move the willing ally up to 2 squares along a legal route without movement reactions. This protects against one attack only and requires no additional roll.

## Borrowed Dawn
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: restoration:3, medicine:first-aid:4
Main Action · Knowledge · 4 SP. Restore 8/12/16 HP to one living willing or helpless creature within reach. Also reduce its Bleeding by 2. This is magical healing, not first aid: it neither spends nor refreshes the recipient’s first-aid allowance. It cannot raise the dead, restore Grit or exceed maximum HP.

## Starfall Volley
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: flame:3, marksmanship:precision:4, scholarship:lore:4
Main Action · Agility · 4 SP, with a ranged weapon. Attack up to three visible enemies within 12 squares whose weaknesses you identified with Recall the Record. Spend one projectile per target and roll once; each takes 4/7/10 fire damage and Burning 1. Each target is struck once; allies and other creatures nearby are unaffected. This grants no bonus to other attacks.

## Step Between Heartbeats
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: passage:3, subterfuge:stealth:4, athletics:leaping:4
Main Action · Agility · 4 SP, with a melee weapon. Teleport to a visible unoccupied square within 10 squares, then attack one adjacent creature for 4/7/10 physical damage. After the attack, teleport up to 4 squares to another visible legal destination. Neither teleport triggers movement reactions. If you finish behind cover that conceals you from every enemy, become Hidden. An explicit teleportation barrier can block either movement.

## Sanctuary of Steel
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: sanctuary:3, armor:bracing:4, guard:shelter:4
Main Action · 4 SP, wearing armor and holding a shield. Create a fixed sanctuary within 3 squares of your position until your next turn ends. You and up to four chosen allies inside it reduce attack damage by 4, minimum 1, and cannot gain Frightened or Dazed while inside. Existing conditions remain. Leaving ends a creature’s protection until it returns; the boundary blocks neither movement nor attacks. Recasting replaces your previous sanctuary.

## Colossus of the Forge
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: flame:3, imbuement:3, arms:bladework:4, craft:construction:4
Main Action · Knowledge · 6 SP, with a melee weapon. Form an incandescent shell around yourself through the end of your next turn, then immediately sweep up to four enemies within 3 squares for 4/7/10 physical damage and Burning 1, using one roll. While the shell lasts, gain Stability 3 and reduce attack damage by 3, minimum 1. On your next turn, you may spend a Main Action to repeat that sweep without additional SP. The shell cannot stack with another copy and creates no lasting material.

## The Road for the Lost
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: passage:3, seals:3, wilderness:tracking:4, scholarship:planning:4
10 minutes · 6 SP. Open a visible passage from your position to a landmark on the same plane within 100 squares that you personally visited and surveyed. Both endpoints must have safe, unoccupied footing. Up to twelve willing creatures you name may cross during the next minute; no other creature or attack can pass through it. You may close it early. It transports carried belongings but no unattended freight. Teleportation barriers block the working; it does not bypass an unknown destination or create safe ground.

## Dawn on the Battlefield
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: restoration:3, sanctuary:3, medicine:first-aid:4, influence:leadership:4
Main Action · Knowledge · 6 SP. Choose up to six living willing or helpless allies within 6 squares whom you can see. Roll once; each restores 6/10/14 HP and reduces Bleeding and Frightened by 2 each. A helpless ally need not respond to be included. This is magical healing and neither uses nor refreshes first-aid allowances. It cannot raise the dead, restore Grit or exceed maximum HP. Each recipient is affected once per use.

## Meteor Swarm
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: flame:3, telekinesis:3
Main Action · Knowledge · 6 SP. Choose up to three visible points within 12 squares. Roll once; every creature within 1 square of a chosen point takes 4/7/10 fire damage. A creature in overlapping areas takes damage once. Allies are not exempt. Unattended flammable objects ignite. This is one spell, not three separate castings.

## Wall of Thorns — Living Fortress
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: growth:3, shaping:3
Main Action · Reason · 5 SP. Raise up to eight connected thorn-wall squares on unoccupied ground within 8 squares. The walls last 10 minutes, are 2 squares high, block movement and provide cover. Name up to six creatures when casting; each may pass through at normal movement cost. Other creatures can spend a Main Action cutting through one adjacent square with a suitable edged tool, taking 3 physical damage, minimum 1 after resistance. Fire destroys an affected wall square after that attack resolves. No roll.

## True Resurrection
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: restoration:3, spirits:3
Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours · Reason · 8 SP, with remains of one person dead no longer than seven days and unique funerary materials worth 500 coins. The DA establishes availability before preparation. The spirit must freely agree. At completion, consume the materials and return that person to life at 1 HP in a restored body, clearing both death roll tallies but retaining descriptive scars. No roll. No duplicate body, restored Grit or SP is created. Each person has one lifetime return to life, shared with Lifegiving and Mass Lifegiving. This ritual restores a life ended before its natural span.

## Simulacrum
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: perception:3, animation:3
Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours · Knowledge · 8 SP, with a willing adjacent living model and sculpting materials worth 300 coins, consumed on completion. Create one likeness with 10 HP, no SP or Grit, your Speed, and the model’s appearance and voice. It lasts until destroyed or you create another. It cannot cast, use learned abilities or recover HP. On your turn, spend a Main Action to command it to move up to its Speed and perform an ordinary task or Basic Melee attack using your Knowledge and 2/3/5 damage. It has no independent actions or Reactions; you roll its defenses with your attributes. It cannot create a duplicate. Destroying it does not harm the model.

## Demiplane
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: passage:3, seals:3
Ritual · 1 hour · Knowledge · 6 SP. Open a doorway on an adjacent solid wall to your private 6-by-6-square refuge, 3 squares high. The doorway lasts 10 minutes and may be used by anyone who can reach it. You may close it with a Maneuver. A Maneuver inside opens an exit to the last entrance location; if obstructed, use the nearest unoccupied square within 3 squares. The refuge holds ordinary objects and up to twelve creatures, with air and normal time but no created food or water. You have one refuge; recasting reopens it from the new wall. It grants no travel to any other destination. No roll.

## Foresight — Shared Vigil
DP: 3 | Level: 7
Requires: thought:3, portents:3
Ritual · 10 minutes · Reason · 5 SP. Link yourself and up to three willing creatures within 3 squares for one hour. While within 12 squares of you, linked creatures cannot be surprised by a threat any linked member perceives. Once each round, after one linked creature rolls a defense, it may reroll the entire roll and keep either result. The entire group shares this one reroll. The link carries warnings, not unrestricted thoughts. Recasting replaces the previous link. No roll.

## Storm of Vengeance
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: air:3, growth:3, sanctuary:3
Main Action · Reason · 7 SP. Choose a visible point within 12 squares and a 1-square sanctuary within 2 squares of that point. For three rounds, a storm fills a 3-square radius around the point. When cast, roll once: creatures in the storm outside the sanctuary take 3/5/7 lightning damage. At the start of your next two turns, spend your Main Action and roll again to continue it, dealing the same damage and pushing each affected creature 1 square away from the center where legal. If you cannot or do not spend the action, the storm ends. Everyone in the sanctuary is exempt. Recasting ends the earlier storm.

## Mass Lifegiving
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: restoration:3, spirits:3, sanctuary:3
Ritual · 1 uninterrupted hour · Reason · 10 SP, within a safe prepared sanctuary. Choose up to three willing dead people whose bodies are present and who died within the previous hour. Consume 200 coins of consecrated materials for each; the DA establishes availability beforehand. Restore their fatal injuries, return them to life at 1 HP and clear both death roll tallies. No roll. Scars remain; SP and Grit are not restored. Each person has one lifetime return to life, shared with Lifegiving and True Resurrection.

## Foresight — Oracle’s Dream
DP: 4 | Level: 7
Requires: dreams:3, portents:3, spirits:3
Ritual · 1 hour of shared sleep · Reason · 6 SP. You and up to three willing nearby sleepers seek the help of a willing spirit for one named undertaking in the next 24 hours. The DA gives one truthful warning of a foreseeable obstacle and one usable clue about addressing it, subject to the spirit’s knowledge. Each dreamer may once reroll an entire roll directly addressing the undertaking and keep either result. Unused rerolls expire after 24 hours; a creature cannot hold a benefit from more than one Oracle’s Dream. No casting roll. This does not guarantee success or reveal unknowable future decisions.

## Gate — World Anchor
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: earth:3, passage:3, imbuement:3, seals:3
Ritual · 7 days, 8 hours of work per day · Knowledge · 10 SP on each workday, with prepared stone anchors at two known locations, each accessible during preparation, and 1,000 coins of materials consumed in total. The DA establishes availability and site permissions first. Create a permanent paired gateway, 2 squares wide, between those anchors, even across planes. At an anchor, a holder of either of the two keys you create can spend a Main Action and 3 SP to open both ends for 10 minutes, or a Maneuver to close them. Anyone can pass while open. A key conveys no control over travelers. Destroying either anchor ends the connection; each has 30 HP and physical resistance 3, and always takes at least 1 attack damage. No roll. You may maintain one pair; completing another ends the first.

## Guards and Wards — Living Citadel
DP: 5 | Level: 7
Requires: growth:3, shaping:3, earth:3, sanctuary:3
Ritual · 3 days, 8 hours of work per day · Reason · 10 SP each day, with sound stone, fertile soil, water and 600 coins of materials consumed. On a prepared 12-by-12-square site, raise a one-story living keep with six rooms, a courtyard, a gate and walls 3 squares high. No roll. Walls block movement and sight; each 1-square section has 20 HP and physical resistance 3, with minimum 1 attack damage. You designate up to twelve residents. At dawn, paying 4 SP renews its wards for 24 hours: residents within gain 1 Boon to defenses, and you sense the location of any breached wall while inside. Missing upkeep removes the wards, not the building. The keep needs ordinary food, water and repairs. You may ward only one citadel at a time.
