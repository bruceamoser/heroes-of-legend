# Classes

Choose a class whose attribute minimums you meet. It supplies one starting discipline, HP and SP contributions, the attribute you use to cast spells, prices for further disciplines, and signature choices at levels 1, 3 and 7. At each milestone, choose one of your class’s signatures for that level. Each has its own rules, including any extra benefit from another signature you know.
