# Who do you want to be?

Start with the character you picture. Choose what they do for the group, then use
the linked abilities to build toward it. These paths belong to no single HOL class.
A player can combine a warrior’s mind, a beast’s fury and a healer’s compassion.

These are fresh proposals, not approved subclasses or mandatory shopping lists.
D&D families are a coverage lens; [research notes](references.md) distinguish direct
reading from broader fantasy additions.

## Mix the roles

The Beast Within is a frontline hunter; Green Healer restores allies; Root Keeper
controls access and space. All can look like a druid, and one character can grow
across them. Thinking Fighter, Daring Scoundrel and Still-Hand Adept put Knowledge,
Guile and Reason directly on distinct combat cards. They do not turn every roll
into the character’s best attribute.

## Coverage boundary

The twelve core D&D class families inform this pass, with contrasting subclass
spirits and additional familiar fantasies. This is not a reproduction of every
subclass ever published. Mounted knights, ocean-depth campaigns and permanent
pet advancement need further dedicated rules; these paths do not quietly settle them.
