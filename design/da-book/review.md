# First manuscript review — October 16, 2026

## What is ready

- Ten chapter introductions/narratives, with the full creature chapter assembled
  from the 192 approved authored cards. Chapters 1–9 are substantive new guidance.
- A complete adaptable opening adventure, The Bell at the Crossing, including
  connected locations, two optional combat rosters, clues, NPCs and rewards.
- Five example encounters with the existing CV arithmetic, same-level and mixed-level
  budget examples, and explicit uncertainty about actual difficulty.
- Four attack/control hazard entries and one progress obstacle, with signs,
  triggers, timing, full outcomes and counterplay.
- Four appendices, with all 93 monster abilities, 73 summon profiles, condition
  reference and 280 wound results supplied from existing sources.
- Individual indexes by name, CV, family, every listed habitat, and role.
- CV worksheets and creature-roll authoring references collected at the end,
  outside table-facing creature entries.

## Review boundaries

The new writing is a first manuscript draft, not a released book. Bruce's approval
of previous rules does not imply approval of the new adventure's reward values,
hazard numbers or every editorial example. Those new values are visibly labeled.
No established creature statistics or player abilities were changed for this draft.

CV remains uncalibrated. No new balance simulations were performed. This manuscript
supports preparing and recording playtests; it does not certify their results.
