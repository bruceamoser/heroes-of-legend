# Monster Ability Reference

These **126 monster-only abilities** are tools for preparing and adapting creatures.
They are separate from learned player abilities, with no DP price or permission
to purchase them. A creature gains only the abilities printed in its own entry.

Templates may describe attacker outcomes for construction. During play, use the
creature's complete defense-outcome table or the appropriate summoned player
profile. Per-creature values and restrictions govern where they specialize a template.

Entries follow in alphabetical order. Their mechanical text comes directly from
the authored monster catalog. The assembled reference omits editorial status lines
and shared navigation links; it retains every specific action, effect and limit.
