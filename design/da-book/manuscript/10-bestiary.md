# Bestiary

Choose a creature for what it brings to the adventure: a dangerous appetite, a
boundary it will not abandon, a strange bargain, or an ally whose needs are unlike
your own. The same statistics can serve different individuals and places. Change
their names and relationships freely while keeping their printed capabilities clear.

This chapter contains all **192 creatures** in the current catalog. Families appear
alphabetically, with variants together. An individual name index provides a direct
route when you already know what you need. CV, habitat and role indexes help when
you are searching for a suitable opponent or inhabitant.

Every attack table uses **Weak / Standard / Strong player defense** outcomes.
Read the applicable damage and effects together. Details supply targets, ranges,
durations and limits. A separate player profile governs an eligible summoned form.

Family membership is a filing aid, not a rule package. Use only the card's printed
immunities, senses, actions and permissions. An undead name does not silently grant
every undead immunity from another game.
