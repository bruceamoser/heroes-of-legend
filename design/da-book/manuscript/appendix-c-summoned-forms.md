# Summoned Form Reference

Find the category named by the summoning spell, then choose a profile at or below
the rating it allows. Follow the spell’s placement and duration. Give the chosen
profile to the player who controls the summon.

Each profile prints the form's attributes, permitted actions and attacker outcomes.
The controlling player handles its rolls. Command the form using its listed actions. A summon acts only through commands
and has no independent Reactions or opportunity attacks. The initial free Main command counts as its one command for that turn.

Summoned creatures require constant interaction and concentration to maintain
their presence in reality. At the end of the controller's turn, each summon that
received no command during that turn disappears. The initial free command counts
for that turn.

A form cannot be healed. At 0 HP, beyond its six-square tether, or when its controller
becomes unconscious or dies, it disappears. Its energy nature grants no unprinted
immunity or incorporeality. Existing conditions and durations still resolve on its
controller's turn even when no command is issued.

The category and tier index precedes the profiles. When a summoned healer acts,
its controller pays the printed SP cost once, before resolution, and must have enough SP; use the form's own attribute and outcomes.
