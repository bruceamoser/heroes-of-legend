# Summoned Form Reference

Use these **73 player profiles** when a summoning card permits the listed category
and tier. A familiar name, a low CV or membership in a creature family does not
provide eligibility by itself. Follow the summoning card's placement and duration.

Each profile prints the form's attributes, permitted actions and attacker outcomes.
The controlling player handles its rolls. These profiles differ deliberately from
the DA creature cards: a summon has no independent activity, Reactions or opportunity
attacks. The initial free Main command counts as its one command for that turn.

A form cannot be healed. At 0 HP, beyond its six-square tether, or when its controller
becomes unconscious or dies, it disappears. Its energy nature grants no unprinted
immunity or incorporeality. Existing conditions and durations still resolve on its
controller's turn even when no command is issued.

The category and tier index precedes the profiles. When a summoned healer acts,
its controller pays the printed HP cost once, before resolution, bypassing resistance
and remaining above 0; use the form's own attribute and outcomes.
