# Last Light of the Watch

*You thrust a shining palm between your companion and the blow; for an instant their outline burns brighter than the danger.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible willing ally within 3 squares, before a defense roll.

Grant 2 Boons to that defense. On a Strong defense, reduce the target’s temporary Blinded by 1 after the attack resolves. Starred blindness remains. Use the existing roll; this does not create a defense against an automatic effect.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 3

**Type:** Magic / Defense / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** last-light-of-the-watch
