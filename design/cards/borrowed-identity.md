# Borrowed Identity

- **ID:** borrowed-identity
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 7
- **DP:** 8
- **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 3
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Guile
- **Target / range / shape:** People evaluating a prepared mundane disguise; 10 minutes preparation
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward a plausible impersonation objective. | 2 progress. | 3 progress; learn one immediate suspicion held by the audience. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. Requires plausible clothing and evidence. Does not imitate memories or override another player’s choices.
