# Borrowed Identity

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** People evaluating a prepared mundane disguise; 10 minutes preparation

Requires plausible clothing and evidence. Does not imitate memories or override another player’s choices.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward a plausible impersonation objective.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; learn one immediate suspicion held by the audience.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 3

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** borrowed-identity · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
