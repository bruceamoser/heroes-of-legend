# Take Them Alive

*Choose a creature reduced to 0 HP by your melee attack. Before making a melee attack, declare that you intend to capture the target. If it reduces an NPC to 0 HP, the NPC becomes Unconscious until the scene ends or it receives healing. This finishing blow leaves it alive and inflicts no wound.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** A creature reduced to 0 HP by your melee attack
