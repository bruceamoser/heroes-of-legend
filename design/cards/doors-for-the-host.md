# Doors for the Host

*You open two small doorways with a sweep of your arms, pouring your summoned shapes through them like colored smoke.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 2 visible summons of yours within 6 squares.

Teleport each chosen summon up to 4 squares to a visible free supported space within 6 squares of you. Each footprint must fit; destinations cannot overlap. HP, conditions, durations and command history remain unchanged. This grants neither commands nor activity.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 3

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** doors-for-the-host
