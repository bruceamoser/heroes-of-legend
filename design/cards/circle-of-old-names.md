# Circle of Old Names

*You speak the names that once meant safety, and hear your companions breathe more steadily.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and willing allies within 2 squares.

Call the names of those who kept you safe. Until the start of your next turn, these allies gain 1 Boon to defense against forced movement and may reduce temporary Frightened by 1 immediately.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2 and Guard 1

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use. No earlier path card is required; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
