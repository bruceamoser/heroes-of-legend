# Circle of Old Names

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and willing allies within 2 squares.

Call the names of those who kept you safe. Until your next turn, these allies gain 1 Boon to defense against forced movement and may reduce temporary Frightened by 1 immediately.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
