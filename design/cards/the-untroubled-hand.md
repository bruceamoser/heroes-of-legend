# The Untroubled Hand

*Choose one adjacent enemy. Make a Reason unarmed attack. On a Standard result, deal 3 physical damage. Push 1 square away from you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Reason unarmed attack.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage; push 1 square away from you.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage; push 2 squares away from you.
