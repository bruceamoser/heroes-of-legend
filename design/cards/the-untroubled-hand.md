# The Untroubled Hand

*A quiet breath, an open hand, and all their strength goes past you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature.

Strike with an empty hand, foot or shoulder. You read balance, not anatomy alone.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage and push 1 square.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage and push 2 squares.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
