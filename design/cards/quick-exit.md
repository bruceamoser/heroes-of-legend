# Quick Exit

*When you use disengage, gain 1 additional square of available movement this turn. Spend it using normal terrain costs and movement restrictions. The bonus applies to the Disengage movement. Your Speed and converted movement allowances stay unchanged. Disengage's protection from opportunity attacks applies normally.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when you use Disengage.

Gain 1 additional square of available movement this turn. Spend it using normal terrain costs and movement restrictions. The bonus applies to the Disengage movement; your Speed and converted movement allowances stay unchanged. Disengage's protection from opportunity attacks applies normally.

**Duration:** Until the end of this turn; unused movement expires.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Tier / minimum level:** 0 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Movement

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once; this benefit can trigger only once per turn.
