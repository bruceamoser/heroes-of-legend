# Road of Rime

*You sweep your hand along the ground. A broad ribbon of packed snow covers the sucking mud and jagged rubble.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 8 connected ground squares within 6 squares.

The chosen squares cost 1 movement to enter for everyone, replacing difficult terrain. They need existing support and do not cross gaps or suppress hazards or damaging terrain. You may then move up to 3 squares along the ribbon, following normal movement and opportunity rules.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Frost 3

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** road-of-rime
