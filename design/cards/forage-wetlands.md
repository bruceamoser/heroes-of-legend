# Forage Wetlands

*You kneel beside the reeds, finding nourishment where others see only mud.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** A viable marsh or riverside; one hour

Find usable food or plant materials. Increase one bundle 1/2/3 inventory steps, maximum d8. No usable resources means no attempt.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Once per wound per day for treatment; otherwise repeatable within the stated interval.

**ID:** forage-wetlands · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
