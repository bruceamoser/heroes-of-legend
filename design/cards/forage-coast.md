# Forage Coast

*Choose one viable shore. Choose one food or ordinary material supply for which the area contains usable resources. Make a Knowledge roll and raise that supply by 1/2/3 inventory steps on Weak/Standard/Strong, up to d8. The first step from out creates d4. The DA identifies which resources the patch can actually provide and when it is exhausted.*
