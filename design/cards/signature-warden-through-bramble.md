# Through Bramble

*Your paired weapons move like branches in a sudden wind.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One or two creatures within melee reach.

Make one attack with each of two light melee weapons, one in each hand. Each attack uses your normal attack procedure and deals 1/2/3 physical damage on Weak/Standard/Strong. You can target the same creature with both attacks or one creature with each. After the attacks, gain 1 Boon to defense until the start of your next turn.

Once on your turn, after resolving both attacks, you may step 1 square to a legal empty square without spending movement or provoking opportunity attacks. Use ordinary terrain and movement restrictions for that step.

**Duration:** Attacks and step immediate; defense Boon until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Two light melee weapons, one in each hand.
