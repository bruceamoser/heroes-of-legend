# The Wind Makes Room

*A low gust lifts your companions' steps over the rubble, giving each a little more room to choose the next move.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three visible willing creatures within 4 squares, including yourself.

Each recipient may move up to 2 squares without provoking. Difficult terrain costs 1 movement per square for this move.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 3 and Guard 1

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**ID:** the-wind-makes-room
