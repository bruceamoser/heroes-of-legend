# The Wind Makes Room

*You spread your arms and send a low gust sweeping around your companions’ legs, lifting their steps over the rubble.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three willing allies visible within 4 squares.

Each chosen ally may immediately move up to 2 squares along a legal route, one at a time, without provoking. Difficult terrain costs 1 for this movement. They must be able to move and end in supported free squares; the gust does not carry them over gaps, creatures or barriers. They spend no actions.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 3 and Guard 1

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Each recipient moves once per use. A further use requires another Maneuver.

**ID:** the-wind-makes-room
