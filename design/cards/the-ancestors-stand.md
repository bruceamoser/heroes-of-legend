# Call Spirit: Master

*You speak the names in their proper order. The air grows still, and the oldest voice answers first.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Spirit entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 7 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 3

**Type:** Magic / Summoning
