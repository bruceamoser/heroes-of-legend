# The Ancestors Stand

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Three visible free squares within 3 squares.

Call three ancestor shades, each HP 1, Speed 4, no resistance and no attack. They occupy squares and can Move or Defend when commanded. Apply normal commands and tether; only one receives the immediate free Main command. They last until combat ends or for 10 minutes. They are summons, not an unbreakable wall.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Type:** Magic
