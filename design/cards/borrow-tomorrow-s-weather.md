# Borrow Tomorrow’s Weather

*Prepare a disguise in ten minutes, learn a detail that sells the role and gain 1 Boon to Guile rolls to pass as that role.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Visible unoccupied ground squares within 4 squares, joined along their edges.

Choose smoke or ice and make a Guile roll. Place one connected area in up to 3/5/7 squares on Weak/Standard/Strong. Smoke supplies heavy cover; ice supplies difficult terrain. The area lasts until the end of your next turn. On later turns, you may spend a Maneuver to sustain it until the end of your next turn.
