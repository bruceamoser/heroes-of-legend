# Borrow Tomorrow’s Weather

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** A visible free square within 4 squares.

The lowest retained die chooses the weather: odd makes smoke giving heavy cover across that square; even makes ice there difficult terrain. No damaging entry effect.

- **Weak:** Lasts until your next turn.
- **Standard:** Lasts until the end of your next turn.
- **Strong:** Lasts until the end of your next turn; place a second identical effect in an adjacent free square.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Type:** Magic
