# Basic Unarmed

*You close the distance with fist, heel or shoulder, putting your whole body behind the blow.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature

Choose your target and make the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 2 physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 physical damage.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** No equipment required or granted.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Attack

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.

**ID:** basic-unarmed · **Status:** Agreed universal permission; damage and ranged distances are proposals.
