# Basic Unarmed

*Strike an adjacent creature with your bare hands or a kick. Roll Brawn for 1/2/4 physical damage.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature

Choose your target and make the attack.

- **Weak:** 1 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 2 physical damage.
- **Strong:** 4 physical damage.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** No equipment required or granted.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** basic-unarmed
