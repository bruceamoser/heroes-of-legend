# Stitch the Living Pattern

*Blue threads gather above the wound. As they draw together, a matching ache closes around your own hand.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One visible willing creature within 3 squares, including yourself, or one creature within that range harmed by healing.

Pay 1 HP and make a Knowledge healing roll.

- **Weak:** Restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 5 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 7 HP; a recipient that regains HP gains 1 Boon to its next spell attribute roll before the end of its next turn.
