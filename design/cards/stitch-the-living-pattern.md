# Stitch the Living Pattern

*You trace fine lines of blue light above the injury, drawing the broken pattern together as a matching ache runs through your own hand.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One visible willing creature within 3 squares, including self.

Pay 1 HP before rolling, ignoring resistance; you must remain above 0 HP. Requires a free hand. Life resistance reduces HP restored, to a minimum of 1 where HP is missing, capped by missing HP. A willing creature harmed by healing instead takes the printed HP amount as Life damage, with normal resistance and minimum damage, and gains no beneficial rider. This card does not permit targeting an unwilling creature. No resurrection, wound removal or Grit restoration.

- **Weak:** Restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 5 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 7 HP. If HP was restored, the recipient also gains 1 Boon to its next spell roll before the end of its next turn.

The Boon modifies an ordinary spell roll, not the spell’s HP cost or an inventory check. Repeated applications replace the unused benefit rather than multiply it.

**Duration:** Healing is immediate; the Strong Boon ends when used or at the end of the recipient’s next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
