# Rouse the Senses

*Choose one adjacent willing creature. Make a Reason roll. Weak — Reduce temporary Dazed by 1. Standard — Reduce temporary Dazed by 2. Strong — Reduce temporary Dazed by 3.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing creature.

Make a Reason roll.

- **Weak:** Reduce temporary Dazed by 1.
- **Standard:** Reduce temporary Dazed by 2.
- **Strong:** Reduce temporary Dazed by 3.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 1

**Type:** Skill / Recovery
