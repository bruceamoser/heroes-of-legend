# Sustain the Failing

*You hold out your hand. Warmth gathers in your companion's wounds while your own knees threaten to give.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 3 squares, including yourself, or one creature in range harmed by healing.

Pay 3 HP and make a Reason healing roll.

- **Weak:** Restore 5 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 10 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 15 HP and reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 2

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**ID:** sustain-the-failing
