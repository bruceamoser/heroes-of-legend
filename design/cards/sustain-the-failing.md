# Sustain the Failing

*You kneel beside your companion and hold out your hand. Strength passes into their injured body, leaving you pale and breathing hard.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 3 squares, including self

Moser’s law of conservation of Life energy: pay 3 HP before rolling; bypass resistance and remain above 0 HP. Requires a free hand. Life resistance reduces restoration, minimum 1 where HP is missing. Healing-harmed creatures take the rolled result as Life damage. No resurrection or removal of wounds/Grit loss.

- **Weak:** Restore 5 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 10 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 15 HP and reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 2

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** sustain-the-failing · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.

[Card conventions](../card-conventions.md) · [Catalog](../catalog.md)

**Offensive healing — latest agreement:** this spell may target an unwilling
creature harmed by healing. Resolve it as an attack using its printed attribute,
range, action and HP cost; the HP result becomes Life damage, without beneficial
recovery riders. This is the explicit exception to willing-only targeting above.
Use the creature’s printed defense when a player defends; no opposed second roll.
