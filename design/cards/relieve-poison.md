# Relieve Poison

*Self or one adjacent willing creature. Make a Knowledge roll. Weak — Reduce temporary Poisoned by 1. Standard — Reduce temporary Poisoned by 2. Strong — Reduce temporary Poisoned by 3.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent willing creature.

Make a Knowledge roll.

- **Weak:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.
- **Standard:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 2.
- **Strong:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 3.
