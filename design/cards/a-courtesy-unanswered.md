# A Courtesy Unanswered

*Make a Guile melee attack that can Slow your target. Gain 1 Boon if you started Hidden.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Guile melee attack. If you began Hidden, gain 1 Boon to the attack.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage and Slowed 1.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage and Slowed 2.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary melee weapon (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.
