# Braced Harness

*You lean into your harness and let every strap take its part of the blow.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** Self while armored, before Stability is applied to forced movement against you.

Make a Fortitude roll.

- **Weak:** Gain Stability 1 against the forced movement.
- **Standard:** Gain Stability 2 against it.
- **Strong:** Gain Stability 3 against it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Worn armor.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 1
