# Dread Reflection

*You spread your arms, and your outline twists into something your enemy dreads. They recoil as the vision presses against their thoughts.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 5 squares

Proposed NPC defense attribute: Reason.

- **Weak:** 3 mental damage.
- **Standard:** 3 mental damage; Frightened 1 of you.
- **Strong:** 5 mental damage; Frightened 2 of you.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Type:** Magic / Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
