# Arrive under Guard

*You hurry to your companion’s side with your guard raised, catching their eye as you settle between them and danger.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one visible willing ally within 2 squares.

Move up to 2 squares toward the ally along a legal route. This movement provokes normally. If you finish adjacent, the ally gains 1 Boon to its next defense before the start of your next turn, provided it is still adjacent to you and you can act when that defense begins. The ally spends no action.

**Duration:** Movement is immediate; defense benefit ends when used or at the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2 and Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Defense / Movement

**Limits:** A recipient can hold only one unused defense benefit from this card.

**ID:** arrive-under-guard
