# Arrive under Guard

*You hurry to your companion's side and settle between them and the threat, close enough that each can read the other's next movement.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one visible willing ally within 3 squares.

Move up to 3 squares toward the ally without provoking. If you end adjacent, you and the ally gain 1 Boon to defenses until the end of your next turn while you remain adjacent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2 and Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Defense / Movement

**ID:** arrive-under-guard
