# Diagnose the Failure

*You recognize the useful detail and show your companions how to act on it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** An ally within 5 squares that rolled Weak on an attack or objective attempt, before resolving it.

Once per scene, communicate an applicable correction the ally can perceive and understand. The ally rerolls with 1 additional Boon and uses the new result, including its natural dice. Resolve damage, conditions or progress only after that replacement roll.

**Duration:** The replacement roll.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** Class choice · **Requirements to learn:** Class Intellect; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Signature

**Limits:** One signature choice at each of levels 1, 3 and 7.
