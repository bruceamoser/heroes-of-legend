# Diagnose the Failure

*You stop the turning wheel with a tool, listen to the last click and show your companions exactly where the mechanism went wrong.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Time:** One minute.

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible ordinary mechanism, damaged or functioning.

Examine it for one minute. Learn its purpose, its working parts and a specific safe way to disable or repair it, if one exists. You can perform a simple accessible adjustment with suitable tools during that minute; a job requiring materials, dismantling or crafting still needs its usual permission and work. For such a job, you or one ally gains 2 Boons to the next appropriate roll following your diagnosis. This does not reveal a magical mechanism or a recipe.

**Duration:** Information persists; the Boons last until used or the mechanism changes.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Suitable tools required for adjustments; grants craft tools, Bulk 1, if absent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Intellect; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** One Boon grant per mechanism until it materially changes.
