# Diagnose the Failure

*Choose one ally within 5 squares that can hear and understand you, after a Weak result on that ally’s attack or skill roll. Diagnose the mistake and show the ally the correction. The ally may reroll the roll with 1 Boon. The reroll is part of your Reaction. The ally keeps their own Reaction.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One ally within 5 squares that can hear and understand you, after a Weak result on that ally’s attack or skill roll.

Diagnose the mistake and show the ally the correction. The ally may reroll the roll with 1 Boon. The reroll is part of your Reaction; the ally keeps their own Reaction.
