# Call Elemental: Master

*Summon an Animal of Master tier or lower within 3 squares for one scene.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Elemental entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 7 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 3

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** call-a-tempest-beast
