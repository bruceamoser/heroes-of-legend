# Force

*You set your shoulder, plant your feet and make the stubborn thing give ground.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** An adjacent obstacle, grapple, or creature

Choose the intended result before rolling: reduce an obstacle’s remaining strength, reduce your Grappled rating, or push a creature. DA establishes any obstacle target. Grappled 3 leaves no Main Action to spend.

- **Weak:** 1 progress, grapple reduction, or square of push.
- **Standard:** 2 progress, grapple reduction, or squares of push.
- **Strong:** 3 progress, grapple reduction, or squares of push.

**Stability:** apply it only to the push option, not obstacle progress or Grappled reduction.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** force
