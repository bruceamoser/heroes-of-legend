# Force

*Put your weight into an obstacle, wrench free of a grapple, or shove a creature back.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** An adjacent obstacle, grapple, or creature

Before rolling, choose to reduce an obstacle’s remaining strength, reduce your own Grappled rating, or push a creature. The DA establishes any obstacle target. At Grappled 3 or higher, escape requires release by a grappler or an outside intervention that breaks the hold.

- **Weak:** Add 1 progress, reduce Grappled by 1, or push 1 square.
- **Standard:** Add 2 progress, reduce Grappled by 2, or push 2 squares.
- **Strong:** Add 3 progress, reduce Grappled by 3, or push 3 squares.

**Stability:** apply it only to the push option, not obstacle progress or Grappled reduction.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** force
