# Force

- **ID:** force
- **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
- **Type:** Universal
- **Tier / minimum level:** Universal
- **DP:** Not purchased
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Brawn
- **Target / range / shape:** An adjacent obstacle, grapple, or creature
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress, grapple reduction, or square of push. | 2 progress, grapple reduction, or squares of push. | 3 progress, grapple reduction, or squares of push. |

Choose the intended result before rolling: reduce an obstacle’s remaining strength, reduce your Grappled rating, or push a creature. DA establishes any obstacle target. Grappled 3 leaves no Main Action to spend.
