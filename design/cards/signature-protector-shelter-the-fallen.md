# Over My Body

*Choose one ally within 3 squares. When a single-target attack against an ally within 3 squares would bring them to 0 HP, or would damage an ally at 0 HP with two failure marks, move up to 2 squares to a square adjacent to that ally and take the damage instead. If that damage would bring you to 0 HP, stay at 1 HP.*
