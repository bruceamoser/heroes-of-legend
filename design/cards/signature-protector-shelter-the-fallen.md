# Shelter the Fallen

*You steady the fallen, then draw them away from the place where the blows are landing.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** The trigger and recipients are specified in the rule below.

Stabilize one adjacent Dying ally, then move that ally into a free adjacent square. No HP restoration; no opportunity from this forced movement.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Protector; level 3 only

**Type:** Signature

**Limits:** Normal action economy; any additional limit is printed above.

**ID:** signature-protector-shelter-the-fallen · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
