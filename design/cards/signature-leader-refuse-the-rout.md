# Refuse the Rout

*You give frightened companions a place to stand together.*

## Rules

### Stand Together

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one adjacent willing ally.

You and the ally gain 1 Boon to defense rolls while remaining adjacent, until the start of your next turn. Once per scene, before either recipient rolls one of these defenses, you may increase that defense’s benefit to 2 Boons, without an additional action.

**Duration:** Until your next turn or adjacency is lost.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

### Refuse the Rout

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and up to three willing allies within 4 squares that can hear you.

Each recipient reduces one Frightened or Dazed condition by up to 2, to a minimum of 0, and gains 1 Boon to its next defense before the start of your next turn. Each may immediately move 1 square without provoking. Resolve movement in an order you choose. This applies to ordinary conditions.

**Duration:** Condition reduction and movement are immediate; Boons expire when used or the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Use once per scene. A starred condition requires its own stated remedy.

### If you learned Blow the Horn at level 3

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Every enemy within 5 squares that can hear you.

Make one Guile roll and apply the result to each target. On a Weak result, apply Frightened 1. On a Standard result, apply Frightened 1 and Dazed 1. On a Strong result, apply Frightened 2 and Dazed 1.

**Duration:** Conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Use once per scene. Roll using the least favorable applicable Boons/Banes. Apply hearing requirements and condition immunities normally.
