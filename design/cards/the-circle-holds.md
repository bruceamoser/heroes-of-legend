# The Circle Holds

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and allies within 2 squares.

Until your next turn, each gains 1 Boon to defense against spells. The first forced movement against each is reduced by 2 squares. The ward is not physical cover and does not stack with another same-kind movement reduction.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 3

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
