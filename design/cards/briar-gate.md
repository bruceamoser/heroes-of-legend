# Briar Gate

*Create a bridge across a gap up to 3 squares wide for ten minutes.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Visible unoccupied ground squares within 4 squares, joined along their edges.

Make a Reason roll and grow a connected hedge in the indicated number of squares.

- **Weak:** Up to 3 squares.
- **Standard:** Up to 5 squares.
- **Strong:** Up to 7 squares.

The hedge supplies difficult terrain and heavy cover across it until the end of your next turn. On later turns, you may spend a Maneuver to sustain it until the end of your next turn.
