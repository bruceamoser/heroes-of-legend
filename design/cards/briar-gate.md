# Briar Gate

*You sweep your hand across the ground, calling a tangle of thorns into the opening.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 3/5/7 connected visible unoccupied ground squares, each within 4 squares.

Roll Reason, then raise a waist-high hedge in up to the indicated number of squares. Place one connected area, with squares joined along their edges.

- **Weak:** 3 squares.
- **Standard:** 5 squares.
- **Strong:** 7 squares.

The hedge makes those squares difficult terrain and gives heavy cover across them until the end of your next turn. It remains passable and deals no damage.

**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 2

**Type:** Magic / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
