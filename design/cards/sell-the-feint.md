# Sell the Feint

*You show an opening with your shoulder and flick your weapon toward it, drawing their attention away from your real stroke.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature that can see you.

Gain 1 Boon to your next melee attack against the chosen creature this turn. The target need not move, spend a Reaction or accept a particular response. This grants no attack.

**Duration:** Until used or the end of this turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a melee weapon; grants none.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Arms 1 and Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Limits:** Repeated uses do not multiply this card’s benefit on the same recipient or prepared attack. Pay a Maneuver for each use.

**ID:** sell-the-feint
