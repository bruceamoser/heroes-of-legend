# Derive a Mechanism

- **ID:** derive-a-mechanism
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Craft 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One accessible ordinary mechanical item; four hours
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 derivation progress. | 2 derivation progress. | 3 derivation progress. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. Proposal: target 12 for an ordinary mechanism. Requires tools and access to the item at every attempt. Creates a recipe, not the item or permission to craft it. Special items excluded.
