# Coordinated Rush

*You signal the rush and watch two companions strike together, each arriving where the other needs them.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Two willing allies within 4 squares that can perceive your signal.

Each ally may move up to 3 squares without provoking, then make one free Basic Melee attack against an adjacent enemy if able. Resolve one ally completely before the other. Each attack uses that ally’s Brawn and Basic Melee outcomes; on Strong it may push its target 1 square. Weapons are required. These attacks trigger no further free attacks.

**Duration:** Immediate movement and attacks.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Leader; minimum level 7
