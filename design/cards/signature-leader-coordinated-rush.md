# Coordinated Rush

*You give the signal, trusting your companions to finish what you have begun together.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Two willing allies within 4 squares that can perceive your signal.

Two willing allies within 4 squares each move 2 squares without provoking, then each makes a free Basic Melee attack if a target is adjacent. No chains; no specialty attacks.

Each granted Basic Melee attack uses its attacker’s Brawn and Basic Melee outcomes; this card adds no separate roll beyond any roll it explicitly requires.

**Duration:** Instant. Applied conditions follow their normal duration and decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Leader; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Limits:** Normal action economy; any additional limit is printed above.

**ID:** signature-leader-coordinated-rush · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
