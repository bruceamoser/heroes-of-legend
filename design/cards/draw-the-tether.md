# Draw the Tether

*You wind the shining thread around your wrist and step into its pull. The shape at the other end sharpens as you draw closer.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one of your visible summons within 6 squares.

Move up to 3 squares toward your summon without provoking. It gains 1 Boon to its next attack this turn when you command it.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1
