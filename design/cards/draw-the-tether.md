# Draw the Tether

*You gather the shining tether around your wrist and step toward your summoned companion, drawing its outline into sharper focus.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one of your visible summons within 6 squares.

Move up to 2 squares toward the summon, following normal movement and opportunity rules. The summon gains 1 Boon to its next attack this turn if you subsequently command it. This does not command it, move it or grant an attack.

**Duration:** Until used or the end of this turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Repeated uses do not multiply this card’s benefit on the same summon.

**ID:** draw-the-tether
