# Furnace Sweep

*Every creature in a 3-wide, 2-deep rectangle immediately ahead of you. Make a Knowledge area spell attack. On a Standard result, deal 3 fire damage. Push 1 square away from you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Every creature in a 3-wide, 2-deep rectangle immediately ahead of you.

Make a Knowledge area spell attack.

- **Weak:** 3 fire damage.
- **Standard:** 3 fire damage; push 1 square away from you.
- **Strong:** 5 fire damage and Burning 1; push 2 squares away from you.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Flame 2

**Type:** Magic / Attack / Control

**ID:** furnace-sweep
