# Field Triage

*Choose up to three adjacent willing or unconscious creatures, including yourself. For each target, choose temporary Bleeding, Burning, Freezing or Poisoned and reduce it by 2. You may choose a different condition for each.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three adjacent willing or unconscious creatures, including yourself.

For each target, choose temporary Bleeding, Burning, Freezing or Poisoned and reduce it by 2. You may choose a different condition for each.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand and suitable first-aid materials, such as cloth and water.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 3

**Type:** Skill / Recovery

**ID:** field-triage
