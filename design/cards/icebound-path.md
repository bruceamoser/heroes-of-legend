# Icebound Path

*You lay winter across the approach. Frost catches each hurried step while your companions choose the route around it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Visible unoccupied ground squares within 5 squares, joined along their edges.

Make a Knowledge roll and cover up to 3/5/7 connected squares on Weak/Standard/Strong with ice. The area is difficult terrain until the end of your next turn. The first time an enemy enters one of its squares during a turn, it gains Slowed 1. On later turns, you may spend a Maneuver to sustain the area until the end of your next turn.
