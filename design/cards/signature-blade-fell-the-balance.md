# Not So Fast

*Your blade finds room in the press of bodies.*

## Rules

### Cleave

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** All creatures adjacent to you, including allies. You must be wielding a melee weapon.

Make one Brawn roll using the least favorable net Boons/Banes among the targets. On a Weak result, deal 1 physical damage to each creature adjacent to you. On a Standard result, deal 1 physical damage and push each creature 1 square directly away from you. On a Strong result, deal 1 physical damage and push each creature 2 squares directly away from you. Resolve collisions normally.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Frequency:** Once on your turn.

### Not So Fast

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** An enemy voluntarily entering your melee reach from outside it.

Make one Basic Melee attack with your melee weapon. On Standard or Strong, its movement ends and it cannot move again until the start of its next turn. On Weak, resolve the attack’s damage normally and the creature continues moving. This uses your ordinary Reaction.

**Duration:** Attack immediate; movement restriction until the target’s next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ready melee weapon.
