# Take the Measure

*Choose one visible creature within your ranged attack's range. While you hold your position, your next ranged attack against the target this turn gains 1 Boon.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within your ranged attack's range.

While you hold your position, your next ranged attack against the target this turn gains 1 Boon.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ranged weapon.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Marksmanship 1
