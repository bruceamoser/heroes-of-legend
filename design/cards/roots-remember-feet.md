# Roots Remember Feet

*The ground has been listening. Now it answers.*

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature standing on ground within 4 squares.

Roots spring from cracks or soil to catch its feet. They wither immediately after the effect; you are not maintaining a grapple.

- **Weak:** Slowed 1.
- **Standard:** Slowed 2 and pull 1 square toward you.
- **Strong:** Slowed 2 and knock prone.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 1

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
