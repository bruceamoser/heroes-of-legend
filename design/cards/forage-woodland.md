# Forage Woodland

- **ID:** forage-woodland
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Knowledge
- **Target / range / shape:** A viable forest patch; one hour
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Raise one eligible supply by 1 step. | Raise it by 2 steps. | Raise it by 3 steps. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. Choose food or ordinary plant crafting material; one bundle only, maximum d8. Start from out if needed. DA establishes whether the patch has usable resources; no repeat harvesting of an exhausted patch.
