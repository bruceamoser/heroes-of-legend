# Trail Workshop

- **ID:** trail-workshop
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Craft 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Knowledge
- **Target / range / shape:** One known compatible ordinary recipe; recipe interval
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress or inventory step. | 2 progress or inventory steps. | 3 progress or inventory steps. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. Requires a known ordinary recipe. Grants craft tools if absent and two compatible ordinary recipe choices. Recipe defines materials, time and completion; no free materials.
