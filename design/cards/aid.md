# Aid

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent ally

Plausibly assist a declared task. Proposal: the ally gains 1 Boon to that task’s next roll before your next turn. Outside combat, contribute Aid or your own group roll, not both.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** aid · **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
