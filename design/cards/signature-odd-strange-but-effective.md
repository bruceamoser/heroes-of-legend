# Borrowed Life

*The spirits lend you words, perspective and remembered lives.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

**Magic:** Spell · **SP:** 0

Once between safe overnight rests, invite a spirit’s expertise for the scene. Choose one named tool practice or one specific noncombat objective approach, such as interpreting old boundary stones. Gain 3 Boons to rolls using that expertise. The spirit lends mannerisms and speech while you remain in control. Use your own equipment and learned capabilities; the expertise is not weapon training or a spell repertoire.

**Duration:** Until scene end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.
