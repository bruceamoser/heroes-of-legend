# Create a Distraction

*Choose one creature within 3 squares that sees or hears your distraction. Make a Guile roll. Choose an ally within 3 squares who sees or hears your cue. Its next attack against the target or roll moving past it unnoticed before the end of your next turn gains 1/2/2 Boons on Weak/Standard/Strong. On Strong, a second such ally gains 1 Boon to its next qualifying roll.*
