# Carry the Sunrise

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Willing allies within 2 squares.

Carry the light forward. Move up to 2 squares, then each willing ally initially in range may move up to 2 squares toward your new position without provoking. At their endpoints they gain 1 Boon to their next defense before your next turn.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 3

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
