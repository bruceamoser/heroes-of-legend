# Anatomy of an Opening

*Make a Knowledge melee attack that can reveal the target's physical resistance. On Strong, you can also move 1 square without provoking.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Knowledge melee attack.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage; learn the target's physical resistance.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage; learn the target's physical resistance and move 1 square without provoking.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary melee weapon (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.
