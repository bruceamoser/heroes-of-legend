# Call Animal: Adept

*Give up to three willing creatures 1 Boon to attack and defense rolls. Sustain the blessing with a Maneuver each turn.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Animal entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 3 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** call-the-hunting-cat
