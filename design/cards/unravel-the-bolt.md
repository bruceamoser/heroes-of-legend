# Unravel the Bolt

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One visible caster within 4 squares, before a single-target spell resolves.

Unpick part of the spell’s shape. Choose its target to receive the effect below. Resolve this before that target’s normal defense; no effect on an area spell.

- **Weak:** Target gains 1 Boon to its defense.
- **Standard:** Target gains 2 Boons.
- **Strong:** Target gains 3 Boons and may move 1 square without provoking after resolution.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
