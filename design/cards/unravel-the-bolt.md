# Unravel the Bolt

*You catch the spell’s shape in your mind and pull at the thread that holds it straight.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one willing ally targeted by a single-target spell from a visible caster within 4 squares.

Spend your Reaction before the recipient’s normal defense roll. Grant 2 Boons to that defense. If it is Strong, the recipient may move 1 square without provoking after the spell resolves. Make no additional roll. This does not cancel the spell and cannot affect an area spell.

**Duration:** Resolve once at the stated trigger; applied conditions follow normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2

**Type:** Magic / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
