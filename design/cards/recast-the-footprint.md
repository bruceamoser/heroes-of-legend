# Recast the Footprint

*You rub out the shape in the air and draw it again nearby; your summon dissolves and reforms at your fingertip.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible summon of yours within 6 squares.

Teleport the summon up to 3 squares to a visible free supported space within 6 squares of you, with room for its whole footprint. It retains its HP, conditions, remaining duration and command history. This is not a command and grants no activity.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** recast-the-footprint
