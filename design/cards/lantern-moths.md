# Lantern Moths

*A small constellation of moths rises from your open palm.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three visible unoccupied squares within 4 squares

Lights last one scene, illuminate their square and adjacent squares, and cannot blind or deal damage. With one Maneuver you may move each light up to 2 squares; they vanish beyond 6 squares from you. They are effects, not summons or blockers.

**Duration:** Until the scene ends, unless ended earlier as stated above.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** lantern-moths
