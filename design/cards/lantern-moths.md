# Lantern Moths

*A small constellation of moths rises from your open palm.*

- **ID:** lantern-moths
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** None — no roll
- **Target / range / shape:** Up to three visible unoccupied squares within 4 squares
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Create gentle lights. | Create gentle lights. | Create gentle lights. |

Lights last one scene, illuminate their square and adjacent squares, and cannot blind or deal damage. You may move them up to 2 squares as a Maneuver; they vanish beyond 6 squares from you. They are effects, not summons or blockers.
