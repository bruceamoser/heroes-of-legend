# The Shared Cup

*Two willing creatures within 5 squares, one donor and one recipient. You can be either. Move up to 6 HP from the donor to the recipient. The donor must remain above 0 HP. The recipient cannot exceed maximum HP. Move the same amount out of the donor’s HP and into the recipient’s HP, ignoring Life resistance. If the recipient rises above 0 HP, clear both death roll tallies and end Dying and HP-caused unconsciousness.*
