# Sever the Pursuit

*Choose up to three visible enemies within 8 squares. Make an Agility ranged attack against the chosen targets. On a Standard result, deal 4 physical damage and Slowed 2.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three visible enemies within 8 squares.

Make an Agility ranged attack against the chosen targets.

- **Weak:** 4 physical damage and Slowed 1.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage and Slowed 2.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage and Slowed 3.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary ranged weapon and ammunition, supplied on learning if needed: one Bulk 1 weapon and one Bulk 1 d8 ammunition bundle.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Marksmanship 3

**Type:** Skill / Attack / Control
