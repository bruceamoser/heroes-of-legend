# Sever the Pursuit

*The pursuit falters as your shots bite into its leading ranks.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three visible creatures within 8 squares

One player roll uses the worst applicable net Boon/Bane situation. Areas include allies unless explicitly excluded; selected targets may exclude allies. NPC attacks give each affected player its defense roll.

- **Weak:** 4 physical damage to each; Slowed 1.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage to each; Slowed 2.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage to each; Slowed 3.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires and grants one ordinary ranged weapon and one d8 ammunition bundle if each is absent; Bulk 1 each. Check ammunition once per attack, not per target.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Marksmanship 3

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
