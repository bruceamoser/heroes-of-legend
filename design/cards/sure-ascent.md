# Sure Ascent

*Your companions see a cliff; you see the next three handholds.*

- **ID:** sure-ascent
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Brawn
- **Target / range / shape:** A climb you could ordinarily attempt
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Gain 1 progress and retain a safe grip. | Gain 2 progress and bring one carried item securely. | Gain 3 progress and leave a usable handhold or rope position for an ally. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.
