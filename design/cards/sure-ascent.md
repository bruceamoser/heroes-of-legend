# Sure Ascent

*You find a seam with the side of your boot and pull upward, letting the rock take your weight one steady point at a time.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self during a climb.

Climbing costs you 1 movement per square. When you climb using a secured safety rope, gain 1 Boon to attribute rolls for that climb.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Movement / Utility

**ID:** sure-ascent
