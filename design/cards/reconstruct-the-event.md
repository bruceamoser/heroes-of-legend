# Reconstruct the Event

- **ID:** reconstruct-the-event
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 7
- **DP:** 8
- **Requirements to learn:** Scholarship 3
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** An accessible evidence set; one hour
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward an evidence-supported account. | 2 progress; identify one gap in the account. | 3 progress; identify the next kind of evidence that could resolve a gap. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.
