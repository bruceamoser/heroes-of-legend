# A Splendid Mistake

*Roll Guile to deal fire or lightning damage in a 3-by-3 area. Standard and Strong results also push creatures outward.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Every creature in one visible 3-by-3 area within 5 squares.

Choose fire or lightning and make a Guile area spell attack.

- **Weak:** 4 damage of the chosen type.
- **Standard:** 4 damage of that type; push each target 1 square away from the area's center.
- **Strong:** 6 damage of that type; push each target 2 squares away from the center.

Choose the center target's push direction and the order of pushes.
