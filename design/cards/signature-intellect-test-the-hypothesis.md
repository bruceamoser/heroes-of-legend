# Test the Hypothesis

*You recognize the useful detail and show your companions how to act on it.*

## Rules

### Spot the Leverage

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature or obstacle within 5 squares; you and each ally within 5 squares who can hear and understand you.

Choose one visible creature or obstacle within 5 squares. Learn one useful opening: for a creature, one of its defenses or one visible weakness; for an obstacle, whether Force can overcome it and its remaining progress target. You and each ally within 5 squares who can hear and understand you gain 1 Boon to your next action against the target before the start of your next turn.

**Duration:** Information persists; each Boon expires when used or the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Use this on a given target once per scene. The DA describes observable defenses or weaknesses and the obstacle’s objective; hidden personal history is not revealed.

### Test the Hypothesis

**Action:** One minute · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Time:** One minute.

**Target / range / shape:** Self and up to three allies who can hear and understand you.

Spend one minute forming a plan from the evidence and observations available. State the plan. The DA confirms what is true, what remains uncertain, and one contradiction or risk. You and each chosen ally gain 2 Boons to your next roll that directly follows the plan.

**Duration:** The plan and its stated limits remain known; each Boon expires when used or at the end of the scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

You can maintain one plan at a time. A new plan replaces its unused Boons; new relevant evidence is required to plan again for the same objective this scene.

**Frequency:** Once per scene.

### If you learned Diagnose the Failure at level 3

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** An ally within 5 squares that rolled Weak on an attack or objective attempt, before resolving it.

Once per scene, communicate an applicable correction the ally can perceive and understand. The ally rerolls with 1 additional Boon and uses the new result, including its natural dice. Resolve damage, conditions or progress only after that replacement roll.

**Duration:** The replacement roll.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
