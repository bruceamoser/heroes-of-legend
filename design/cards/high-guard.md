# High Guard

*You lift the battered shield and turn with every impact. There is always another edge waiting behind its rim.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self before a shield-blockable defense.

Use Shield Block as part of this Reaction, granting 2 Boons to eligible defenses until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use a held ordinary shield (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 3

**Type:** Skill / Defense

**ID:** high-guard
