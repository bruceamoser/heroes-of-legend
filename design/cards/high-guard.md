# High Guard

*You lift your shield and turn with each blow, catching steel and splinters on its battered face.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self, before a shield-blockable defense roll.

Use Shield Block as part of this Reaction, granting 2 Boons instead of its usual 1 to eligible defenses until the start of your next turn. This replaces your Shield Block benefit; it does not add a second Shield Block effect. The shield must remain held and the DA still judges which attacks it can block.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a held shield. Grants one ordinary shield, Bulk 1, if absent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 3

**Type:** Skill / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** high-guard
