# Use a Healing Potion

*Drink a healing potion or give it to an adjacent creature.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent willing living creature

Use a free hand to drink or administer the potion. For this action, an unconscious or restrained recipient counts as willing. Another recipient spends a Reaction if conscious and above 0 HP. Apply Life resistance to the bottle's printed healing, with a minimum of 1 HP when HP is missing and a maximum of the HP missing. Check the bottle's inventory die after use.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** A healing potion and a free hand.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** use-a-healing-potion
