# Behind the Hawthorn

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self, after a visible attack resolves against you.

Vanish into a swirl of leaves and reappear in a visible free square within 2 squares. No opaque barrier may separate the squares. This teleport does not provoke.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
