# Name What Is Real

*You hold a familiar image in your thoughts and press it toward your companion as their attention begins to scatter.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one visible willing ally within 3 squares, immediately after temporary Dazed is applied.

Reduce the newly applied Dazed by 1, minimum 0. Existing and starred contributions remain. This does not recalculate action categories already chosen for the current turn.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** name-what-is-real
