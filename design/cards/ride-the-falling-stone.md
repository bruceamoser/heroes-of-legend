# Ride the Falling Stone

*You run along the wreckage before the world catches up.*

- **ID:** ride-the-falling-stone
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 2
- **Action:** Reaction
- **Attribute:** Agility
- **Target / range / shape:** Self, when a floor, ledge or support gives way
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Move 1 square toward a safe surface. | Move 2 squares toward a safe surface. | Move 3 squares and carry one adjacent willing ally with you. |

Resolve before the fall. Movement can cross collapsing support but not empty air; end on a safe free square or suffer the remaining fall normally. No opportunity attacks.
