# The Patient Metal

*You warm the bent hinge between your palms until the metal remembers the line it used to hold.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** One minute.

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible damaged component of an ordinary metal item, small enough to hold between your hands.

Reshape a damaged fitting, fastener, edge or link that fits between your hands. Make a Knowledge roll.

- **Weak:** Restore its ordinary function for the scene.
- **Standard:** Restore its ordinary function permanently.
- **Strong:** Also restore a second adjacent damaged component of the same item.
