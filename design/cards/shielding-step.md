# Shielding Step

*You are already moving before your friend sees the blow.*

- **ID:** shielding-step
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Guard 1
- **Action:** Reaction
- **Attribute:** None — no roll
- **Target / range / shape:** An adjacent willing ally, before an attack resolves
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a shield. Grants one ordinary shield, Bulk 1, if absent.
