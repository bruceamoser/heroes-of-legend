# Shielding Step

*Choose one adjacent willing ally after a single-target attack is declared and before its roll. Replace the ally as the target if you meet the attack's range and targeting requirements. Resolve the attack against you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing ally after a single-target attack is declared and before its roll.

Replace the ally as the target if you meet the attack's range and targeting requirements. Resolve the attack against you.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use a held ordinary shield (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 1

**Type:** Skill / Defense

**ID:** shielding-step
