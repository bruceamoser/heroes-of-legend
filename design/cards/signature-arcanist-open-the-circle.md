# Open the Circle

*You sweep a ring of light around your companions, its edge rising like a thin glass wall when magic strikes.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and willing allies within 2 squares when used.

Each recipient gains 1 Boon to defenses against spells until the start of your next turn. During that time, each recipient gains Stability 2.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Arcanist; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Magic / Defense

**Limits:** Reapplying replaces your previous ward.
