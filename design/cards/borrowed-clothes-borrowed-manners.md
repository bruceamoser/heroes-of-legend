# Borrowed Clothes, Borrowed Manners

*You adjust the collar and try the little habit that will make the borrowed clothes look lived in.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Time:** Ten minutes preparation; resolve challenges during the encounter.

**Target / range / shape:** One mundane disguise; ten minutes preparation.

Use suitable clothing and describe a plausible role. Before a consequential encounter, the DA states the impersonation objective, progress target and whether progress is cumulative. Ordinary unchallenged disguise needs no roll. The card supplies no language, memories or authority.

- **Weak:** 1 progress; notice one visible detail of your disguise likely to attract attention.
- **Standard:** 2 progress; notice that detail and one observable habit useful to the role.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; gain those details and identify one concern the observer expresses about your presence.

**Duration:** Information and earned progress persist for the stated task; completed changes remain until circumstances alter them.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Suitable clothing required; none granted.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Ordinary activities remain available without this card. Roll only when uncertainty has consequences.
