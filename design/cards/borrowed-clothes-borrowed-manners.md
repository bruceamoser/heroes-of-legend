# Borrowed Clothes, Borrowed Manners

*You adjust the collar and practice the little habit that makes the clothes look lived in. Then you join the room as if you have always belonged.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Time:** Ten minutes of preparation.

**Target / range / shape:** One mundane disguise and plausible role.

Prepare a plausible role with suitable clothing. Learn one observable detail or habit that helps sell it. While maintaining that disguise, gain 1 Boon to Guile rolls passing as the role.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Suitable clothing.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**ID:** borrowed-clothes-borrowed-manners
