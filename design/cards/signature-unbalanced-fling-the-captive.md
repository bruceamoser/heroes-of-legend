# Over the Top

*Your hands turn bodies and momentum into weapons.*

## Rules

### Grab and Hurl

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature your size or smaller.

Make a Brawn roll against the target. Weak: it has Grappled 1. Standard: throw it up to 2 squares in a direction you choose; it lands Prone. Strong: throw it up to 3 squares in a direction you choose; it lands Prone and takes 2 physical damage. Resolve collisions normally. The throw ends any grapple from this ability.

**Duration:** Immediate; Grappled and Prone use their normal rules.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Requires a free hand and a target your Size or smaller. A control-only Weak result deals no damage; it does not acquire an attack damage minimum.

### Over the Top

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally your size or smaller within reach.

Throw the ally up to 4 squares in a direction you choose, ignoring difficult terrain and passing over occupied squares. The ally must land standing in a free square. If it lands adjacent to an enemy, it may immediately make one Basic Melee attack against that enemy with 1 Boon. That attack is part of your action, not the ally's turn. The ally takes no damage from the throw.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand; the ally needs a ready melee weapon or free hand for its Basic Melee attack.

Use once per scene. The landing square must be legal and empty; a granted attack cannot grant further attacks.
