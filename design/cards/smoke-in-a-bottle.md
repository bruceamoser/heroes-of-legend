# Smoke in a Bottle

*You shake the bottle once and throw. It breaks with a pop, spilling a thick grey screen across the space your friends need.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** Main Action to throw; one hour per crafting attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** One connected smoke area within 3 squares, or one compatible recipe while crafting.

Use a prepared Smoke Flask supply and make a Knowledge roll. Fill up to 3/5/7 connected visible unoccupied ground squares within 3 squares with smoke on Weak/Standard/Strong. The smoke supplies heavy cover until the end of your next turn.

**Make the supply:** Learn Smoke Flask and Travel Food. You can craft either recipe using its materials, tools, full time and supply cap. Make a Knowledge crafting roll for 1/2/3 inventory steps on Weak/Standard/Strong.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A mixing kit (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed; a prepared supply when throwing.
