# Wings over the Battle

*Wings burst from your shoulders. One powerful beat carries you over the locked shields, and another sets you down beyond them.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self to a visible free supported square within 6 squares.

Fly up to 6 squares over creatures, gaps and difficult ground to a visible free supported square, then land. This movement does not provoke.
