# Disengage

*You give ground carefully, refusing to offer the opening your enemy is waiting for.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Your voluntary movement does not provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of this turn.

**Duration:** Remainder of this turn

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.

**ID:** disengage · **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
