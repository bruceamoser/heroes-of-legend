# Ironbound Advance

*You put your armored shoulder behind your weapon and walk straight into the press of steel.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Move up to 3 squares without provoking. While wearing armor and holding a melee weapon, gain Stability 3 until the start of your next turn. Movement must follow a legal path and end in a free supported square. This grants no attack.

**Duration:** Movement is immediate; Stability lasts until your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires worn armor and a melee weapon; grants neither.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 3 and Arms 2

**Type:** Skill / Movement / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** ironbound-advance
