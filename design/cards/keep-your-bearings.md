# Keep Your Bearings

- **ID:** keep-your-bearings
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** An unfamiliar place with usable landmarks; one minute
- **Duration:** As specified
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward finding your way. | 2 progress and identify your return direction. | 3 progress and describe a recognizable landmark to companions. |

Ordinary navigation is possible without the card. No routine travel roll; only consequential uncertainty. No magic compass when evidence is absent.
