# Spot the Tell

*You watch the creature set its feet and draw breath, matching the motion to an attack you have studied.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 6 squares that has already used an attack you witnessed this scene.

Choose that attack. The DA tells you which attribute defends against it and its range. You may share this observation aloud. This reveals no hidden attacks, resistances or other statistics and grants no Boon.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Scholarship 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** spot-the-tell
