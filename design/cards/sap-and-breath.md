# Sap and Breath

*Green warmth passes from your palm into the wound. The bleeding slows as your fingers turn cold.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 2 squares, including yourself, or one creature in range harmed by healing.

Pay 1 HP and make a Reason healing roll.

- **Weak:** Restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 5 HP and reduce temporary Bleeding by 1.
- **Strong:** Restore 7 HP and reduce temporary Bleeding by 2.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 1
