# Sap and Breath

*You kneel beside your companion and extend your hand. Green light passes from your palm into their wounds, leaving your own fingers cold.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 2 squares.

A green warmth passes from your palm into their hurt. Pay 1 HP first, leaving yourself above 0; ignore resistance for the cost. Requires a free hand. Apply Life resistance to healing, minimum 1, capped by missing HP.

- **Weak:** Restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 5 HP and reduce temporary Bleeding by 1.
- **Strong:** Restore 7 HP and reduce temporary Bleeding by 2.

**Duration:** Instant. Applied conditions follow their normal duration and decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** sap-and-breath

range, action and HP cost; the HP result becomes Life damage, without beneficial
recovery riders. This is the explicit exception to willing-only targeting above.
