# The Grove Gives Back

*You open your hands to the circle, feeling your own warmth flow toward your friends.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares.

Offer your strength to the whole circle. Pay 7 HP once before rolling, bypassing resistance and remaining above 0; requires a free hand. Allocate the rolled healing pool among the targets as you choose, minimum 1 allocated per chosen target. Each applies Life resistance to its share, minimum 1 while missing HP.

- **Weak:** Share 10 HP.
- **Standard:** Share 16 HP.
- **Strong:** Share 22 HP; each recipient may reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.

**Duration:** Instant. Applied conditions follow their normal duration and decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 3 and Wilderness 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
