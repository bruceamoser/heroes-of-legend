# Notice the Human Detail

- **ID:** notice-the-human-detail
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** An accessible scene or person you can examine; one minute
- **Duration:** As specified
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Learn one visible detail relevant to your stated question. | Also identify a plausible interpretation. | Also identify a concrete way to check that interpretation. |

No secret fact is invented. Obvious clues require no roll. This grants no lie detection, mind reading or an Observation attribute.
