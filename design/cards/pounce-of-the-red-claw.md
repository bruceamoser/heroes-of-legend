# Pounce of the Red Claw

*Choose one enemy reachable by a legal move of up to 3 squares. Move up to 3 squares without provoking, then make an Agility unarmed attack against an adjacent enemy. On a Standard result, deal 5 physical damage and Prone.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One enemy reachable by a legal move of up to 3 squares.

Move up to 3 squares without provoking, then make an Agility unarmed attack against an adjacent enemy.

- **Weak:** 4 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 5 physical damage and Prone.
- **Strong:** 7 physical damage and Prone; move 1 square without provoking afterward.
