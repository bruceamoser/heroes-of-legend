# Pounce of the Red Claw

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One creature reachable by a legal move of up to 2 squares.

Claws or a fleeting beast-shape carry you into the leap. Move up to 2 squares, then strike an adjacent target; no prior transformation is required. Movement provokes normally.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage and knock prone.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage and knock prone; move 1 square without provoking afterward.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Type:** Skill / Attack
