# The Strong Arm

*Choose one visible creature within 4 squares. You can throw weapons or improvised items that a normal person would require two hands to throw. Make a Brawn attack. On a Standard result, deal 4 physical damage. The target is pushed 1 square.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 4 squares.

You can throw weapons or improvised items that a normal person would require two hands to throw. Make a Brawn attack.

- **Weak:** 3 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage; the target is pushed 1 square.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage; the target is knocked Prone.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a weapon or improvised item that a normal person would require two hands to throw.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Attack

**Tags:** Universal / Attack / Skill

**Limits:** One object per attack.
