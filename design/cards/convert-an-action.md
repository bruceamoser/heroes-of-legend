# Convert an Action

*You abandon the opening you were watching and throw your effort into what matters now.*

## Rules

**Action:** Action conversion · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self

Spend Main Action to gain a Maneuver; spend a Maneuver to gain another movement allowance. One-way conversion only. Converting both gives a full sprint.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.

**ID:** convert-an-action · **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
