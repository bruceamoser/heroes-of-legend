# Stone on Your Shoulders

*The rune drinks the impact and sends its weight through your boots. You take the step the blow was meant to steal.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self before one event deals physical damage.

Gain physical resistance 3 against the event. After it resolves, move 1 square without provoking.
