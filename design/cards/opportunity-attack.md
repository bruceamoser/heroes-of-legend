# Opportunity Attack

- **ID:** opportunity-attack
- **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
- **Type:** Universal
- **Tier / minimum level:** Universal
- **DP:** Not purchased
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Reaction
- **Attribute:** None — no roll
- **Target / range / shape:** An adjacent creature triggering an opportunity
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| No roll; apply the effect. | No roll; apply the effect. | No roll; apply the effect. |

Make Basic Melee only; no specialty card. Leaving reach voluntarily provokes; casting, standing, stowing and retrieving are intended triggers. Exact interruption timing remains a decision for review. Forced movement does not provoke.
