# Cut and Turn

*You follow the stroke through with your feet and slip around the edge of your opponent’s guard.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self, after your Main Action melee attack resolves.

Move up to 1 square without provoking. On a Standard or Strong attack, you may move up to 2 squares instead. Use a legal route and free endpoint; this grants no additional attack.

**Duration:** Immediate movement.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Blade; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Movement

**Limits:** Once on your turn.
