# Cast Off the Venom

*Place a casting point that gives your next Main Action spell this turn 1 Boon and changes where its attack path or area begins.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Every willing living creature within 3 squares, including yourself.

Each target reduces temporary Poisoned by 3.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 3

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**ID:** cast-off-the-venom
