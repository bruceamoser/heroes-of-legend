# The Mountain Walks

*Charge in a straight line, pushing creatures out of your path and breaking through nonmagical barriers you can damage.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Cost:** Your Main Action and your movement for the turn.

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one straight line up to your Speed.

You must have movement remaining. Move in a straight line. When your line reaches a square occupied by a creature, push that creature 2 squares out of your path, applying Stability. If the creature leaves the square, you can continue through it. If the creature cannot leave, it is knocked Prone and your charge stops in the last legal square before it. Apply the normal collision rules.

When your line reaches a nonmagical barrier no thicker than 1 square, the barrier breaks and leaves a passage. You can continue through the passage. The DA confirms the barrier is breakable and the line is clear enough for the charge.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
