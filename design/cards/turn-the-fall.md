# Turn the Fall

*You turn the falling body with both hands raised. The wind catches enough of the weight to make the landing a chance instead of a sentence.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one visible willing or unconscious creature within 3 squares immediately before it lands from a fall.

Reduce the distance used for this fall's damage by 4 squares. You may shift the landing to a visible free supported square within 1 square of its original location, along an open route.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 2 and Athletics 1

**Type:** Magic / Defense / Movement

**ID:** turn-the-fall
