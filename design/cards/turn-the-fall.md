# Turn the Fall

*You lift both hands, and a turning cushion of wind takes some of the weight from the falling body.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one visible willing creature within 3 squares, immediately before it lands from a fall.

Reduce the distance used to calculate this fall’s damage by 4 squares, minimum 0. The actual landing position and any hazard there remain unchanged. This grants no flight and does not reduce collision damage from a push or pull.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 2 and Athletics 1

**Type:** Magic / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** turn-the-fall
