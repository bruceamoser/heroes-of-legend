# Guide the Crossing

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One noncombat natural hazard and companions; 10 minutes

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward preparing a safe route.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress and one traveler gains 1 Boon to its next roll on this crossing.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 3

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** guide-the-crossing · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
