# Guide the Crossing

- **ID:** guide-the-crossing
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 7
- **DP:** 8
- **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 3
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One noncombat natural hazard and companions; 10 minutes
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward preparing a safe route. | 2 progress. | 3 progress and one traveler gains 1 Boon to its next roll on this crossing. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.
