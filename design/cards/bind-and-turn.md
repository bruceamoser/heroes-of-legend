# Bind and Turn

*You catch their momentum and feel the instant when strength becomes imbalance.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** An adjacent enemy after it attacks you.

Catch its weapon or striking limb and turn the struggle. This is a fresh maneuver, not another defense roll.

- **Weak:** Push it 1 square.
- **Standard:** Push it 2 squares.
- **Strong:** Push it 2 squares and knock it prone.

**Duration:** For the triggering event; granted movement resolves immediately and conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Arms 2

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use. No earlier path card is required; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
