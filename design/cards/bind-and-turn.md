# Bind and Turn

*Make a Fortitude melee attack against an adjacent creature for 3, 5 or 7 physical damage.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature immediately after its melee attack against you resolves.

After an adjacent creature finishes a melee attack against you, make an Agility control attack against it. NPC defense: Agility.

- **Weak:** Push it 1 square directly away from you.
- **Standard:** Push it 2 squares directly away from you.
- **Strong:** Push it 2 squares directly away from you and apply Prone.
