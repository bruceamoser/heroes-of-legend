# The Web of Whispers

*One question about a person, place, faction or event your network or research could plausibly know. Ask one question. The DA answers truthfully from what that network would know. The answer may be incomplete or point to a source. You also learn who can tell you more.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one question about a person, place, faction or event your network or research could plausibly know.

Ask one question. The DA answers truthfully from what that network would know. The answer may be incomplete or point to a source. You also learn who can tell you more.

**Duration:** The answer and contact remain known.
