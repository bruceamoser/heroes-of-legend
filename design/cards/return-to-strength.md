# Return to Strength

*You reach toward your injured companion and send a surge of warmth into their body. Their breathing strengthens as your own knees briefly weaken.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 3 squares, including self

Moser’s law of conservation of Life energy: pay 7 HP before rolling; bypass resistance and remain above 0 HP. Requires a free hand. Life resistance reduces restoration, minimum 1 where HP is missing. Healing-harmed creatures take the rolled result as Life damage. No resurrection or removal of wounds/Grit loss.

- **Weak:** Restore 8 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 16 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 24 HP; the recipient may stand without spending movement and move 1 square without provoking.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 3

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** return-to-strength

[Card conventions](../card-conventions.md) · [Catalog](../catalog.md)

**Offensive healing — latest agreement:** this spell may target an unwilling
creature harmed by healing. Resolve it as an attack using its printed attribute,
range, action and HP cost; the HP result becomes Life damage, without beneficial
recovery riders. This is the explicit exception to willing-only targeting above.
Use the creature’s printed defense when a player defends; no opposed second roll.
