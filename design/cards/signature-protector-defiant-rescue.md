# Defiant Rescue

*You see the killing blow and throw yourself into its path.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally within 2 squares, just before damage from a single-target attack would reduce it to 0 HP.

When an ally within 2 squares would enter Dying, move adjacent if a legal route exists, then become the target of the triggering attack if legal and resolve it against you instead. No duplicate damage on the ally.

**Duration:** Resolve once at the stated trigger; applied conditions follow normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Protector; minimum level 7

**Type:** Signature

**Limits:** Normal action economy; any additional limit is printed above.

**ID:** signature-protector-defiant-rescue · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
