# Thunder Ring

*Your shout breaks across the battlefield like a bell of thunder.*

- **ID:** thunder-ring
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Attack
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Storm 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** Every other creature within 1 square of you
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 3 physical damage. | 3 physical damage; push 1 away from you. | 5 physical damage; push 1 and Deafened 1. |

One player roll uses the worst applicable net Boon/Bane situation. Areas include allies unless explicitly excluded; selected targets may exclude allies. NPC attacks give each affected player its defense roll. Proposed NPC defense attribute: Fortitude.
