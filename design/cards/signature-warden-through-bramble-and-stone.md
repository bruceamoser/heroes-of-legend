# Entangling Growth

*You speak to the roots, and they remember they were here first.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One or two creatures within 8 squares that are adjacent to each other.

Make one Knowledge roll and apply it to each target. Weak: 2 physical damage, and the target is Slowed 1. Standard: 3 physical damage, and the target is Slowed 2. Strong: 5 physical damage, and the target is Slowed 2 and Grappled 1.

**Duration:** Immediate; Slowed and Grappled use their normal rules.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Warden; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Magic / Attack / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; normal action economy applies.
