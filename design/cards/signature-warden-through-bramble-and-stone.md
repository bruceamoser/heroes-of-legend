# Through Bramble and Stone

*You lift a thorny branch, find a foothold between the stones and turn to show your companion the way through.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one willing ally initially adjacent.

Move up to 3 squares along a legal route, ignoring difficult-terrain surcharges and without provoking. Then the ally may move up to 1 square with the same benefits, ending adjacent to you. Both must be able to move and finish in supported free squares. If the ally cannot reach a legal adjacent endpoint, it stays where it is. No gaps, barriers or occupied standing squares are crossed.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Warden; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Movement

**Limits:** Each participant moves once per use.
