# Follow My Leap

*Self and up to three willing allies within 2 squares who hear you. You and up to three willing allies who hear you each jump up to 5 squares from your own positions to separate visible free supported landings, with no approach required. These jumps do not provoke.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and up to three willing allies within 2 squares who hear you.

You and up to three willing allies who hear you each jump up to 5 squares from your own positions to separate visible free supported landings, with no approach required. These jumps do not provoke.
