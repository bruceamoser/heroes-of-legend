# A Knot You Can Trust

*You tie the knot without looking, then tug it once before trusting it with a friend’s weight.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** A rope you hold and a reachable suitable anchor.

While making a climb, you may secure or release one held rope at a suitable anchor you reach as part of that climb, without a separate action. You still need a free hand. The rope must be long and strong enough for its intended use; this grants no automatic success, extra movement or immunity to falling.

**Duration:** During the climb.

**Equipment / acquisition:** One ordinary rope coil, Bulk 1, on learning this card if absent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Ordinary activities remain available without this card. Roll only when uncertainty has consequences.
