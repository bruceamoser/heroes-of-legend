# A Knot You Can Trust

*You tie the knot without looking and test it with a sharp pull. Then you put your friend's weight on it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** A rope you hold and a reachable suitable anchor during a climb.

During a climb, secure or release a held rope at a suitable anchor you reach as part of the climb, without a separate action.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand; one ordinary rope coil (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**ID:** a-knot-you-can-trust
