# Carry to Safety

*Use a Maneuver to reduce temporary Poisoned by 3 on willing living creatures within 3 squares, including yourself.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and one adjacent willing or unconscious ally whose weight you can physically support.

Move yourself and the ally up to your Speed without provoking. Carry or support it with a free hand, ending in separate adjacent free supported squares.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 3

**Type:** Skill / Movement / Defense

**ID:** carry-to-safety
