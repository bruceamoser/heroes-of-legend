# Not Yet Your Name

*You seize your companion's hand. The strength you give them leaves your own breath thin, but theirs comes back.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing or unconscious creature after it enters Dying and its wound is resolved.

Pay 7 HP and restore 12 HP to the recipient.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 3 and Medicine 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**ID:** not-yet-your-name
