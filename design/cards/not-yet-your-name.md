# Not Yet Your Name

*You seize your friend’s hand and give a painful measure of your own strength to keep them here.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** An adjacent willing creature after it enters Dying.

Pay 7 HP first, leaving yourself above 0; the payment bypasses resistance. With a free hand, restore 8 HP before Life resistance, minimum 1. The wound still occurs. This cannot reverse Last Words. The cost makes this urgent rescue different from an ordinary large heal.

**Duration:** For the triggering event; granted movement resolves immediately and conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 3 and Medicine 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use. No earlier path card is required; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
