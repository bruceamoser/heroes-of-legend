# A Friend Beneath the Door

*Something with bright eyes and improbable whiskers answers.*

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One free adjacent square

HP 1, Speed 4, resistance 0, no attack. May pass small openings appropriate to its described size; cannot cross occupied standing creature squares. Can carry one incidental object, not Bulk 1 equipment, and report simple sights through gestures. Uses normal summon commands and 6-square tether. Lasts 10 minutes; immediate Main command can convert to movement.

**Duration:** As stated in the effect

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
