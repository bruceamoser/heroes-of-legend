# Call Fey: Novice

*Summon a Novice Fey within 3 squares for one scene. Choose its form each time you cast.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Fey entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 1 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** a-friend-beneath-the-door
