# Stride of the Giant

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** Each other adjacent creature.

Your outline swells like a mountain’s shadow and your heel strikes the ground. Roll once using the worst applicable situation. The size is momentary; you occupy your normal square.

- **Weak:** 2 physical damage and push 1.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage and push 2.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage, push 2 and knock prone.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
