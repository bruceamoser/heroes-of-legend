# The Hunt Ends Here

*Choose one visible enemy within 8 squares after it moves voluntarily. Make a Knowledge ranged attack. On a Standard result, deal 5 physical damage and Slowed 1.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One visible enemy within 8 squares after it moves voluntarily.

Make a Knowledge ranged attack.

- **Weak:** 4 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 5 physical damage and Slowed 1.
- **Strong:** 7 physical damage and Prone.

**Equipment / acquisition:** An ordinary ranged weapon and d8 ammunition bundle (Bulk 1 each), supplied on learning if needed.
