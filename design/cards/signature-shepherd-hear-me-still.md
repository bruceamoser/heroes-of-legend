# Come Back

*You are already moving when they fall.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One ally within 3 squares.

Trigger: one ally within 3 squares enters Dying. Move up to 3 squares to a square adjacent to that ally, without provoking. Then stabilize it. Stabilization clears death marks and restores no HP; the wound remains, and Last Words cannot be interrupted.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Shepherd; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Recovery / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; uses your Reaction.
