# Call a Sentinel

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One free visible square within 3 squares

Creature: HP 9, Speed 4, resistance 0. Its Main attack affects one adjacent creature: controller rolls Knowledge for physical 3/4/6. It may Move or Defend when commanded. No innate Reaction, healing or summoning. Apply universal command rules, including the immediate Main command. Disappears immediately beyond 6 squares from controller, including forced movement. Its commanded Maneuver may grant an adjacent ally 1 Boon to its next defense before your next turn. It cannot provide this protection while uncommanded. NPC-versus-summon resolution remains an open foundation decision.

**Duration:** One combat or 10 minutes outside combat, whichever ends first

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
