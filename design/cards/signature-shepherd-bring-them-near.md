# The Fold

*Your steady presence gathers scattered courage.*

## Rules

### Steady Voice

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One ally within 3 squares that can hear and understand you.

Make a Guile roll. Choose Frightened or Dazed on the target. Weak: reduce that condition by 1. Standard: reduce it by 1, and the target gains 1 Boon to its next roll to resist fear or Daze. Strong: reduce it by 2, and the target gains 1 Boon to its next roll to resist fear or Daze. If the target has neither condition, it gains the Boon on a Standard or Strong result.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

An unused Boon expires at the start of your next turn. This reassuring voice is an ordinary ability, not a spell.

### The Fold

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** A 3 × 3 area centered on a point within 5 squares.

Call divine energy. The area becomes the Fold until the end of your next turn. You and each ally in the Fold gain 1 Boon to all rolls. The Fold does not move. As a Maneuver on a later turn, you can sustain the Fold until the end of your next turn. Sustaining costs the Maneuver and requires you to be able to act.

**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn; sustained one round at a time.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 0 SP. Sustain with a Maneuver and 0 SP, for up to 1 minute; it occupies your one sustained-spell allowance.

**Frequency:** Once per scene to create; later upkeep spends a Maneuver.
