# Call Undead: Novice

*You draw a pale line through the air. Spectral bones settle around it, then lift their empty gaze to you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Undead entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 1 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** the-bones-rise
