# The Bones Rise

*You ask the dead for one more journey, and give the bones a purpose.*

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible free square within 3 squares.

Raise a bone-shaped servant from spectral fragments; no real corpse is required. HP 5, Speed 4, resistance 1 physical. Main attack uses controller Knowledge, adjacent, physical 2/3/5. It may Move or Defend when commanded. Normal commands, immediate command and 6-square tether apply; lasts one scene. It is explicitly harmed by healing and cannot carry more than one Bulk 1 item.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
