# Call the Wild

*You learn to work with the wild rather than merely command it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** One willing wild companion arriving in a visible empty square within 6 squares.

**Magic:** Spell · **SP:** 2

Once per scene, call your bonded companion with 2 SP. Its profile is: Size Medium; HP 16; Speed 6; physical resistance 1; Brawn +1, Fortitude +1, Agility +1, Guile 0, Knowledge −1, Reason 0; Stability 0. Its natural Main Action attack uses Brawn against an adjacent target for 2/4/6 physical damage; Strong also applies Prone. It has the ordinary movement and basic actions its body can perform. You control its rolls. Once on your turn, command it within 6 squares: your Main Action grants it one Main Action and movement; your Maneuver grants it one Maneuver and movement. It receives no separate combat turn and has no Reaction. Without a command it defends normally but takes no combat actions. It is a living creature, can heal, and uses the normal Dying rules at 0 HP. You can have one bonded companion. Further castings recall that same creature with its current HP and conditions; casting neither restores nor revives it. It remains when uncommanded. If it dies or is released, finding a new willing companion requires a safe overnight rest and the DA’s appropriate wilderness fiction; the bond, not each casting, determines the creature.

**Duration:** Indefinite companionship; summoning is immediate and requires no Sustain.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.
