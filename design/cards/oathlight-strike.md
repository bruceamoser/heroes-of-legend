# Oathlight Strike

*You speak your promise as steel catches the light. A spark from the blow settles beside a companion like a waiting shield.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Reason melee attack.

- **Weak:** 2 physical and 1 radiant damage.
- **Standard:** 2 physical and 1 radiant damage; one willing ally within 2 squares gains 1 Boon to its next defense before the end of your next turn.
- **Strong:** 3 physical and 2 radiant damage; grant the same Boon, and that ally may move 1 square without provoking.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary melee weapon (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.
