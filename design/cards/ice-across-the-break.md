# Ice across the Break

*You sweep your hand across the broken path. Ice grows from both edges and meets with a sharp crack, leaving a narrow bridge shining between them.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** A straight line of up to 3 empty squares, all visible within 4 squares.

Form a one-square-wide bridge across a gap or water. Both ends must join solid supports at the same height; the bridge fills the selected squares and supports creatures crossing one at a time. Those squares cost 1 movement and are not slippery. Everyone can use the bridge. It causes no damage, traps no creature and cannot replace an existing surface or pass through a barrier. Only one bridge from this card per caster can exist; casting again ends the previous bridge.

**Duration:** Ten minutes; announce its impending end to anyone on it.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Frost 2 and Wilderness 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** One active bridge per caster. Creatures cannot be enclosed or lifted by its creation.

**ID:** ice-across-the-break · **Status:** Proposal for Bruce’s review; not balance-tested.
