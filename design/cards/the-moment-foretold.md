# The Moment Foretold

*You recognize the instant from your dream and call the warning before it is too late.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** An ally within 3 squares before it makes a declared roll.

If it hears your warning, grant 2 Boons to that roll. If it achieves Strong, it may move 1 square without provoking immediately afterward. Only rolls using an attribute; no inventory or wound rolls and no change after dice land.

**Duration:** The triggering roll and any immediately granted movement.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Scholarship 3

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** the-moment-foretold · **Status:** Proposal for Bruce’s review.
