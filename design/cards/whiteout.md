# Whiteout

*You grind a pinch of frost between your fingers and fling it skyward. A curtain of whirling snow swallows the battlefield.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Connected ground squares within 6 squares, beginning at one chosen square.

Roll, then place up to the number of squares below. Every square provides heavy cover: attacks passing through or into it suffer 3 Banes. Creatures remain visible and are not automatically Hidden. The snow causes no damage and does not make difficult terrain.

- **Weak:** Place 5 squares.
- **Standard:** Place 8 squares.
- **Strong:** Place 12 squares.

**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn; one area at a time.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Frost 3

**Type:** Magic / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** whiteout
