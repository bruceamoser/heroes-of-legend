# Whiteout

*Choose one connected area of visible ground squares within 6 squares. Make a Knowledge roll. Place heavy-cover snow in up to 5/8/12 connected ground squares on Weak/Standard/Strong, all within 6 squares. It lasts until the end of your next turn. On later turns, you may spend a Maneuver to sustain it until the end of your next turn.*
