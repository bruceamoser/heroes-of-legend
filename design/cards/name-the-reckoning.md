# Name the Reckoning

*You name the deed they hoped no one would answer for. Your voice carries across the fight, and the certainty leaves their face.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One enemy within 4 squares who hears and understands you.

Make a Guile control attack. You are the source of the Frightened condition.

- **Weak:** Frightened 1.
- **Standard:** Frightened 2.
- **Strong:** Frightened 2 and Slowed 1.
