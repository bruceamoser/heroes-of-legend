# The Forest Takes the Blow

*You call to your companion as the branches twist between them and the blow, then part to show a way out.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible willing ally within 3 squares, before a defense roll against an attack dealing physical damage while it occupies rooted vegetation.

Grant 2 Boons to this defense. On Strong, after the attack resolves, the ally may move up to 2 squares without provoking along a legal route entirely through vegetation. This grants no Hidden state and does not remove existing terrain costs.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 3

**Type:** Magic / Defense / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
