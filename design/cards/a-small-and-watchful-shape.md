# A Small and Watchful Shape

*You fold into a small furred shape. Chairs become towers, and the gap beneath the door suddenly looks like an invitation.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Become a cat-sized nonflying land animal for ten minutes. Use your own Speed and fit through openings suitable for that size. Gain 1 Boon to Agility rolls hiding in the form. Equipment folds away and resumes its benefits when you return. Return with a Maneuver to speak, attack, cast spells, use carried items or command summons.

When the form ends, reform in your current space or an adjacent free space fitting your usual body. Choose a legal return space before entering a passage.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 2 and Subterfuge 1
