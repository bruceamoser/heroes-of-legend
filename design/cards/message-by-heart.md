# Message by Heart

*You repeat the message under your breath, matching every word until you can hear it without the speaker.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Time:** One minute of rehearsal.

**Target / range / shape:** One message of up to 50 words that you can hear, or read in a script you know.

After rehearsing the message for one minute, you can reproduce its words exactly from memory. You may retain unfamiliar words this way without understanding them. You can retain only one message with this ability; memorizing another replaces it. Recalling the stored message requires only the normal time to speak or write it, not another minute of rehearsal. This grants no translation or additional ability.

**Duration:** Until you memorize a replacement message.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Purchase once; one retained message at a time.

**ID:** message-by-heart
