# Sustain Spell

*Keep your attention on the magic still answering you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One spell you cast that names Sustain in its duration

Starting on the turn after casting, continue that spell through the end of your following turn, up to its printed limit. Spend 0 SP. You can sustain one spell at a time; choosing another ends the earlier one. Follow the spell’s other ending conditions. Fixed-duration spells continue for their printed time and directed effects use the action printed to move them.

**Duration:** Through the end of your following turn

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** One sustained spell at a time.

**ID:** sustain-spell
