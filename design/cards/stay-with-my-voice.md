# Stay with My Voice

*You take your companion’s face between your hands and speak slowly until their eyes focus on yours again.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent willing creature, immediately before it chooses action categories at the start of its turn.

Reduce temporary Dazed by 2, minimum 0, before the target chooses its action categories. Starred contributions and Grappled remain. The target must be able to hear you. This restores no HP and cannot reverse Last Words.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a free hand; grants no item.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 3

**Type:** Skill / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** stay-with-my-voice
