# Sheltering Hands

*Your touch brings a wounded companion back into the fight.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Class

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one living creature you touch.

Restore 3/5/7 HP on Weak/Standard/Strong. Apply the recipient’s Life resistance, minimum 1 healing when HP is missing, and its maximum HP. Healing above 0 ends Dying and clears death marks normally. On Strong, also reduce one ordinary Bleeding, Burning or Freezing condition by 1. A starred condition still needs its own stated remedy.

After this healing, the recipient gains 2 Boons to its next defense before the start of your next turn. Once between safe overnight rests, as part of this healing action you may also stabilize a second adjacent Dying ally: it remains at 0 HP, clears its death marks and stops Dying.

**Duration:** Healing and stabilization immediate; defense Boons until used or your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand touching the healing recipient.

This is a spell costing 1 SP.

If you also know Restoring Hands, apply its condition reduction of 1/1/2 on Weak/Standard/Strong instead of this signature’s Strong-only reduction. This remains one healing action, not two castings.
