# Sheltering Hands

*Your touch brings a wounded companion back into the fight.*

## Rules

### Healing Hands

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one living creature you touch.

**Magic:** Spell · **SP:** 1

Restore 3/5/7 HP on Weak/Standard/Strong. Apply the recipient’s Life resistance, minimum 1 healing when HP is missing, and its maximum HP. Healing above 0 ends Dying and clears death marks normally. On Strong, also reduce one ordinary Bleeding, Burning or Freezing condition by 1.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand touching the recipient.

### Sheltering Hands

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** The recipient of Healing Hands.

After Healing Hands, the recipient gains 2 Boons to its next defense before the start of your next turn. Once between safe overnight rests, as part of that healing action you may also stabilize a second adjacent Dying ally: it remains at 0 HP, clears its death marks and stops Dying.

**Duration:** Healing and stabilization immediate; defense Boons until used or your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

### If you learned Restoring Hands at level 3

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** A recipient of Healing Hands.

Healing Hands also reduces one ordinary Bleeding, Burning or Freezing condition by 1 on Weak or Standard, or by 2 on Strong. Choose the condition when healing. A starred condition still needs its own stated remedy.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
