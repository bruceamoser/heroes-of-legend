# Look Over Here!

*You snap your cloak across the attacker's view, drawing their eye just as your companion needs an opening.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One ally about to make a defense roll against a sight-dependent attack declared by an adjacent enemy that can see you. Before the roll.

Grant the ally 1 Boon to that defense roll. The distraction affects the defense roll; the attack resolves normally. Apply the normal Boon/Bane rules.

**Duration:** The triggering defense roll.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 1 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Defense

**Tags:** Universal / Skill / Reaction

**Limits:** Purchase once; each use spends your Reaction.
