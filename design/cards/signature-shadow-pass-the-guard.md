# Read the Room

*Your words turn a room before your enemies can settle it.*

## Rules

### Fast Talk

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One creature within 5 squares that can hear and understand you.

State the social goal you are pursuing and what you want the target to do. Make a Guile roll. The result adds progress toward that goal: Weak: 2 progress. Standard: 3 progress. Strong: 5 progress. The DA sets the progress target before the roll. When the total reaches it, the target agrees within its wants and limits. This does not compel self-harm, betrayal, or a change to its loyalties.

**Duration:** Immediate progress; the negotiation persists.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Repeated attempts toward the same objective require a new meaningful contribution; use the DA’s objective and progress rules.

### Read the Room

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One creature within 5 squares that you can see.

Make a Guile roll against the target. An intent is what the creature wants from this exchange or what it means to do next. Weak: learn one intent. Standard: learn one intent, and gain 1 Boon to your next social roll against it. Strong: learn one intent, and gain 2 Boons to your next social roll against it.

**Duration:** The scene.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Information about intention does not compel the target to follow that intention.

**Frequency:** Once per scene per creature.
