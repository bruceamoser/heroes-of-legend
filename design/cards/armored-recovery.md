# Armored Recovery

*Move toward an ally without provoking. If you end adjacent, you both gain 1 Boon to defenses while you stay together until the end of your next turn.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self while wearing armor.

Stand if Prone, then move up to 2 squares without provoking.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Worn armor.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 2

**Type:** Skill / Movement
