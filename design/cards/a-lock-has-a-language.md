# A Lock Has a Language

*You close your eyes and listen through your fingertips for the lockmaker’s last little secret.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent ordinary lock; one minute.

Read the maker’s intention through the lock. Requires lock tools; grants a set if absent.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward its declared target; learn whether the lock shows visible damage or tampering.
- **Standard:** 2 progress.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; identify a way to relock it without another roll.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 2

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use. No earlier path card is required; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
