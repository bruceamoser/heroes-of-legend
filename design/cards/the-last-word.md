# The Last Word

*The blow lands, the world goes white, and you spend your last breath on one final strike.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, when you enter Dying.

Take one Main Action before the Dying procedure. Then mark the wound and resolve Dying normally. Once per scene.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 3 · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Utility

**Tags:** Universal / Reaction / Recovery

**Limits:** Once per scene; one Main Action.
