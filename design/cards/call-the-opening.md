# Call the Opening

- **ID:** call-the-opening
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Influence 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One visible enemy within 5 squares
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| One ally gains 1 Boon to its next attack on that enemy. | One ally adjacent to it makes a free Basic Melee attack. | Two distinct adjacent allies each make a free Basic Melee attack. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. The Weak Boon expires at the start of your next turn. Free attacks never trigger additional free attacks.
