# Call Fey: Master

*Silver light gathers where your shadow should be. A creature of the old stories steps from it and lowers its head to hear you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Fey entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 7 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 3

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** summon-fey-master
