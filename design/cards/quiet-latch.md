# Quiet Latch

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent mundane lock; one minute per attempt

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward opening.
- **Standard:** 2 progress toward opening.
- **Strong:** 3 progress; leave no tool marks.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 1

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** quiet-latch · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
