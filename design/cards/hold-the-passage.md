# Hold the Passage

*You set your shield and call the line close. Every companion at your shoulder feels the stance settle beneath them.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and willing adjacent allies.

You and willing adjacent allies gain 1 Boon to defenses and Stability 1 until the end of your next turn while adjacent to you and while you hold the shield.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use a held ordinary shield (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2

**Type:** Skill / Defense

**ID:** hold-the-passage
