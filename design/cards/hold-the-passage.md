# Hold the Passage

- **ID:** hold-the-passage
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** None — no roll
- **Target / range / shape:** Self and adjacent willing allies
- **Duration:** Until the start of your next turn
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a shield. Grants one ordinary shield, Bulk 1, if absent.
