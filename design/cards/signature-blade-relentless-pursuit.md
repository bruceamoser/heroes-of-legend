# Relentless Pursuit

*You carry the fight forward before the enemy can settle.*

## Rules

### Reckless Charge

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** Self, then one enemy within melee reach.

Once per scene, move up to 2 squares toward a visible enemy, following ordinary opportunity attack rules. Make one Basic Melee attack with your melee weapon against a creature you can reach. Take 1 Bane to defenses until the start of your next turn. This attack is part of the Maneuver and leaves your Main Action available.

**Duration:** Movement and attack immediate; defense penalty until your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ready melee weapon.

### Relentless Pursuit

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** A visible enemy within 2 squares when it begins voluntary movement.

After it finishes moving, move up to your Speed toward it without provoking. If you end adjacent, make one free Basic Melee attack against it using Brawn with 1 Boon. On a Weak result, apply Slowed 1. On a Standard result, apply Slowed 2. On a Strong result, apply Slowed 3. Slowed uses normal decay.

**Duration:** Immediate pursuit and attack.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A melee weapon is required for the attack; grants one ordinary melee weapon, Bulk 1, if absent.

Use this Reaction once per scene. Follow legal movement routes and end in a supported free square; barriers still obstruct pursuit.

### If you learned Press On at level 3

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** Your Reckless Charge.

You may use Reckless Charge twice per scene. After either charge’s attack, you may step 1 square to a legal empty square without provoking. Each charge still costs its Maneuver and imposes its defense penalty; repeated uses do not multiply that penalty.

**Duration:** Each use of Reckless Charge.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
