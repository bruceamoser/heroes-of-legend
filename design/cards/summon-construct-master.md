# Call Construct: Master

*You draw the first line with both arms. Great interlocking pieces answer it, settling into a frame that makes the ground seem smaller.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Construct entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 7 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.
