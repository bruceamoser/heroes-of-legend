# Rime Footholds

*You press your palm to the rock, leaving a short ladder of rough ice where bare fingers could find no grip.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 3 connected surface squares within 1 square.

Place icy handholds on a solid surface. Those squares can be climbed using the ordinary climbing rules, even if the surface was too smooth to grip. They do not bridge gaps, support a ceiling crossing or reduce movement costs. Everyone can use them. They cannot be dismissed or replaced while supporting a creature. At expiry, they last until current climbers reach another secure hold; no new climber can use them.

**Duration:** 10 minutes, with the stated safe expiry; one set at a time.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Frost 1

**Type:** Magic / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
