# Make Something Useful

*A broken cart becomes a brace; discarded cord becomes a way home.*

- **ID:** make-something-useful
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Craft 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Knowledge
- **Target / range / shape:** One known ordinary recipe and plausible local materials; recipe interval
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress or inventory step. | 2 progress or inventory steps. | 3 progress or inventory steps; leave the workspace ready for immediate reuse. |

Grants craft tools if absent and two ordinary recipes. For this card, equivalent salvaged materials may replace the recipe’s ordinary materials when the DA judges them suitable. Still requires a recipe; no magic or free ingredients.
