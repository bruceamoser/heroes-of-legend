# Healing Hands

*Your touch brings a wounded companion back into the fight.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one living creature you touch.

**Magic:** Spell · **SP:** 1

Restore 3/5/7 HP on Weak/Standard/Strong. Apply the recipient’s Life resistance, minimum 1 healing when HP is missing, and its maximum HP. Healing above 0 ends Dying and clears death marks normally. On Strong, also reduce one ordinary Bleeding, Burning or Freezing condition by 1.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand touching the recipient.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** Class choice · **Requirements to learn:** Class Protector; minimum level 1

**Type:** Signature

**Tags:** Skill / Signature

**Limits:** One signature choice at each of levels 1, 3 and 7.
