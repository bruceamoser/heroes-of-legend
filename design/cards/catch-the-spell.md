# Catch the Spell

*Before defending against a single-target spell from a visible caster within 4 squares, gain 2 Boons to the defense. On a Strong defense, after the spell resolves, move up to 2 squares without provoking.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, before defending against a single-target spell from a visible caster within 4 squares.

Gain 2 Boons to the defense. On a Strong defense, after the spell resolves, move up to 2 squares without provoking.
