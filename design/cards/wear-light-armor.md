# Wear Light Armor

*You snug the straps of your light armor and twist at the waist, feeling its protection follow your movements.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self

While wearing light armor, gain physical resistance 1. Use the highest matching resistance; normal cap 3.

**Duration:** While wearing the trained armor.

**Equipment / acquisition:** One ordinary light armor, Bulk 1, if absent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Armor 1

**Type:** Training / Passive

**Limits:** Purchase once.

**ID:** wear-light-armor
