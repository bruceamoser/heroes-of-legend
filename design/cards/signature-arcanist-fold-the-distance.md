# Folded Aegis

*You turn unstable magic into motion and shelter.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

For 2 hours, you are wrapped in magical heavy armor. You gain physical resistance 3, fire resistance 3 and cold resistance 3. Use the highest matching resistance for each damage type, to the normal cap of 3. The armor is weightless and needs no armor training.

**Duration:** 2 hours.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 0 SP, usable once between safe overnight rests. Its duration is fixed and requires no Sustain. Cancellation ends the armor; the cancellation progress target is 2.
