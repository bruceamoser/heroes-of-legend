# Wind the Sight

*You tighten the sight until the narrow opening settles exactly between its marks. The shot follows the line you understand.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Your ranged weapon attacks.

Your ranged weapon attacks may use Knowledge in place of their printed attack attribute. Treat cover Banes against those attacks as 1 lower.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ranged weapon fitted using craft tools; neither is granted.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Craft 2 and Marksmanship 1

**Type:** Skill / Training / Attack

**ID:** wind-the-sight
