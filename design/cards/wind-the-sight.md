# Wind the Sight

*You tighten the little sight on your weapon and watch the narrow opening settle between its marks.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Your next ranged attack this turn.

For that attack, ignore the 1 Bane from partial cover, or reduce heavy cover from 3 Banes to 2. Other Banes apply normally. The target must still be visible with an attack path. This does not grant an attack.

**Duration:** Until used or the end of this turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon and craft tools; grants neither.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Craft 2 and Marksmanship 2

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Limits:** Repeated uses do not improve the same attack again.

**ID:** wind-the-sight
