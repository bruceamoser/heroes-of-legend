# A Shelter of Light

*You raise your hand. A quiet brightness gathers around your companions, turning each incoming blade a little from its path.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and willing allies within 2 squares.

Until the end of your next turn, you and willing allies within 2 squares gain 1 Boon to defenses while in that area. On later turns, you may spend a Maneuver to sustain the shelter until the end of your next turn.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 2

**Type:** Magic / Defense

**Limits:** Maintain one shelter at a time.

**ID:** a-shelter-of-light
