# Read the Ward

*Choose one visible ongoing magical effect within 6 squares. Learn the effect's remaining duration and any stated way to escape or end it. Choose yourself or one ally within 6 squares who hears you. That creature gains 2 Boons to its next attribute roll to escape or disrupt the effect before the end of your next turn.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible ongoing magical effect within 6 squares.

Learn the effect's remaining duration and any stated way to escape or end it. Choose yourself or one ally within 6 squares who hears you; that creature gains 2 Boons to its next attribute roll to escape or disrupt the effect before the end of your next turn.
