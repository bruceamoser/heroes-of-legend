# Stillness at the Center

*You find the still point in the struggle. One touch there, and the bodies locked around it begin to fail in sequence.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy and every other adjacent Grappled enemy.

Choose one adjacent enemy and every other adjacent Grappled enemy. Make a Reason unarmed attack.

- **Weak:** 4 physical damage and Slowed 1.
- **Standard:** 4 physical damage, Slowed 1 and Dazed 1.
- **Strong:** 6 physical damage, Slowed 1 and Dazed 2.
