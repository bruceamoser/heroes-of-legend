# A Road through Rain

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** A straight route of up to 3 visible squares beginning adjacent to you.

Part nonmagical rain, airborne dust or smoke along that route until your next turn. It removes those visibility obstructions only; solid cover and magical darkness remain. Allies gain a clear view, not free movement.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 2

**Type:** Magic
