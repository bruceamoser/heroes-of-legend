# Call Their Name

*You call the name their oldest friend would use. Their eyes find yours through the panic, and their breathing begins to slow.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally within 3 squares who hears you, before an attribute roll penalized by Frightened.

Reduce the ally's temporary Frightened by 1 before its roll.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Influence 1

**Type:** Skill / Recovery

**ID:** call-their-name
