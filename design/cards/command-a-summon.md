# Command a Summon

*You give a word or gesture, and the creature turns toward the purpose you called it here to serve.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action or Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One of your summons within its tether

Main grants the summon Main plus Maneuver; Maneuver grants it Maneuver plus movement. One command per summon per turn. Same conversions apply. Two different summons can receive your two commands. A new summon receives an immediate free Main command, counted as its command.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.

**ID:** command-a-summon · **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
