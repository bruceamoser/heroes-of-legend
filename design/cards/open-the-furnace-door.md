# Open the Furnace Door

*You pull an unseen latch. The blast drives your enemy line backward, and the shape in the flame follows them with your eyes.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** Every creature in a 3-wide, 2-deep rectangle immediately ahead of you.

Make a Guile area spell attack. You are the source of its Frightened condition.

- **Weak:** 4 fire damage; push 1 square away from you.
- **Standard:** 5 fire damage; push 2 squares away from you.
- **Strong:** 7 fire damage and Frightened 1; push 2 squares away from you.
