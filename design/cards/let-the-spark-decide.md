# Let the Spark Decide

*You snap your fingers and send a bright spark toward your target. In flight it swells into a burst of flame or a spray of frost; even you do not know which until it changes.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 5 squares.

Choose one creature you can see within 5 squares. Make your attack roll using Guile and the normal Boon/Bane rules.

Look at the smallest number on the three dice used for the result, before adding Guile. A 1, 3 or 5 makes this attack deal fire damage; a 2, 4 or 6 makes it deal cold damage. This uses the attack dice, not an extra roll.

Use the attack’s Weak, Standard or Strong result for its damage and push:

- **Weak:** 3 damage of the chosen type.
- **Standard:** 3 damage of that type; push the target 1 square.
- **Strong:** 5 damage of that type; push the target 2 squares.

**Duration:** Instant; temporary conditions resolve and decay at the affected creature’s turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
