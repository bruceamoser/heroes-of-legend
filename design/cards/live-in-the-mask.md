# Live in the Mask

*You remember to be the borrowed person even when no one seems to be watching.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Your mundane disguise or impersonation.

Gain 1 Boon to a consequential Guile roll maintaining a plausible disguise during conversation. After a Standard or Strong result, learn one expectation the listener has visibly expressed about your assumed role. This does not reveal unspoken thoughts or supply clothing, languages or memories.

**Duration:** While the disguise is in use.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 2 and Influence 1

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Ordinary activities remain available without this card. Roll only when uncertainty has consequences.

**ID:** live-in-the-mask · **Status:** Proposal for Bruce’s review; follows the approved utility and writing direction.
