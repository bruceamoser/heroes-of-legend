# Speak with the Hearth

*Coals remember the hands held above them.*

- **ID:** speak-with-the-hearth
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Flame 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Knowledge
- **Target / range / shape:** One ordinary fire you can touch without harm; 10 minutes
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Learn whether it was lit within the last day. | Also learn how many people recently warmed themselves beside it. | Also glimpse one distinctive item carried by the most recent visitor. |

The touch is safe only for this divination. Information is limited to the fire’s actual recent history, not distant events. One reading per fire per day. A missing clue cannot be invented.
