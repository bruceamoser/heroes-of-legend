# Translate the Fragment

*Choose one accessible page of unfamiliar writing or an inscription of similar length. Translate up to one page of unfamiliar writing, including an ancient or uncommon language, into a language you know. Preserve damaged or missing passages as gaps. You can read the translation aloud and make a copy for your companions.*
