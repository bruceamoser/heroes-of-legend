# Translate the Fragment

*You sound out a dead king's boast. Beneath the flourishes, the warning is suddenly plain.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Time:** Ten minutes.

**Target / range / shape:** One accessible page of unfamiliar writing or an inscription of similar length.

Translate up to one page of unfamiliar writing, including an ancient or uncommon language, into a language you know. Preserve damaged or missing passages as gaps. You can read the translation aloud and make a copy for your companions.
