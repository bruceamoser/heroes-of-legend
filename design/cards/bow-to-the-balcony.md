# Bow to the Balcony

*Your opponent crumples. You salute the watching gallery, slip through the falling guard, and let the next challenger discover that the flourish was a strike.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** Brawn (Basic Melee attack)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, immediately after a melee attack you made by spending an action reduces a creature to 0 HP.

Move up to 3 squares without provoking. Then make one free Basic Melee attack against a different adjacent creature.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary melee weapon (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.
