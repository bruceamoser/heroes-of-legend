# Death from the Blind Spot

*Your paired weapons move like branches in a sudden wind.*

## Rules

### Twin Strike

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One or two creatures within reach.

Requires two light melee weapons, one in each hand. Make one attack with each weapon. Each attack uses your normal attack procedure and deals 1 physical damage on Weak, 2 on Standard, 3 on Strong. You can target the same creature with both attacks or one creature with each. After the attacks, gain 1 Boon to defense until the start of your next turn.

**Duration:** Immediate; the defense Boon lasts until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Two light melee weapons, one in each hand.

### Death from the Blind Spot

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One creature you have damaged or tracked this scene.

This ability consumes your Main Action and your movement for the turn. Move up to 6 squares to a square adjacent to the target, ignoring difficult terrain and without provoking. Then make one melee attack with 1 Boon. Weak: 6 physical damage. Standard: 9 physical damage, and the target is Prone. Strong: 12 physical damage, and the target is Prone and Dazed 1. If you cannot reach a square adjacent to the target, you cannot use this ability.

**Duration:** Immediate; Prone and Dazed use their normal rules.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A melee weapon.

**Frequency:** Once per scene.

### If you learned Through Bramble at level 3

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** Self after resolving Twin Strike.

Once on your turn, after Twin Strike, step 1 square to a legal empty square without spending movement or provoking opportunity attacks. Use the ordinary terrain and movement restrictions for that step.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
