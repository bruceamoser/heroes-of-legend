# Traverse the Impossible

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** A visible unoccupied landing within 6 squares

Choose a landing within the rolled distance. If none is safe, remain at the origin. This crosses gaps, not walls; it is not flight. Ordinary jumping remains available without this card.

- **Weak:** Traverse up to 2 squares.
- **Standard:** Traverse up to 4 squares.
- **Strong:** Traverse up to 6 squares.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 3

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** traverse-the-impossible · **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
