# Traverse the Impossible

*You look across the gap once, take the stride you need, and trust the leap you have practiced all your life.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self to a visible free supported landing within 8 squares.

Leap up to 8 squares to a visible free supported landing, clearing creatures and obstacles up to 2 squares high along the route. This movement does not provoke.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 3

**Type:** Skill / Movement

**ID:** traverse-the-impossible
