# Press On

*You carry the fight forward before the enemy can settle.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** Self, then one enemy within melee reach.

Move up to 2 squares toward a visible enemy, following ordinary opportunity attack rules. Make one Basic Melee attack with your ready melee weapon against a creature you can reach. After the attack, you may step 1 square to a legal empty square without provoking. The attack and step are part of this Maneuver and leave your Main Action available. Take 1 Bane to defenses until the start of your next turn; repeated uses do not multiply that penalty.

**Duration:** Movement and attack immediate; defense penalty until your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ready melee weapon.

Use twice per scene. If you also know Reckless Charge, its uses count against these same two uses; learning both does not add a third charge.
