# Read a Dangerous Trail

- **ID:** read-a-dangerous-trail
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** An accessible trail; 10 minutes
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward finding the trail’s destination. | 2 progress and detect one supported warning sign. | 3 progress and identify a usable route around one known hazard. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.
