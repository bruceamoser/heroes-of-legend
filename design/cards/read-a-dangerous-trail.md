# Read a Dangerous Trail

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** An accessible trail; 10 minutes

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence.

- **Weak:** 1 progress toward finding the trail’s destination.
- **Standard:** 2 progress and detect one supported warning sign.
- **Strong:** 3 progress and identify a usable route around one known hazard.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Wilderness 2

**Type:** Skill

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
