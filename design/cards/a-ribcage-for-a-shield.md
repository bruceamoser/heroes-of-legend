# A Ribcage for a Shield

*Pale ribs close around your companion and take the shape of the blow. Then they spring outward with a crack like breaking ice.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible willing ally within 3 squares, before it defends against a physical attack.

The ally gains 2 Boons to the defense. After the attack resolves, push an attacker adjacent to that ally 2 squares directly away from it.
