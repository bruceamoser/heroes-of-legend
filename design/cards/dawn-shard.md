# Dawn Shard

- **ID:** dawn-shard
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Attack
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 5 squares
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 3 radiant damage. | 3 radiant damage; mark it with visible light. | 5 radiant damage; mark it and apply Blinded 1. |

The light lasts until the start of your next turn; it does not reveal a target through opaque cover or override magical invisibility. Proposed NPC defense attribute: Agility.
