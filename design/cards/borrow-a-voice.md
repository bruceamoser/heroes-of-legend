# Borrow a Voice

*Take the form of a land beast for the scene. Follow trails by scent with 1 Boon and attack with claws or teeth.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One point within 4 squares with an open path for sound.

Create one brief nonverbal sound or speak a phrase of up to twenty words from the chosen point, using your own voice or a voice you have heard.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**ID:** borrow-a-voice
