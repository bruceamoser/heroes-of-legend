# Borrow a Voice

*The guard hears his name from the empty stairwell.*

- **ID:** borrow-a-voice
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Mind 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Guile
- **Target / range / shape:** An audible point within 4 squares
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Create a brief nonverbal sound. | Create one short phrase in your own voice. | Create one short phrase in a voice you have heard. |

Lasts only long enough to deliver the sound. No mind control or automatic deception. The listener responds to the fiction; DA calls further rolls only when consequences require them.
