# Bank the Embers

*You cup a hand over the flames and draw their heat into a tiny coal that crumbles between your fingers.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent willing creature.

Reduce temporary Burning by 1, minimum 0. This does not remove a starred source or prevent the surrounding hazard from applying Burning again. It causes no damage and restores no HP.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Flame 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** bank-the-embers
