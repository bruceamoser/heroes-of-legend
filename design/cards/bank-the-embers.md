# Bank the Embers

*Roll Brawn to strike an adjacent creature with a melee weapon for 2, 3 or 5 physical damage.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self, one adjacent willing creature, or one adjacent hand-sized ordinary flame.

Reduce temporary Burning by 2. You may also extinguish an ordinary unattended flame small enough to cover with your hand.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Flame 1
