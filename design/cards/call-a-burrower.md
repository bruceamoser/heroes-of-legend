# Call a Burrower

*You tap the earth, and something eager shoulders its way into the light.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One free visible square within 3 squares

Creature: HP 5, Speed 4, resistance 0. Its Main attack affects one adjacent creature: controller rolls Knowledge for physical 2/3/5. It may Move or Defend when commanded. No innate Reaction, healing or summoning. Apply universal command rules, including the immediate Main command. Disappears immediately beyond 6 squares from controller, including forced movement. On its Standard attack it may pull the target 1 square; on Strong it may pull 2. It can dig through loose soil during commanded movement at difficult-terrain cost, but never through stone or occupied squares. NPC-versus-summon resolution remains an open foundation decision.

**Duration:** One combat or 10 minutes outside combat, whichever ends first

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 1 and Wilderness 1

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
