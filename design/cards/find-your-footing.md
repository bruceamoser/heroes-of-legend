# Find Your Footing

*You test a foothold, shift your grip and pull yourself clear of the worst of the climb.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self while climbing or swimming.

Move up to 2 squares along a route you can already climb or swim. For this movement only, climbing and swimming cost 1 per square; other difficult terrain still costs 2. Normal opportunity rules apply. This supplies no hold on an unclimbable surface and no passage through an impassable current.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1

**Type:** Skill / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** find-your-footing
