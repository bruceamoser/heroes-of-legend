# Storm on a String

*You curl a finger toward the unattended object. A thin spiral of wind catches it and draws it across the ground toward your hand.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unattended object of up to Bulk 1 within 5 squares

Cannot take worn or held objects, deal impact damage, open locks or carry creatures. Path must be clear; use ordinary terrain cost. Repeatable.

- **Weak:** Pull it 1 square.
- **Standard:** Pull it 3 squares.
- **Strong:** Pull it 5 squares into your free hand if reachable.

**Duration:** Instant. Applied conditions follow their normal duration and decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 1 and Craft 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** storm-on-a-string
