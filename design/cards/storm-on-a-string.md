# Storm on a String

*A hook of wind snatches the key before it reaches the drain.*

- **ID:** storm-on-a-string
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Storm 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One visible unattended object of up to Bulk 1 within 5 squares
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Pull it 1 square. | Pull it 3 squares. | Pull it 5 squares into your free hand if reachable. |

Cannot take worn or held objects, deal impact damage, open locks or carry creatures. Path must be clear; use ordinary terrain cost. Repeatable.
