# Rampage

*You strike the line with the force of your whole approach.*

## Rules

### Charge

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One creature at least 2 squares away and within your Speed, in a straight line.

Move up to your Speed in a straight line toward the target. This movement provokes normally. If you end adjacent to it, make a Brawn roll against it. Weak: 2 physical damage, and push it 1 square. Standard: 3 physical damage, push it 2 squares, and it is Prone. Strong: 5 physical damage, push it 3 squares, and it is Prone. Resolve collisions normally. If you cannot move at least 2 squares, you cannot use this ability.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

Movement used in the approach spends your remaining movement for the turn, rather than granting a second full Speed allowance.

### Rampage

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** Self; a straight line up to your Speed.

This ability consumes your Main Action and your movement for the turn. Move up to your Speed in a straight line. Each enemy within 1 square of your path takes 2 physical damage and is pushed 1 square directly away from the path. Then make a Brawn roll against one enemy adjacent to you at the end of the movement. Weak: 3 physical damage. Standard: 4 physical damage, and it is Prone. Strong: 6 physical damage, and it is Prone. If you cannot move at least 2 squares in a straight line, you cannot use this ability. You cannot move again after the charge.

**Duration:** Immediate.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

A creature takes the path damage at most once per use. The endpoint attack is a separate damage event.

**Frequency:** Once per scene.
