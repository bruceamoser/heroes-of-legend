# A Shadow at the Heart

*Make a Guile melee attack that can Daze your target. Gain 2 Boons if you started Hidden.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Guile

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Guile melee attack. If you began Hidden, gain 2 Boons to the attack.

- **Weak:** 5 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 5 physical damage and Dazed 1.
- **Strong:** 7 physical damage and Dazed 2.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use an ordinary melee weapon (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.
