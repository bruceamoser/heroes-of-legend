# The Cannonball

*You back up three steps, lower your head, and throw yourself at them like a stone from a sling.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One creature within 4 squares.

Move up to 4 squares in a straight line toward the target, ignoring difficult terrain. If you end adjacent to it, make a Brawn roll. Weak: 4 physical damage to the target. You take 3 physical damage and are Prone. Standard: 8 physical damage, and the target is Prone. You take 2 physical damage. Strong: 12 physical damage, and the target is Prone and Dazed 1. You take 1 physical damage and are Dazed 1. If you cannot move at least 2 squares, you cannot use this ability. Resolve collisions normally.

**Duration:** Immediate; Prone and Dazed use their normal rules.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
