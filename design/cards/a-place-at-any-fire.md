# A Place at Any Fire

*You arrive a stranger and leave with someone calling you by name.*

- **ID:** a-place-at-any-fire
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Influence 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Guile
- **Target / range / shape:** A nonhostile gathering that can understand you; a short conversation
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Make 1 progress toward acceptance. | Make 2 progress and learn a useful local custom. | Make 3 progress and gain an introduction to a willing local contact. |

No forced friendship or compelled service. If ordinary hospitality is freely offered, take it without a roll. DA names the objective before a consequential attempt.
