# Breath of the Wyrmling

*Roll Knowledge to deal cold damage in three connected squares next to you. A Strong result also inflicts Freezing.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Fortitude

**Target / range / shape:** Every creature in three contiguous squares in a straight line beginning adjacent to you.

Make a Fortitude line spell attack.

- **Weak:** 3 fire damage.
- **Standard:** 3 fire damage and Burning 1.
- **Strong:** 5 fire damage and Burning 1.
