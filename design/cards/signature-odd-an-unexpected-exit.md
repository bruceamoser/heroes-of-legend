# The Unseen Procession

*You make the familiar world answer in an unfamiliar voice.*

## Rules

### Use Their Voice

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One enemy within 5 squares that can hear you; one willing ally within 5 squares that can see or hear you.

The spirit calls out in the target’s own voice from a point you choose within 5 squares. The target turns toward the voice. The ally may move up to 2 squares without provoking and gains 1 Boon to its next attack against that target before the start of your next turn.

**Duration:** Movement is immediate; the Boon expires when used or at the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 0 SP, usable once per scene. Turning toward the voice costs the target no action and does not compel any other behavior.

### The Unseen Procession

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** You and each ally within 5 squares; each enemy within 5 squares.

Once per scene, spectral figures fill the area. You and each ally gain 1 Boon to your next defense and may move 1 square without provoking. Each enemy gains 1 Bane to its next attack.

**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 0 SP. When the same attack would suffer both this spell’s attack Bane and its recipient’s defense Boon, apply only one of them.

### If you learned A Door Where There Wasn’t at level 3

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self; one nonmagical barrier within reach.

A door-sized opening appears in the barrier. Only you and your worn or carried gear can pass through it. The opening closes immediately after you pass through or at the end of your next turn.

**Duration:** Until you pass through or the end of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand for casting.

This is a spell costing 1 SP, usable once per scene. The barrier can be at most 1 square thick, and you must see a safe, empty destination on its far side. Movement through the opening uses ordinary movement. Cancellation target 3 closes the opening.
