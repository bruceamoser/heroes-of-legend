# Treat Elemental Injury

*You clean away the ash, frost or branching burn and tend the living tissue beneath it.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** One hour.

**Target / range / shape:** One wound on yourself or a willing patient listing Elemental treatment.

Make a Knowledge treatment roll.

- **Weak:** Add 1 recovery progress.
- **Standard:** Add 2 recovery progress.
- **Strong:** Add 3 recovery progress.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 1

**Type:** Skill / Recovery
