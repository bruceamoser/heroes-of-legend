# Borrow the Beast

*Fur rises along your arms, and the world fills with scent.*

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self.

Become a wolf, great cat, boar or similarly sized land beast for the scene. Keep your HP, wounds, attributes, occupied square and Speed; equipment folds into the form and grants no benefits until you return. You have no free hand for healing or potions. Returning costs a Maneuver. While transformed, your Main claw/bite attack rolls Brawn against one adjacent target: 3/3/5 physical damage; Standard pushes 1, Strong pushes 1 and knocks prone. Choose the beast’s appearance; use these rules for its capabilities. You cannot speak or cast spells in this form. Becoming a different form ends the previous one; its HP loss and conditions remain yours.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
