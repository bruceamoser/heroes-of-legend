# Basic Ranged

- **ID:** basic-ranged
- **Status:** Agreed universal permission; damage and ranged distances are proposals.
- **Type:** Attack
- **Tier / minimum level:** Universal
- **DP:** Not purchased
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Agility
- **Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 8 squares
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon, Bulk 1, and ammunition, Bulk 1; grants neither. Check ammunition after use.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 2 physical damage. | 3 physical damage. | 5 physical damage. |
