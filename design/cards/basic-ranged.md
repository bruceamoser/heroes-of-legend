# Basic Ranged

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Agility

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 8 squares

Choose your target and make the attack.

- **Weak:** 2 physical damage.
- **Standard:** 3 physical damage.
- **Strong:** 5 physical damage.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon, Bulk 1, and ammunition, Bulk 1; grants neither. Check ammunition after use.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Attack

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** basic-ranged · **Status:** Agreed universal permission; damage and ranged distances are proposals.
