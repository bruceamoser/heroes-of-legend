# Hook with the Shield

*You catch an ankle behind your shield’s lower edge, lift it and drive your opponent backward.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature.

NPC defense: Agility. This is an attack with no HP damage.

- **Weak:** Push 1 square directly away from you.
- **Standard:** Push 2 squares directly away from you.
- **Strong:** Push 2 squares directly away from you and apply Prone.

Use the normal forced-movement and collision rules. Stability reduces the push, not Prone.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a held shield. Grants one ordinary shield, Bulk 1, if absent.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2 and Arms 1

**Type:** Skill / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** hook-with-the-shield
