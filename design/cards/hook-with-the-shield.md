# Hook with the Shield

*Choose one adjacent enemy. Make a Brawn control attack. On a Standard result, push 2 squares away from you.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Brawn

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent enemy.

Make a Brawn control attack.

- **Weak:** Push 1 square away from you.
- **Standard:** Push 2 squares away from you.
- **Strong:** Push 2 squares away from you and apply Prone.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Use a held ordinary shield (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Guard 2 and Arms 1

**Type:** Skill / Control

**ID:** hook-with-the-shield
