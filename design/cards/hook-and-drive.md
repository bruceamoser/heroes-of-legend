# Hook and Drive

*Catch their guard and haul them into the place they least want to stand.*

- **ID:** hook-and-drive
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Attack
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Arms 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Brawn
- **Target / range / shape:** One adjacent creature
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** Requires and grants one ordinary melee weapon, Bulk 1, only if none is owned.
