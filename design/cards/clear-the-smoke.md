# Clear the Smoke

*You sweep a hand across your face. A clean breath of wind drives a channel through the hanging smoke.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three connected visible squares within 3 squares containing ordinary smoke or fog.

Disperse ordinary smoke or fog in the chosen squares until the end of your next turn.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 1
