# Clear the Smoke

*You sweep a hand across your face and drive a clean breath of air through the hanging smoke.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 3 connected squares within 3 squares.

Disperse ordinary smoke or fog in the chosen squares. For the duration it supplies no cover there. This does not disperse a magical area, neutralize poison or extinguish fire; smoke may flow back afterward. Visibility and Hidden follow the changed circumstances normally.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Storm 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility / Control

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** clear-the-smoke
