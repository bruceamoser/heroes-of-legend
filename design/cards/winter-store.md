# Winter Store

*You trace a rim of frost around a bundle; cold beads on its wrapping as the contents settle into a deep chill.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One unattended or willingly offered bundle within reach, up to Bulk 1.

Chill the bundle enough to preserve perishable food, herbs or remains for the duration. Time spent chilled does not count toward ordinary spoilage. This does not restore spoiled material, replenish an inventory die, provide a recipe ingredient or make a harmful substance safe. It causes no damage.

**Duration:** 24 hours; one bundle at a time.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Frost 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** winter-store
