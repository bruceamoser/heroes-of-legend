# Running Vault

- **ID:** running-vault
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 2
- **Action:** Maneuver
- **Attribute:** Agility
- **Target / range / shape:** A visible unoccupied landing within 3 squares
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Jump up to 1 square. | Jump up to 2 squares. | Jump up to 3 squares. |

Choose a landing within the rolled distance. If none is safe, remain at the origin. This crosses gaps, not walls; it is not flight. Ordinary jumping remains available without this card.
