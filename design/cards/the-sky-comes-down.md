# The Sky Comes Down

*Every creature in one visible 3-by-3 area within 6 squares. Make a Reason area spell attack. On a Standard result, deal 4 lightning damage. Push each target 1 square away from the center.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** Every creature in one visible 3-by-3 area within 6 squares.

Make a Reason area spell attack.

- **Weak:** 4 lightning damage.
- **Standard:** 4 lightning damage; push each target 1 square away from the center.
- **Strong:** 6 lightning damage and Prone; push each target 2 squares away from the center.

Choose the center target's push direction and the order of pushes.
