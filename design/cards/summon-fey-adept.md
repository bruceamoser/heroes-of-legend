# Call Fey: Adept

*A thorned doorway flowers beside you. Your guest steps through, brushes a petal from its sleeve, and waits.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible unoccupied placement within 3 squares that fits the chosen form's entire footprint.

Choose one Fey entry from the summon selection with a minimum tier of 3 or lower. Call one instance using its summon profile. Choose the form anew each casting. It remains for one scene.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Calling 2

**Type:** Magic / Summoning

**ID:** summon-fey-adept
