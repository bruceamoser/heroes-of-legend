# Reduce a Condition

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent creature

With a plausible method, reduce Bleeding, Burning, or Freezing by 1. This is not a general cure for all conditions. A persistent source can require a specific remedy.

**Duration:** Instant

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** Universal · **DP:** Not purchased · **Requirements to learn:** None

**Type:** Universal

**Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.

**ID:** reduce-a-condition · **Status:** Agreed foundation; any proposal is identified in the effect.
