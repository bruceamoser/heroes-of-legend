# Treat Radiant Injury

*You shade the injured eyes and work gently where the light has left its mark.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Time:** One hour.

**Target / range / shape:** One wound on yourself or a willing patient listing Radiant treatment.

Make a Reason treatment roll.

- **Weak:** Add 1 recovery progress.
- **Standard:** Add 2 recovery progress.
- **Strong:** Add 3 recovery progress.
