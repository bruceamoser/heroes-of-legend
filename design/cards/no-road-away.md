# No Road Away

*Choose a visible enemy within 3 squares when it begins voluntary movement. You and one willing ally within 3 squares who hears you. After the enemy finishes moving, you and the ally may each move up to 3 squares toward it without provoking.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** A visible enemy within 3 squares when it begins voluntary movement; you and one willing ally within 3 squares who hears you.

After the enemy finishes moving, you and the ally may each move up to 3 squares toward it without provoking.
