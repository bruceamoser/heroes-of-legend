# Shared Breath

*Your breath catches as theirs steadies.*

- **ID:** shared-breath
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic / Recovery
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Life 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 3 squares, including self
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Restore 3 HP. | Restore 6 HP. | Restore 9 HP. |

Moser’s law of conservation of Life energy: pay 1 HP before rolling; bypass resistance and remain above 0 HP. Requires a free hand. Life resistance reduces restoration, minimum 1 where HP is missing. Healing-harmed creatures take the rolled result as Life damage. No resurrection or removal of wounds/Grit loss.
