# Shared Breath

*Choose one willing creature within 3 squares, including yourself, or one creature in range harmed by healing. Pay 1 HP and make a Reason healing roll. On a Standard result, restore 6 HP.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One willing creature within 3 squares, including yourself, or one creature in range harmed by healing.

Pay 1 HP and make a Reason healing roll.

- **Weak:** Restore 3 HP.
- **Standard:** Restore 6 HP.
- **Strong:** Restore 9 HP.

**Tier / minimum level:** 1 · **DP:** 2 · **Requirements to learn:** Life 1

**Type:** Magic / Recovery
