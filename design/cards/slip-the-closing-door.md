# Slip the Closing Door

*As the enemy steps close, you duck through the gap beside them and vanish behind the nearest screen.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self, after a visible enemy ends voluntary movement adjacent to you.

Move up to 2 squares without provoking, ending behind actual cover or concealment. If no legal route and endpoint exist, you cannot use this card. This does not grant Hidden, pass through the enemy or interrupt its remaining actions. Forced movement does not trigger this card.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Subterfuge 2

**Type:** Skill / Movement / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** slip-the-closing-door
