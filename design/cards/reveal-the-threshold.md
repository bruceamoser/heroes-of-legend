# Reveal the Threshold

- **ID:** reveal-the-threshold
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Magic
- **Tier / minimum level:** 3
- **DP:** 4
- **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 2
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Reason
- **Target / range / shape:** A 3-by-3 visible area within 5 squares; 10 minutes examination
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Reveal one magical concealment’s presence, if any. | Also reveal its location. | Also reveal one activation or bypass requirement. |
