# Reveal the Threshold

*You sweep light along the old wall. At the false doorway it gathers into a bright seam, showing exactly where the deception begins.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Time:** One minute of examination.

**Target / range / shape:** One visible 3-by-3 area within 5 squares.

Make a Reason roll while examining the area.

- **Weak:** Locate one magical concealment relevant to your stated search, if one is present.
- **Standard:** Also reveal how it is activated or sustained.
- **Strong:** Also reveal one available way to bypass or end it, if a method exists.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 2

**Type:** Magic / Utility
