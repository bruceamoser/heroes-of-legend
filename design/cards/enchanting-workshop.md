# Enchanting Workshop

- **ID:** enchanting-workshop
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill
- **Tier / minimum level:** 7
- **DP:** 8
- **Requirements to learn:** Craft 3
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Knowledge
- **Target / range / shape:** One known special-item recipe; recipe interval
- **Duration:** Instant
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress. | 2 progress. | 3 progress. |

DA declares the objective, stakes and any cumulative target before the roll. Progress is not automatic completion. No routine roll when there is no consequence. Requires a special recipe and its printed materials. Also permits ordinary recipes listed under Trail Workshop; grants two of those ordinary recipe choices and missing craft tools. No special recipe is granted. This is campaign-dependent access and should not be a recommended starter purchase.
