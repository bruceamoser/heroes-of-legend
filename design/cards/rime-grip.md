# Rime Grip

*Frost closes around your enemy's wrists and ankles. Their next step breaks with a sound like cracking glass.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** One visible enemy within 5 squares.

Make a Knowledge spell attack.

- **Weak:** 3 cold damage.
- **Standard:** 3 cold damage and Slowed 1.
- **Strong:** 5 cold damage, Slowed 1 and Freezing 1.
