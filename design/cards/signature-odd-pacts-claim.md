# Pact’s Claim

*A supernatural compact lends your purpose a voice and a weapon.*

## Rules

### The Bargain

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** Reason

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within 8 squares.

**Magic:** Spell · **SP:** 0

Choose a patron or supernatural compact with the DA when learning this signature; your choices remain your own. Cast Pact Bolt for 2/3/5 force damage on Weak/Standard/Strong. Once per scene, before this roll, you may accept 1 Bane to defenses until the start of your next turn to gain 1 Boon on the bolt’s roll.

**Duration:** Attack immediate; accepted defense penalty until your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand.

### Pact’s Claim

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None

**Target / range / shape:** Your Pact Bolt.

Before rolling Pact Bolt, you may choose to bind as well as wound. On Standard reduce its damage by 1 and apply Slowed 1; on Strong reduce damage by 1 and apply Slowed 2. Weak deals the ordinary damage. Apply resistance and attack minimum normally; Slowed decays normally.

**Duration:** The bolt and ordinary condition decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.
