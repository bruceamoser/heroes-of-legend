# A Company of Reflections

*You sweep your hand past your companions, leaving a wavering double beside each one. The doubles lean into the path of approaching blows.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares.

Give each a flickering double until the start of your next turn. Each gains 1 Boon to its next defense in that time; the double then vanishes. Duplicates occupy no squares, block nothing, and make no attacks.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn; a next-roll benefit ends when used.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 2 and Guard 2

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Pay the printed action and costs each use. No earlier path card is required; any stated daily or scene limit also applies.
