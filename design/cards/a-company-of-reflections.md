# A Company of Reflections

*You sweep your hand past your companions, leaving a wavering double beside each one. The doubles lean into the path of approaching blows.*

## Rules

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares.

Give each target a flickering double until the start of your next turn. Each target gains 1 Boon to all defense rolls during that time. The doubles occupy no squares, block nothing, and make no attacks. Reapplying this card replaces your previous doubles.

**Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 2 and Guard 2

**Type:** Magic / Defense

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** a-company-of-reflections
