# A Company of Reflections

**Action:** Main Action · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares.

Give each a flickering double until your next turn. Each gains 1 Boon to its next defense in that time; the double then vanishes. Duplicates occupy no squares, block nothing, and make no attacks.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 3

**Type:** Magic

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action each time and follow any stated limit. No earlier path card is required.
