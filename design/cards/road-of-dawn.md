# Road of Dawn

*You spread a warm path of light at your companions’ feet, calling them out of the shadows and toward one another.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Up to 3 visible willing allies within 4 squares.

Reduce each ally’s temporary Frightened by 1, minimum 0. Each may then spend its Reaction to move up to 2 squares without provoking along a legal route to a free supported square. Starred fear remains. Resolve the moves one at a time.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Radiance 3

**Type:** Magic / Recovery / Movement

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.

**ID:** road-of-dawn
