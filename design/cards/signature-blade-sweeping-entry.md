# Sweeping Entry

*You arrive with your weapon already describing the space you mean to claim.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** Self; move toward a visible enemy.

Move 2 squares toward a visible enemy without provoking. Your next melee attack this turn may push its target 1 square after damage.

**Duration:** Movement is immediate; the push rider expires after your next melee attack this turn or at turn end.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Class Blade; minimum level 3

**Type:** Signature

**Limits:** Normal action economy; any additional limit is printed above.
