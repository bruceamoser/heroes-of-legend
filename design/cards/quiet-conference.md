# Quiet Conference

*Self and up to three visible willing allies within 3 squares. Link the participants for silent conversation for ten minutes. Each can send short spoken-length messages to the group without an action, in languages the listeners understand. A participant beyond 10 squares from you leaves the link until it returns.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** Self and up to three visible willing allies within 3 squares.

Link the participants for silent conversation for ten minutes. Each can send short spoken-length messages to the group without an action, in languages the listeners understand. A participant beyond 10 squares from you leaves the link until it returns.

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Mind 2 and Influence 1

**Type:** Magic / Utility

**Limits:** One conference at a time.
