# Keep the Door Closed

*You grip your companion's hand as their breath breaks. For one more heartbeat, they find the strength to hold on.*

## Rules

**Action:** Reaction · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One adjacent Dying player character with an existing death mark, immediately before a death mark would be added.

Before the new death mark is added, clear one existing death mark from the target.
