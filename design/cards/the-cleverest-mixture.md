# The Cleverest Mixture

*You shake the bottle once, judge the color, and offer it with the confidence of long practice.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** Maneuver to administer; one hour per crafting attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent willing creature.

**Use the tonic — Maneuver.** Use a prepared Clearing Tonic supply and a free hand. Another recipient spends a Reaction unless at 0 HP. Roll Knowledge, apply one result, then check the tonic’s inventory die.

- **Weak:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.
- **Standard:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 2 or Dazed by 1.
- **Strong:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 3 or Dazed by 2.

Choose one listed condition; reduce it no lower than 0. This restores no HP and does not remove starred conditions.

**Brew — one hour per attempt.** This card permits and grants Clearing Tonic and Travel Food recipes. With the recipe’s tools and ingredients, roll Knowledge: Weak raises one supply by 1 inventory step; Standard by 2; Strong by 3, capped by the recipe. Check carried ingredients after the attempt. No group crafting.

**Duration:** Condition reduction is immediate; crafted supplies persist until used.

**Equipment / acquisition:** One mixing kit, Bulk 1, if absent; both named recipes. No ingredients or finished tonic.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Craft 3 and Medicine 1

**Limits:** Repeatable; pay the action and any stated costs each use.
