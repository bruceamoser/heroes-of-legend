# The Cleverest Mixture

*Self or up to two adjacent willing or unconscious creatures. On a Standard result, reduce Poisoned by 3 or Dazed by 2.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Time:** Maneuver to administer; one hour per crafting attempt.

**Target / range / shape:** Self or up to two adjacent willing or unconscious creatures.

**Use the tonic:** Use a prepared Clearing Tonic supply for each recipient and make one Knowledge roll. Choose temporary Poisoned or Dazed for each.

- **Weak:** Reduce Poisoned by 2 or Dazed by 1.
- **Standard:** Reduce Poisoned by 3 or Dazed by 2.
- **Strong:** Reduce Poisoned by 4 or Dazed by 3.

Check the supply after each use.

**Brew:** Learn Clearing Tonic and Travel Food. With the Clearing Tonic recipe's tools and materials, make or refill one tonic supply to d4 in one hour, without a roll. Craft Travel Food with its full interval and materials, making a Knowledge roll for 1/2/3 inventory steps on Weak/Standard/Strong, up to d8.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A free hand; mixing kit (Bulk 1), supplied on learning if needed; prepared tonic when administering.
