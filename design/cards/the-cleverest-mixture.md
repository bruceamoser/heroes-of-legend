# The Cleverest Mixture

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** Knowledge

**Target / range / shape:** Self or one adjacent willing creature.

Use one prepared tonic supply and a free hand; check its inventory die after use. Recipient spends a Reaction unless at 0 HP. Grants a mixing kit if absent (Bulk 1), the Clearing Tonic and Travel Food recipes, and permits crafting them with 1/2/3 steps. This tonic restores no HP.

- **Weak:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 1.
- **Standard:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 2 or Dazed by 1.
- **Strong:** Reduce temporary Poisoned by 3 or Dazed by 2.

**Duration:** Immediate unless a duration is given above; conditions use normal decay.

**Equipment / acquisition:** No item needed unless named above.
