# Swing into Trouble

*The chandelier was never just decoration.*

- **ID:** swing-into-trouble
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** Athletics 1
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Agility
- **Target / range / shape:** A secured rope or hanging fixture, and a visible free landing within 4 squares
- **Duration:** As stated in the effect
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| Swing 2 squares. | Swing 3 squares and push one adjacent creature at the landing 1 square. | Swing 4 squares; push that creature 2 squares and knock it prone. |

Requires an actual usable support. No automatic extra attack. If the rolled distance cannot reach a safe landing, remain hanging at a reachable position; the DA states the risk before the roll.
