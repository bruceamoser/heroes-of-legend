# A Steady Hand at Dawn

*You return to the bedside with clean hands and the patience to do the small things properly.*

## Rules

**Action:** Passive · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** A wound you treat using a learned treatment card.

When your treatment roll is Weak, add 2 recovery progress instead of 1. Standard and Strong remain unchanged. The wound must list that treatment; time, tools and the once-per-wound-per-day limit still apply. This grants no treatment permission and restores no HP.

**Duration:** When resolving an eligible treatment.

**Equipment / acquisition:** None.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 3 · **DP:** 4 · **Requirements to learn:** Medicine 2

**Type:** Skill / Utility

**Limits:** Cannot exceed the treatment’s normal Strong recovery result or add a second treatment attempt.

**ID:** a-steady-hand-at-dawn · **Status:** Proposal for Bruce’s review; follows the approved utility and writing direction.
