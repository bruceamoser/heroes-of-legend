# Hold a Conversation

- **ID:** hold-a-conversation
- **Status:** Proposal — fresh design; not approved or balance-tested.
- **Type:** Skill / Utility
- **Tier / minimum level:** 1
- **DP:** 2
- **Requirements to learn:** None
- **Action:** Main Action
- **Attribute:** Guile
- **Target / range / shape:** One nonhostile creature you share a language with; one minute
- **Duration:** As specified
- **Limits:** Repeatable within the normal action economy.
- **Equipment / acquisition:** None.

## Resolution

| Weak | Standard | Strong |
|---|---|---|
| 1 progress toward a plausible conversation objective. | 2 progress and learn a subject it welcomes. | 3 progress and learn a subject it would rather avoid. |

Expert social practice, not compulsory speech or a replacement for roleplay. No card is required to talk, persuade or ask questions.
