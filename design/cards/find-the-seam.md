# Find the Seam

*Choose one visible creature within your ranged weapon attack's range. Your next ranged weapon attack against the target this turn treats its physical resistance and cover Banes as 0.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within your ranged weapon attack's range.

Your next ranged weapon attack against the target this turn treats its physical resistance and cover Banes as 0.

**Equipment / acquisition:** A ranged weapon.

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Marksmanship 3

**Type:** Skill / Attack
