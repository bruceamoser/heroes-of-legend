# Find the Seam

*You follow the shifting plates until the same small gap opens twice, then settle your aim on the next opening.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None — no roll

**Target / range / shape:** One visible creature within your ranged weapon’s attack range.

Your next ranged attack against that creature this turn treats its physical resistance as 1 lower, minimum 0. It also ignores partial cover’s 1 Bane, but not heavy cover. The target still requires an attack path. This grants no attack and does not alter any other damage type or later attack.

**Duration:** Until used or the end of this turn.

**Equipment / acquisition:** Requires a ranged weapon; grants no item.

---

**Tier / minimum level:** 7 · **DP:** 8 · **Requirements to learn:** Marksmanship 3

**Type:** Skill / Attack

**Limits:** Repeated uses do not improve the same attack again.

**ID:** find-the-seam
