# A Verse for Courage

*You strike the first line of the marching song. Your companion answers with a step forward and a grin they had almost forgotten.*

## Rules

**Action:** Maneuver · **Attribute:** None (no roll)

**Target / range / shape:** One willing ally within 3 squares who hears you.

The ally may move 1 square without provoking and gains 1 Boon to its next attribute roll before the start of your next turn.
