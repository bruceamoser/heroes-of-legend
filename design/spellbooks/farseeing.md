## Spells

### Nightsight

**ID:** spell:farseeing:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 10 minutes

See clearly in dim light within 6 squares. In complete darkness, perceive only shapes already illuminated by a light source.

### Clairvoyance

**ID:** spell:farseeing:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One visible point within 12 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Place an invisible stationary sensor. Choose sight or hearing when casting; spend a Main Action to use that sense from the sensor until your next turn starts. Your own corresponding sense is occupied during that interval.

### Locate Object

**ID:** spell:farseeing:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One familiar object within 100 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Sense its direction while holding an item of the same material. Choose a specific object you have handled or the nearest example of a common kind. Magical concealment expressly blocking divination stops the signal.

### Locate Creature

**ID:** spell:farseeing:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One creature you personally know within 100 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Sense its direction and whether it is moving closer or farther away. You need a personal possession it used within the last month. The reading follows the creature's current body.

### Arcane Eye

**ID:** spell:farseeing:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One sensor initially within 3 squares; remains within 12 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Create a tiny visible glimmering eye. Each upkeep moves it up to 4 squares through open passages. Spend a Main Action to see from it until your next turn begins. The sensor has 1 HP and uses your Agility for player-rolled defenses.

### Scrying

**ID:** spell:farseeing:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One consenting creature you personally know on the same plane
**Duration:** 1 minute

Receive an image and sound of the space within 3 squares of the subject. Weak gives one brief glimpse, Standard up to thirty seconds, Strong the full minute. The subject senses your presence and may end the connection.

### Long Ear

**ID:** spell:farseeing:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One point you have visited within 1 kilometer
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Place a stationary listening sensor. Spend a Main Action to listen from it until your next turn begins. It hears ordinary sounds within 6 squares of that point, subject to intervening barriers and noise.

### Watch II

**ID:** spell:farseeing:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Two fixed points within 12 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Mark two observation points. While within 12 squares of either, you may spend a Main Action to see and hear from one until your next turn begins. Switching points takes another Main Action. Each viewpoint follows normal line of sight and hearing.

### Watch True

**ID:** spell:farseeing:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to four fixed points you have visited within 1 kilometer
**Duration:** 8 hours

Prepare a network of stationary observation points. A Main Action lets you see and hear from one until your next turn begins. Choose a different point with another Main Action. Magical concealment and ordinary barriers operate at each viewpoint.

### Long Sense

**ID:** spell:farseeing:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One point you have visited within 10 kilometers
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Send sight and hearing to that fixed location. Spend a Main Action each round to observe until your next turn begins. While observing, your own sight and hearing rest at the remote point; touch can still alert you to nearby contact.

### Long Sense True

**ID:** spell:farseeing:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature you personally know on the same plane
**Duration:** 1 hour

Establish a remote sensory link. While both consent, use a Main Action to share what it sees and hears until your next turn begins. It continues using its own senses normally and may end the link at any time.
