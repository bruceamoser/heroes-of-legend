## Spell descriptions

### Nightsight
Improve your sight in ordinary darkness for a short time; this minor form does not penetrate magical darkness.

### Clairvoyance
Place a remote viewing or listening sensor at a qualifying location and concentrate on its observations.

### Locate Object
Sense the direction of a known object within a bounded range, subject to blocking materials and familiarity limits.

### Locate Creature
Seek a familiar creature or a defined creature type within a bounded range.

### Arcane Eye
Move an invisible visual sensor through accessible openings while concentrating.

### Scrying
Observe a known or linked subject from a distance under explicit connection and resistance rules.

### Long Ear
Move your listening point along a physically accessible route; it cannot simply pass through sealed walls.

### Watch II
Observe two prepared viewpoints under a shared concentration limit.

### Watch True
Maintain a prepared viewpoint with less active attention, still reserving the required sustained resource.

### Long Sense
Extend several senses to a remote point through a stronger version of the sensory working.

### Long Sense True
Establish an exceptional long-distance sensory connection to a defined destination; HOL fixes its scope rather than granting omniscience.
