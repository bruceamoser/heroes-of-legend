## Spells

### Landing

**ID:** spell:passage:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Reaction · when you fall
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** Until you land this turn

Reduce your effective falling distance by 3 squares and land upright if the landing supports you. Resolve any remaining distance normally.

### Misty Step

**ID:** spell:passage:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self; an unoccupied supported square you can see within 2 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Step through a curl of mist to the chosen square with your carried gear. This teleportation traverses the intervening space instantly.

### Blink

**ID:** spell:passage:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 1 minute or three uses

After an attack against you resolves, you may spend your Reaction to teleport to an unoccupied supported square within 2 squares that you can see. Each use consumes one of this spell's three blinks.

### Dimension Door

**ID:** spell:passage:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and one adjacent willing creature; a visible destination within 8 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Step together through a brief doorway. Arrive in unoccupied supported squares beside the destination with carried gear. If the destination lacks room, remain at your origin and receive a clear impression of the obstruction.

### Long Door

**ID:** spell:passage:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to three adjacent willing creatures; a known destination within 100 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Travel to a place you personally visited within the last day. Arrive in open supported squares at that location. If occupied or obstructed, arrive at the nearest safe space within 3 squares; if none exists, the ritual leaves you at your origin.

### Teleportation Circle

**ID:** spell:passage:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing creatures beside you; a known circle on the same plane
**Duration:** Instant

Consume 25 coins of chalk and incense to travel to a permanent circle whose exact inscription you have studied. Its receiving area must have space for the party. A blocked destination leaves you at the casting site and reveals the obstruction's nature.

### Passwall

**ID:** spell:passage:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent ordinary wall up to 2 squares thick
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Open a passage 1 square wide and 2 high through wood, earth or stone. At expiry, creatures inside move to the nearest open end before the material returns. The opening preserves the surrounding structure's ordinary support.

### Plane Shift

**ID:** spell:passage:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing adjacent creatures; one known planar anchor
**Duration:** Instant

Consume 100 coins of tuned incense and travel to an anchor you personally visited or whose attunement you possess. Arrive beside it in safe open space. A sealed or unavailable anchor leaves the party at its origin with an impression of the obstruction.

### Teleport

**ID:** spell:passage:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing adjacent creatures; a personally visited place within 100 kilometers
**Duration:** Instant

Travel with carried gear to the remembered place. Arrive in safe open squares within 3 squares of it. If that space is unavailable, remain at your origin and learn that the destination is obstructed.

### Gate — Passage

**ID:** spell:passage:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One portal within 3 squares linking a known planar anchor
**Duration:** 1 minute

Consume 100 coins of tuned incense to open a two-way doorway 2 squares wide. Any creature that can reach it may cross while open. The far end must be at an anchor you personally visited and currently have permission to open. You can close it with a Maneuver.

### Etherealness — Company

**ID:** spell:passage:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to three willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Shift the company into pale spirit forms. Each uses Etherealness's movement and interaction procedure and may return with a Maneuver. Your upkeep sustains all recipients; on expiry each returns to the nearest open square within 3 squares.
