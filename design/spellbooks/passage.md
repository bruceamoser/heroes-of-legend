## Spell descriptions

### Landing
Reduce the danger of a short fall. The free minor form has a fixed safe-drop limit and is not flight.

### Misty Step
Move yourself a short distance to a visible unoccupied destination using a clearly specified action-economy cost.

### Blink
Slip intermittently into an adjacent plane, with predictable HOL timing and clear restrictions on interaction.

### Dimension Door
Transport yourself and an eligible passenger to a specified destination within range, with explicit blocked-destination consequences.

### Long Door
Teleport along a defined short route, then gain the learned ability to cross intervening barriers; scope is fixed by this page.

### Teleportation Circle
Create a temporary connection to a known prepared destination circle.

### Passwall
Open a temporary passage through an eligible nonliving barrier without destroying an entire structure.

### Plane Shift
Travel with an eligible group to a specified plane using an appropriate attuned focus.

### Teleport
Transport a group over a great distance; familiarity and destination preparation determine the safe-use procedure.

### Gate — Passage
Create a major traversable connection between planes. This page grants the passage application rather than compulsory creature calling.

### Etherealness — Company
Carry a willing group into an adjacent incorporeal state under shared duration and return limits.
