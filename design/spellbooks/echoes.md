## Spells

### Origins

**ID:** spell:echoes:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended object held in your hands
**Duration:** Instant

Sense its principal ordinary material and whether it was grown, shaped, mined or assembled. This gives a useful starting point for further investigation.

### Past Visions

**ID:** spell:echoes:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent fixed place or unattended object
**Duration:** Instant

Receive 1/2/3 sensory impressions of its most recent notable event within the past day. The DA presents concrete sights, sounds or touches from that event.

### Power Lore

**ID:** spell:echoes:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent magical object
**Duration:** Instant

Learn 1/2/3 historical facts about how its enchantment was made or previously used. Facts concern its actual history, not current activation instructions unless the remembered event demonstrates them.

### Item Vision

**ID:** spell:echoes:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One object held throughout the ritual
**Duration:** Instant

See a brief scene involving its most recent owner within the last month. Weak reveals the owner's appearance, Standard also the scene's surroundings, and Strong also one spoken exchange of up to twenty words.

### Vision Guide

**ID:** spell:echoes:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent site
**Duration:** Instant

Ask how one visible trace came to be here. Add 1/2/3 progress toward a historical inquiry objective, target 3. At completion see the event that left that trace, if it occurred within the last year.

### Delving

**ID:** spell:echoes:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent object or ruin
**Duration:** Instant

Add 2/4/6 progress toward uncovering one named part of its history, target 6. At completion learn who made it, its intended purpose, and one subsequent event relevant to your question, reaching back up to a century.

### Vision Location

**ID:** spell:echoes:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One object held; its former location within 10 kilometers
**Duration:** Instant

See the surroundings where the object spent the longest uninterrupted time during the last month. Learn the direction and approximate distance from your present location if that place lies within range.

### Past Store

**ID:** spell:echoes:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held crystal or polished stone
**Duration:** Until replayed or 7 days pass

Store up to one minute of what you personally see and hear during the ritual. A later Main Action holding the stone plays the memory as a small shared image and sound. The stone holds one recording; you may keep three prepared stones.

### Legend Lore

**ID:** spell:echoes:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent historic object or site; 25 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** Instant

Ask one question about an important event in its history. Weak reveals one truthful clue, Standard a brief accurate account, and Strong the account plus a practical lead to evidence or another witness. The DA draws from the object's real past.

### Past Truth

**ID:** spell:echoes:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent site and one specific event within the past year
**Duration:** Instant

View up to ten minutes of that event from a fixed point at the site. The vision shows what happened there, including audible speech. Magical concealment that obscured the event remains part of the vision.

### Vision Behind — Ages

**ID:** spell:echoes:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent ancient site; 100 coins of rare incense consumed
**Duration:** Instant

Explore one named event up to a thousand years old. Add 3/5/7 progress toward target 7. At completion witness up to one hour from a fixed position and learn one surviving location or object connected to it. Each further attempt requires a new ritual and its costs.
