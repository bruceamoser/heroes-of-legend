## Spell descriptions

### Origins
Learn a broad impression of where a touched object originated. The minor page provides no detailed ownership history.

### Past Visions
View a recent event associated with a touched object or place, observing only what could have been perceived there.

### Power Lore
Discover clues to the origin of an item's magical power.

### Item Vision
Witness a significant event from a touched item's history.

### Vision Guide
Locate a significant moment to investigate before undertaking a historical vision.

### Delving
Learn about an item's construction and intended purpose without automatically identifying every magical power.

### Vision Location
Use a concrete description of an event to guide a subsequent vision toward the closest matching historical scene.

### Past Store
Preserve a person or place seen in a historical vision as an anchor for later investigation.

### Legend Lore
Recover significant lore about a genuinely important person, place or object through a prepared working.

### Past Truth
Investigate whether statements heard within a historical vision were knowingly false.

### Vision Behind — Ages
Reach much older events associated with a prepared historical anchor, with explicit age and viewing limits.
