## Spell descriptions

### Ray of Frost
Strike one target with cold and briefly hinder its movement. Damage and hindrance follow a fixed HOL result profile.

### Create or Destroy Water
Produce a bounded amount of ordinary water or remove it from an eligible container or area; never remove bodily fluids.

### Purify Food and Drink
Remove ordinary spoilage and poison from existing provisions. It does not create additional food or cure a poisoned creature.

### Water Breathing
Give a willing group the ability to breathe underwater for a defined duration.

### Water Walk
Allow willing creatures to move over a liquid surface without turning that surface into solid material.

### Control Water
Manipulate an existing body of water through an authored set of currents, lowered surfaces and flooding effects.

### Ice Storm
Pelt an area with ice and leave hazardous footing. The spell combines impact, cold and temporary terrain.

### Wall of Ice
Build a finite barrier of ice that can be broken or melted and leaves a dangerous cold breach.

### Cone of Cold
Project a broad cone of extreme cold with substantial damage and a clearly defined area.

### Freezing Sphere
Create a concentrated freezing burst that can also freeze a bounded surface of water.

### Tsunami
Raise a massive moving wave through a major working. Its travel, damage and ability to carry creatures are explicitly bounded.
