### Ray of Frost

**ID:** spell:water:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One creature within 8 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Send a needle of frost into the target. Deal 2/3/5 cold damage.

### Create or Destroy Water

**ID:** spell:water:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An unattended open container or ground within 1 square
**Duration:** Instant

Create up to 10 liters of ordinary clean water or remove that quantity of free-standing water. Water inside living creatures and sealed vessels stays unchanged.

### Purify Food and Drink

**ID:** spell:water:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to six meals and 10 liters of drinking water within 1 square
**Duration:** Instant

Remove ordinary spoilage, poison and disease from the chosen food and drink. The purified provisions remain subject to later contamination.

### Water Breathing

**ID:** spell:water:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Give the recipients the ability to breathe water as readily as air. Their movement and pressure tolerance remain those of their bodies.

### Water Walk

**ID:** spell:water:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to three willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Let the recipients walk on calm or flowing liquid as though on firm ground, using normal movement. Waves and hazardous liquids retain their ordinary movement and damage effects. A recipient may end its own benefit with a Maneuver.

### Control Water

**ID:** spell:water:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 4-by-4-square area of existing water within 8 squares, up to 2 squares deep
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Part, raise or redirect the water by up to 2 squares. Each upkeep can reshape it within that area. Swimming creatures move with the flow up to 2 squares each upkeep; a creature holding a fixed support stays in place. Released water returns to its natural course.

### Ice Storm

**ID:** spell:water:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature in a 2-square-radius burst within 10 squares
**Duration:** Instant; hail remains until your next turn ends

Hammer the area with sleet and hail. Deal 3/5/7 cold damage. The ground becomes difficult terrain until your next turn ends.

### Wall of Ice

**ID:** spell:water:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Six connected unoccupied squares within 8 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Raise a solid ice wall 2 squares high. Each square has 8 HP and physical resistance 1; fire damage uses its full value. A destroyed square opens a passage. Existing structures support the wall's weight.

### Cone of Cold

**ID:** spell:water:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature in a 4-square cone from you
**Duration:** Instant

Release a roaring blast of winter. Deal 4/6/9 cold damage and apply Freezing 1 to each creature in the cone.

### Freezing Sphere

**ID:** spell:water:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature in a 2-square-radius burst within 10 squares
**Duration:** Instant; frozen water lasts 10 minutes

Burst a globe of intense cold for 4/7/10 cold damage. Exposed surface water in the area freezes into a walkable crust; a creature can break one adjacent ice square with a Main Action and a suitable tool.

### Tsunami

**ID:** spell:water:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature in a 5-square-wide, 3-square-long area within 8 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Raise a surging wave from nearby water or conjured spray. Deal 4/6/8 physical damage, push each affected creature 2 squares away from you, and make it Prone on Strong. Stability applies to the push; the water drains away after resolution.
