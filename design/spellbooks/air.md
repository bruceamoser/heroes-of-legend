## Spells

### Elementalism — Air

**ID:** spell:air:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended object of Bulk 0 within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Stir a light breeze to turn pages, scatter loose dust, ring a small chime or move the object 1 square along a surface. The breeze can extinguish a candle-sized flame.

### Feather Fall

**ID:** spell:air:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Reaction · when a creature begins falling
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One visible creature within 6 squares
**Duration:** Until it lands, up to 1 minute

A cushion of air slows the descent to 2 squares each round. The recipient lands upright and unharmed from this fall if the destination supports it.

### Fog Cloud

**ID:** spell:air:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 2-square-radius cloud centered within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Gather an opaque bank of fog that blocks sight through its area. Strong wind disperses the cloud. Creatures may navigate by other senses and remembered positions.

### Gust of Wind

**ID:** spell:air:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature along a straight 6-square line from you
**Duration:** Instant

Drive a powerful gust along the line. Push each affected creature 1/2/3 squares away from you and disperse ordinary smoke or fog in the line. Stability applies to the push.

### Levitate

**ID:** spell:air:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Lift the recipient up to 3 squares above its starting height. Each upkeep can raise or lower it 2 squares, keeping it within 6 squares of you. It can pull itself along reachable solid surfaces using movement. On expiry it descends safely up to 3 squares, then falls normally.

### Lightning Bolt

**ID:** spell:air:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Every creature along an 8-square line from you
**Duration:** Instant

Send a crackling stroke along the line. Deal 3/5/7 lightning damage to each creature, once each.

### Fly

**ID:** spell:air:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

The recipient flies using its normal Speed and movement. On expiry it descends safely up to 3 squares; remaining unsupported distance is a normal fall.

### Wind Wall

**ID:** spell:air:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Six connected unoccupied squares within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Raise a wind barrier 3 squares high. Physical ranged attacks through it receive 2 Banes. The wall disperses ordinary airborne smoke and gas in its squares. Creatures pass through at an extra movement cost of 1 per wall square.

### Chain Lightning

**ID:** spell:air:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Up to four creatures within 10 squares; each within 3 squares of another target
**Duration:** Instant

Lightning leaps along your chosen chain. Roll once and deal 4/6/8 lightning damage to each target. Affect each creature once.

### Control Weather

**ID:** spell:air:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** The open sky within 1 kilometer of your ritual site
**Duration:** 8 hours

Gradually change local weather over the casting hour: clear or gather clouds, bring ordinary rain or snow, or raise or calm strong wind. Choose one weather change. Seasonal temperatures and sound shelter remain relevant. The change creates ordinary weather rather than attack damage.

### Wind Walk

**ID:** spell:air:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 8 hours

Transform the company and carried gear into windborne mist. Each travels at Speed 12, can rise freely, and can speak to fellow travelers. Returning to solid form or mist takes 1 uninterrupted minute. While mist, use movement and observation; handling objects and casting require solid form. On expiry, choose the nearest safe ground within 6 squares.
