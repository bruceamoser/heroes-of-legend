## Spell descriptions

### Elementalism — Air
Use the air application of Elementalism: stir dust, ripple cloth and move a small amount of air. Other elemental applications are not included.

### Feather Fall
Use a reaction to slow nearby falling creatures and carry them to a safe landing within the spell's limits.

### Fog Cloud
Fill a bounded area with obscuring mist. The fog conceals both allies and enemies and can be dispersed by wind.

### Gust of Wind
Drive a strong line of wind through an area, pushing against movement and dispersing light gases.

### Levitate
Raise one creature or object vertically. Horizontal movement requires some other means of propulsion.

### Lightning Bolt
Send a line of lightning through a sequence of targets and conductive surroundings.

### Fly
Grant a willing creature controlled flight for a limited duration, including an explicit procedure for the spell ending.

### Wind Wall
Create a wall of churning air that obstructs light missiles, flying debris and small airborne creatures.

### Chain Lightning
Strike one target with lightning and arc to several nearby secondary targets within a shared damage budget.

### Control Weather
Change regional weather gradually through a sustained major working; it is not an instant indoor attack.

### Wind Walk
Transform a willing traveling group into cloud-like forms with exceptional movement but restricted interaction.
