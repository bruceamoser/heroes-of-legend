## Spells

### Mending

**ID:** spell:shaping:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended mundane item of Bulk 1 or less in your hands
**Duration:** Instant

Rejoin a small clean break or tear while holding all its pieces together. Restore ordinary function to that part. Missing components and substantial reconstruction use repair projects.

### Grease

**ID:** spell:shaping:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Four connected ground squares within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Coat the ground in slippery magical grease. It becomes difficult terrain. A creature entering from outside may move carefully for 2 additional movement, or become Prone after moving into its first greasy square. Choose when entering.

### Enlarge/Reduce — Objects

**ID:** spell:shaping:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended nonmagical object of Bulk 2 or less within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Double or halve its dimensions and Bulk, rounding Bulk up with a minimum of 1. Its material and function remain ordinary. It returns to original size at expiry; allow enough space for either size when casting.

### Unlocking Ways

**ID:** spell:shaping:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One adjacent mundane lock
**Duration:** Instant

Guide its mechanism with invisible tools. Add 1/2/3 progress toward the lock's DA-set opening objective. The spell supplies delicate manipulation; an explicit magical seal uses its named remedy.

### Heat Metal — Forge

**ID:** spell:shaping:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended metal workpiece of Bulk 3 or less within reach
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Heat it evenly to forging temperature or cool it to a safe working temperature. Use suitable tools to shape it with ordinary Craft procedures. The workpiece cools naturally when the duration ends.

### Stone Shape — Craft

**ID:** spell:shaping:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent unattended stone mass up to one square in each dimension
**Duration:** Instant

Shape a simple brace, slab, stair or other solid construction component, preserving its volume. Fine mechanisms and final fitting use ordinary tools. Living stone creatures follow creature-targeting spells.

### Fabricate

**ID:** spell:shaping:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Prepared ordinary materials and tools beside you
**Duration:** Instant

Transform the materials into one mundane item or component of Bulk 6 or less whose construction you understand. Add 3/5/7 progress toward its ordinary crafting objective, using the normal recipe and material quantities. Finish fine details with the required tools.

### Earthen Transmutations

**ID:** spell:shaping:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent unattended square of earth or stone, up to 1 square deep
**Duration:** Instant

Turn loose earth into ordinary stone or ordinary stone into compacted earth. Preserve the material's volume. The DA identifies unsupported structures before casting; changing their support uses the normal collapse procedure.

### Passwall — Workshop

**ID:** spell:shaping:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent ordinary wall up to 2 squares thick
**Duration:** 1 hour

Make a work opening 1 square wide and 2 high through stone or earth. At expiry the opening closes; occupants emerge at the nearest end first. Secure ordinary supports before using it in a load-bearing wall.

### Meld Wall

**ID:** spell:shaping:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Two adjacent ordinary stone surfaces, each up to 4 squares in area
**Duration:** Instant

Join them into one sound wall or floor, or separate them along one clean seam. Supply adequate support and matching stone. The resulting structure has the strength and vulnerabilities of ordinary masonry.

### True Polymorph — Objects

**ID:** spell:shaping:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended nonmagical object of Bulk 6 or less; 100 coins of reagents consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

Reshape it into another mundane object of equal or smaller Bulk using stone, wood, glass or common iron. Specify its ordinary function and include all necessary parts. At expiry it returns to its original form. It provides ordinary material qualities rather than learned abilities or precious commodities.
