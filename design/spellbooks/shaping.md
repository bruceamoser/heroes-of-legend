## Spell descriptions

### Mending
Repair one small break or tear in an object when the necessary material is present; it does not restore magical properties.

### Grease
Cover a bounded surface or eligible object in slippery magical grease.

### Enlarge/Reduce — Objects
Change an unattended object's size under a fixed mass, duration and interaction limit.

### Unlocking Ways
Make progress on an eligible mundane lock. Higher complexity is a purchased capability; the spell does not automatically disarm traps.

### Heat Metal — Forge
Use controlled heating on unattended metal during skilled work; this workshop page grants no combat targeting.

### Stone Shape — Craft
Shape a bounded stone object using your own design and craft knowledge.

### Fabricate
Turn suitable raw materials into a finished object. Complex quality still requires the appropriate expertise.

### Earthen Transmutations
Convert between learned forms of stone, packed earth, loose earth and mud without creating precious material.

### Passwall — Workshop
Create a temporary opening through an eligible material during prepared construction or exploration.

### Meld Wall
Fuse touching stone structures along a bounded seam into a continuous construction.

### True Polymorph — Objects
Perform a major transformation of an eligible object; lasting change requires sustained work and explicit reversal rules.
