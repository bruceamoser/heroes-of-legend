## Spells

### Message

**ID:** spell:thought:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One creature you can see within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Whisper a message of up to ten words into the recipient's hearing. It may answer with up to ten words as part of the exchange. The message travels through ordinary ambient noise.

### Comprehend Languages

**ID:** spell:thought:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self; spoken words heard or writing touched
**Duration:** 1 hour

Understand the literal meaning of one language at a time. Read at an ordinary pace. Cultural references, ciphers and deliberate codes retain their own interpretation challenges.

### Detect Thoughts

**ID:** spell:thought:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 3 squares that you can see
**Duration:** Instant

Read surface impressions. Learn 1/2/3 immediate concerns or sensory impressions, chosen by the DA for an NPC or its player for a hero. The target senses the intrusion after the reading. Deep memories use stronger workings.

### Tongues

**ID:** spell:thought:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

The recipient can understand and speak the ordinary languages of creatures addressing it. Its voice remains its own. Reading and writing use languages it already knows.

### Sending

**ID:** spell:thought:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One creature you personally know on the same plane
**Duration:** Instant

Send up to twenty-five words through a mental thread. The recipient knows who sent it and may answer with twenty-five words. A creature that chooses silence still receives the message.

### Telepathic Bond

**ID:** spell:thought:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Create a shared mental conversation reaching 1 kilometer. Participants exchange words and simple images by intent while conscious. Each participant may leave the link at any time.

### Modify Memory

**ID:** spell:thought:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes of conversation; altered memory lasts until restored

Help the recipient replace or soften one memory covering up to five minutes of a real event. It chooses the replacement account with you. Add 1/2/3 progress toward target 3, with each further conversation attempt taking a Main Action and a new casting. At completion the altered recollection becomes its remembered account. Greater Restoration or a matching consensual working restores the original.

### Inner Thoughts

**ID:** spell:thought:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Read a thought connected to one question you speak aloud. Learn 1/2/3 relevant images or associations from its present thinking. The recipient knows the question and intrusion; responses remain bounded by its actual knowledge.

### Mind Scan

**ID:** spell:thought:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One willing adjacent creature throughout the ritual
**Duration:** Instant

Search one area of its remembered experience together. Add 2/4/6 progress toward a DA-set recollection objective. The subject chooses which memories to explore and can halt the ritual.

### Mind Probe

**ID:** spell:thought:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Press into a guarded thought. Deal 3/5/7 mental damage and learn one immediate intention the target is considering. For a hero, its player states that intention. The target recognizes your intrusion.

### Thought Steal

**ID:** spell:thought:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing adjacent creature
**Duration:** 1 hour

Borrow one ordinary language or one factual recollection the subject agrees to share. You can use it for the duration; the subject retains it. Record the exact knowledge gained. Learned abilities and SP remain with their owner.
