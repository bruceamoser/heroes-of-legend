## Spell descriptions

### Message
Send a short whispered message to one nearby subject and hear its reply. It does not read unspoken thoughts.

### Comprehend Languages
Understand the literal meaning of spoken or written language without automatically understanding ciphers or cultural context.

### Detect Thoughts
Read surface thoughts and attempt a deeper probe within a clearly defined scope; resistance matters.

### Tongues
Let a creature understand speech and make its own speech understood across language barriers.

### Sending
Deliver a short message to a familiar creature over a great distance and receive a brief reply.

### Telepathic Bond
Connect willing participants in a shared mental conversation without giving access to their private memories.

### Modify Memory
Alter a bounded remembered event under explicit resistance, duration and restoration rules.

### Inner Thoughts
Probe the reasoning behind a subject's immediate surface thoughts; it does not reveal an entire life history.

### Mind Scan
Search conscious memories for a bounded subject through sustained investigation.

### Mind Probe
Reach deeper stored memories through a major resisted working with explicit limits and consequences.

### Thought Steal
Remove a specific accessible memory through a resisted working. The page defines restoration and what remains discoverable.
