## Spells

### Minor Illusion

**ID:** spell:perception:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One point within 6 squares; a sound or still image fitting one square
**Duration:** 1 minute

Create a simple sound or stationary visual image. An image is insubstantial; a sound can be as loud as ordinary speech. Physical interaction reveals the illusion. You can keep one Minor Illusion at a time.

### Silent Image

**ID:** spell:perception:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An image fitting a 2-by-2-square space within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Shape a silent moving image. Each upkeep may move it up to 3 squares and alter its gestures. Physical interaction reveals its insubstantial nature; observers may examine it using the shared illusion procedure.

### Disguise Self

**ID:** spell:perception:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 1 hour

Change your apparent face, clothing and proportions within your existing size. Your voice and tangible shape remain physical. Touch reveals mismatches; close inspection uses the shared illusion procedure.

### Mirror Image

**ID:** spell:perception:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 1 minute or until three attacks resolve against you

Create three shifting doubles. Gain 1 Boon to defenses against attacks that depend on seeing you. After each such attack resolves, one double vanishes. The remaining doubles provide the same single Boon.

### Invisibility

**ID:** spell:perception:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Make the recipient and its carried gear Invisible. Attacking or casting another spell ends this casting's invisibility after that action resolves. Other senses can still locate the recipient.

### Major Image

**ID:** spell:perception:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An image fitting a 3-by-3-square space within 8 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Create a moving illusion with sound, scent and harmless temperature. Each upkeep can move it 3 squares and change its performance. Touch reveals its insubstantial nature. It may conceal a real feature visually; physical terrain remains as it was.

### Greater Invisibility

**ID:** spell:perception:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Make the recipient and carried gear Invisible. It may continue attacking and casting while this effect lasts. Attacking still reveals its location and ends Hidden normally.

### Hallucinatory Terrain

**ID:** spell:perception:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A fixed 10-by-10-square outdoor area within 10 squares
**Duration:** 8 hours

Change the area's apparent plants, surfaces and small terrain features. Buildings and creatures retain their appearance. Real footing remains physical; touch or travel through a mismatch reveals it, and observation uses the illusion procedure.

### Mislead

**ID:** spell:perception:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and an illusory double within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Become Invisible while creating a visible, speaking double of yourself. Each upkeep may move the double 3 squares. You may see and hear from either your own position or the double's, choosing during upkeep. Attacking ends your invisibility after resolution but leaves the double until sustain ends.

### Seeming

**ID:** spell:perception:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to six willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Give each recipient an apparent humanoid disguise using Disguise Self's limits. Design each disguise when casting. Each recipient may dismiss its own disguise with a Maneuver.

### Programmed Illusion

**ID:** spell:perception:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One fixed 3-by-3-square space within reach
**Duration:** 24 hours or until triggered

Prepare a visual and audible performance lasting up to 1 minute. State a visible or audible trigger within 6 squares when casting. The first matching event plays the recorded illusion once. Physical interaction reveals it; afterward the spell ends.
