## Spell descriptions

### Minor Illusion
Create one small sound or static visual illusion. It has no physical substance and does not deal damage.

### Silent Image
Create and move a visual illusion while concentrating; it contains no sound or tactile substance.

### Disguise Self
Change your apparent clothing and appearance without changing your physical body.

### Mirror Image
Create shifting duplicates around yourself that interfere with attacks through a bounded defensive profile.

### Invisibility
Conceal a subject from ordinary sight until the effect ends or its stated breaking action occurs.

### Major Image
Create a larger moving illusion incorporating several senses while retaining no physical substance.

### Greater Invisibility
Maintain invisibility through actions that would end the lesser spell, at higher cost and limited duration.

### Hallucinatory Terrain
Disguise a landscape's appearance without physically changing its obstacles or hazards.

### Mislead
Become concealed while projecting a mobile illusory double.

### Seeming
Apply coordinated illusory disguises to a willing group.

### Programmed Illusion
Prepare a detailed illusion with a fixed trigger and sequence of behavior.
