## Spells

### Minor Illusion

**ID:** spell:perception:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One point within 6 squares; a sound or still image fitting one square
**Duration:** 1 minute

Create a simple sound or stationary visual image. An image is insubstantial; a sound can be as loud as ordinary speech. Physical interaction reveals the illusion. You can keep one Minor Illusion at a time.

### Silent Image

**ID:** spell:perception:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An image fitting a 2-by-2-square space within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Shape a silent moving image. A Maneuver may move it up to 3 squares and alter its gestures. Physical interaction reveals its insubstantial nature; observers may examine it using the shared illusion procedure. Without directing it, the image stays in place and repeats its last simple performance.

### Disguise Self

**ID:** spell:perception:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 1 hour

Change your apparent face, clothing and proportions within your existing size. Your voice and tangible shape remain physical. Touch reveals mismatches; close inspection uses the shared illusion procedure.

### Mirror Image

**ID:** spell:perception:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 1 minute or until three attacks resolve against you

Create three shifting doubles. Gain 1 Boon to defenses against attacks that depend on seeing you. After each such attack resolves, one double vanishes. The remaining doubles provide the same single Boon.

### Invisibility

**ID:** spell:perception:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Make the recipient and its carried gear Invisible. Attacking or casting another spell ends this casting's invisibility after that action resolves. Other senses can still locate the recipient.

### Major Image

**ID:** spell:perception:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An image fitting a 3-by-3-square space within 8 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Create a moving illusion with sound, scent and harmless temperature. A Maneuver can move it 3 squares and change its performance. Touch reveals its insubstantial nature. It may conceal a real feature visually; physical terrain remains as it was. Without directing it, the image stays in place and repeats its last simple performance.

### Greater Invisibility

**ID:** spell:perception:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Make the recipient and carried gear Invisible. It may continue attacking and casting while this effect lasts. Attacking still reveals its location and ends Hidden normally.

### Hallucinatory Terrain

**ID:** spell:perception:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A fixed 10-by-10-square outdoor area within 10 squares
**Duration:** 8 hours

Change the area's apparent plants, surfaces and small terrain features. Buildings and creatures retain their appearance. Real footing remains physical; touch or travel through a mismatch reveals it, and observation uses the illusion procedure.

### Mislead

**ID:** spell:perception:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and an illusory double within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Become Invisible while creating a visible, speaking double of yourself. A Maneuver may move the double 3 squares and choose whether you see and hear from your own position or the double’s until your next turn. Without directing it, the double stays still and repeats its last simple performance; your senses remain at your own body. Attacking ends your invisibility after resolution but leaves the double until expiry. Keep one Mislead casting active.

### Seeming

**ID:** spell:perception:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to six willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Give each recipient an apparent humanoid disguise using Disguise Self's limits. Design each disguise when casting. Each recipient may dismiss its own disguise with a Maneuver.

### Programmed Illusion

**ID:** spell:perception:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One fixed 6-by-6-square space within reach
**Duration:** 7 days

Prepare a moving illusion with sound, scent and harmless temperature, performing a sequence lasting up to 5 minutes. Set one visible or audible trigger within 12 squares when casting. A matching event begins the performance; after it finishes, the illusion waits 10 minutes before it can trigger again. The scene stays within its prepared area and follows the same recorded sequence each time. Physical interaction reveals its insubstantial nature; observers may examine it using the shared illusion procedure. Keep one Programmed Illusion active; casting another ends the first.
