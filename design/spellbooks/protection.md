## Spell descriptions

### Sacred Flame
Call a small radiant flame onto one visible target. HOL makes it a dependable 0-SP attack with a defined cover interaction.

### Shield
Use a reaction to raise a brief force defense against incoming harm, with a bounded protection value.

### Mage Armor
Surround a willing unarmored creature with a sustained magical defense that does not stack freely with armor.

### Bladeturn
Use a reaction to turn aside one perceived melee attack within the learned range.

### Protection from Evil and Good
Protect a willing subject against a defined set of supernatural creature types and intrusive effects.

### Protection from Energy
Protect one willing subject against a learned energy type for a limited duration.

### Death Ward
Protect a willing creature against one defined fatal event; it does not erase previous death strikes or scars.

### Warding Bond
Link willing participants so protection and shared harm follow one explicit combined damage budget.

### Globe of Invulnerability
Maintain a protective area that excludes a defined range of lesser hostile magic.

### Mind Blank
Shield one subject from specified mental intrusion and divination for a bounded duration.

### Prismatic Wall
Raise a layered magical barrier whose distinct defenses, hazards and methods of removal are explicitly authored.
