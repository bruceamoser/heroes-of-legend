## Spell descriptions

### Poison Spray
Project a short-range cloud of poison at one target. Its attack profile is fixed; immunity and protection still matter.

### Ray of Sickness
Damage a target with a sickening ray and apply a defined temporary poison effect.

### Ray of Enfeeblement
Weaken one target's physical attacks through a sustained effect with an explicit escape condition.

### Protection from Poison
Neutralize an eligible poison and protect a willing creature against further poisoning for a limited duration.

### Vampiric Touch
Drain life through repeated touch attacks while maintaining the spell. Healing cannot exceed the actual life taken or bypass recovery limits.

### Bestow Curse
Place one authored curse on a target. Its effect, duration and removal conditions are known when the page is learned.

### Blight
Wither a living target with destructive magic, especially effective against plants.

### Contagion
Afflict a target with a specified supernatural disease whose onset, effects and removal are explicit.

### Harm
Inflict severe bodily deterioration on one creature without using an automatic-death rule.

### Finger of Death
Deliver a potent necrotic attack. Any resulting undead servant uses a strict control and action budget.

### Circle of Death
Release a large-area pulse of destructive life-draining magic with substantial cost and collateral danger.
