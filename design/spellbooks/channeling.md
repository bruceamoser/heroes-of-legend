# Talven’s Book of Channeling
Discipline: Spellcraft
List: Channeling
ID: channeling
Endpoint: Master Channels

Draw, give and redirect magical power. Transfer only existing SP; casting costs are additional. Never exceed the donor’s available SP or the recipient’s maximum. Unwilling interactions use the player-facing HOL roll; an NPC never rolls resistance. Final amounts and action profiles remain draft.

## Spell descriptions

### Power Shock
Attack cantrip: strike one creature with a pulse of raw power. Use the normal HOL attack result for damage; final damage and condition profile TBD. This is the reliable attack page, not a free source of SP.

### Give Power
Send some of your existing SP to a touched willing creature, paying the casting cost separately. Rolemaster’s Channeling skill transfers power points with efficiency losses; HOL will use a bounded transfer profile instead of two percentage calculations.

### Draw Power
Receive existing SP from a touched willing donor. Rolemaster supports willing power-point transmission; HOL makes receiving a learned spell page without requiring a second skill roll. A donor cannot give what they do not have.

### Siphon Power
Drain existing SP from an unwilling creature into your reserve. Use HOL’s player-facing result to determine transfer or progress against resistance. No SP from an empty target; no HP damage is implied. This is a deliberate mana variant of life drain.

### Distant Channel
Give or receive SP through a visible willing partner at range. Source Channels III extends the spell levels transmitted; HOL instead extends the transfer’s reach.

### Channel Spell
Transmit one eligible known spell to a prepared willing ally for immediate release. Pay its normal SP and this channel’s cost. The ally neither learns nor stores the spell. Exact eligible scope remains draft.

### Directed Siphon
Draw SP from an unwilling target and send the recovered amount directly to a chosen recipient, willing or unwilling. Resistance affects progress; transfer cannot overfill a reserve or cause overflow damage.

### Greater Channel
Move a larger bounded amount of existing SP in one casting, giving or drawing between creatures. Unwilling donors and recipients require HOL resolution; an empty donor provides nothing.

### Lord Channels
Immediately learned advanced spell: coordinate several willing donors to fund one known spell. Contributions and casting overhead are paid once from existing reserves; participants do not gain spell knowledge.

### Rechanneling
Immediately learned advanced spell: intercept an active magical transfer and redirect its spell or SP to a valid recipient. Apply HOL progress against its controller; never duplicate the intercepted resource.

### Power Bridge
Immediately learned advanced spell: establish a sustained conduit between two creatures, willing or unwilling. Define resistance, throughput and maintenance in the final profile; every point received must leave the source. The source spell contacts a deity; this is a narrower HOL adaptation.
