## Spell descriptions

### Fire Bolt
Hurl a small bolt of fire at one target. HOL keeps the dependable attack and resolves damage through Weak, Standard and Strong results.

### Boil Liquid
Bring an existing container of liquid to a boil. Heating takes time; it does not instantly boil a creature's blood.

### Burning Hands
Release a short fan of fire from your hands. Its identity is close-range area damage, rather than a longer Fire Bolt.

### Heat Metal
Heat an existing metal object. Worn armor and held weapons make this a pressure spell with a sustained cost.

### Scorching Ray
Launch several separate rays of fire. The page specifies their combined damage budget and permitted targets.

### Wall of Fire
Create a dangerous line or ring of flame. Crossing it and remaining beside it have explicit, bounded consequences.

### Fireball
Detonate a visible point in a burst of fire. Allies in the area are at risk; the roll controls damage, not whether the blast exists.

### Fire Shield
Surround yourself with a thermal ward that protects against one opposing element and burns creatures striking you nearby.

### Fire Storm
Shape several connected areas of violent fire into one major battlefield effect.

### Delayed Blast Fireball
Hold a growing fireball at a visible point before releasing it. Delay, interruption and safe handling need explicit limits.

### Incendiary Cloud
Create a moving cloud of smoke and searing heat that threatens a large area while maintained.
