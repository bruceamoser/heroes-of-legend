## Spell descriptions

### Resistance
Give one willing creature a small, short-lived defense against a specified imminent danger. Repeated uses do not stack.

### Sanctuary
Protect a nonaggressive subject by interfering with direct hostile targeting; harmful actions end the shelter.

### Quiet Ways
Create a small zone through which sound does not pass, including the sounds allies may want to make.

### Tiny Hut
Create a temporary enclosed refuge for a bounded resting group, with explicit entry and environmental limits.

### Private Sanctum
Protect a prepared place against specified observation and intrusion methods.

### Hallow
Consecrate a place against specified supernatural threats through a substantial prepared working.

### Guards and Wards
Prepare layered obstacles and protective effects throughout a bounded structure using a fixed authored configuration.

### Antilife Shell
Maintain a barrier against eligible living creatures while leaving clear exceptions for other threats.

### Forbiddance
Protect a prepared site against specified planar travel and supernatural intruders through a major working.

### Magnificent Mansion
Create a temporary extradimensional refuge with a defined entrance, occupancy and expiry procedure.

### Prismatic Wall — Sanctum
Establish a prepared layered barrier as part of a refuge, with the same explicit breach methods as the source-inspired working.
