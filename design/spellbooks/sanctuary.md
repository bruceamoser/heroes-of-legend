## Spells

### Resistance

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Until your next turn ends

Choose fire, cold, lightning or poison. Grant resistance 1 to that type, using the highest matching resistance.

### Sanctuary

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Give the recipient 1 Boon to defenses while it attends to healing, movement or other peaceful actions. Its first attack or harmful spell ends the ward after that action resolves.

### Quiet Ways

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to three willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Soften footfalls and rustling gear. Recipients gain 1 Boon to sneaking that depends on quiet movement. Speech and deliberate noise remain audible.

### Tiny Hut

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A fixed 2-square-radius dome on clear ground beside you
**Duration:** 8 hours

Create a weatherproof shelter for six Medium creatures and their ordinary gear. It has an openable entrance and translucent walls. Ordinary rain, snow and wind stay outside; occupants can leave and return. The shelter provides cover but no resistance to attacks. Choose a safe site for overnight recovery.

### Private Sanctum

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One fixed room up to 6 by 6 squares beside you
**Duration:** 8 hours

Seal ordinary sound inside the room and make its windows opaque from outside. Remote magical sensors report only a blank space unless their caster completes a cancellation objective against this working. Doors remain usable by ordinary means.

### Hallow

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A fixed 4-square-radius site beside you; 50 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

Consecrate the site. Willing occupants gain 1 Boon to defenses against undead and spirits while inside. Ordinary remains there resist decay for the duration. Choose the site's visible symbol when casting.

### Guards and Wards

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A building of up to six connected rooms beside you
**Duration:** 8 hours

Ward the rooms with soft warning bells. Name up to six guests. When another creature opens an exterior door or window, you hear its location while within 100 squares. It receives Slowed 1 on first entry, once per creature. Doors remain physically operable and warnings persist even after that slowing ends.

### Antilife Shell

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 1-square-radius ring centered on you
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

A barrier of repelling force surrounds you. A living creature crossing its boundary spends 2 extra movement and becomes Slowed 1, once per round. The ring moves with you; moving it over a creature applies the same effect rather than pushing it automatically.

### Forbiddance

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A fixed 6-by-6-square site beside you; 100 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

Seal the site against teleportation and planar passage. A travel spell entering or leaving arrives at the nearest safe boundary square instead. Ordinary movement remains available. A successful cancellation objective ends the seal.

### Magnificent Mansion

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One doorway on an adjacent solid surface
**Duration:** 8 hours

Open a comfortable refuge with six furnished rooms, clean water and bedding for twelve. The entrance remains fixed and visible; you may open or close it with a Maneuver while beside it. At expiry occupants and their possessions emerge into safe space beside the original entrance. Furnishings remain part of the refuge. Food is brought by the guests.

### Prismatic Wall — Sanctum

**ID:** spell:sanctuary:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** A fixed ring of up to twelve connected unoccupied squares beside you
**Duration:** 1 hour

Create a luminous boundary 2 squares high and name up to six guests. Others crossing take 3/5/7 radiant damage, set by your ritual roll, and Frightened 1 with the boundary as source, once per creature per round. Named guests cross freely. The ring blocks sight through its colors.
