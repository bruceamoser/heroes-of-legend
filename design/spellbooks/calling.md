## Spell descriptions

### Familiar
Begin a bond with an existing small willing animal through repeated care and attunement. The free page does not create replacement animals or an army.

### Find Familiar
Call a minor familiar with defined senses, assistance and replacement costs; it does not gain unrestricted combat actions.

### Unseen Servant
Call an invisible helper for simple noncombat tasks with fixed strength, reach and duration.

### Summon Animal
Call an eligible animal for a bounded purpose; disposition and control follow the authored spell profile.

### Phantom Steed
Create a temporary supernatural mount with clear fragility and travel limits.

### Conjure Animals
Manifest an animal-themed supernatural effect with a fixed shared action and damage budget.

### Conjure Elemental
Manifest elemental power at a chosen location under a maintained, bounded effect.

### Summon Dragon
Call a draconic spirit with a fixed profile, finite durability and a shared command economy.

### Conjure Celestial
Manifest a potent celestial presence with a defined protective and offensive area.

### Planar Ally
Request service from a powerful otherworldly being. Terms and payment matter; summoning does not compel agreement.

### Gate — Calling
Open Gate's calling application to invite or draw a specified being. The page does not grant general free planar travel.
