## Spells

### Familiar

**ID:** spell:calling:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing ordinary Small animal beside you
**Duration:** Until either ends the bond

Form a companionship bond through food and care. You sense whether the animal is calm, frightened or hurt while within 6 squares. It remains an ordinary independent animal with its own needs. You may keep one Familiar bond; forming another releases the first.

### Find Familiar

**ID:** spell:calling:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open square within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes, with commands

Shape a familiar using the Raven or Cave Bat summoned profile. Apply the normal six-square tether and command rules. Give its immediate Main command when it appears. It returns to energy when its duration ends.

### Unseen Servant

**ID:** spell:calling:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open square within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes, with commands

Call a Clay Helper energy form from Summoned Companions. Its appearance is a faint translucent outline. Use its printed profile, immediate Main command, six-square tether and continuing commands for chores and movement.

### Summon Animal

**ID:** spell:calling:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open square within 3 squares

### Phantom Steed

**ID:** spell:calling:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open square within 3 squares

### Conjure Animals

**ID:** spell:calling:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open space within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute, with commands

Call one Animal profile rated 3 or lower. It may appear as a small pack moving as one form: use that single profile, footprint and shared HP. Give one immediate Main command and use the normal command and tether rules.

### Planar Ally

**ID:** spell:calling:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One named otherworldly being with which you have an established relationship
**Duration:** Up to 10 minutes of conversation

Invite the being to negotiate one specific service. The DA presents its response, terms and requested payment. Agreement establishes when and how it can assist through its actual capabilities. Any creature that arrives is an independent guest, with the agreed role and duration of service.

### Gate — Calling

**ID:** spell:calling:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One named willing being on another plane; arrival within 3 squares of you
**Duration:** 1 minute

Send an invitation naming the destination and purpose. If accepted, open a doorway through which that being and its carried possessions may enter once. The door closes after its arrival or after one minute. The guest acts independently; agree on its service through conversation.
