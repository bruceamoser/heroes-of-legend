## Spells

### Druidcraft

**ID:** spell:growth:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One ordinary plant or patch of soil within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Open a flower, ripen one already-growing fruit, make a seed sprout, or read whether rain is likely here in the next day. The growth affects one hand-sized part of the plant.

### Entangle

**ID:** spell:growth:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** Every grounded creature in a 2-square-radius area within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Roots grasp through soil. Apply Slowed 1/2/3 to affected creatures. The area is difficult terrain while sustained. Each upkeep renews Slowed 1 on creatures still in the area; ordinary decay applies after they leave.

### Goodberry

**ID:** spell:growth:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to six ordinary berries held by you
**Duration:** 24 hours

Each berry nourishes one creature for one meal when eaten. A berry is consumed by eating it and retains its ordinary Bulk. Food and hunger use this nourishment; HP recovery follows healing spells and treatment.

### Speak with Plants

**ID:** spell:growth:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One ordinary plant within 1 square
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Converse through impressions of light, weather, touch and nearby movement experienced during the past day. Ask up to three questions. The DA answers from what this plant could sense.

### Spike Growth

**ID:** spell:growth:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** A 2-square-radius patch of soil within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Raise sharp growth that makes the area difficult terrain. Roll when cast to set physical damage at 2/3/5. Each creature takes that damage the first time each round it moves through at least one affected square. The growth is visible on inspection.

### Plant Growth

**ID:** spell:growth:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 6-by-6-square patch of existing ordinary vegetation within 6 squares
**Duration:** 24 hours

Thicken the plants into difficult terrain. You may leave a connected walking path clear when casting. Alternatively, tend the same area for healthy seasonal growth; that use improves its next ordinary harvest rather than creating immediate food.

### Tree Skin

**ID:** spell:growth:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Bark covers the recipient's skin. Grant physical resistance 2 and Stability 1. Use the highest applicable resistance and Stability.

### Tree Stride

**ID:** spell:growth:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self; one adjacent living tree large enough to admit you
**Duration:** Instant

Step through the tree and emerge beside another living tree you can see within 12 squares. Carry your worn and held equipment. Choose an unoccupied destination with firm support.

### Awaken

**ID:** spell:growth:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing ordinary animal or living tree beside you; 100 coins of rare incense consumed
**Duration:** Permanent

Awaken a speaking mind with one language you know. The being keeps its body, natural senses and ordinary capabilities. It becomes an independent person; agree on any companionship or service through conversation. You can complete this awakening once between safe overnight rests.

### Wall of Thorns

**ID:** spell:growth:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** Six connected unoccupied ground squares within 8 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Grow a thorn wall 2 squares high. Moving through one square costs 3 movement and deals 3/5/7 physical damage, set by your casting roll, once per creature each round. Each wall square has 8 HP; fire damages it normally.

### Transport via Plants

**ID:** spell:growth:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing creatures beside a living tree
**Duration:** Instant

Travel through the tree to another living tree you personally touched within the past month, up to 10 kilometers away on the same plane. Everyone emerges in safe adjacent squares with carried gear. The destination tree must remain alive and large enough to admit a traveler.
