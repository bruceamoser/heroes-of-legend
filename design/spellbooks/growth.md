## Spell descriptions

### Druidcraft
Perform a minor natural sign: forecast nearby weather, open a blossom or create a harmless sensory effect.

### Entangle
Cause roots and vines to hinder creatures in a bounded area. Escape uses a defined progress procedure.

### Goodberry
Enchant a small supply of berries that nourish and provide limited healing. Their creation consumes SP and obeys recovery limits.

### Speak with Plants
Communicate with nearby plants about their limited experience and ask for modest cooperation.

### Spike Growth
Transform a patch of ground into a thorny hazard whose damage is tied to movement through it.

### Plant Growth
Thicken existing vegetation or improve cultivated land over time. The spell does not create mature food from nothing.

### Tree Skin
Give a willing subject bark-like protection while preserving its living form and movement.

### Tree Stride
Move between suitable living trees within a bounded connected route.

### Awaken
Grant awareness to an eligible plant through a costly prepared working; awakening does not create permanent obedience.

### Wall of Thorns
Create a substantial thorn barrier that obstructs movement and harms creatures forcing their way through.

### Transport via Plants
Open a temporary route between suitable known living plants for travelers to pass through.
