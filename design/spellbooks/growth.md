## Spells

### Druidcraft

**ID:** spell:growth:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One ordinary plant or patch of soil within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Open a flower, ripen one already-growing fruit, make a seed sprout, or read whether rain is likely here in the next day. The growth affects one hand-sized part of the plant.

### Entangle

**ID:** spell:growth:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Class
**Defense:** Agility
**Target / range:** Every grounded creature in a 2-square-radius area within 6 squares
**Hold attribute:** Reason
**Escape attribute:** Agility
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Roots grasp through soil. Apply Slowed 1/2/3 to creatures in the area when cast. The area is difficult terrain while sustained. Each upkeep renews Slowed 1 on those originally caught who remain in the area. A conscious caught creature may spend a Main Action to escape; success removes this casting’s Slowed and protects it from being caught again by the same casting. Leaving the area or ending Sustain also removes this casting’s Slowed. Difficult terrain remains while the roots persist.

### Goodberry

**ID:** spell:growth:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to six ordinary berries held by you
**Duration:** 24 hours

Use a Maneuver and a free hand to eat one enchanted berry, restoring 1 HP and receiving nourishment for one meal. You may instead feed it to an adjacent willing living creature; use the recipient and Reaction rules of Use a Healing Potion. The berry is consumed, with no inventory die roll. Apply the normal magical-healing rules. Each berry keeps its magic for 24 hours after the ritual and retains its ordinary Bulk.

### Speak with Plants

**ID:** spell:growth:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One ordinary plant within 1 square
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Converse through impressions of light, weather, touch and nearby movement experienced during the past day. Ask up to three questions. The DA answers from what this plant could sense.

### Spike Growth

**ID:** spell:growth:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Class
**Defense:** Agility
**Target / range:** A 2-square-radius patch of soil within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Raise sharp growth that makes the area difficult terrain. Roll when cast to set physical damage at 2/3/5. Each creature takes that damage the first time each round it moves through at least one affected square. The growth is visible on inspection. Keep one Spike Growth active; casting another ends the first.

### Plant Growth

**ID:** spell:growth:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 6-by-6-square patch of existing ordinary vegetation within 6 squares
**Duration:** 24 hours

Existing plants immediately swell into dense, tangled growth, making the affected squares difficult terrain. Use the normal difficult-terrain movement costs, including when crawling. Choose any squares to leave clear when casting, including paths through the growth. Creatures already there remain in place and can act normally. The growth lasts 24 hours without Sustain.

### Tree Skin

**ID:** spell:growth:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Bark covers the recipient's skin. Grant physical resistance 2 and Stability 1. Use the highest applicable resistance and Stability.

### Tree Stride

**ID:** spell:growth:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self; one adjacent living tree large enough to admit you
**Duration:** Instant

Step through the tree and emerge beside another living tree you can see within 12 squares. Carry your worn and held equipment. Choose an unoccupied destination with firm support.

### Awaken

**ID:** spell:growth:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing ordinary animal or living tree beside you; 100 coins of rare incense consumed
**Duration:** Permanent

Awaken a speaking mind with one language you know. The being keeps its body, natural senses and ordinary capabilities. It becomes an independent person; agree on any companionship or service through conversation. You can complete this awakening once between safe overnight rests.

### Wall of Thorns

**ID:** spell:growth:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Class
**Defense:** Agility
**Target / range:** Six connected unoccupied ground squares within 12 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Grow a thorn wall 2 squares high. Name up to six creatures when casting; they pass through it at ordinary movement cost and take no damage from its thorns. Others spend 3 movement to cross one square and take 3/5/7 physical damage, set by your casting roll, once per creature each round. Each wall square has 8 HP; fire damages it normally. The wall provides cover. Keep one Wall of Thorns active; casting another ends the first.

### Transport via Plants

**ID:** spell:growth:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to five willing creatures beside a living tree
**Duration:** Instant

Travel through the tree to another living tree you personally touched within the past month, up to 100 kilometers away on the same plane. Everyone emerges in safe adjacent squares with carried gear. The destination tree must remain alive and large enough to admit a traveler. An explicit teleportation barrier blocks the journey.
