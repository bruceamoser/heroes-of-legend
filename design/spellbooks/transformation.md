## Spells

### Hues

**ID:** spell:transformation:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one willing adjacent creature
**Duration:** 1 hour

Change the color and pattern of hair, skin or fur. Choose ordinary or vivid colors. The recipient retains its silhouette, voice and other identifying features.

### Jump

**ID:** spell:transformation:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Until the end of its next turn

The recipient's next jump can cover up to 4 squares horizontally or 2 vertically using its normal movement. Choose a reachable landing place and spend movement for the distance traveled.

### Longstrider

**ID:** spell:transformation:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Increase the recipient's Speed by 2. Apply the highest magical Speed increase rather than adding repeated castings.

### Alter Self

**ID:** spell:transformation:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Choose one adaptation: gills for water breathing, webbing for swimming at normal Speed, or a changed face and voice. Keep your size and attributes. Changing the chosen adaptation requires another casting.

### Enlarge/Reduce — Living

**ID:** spell:transformation:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Change its size by one category, Small to Medium or Medium to Large, or the reverse. Adjust occupied space to 1 or 2 squares on each side as appropriate. Enlarged creatures gain Stability 1; reduced creatures gain 1 Boon to moving through tight spaces. Equipment changes with the body; attacks retain their printed damage.

### Darkvision

**ID:** spell:transformation:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Give the recipient darkvision to 12 squares. It sees shapes in ordinary darkness; colors appear as shades of gray. Magical darkness follows its own visibility rules.

### Polymorph

**ID:** spell:transformation:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Give the recipient the body of a familiar ordinary Small or Medium animal. It keeps its HP, attributes and mind. Choose walking, swimming or climbing at its normal Speed; use claws or a bite for Basic Unarmed attacks. Worn gear merges into the form. Returning costs a Maneuver; speech, item use and casting resume in the original body.

### Freedom of Movement

**ID:** spell:transformation:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

The recipient moves through nonmagical difficult terrain at ordinary cost and immediately reduces Slowed by 3. Once each turn it may spend a Maneuver to reduce Grappled by 2, using your movement magic to slip its bonds.

### Animal Shapes

**ID:** spell:transformation:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to three willing creatures within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Transform each recipient using Polymorph's animal-form procedure. Choose a form and movement mode separately for each. Your single upkeep sustains the group. Each recipient may return to its original body with a Maneuver.

### Shapechange

**ID:** spell:transformation:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Assume a familiar animal shape of Small, Medium or Large size. Keep your HP and attributes. Choose flying, swimming or climbing at your normal Speed, physical resistance 1, and a natural attack using Brawn or Agility for 3/5/7 physical damage. Your upkeep may change the form. Gear merges; return with a Maneuver to speak, use items or cast.

### True Polymorph — Living

**ID:** spell:transformation:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing adjacent creature; 100 coins of transformative incense consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

Shape an ordinary living body of Small or Medium size: a humanoid guise or familiar animal. The recipient keeps its mind, HP and attributes. Choose one movement adaptation: swimming or climbing at normal Speed, or flight at Speed 4. Animal bodies use Polymorph's equipment and action rules. The recipient may return with a Maneuver; Greater Restoration also ends the transformation.
