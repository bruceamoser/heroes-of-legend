## Spell descriptions

### Hues
Blend your coloration into nearby surroundings. This minor form changes color, not shape, size or identity.

### Jump
Give one willing creature a greatly improved leap, with an explicit movement cost and landing requirement.

### Longstrider
Improve a willing creature's ordinary movement speed for a limited duration.

### Alter Self
Adopt one authored physical adaptation or appearance change; the page defines which forms are learned.

### Enlarge/Reduce — Living
Change a willing or contested creature's size. The corresponding strength, equipment and space effects are stated together.

### Darkvision
Adapt a creature's sight to darkness without revealing invisible objects or defeating every magical obscuration.

### Polymorph
Transform a creature into an eligible beast form with a fixed profile and clear rules for damage and reversion.

### Freedom of Movement
Allow a willing creature to move through specified restraints and difficult environments.

### Animal Shapes
Transform a willing group into eligible animal forms under one sustained working.

### Shapechange
Assume a powerful studied form with clearly bounded access to its abilities.

### True Polymorph — Living
Perform a major transformation involving a living subject. Lasting change requires sustained commitment and explicit reversal rules.
