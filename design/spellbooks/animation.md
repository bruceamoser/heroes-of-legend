## Spells

### Prestidigitation

**ID:** spell:animation:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended object or 1-square space within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Clean or soil a hand-sized surface, change a small object's harmless scent or color, or create a tiny visual spark. Choose one effect when casting; its physical scale remains hand-sized.

### Unseen Servant — Workshop

**ID:** spell:animation:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One open square within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes, with commands

Create a Clay Helper energy form from Summoned Companions. Give its immediate Main command. Use its printed abilities, normal commands and six-square tether for workshop chores; provide real tools and materials.

### Enchanted Rope — Rigging

**ID:** spell:animation:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended rope up to 12 squares long within 3 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Teach the rope a sequence of up to three knots or anchors in reachable open space. Spend a Maneuver to advance one step. It supports ordinary rope loads after securing itself and retains its normal strength.

### Dancing Weapon

**ID:** spell:animation:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One unattended melee weapon within 3 squares; attack within 6 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute, with commands

Set the weapon dancing as a single summoned form with 6 HP, Speed 4 and your attributes for defenses. Give one immediate Main command to attack for 2/4/6 physical damage or perform an ordinary manipulation. Further activity uses normal commands and the six-square tether; it has no independent Reaction.

### Animate Objects

**ID:** spell:animation:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Up to three unattended objects, combined Bulk at most 3, within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute, with commands

Animate them as one group with shared 10 HP, Speed 4, and your attributes for defenses. They occupy one square together. Give one immediate Main command to attack one adjacent target for 3/5/7 physical damage or perform a simple task. Use normal commands and the six-square tether thereafter.

### Low Intelligence

**ID:** spell:animation:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One prepared object beside you; 50 coins of reagents consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Give it the ability to recognize its name, one voice and three spoken commands. It answers yes or no by a tiny movement or sound and remembers one short instruction. Movement or work uses a separate animation spell and its commands. You may keep one intelligent item of any degree at a time.

**Duration:** 7 days

Give it one spoken language and memory of up to ten short instructions or messages. It can converse in simple sentences and report what was said within 1 square. Movement and work use a separate animation spell's commands. This replaces your prior intelligent item.

Give it one language, a chosen conversational personality and memory of its week's experiences. It hears within 3 squares and can explain recorded instructions. It acts through speech; movement and work require separate animation and commands. This replaces your prior intelligent item.

### Very High Intelligence

**ID:** spell:animation:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One prepared object beside you; 250 coins of reagents consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Give it two languages you know, a personality and clear memory of its experiences. It sees and hears within 3 squares and can discuss one field of ordinary knowledge you teach during the ritual. Its physical actions use separate animation and commands. This replaces your prior intelligent item.

### Simulacrum

**ID:** spell:animation:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A willing adjacent living model and 150 coins of shaping materials consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours, with commands

Make a likeness with 6 HP, Speed 4, your defense attributes, and the model's appearance and voice. It uses your commands and a six-square tether. A Main command permits one ordinary task or Basic Melee attack for 2/3/5 physical damage using your Knowledge. It has 0 SP and 0 Grit; the form disappears at 0 HP. You may keep one such likeness.

### Animate Objects — Greater

**ID:** spell:animation:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Up to six unattended objects, combined Bulk at most 6, within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute, with commands

Animate one group occupying a 2-by-2-square space, with shared 18 HP, Speed 4 and your attributes for defenses. Give one immediate Main command. A Main command can attack up to two adjacent targets for 3/5/7 physical damage each, rolling once, or perform one larger ordinary task. Normal commands and six-square tether apply.
