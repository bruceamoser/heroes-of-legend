## Spell descriptions

### Prestidigitation
Create a tiny harmless prop or sensory trick. This minor page grants no autonomous worker or combatant.

### Unseen Servant — Workshop
Direct an invisible helper through simple workshop chores using a fixed noncombat task budget.

### Enchanted Rope — Rigging
Direct a longer existing rope through an authored sequence of knots and supports; it cannot independently seize a creature.

### Dancing Weapon
Animate one weapon to fight under your direction. Commands consume the specified action and do not create an extra full turn.

### Animate Objects
Animate a bounded group of eligible objects under a shared command, durability and damage budget.

### Low Intelligence
Imbue a prepared object with very limited understanding through costly item creation; it cannot improvise like a person.

### Medium Intelligence
Develop a prepared object's ability to interpret a broader set of explicit instructions through item creation.

### High Intelligence
Create a sophisticated intelligent item through a substantial working; intelligence is not an unlimited action engine.

### Very High Intelligence
Create an exceptional intelligent item with a defined personality and capabilities through a major crafting project.

### Simulacrum
Create a costly, limited duplicate with explicit resource and reproduction limits; it cannot manufacture an endless chain of copies.

### Animate Objects — Greater
Animate a larger or more capable collection while retaining one bounded shared command and damage budget.
