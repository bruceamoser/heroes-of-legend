## Spell descriptions

### Mage Seal
Magically hold one mundane closure shut. The free minor form is weak, nonstacking and ends when a new minor seal is made.

### Alarm
Set a clear warning when an eligible boundary or opening is crossed under the chosen trigger conditions.

### Arcane Lock
Strengthen an existing closure and give specified creatures or a password access.

### Knock
Release an eligible lock or fastening with a conspicuous magical report; it does not conceal the intrusion.

### Magic Mouth — Warning
Place a fixed audible warning on a closure, triggered by specified observable events.

### Glyph of Warding
Prepare a stationary magical trap with an explicit trigger, stored cost and detection or removal procedure.

### Rune Mastery — Seal
Store one eligible known spell in a prepared seal for later release, paying both inscription and stored-spell costs.

### Secret Chest
Hide a prepared container beyond ordinary access through a maintained magical connection and substantial material requirements.

### Symbol
Prepare a powerful fixed magical inscription with one authored effect, trigger and removal procedure.

### Sequester
Conceal and suspend an eligible willing creature or object under specified release conditions.

### Imprisonment — Seal
Create a major prepared confinement with substantial requirements and an explicit way to release its subject.
