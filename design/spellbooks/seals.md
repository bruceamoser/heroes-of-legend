## Spells

### Mage Seal

**ID:** spell:seals:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One closed unattended container within 1 square
**Duration:** 1 hour

Place a visible personal mark across its opening. Opening the container visibly breaks the mark. You recognize your own seal and whether it was broken when you next inspect it.

### Alarm

**ID:** spell:seals:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One doorway or 2-square-radius area beside you
**Duration:** 8 hours

Name up to six guests. When another creature crosses the boundary, a chime sounds within 6 squares or you receive a mental warning while within 100 squares; choose when casting. The first warning ends the spell.

### Arcane Lock

**ID:** spell:seals:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent closed door or container
**Duration:** 8 hours

Add magical fastening. An ordinary opening objective increases by 3 progress. Name up to six creatures who may operate it normally. Knock or a completed cancellation objective ends the magical fastening; physical locks remain as they were.

### Knock

**ID:** spell:seals:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One lock or fastening within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

A sharp audible knock carries 12 squares. Add 2/3/4 progress toward an ordinary opening objective. Against Arcane Lock, use the same progress toward its cancellation objective. Resolve one fastening per casting.

### Magic Mouth — Warning

**ID:** spell:seals:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One fixed object within reach
**Duration:** 24 hours or until triggered

Record a warning of up to twenty-five words and a visible or audible trigger within 6 squares. The first matching event speaks the warning loudly enough to hear within 12 squares, then ends the spell.

### Glyph of Warding

**ID:** spell:seals:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One fixed surface within reach; burst within 1 square of it
**Duration:** 8 hours or until triggered

Inscribe a visible glyph and define a creature crossing or touching its surface as the trigger. On the first trigger, every creature in the burst takes 3/5/7 force damage, set by your ritual roll. The glyph triggers once. Moving its surface more than 1 square ends it safely.

### Rune Mastery — Seal

**ID:** spell:seals:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One fixed seal and one known Main Action spell costing at most 2 SP
**Duration:** 8 hours or until triggered

Pay the stored spell's SP in addition to the ritual. Choose its targets relative to the seal and a visible or audible trigger within 3 squares. At the first trigger, release it using your attribute and legal targets, then end the seal. Keep one such prepared seal at a time.

### Secret Chest

**ID:** spell:seals:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent chest holding up to 12 Bulk; 25 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

Hide the chest and its contents in a private folded space. While beside its original location, use a Main Action to return it there. At expiry it returns automatically to the nearest open space. Living creatures remain outside; keep one Secret Chest at a time.

### Symbol

**ID:** spell:seals:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One fixed visible inscription; targets within 2 squares
**Duration:** 8 hours or until triggered

Choose a visible trigger and either dread or bewilderment. On its first occurrence, up to three creatures you specify by position gain Frightened 2/3/4 or Dazed 1/1/2, set by your ritual roll. Dread names the inscription as source. The inscription then fades.

### Sequester

**ID:** spell:seals:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature or unattended object beside you; 100 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Place it in a concealed, motionless magical sleep or stasis. It becomes Invisible and its bodily needs pause. Touching it with the token you designate, Remove Curse, or expiry awakens and reveals it. Record its HP and SP on entry; those values return unchanged.

### Imprisonment — Seal

**ID:** spell:seals:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing or continuously restrained adjacent creature; a 100-coin crystal consumed
**Duration:** Until released or its seal breaks

Bind the creature sleeping inside a fixed warded chamber of at least one square. Its needs pause. Mark the chamber's door with a visible seal: breaking the door, Remove Curse, or your Main Action at the seal releases the prisoner at its previous HP and SP. Keep one such prison at a time.
