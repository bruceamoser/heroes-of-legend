**Page:** 1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares that can hear you
**Duration:** Instant

Turn a cutting phrase into a psychic sting. Deal 2/3/5 mental damage.

### Heroism

**ID:** spell:emotion:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Bolster its courage. Reduce Frightened by 2 immediately and grant 1 Boon to rolls made while confronting a visible source of fear.

### Charm Person

**ID:** spell:emotion:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One creature within 3 squares that can hear and understand you
**Duration:** Until the social exchange ends, up to 10 minutes

Offer an appealing introduction. Add 1/2/3 progress toward an attainable social objective involving a friendly hearing or small favor. During the exchange, the target senses you as approachable. The DA establishes the concession and its boundaries before casting.

### Calm Emotions

**ID:** spell:emotion:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** Up to three creatures within 6 squares that can hear you
**Duration:** Instant

Send a steadying hush through the group. Reduce each target's Frightened by 1/2/3. Willing listeners may also pause an argument long enough for one person to speak.

### Suggestion

**ID:** spell:emotion:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares that understands your words
**Duration:** One social objective, up to 10 minutes

Frame a specific, feasible course of action that appeals to the target. Add 2/3/4 progress toward the DA's stated persuasion objective. Each further casting needs a new relevant reason or changed circumstance, following social progress rules.

### Fear

**ID:** spell:emotion:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** Every creature in a 3-square cone that can perceive you
**Duration:** Instant; conditions decay normally

Appear as an overwhelming source of dread. Apply Frightened 1/2/3, with you as its source. Each creature retains control of its movement and decisions.

### Charm Monster

**ID:** spell:emotion:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares able to understand an offered image or emotion
**Duration:** One social objective, up to 10 minutes

Establish rapport through shared sensations even across a language barrier. Add 2/4/6 progress toward a feasible request stated by the DA. The creature's needs and motives shape what you can ask of it.

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** Up to three creatures within 6 squares

Scatter attention with conflicting impulses. Apply Dazed 1/1/2. Each affected creature chooses its remaining action categories normally when its turn begins.

### Mass Suggestion

**ID:** spell:emotion:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** Up to six creatures within 6 squares that understand you and share a motive
**Duration:** One social objective, up to 10 minutes

Propose one common course of action. Add 3/5/7 progress toward a single attainable group objective. The DA sets the concession and boundaries before casting. A later attempt needs a new relevant contribution.

### Dominate Person

**ID:** spell:emotion:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares that understands you
**Duration:** Until the end of its next turn

State one brief command: move to a named safe place, release a held object, or perform one ordinary interaction. On its turn the target chooses to perform that command using its own actions or suffer Dazed 1/2/3 from resisting the pressure. Once that choice resolves, the command ends.

### Antipathy/Sympathy

**ID:** spell:emotion:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Guile
**Target / range:** One fixed object or 3-square-radius place within reach
**Duration:** 1 hour

Choose a recognizable group and either welcome or dread. When a matching creature first enters, it feels the chosen pull and recognizes it as magic. Welcome supplies 1 Boon to your group's first relevant social contribution there; dread applies Frightened 1/2/3, set by your ritual roll, once per creature. Identify the object or place as the fear source.
