## Spell descriptions

### Vicious Mockery
Deliver a cutting magical insult that causes psychic harm and briefly undermines the target's next attack.

### Heroism
Bolster a willing creature against fear and give a small, explicitly limited protective benefit.

### Charm Person
Make one person regard you more favorably temporarily. It does not grant obedience, erase evidence or override every relationship.

### Calm Emotions
Suppress a defined emotional disturbance or temporarily reduce hostility within a bounded group.

### Suggestion
Plant one plausible course of action, subject to clear limits, resistance and ending conditions.

### Fear
Project a terrifying presence that drives a bounded fear response rather than unlimited control.

### Charm Monster
Extend temporary charm to a broader range of creatures, retaining the limits on obedience.

### Confusion
Disrupt a group’s ability to act coherently using a short, predictable HOL behavior procedure.

### Mass Suggestion
Give one bounded suggestion to several creatures; the instruction and its limits are shared.

### Dominate Person
Direct one person's actions under a costly maintained effect, with explicit breaks in control and no extra caster turn.

### Antipathy/Sympathy
Make a prepared subject strongly repel or attract a defined category of creature under explicit resistance rules.
