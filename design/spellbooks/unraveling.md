## Spell descriptions

### Detect Magic — Threads
Sense an accessible active working before attempting to unravel it. The free form does not identify every property.

### Counterspell
Use a reaction to interfere with a perceived spell as it is cast; the result determines bounded suppression or interruption progress.

### Remove Curse
End an eligible curse or release its hold under explicit scope restrictions; it does not destroy every cursed object.

### Dispel Magic
Make progress toward ending an accessible ongoing spell instead of introducing a second casting-roll subsystem.

### Cancel Essence
Maintain a defense against a specified family of elemental or material magic, remapped from Rolemaster's Essence realm.

### Cancel Mentalism
Maintain a defense against specified mental magic without granting immunity to ordinary persuasion.

### Cancel Channeling
Maintain a defense against specified invoked or spiritual magic, remapped from Rolemaster's Channeling realm.

### Dispel True
Extend unravelling across the learned magical families in a bounded area, affecting allied workings too.

### Antimagic Field
Maintain a powerful area that suppresses eligible magic, including your own, with explicit exceptions and boundaries.

### Cancel True
Maintain an exceptional personal defense against the three authored magical families without universal permanent immunity.

### Unessence
Temporarily suppress one target's eligible magical channel after overcoming its resistance; HOL sets a bounded duration and recovery procedure.
