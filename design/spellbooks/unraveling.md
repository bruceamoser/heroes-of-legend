## Spells

### Detect Magic — Threads

**ID:** spell:unraveling:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One visible magical effect within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

See its magical threads. Learn whether it is sustained, fixed-duration or permanent, and which creature is presently sustaining it if that creature is visible.

### Counterspell

**ID:** spell:unraveling:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Reaction · when a visible caster finishes a spell
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** The spell; caster within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Add 1/2/3 cancellation progress against the triggering spell. Its target is 2 plus its printed SP cost. At completion it ends before resolving. Otherwise its caster receives 1 Bane on any roll made to resolve it; the incomplete cancellation progress then expires.

### Remove Curse

**ID:** spell:unraveling:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One adjacent cursed creature or object
**Duration:** Instant

Add 2/3/4 cancellation progress toward one curse's target, normally 2 plus the source spell's SP cost. Accumulate progress across attempts against that same curse. A source that explicitly names Remove Curse as an immediate remedy ends automatically instead.

### Dispel True

**ID:** spell:unraveling:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Up to three visible ongoing spells within 6 squares

Choose one shared cancellation total of 4/6/8 progress and divide it among the effects, assigning at least 1 to each. Each target is 2 plus that spell's printed SP cost. End each effect whose objective is completed.

### Antimagic Field

**ID:** spell:unraveling:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A 1-square-radius area centered on you
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Create a quiet zone where magical effects, including your own, are suspended except for this field and its upkeep. Casting and magical targeting operate outside the zone; ongoing durations continue to pass inside it. Effects resume after leaving if their durations remain. Mundane equipment and ordinary actions work normally.

### Unessence

**ID:** spell:unraveling:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One adjacent permanent enchantment; 100 coins of cleansing reagents consumed
**Duration:** Instant

Add 3/5/7 progress toward target 12 for an ordinary permanent enchantment, or a target specified by the item's own removal rules. At completion remove one identified magical property while preserving the physical item. Each attempt consumes its materials; exceptional artifacts use their printed remedies.
