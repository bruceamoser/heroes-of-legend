## Spells

### Thaumaturgy

**ID:** spell:binding:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self or one unattended feature within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 minute

Magnify your voice to carry across a hall, make a small flame flicker, or cause one unlocked door to creak. Choose one harmless display when casting.

### Command

**ID:** spell:binding:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 4 squares that understands you
**Duration:** Until the end of its next turn

Give a one-word request to approach a safe square, halt movement, or drop a held object. The target may comply using its own actions or take Frightened 1/2/3, with you as source, from resisting the pressure. Its player makes this choice for a hero.

### Hold Person

**ID:** spell:binding:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One humanoid creature within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant; conditions decay normally

Bands of pale light grip its limbs. Apply Slowed 1/2/3; on Strong also apply Paralyzed 1.

### Magic Circle

**ID:** spell:binding:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** A marked 2-square-radius circle centered beside you
**Duration:** 1 hour

Choose summoned forms, undead or spirits. Creatures of that kind gain 2 Banes on attacks crossing the circle boundary. A creature may cross the line by spending a Main Action; crossing breaks no physical barrier. The circle remains while its drawn line is intact.

### Banishment

**ID:** spell:binding:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares
**Duration:** Until your next turn ends

Deal 3/5/7 radiant damage to a summoned energy form; at 0 HP it disperses. An independent creature instead takes Frightened 1/2/3, with an image of its home as source. On Strong, a willing extraplanar creature may return to its home plane rather than take that fear.

### Planar Binding

**ID:** spell:binding:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing extraplanar creature beside you; 50 coins of incense consumed
**Duration:** One agreed service, up to 24 hours

Seal a clearly spoken agreement defining service, payment and release. Both parties hear the terms. Each gains immediate awareness if the other deliberately breaks them; the injured party may then end the binding and any unpaid obligation. The creature retains its own actions and personality.

### Geas

**ID:** spell:binding:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing creature beside you
**Duration:** 7 days

Bind a mutual oath naming one feasible duty and its completion condition. A participant who knowingly abandons its promised duty gains Frightened 1* with the oath as source until it resumes the duty, makes agreed restitution, or receives Remove Curse. Each participant may carry one Geas oath.

### Hold Monster

**ID:** spell:binding:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant; conditions decay normally

Close binding bands around the target. Apply Slowed 2/3/4 and Paralyzed 1/1/2.

### Dominate Monster

**ID:** spell:binding:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One creature within 6 squares that perceives your command
**Duration:** Until the end of its next turn

Command one ordinary movement or interaction within the creature's abilities. It may obey using its own actions or take 4/6/8 mental damage and Dazed 1/1/2. Its player chooses for a hero. The command ends after that choice resolves.

### Forcecage

**ID:** spell:binding:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An unoccupied 2-by-2-square cage boundary within 6 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Form a cage around creatures fitting inside its boundary. Bars block bodily passage but allow sight and ranged targeting. The cage has 15 HP and resistance 2 to all damage; attacks always deal at least 1. Teleportation or destroying the cage permits escape. The caster may end it with a Maneuver.

### Imprisonment

**ID:** spell:binding:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing or continuously restrained adjacent creature; a 100-coin crystal consumed
**Duration:** Until released or the crystal is broken

Enclose the creature in a palm-sized crystal where it sleeps and its needs pause. The crystal has 10 HP and physical resistance 1. Breaking it, Remove Curse, or your Main Action holding it releases the creature into the nearest open square with its prior HP and resources. Keep one such prison at a time.
