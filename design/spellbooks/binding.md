## Spell descriptions

### Thaumaturgy
Manifest a minor supernatural sign or commanding voice. It conveys presence, not obedience or damage.

### Command
Give one brief command with clear comprehension, resistance and harmful-instruction limits.

### Hold Person
Make progress toward immobilizing an eligible person; continuing resistance can end the hold.

### Magic Circle
Prepare a boundary that contains or excludes specified supernatural beings under explicit crossing rules.

### Banishment
Remove an eligible creature temporarily or return an outsider under defined duration and resistance conditions.

### Planar Binding
Bind an eligible summoned being to service through a prepared working with explicit duration and terms.

### Geas
Impose a specified supernatural obligation and a bounded consequence for deliberate violation; it is not unrestricted puppetry.

### Hold Monster
Extend a powerful holding effect to a broader range of creatures with continuing escape opportunities.

### Dominate Monster
Direct one eligible creature through a costly sustained effect and a shared action budget.

### Forcecage
Confine creatures within a powerful fixed enclosure with explicit magical escape conditions.

### Imprisonment
Perform a major binding using a chosen form, significant preparation and a specified release condition.
