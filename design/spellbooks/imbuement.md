## Spells

### Light

**ID:** spell:imbuement:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing held or unattended object within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Make it shed bright light to 6 squares and dim light for another 3. Choose the color. You may keep one Light casting at a time and end it with a Maneuver.

### Magic Weapon

**ID:** spell:imbuement:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held weapon belonging to a willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Give the weapon's damaging attacks +1 physical damage before resistance. Apply this once per target per attack. Use the highest magical weapon damage increase.

### Elemental Weapon

**ID:** spell:imbuement:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held weapon belonging to a willing creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Choose fire, cold or lightning. Its attacks deal that type in place of their normal damage type and add 1 damage before resistance. Use the highest magical weapon damage increase; each attack remains one damage event per target.

### Rune Mastery

**ID:** spell:imbuement:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held rune-stone and one known Main Action spell costing at most 2 SP
**Duration:** 24 hours or until released

Pay the stored spell's SP in addition to this ritual. Inscribe its power into the stone. A bearer uses a Main Action to release it, choosing legal targets from their position and using your casting attribute. The stone holds one use; you may keep one prepared Rune Mastery stone at a time.

### Magic Mouth

**ID:** spell:imbuement:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One adjacent unattended object
**Duration:** 24 hours or until triggered

Record up to twenty-five spoken words and one visible or audible trigger within 3 squares of the object. The first matching event makes the object speak the message once, then ends the spell.

### Continual Flame

**ID:** spell:imbuement:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One unattended object held throughout the ritual; 10 coins of luminous oil consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Kindle a cool magical flame that sheds bright light to 6 squares. It illuminates without consuming fuel. You may keep up to three such flames; creating a fourth ends the oldest.

### Holy Symbol

**ID:** spell:imbuement:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held holy symbol
**Duration:** 24 hours

Consecrate it to your chosen spiritual tradition. A bearer holding it gains 1 Boon to its first healing roll or defense against an undead creature, then the blessing ends. You may keep one consecrated symbol at a time.

### Weapon I

**ID:** spell:imbuement:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One weapon beside you; 25 coins of enchanting oil consumed
**Duration:** 24 hours

The weapon's damaging attacks gain +1 damage of their normal type before resistance, once per target per attack. Use the highest magical weapon increase. You may keep one Weapon I enchantment at a time.

### Contingency

**ID:** spell:imbuement:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and one known Main Action spell costing at most 2 SP
**Duration:** 24 hours or until triggered

Pay the chosen spell's SP in addition to this ritual. Name a visible or bodily trigger and legal self-directed effect. When triggered while conscious, spend your Reaction to release that spell on yourself. You may keep one Contingency; casting another replaces the earlier preparation.

### Armor IV

**ID:** spell:imbuement:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One suit of armor beside you; 100 coins of enchanting oil consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Choose fire, cold, lightning, poison, life, radiant, mental or force. While worn with its normal training, the suit grants resistance 2 to that damage type. Use the highest matching resistance. You may keep one Armor IV enchantment at a time.

### General IV

**ID:** spell:imbuement:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Ritual · 8 hours
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One held mundane item; 100 coins of reagents consumed
**Duration:** 7 days

Bind one known automatic, non-attack Maneuver spell costing 0 SP to the item. A bearer can use a Maneuver to produce that spell's printed effect from their own position. Only one instance from the item can persist at a time. You may keep one General IV item at a time.
