## Spell descriptions

### Light
Make an object shine with ordinary light. The minor page has a strict simultaneous-object limit.

### Magic Weapon
Grant one weapon a temporary magical improvement using a fixed HOL profile.

### Elemental Weapon
Imbue a weapon with a specified elemental attack property selected when the page is learned.

### Rune Mastery
Inscribe an eligible known spell for later casting, paying its storage and spell costs without creating free SP.

### Magic Mouth
Place a recorded message on an object with a clearly defined sensory trigger.

### Continual Flame
Create a lasting magical light through a prepared expenditure; the flame produces light rather than usable combustion heat.

### Holy Symbol
Consecrate a personal focus through sustained work. Any SP improvement is bounded and cannot be multiplied by making extra symbols.

### Weapon I
Begin lasting weapon enchantment through skilled work, time and materials; the page does not provide instant free permanent bonuses.

### Contingency
Prepare one eligible known spell to occur under a fixed trigger, reserving its required resources.

### Armor IV
Create a substantial lasting protective enchantment through a major crafting project with finite improvement limits.

### General IV
Create a powerful nonweapon magical item from a defined recipe through a major crafting project.
