## Spell descriptions

### Detect Magic
Sense nearby active magic while concentrating. Further details require inspection; the spell is not automatic item identification.

### Detect Poison and Disease
Locate eligible poisons or diseases within a bounded area and identify their general nature.

### Detect Evil and Good
Sense specified supernatural creature types and consecrated or desecrated places; it does not reveal everyone's morality.

### Find Traps
Notice the presence of qualifying traps in view without automatically finding every mechanism or disarming them.

### See Invisibility
Perceive invisible creatures and objects within ordinary line of sight.

### Identify
Study an accessible object or effect to learn defined magical properties, requirements and limitations.

### True Seeing
Perceive through specified forms of visual deception within a bounded range and duration.

### Analysis
Study one target to learn one broad, bounded characteristic; HOL does not reveal a complete character sheet.

### Detect Magic — Scaling Analysis
Inspect a known effect to determine how it has been strengthened or altered, using HARP's deeper detection application.

### Find the Path
Receive guidance along a viable route to a specifically known destination, with the required connection and preparation.

### Sight True
See through bounded intervening nonliving material while concentrating; the HOL page fixes its depth and range.
