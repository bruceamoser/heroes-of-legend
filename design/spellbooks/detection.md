## Spells

### Detect Magic

**ID:** spell:detection:1
**Page:** 1
**Action:** Maneuver
**SP:** 0
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One visible object or creature within 3 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Sense whether an active magical effect is present on the target. Learn whether the strongest visible aura is protective, transformative, harmful or informative. Closely concealed sources require examination through stronger pages.

### Detect Poison and Disease

**ID:** spell:detection:2
**Page:** 2
**Action:** Ritual · 1 minute
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Food, drink or one willing creature within 1 square
**Duration:** Instant

Determine whether ordinary poison or disease is present and identify its broad type: ingested, inhaled, contact or bodily infection. The reading helps select suitable treatment.

### Detect Evil and Good

**ID:** spell:detection:3
**Page:** 3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 1
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Visible creatures or objects within 6 squares
**Duration:** Until your next turn ends

Perceive the presence of summoned forms, undead and active spirit bindings. Learn which visible targets bear those properties. Their motives are discovered through interaction.

### Find Traps

**ID:** spell:detection:4
**Page:** 4
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One visible 3-by-3-square area within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Sense intentionally prepared mechanisms or magical triggers that could harm an entrant. Add 1/2/3 progress toward a search objective set by the DA. At completion identify each detected trigger's location and how it is activated; disarming uses its own procedure.

### See Invisibility

**ID:** spell:detection:5
**Page:** 5
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

See Invisible creatures and objects within 6 squares as translucent outlines. This satisfies visual targeting at that distance; solid cover still blocks sight.

### Identify

**ID:** spell:detection:6
**Page:** 6
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent object examined throughout the ritual
**Duration:** Instant

Learn 1/2/3 facts about its active magical properties, chosen from activation, charges or costs, duration, and an explicit drawback. The DA gives accurate facts available from the item's current enchantment.

### True Seeing

**ID:** spell:detection:7
**Page:** 7
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** Sustained, up to 1 minute

Within 6 squares, see Invisible targets and perceive the original outline beneath visual illusions or disguising magic. You recognize illusion as illusion while retaining sight of the underlying scene. Solid barriers retain their normal visibility limits.

### Analysis

**ID:** spell:detection:8
**Page:** 8
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** One adjacent object, creature or fixed magical effect
**Duration:** Instant

Add 2/4/6 progress toward understanding one magical effect, with target 6. Each completed analysis reveals its purpose, remaining duration, SP upkeep and one named remedy or interruption method. Use further castings for further effects.

### Detect Magic — Scaling Analysis

**ID:** spell:detection:a1
**Page:** A1
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** Up to three visible magical sources within 6 squares
**Duration:** Instant

Read their relative strength. Learn each source's spell name if known, its SP cost or equivalent strength, and its remaining duration on Standard or Strong; Weak reveals only the strongest source and whether it is sustained. Choose all targets before rolling.

### Find the Path

**ID:** spell:detection:a2
**Page:** A2
**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self; a known fixed destination within 1 kilometer
**Duration:** 1 hour

Sense the direction of an ordinary traversable route to a location you previously visited. At each junction, learn which way continues along that route. Terrain and locks still require ordinary travel and interaction; if no traversable route exists, the reading identifies the nearest obstruction.

### Sight True

**ID:** spell:detection:a3
**Page:** A3
**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self
**Duration:** 10 minutes

See Invisible targets and recognize visual illusions within 12 squares. Also see the outlines of creatures immediately beyond ordinary cover up to 1 square thick, within 6 squares. Such outlines reveal position; attacks still require a clear path.
