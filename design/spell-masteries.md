# Spellbook masteries

At level 7, deepen a completed spellbook with one of these spells. Learn section IV of the named book, then spend the listed DP to learn that mastery. Each spell is a separate choice; another discipline or another mastery is not required. Casting uses its printed action and SP. The eleven spells already in each book remain available through its four sections, including its advanced spells at level 3.

These folios accompany their named books. A spell requiring a weapon, shield, armor, tools or materials uses the equipment you actually carry. Casting normally needs a free hand; for these weapon-and-shield workings, a required held weapon or shield serves as the focus instead. This changes only the hand requirement. Normal casting opportunity attacks, damage, immunity, resistance and player-facing defense rules still apply.

## Stormsteel Arc
ID: spell-mastery:stormsteel-arc
Book: air
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 3
**Attribute:** Brawn
**Target / range:** Up to three visible enemies within 3 squares; a melee weapon
**Duration:** Instant
**Defense:** Agility

Main Action · Brawn · 3 SP, with a melee weapon. Sweep lightning through up to three visible enemies within 3 squares. Roll once; each takes 3/6/9 lightning damage. On Strong, each also gains Dazed 1. This is a new magical attack, not a use of Measured Cut; its improvements do not apply.

## The Invisible Shieldbearer
ID: spell-mastery:the-invisible-shieldbearer
Book: telekinesis
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Reaction · before an ally within 8 squares takes attack damage
**SP:** 2
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing ally within 8 squares; a held shield
**Duration:** The triggering attack and immediate movement

Reaction · 2 SP, with a held shield. When an ally within 8 squares is about to take damage from an attack, project a force barrier to it. Reduce that attack’s damage by 4, minimum 1, then move the willing ally up to 2 squares along a legal route without movement reactions. This protects against one attack only and requires no additional roll.

## Borrowed Dawn
ID: spell-mastery:borrowed-dawn
Book: restoration
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** One living willing, Dying or stabilized creature within reach
**Duration:** Instant

Main Action · Knowledge · 4 SP. Restore 8/12/16 HP to one living willing, Dying or stabilized creature within reach. Also reduce its Bleeding by 2. This is magical healing, not first aid: it neither spends nor refreshes the recipient’s first-aid allowance. It cannot raise the dead, restore Grit or exceed maximum HP. Apply each recipient’s Life resistance, with at least 1 HP restored when HP is missing.

## Starfall Volley
ID: spell-mastery:starfall-volley
Book: flame
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Agility
**Target / range:** Up to three visible enemies within 12 squares; a ranged weapon and ammunition
**Duration:** Instant
**Defense:** Agility

Main Action · Agility · 4 SP, with a ranged weapon. Attack up to three visible enemies within 12 squares whose visible defenses you studied with a Main Action earlier this scene. Spend one projectile per target and roll once; each takes 4/7/10 fire damage and Burning 1. Each target is struck once; allies and other creatures nearby are unaffected. This grants no bonus to other attacks.

## Step Between Heartbeats
ID: spell-mastery:step-between-heartbeats
Book: passage
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** Agility
**Target / range:** Self and one enemy adjacent to your arrival square; a melee weapon
**Duration:** Instant
**Defense:** Agility

Main Action · Agility · 4 SP, with a melee weapon. Teleport to a visible unoccupied square within 10 squares, then attack one adjacent creature for 4/7/10 physical damage. After the attack, teleport up to 4 squares to another visible legal destination. Neither teleport triggers movement reactions. If you finish behind cover that conceals you from every enemy, become Hidden. An explicit teleportation barrier can block either movement.

## Sanctuary of Steel
ID: spell-mastery:sanctuary-of-steel
Book: sanctuary
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 4
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to four chosen allies within a fixed 3-square radius of you; armor and a shield
**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn

Main Action · 4 SP, wearing armor and holding a shield. Create a fixed sanctuary within 3 squares of your position until your next turn ends. You and up to four chosen allies inside it reduce attack damage by 4, minimum 1, and cannot gain Frightened or Dazed while inside. Existing conditions remain. Leaving ends a creature’s protection until it returns; the boundary blocks neither movement nor attacks. Recasting replaces your previous sanctuary.

## Colossus of the Forge
ID: spell-mastery:colossus-of-the-forge
Book: imbuement
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** Knowledge
**Target / range:** Self and up to four enemies within 3 squares; a melee weapon
**Duration:** Until the end of your next turn
**Defense:** Agility

Main Action · Knowledge · 6 SP, with a melee weapon. Form an incandescent shell around yourself through the end of your next turn, then immediately sweep up to four enemies within 3 squares for 4/7/10 physical damage and Burning 1, using one roll. While the shell lasts, gain Stability 3 and reduce attack damage by 3, minimum 1. On your next turn, you may spend a Main Action to repeat that sweep without additional SP. The shell cannot stack with another copy and creates no lasting material.

## The Road for the Lost
ID: spell-mastery:the-road-for-the-lost
Book: passage
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One surveyed same-plane landmark within 100 squares; up to twelve willing travelers
**Duration:** 1 minute

Ritual · 10 minutes · 6 SP. Open a visible passage from your position to a landmark on the same plane within 100 squares that you personally visited and surveyed. Both endpoints must have safe, unoccupied footing. Up to twelve willing creatures you name may cross during the next minute; no other creature or attack can pass through it. You may close it early with a Maneuver. It transports carried belongings but no unattended freight. Teleportation barriers block the working; it does not bypass an unknown destination or create safe ground.

**SP:** 6

**Target / range:** Up to six visible living willing, Dying or stabilized allies within 6 squares

Main Action · Knowledge · 6 SP. Choose up to six living willing, Dying or stabilized allies within 6 squares whom you can see. Roll once; each restores 6/10/14 HP and reduces Bleeding and Frightened by 2 each. A Dying or stabilized ally need not respond to be included. This is magical healing and neither uses nor refreshes first-aid allowances. It cannot raise the dead, restore Grit or exceed maximum HP. Each recipient is affected once per use. ## Wall of Thorns — Living Fortress
ID: spell-mastery:wall-of-thorns-living-fortress
Book: growth
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to eight connected unoccupied ground squares within 8 squares
**Duration:** 10 minutes

Main Action · Reason · 5 SP. Raise up to eight connected thorn-wall squares on unoccupied ground within 8 squares. The walls last 10 minutes, are 2 squares high, block movement and provide cover. Name up to six creatures when casting; each may pass through at normal movement cost. Other creatures can spend a Main Action cutting through one adjacent square with a suitable edged tool, taking 3 physical damage before resistance. Fire destroys an affected wall square after that attack resolves. No roll. Damage from cutting thorns is environmental, so resistance may reduce it to 0.

## True Resurrection
ID: spell-mastery:true-resurrection
Book: restoration
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours
**SP:** 8
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Remains of one willing person dead at most seven days; 500 coins of funerary materials
**Duration:** Instant

Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours · Reason · 8 SP, with remains of one person dead no longer than seven days and unique funerary materials worth 500 coins. The DA establishes availability before preparation. The spirit must freely agree. At completion, consume the materials and return that person to life at 1 HP in a restored body, clearing both death roll tallies but retaining descriptive scars. No roll. No duplicate body, restored Grit or SP is created. Each person has one lifetime return to life, shared with Lifegiving and Mass Lifegiving. This ritual restores a life ended before its natural span.

## Simulacrum
ID: spell-mastery:simulacrum
Book: animation
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours
**SP:** 8
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One willing adjacent living model; 300 coins of sculpting materials
**Duration:** Until destroyed or replaced

Ritual · 8 uninterrupted hours · Knowledge · 8 SP, with a willing adjacent living model and sculpting materials worth 300 coins, consumed on completion. Create one likeness with 10 HP, no SP or Grit, your Speed, and the model’s appearance and voice. It lasts until destroyed or you create another. It cannot cast, use learned abilities or recover HP. On your turn, spend a Main Action to command it to move up to its Speed and perform an ordinary task or Basic Melee attack using your Knowledge and 2/3/5 damage. It has no independent actions or Reactions; you roll its defenses with your attributes. It cannot create a duplicate. Destroying it does not harm the model. This mastery and the Animation book’s Simulacrum share one likeness allowance; creating either ends the other.

## Demiplane
ID: spell-mastery:demiplane
Book: passage
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** An adjacent solid wall; one private 6-by-6-square refuge
**Duration:** Permanent refuge; doorway 10 minutes

Ritual · 1 hour · Knowledge · 6 SP. Open a doorway on an adjacent solid wall to your private 6-by-6-square refuge, 3 squares high. The doorway lasts 10 minutes and may be used by anyone who can reach it. You may close it with a Maneuver. A Maneuver inside opens an exit to the last entrance location; if obstructed, use the nearest unoccupied square within 3 squares. The refuge holds ordinary objects and up to twelve creatures, with air and normal time but no created food or water. You have one refuge; recasting reopens it from the new wall. It grants no travel to any other destination. No roll.

## Foresight — Shared Vigil
ID: spell-mastery:foresight-shared-vigil
Book: portents
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 10 minutes
**SP:** 5
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to three willing creatures within 3 squares
**Duration:** 1 hour

Ritual · 10 minutes · Reason · 5 SP. Link yourself and up to three willing creatures within 3 squares for one hour. Linked creatures within 12 squares of you receive a warning when any linked member perceives a threat. If the warning alerts the group before the attack, roll initiative normally. Once each round, after one linked creature rolls a defense, it may reroll the entire roll and keep either result. The entire group shares this one reroll. The link carries warnings, not unrestricted thoughts. Recasting replaces the previous link. No roll.

## Storm of Vengeance
ID: spell-mastery:storm-of-vengeance
Book: air
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Main Action
**SP:** 7
**Attribute:** Reason
**Target / range:** A 3-square-radius storm centered within 12 squares, with one safe square within 2 of its center
**Duration:** 3 rounds
**Defense:** Agility

Main Action · Reason · 7 SP. Choose a visible point within 12 squares and a 1-square sanctuary within 2 squares of that point. For three rounds, a storm fills a 3-square radius around the point. When cast, roll once: creatures in the storm outside the sanctuary take 3/5/7 lightning damage. On each of your next two turns, you may spend a Main Action and roll again to strike with the storm, dealing the same damage and pushing each affected creature 1 square away from the center where legal. Without that action the storm remains visible but deals no further damage that turn; it ends after the third round. Everyone in the sanctuary is exempt. Recasting ends the earlier storm.

## Mass Lifegiving
ID: spell-mastery:mass-lifegiving
Book: restoration
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 1 uninterrupted hour
**SP:** 10
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Up to three willing dead people, bodies present, dead within one hour; safe sanctuary and 200 coins per person
**Duration:** Instant

Ritual · 1 uninterrupted hour · Reason · 10 SP, within a safe prepared sanctuary. Choose up to three willing dead people whose bodies are present and who died within the previous hour. Consume 200 coins of consecrated materials for each; the DA establishes availability beforehand. Restore their fatal injuries, return them to life at 1 HP and clear both death roll tallies. No roll. Scars remain; SP and Grit are not restored. Each person has one lifetime return to life, shared with Lifegiving and True Resurrection.

## Foresight — Oracle’s Dream
ID: spell-mastery:foresight-oracles-dream
Book: dreams
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 1 hour of shared sleep
**SP:** 6
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Self and up to three willing nearby sleepers; one willing spirit
**Duration:** One undertaking within 24 hours

Ritual · 1 hour of shared sleep · Reason · 6 SP. You and up to three willing nearby sleepers seek the help of a willing spirit for one named undertaking in the next 24 hours. The DA gives one truthful warning of a foreseeable obstacle and one usable clue about addressing it, subject to the spirit’s knowledge. Each dreamer may once reroll an entire roll directly addressing the undertaking and keep either result. Unused rerolls expire after 24 hours; a creature cannot hold a benefit from more than one Oracle’s Dream. No casting roll. This does not guarantee success or reveal unknowable future decisions.

## Gate — World Anchor
ID: spell-mastery:gate-world-anchor
Book: seals
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 7 days, 8 hours of work each day
**SP:** 10
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** Two known prepared stone anchors and 1,000 coins of materials
**Duration:** Permanent, until an anchor is destroyed or the pair is replaced

Ritual · 7 days, 8 hours of work per day · Knowledge · 10 SP on each workday, with prepared stone anchors at two known locations, each accessible during preparation, and 1,000 coins of materials consumed in total. The DA establishes availability and site permissions first. Create a permanent paired gateway, 2 squares wide, between those anchors, even across planes. At an anchor, a holder of either of the two keys you create can spend a Main Action and 3 SP to open both ends for 10 minutes, or a Maneuver to close them. Anyone can pass while open. A key conveys no control over travelers. Destroying either anchor ends the connection; each has 30 HP and physical resistance 3, and always takes at least 1 attack damage. No roll. You may maintain one pair; completing another ends the first.

## Guards and Wards — Living Citadel
ID: spell-mastery:guards-and-wards-living-citadel
Book: sanctuary
DP: 4 | Level: 7

**Action:** Ritual · 3 days, 8 hours of work each day
**SP:** 10
**Attribute:** None
**Target / range:** One prepared 12-by-12-square site; soil, water, stone and 600 coins of materials
**Duration:** Permanent structure; wards renewed for 24 hours

Ritual · 3 days, 8 hours of work per day · Reason · 10 SP each day, with sound stone, fertile soil, water and 600 coins of materials consumed. On a prepared 12-by-12-square site, raise a one-story living keep with six rooms, a courtyard, a gate and walls 3 squares high. No roll. Walls block movement and sight; each 1-square section has 20 HP and physical resistance 3, with minimum 1 attack damage. You designate up to twelve residents. At dawn, paying 4 SP renews its wards for 24 hours: residents within gain 1 Boon to defenses, and you sense the location of any breached wall while inside. Missing upkeep removes the wards, not the building. The keep needs ordinary food, water and repairs. You may ward only one citadel at a time.
