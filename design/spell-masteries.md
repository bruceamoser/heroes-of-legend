# Spellbook masteries

Each spellbook has one mastery spell in section VII. Learn its six earlier sections, then learn its mastery for 5 DP at character level 7. Sections V and VI each teach one advanced spell for 4 DP at level 3. Casting uses the action and SP printed in that spell’s block.

The complete rules for every spell, including mastery, appear with its book. Follow the book’s seven sections in order.
