# Discipline, action and origin coverage review

October 18, 2026 · Issue [#1277](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend/issues/1277).
New abilities and changed origin choices in this pass are submitted for Bruce’s review.
This is a breadth and usability review, not encounter-balance certification.

## What changed

The 357-card baseline had one Frost Novice ability and one Frost Master ability.
Across all eighteen disciplines, 55 tier/action cells lacked a Maneuver or Reaction,
even when mixed requirements counted. Cultures had eight Wilderness choices and
none for Arms, Frost, Mind or Radiance. Backgrounds ranged from one to six choices
per discipline. These counts support the reported character-creation concerns.

Added **62 shared abilities: 27 Novice, 14 Adept and 21 Master**. They comprise
**27 Maneuvers, 31 Reactions and 4 Main Actions**, with no added Passives.
There are now **419 abilities** including Universal actions and signatures.
Among paid shared abilities, the action distribution is now 173 Main Actions,
60 Maneuvers, 60 Reactions and 18 Passives; previously 169/33/29/18.

There are still eighteen disciplines. Bruce clarified that his mention of a separate
Protection discipline was a mistake. Guard retains defensive wards and gains
explicit shield expertise; Armor retains armor training.

Every discipline now has a Maneuver and Reaction at each tier. The audit also
requires meaningful investment: rank 1 at Novice, at least rank 2 at Adept/Master.
A rank-1 side requirement on another discipline’s Master ability cannot conceal a
gap. Mixed requirements remain genuine cross-training choices rather than a claim
that one rank unlocks the whole discipline. Every discipline retains a rank-3 destination.
This does not promise a pure single-discipline option for every action at every tier.

Frost now has a starting identity beyond a single attack: cold breath, protective
ice, climbing handholds and preservation of supplies. Its higher tiers add a shield
counter, a temporary winter road, protection against a cold impact and a broad whiteout.
Marksmanship gains a close shot and armor-reading setup, with Craft combinations
for sight adjustment and projectile interception. Radiance gains protective and
rallying responses. Medicine’s many wound treatments no longer conceal the lack of
quick field support at higher tiers.

## Shield expertise

- [Rim Strike](cards/rim-strike.md): a shield is the attack, with physical damage and a push.
- [Shielded Advance](cards/shielded-advance.md): advance and cover an adjacent ally’s next defense.
- [Hook with the Shield](cards/hook-with-the-shield.md): an Adept Maneuver pushes; Strong also applies Prone.
- [Shatterguard](cards/shatterguard.md): Frost/Guard turns a Strong defense into a push.
- [High Guard](cards/high-guard.md): Master Shield Block grants two Boons instead of one; it does not stack a second copy of Shield Block.
- [Shield Roof](cards/shield-roof.md): a tight group gains Stability and protection against blockable area attacks.

Universal Shield Block remains useful without training. Ordinary shields gain no
new item statistics. The DA still decides whether a shield can block an attack.
Stability, normal Boon/Bane limits, the single Reaction and ordinary opportunity
rules continue to apply. High Guard plus Defend can reach three defense Boons;
further Boons still use cancellation and the existing net cap, not a new ceiling.

Nine cultures change: cloister lights, woodland voices, silent cues among gathered
peoples, grassland sword traditions, highland cold stores and storm warnings, reed
village handholds and ancestors, river rescue, terrace lanterns and traveling blade
performers. Their narratives explain the new choices. No ancestry is tied to a culture.

Three backgrounds change one alternative each: Duelist’s Second can learn light
armor, Oath Witness can bear an oathlight blade, and Poacher can preserve a hidden
catch with Frost. These are useful lives a character can imagine, not new origin lists
created only to inflate counts. Every existing example hero’s selected pair remains
available; no starting ledger or inventory changes are needed.

## Review boundaries and next table checks

- Summon positioning never grants a command, independent activity, restored HP or
  a fresh command allowance. Recall the Shape is an explicit Master tether rescue;
  remaining forced movement and the normal tether still resolve afterward.
- Defensive Reactions use the existing roll, or a printed automatic effect. They
  do not create an opposed defense against a player’s attack.
- Close Shot grants only Basic Ranged, checks ammunition and cannot chain free attacks.
  Test its competition with defensive Reactions on an archer who expects melee pressure.
- New condition care restores no HP and does not cure starred wounds. Compare the
  value of Press and Bind/Keep Pressure with the existing Main Action and Adept
  Urgent Bandaging; their one-point reliability buys action efficiency, not better treatment.
- Shield hooks and ice counters use Stability. Prone still applies when a push is
  reduced to zero. Test whether spending a Maneuver on Hook with the Shield crowds
  out other Guard setups; do not infer balance from the printed damage being zero.
- Ice handholds cannot be withdrawn to manufacture an automatic falling attack.
  Winter Store preserves supplies; it never replenishes their inventory dice.
- Leader’s Watch the Rear still protects an ally at range 5 and grants three squares
  of movement after any outcome. The Forest Takes the Blow needs vegetation,
  range 3 and a Strong result for two squares; its shared niche does not replace
  that signature. Protector’s Defiant Rescue retains its mobile interception.
  Compare the whole cost and trigger at the table; no signature is weakened here.

## Reproduce the survey

```sh
python3 design/discipline_coverage.py --check
python3 design/discipline_coverage.py --baseline a47151dd6484e4962bd90b8ce93490be3bbbab63 --json
python3 design/audit.py
```

The baseline is the current approved catalog immediately before this pass, not a
legacy rules source. The survey reads authored Markdown and never rewrites it.
The main authoring audit now checks action and origin coverage too. Equal counts
and equal prices are not evidence of equal power; the new combinations need playtesting.
