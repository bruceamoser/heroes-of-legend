# Three characters to imagine at the table

These are legal draft shopping examples, not mandatory builds or simulated results.
All levels below refer to character creation. Equipment is granted only where stated.
Prices use the proposed fresh affinities; changing those requires updating the ledger.

## The bridge guard who brings everyone home

Dwarf Protector. Brawn 1, Fortitude 2, Agility 0, Guile 0, Knowledge −1, Reason 1.
Total +3; mental sum 0; both stages start with 10 DP. Choose Stubborn as Bedrock and
A Grudge Well Kept. The grudge offers one memorable answer when an enemy hurts you.

Level 0: Walled Communities grants Armor 1 and Wear Medium Armor; Household Guard
grants Arms 1 and Hook and Drive. Buy Braced Harness (2), Take Them Alive (2) and
Conditioned Body (2). Buy Catch a Falling Friend (2) and Tell a Worthy Story (2). Spend all 10; no discipline purchases.

Level 1: choose Armor 1 and Guard 1. Duplicate Armor adds 1 DP: pool 11. Choose
Between Them as the free signature. Buy Shielding Step (2), Medicine 1 at Adjacent
cost 2, Treat Soft Tissue (2), Athletics 1 at Foreign cost 3, and Sure Ascent (2). Spend all 11.

HP 12 + 4 + 5 + 2 + 1 trait + 1 card = **25**. Speed **5** before encumbrance;
capacity **13**. Granted medium armor, melee weapon and shield: **3 Bulk**.
The sword and shield occupy both hands; potion use needs an equipment decision.

Picture the moment: the guard hooks a foe out of a doorway, takes the next blow for
a friend, and decides whether this enemy will live to answer questions. Treatment
allows useful work after the fight without pretending the scar disappears.

## The keeper of dangerous little wonders

Elf Arcanist. Brawn −1, Fortitude 0, Agility 1, Guile 0, Knowledge 2, Reason 1.
Total +3; mental sum 3; each stage starts at 13 DP. Choose A Lifetime Ago and Dream
beneath the Stars. HP **16**, Speed **7**, capacity **7** before any later benefits.

Level 0: Learning Houses grants Flame 1 and Cinder Cast; Stage Assistant grants
Mind 1 and Intrusive Echo. Buy Speak with the Hearth (2), Borrow a Voice (2),
Conditioned Body (2), Notice the Human Detail (2), Keep Your Bearings (2), and
Tell a Worthy Story (2). Spend 12; 1 DP expires. HP is now **17**.

Level 1: choose Scholarship 1 and Calling 1, no duplicates. Choose Bent Trajectory
as the free signature. Buy Read the Remains (2), Identify an Enchantment (2),
Wilderness 1 at Foreign cost (3), Call a Burrower (2), and A Friend Beneath the Door (2).
Spend 11; 2 expire. Calling 1 and Wilderness 1 both precede the burrower purchase. No items are implicitly granted by these spells.

Picture the moment: a tiny scout wriggles through a barred doorway while the caster
asks the dying hearth who visited last. In combat, they bend a cinder around a nearby
corner and send a burrower to drag an enemy out of position. No spell slots to hoard.

## The traveler who makes things work

Human Intellect. Brawn 0, Fortitude 0, Agility 0, Guile 0, Knowledge 2, Reason 1.
Total +3; mental sum 3; each stage starts at 13 DP. Choose A Foot in Two Worlds and Road in the Blood.
HP **16**, Speed **7**, capacity **10**.

Level 0: Highland Holds grants Craft 1 and Trail Workshop; Bridge Worker chooses
Athletics 1 and Sure Ascent. Buy Ordinary Recipe Study (2), Swing into Trouble (2),
Expert River Crossing (2), Secure a Hold (2), Make Something Useful (2), Conditioned
Body (2). Spend 12; 1 expires. The two crafting cards share one tools grant.
Choose six distinct ordinary recipes across the three two-recipe grants. HP **17**.

Level 1: choose Craft 1 and Scholarship 1; duplicate Craft adds 1 DP, pool 14.
Choose Spot the Leverage. Buy Read the Remains (2), Identify an Enchantment (2),
Identify a Mixture (2), Medicine 1 at Adjacent cost 2, Treat Soft Tissue (2),
Treat Bone (2), Treat Mental Trauma (2). Spend 14. Craft tools are **1 Bulk**;
materials still need to be gathered or acquired. These treatments need no magical HP cost.

Picture the moment: the traveler turns wreckage into a way across, swings on the
rope first, and talks the others through the crossing. The intellect is useful on
the adventure, not only during shopping or between sessions.

## What these examples reveal

General Novice expertise gives characters useful choices without requiring a
level-0 discipline purchase. Each example has a combat contribution and things
to do beyond combat. These are hand-checked authoring examples, not an exhaustive
build-validity test or evidence that the prices are balanced.
