# Three very different people at the same table

These shopping examples use the current proposed prices. They show that the fantasy
is reachable; they are not optimized builds or results from a simulation.

## The physician who sees the fight coming

A Human Intellect: Brawn 0, Fortitude 1, Agility 0, Guile 0, Knowledge 2, Reason 0.
The scores total +3; the mental sum is +2. Choose Road in the Blood and A Foot in
Two Worlds. HP starts at 17; Speed 7; capacity 10.

Learning Houses grants Scholarship 1 and Read the Remains. Physician’s Student
chooses Scholarship 1 and Anatomy of an Opening. The duplicate rank gives 1 DP:
level-0 pool 13. Buy Identify an Enchantment, Identify a Mixture, Notice the Human
Detail, Keep Your Bearings, Tell a Worthy Story and Conditioned Body, 2 DP each.
Spend 12; 1 expires. HP becomes 18.

At level 1 choose Scholarship 1 and Craft 1; the duplicate Scholarship gives 1 DP,
so the new pool is 13. Choose Spot the Leverage as the signature. Buy Armor 1 at
Foreign cost 3, Wear Medium Armor for 2, Scholarship 2 at Home cost 2, Trail Workshop
for 2, and Catch a Falling Friend for 2. Spend 11; 2 expire. The early Scholarship 2
purchase is allowed, but does not unlock level-3 cards before level 3. Armor and
craft tools are granted, Bulk 1 each; choose two ordinary recipes. Anatomy of an
Opening also offers one ordinary melee weapon if wanted; choosing it makes total
granted Bulk 3 rather than 2.

Anatomy of an Opening uses **Knowledge** for a useful unarmed or melee attack now,
not only after becoming a Master. At level 3, Read the Next Blow makes the defensive
side of the fantasy explicit. The character still has different strengths on other
rolls. No rule says to use the highest attribute for everything.

## The bear who brings the fight to them

A Human Unbalanced: Brawn 2, Fortitude 1, Agility 0, Guile 0, Knowledge 0, Reason 0.
Choose Never Quite Finished and Road in the Blood. HP 22, Speed 7, capacity 16.
Each creation pool begins at 10 DP.

Terrace Towns grants Wilderness 1 and Roots Remember Feet. Beast-Reared grants
Wilderness 1 and Borrow the Beast; duplicate compensation makes level-0 pool 11.
Buy Forage Woodland, The Otter’s Bargain, Guide a Frightened Animal, Catch a Falling
Friend and Conditioned Body: 10 DP; 1 expires. HP becomes 23.

At level 1 choose Arms 1 and Athletics 1. Take Laugh at the Fall as signature.
Buy Hook and Drive, Take Them Alive, Sure Ascent, Swing into Trouble and Secure a
Hold, 2 DP each. Spend all 10. Hook and Drive grants a melee weapon, Bulk 1, for
human-form fighting. The beast attack itself needs no weapon.

In beast form the character keeps those 23 HP and every wound. The form’s own
attack is available immediately. Equipment benefits fold away, and there is no
free hand for a potion. At level 3 the character can buy Wilderness 2 at Foreign
cost 6 and Pounce of the Red Claw for 4, within the new 11-DP award. No prior card
chain is required; it is an intentional investment in a different combat style.

## The keeper of a healing grove

An Elf Shepherd: Brawn −1, Fortitude 0, Agility 0, Guile 1, Knowledge 1, Reason 2.
Choose Footfall on the Leaves and Dream beneath the Stars. HP 17, Speed 6,
capacity 7. The mental sum is +4: each creation pool begins at 14 DP.

Terrace Towns grants Wilderness 1 and Roots Remember Feet. Grove Tended chooses
Life 1 and Sap and Breath. Buy Forage Woodland, Forage Wetlands, Guide a Frightened
Animal, The Otter’s Bargain, Shared Breath, Keep the Door Closed and Conditioned
Body: 14 DP. HP becomes 18. No discipline purchase occurred at level 0.

At level 1 choose Life 1 and Medicine 1. The duplicate Life adds 1 DP, pool 15.
Choose Gentle Return as signature. Buy Treat Soft Tissue, Treat Bone, Treat Mental
Trauma, Relieve Poison and Rouse the Senses (10 DP). Buy Life 2 at Home cost 2 and
Tell a Worthy Story for 2: spend 14; 1 expires. Alternatively, those last 4 DP could buy Wilderness 2 at the Adjacent rate; this
version chooses Life 2 and the story card.

At level 3, buy Wilderness 2 for 4 and Briar Gate for 4, leaving 3 banked DP from
that level’s 11. Another route buys A Bough for the Fallen for 4 using the Life 2
already owned, and saves for terrain control later. Both are recognizable nature
characters; one leans toward rescue, the other toward controlling the approach.

## Put them together

The grove keeper moves an enemy off the route. The bear reaches the opening. The
physician recognizes where to strike or reaches the fallen companion. Each has a
clear reason to act, and each can buy something from another path later.
