# What play should feel like

These examples demonstrate choices, not prescribed scripts or simulated outcomes.

## The bell tower rescue

A friend is hanging from a ruined stair. The enemy stands between them and the
Protector. The Blade uses Hook and Drive: Standard damage stays modest, but the
pull clears the landing. The Protector can now reach the friend. A character with
Catch a Falling Friend keeps their Reaction ready rather than spending it on a
shield. The risk is visible, and each player has something useful to decide.

## The door the party never opened

The Arcanist calls a tiny scout to inspect the room beyond the gap under the door.
It needs commands and must remain within six squares. The party discovers a guard
beside a hearth. Speak with the Hearth may later recover a clue; Borrow a Voice
might first draw the guard away. A lock specialist still has a different solution.
The abilities create approaches; none says the whole encounter automatically succeeds.

## The bridge the enemy cannot hold

A traveler makes an ice bridge while the rest hold the ledge. It is a route, not an
infinite source of collision damage. A wind spell can move enemies off the approach;
a Shield Block can help someone survive long enough to cross. The objective is
getting people across, so damage is one tool among several.

## A healer’s last comfortable choice

The Shepherd has enough HP to pay for a stronger heal but doing so leaves them
exposed. They can use the small heal, spend more of themselves, or call for someone
to deliver a potion. A shield-bearing ally has to free a hand first. Healing is
powerful and available; the scene asks who accepts the danger, not whether a slot
remains on a sheet.

## After the shouting stops

The group catches its breath and HP returns. A scar remains. The healer checks the
wound’s treatment and adds progress during the next safe day. The craft specialist
begins planning a replacement for a permanent loss. The victory changes the people
who won it without turning every future turn into arithmetic.
