# Root Snare

*Roots punch through the ground and lace together around the trespassers’ legs.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-root-snare


**Action:** Main Action; spell

Choose the printed number of connected visible ground squares within range. They become difficult terrain until the start of this creature’s next turn. A creature in a chosen square when the ability resolves defends with Agility: Weak attack adds no condition, Standard applies Slowed 1, Strong Slowed 2. No damage; no repeated entry or turn-end attack. The creature’s card prints the controller’s attack attribute. Flying creatures above the ground are unaffected.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
