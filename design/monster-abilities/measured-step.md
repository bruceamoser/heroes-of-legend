# Measured Step

*It chooses its footing, shifting just far enough to clear the worst angle.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-measured-step

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

Move up to 2 squares, paying normal terrain costs. This movement provokes normally and does not grant Hidden.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
