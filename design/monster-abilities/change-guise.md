# Change Guise

*The creature's silhouette folds between a familiar person and an unmistakably animal shape; the eyes keep the same urgent recognition.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-change-guise


**Action:** Maneuver

Change between humanoid and hybrid appearance. Keep current HP, attributes, resistances, conditions and printed movement. This never heals, refreshes a resource or changes the turn budget. Listed natural attacks require hybrid form; a held weapon attack and Basic Melee remain available in humanoid form. Equipment stays carried or worn where it fits; no free equipment duplication or extra inventory capacity.

This ability grants no infection, control over another character or permanent change to a player character.
