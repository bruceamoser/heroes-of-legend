# Scatter Body

*The shape bursts apart around the weapon and closes again as crawling fragments.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-scatter-body

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Passive

The creature’s printed physical resistance represents its dispersed body. An area attack ignores that physical resistance, but not unrelated resistance. It still occupies its footprint and cannot overlap another creature at rest. No extra attacks from individual members; one HP pool and one turn.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
