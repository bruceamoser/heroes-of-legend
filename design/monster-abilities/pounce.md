# Pounce

*It gathers itself, then arrives in a rush of claws and weight.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-pounce

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed attack. If the creature moved at least 2 squares toward this target immediately before the attack this turn, Standard or Strong also makes the target Prone. Movement is paid normally, not supplied by Pounce.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
