# Closing Jaws

*The mouth opens wide enough to swallow the light behind its prey.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-swallow-threat


**Action:** Main Action

One target already Grappled by this creature and no larger than 1×1: use the printed bite attack; Strong also applies Blinded 1 as the jaws close around the victim. The victim remains in its square and the holder keeps all grapple restrictions. This card does not create an off-map stomach, automatic death or an extra damage tick.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
