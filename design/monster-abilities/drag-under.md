# Drag Under

*A hooked limb braces against the bank and heaves its catch toward the dark water.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-drag-under


**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed attack against one target. Standard pulls 1 square; Strong pulls 2. Stability applies. This does not automatically grapple, submerge, drown or transport the attacker. A hazardous destination must actually be reached.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
