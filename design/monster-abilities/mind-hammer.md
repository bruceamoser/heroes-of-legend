# Mind Hammer

*For a moment, the victim remembers a pain that has not happened yet.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-mind-hammer


**Action:** Main Action; spell

Use the printed mental attack. Standard also applies Dazed 1; Strong applies Dazed 2. Ordinary turn-start action choices and end-turn decay apply. Damage and the condition use one outcome, not two rolls.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
