# Driving Gust

*Dust lifts in a sheet and cloaks snap straight behind their wearers.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-gust


**Action:** Main Action

The printed area attack pushes 1/2/3 squares on Weak/Standard/Strong, away from its origin. Apply Stability to each target. No direct damage; collisions can hurt. A single target is moved once even if it occupies several affected squares.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
