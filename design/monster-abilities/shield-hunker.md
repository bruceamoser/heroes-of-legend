# Shield Hunker

*The shield rises, scraping stone as its bearer crouches behind the rim.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-shield-hunker


**Action:** Reaction

Before a shield-blockable attack resolves, spend Reaction to gain 1 defense Boon until the start of this creature’s next turn. For an NPC, apply 1 Bane to player attacks it can block instead. The DA decides whether the shield can block an attack. This stacks with Defend through ordinary Boon/Bane rules. No repeated activation adds another copy.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
