# Borrow a Voice

*A familiar voice comes from an unfamiliar mouth with exactly the remembered hesitation.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-borrowed-voice

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

Copy the sound of a voice it has heard until its next turn. This grants no knowledge, obedience, language or combat modifier. A suspicious listener may use Reason with normal Boons/Banes when being deceived has consequences.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
