# Cover a Companion

*The guardian steps into the line of danger, making the attacker work around its raised guard.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-cover-a-companion

**Action:** Reaction

Before a visible enemy attacks a willing ally adjacent to this creature, hinder the attack if this creature can see the attacker. Apply 1 Bane if the attacker rolls or grant the ally 1 Boon if the ally rolls defense. This creature must also be adjacent to the attacker, or hold a shield the DA judges can obstruct the attack. No movement, target substitution or damage transfer. For a single player area attack, use the normal worst-target modifiers; do not roll again.
