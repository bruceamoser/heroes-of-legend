# Elemental Burn

*Heat crawls along the strike, or frost flowers across the skin where it passes.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-elemental-burn


**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed attack. Standard also applies the printed Burning 1 or Freezing 1; Strong applies that condition at 2. The creature card chooses one condition, not either at use. Burning is fire damage; Freezing is cold damage. Lightning attacks may explicitly leave Burning.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
