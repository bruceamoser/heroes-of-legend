# Flatten into Cover

*The cat lowers its body into the broken outline of the brush.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-great-cat-flatten-into-cover


**Action:** Maneuver

In actual concealment, gain 1 Boon to its next hiding roll before turn end. This is not a free Hide action or automatic Hidden.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
