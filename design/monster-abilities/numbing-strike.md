# Numbing Strike

*The struck limb goes rigid for one terrifying instant.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-numbing-strike

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action

The worst player defense outcome applies Paralyzed 1 as printed. This attack cannot be used on consecutive turns, regardless of outcome. This is not lasting petrification.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
