# Spore Curtain

*A slow shake of its crown releases a drifting veil of pale spores that hangs between the trunks.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-spore-curtain

**Action:** Maneuver

Fill up to 3 connected empty squares within 3 squares with spores until the start of this creature's next turn. The spores provide partial cover when an attack crosses them. Use the usual cover rules. The spores do not grant Hidden or cause a condition or damage. Reusing this ability replaces its previous cloud.
