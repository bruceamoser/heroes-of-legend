# Knockdown Strike

*The target’s footing gives way beneath the force of the blow.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-knockdown-strike


**Action:** Main Action

Apply Prone only at the printed outcomes. Stability does not prevent Prone. Standing costs 2 squares of movement; no extra attack modifier is added.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
