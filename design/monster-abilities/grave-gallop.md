# Driving Stride

*Hooves hammer a straight course through dust and broken grass.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-grave-gallop

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

Move up to 3 squares in a straight line, paying normal terrain costs. This movement provokes normally.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
