# Spectral Passage

*The outline goes thin as candle smoke and spills through a crack in the stone.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-spectral-passage

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

Move up to 2 squares, including through nonliving walls at movement cost 1, but finish in a visible legal unoccupied space. Cannot pass through living occupants or remain inside a wall. This voluntary movement can provoke when leaving reach. It does not grant invisibility, flight, damage immunity or passage through a ward expressly barring spirits.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
