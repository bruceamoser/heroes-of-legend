# Pack Hunter

*The pack spreads without a sound; when you turn toward one set of teeth, another closes in.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-pack-hunter


**Action:** Passive

When this creature attacks a target adjacent to another active ally, that attack has 1 additional Threat Bane (or 1 attack Boon when player-controlled). An unconscious, Paralyzed, or uncommanded summoned ally does not qualify. This does not grant an extra attack.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
