# Thunderclap

*A sharp report rolls through the space like a door slammed by the sky.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-thunderclap

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

One hearing creature within 2 squares gains Deafened 1. Once per turn; no damage.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
