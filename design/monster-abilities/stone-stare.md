# Stone Stare

*Gray veins climb from the victim’s feet while the creature holds an unblinking stare.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-stone-stare

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action; spell

One target within the printed range that can see the creature: Weak Slowed 1; Standard Slowed 2; Strong Paralyzed 1. No HP damage. A target may avert their eyes until their next turn: the gaze cannot select them, but they have 1 Bane on attacks against this creature and defenses against its attacks during that time. Declare before the gaze resolves. There is no permanent petrification or instant death in this card.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
