# Grave Stench

*The creature exhales a breath of wet earth and spoiled meat.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-foul-exhalation

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

One adjacent breathing creature gains Poisoned 1. Once per turn; no damage.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
