# Anchor Bound

*Every step drags an invisible chain back toward the thing it cannot abandon.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-anchor-bound


**Action:** Passive

The entry names a visible anchor and its interaction. Destroying or resolving that anchor has only the printed effect. Unless stated otherwise, the creature dies at 0 and does not regenerate a new body. A story hook is not automatic immunity.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
