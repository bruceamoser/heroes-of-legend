# Dragging Strike

*A hook, current or reaching limb pulls the target toward the threat.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-dragging-strike


**Action:** Main Action

The printed attack pulls the listed distance toward the attacker. Apply Stability and normal terrain/collision rules. No extra grapple unless printed.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
