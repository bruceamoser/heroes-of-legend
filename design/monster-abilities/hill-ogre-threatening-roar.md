# Threatening Roar

*A bellow rolls out of the ogre’s broad chest.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-hill-ogre-threatening-roar

**Status:** Revised creature proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Action:** Maneuver

One hearing creature within 3 squares gains Frightened 1 of the ogre; once per turn.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
