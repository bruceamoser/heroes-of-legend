# Monster abilities

Original HOL creature capabilities. These are separate from learned character
abilities: no DP, no player Discipline requirements, no automatic access from an
animal form or a summoning category. Each creature supplies its own attack values.
Shared attack templates describe attacker
outcomes for authoring; the DA creature card prints the corresponding defense
outcomes explicitly. The player reference prints attacker outcomes. Never ask the
DA to convert a template’s result during play.

The creature entry prints complete action rules. Per-creature outcomes are
authoritative where they specialize a shared template.

Reuse an existing capability when its trigger, target, effect and limits fit.
Creature-specific descriptive text may change its presentation without creating
another mechanical ability. Different anatomy can express the same rule: Clinging
Recoil works for a corpse's clutch, adhesive flesh or slime. New templates should
add a meaningful option rather than rename an existing effect. See the
[complete action and trope review](../bestiary/action-coverage.md) for assignments,
deliberate omissions and the limits of its CV estimates.
