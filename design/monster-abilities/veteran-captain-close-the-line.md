# Close the Line

*The captain points to the gap before anyone else sees it.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-veteran-captain-close-the-line

**Status:** Revised creature proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Action:** Maneuver

One willing ally within 3 squares that can hear may move 1 square immediately, ending adjacent to another ally. This voluntary movement provokes normally; once per turn.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
