# Lifeless Body

*Nothing breathes beneath the torn coat; nothing living looks through the visor.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-lifeless-body


**Action:** Passive

The card must list its exact condition immunities; this trait grants no additional unlisted ones. It does not breathe or need food. Undead entries marked healing-harmed take a healing result as Life damage; constructs marked repair-only do not regain HP from ordinary healing. Neither rule prevents physical attacks from dealing their normal minimum damage.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
