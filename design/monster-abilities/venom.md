# Venom

*A needle catches the skin, and a bitter numbness spreads from the puncture.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-venom


**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed attack. Standard also applies Poisoned 1; Strong applies Poisoned 2. Poisoned gives Banes, not additional HP damage, and follows ordinary decay. No second resistance roll.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
