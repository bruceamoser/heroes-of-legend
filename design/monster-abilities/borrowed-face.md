# Borrowed Face

*The face rearranges itself like a reflection disturbed by a stone.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-borrowed-face


**Action:** Maneuver

Adopt the visible appearance of a person of the same footprint, keeping all statistics and carried equipment. The voice and appearance pass casual observation; suspicious examination uses the ordinary player Reason procedure. It supplies no memories, languages, identity credentials or copied powers. Lasts until dismissed or this creature reaches 0 HP.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
