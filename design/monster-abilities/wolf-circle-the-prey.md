# Circle the Prey

*The wolf slips around the prey while its pack keeps the jaws occupied.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-wolf-circle-the-prey

**Status:** Revised creature proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Action:** Maneuver

If another conscious, non-Paralyzed ally (not an uncommanded summon) is adjacent to an adjacent enemy, move up to 2 squares without provoking from that enemy. End adjacent to it. Other opportunity attacks and terrain costs apply.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
