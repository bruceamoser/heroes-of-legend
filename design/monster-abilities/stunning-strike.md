# Stunning Strike

*The blow leaves thought and motion briefly out of step.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-stunning-strike

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action

The printed attack applies Dazed. Retained action categories are selected at turn start; do not retroactively remove spent actions.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
