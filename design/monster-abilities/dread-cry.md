# Dread Cry

*Its cry seems to come from somewhere behind your ribs.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-dread-cry


**Action:** Main Action

A printed mental attack applies Frightened 1/2/3 on Weak/Standard/Strong, with no damage unless separately printed. The source is this creature; Banes apply while the target perceives it. The cry must be heard. Ordinary decay applies and no second save is made.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
