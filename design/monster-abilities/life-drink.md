# Life Drink

*The victim pales as a stolen flush of warmth returns to the attacker’s face.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-life-drink


**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed Life attack. If at least 1 HP is actually lost, the attacker recovers 1 HP on Standard or 2 HP on Strong, capped by the target’s actual HP loss and the attacker’s maximum. This explicitly grants undead-compatible restoration, not a healing spell. An uncommanded summon cannot trigger this attack. Defeat at 0 still happens immediately.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
