# Scatter Embers

*A spray of glowing cinders hangs between the creature and its pursuer.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-scatter-embers

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

One adjacent creature gains Burning 1. Once per turn; no immediate damage. The condition uses normal resistance and decay.

Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed limits apply.
