# Take the Scent

*The hound lifts its nose and finds the freshest thread of scent.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-trail-hound-take-the-scent


**Action:** Maneuver

Gain 1 Boon on its next Reason scent-tracking roll before turn end; needs a scent trail. No automatic discovery.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
