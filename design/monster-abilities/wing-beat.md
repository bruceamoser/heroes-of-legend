# Wing Beat

*Great wings beat downward, throwing loose earth into every nearby face.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-wing-beat

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Maneuver

Choose one adjacent creature. It becomes Slowed 1 until its normal decay removes it. No damage or roll. This is not a push, extra movement or a free attack. Once per turn; immunity to Slowed applies. The narrative dust does not also blind anyone.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
