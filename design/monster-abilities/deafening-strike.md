# Deafening Strike

*A concussive report swallows every other sound.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-deafening-strike

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action

The printed attack applies Deafened for the listed duration, then normal decay. It adds no unprinted damage or interruption.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
