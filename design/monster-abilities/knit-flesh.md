# Knit Flesh

*The torn flesh crawls together, leaving a wet seam that opens again when touched by flame.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-knit-flesh

**Status:** Proposed monster-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Passive

At the end of its turn, if above 0 HP, recover the printed amount. Taking any fire damage since the end of its previous turn suppresses this recovery once. This is innate regeneration, not spell healing; it never revives the dead. A summoned version lacks this trait unless its summon entry explicitly permits it.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
