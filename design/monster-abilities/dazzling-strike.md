# Dazzling Strike

*Light or a sudden wash of sensation overwhelms the target’s sight.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-dazzling-strike

**Status:** Proposed creature-only ability; not purchasable with DP.

**Action:** Main Action

The printed attack applies Blinded for the listed duration. Use normal decay; no permanent blindness is implied.

Actual damage, targeting and defense attribute are printed on the creature card.
