# Warning Bellow

*The bear’s roar fills the space between itself and its cubs.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-brown-bear-warning-bellow

**Status:** Revised creature proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Action:** Maneuver

One hearing creature within 3 squares gains Frightened 1 of the bear; once per turn.

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
