# Lower the Tusks

*Its shoulders bunch behind the lowered tusks.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-wild-boar-lower-the-tusks

**Status:** Revised creature proposal for Bruce’s review.

**Action:** Maneuver

Gain Stability 1 until its next turn. Use the highest Stability; also gain 1 Boon on its next Tusks attack this turn (1 extra Bane on that target’s defense).

**Limits:** Costs its Maneuver; no extra action is granted.
