# Grave Chill

*The touch is colder than winter iron, and the limb refuses to answer.*

## Rules

**ID:** monster-grave-chill


**Action:** Main Action

Use the printed cold attack. Strong also applies Paralyzed 1. This ability cannot be used on consecutive turns; use another action on the intervening turn. Paralysis takes effect immediately and decays at the target’s turn end.

**Limits:** Only creatures whose entries list this ability possess it. Printed
entry values and restrictions apply; no ability grants unlisted actions.
