# Where you grew up, and what you did

Ancestry does not dictate culture. Choose one culture and one background, then
one paired gift from each. A past can suggest a personality without prescribing it.

## 18 cultures

- [Border Marches](origins/culture-border-marches.md) — Your neighbors know the difference between vigilance and fear, though keeping that distinction takes work.
- [Cairn Communities](origins/culture-cairn-communities.md) — The dead are remembered at supper and in council, while the living argue freely about their advice.
- [Cloister Valleys](origins/culture-cloister-valleys.md) — Bells mark the day, but no two residents agree about what a disciplined life should become.
- [Deep Halls](origins/culture-deep-halls.md) — Homes, gardens and markets fill the chambers beneath the hills; the surface is only one neighborhood.
- [Ember Cities](origins/culture-ember-cities.md) — Kilns, glassworks and night markets make the streets glow after sundown.
- [Forest Communities](origins/culture-forest-communities.md) — Paths change with the season, and your neighbors know the trees by more than their timber.
- [Free Harbors](origins/culture-free-harbors.md) — Cargoes and rumors change hands faster than the tide. You learned to listen in three directions.
- [Gathered Peoples](origins/culture-gathered-peoples.md) — Displacement brought strangers together; shared work made a new home.
- [Grass Sea Clans](origins/culture-grass-sea-clans.md) — A roof of stars, a road of grass, and neighbors who may be a day’s ride away.
- [Highland Holds](origins/culture-highland-holds.md) — The climb is long, the welcome warm, and every tool has a place by the hearth.
- [Island Kinships](origins/culture-island-kinships.md) — You grew up with a horizon on every side and family on more than one shore.
- [Learning Houses](origins/culture-learning-houses.md) — Debate followed you from the lesson room to supper. Curiosity was a habit before it was a profession.
- [Oasis Cities](origins/culture-oasis-cities.md) — Water is hospitality, memory and responsibility. Every caravan brings another version of the world.
- [Reed Villages](origins/culture-reed-villages.md) — Raised walkways and low boats connect homes that seem to float between sky and water.
- [River Settlements](origins/culture-river-settlements.md) — The river brought work, travelers and news long before it brought you an urge to leave.
- [Terrace Towns](origins/culture-terrace-towns.md) — Gardens climb the cliffs and stairs stitch whole neighborhoods together.
- [Traveling Companies](origins/culture-traveling-companies.md) — Home was whoever helped raise the awning when the rain began.
- [Walled Communities](origins/culture-walled-communities.md) — Your home knows the comfort of a gate and the danger of believing it solves everything.
