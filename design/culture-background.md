# Where you grew up, and what you did

Ancestry does not dictate culture. Choose one culture and one background, then
one paired gift from each. A past can suggest a personality without prescribing it.

## 18 cultures

- [Border Marches](origins/culture-border-marches.md) — *Your neighbors know the difference between vigilance and fear, though keeping that distinction takes work.*
- [Cairn Communities](origins/culture-cairn-communities.md) — *The dead are remembered at supper and in council, while the living argue freely about their advice.*
- [Cloister Valleys](origins/culture-cloister-valleys.md) — *Little lights drift above the cloister paths after dusk. In the quiet rooms below, patient listeners teach troubled minds to find their footing again.*
- [Deep Halls](origins/culture-deep-halls.md) — *Homes, gardens and markets fill the chambers beneath the hills; the surface is only one neighborhood.*
- [Ember Cities](origins/culture-ember-cities.md) — *Kilns, glassworks and night markets make the streets glow after sundown.*
- [Forest Communities](origins/culture-forest-communities.md) — *You learned the woodland paths by gathering beside your elders and learned its stories by giving each remembered speaker a different voice.*
- [Free Harbors](origins/culture-free-harbors.md) — *Cargoes and rumors change hands faster than the tide. You learned to listen in three directions.*
- [Gathered Peoples](origins/culture-gathered-peoples.md) — *Many peoples share your streets. A quiet thought across a crowded room and a practiced hand with a bandage both say that someone is looking out for you.*
- [Grass Sea Clans](origins/culture-grass-sea-clans.md) — *You grew up beneath an open sky, watching riders test their aim and blades at the gathering grounds before the herds moved on.*
- [Highland Holds](origins/culture-highland-holds.md) — *Your neighbors keep winter in their storehouses and thunder in their songs. Frost preserves the harvest; a sharp report carries a warning across the crags.*
- [Island Kinships](origins/culture-island-kinships.md) — *You grew up with a horizon on every side and family on more than one shore.*
- [Learning Houses](origins/culture-learning-houses.md) — *Debate followed you from the lesson room to supper. Curiosity was a habit before it was a profession.*
- [Oasis Cities](origins/culture-oasis-cities.md) — *Water is hospitality, memory and responsibility. Every caravan brings another version of the world.*
- [Reed Villages](origins/culture-reed-villages.md) — *On cold mornings, elders shape icy handholds along the pilings. At night, families leave a place by the fire for the ancestors who still watch over them.*
- [River Settlements](origins/culture-river-settlements.md) — *The river gives your people passage and livelihood. You learned to cross its currents and to lend breath to those it almost took.*
- [Terrace Towns](origins/culture-terrace-towns.md) — *Children swing between the terraces while lamplighters carry warm light along the evening stairs. You learned to make a steep place feel like home.*
- [Traveling Companies](origins/culture-traveling-companies.md) — *You learned to hold an audience with a flashing blade, a sweeping bow and the well-timed distraction that lets a companion make their entrance.*
- [Walled Communities](origins/culture-walled-communities.md) — *Your home knows the comfort of a gate and the danger of believing it solves everything.*
