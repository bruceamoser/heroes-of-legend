# Daring Scoundrel

Smile first. They should know this was your idea.

**What you bring:** Guile melee; movement; social. **Inspiration lens:** Rogue: swashbuckler fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Smile Before Steel](../cards/smile-before-steel.md) with Arms 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Arms 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your confidence is a fighting technique. Use Guile to strike, create an opening to move, and make sure the room notices the exit. The character can be a swaggering pirate or someone whose smile conceals how frightened they are.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Under Their Guard](../cards/under-their-guard.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Bow to the Balcony](../cards/bow-to-the-balcony.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Story Singer](story-singer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
