# Field Alchemist

Three bottles, two smells, and a plan nobody should breathe too deeply.

**What you bring:** Consumables; smoke; condition care. **Inspiration lens:** Alchemist fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Smoke in a Bottle](../cards/smoke-in-a-bottle.md) with Craft 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Craft 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Prepare before trouble, then choose when a bottle is worth the hand and action it needs. Smoke changes a route; a brewed potion restores life; a tonic clears an ally’s head. Recipes and ingredients keep preparation real, while the inventory die keeps counting light.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Bottled Morning](../cards/bottled-morning.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Cleverest Mixture](../cards/the-cleverest-mixture.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Thinking Fighter](thinking-fighter.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
