# Shape Scholar

Stone, rope, iron: every substance has another way it could be.

**What you bring:** Environment; transformation; tools. **Inspiration lens:** Wizard: Transmutation. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [The Patient Metal](../cards/the-patient-metal.md) with Scholarship 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Scholarship 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Give the party another relationship with the obstacle. A bent fitting can be repaired, stone can become a route with time, and your own body can slip through a gap. Transformation should produce plans as often as attacks.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Teach Stone to Bend](../cards/teach-stone-to-bend.md) (Scholarship 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Shape of Escape](../cards/the-shape-of-escape.md) (Scholarship 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Roadside Artificer](roadside-artificer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
