# Reader of Omens

You have already spilled this cup in a dream.

**What you bring:** Prediction; information; rescue. **Inspiration lens:** Wizard: Divination. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [I Have Seen This Step](../cards/i-have-seen-this-step.md) with Scholarship 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Scholarship 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Say the warning before the dice, when it can still change a friend’s choice. Your questions reveal risks and ways to investigate them, not an infallible plot outline. The pleasure is recognizing a moment you were prepared for.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Ask the Falling Ash](../cards/ask-the-falling-ash.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Moment Foretold](../cards/the-moment-foretold.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Thinking Fighter](thinking-fighter.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
