# Still-Hand Adept

A quiet breath, an open hand, and all their strength goes past you.

**What you bring:** Unarmed control; Reason combat. **Inspiration lens:** Monk: Open Hand. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [The Untroubled Hand](../cards/the-untroubled-hand.md) with Athletics 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Athletics 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your Reason is visible in the way you yield and redirect. Push someone away from a vulnerable friend, wait for a blow worth turning, then place a decisive touch. You need no weapon and no pool of points to remember.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Turn the River](../cards/turn-the-river.md) (Athletics 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Stillness at the Center](../cards/stillness-at-the-center.md) (Athletics 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Elemental Adept](elemental-adept.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
