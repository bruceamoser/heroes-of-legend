# Sun Knight

Your promise becomes something others can stand behind.

**What you bring:** Protection; radiant offense. **Inspiration lens:** Paladin: Devotion; Cleric: War and Light. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Oathlight Strike](../cards/oathlight-strike.md) with Radiance 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Radiance 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your attacks leave room for someone else to survive or advance. Put a friend beside you and make the promise visible. Devotion can be to a person, a cause or a god; the card does not assign an alignment.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [A Shelter of Light](../cards/a-shelter-of-light.md) (Radiance 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Carry the Sunrise](../cards/carry-the-sunrise.md) (Radiance 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Green Healer](green-healer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
