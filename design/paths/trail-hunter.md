# Trail Hunter

You find the trail, choose the ground, and make the first shot count.

**What you bring:** Ranged pressure; tracking. **Inspiration lens:** Ranger: Hunter. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Read the Quarry](../cards/read-the-quarry.md) with Marksmanship 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Marksmanship 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Reading tracks and reading an enemy’s next step feel like one profession. Knowledge can guide your first ranged attack; later cards make escape costly. A hunter can be a guardian, a provider or a relentless pursuer rather than simply an archer.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Cut Off the Escape](../cards/cut-off-the-escape.md) (Marksmanship 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Hunt Ends Here](../cards/the-hunt-ends-here.md) (Marksmanship 3 and Wilderness 1) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Beast Companion](beast-companion.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
