# Light-Fingered Adventurer

A lock, a ledge, a dangling key: the world is full of invitations.

**What you bring:** Infiltration; object play. **Inspiration lens:** Rogue: Thief and Arcane Trickster. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [The Key Changes Hands](../cards/the-key-changes-hands.md) with Subterfuge 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Subterfuge 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Notice the useful object as readily as the dangerous enemy. An early theft still requires proximity; lock-reading brings Reason to entry; the distant theft makes a moment of a key nobody can reach. The rules specify objects so the player can invent the opportunity.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [A Lock Has a Language](../cards/a-lock-has-a-language.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [An Impossible Theft](../cards/an-impossible-theft.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Roadside Artificer](roadside-artificer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
