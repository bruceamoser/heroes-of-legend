# Ward Weaver

Draw a line in the air and tell the danger where it ends.

**What you bring:** Protection; countermagic. **Inspiration lens:** Wizard: Abjuration; Cleric: protective priest. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Sign against Harm](../cards/a-sign-against-harm.md) with Guard 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Guard 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Keep your Reaction for the threat worth meeting. A small sign protects one person; unravelling a spell is a more deliberate contest; the circle protects a gathering. The ward is something you choose to place, not a blanket immunity attached to the character.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Unravel the Bolt](../cards/unravel-the-bolt.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Circle Holds](../cards/the-circle-holds.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Sun Knight](sun-knight.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
