# Ancestral Guardian

The people who raised you still stand at your shoulder.

**What you bring:** Protection; spirit support. **Inspiration lens:** Barbarian: ancestral/totemic protector fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Hand from Before](../cards/a-hand-from-before.md) with Calling 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Calling 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your ancestors appear at the places where your friends need them. Give one companion the confidence to hold, or use a shade to occupy the route an enemy wanted. Their faces and opinions can belong to your character’s history.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Circle of Old Names](../cards/circle-of-old-names.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Ancestors Stand](../cards/the-ancestors-stand.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Grave Warden](grave-warden.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
