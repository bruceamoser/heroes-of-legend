# Story Singer

You know the verse that makes people remember who they wanted to be.

**What you bring:** Support; knowledge; social. **Inspiration lens:** Bard: Lore. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Verse for Courage](../cards/a-verse-for-courage.md) with Influence 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Influence 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Ask a friend what they need to attempt, then give the next roll a verse. Beyond combat, tell stories that earn introductions and reveal local hopes. The strongest moment is calling someone back to the fight with their own remaining Grit, not granting an endless reserve.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Remember This Name](../cards/remember-this-name.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Tale Will Tell of You](../cards/the-tale-will-tell-of-you.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Mirror Weaver](mirror-weaver.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
