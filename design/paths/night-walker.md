# Night Walker

The darkness is a route you have already learned.

**What you bring:** Stealth; mobility; ambush. **Inspiration lens:** Monk: Shadow; Ranger: night-hunter fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Step Between Lanterns](../cards/step-between-lanterns.md) with Subterfuge 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Subterfuge 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Treat the map as a pattern of light and dark. Your early movement still needs a real concealed route; later shadow travel gives you a different way across. You are an infiltrator who cares where the lanterns are, not someone permanently exempt from being seen.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [The Unlit Road](../cards/the-unlit-road.md) (Subterfuge 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [No Footfall at All](../cards/no-footfall-at-all.md) (Subterfuge 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Quiet Knife](quiet-knife.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
