# Battle Singer

Your song gives the charge a heartbeat.

**What you bring:** Melee support; formation. **Inspiration lens:** Bard: Valor. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Keep Time with Steel](../cards/keep-time-with-steel.md) with Influence 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Influence 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Fight close enough for your rhythm to matter. Your Guile-based strike opens a step for a companion; your chorus eventually coordinates attacks. A drum, marching chant or ringing battle-cry can carry the same spirit without requiring a particular instrument.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [The Chorus Advances](../cards/the-chorus-advances.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [All Voices as One](../cards/all-voices-as-one.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Sun Knight](sun-knight.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
