# Beast Companion

You know the meaning of that lowered head before anyone else moves.

**What you bring:** Companion; scouting; flanking. **Inspiration lens:** Ranger: Beast Master. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Call the Faithful Hound](../cards/call-the-faithful-hound.md) with Calling 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Calling 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Give the companion a name and a manner long before describing its statistics. It needs your command, so every shared turn asks what you do and what it does. Recalling the same spirit preserves a relationship without hiding an extra uncommanded turn.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Call the Hunting Cat](../cards/call-the-hunting-cat.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Call the Elder Stag](../cards/call-the-elder-stag.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Trail Hunter](trail-hunter.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
