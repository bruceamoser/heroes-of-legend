# Quiet Knife

One deliberate motion ends the argument before it becomes a battle.

**What you bring:** Ambush; precision; escape. **Inspiration lens:** Rogue: Assassin. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Courtesy Unanswered](../cards/a-courtesy-unanswered.md) with Subterfuge 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Subterfuge 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Prepare the approach and decide whether you mean to finish the fight or simply stop the alarm. Guile expresses concealed intent, and an actual Hidden approach helps. For a captor rather than a killer, add the shared Take Them Alive ability.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Silence the Alarm](../cards/silence-the-alarm.md) (Subterfuge 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [A Shadow at the Heart](../cards/a-shadow-at-the-heart.md) (Subterfuge 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Night Walker](night-walker.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
