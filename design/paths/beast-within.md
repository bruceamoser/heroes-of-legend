# Beast Within

Fur rises along your arms, and the world fills with scent.

**What you bring:** Shapeshifting; melee; mobility. **Inspiration lens:** Druid: Circle of the Moon. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Borrow the Beast](../cards/borrow-the-beast.md) with Wilderness 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Wilderness 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Begin by changing form, then commit to claws, teeth and body weight. You carry the same wounds in every shape, and folded equipment cannot keep protecting you. Pounce can also be a momentary transformation, letting a human-form fighter borrow the leap without staying a beast.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Pounce of the Red Claw](../cards/pounce-of-the-red-claw.md) (Wilderness 2 and Athletics 1) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Wear the Great Beast](../cards/wear-the-great-beast.md) (Wilderness 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Root Keeper](root-keeper.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
