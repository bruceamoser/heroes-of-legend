# Unruly Spark

You catch unstable magic and give it a shape just before it bursts.

**What you bring:** Volatile magic; improvisation. **Inspiration lens:** Sorcerer: Wild Magic. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Let the Spark Decide](../cards/let-the-spark-decide.md) with Flame 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Flame 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Choose the element or weather before rolling, then let the outcome determine its impact. A curtain of smoke can cover a retreat; ice can break up an approach. The magic looks unruly without asking you to decode individual dice.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Borrow Tomorrow’s Weather](../cards/borrow-tomorrow-s-weather.md) (Flame 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [A Splendid Mistake](../cards/a-splendid-mistake.md) (Flame 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Dragon Heir](dragon-heir.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
