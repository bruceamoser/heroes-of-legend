# Duelling Master

You give your enemy one opening, and it is the wrong one.

**What you bring:** Precision; control. **Inspiration lens:** Fighter: Battle Master and Champion. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Invitation to the Blade](../cards/invitation-to-the-blade.md) with Arms 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Arms 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Watch one opponent closely. Invite the attack, survive it, then turn its position against it. The character can be a courteous fencing tutor, a scarred mercenary or someone who learned elegance in a very ugly place.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Bind and Turn](../cards/bind-and-turn.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Write the Final Line](../cards/write-the-final-line.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Daring Scoundrel](daring-scoundrel.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
