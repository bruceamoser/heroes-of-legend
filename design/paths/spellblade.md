# Spellblade

Steel carries the spark exactly where you want it.

**What you bring:** Melee magic; flexible range. **Inspiration lens:** Fighter: Eldritch Knight. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Ember Along the Edge](../cards/ember-along-the-edge.md) with Flame 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Flame 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

The weapon is a conductor for your magic, not a second equipment damage chart. Get close, deliver heat with the strike, then use a spark-step when the line shifts. The same training could look like a burning walking stick or a ceremonial sword.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Step Through the Spark](../cards/step-through-the-spark.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [A Sword Made of Dawn](../cards/a-sword-made-of-dawn.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Ward Weaver](ward-weaver.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
