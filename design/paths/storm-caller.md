# Storm Caller

You raise your hand as if the sky were a sail.

**What you bring:** Area pressure; forced movement. **Inspiration lens:** Cleric: Tempest; elemental caster. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Thunder at the Door](../cards/thunder-at-the-door.md) with Storm 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Storm 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Watch for a crowded approach and the friend who needs a clear line through smoke. Thunder, wind and lightning do different jobs. The Master blast is powerful partly because pushing several targets changes what happens next.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [A Road through Rain](../cards/a-road-through-rain.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Sky Comes Down](../cards/the-sky-comes-down.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Elemental Adept](elemental-adept.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
