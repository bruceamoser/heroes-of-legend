# Deep-Water Wanderer

The tide has tried to take you before. You learned its grip.

**What you bring:** Water movement; rescue; control. **Inspiration lens:** Ranger/druid: coastal fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [The Otter’s Bargain](../cards/the-otter-s-bargain.md) with Wilderness 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Wilderness 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

You are the person the group trusts when the bank gives way. Swim with ease, use existing water to move an enemy, or bring someone helpless toward safety. These are useful shoreline adventures without pretending the draft has already settled an entire underwater campaign.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Undertow Answer](../cards/undertow-answer.md) (Wilderness 2 and Frost 1) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Carry Them to Shore](../cards/carry-them-to-shore.md) (Wilderness 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Green Healer](green-healer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
