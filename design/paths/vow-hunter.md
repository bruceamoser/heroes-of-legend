# Vow Hunter

You made a promise. Running will not release them from it.

**What you bring:** Pursuit; single-target pressure. **Inspiration lens:** Paladin: Vengeance. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Name the Reckoning](../cards/name-the-reckoning.md) with Influence 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Influence 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Choose the person you must reach and make pursuit part of the scene. Your words drive a Guile-based attack, and your Reaction keeps you close when they flee. Decide what satisfying the vow means before it makes every decision for you.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [No Road Away](../cards/no-road-away.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Answer for What You Did](../cards/answer-for-what-you-did.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Reader of Omens](reader-of-omens.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
