# Elemental Adept

Your stance changes, and so does the weather.

**What you bring:** Unarmed magic; displacement. **Inspiration lens:** Monk: Four Elements. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Palm of the Gale](../cards/palm-of-the-gale.md) with Storm 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Storm 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your body is how the weather enters the room. Fortitude makes the palm strike work; movement along a wall opens a new approach; the final turn sends the crowd away. Pick a martial style and let the wind follow its shapes.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Walk the Thunder](../cards/walk-the-thunder.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Four Winds at Once](../cards/four-winds-at-once.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Storm Caller](storm-caller.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
