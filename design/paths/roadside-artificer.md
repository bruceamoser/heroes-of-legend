# Roadside Artificer

The contraption rattles. You insist that means it is working.

**What you bring:** Devices; control; useful crafting. **Inspiration lens:** Inventor/artificer fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Spring in the Sleeve](../cards/spring-in-the-sleeve.md) with Craft 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Craft 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Bring one useful contraption into the scene and explain its familiar little fault. A sleeve-hook pulls enemies, a walking toolbox moves tools, and a folding launcher creates a dramatic shove. Devices give the character a recognizable practice without requiring a separate engineering game.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [The Walking Toolbox](../cards/the-walking-toolbox.md) (Craft 2 and Calling 1) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [A Marvel on Wheels](../cards/a-marvel-on-wheels.md) (Craft 3 and Marksmanship 1) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Field Alchemist](field-alchemist.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
