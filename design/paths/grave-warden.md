# Grave Warden

The living deserve their tomorrow. The dead deserve their rest.

**What you bring:** Rescue; undead opposition. **Inspiration lens:** Cleric: Life; death-watch priest fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Keep the Door Closed](../cards/keep-the-door-closed.md) with Life 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Life 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

You stand where the fight’s consequences become personal. Clear a death mark, oppose a restless dead creature, or pay dearly to rescue someone at the moment they fall. Respect for death makes the choices sharper; these cards do not casually erase Last Words.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Rest for the Restless](../cards/rest-for-the-restless.md) (Life 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Not Yet Your Name](../cards/not-yet-your-name.md) (Life 3 and Medicine 1) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Ancestral Guardian](ancestral-guardian.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
