# Dragon Heir

Something ancient smiles when you open your mouth.

**What you bring:** Breath; resilience; spectacle. **Inspiration lens:** Sorcerer: Draconic Bloodline. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Breath of the Wyrmling](../cards/breath-of-the-wyrmling.md) with Flame 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Flame 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

You can announce the ancestry with a breath, survive a dangerous moment behind scales, then cross the fight on briefly unfurled wings. The leap of flight ends in a landing, so the hero still shares the battlefield with everyone else.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Scales beneath the Skin](../cards/scales-beneath-the-skin.md) (Flame 2 and Wilderness 1) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Wings over the Battle](../cards/wings-over-the-battle.md) (Flame 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Sun Knight](sun-knight.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
