# Thinking Fighter

The fight happens once in your mind before your hand moves.

**What you bring:** Knowledge melee; prediction. **Inspiration lens:** Fighter: tactician; Symbaroum Tactician. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Anatomy of an Opening](../cards/anatomy-of-an-opening.md) with Scholarship 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Scholarship 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Describe the detail you noticed: a shoulder held too high, a knee that will not bend, an impatient breath. Knowledge drives the attack, while your later defensive technique spends a Reaction to bring Reason into the fight. Your deductions make you dangerous without making you strong.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Read the Next Blow](../cards/read-the-next-blow.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Checkmate in Three Steps](../cards/checkmate-in-three-steps.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Field Alchemist](field-alchemist.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
