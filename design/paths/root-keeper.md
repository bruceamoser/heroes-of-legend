# Root Keeper

The ground has been listening. Now it answers.

**What you bring:** Ground control; displacement; terrain. **Inspiration lens:** Druid: Circle of the Land. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Roots Remember Feet](../cards/roots-remember-feet.md) with Wilderness 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Wilderness 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Look at where people are trying to go. Catch a pursuer, make a hedge across the approach, or gather scattered enemies into a place your companions can exploit. You can spend an entire useful turn changing the fight without dealing damage.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Briar Gate](../cards/briar-gate.md) (Wilderness 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Orchard Wakes](../cards/the-orchard-wakes.md) (Wilderness 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Beast Within](beast-within.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
