# Watcher Beyond

You remember a shape no window in this world could frame.

**What you bring:** Mental pressure; alien insight. **Inspiration lens:** Warlock: Great Old One. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Thought That Is Not Yours](../cards/a-thought-that-is-not-yours.md) with Mind 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Mind 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

You disturb attention and certainty rather than simply choosing another color of fire. Reason carries the impossible thought; later pressure moves or stills the target. Give your character something ordinary they cling to when the stars begin to feel too close.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [The Angle of Unease](../cards/the-angle-of-unease.md) (Mind 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [When the Stars Look Back](../cards/when-the-stars-look-back.md) (Mind 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Reader of Omens](reader-of-omens.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
