# Mirror Weaver

The eye sees a doorway where you have left a question.

**What you bring:** Deception; diversion; protection. **Inspiration lens:** Wizard: Illusion. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Door That Is Not There](../cards/a-door-that-is-not-there.md) with Mind 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Mind 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Make someone ask whether they saw what they think they saw. An illusory doorway opens a plan, a borrowed silhouette protects a heartbeat, and a company of doubles protects your friends. Illusions need believable placement; their interest comes from the scene, not automatic mind control.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Borrowed Silhouette](../cards/borrowed-silhouette.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [A Company of Reflections](../cards/a-company-of-reflections.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Fey Envoy](fey-envoy.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
