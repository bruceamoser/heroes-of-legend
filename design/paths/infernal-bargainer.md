# Infernal Bargainer

The ember on your tongue speaks in a voice you chose to answer.

**What you bring:** Tempting power; fire; fear. **Inspiration lens:** Warlock: Fiend. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [A Coal for a Promise](../cards/a-coal-for-a-promise.md) with Flame 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Flame 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Your power sounds like a negotiation even when you are throwing fire. Its source could be a bargain you regret, one you honor, or one you intend to outwit. The cards supply the flame and fear; they do not script your morality or a compulsory patron quest.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [The Debt Comes Due](../cards/the-debt-comes-due.md) (Flame 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Open the Furnace Door](../cards/open-the-furnace-door.md) (Flame 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Vow Hunter](vow-hunter.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
