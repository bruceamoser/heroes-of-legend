# Fey Envoy

You learned your manners at a table where every promise mattered.

**What you bring:** Misdirection; displacement; social. **Inspiration lens:** Warlock: Archfey. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [An Invitation Elsewhere](../cards/an-invitation-elsewhere.md) with Mind 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Mind 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Make courtesy physically disorienting. Pull someone into the wrong place, vanish after the blow, or exchange two willing companions to change the arrangement. The performance can be beautiful or unnerving, and its promises remain yours to write.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Behind the Hawthorn](../cards/behind-the-hawthorn.md) (Mind 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Dance beyond the Circle](../cards/dance-beyond-the-circle.md) (Mind 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Mirror Weaver](mirror-weaver.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
