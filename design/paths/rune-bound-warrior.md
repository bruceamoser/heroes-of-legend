# Rune-Bound Warrior

Every mark on your armor remembers a mountain.

**What you bring:** Front line; control. **Inspiration lens:** Fighter: rune-warrior fantasy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Wake the Iron Rune](../cards/wake-the-iron-rune.md) with Armor 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Armor 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Let someone try to move you. The answer is first a stubborn step, then a flash of stone-hard protection, and eventually a blow that sends the surrounding line backward. A rune can be an inheritance, a craft or a promise carved by your own hand.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Stone on Your Shoulders](../cards/stone-on-your-shoulders.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Stride of the Giant](../cards/stride-of-the-giant.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Roadside Artificer](roadside-artificer.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
