# Red Fury

You do not wait for the battlefield to make room. You make room.

**What you bring:** Melee pressure; dangerous endurance. **Inspiration lens:** Barbarian: Berserker. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Blood in the Teeth](../cards/blood-in-the-teeth.md) with Arms 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Arms 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

You are the person who advances when everyone else takes a breath. The first blow asks you to accept an exposed defense; the pleasure is deciding that now is the moment to do it, not maintaining a rage counter.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [Through the Spearpoints](../cards/through-the-spearpoints.md) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Break the Charge](../cards/break-the-charge.md) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need the stated rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Ancestral Guardian](ancestral-guardian.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
