# Green Healer

Where you kneel, crushed grass begins to rise.

**What you bring:** Healing; recovery; sanctuary. **Inspiration lens:** Druid: Circle of the Land; Cleric: Life. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Sap and Breath](../cards/sap-and-breath.md) with Life 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Life 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

Choose who needs your strength and what else they need besides HP: less bleeding, their feet beneath them, or relief from poison. Healing remains a personal expenditure. The character can be gentle, severe or unsettling; the green magic does not require a kindly personality.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [A Bough for the Fallen](../cards/a-bough-for-the-fallen.md) (Life 2 and Wilderness 1) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [The Grove Gives Back](../cards/the-grove-gives-back.md) (Life 3 and Wilderness 1) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Root Keeper](root-keeper.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
