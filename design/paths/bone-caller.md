# Bone Caller

You ask the dead for one more journey, and give the bones a purpose.

**What you bring:** Undead summons; battlefield tools. **Inspiration lens:** Wizard: Necromancy. This is a character
idea, not a new class, subclass or prerequisite chain.

## Begin the story

Learn [Summon Undead — Novice](../cards/the-bones-rise.md) with Calling 1. Any HOL class can buy it after meeting its
requirements. An origin granting Calling 1 can put the idea within reach at level 0.

## At the table

A skeleton can be a servant, an old family tradition or the shape your magic finds easiest. It needs commands and occupies real space. A porter or a rib-shield gives the necromancer something to contribute besides a bigger pile of attack rolls.

## Give it room to grow

- At level 3, [A Ribcage for a Shield](../cards/a-ribcage-for-a-shield.md) (Calling 2) opens another way to affect the scene.
- At level 7, [Summon Undead — Master](../cards/procession-of-the-quiet.md) (Calling 3) offers a larger moment.

These are independent cards. You need every stated discipline rank, not the earlier card.
Try combining this with [Grave Warden](grave-warden.md). The roles describe what you enjoy,
not everything you may do. Class signatures add a separate personal accent.
