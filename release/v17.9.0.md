# Heroes of Legend v17.9.0

- Gain **6 + your new level** in Development Points at each level after 1. Level 2 grants 8 DP, level 3 grants 9, and level 7 grants 13. Add any explicit trait benefits and carry unused points forward.
- Heroes who already advanced receive 2 additional DP for each level earned after level 1. Starting DP still uses **10 + Guile + Knowledge + Reason**, plus explicit trait benefits.
