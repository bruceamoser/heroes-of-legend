# Heroes of Legend Starter Kit v16.1.0

The starter kit now includes two books for levels 1–10: the **Player’s Book** and
**Dungeon Architect’s Guide & Bestiary**.

- Complete DA guidance, an introductory adventure, maps, hazards and table tools.
- 192 illustrated creatures, 93 monster abilities and 73 summoned forms.
- Compact creature cards with defense attributes, damage types and summon markers.
- Shared language choices and rules, plus guidance for creating your own languages.
- Updated player explanations for weakness, crowded forced movement, NPC-only rolls,
  summoned energy forms, controller-paid healing and the six-square tether.
- A clean current-source repository; superseded editions remain in Git history.

The Player’s Book retains all 350 abilities, character choices, equipment, recipes,
wound results, six example heroes and printable sheets. This is a digital playtest
edition. CV and encounter budgets remain estimates; this release does not claim
new encounter-balance validation.

Download the starter-kit ZIP for both books, or choose the individual PDFs.
`manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt` identify and verify the files.
