# Heroes of Legend v17.8.0

- Use touch, short, long and extreme range at 1, 6, 12 and 18 squares. A weapon attack using the weapon’s normal range can reach one extra band with 1 Bane on the attack or 1 Boon on defense.
- Use your class’s casting attribute when a spell calls for a casting roll. The same attribute contributes to starting SP. Automatic spells and distinct defense, hold and escape rolls keep their own procedures.
- Both books include the updated range rules and casting procedures. Spellbooks, class pages, basic ranged actions and the worked examples reflect the changes.
