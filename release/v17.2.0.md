Fencing gives Agility heroes a way to strike and reposition with a one-handed blade. Direct Thrust deals 1/2/4 damage and grants a one-square step on Standard or Strong without triggering movement reactions. Direct Thrust III extends the step to two squares on Strong.

Tactics adds Knowledge-based melee attacks and ways to prepare your own roll or coordinate an ally. Arms gives you a choice of Brawn or Agility for Measured Cut, Riposte, Duelist’s Boundary, and its unarmed attacks. Sweeping Blade and Iron Tempest use Brawn.

Class signatures are individual choices at levels 1, 3, and 7. Each signature gives its own complete rules. This edition also updates character creation, simplifies the maximum SP calculation, and clarifies spell effects and play procedures.

The Dungeon Architect’s Guide links its contents and creature indexes to their entries.

The Starter Kit includes the complete Player’s Book and Dungeon Architect’s Guide & Bestiary. The ZIP includes both books and their verification files. These rules match the Fencing, Tactics, and Arms updates in [Foundry 3.1.0](https://github.com/bruceamoser/heroes-of-legend-foundry/releases/tag/v3.1.0).
