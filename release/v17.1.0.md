# Heroes of Legend v17.1.0

Every spellbook now follows the seven-step construction used by martial paths. Learn four pairs of spells, then three individually learned advanced spells, ending with one level-7 mastery. The sequence costs 1/2/2/3/4/4/5 DP, with level gates 1/1/1/3/3/3/7.

All 29 spellbooks keep their spell list and eleven complete spell blocks together on a facing two-page spread. The revised masteries replace uneven optional folios. The Player’s Book and Dungeon Architect’s Guide use the same advancement rules.

Mordain's intelligence enchantments now have explicit Knowledge scores and let their caster borrow that score through a telepathic link.

Current construction and layout checks are documented in the linked source PR and Workshop issue #310. This remains a playtest edition; native Foundry integration follows the Workshop.
