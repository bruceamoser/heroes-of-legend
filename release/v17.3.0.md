Fencing and Tactics are now among the starting choices for more cultures, backgrounds, and classes.

When you learn a technique that requires ordinary equipment, take a compatible item for free. Heroes begin with paid food, water, lighting, and a lesser healing potion. Return any you do not want during creation for its full cost. Use the ordinary sale rules after creation.

A basic attack with an untrained weapon family deals 1/1/2 damage. Worn armor gives its printed physical resistance. Subtract that suit’s resistance from effective Agility if you lack its training.

Use Brief Rest for First Aid and learned recovery, and Night’s Rest for a full overnight recovery. The Starter Kit includes the complete Player’s Book and Dungeon Architect’s Guide & Bestiary.
